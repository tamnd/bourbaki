Soient $A, B, C$ des relations d’une théorie logique $\mathcal{T}$. Montrer que les relations suivantes sont des théorèmes de $\mathcal{T}$ :

$$
A \Rightarrow (B \Rightarrow A)
$$

$$
(A \Rightarrow B) \Rightarrow ((B \Rightarrow C) \Rightarrow (A \Rightarrow C))
$$

$$
A \Rightarrow ((\operatorname{non} A) \Rightarrow B)
$$

$$
(A \operatorname{ou} B) \Rightarrow ((A \Rightarrow B) \Rightarrow B)
$$

$$
(A \Leftrightarrow B) \Leftrightarrow ((A \operatorname{et} B) \operatorname{ou} ((\operatorname{non} A) \operatorname{et} (\operatorname{non} B)))
$$

$$
\operatorname{non}((\operatorname{non} A) \Leftrightarrow B) \Rightarrow (A \Leftrightarrow B)
$$

$$
(A \Rightarrow (B \operatorname{ou} (\operatorname{non} C))) \Leftrightarrow ((C \operatorname{et} A) \Rightarrow B)
$$

$$
(A \Rightarrow (B \operatorname{ou} C)) \Leftrightarrow (B \operatorname{ou} (A \Rightarrow C))
$$

$$
(A \Rightarrow B) \Rightarrow ((A \Rightarrow C) \Rightarrow (A \Rightarrow (B \operatorname{et} C)))
$$

$$
(A \Rightarrow C) \Rightarrow ((B \Rightarrow C) \Rightarrow ((A \operatorname{ou} B) \Rightarrow C))
$$

$$
(A \Rightarrow B) \Rightarrow ((A \operatorname{et} C) \Rightarrow (B \operatorname{et} C))
$$

$$
(A \Rightarrow B) \Rightarrow ((A \operatorname{ou} C) \Rightarrow (B \operatorname{ou} C)).
$$
