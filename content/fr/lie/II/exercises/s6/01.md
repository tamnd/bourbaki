Montrer que $\exp(U) . \exp(V) . \exp(-U) = \exp\left( \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{1}{n!} (\mathrm{ad}\ U)^n(V) \right)$. En déduire que
$$
H(U, H(V, -U)) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{1}{n!} (\mathrm{ad}\ U)^n(V) = (\exp(\mathrm{ad}\ U))(V).
$$
