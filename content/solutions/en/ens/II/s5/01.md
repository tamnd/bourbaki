Let $(\mathrm{X}_\iota)_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}}$ be a family of sets and let $(\mathrm{Y}_\iota)_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}}$ be a family of sets such that $\mathrm{Y}_\iota \subset \mathrm{X}_\iota$ for each $\iota \in \mathrm{I}$.

By Definition 1 of §3 (tag 03Q8), the product $\prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{X}_\iota$ is the set of all functional graphs $F$ with domain $\mathrm{I}$ such that $F(\iota) \in \mathrm{X}_\iota$ for every $\iota \in \mathrm{I}$. For each $\alpha \in \mathrm{I}$, the projection $\mathrm{pr}_\alpha$ is defined in the same Definition 1 as the mapping of $\prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{X}_\iota$ into $\mathrm{X}_\alpha$ that sends $F$ to $F(\alpha)$.

For a fixed $\iota \in \mathrm{I}$, the inverse image $\mathrm{pr}_\iota^{-1}(\mathrm{Y}_\iota)$ is therefore
\[
\mathrm{pr}_\iota^{-1}(\mathrm{Y}_\iota) = \{\, F \in \prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{X}_\iota \mid F(\iota) \in \mathrm{Y}_\iota \,\}.
\]
Taking the intersection over all $\iota \in \mathrm{I}$ gives
\[
\bigcap_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{pr}_\iota^{-1}(\mathrm{Y}_\iota)
= \{\, F \in \prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{X}_\iota \mid \forall \iota \in \mathrm{I},\; F(\iota) \in \mathrm{Y}_\iota \,\}.
\]

On the other hand, the product $\prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{Y}_\iota$ is, by the same Definition 1, the set of all functional graphs $F$ with domain $\mathrm{I}$ such that $F(\iota) \in \mathrm{Y}_\iota$ for every $\iota \in \mathrm{I}$. Because $\mathrm{Y}_\iota \subset \mathrm{X}_\iota$ for all $\iota$, Corollary 3 of Proposition 6 (tag 03QD) implies $\prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{Y}_\iota \subset \prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{X}_\iota$. Hence an element of $\prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{Y}_\iota$ is exactly a functional graph $F$ with domain $\mathrm{I}$ that belongs to $\prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{X}_\iota$ and satisfies $F(\iota) \in \mathrm{Y}_\iota$ for all $\iota \in \mathrm{I}$.

Therefore the two sets are equal:
\[
\prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{Y}_\iota = \bigcap_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \overset{-1}{\mathrm{pr}_\iota}(\mathrm{Y}_\iota).
\]
