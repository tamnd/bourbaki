The equivalence \( B \subset \complement_{\mathrm{X}} A \Leftrightarrow A \subset \complement_{\mathrm{X}} B \) follows from the duality between subset relations and their complements stated in §1, after Definition 3 (complement of a set). Specifically, for any subsets \( P \) and \( Q \) of \( \mathrm{X} \), the relation \( P \subset Q \) is equivalent to \( \complement_{\mathrm{X}} Q \subset \complement_{\mathrm{X}} P \). Applying this with \( P = B \) and \( Q = \complement_{\mathrm{X}} A \), we have:
\[
B \subset \complement_{\mathrm{X}} A \quad \text{if and only if} \quad \complement_{\mathrm{X}} (\complement_{\mathrm{X}} A) \subset \complement_{\mathrm{X}} B.
\]
Since \( \complement_{\mathrm{X}} (\complement_{\mathrm{X}} A) = A \), this reduces to:
\[
A \subset \complement_{\mathrm{X}} B.
\]
Similarly, the relation \( \complement_{\mathrm{X}} B \subset A \) is equivalent to \( \complement_{\mathrm{X}} A \subset B \) by applying the same duality with \( P = \complement_{\mathrm{X}} B \) and \( Q = A \). This gives:
\[
\complement_{\mathrm{X}} B \subset A \quad \text{if and only if} \quad \complement_{\mathrm{X}} A \subset \complement_{\mathrm{X}} (\complement_{\mathrm{X}} B) = B.
\]

Hence, both equivalences are established.
