Let $R\{x,y\}$ be a relation with $x$ and $y$ distinct letters, and let $z$ be a letter distinct from $x$ and $y$ which does not appear in $R\{x,y\}$.

By the definition of ordered pairs in §2, no. 1, the relation $z = (x,y)$ is equivalent to “$z$ is an ordered pair and $x = \operatorname{pr}_1 z$ and $y = \operatorname{pr}_2 z$”. Consider the relation $S\{z\}$ defined by
$$S\{z\} \iff (\exists x)(\exists y)\bigl(z = (x,y) \text{ and } R\{x,y\}\bigr).$$
As explained in §2, no. 1, using the equivalence just recalled and criteria C33 (Chapter I, §4, no. 3) and C47 (Chapter I, §5, no. 3), the relation $S\{z\}$ is equivalent to
$$z \text{ is an ordered pair and } R\{\operatorname{pr}_1 z,\ \operatorname{pr}_2 z\}.$$

Now $(\exists z)S\{z\}$ is exactly $(\exists z)(\exists x)(\exists y)\bigl(z = (x,y) \text{ and } R\{x,y\}\bigr)$. Since the letter $z$ does not occur in $R\{x,y\}$, we may interchange the quantifiers to obtain $(\exists x)(\exists y)(\exists z)\bigl(z = (x,y) \text{ and } R\{x,y\}\bigr)$. For fixed $x$ and $y$, the relation $(\exists z)\bigl(z = (x,y) \text{ and } R\{x,y\}\bigr)$ is equivalent to $R\{x,y\}$ by criterion C47 (Chapter I, §5, no. 3), because $z$ is not free in $R\{x,y\}$ and there exists a $z$ (namely $(x,y)$) such that $z = (x,y)$. Hence $(\exists x)(\exists y)(\exists z)\bigl(z = (x,y) \text{ and } R\{x,y\}\bigr)$ is equivalent to $(\exists x)(\exists y)R\{x,y\}$. Therefore
$$(\exists x)(\exists y)R\{x,y\} \iff (\exists z)S\{z\}.$$
Replacing $S\{z\}$ by its equivalent form gives
$$(\exists x)(\exists y)R\{x,y\} \iff (\exists z)\bigl(z \text{ is an ordered pair and } R\{\operatorname{pr}_1 z,\ \operatorname{pr}_2 z\}\bigr),$$
which is the first required equivalence.

For the second equivalence, apply the first equivalence to the relation $\neg R\{x,y\}$. We obtain
$$(\exists x)(\exists y)\neg R\{x,y\} \iff (\exists z)\bigl(z \text{ is an ordered pair and } \neg R\{\operatorname{pr}_1 z,\ \operatorname{pr}_2 z\}\bigr).$$
Taking the negation of both sides and using the logical equivalences $(\forall x)(\forall y)R\{x,y\} \iff \neg(\exists x)(\exists y)\neg R\{x,y\}$ and $\neg(\exists z)(A \land \neg B) \iff (\forall z)(A \Rightarrow B)$ (which follow from criteria C33 and C47 of Chapter I), we get
$$(\forall x)(\forall y)R\{x,y\} \iff (\forall z)\bigl((z \text{ is an ordered pair}) \Rightarrow R\{\operatorname{pr}_1 z,\ \operatorname{pr}_2 z\}\bigr).$$
This completes the proof.
