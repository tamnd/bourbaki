**(a)** Let $(e_i)_{i\in I}$ be a basis of the nonzero free right $A$-module $B$. Since $I$ is nonempty, the homomorphism $\rho$ is injective: indeed, if $\rho(a)=0$, then $e_i\rho(a)=0$ for every $i$, and the linear independence of the basis gives $a=0$.

For every left ideal $J$ of $A$, put
$$
BJ=\sum_{j\in J}B\rho(j).
$$
This is a left ideal of $B$. Moreover, since every element of $B$ has a unique expression $\sum_{i\in I}e_i\rho(a_i)$ with only finitely many $a_i$ nonzero, we have
$$
BJ=\left\{\sum_{i\in I}e_i\rho(a_i)\mathrel{}\middle|\mathrel{}a_i\in J,\ a_i=0\text{ for almost all }i\right\}.
$$
Consequently,
$$
BJ=BJ'
$$
implies $J=J'$.

Suppose first that $B$ is left Artinian. By Definition 2 of § 1, every decreasing sequence of left ideals of $B$ is stationary. If
$$
J_0\supset J_1\supset\cdots
$$
is a decreasing sequence of left ideals of $A$, then
$$
BJ_0\supset BJ_1\supset\cdots
$$
is a decreasing sequence of left ideals of $B$, hence is stationary. The preceding injectivity then gives that $(J_n)$ is stationary. Thus $A$ is left Artinian.

If $B$ is left Noetherian, the same argument, with increasing sequences, shows that every increasing sequence of left ideals of $A$ is stationary. By Definition 2 of § 1, $A$ is therefore left Noetherian.

**(b)** By Exercise 3(a), choose a commutative field $K$ and a proper subfield $K'$ isomorphic to $K$ by an isomorphism $\varphi$, with $K$ finite-dimensional over $K'$. Let
$$
C=K\times K
$$
with multiplication
$$
(x,y)(x',y')=(xx',xy'+y\varphi(x')).
$$
By Exercise 3(a), $C$ is left Artinian and left Noetherian but neither right Artinian nor right Noetherian.

Put
$$
A=K,\qquad B=C^{o},
$$
and embed $A$ in $B$ by the homomorphism corresponding in $C$ to
$$
a\longmapsto(a,0).
$$
Since $C$ is a two-dimensional left vector space over $K$ for this scalar action, $B=C^{o}$ is a two-dimensional right vector space over the commutative field $A=K$.

On the other hand, the left ideals of $B=C^{o}$ are precisely the right ideals of $C$. Hence $B$ is neither left Artinian nor left Noetherian, by Exercise 3(a).
