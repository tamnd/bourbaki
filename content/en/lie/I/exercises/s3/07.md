Let $ g $ be a complex Lie algebra, $ \beta $ its Killing form and $ g_0 $ the Lie algebra derived from $ g $ by restricting the base field to $ \mathbf{R} $. Show that the Killing form of $ g_0 $ is twice the real part of $ \beta $.
