Let $ g $ be a real Lie algebra. The multilinear form

$$
(x_1, \ldots, x_n) \mapsto \operatorname{Tr}(\operatorname{ad} x_1 \circ \operatorname{ad} x_2 \circ \cdots \circ \operatorname{ad} x_n)
$$

on $ g^n $ is invariant.
