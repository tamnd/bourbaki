We adopt the notation $ T, J, T_n $ of Definition 1 and no. 6. Let $ t $ be a homogeneous tensor of $ T $ of order $ p $ and $ \sigma $ a permutation of $ \{1, 2, \ldots, p\} $. Then $ t - \sigma t \in J + T_{p-1} $. (Reduce it to the case where $ \sigma $ is a transposition of two consecutive integers.)
