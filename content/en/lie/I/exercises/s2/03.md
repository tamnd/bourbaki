Suppose that $ \mathbf{K} $ is a field. Let $ g $ be a Lie $ \mathbf{K} $-algebra and $ U $ its enveloping algebra. The left regular representation of $ U $ corresponds to a representation $ \rho $ of $ g $ on the space $ U $. Show that the set $ U_+ $ of elements of $ U $ with no constant term is stable under $ \rho $ and that $ U_+ $ has no supplement in $ U $ stable under $ \rho $. In particular, $ \rho $ is not semi-simple. (This will be considered again in Theorem 2 of § 6.)
