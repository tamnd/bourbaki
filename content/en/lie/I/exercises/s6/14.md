Let $ X $ be a nilpotent matrix in $ \mathfrak{gl}(n, K) $. Show that there exist in $ \mathfrak{gl}(n, K) $ two other matrices $ Y, H $ such that $[H, X] = X,\ [H, Y] = -Y,\ [X, Y] = H$. (Argue by induction on $ n $, using the Jordan form of $ X $; thus reduce it to the case where $ X = E_{12} + E_{23} + \cdots + E_{n-1,n} $; then take $ Y $ to be a combination of the $ E_{k,k-1} $ and $ H $ a combination of the $ E_{jj'} $.)
