Let g be a Lie algebra such that the conditions x ∈ g, y ∈ g, [[x, y], y] = 0 imply [x, y] = 0. Show that g is reductive. (Show that the radical r of g is commutative using Exercise 3 of § 5. Then show that [g, r] = 0.)
