Let $ g $ be a subalgebra of $ \mathfrak{gl}(n, K) $ such that the identity representation of $ g $ is semi-simple. Show that every nilpotent matrix $ X \in g $ is contained in a 3-dimensional simple Lie subalgebra of $ g $. (Show that $ g $ is reductive in $ \mathfrak{gl}(n, K) $; then use Exercises 14 and 15 (c).)
