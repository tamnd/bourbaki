(a) Let $ X, Y $ be two matrices of $ \mathfrak{gl}(n, K) $. Show that, if $[X, Y] = X$, $ X $ is nilpotent. (For every polynomial $ f \in K[T] $, observe that
$$
[f(X), Y] = Xf'(X).
$$
(b) Let $ g $ be a Lie algebra and $ h, x $ two elements of $ g $ such that $[h, x] = x$ and there exists $ z \in g $ with $[x, z] = h $. Show that there exists $ y \in g $ such that $[x, y] = h$ and $[h, y] = -y$. (Observe first that $[z, h] + z $ belongs to the centralizer $ n $ of $ x $ in $ g $. Then show that $ n $ is stable under $ \mathrm{ad}\ h $ and that the restriction to $ n $ of $ I - \mathrm{ad}\ h $ is bijective; to do this, note that $ \mathrm{ad}\ x $ is nilpotent, using (a), and prove that, if $ g_i $ is the image of $ g $ under $ (\mathrm{ad}\ x)^i $, $ I - \mathrm{ad}\ h $ gives on taking quotients a bijection of $ (n \cap g_{i-1})/(n \cap g_i) $; for this use the relation
$$
[\mathrm{ad}\ z, (\mathrm{ad}\ x)^k] = -k(\mathrm{ad}\ x)^{k-1}(\mathrm{ad}\ h + \frac{k-1}{2} I).
$$
(c) Let $ g $ be a subalgebra of $ \mathfrak{gl}(n, K) $; suppose that, for every nilpotent matrix $ X \in g $, there exist two other matrices $ H, Y $ in $ g $ such that $[H, X] = X,\ [H, Y] = -Y,\ [X, Y] = H $. Let $ b $ be a subalgebra of $ g $ such that there exists a subspace $ m $ supplementary to $ h $ in $ g $ and such that $[h, m] \subset m$. Show that $ h $ has the same property as $ g $ for all its nilpotent matrices (use (b)).
