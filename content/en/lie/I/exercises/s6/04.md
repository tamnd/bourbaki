Let $ g $ be a semi-simple Lie algebra and $ \rho $ a non-zero simple representation of $ g $ on a finite-dimensional space $ M $. Let $ h $ be the corresponding semi-direct product. Show that $ h = \mathscr{D}h $, that the centre of $ h $ is zero and that $ h $ is not the product of a semi-simple algebra and a solvable algebra.
