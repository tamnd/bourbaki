Let $ a $ be a Lie algebra and $ r $ its radical. If $ r $ has a decreasing sequence of characteristic ideals $ r = r_0 \supset r_1 \supset \cdots \supset r_n = \{0\} $ such that $ \dim r_i/r_{i+1} = 1 $ for $ 0 \leq i < n $, $ g $ is the product of a semi-simple algebra and a solvable algebra.

(Let s be a Levi subalgebra of g. For all x ∈ s, let ρ(x) be the restriction of ad_gx to r. Then ρ is a direct sum of 1-dimensional representations, which are zero because s = Ds.)
