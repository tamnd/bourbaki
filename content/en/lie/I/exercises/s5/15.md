Let $ K $ be a field of characteristic 2. The identity representation of the nilpotent Lie algebra $ \mathfrak{sl}(2, K) $ on $ K^2 $ defines a semi-direct product $ h $ of $ \mathfrak{sl}(2, K) $ by $ K^2 $. Show that $ h $ is solvable but that $ \mathfrak{D}h $ is not nilpotent. Deduce that $ h $ admits no faithful linear representation by triangular matrices. Show also that the conclusions of Exercise 5 are false.
