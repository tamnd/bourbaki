(a) Verify that the following multiplication table defines a 5-dimensional solvable Lie algebra $g$:

$$
[x_1, x_2] = x_5,\quad [x_1, x_3] = x_3,\quad [x_2, x_4] = x_4 \\
[x_1, x_4] = [x_2, x_3] = [x_3, x_4] = [x_5, g] = 0.
$$

(b) Show that the orthogonal of $g$ with respect to the Killing form is $\mathcal{D}g = Kx_3 + Kx_4 + Kx_5$. Deduce that $\mathcal{D}g$ is the largest nilpotent ideal of $g$.

(c) Show that there exists no supplementary subalgebra of $\mathcal{D}g$ in $g$. Deduce that $g$ is not the semi-direct product of a commutative algebra and a nilpotent ideal. (Show that this nilpotent ideal would necessarily be $\mathcal{D}g$.)
