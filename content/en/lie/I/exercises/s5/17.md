Let $g$ be a Lie algebra. Suppose that for every simple $g$-module $M$ of finite dimension over $K$ the $x_M$ are permutable with one another. Show that $g$ is solvable. (Observe that $\mathfrak{D}g$ is contained in the nilpotent radical and hence is solvable.)

[^1] This result, unedited, was communicated to us by N. Jacobson.
