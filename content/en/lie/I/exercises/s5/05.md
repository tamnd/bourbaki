Let $ g $ be a Lie algebra and $(\mathcal{D}^0 g, \mathcal{D}^1 g, \ldots, \mathcal{D}^n g)$ the sequence of derived algebras of $ g $ ($ n \geq 0,\ \mathcal{D}^{n-1}g \neq \mathcal{D}^n g $). Then $ \dim \mathcal{D}^i g / \mathcal{D}^{i+1} g \geq 2^{i-1} + 1 $ for $ 1 \leq i \leq n-2 $. (Taking quotients by $ \mathcal{D}^n g $, reduce it to the case where $ g $ is solvable. Then use the fact that $ \mathcal{D}g $ is nilpotent and Exercise 7 (c) of § 4.)
