Let $ g $ be a solvable Lie algebra such that the conditions $ x \in g,\ y \in g,\ [[x, y], y] = 0 $ imply $[x, y] = 0$. Show that $ g $ is commutative. (Let $ k $ be the largest integer such that $ \mathcal{D}^{k-1}g \neq \{0\},\ \mathcal{D}^k g = \{0\} $. Assuming $ k \geq 2 $, show first that $[\mathcal{D}^{k-2}g, \mathcal{D}^{k-1}g] = \{0\}$ and then that $[\mathcal{D}^{k-2}g, \mathcal{D}^{k-2}g] = \{0\}$, whence a contradiction.)
