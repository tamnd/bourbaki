Let $ g $ be a Lie algebra, $ \rho $ a finite-dimensional representation of $ g $ and $ E $ the associative endomorphism algebra generated by $ 1 $ and $ \rho(g) $. Show that the largest nilpotency ideal $ n $ of $ \rho $ is equal to the set $ n' $ of $ x \in g $ such that $ \mathrm{Tr}(\rho(x)u) = 0 $ for all $ u \in E $. (To show that $ n' \subset n $, show that $ n' $ is an ideal and that for all $ x \in n' $ the semi-simple component of $ \rho(x) $ is zero, by noting that $ \mathrm{Tr}((\rho(x))^n) = 0 $ for every integer $ n > 0 $.)
