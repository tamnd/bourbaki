Let $ g $ be a Lie algebra, $ r $ its radical, $ n $ its largest nilpotent ideal and $ D $ a derivation of $ g $. Show that $ \mathcal{D}g \cap r \subset n $. (Let $ d $ be the Lie algebra of derivations of $ g $ and $ r' $ its radical. If $ x \in g $ is such that $ Dx \in r $, then

$$
\mathrm{ad}(Dx) = [D, \mathrm{ad}\, x] \in \mathcal{D}d \cap r'
$$

(Corollary 2 to Proposition 5), hence $ \mathrm{ad}(Dx) $ is nilpotent (Theorem 1) and hence $ Dx \in n $.)
