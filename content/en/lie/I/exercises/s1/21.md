(a) Let $ K $ be a field of characteristic $ p > 0 $ and $ L $ a not necessarily associative algebra over $ K $. Show that the derivations of $ L $ form a Lie $ p $-algebra with the $ p $-mapping $ D \mapsto D^p $.

(b) Let $ L $ be the algebra $ K[X]/(X^p) $ and $ w $ the $ p $-algebra of derivations of $ L $. Let $ D_i $ ($ 0 \leq i \leq p-1 $) denote the derivation of $ L $ such that $ D_i(X) = X^i $. Show that the $ D_i $ form a basis of $ w $ over $ K $ and that $[D_i, D_j] = (j - i)D_{i+j-1}$ if $ i + j \leq p $, $[D_i, D_j] = 0$ if $ i + j > p $, and $ D_i^p = 0 $, except for $ i = 1 $, in which case $ D_1^p = D_1 $.

(c) Show that, if $ p \geq 3 $, the Lie algebra $ w $ only admits the ideals $\{0\}$ and $ w $. (Using the multiplication table of the $ D_i $, show that every non-zero ideal of $ w $ contains a non-zero multiple of $ D_{p-1} $.)

(d) Let $ V_k $ be the subspace of $ w $ with basis the $ D_i $ of index $ \geq k $. Show that, if $ p \geqslant 5 $, $ V_k $ is identical with the set of $ Z \in w $ whose centralizer is of dimension $ \geqslant k + 1 $. Deduce that, if $ p \geqslant 5 $, the automorphism group of $ w $ is solvable.
