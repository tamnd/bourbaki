Let $g$ be a Lie algebra and $a$ and $b$ two submodules of $g$. For every integer $i \geqslant 0$ we define the submodules $m_i = m_i(a, b)$ and $n_i = n_i(a, b)$ as follows: $m_1 = b, m_{i+1} = [m_i, b], n_1 = a, n_{i+1} = [n_i, b]$. Show that
$$
[a, m_i] \subset n_{i+1}
$$
for $i = 1, 2, \ldots$ (argue by induction on $i$, observing that
$$
m_i([a, b], b) = m_i(a, b)
$$
and $n_i([a, b], b) = n_{i+1}(a, b)$).
