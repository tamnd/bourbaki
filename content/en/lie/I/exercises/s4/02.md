Let $ g $ be a semi-direct product of an algebra $ b $ of dimension 1 and a commutative ideal $ g' $. Let $ x \in b, x \neq 0 $, and $ u $ be the restriction of $ \mathrm{ad}_x x $ to $ g' $.
   (a) For $ g $ to be nilpotent, it is necessary and sufficient that $ u $ be nilpotent.
   (b) For the Killing form of $ g $ to be zero, it is necessary and sufficient that $ \mathrm{Tr}(u^2) = 0 $.
   (c) Deduce from (a) and (b) that there exist non-nilpotent Lie algebras whose Killing forms are zero.
   (d) Deduce from (a) that in a nilpotent Lie algebra such that $ \mathcal{C}^{p-1} g \neq \{0\} $, $ \mathcal{C}^p g = \{0\} $ it is possible that $ \mathcal{C}_i g \neq \mathcal{C}^{p-i} g $.
