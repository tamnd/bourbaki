Let $ g $ be a nilpotent Lie algebra.
   (a) Every subalgebra of $ g $ is subinvariant. (Use Proposition 3.)
   (b) Let $ b $ be a vector subspace of $ g $ such that $ b + \mathcal{D}g = g $. Show that the subalgebra of $ g $ generated by $ b $ is $ g $. (Apply (a) to this subalgebra. Reduce it thus to the case where $ b $ is an ideal of $ g $ and use Proposition 4 of § 1.) Deduce that the minimum number of generators of $ g $ is $ \dim g / \mathcal{D}g $.
