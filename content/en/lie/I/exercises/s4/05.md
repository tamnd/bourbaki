(a) Show that a Lie algebra $ g $ in which every subalgebra is subinvariant is nilpotent. (Show that if $ \dim g > 1 $ every $ x \in g $ belongs to an ideal $ h \neq g $ of $ g $ which has the same property as $ g $ and hence is nilpotent by induction on the dimension of $ g $; hence $ \mathrm{ad}_x x $ is nilpotent.)
   (b) Show that if in a Lie algebra $ g $ every subalgebra distinct from $ g $ is distinct from its normalizer, $ g $ is nilpotent. (Reduce it to (a).)
