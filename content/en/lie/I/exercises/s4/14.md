Let $g$ be a Lie algebra and $\mathfrak{b}$ a subinvariant subalgebra of $g$ such that the centralizer $\mathfrak{z}(\mathfrak{b})$ of $\mathfrak{b}$ in $g$ is zero.

(a) The centralizer $\mathfrak{z}(\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b})$ of $\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}$ in $g$ is contained in $\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}$. (If $\mathfrak{z}(\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}) \not\subset \mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}$, $\mathfrak{z}(\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}) \not\subset \mathfrak{b}$ by Exercise 13; $\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}$ is an ideal of $g$ (§ 1, Exercise 14); let $c = \mathfrak{b} + \mathfrak{z}(\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b})$; $c$ is a subalgebra, $\mathfrak{b}$ is subinvariant in $c$; $\mathfrak{b}$ is an ideal of $\mathfrak{b}_1 \subset c$, where $\mathfrak{b} \neq \mathfrak{b}_1$; let $y \in \mathfrak{b}_1, y \notin \mathfrak{b}$; $y = x + x'$, where $x \in \mathfrak{z}(\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b})$, $x' \in \mathfrak{b}$; then $x \in \mathfrak{z}(\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}) \cap \mathfrak{b}_1, x \notin \mathfrak{b}$; $\mathfrak{d} = Kx + \mathfrak{b}$ is a subalgebra; $\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b} = \mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}$ because $c \cap \mathfrak{z}(\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}) \subset \mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}$, whence $\mathcal{C}^k \mathfrak{b} \subset \mathcal{C}^k \mathfrak{b}$ for all $k$; $\mathfrak{z}(\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}) \cap \mathfrak{b} \not\subset \mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}$ and hence the centre of $\mathfrak{d}$ is non-zero by Exercise 13; a fortiori, $\mathfrak{z}(\mathfrak{b}) \neq \{0\}$, which is absurd.)

(b) Deduce from (a) that, if $\mathfrak{D}$ denotes the Lie algebra of derivations of $\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}$ and $a$ the centre of $\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}$, then $\dim g \leq \dim \mathfrak{D} + \dim a$ (let $g$ operate on $\mathcal{C}^\infty \mathfrak{b}$ by the adjoint representation).
