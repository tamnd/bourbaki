Let $\mathcal{D}$ be the Lie algebra of derivations of the algebra $g_4$ (Exercise 9). Show that $\mathcal{D}$ is of dimension 7 and of zero centre and that the ideal $\mathcal{D}' \subset \mathcal{D}$ of inner derivations has no supplementary subalgebra in $\mathcal{D}$.
