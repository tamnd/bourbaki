Let $ g $ be a Lie algebra and $ U $ its enveloping algebra.

(a) Show that for all $ u \in U $ ($ u \neq 0 $) there exists a finite-dimensional representation $ \pi $ of $ U $ such that $ \pi(u) \neq 0 $. (Use Exercise 2 and Exercise 5 (b) of § 3.)

(b) Deduce from (a) that if $ g $ is semi-simple there exists an infinity of inequivalent finite-dimensional simple representations of $ g $. (Let $ \rho_1, \ldots, \rho_n $ be finite-dimensional simple representations of $ g $, $ N_1, \ldots, N_n $ the annihilators of the corresponding U-modules and $ N = \bigcap_{i=1}^n N_i $. Show that $ N $ is of finite co-dimension in U. Let $ n \in \mathbf{N} $, $ n \neq 0 $. Apply (a) to $ n $.)
