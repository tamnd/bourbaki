Let G be a compact Lie group.

a) Prove that the set of conjugacy classes of normalizers of integral subgroups of G is finite (consider the operation of G on the grassmannian of subspaces of L(G), and apply Exerc. 9).

b) The set of conjugacy classes of (compact) semi-simple subgroups of G is finite (observe that a Lie algebra can contain only finitely-many semi-simple ideals (Chap. I, §6, Exerc. 7) and use $a$)$)$.
