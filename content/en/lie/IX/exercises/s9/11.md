For any integer $n\geq 1$, define a map $\varphi_n$ from $(0,^1_2(\times \mathbf{T}^2$ to $\mathbf{R}^3$ by putting $\varphi_n(r;\alpha , \beta ) = ((n+r$ cos $2\pi \beta )$ cos $2\pi \alpha ,(n+r$ cos $2\pi \beta )$ sin $2\pi \alpha , r$ sin $2\pi \beta )$. Put

$($ 1 $)$

$$
T_n=\varphi_n0,\times \mathbf{T}^2,S_n=\varphi_n(\{0\} \times \mathbf{T}^2)
$$

2

$a)$ Show that the restriction of $\varphi_n$ to $0,^1_2\times \mathbf{T}^2$ is an isomorphism (of class $C^{\omega})$ from $0,^1_2\times \mathbf{T}^2$ to $T_n$ **--** $S_n$.

$b)$ Let $f_n: T_n\rightarrow T_n$ be the map that coincides with the identity on $S_n$ and is such that

$$
f_n(\varphi_n(r;\alpha , \beta )) =\varphi_n(r;\alpha -(n-1)\beta ,-\alpha +n\beta )
$$

for $r >0$. Show that $f_n$ induces an automorphism of $T_n$ **--** $S_n$.

$c)$ Show that there exists a real-analytic manifold structure on$\bigcup\mathbf{R}^3$ such that the maps $f_n: T_n\rightarrow \mathbf{R}^3$ and the canonical injection $\mathbf{R}^3$ **--** $_nS_n\rightarrow \mathbf{R}^3$ are analytic. Denote by X the real-analytic manifold defined in this way.

$d)$ For $\vartheta \in \mathbf{T}$, denote by $R_{\vartheta}$ the rotation

$(x, y, z) \rightarrow (x$ cos $2\pi \vartheta -y$ sin $2\pi \vartheta , x$ sin $2\pi \vartheta +y$ cos $2\pi \vartheta , z)$

of $\mathbf{R}^3$. Prove that putting, for $\theta \in \mathbf{T}$ and $u\in X$,

$\vartheta .u= R_{\vartheta}(u)$ if $u\in X$ **--** $\bigcup_nS_n$;

$\vartheta .u= R_{n\vartheta}(u)$ if $u\in S_n$ for some integer $n\geq 1$

defines an analytic law of operation of $\mathbf{T}$ on X.

$e)$ Show that the set of orbit types of X (for the operation of $\mathbf{T}$ defined in $d))$ is infinite. $f)$ Show that there is no embedding, compatible with the operation of $\mathbf{T}$, of X in a finite dimensional vector space on which $\mathbf{T}$ operates linearly (use Exerc. 9).
