$b)$ For $\tau \in H$, denote by $T_{\tau}$ the complex torus $\mathbf{C}/(\mathbf{Z}+\mathbf{Z}\tau )$. Show that the map $\tau  \rightarrow T_{\tau}$ induces by passage to the quotient a bijective map from the set $H/\mathbf{S}\mathbf{L}(2,\mathbf{Z})$ to the set of isomorphism classes of complex tori of dimension one.
