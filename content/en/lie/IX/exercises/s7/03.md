Let $G =\mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}(2l,\mathbf{R})$, with $l\geq 2$; we use the notations of Exerc. 2 of §4. Denote by $\sigma$ the representation of G on $\mathbf{C}^{2l}$ obtained from the identity representation by extension of scalars.

$a)$ The $\mathbf{Z}$-module X(T) has basis $\varepsilon_1, . . . , \varepsilon_l$.

$b)$ Denote by $\eta_i$ the element $e^{\varepsilon_i}+e^{-\varepsilon_i}$ of $\mathbf{Z}[X(T)]$ and by $\delta$ the element

$_i\prod_{=1}^l(e^{\varepsilon_i}-e^{-\varepsilon_i})$. Show that every element of $\mathbf{Z}[X(T)]^W$ can be written as

$P(\eta_1, . . . , \eta_l) + Q(\eta_1, . . . , \eta_l)\delta$, where P and Q are symmetric polynomials in $l$ variables with coefficients in $^1_2\mathbf{Z}$.
