4) Let $n$ be an integer $\geq 2$.

a) Let $g\in \mathbf{O}(2n,\mathbf{R})$ with det $g=-1$; show that the automorphism Int $g$ of $\mathbf{O}(2n,\mathbf{R})$ induces an automorphism of $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}(2n,\mathbf{R})$ that is not inner.
