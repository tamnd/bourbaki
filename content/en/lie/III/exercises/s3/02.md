Suppose that K is locally compact. Let G be a Lie group of finite dimension n.

(a) Let $\phi$ be an étale endomorphism of G with finite kernel. Let $\mu$ be a left Haar measure of G. Then $\phi$ is proper and
$$
\phi(\mu) = \alpha \cdot \mu|\phi(G) \quad \text{where} \quad \alpha = \frac{\operatorname{Card}(\operatorname{Ker} \phi)}{\operatorname{mod} \operatorname{det} L(\phi)}.
$$
(Use Proposition 55.)

(b) Suppose that G is compact. Let $\phi$ be an étale endomorphism of G. Then Ker $\phi$ is finite, $\phi(G)$ is of finite index in G and
$$
\frac{\mathrm{Card}(\mathrm{Ker}\,\phi)}{\mathrm{Card}(G/\phi(G))} = \mathrm{mod}\,\det L(\phi).
$$
(Use (a) to transform $\mu(G)$.)
(c) Suppose that G is compact and commutative. Let $r \in \mathbf{Z}$ be such that $r.1 \neq 0$ in K and let $\phi$ be the endomorphism $x \mapsto x^r$ of G. Then
$$
\frac{\mathrm{Card}(\mathrm{Ker}\,\phi)}{\mathrm{Card}(G/\phi(G))} = (\mathrm{mod}\,r.1)^n.
$$
(Observe that $L(\phi)$ is the homothety of ratio r by § 2, Corollary to Proposition 7.)
