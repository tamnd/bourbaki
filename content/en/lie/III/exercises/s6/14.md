Let $ a_1 $ be the commutative Lie algebra $ \mathbf{R}^4 $, identified with $ \mathbf{C}^2 $. Let $ a_2 $ be the commutative Lie algebra $ \mathbf{R} $. Let $ \phi $ be the homomorphism of $ a_2 $ into $ \mathrm{Der}(a_1) $ which maps 1 to the derivation $ (z_1, z_2) \mapsto (iz_1, i\sqrt{2}z_2) $. Let a be the semi-direct product of $ a_2 $ by $ a_1 $ corresponding to $ \phi $.

(a) Show that $ (\mathrm{Int}\, a) | a_1 $ contains, for all $ \phi \in \mathbf{R} $, the automorphism
$$
(z_1, z_2) \mapsto (e^{i\phi} z_1, e^{i\sqrt{2}\phi} z_2).
$$

(b) Show that the closure G of $ (\mathrm{Int}\, a) | a_1 $ in $ \mathbf{GL}(a_1) $ contains, for all $ (\phi, \phi') \in \mathbf{R}^2 $, the automorphism $ (z_1, z_2) \mapsto (e^{i\phi} z_1, e^{i\phi'} z_2) $.

(c) Show that $ L(G) $ contains the endomorphism $ u : (z_1, z_2) \mapsto (z_1, 0) $ of $ a_1 $ and that there exists no $ x \in a $ such that $ u = (\mathrm{ad}\, x) | a_1 $.

(d) Deduce that $ \mathrm{Int}(a) $ is not closed in $ \mathrm{Aut}(a) $.
