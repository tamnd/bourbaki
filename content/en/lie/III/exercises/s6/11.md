Let E be a complete normable space over $ \mathbf{C} $, $ v \in \mathcal{L}(E) $ and $ g = \exp(v) $. Suppose that $ \mathrm{Sp}(v) \cap 2i\pi(\mathbf{Z} - \{0\}) = \emptyset $. Let $ E_1, E_2 $ be closed vector subspaces of E which are stable under v, such that $ E_2 \subset E_1 $. Suppose that the automorphism of $ E_1/E_2 $ derived from g is the identity. Then $ v(E_1) \subset E_2 $.
