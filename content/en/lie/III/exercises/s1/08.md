Let $ \mathbf{T} $ be given the normalized Haar and $ \mathscr{L}(L^2(\mathbf{T})) $ the normic topology. Show that the regular representation of $ \mathbf{T} $ on $ L^2(\mathbf{T}) $ is not continuous. (For all $ g \in \mathbf{T} $ distinct from e, construct a function $ f \in L^2(\mathbf{T}) $ such that $ \|f\| = 1 $, $ \|\gamma(g)f - f\| = \sqrt{2} $.)
