Let I be the set of $ (x, \sqrt{2}x) \in \mathbf{R}^2 $ for $ -\frac{1}{2} < x < \frac{1}{2} $. Let U be the canonical image of I in the real Lie group $ H = \mathbf{T}^2 $. Show that U is a Lie subgroup germ of H but that the subgroup $ H' $ of H generated by U is dense in H and not closed in H.
