Let $ G $ be the Hilbert space of sequences $ (\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \ldots) $ of real numbers such that $ \sum_i \lambda_i^2 < +\infty $. Consider $ G $ as a real Lie group. Let $ G_n $ be the set of $ (\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \ldots) \in G $ such that $ \lambda_m \in \frac{1}{m} \mathbf{Z} $ for $ 1 \leq m \leq n $. The $ G_n $ are closed Lie subgroups of $ G $ and hence $ H = \bigcap_n G_n $ is a closed subgroup of $ G $. But this group is totally disconnected and not discrete and hence is not a Lie subgroup of $ G $.
