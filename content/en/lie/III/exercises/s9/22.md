Let G be a finite-dimensional connected real Lie group such that $ L(G) $ is reductive. Then $ G = (V \times S)/N $ where V is a finite-dimensional real vector space, where S is a simply connected semi-simple real Lie group and N is a central discrete subgroup of $ V \times S $. Then $ G/\overline{D^1}G $ is isomorphic to $ V/\mathrm{pr}_1 N $. Hence $ G/\overline{D^1}N $ is compact if and only if $ \mathrm{pr}_1 N $ generates the vector space V.
