Let V be the hyperplane in $ \mathbf{R}^9 $ with equation $ x_1 + \cdots + x_9 = 0 $. Let R be the subset of V consisting of the points

$$
(2, 2, 2, -1, -1, -1, -1, -1, -1), \quad (-2, -2, -2, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1),
$$
$$
(3, -3, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0)
$$

together with the points obtained from these by permuting the coordinates. Show that R is a root system in V of type $ E_8 $.
