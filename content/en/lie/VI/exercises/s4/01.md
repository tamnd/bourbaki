Let R be a root system of type $ E_8 $.

a) Show that if $ \alpha, \beta \in R $ are congruent modulo $ 2\mathbf{Q}(R) $, then $ \beta = \pm \alpha $.

b) Deduce from a) that, if $ w \in W(R) $ acts trivially on $ \mathbf{Q}(R)/2\mathbf{Q}(R) $, then $ w = \pm 1 $.

c) With the notation of no. 10, show that the quadratic form $ \frac{1}{2}(x|x) $ on $ \mathbf{Q}(R) $ defines by passage to the quotient a non-degenerate quadratic form $ q_8 $ on the $ \mathbf{F}_2 $-vector space $ \mathbf{Q}(R)/2\mathbf{Q}(R) $. Show that the pseudo-discriminant of $ q_8 $ (cf. Algebra, Chap. IX, § 9. Exerc. 9) is zero, and that $ q_8 $ is of index 4.

d) Let $ \mathbf{O}(q_8) $ be the orthogonal group of $ \mathbf{Q}(R)/2\mathbf{Q}(R) $ for this form. Define, by passing to the quotient, a homomorphism

$$
h : W(R) \to \mathbf{O}(q_8).
$$

Show (by comparing orders) that the sequence

$$
1 \to \{1, -1\} \to W(R) \xrightarrow{h} \mathbf{O}(q_8) \to 1
$$

is exact.

e) Show that the image of $ W^+(R) $ under $ h $ is the subgroup $ \mathbf{O}^+(q_8) $ of $ \mathbf{O}(q_8) $ defined in Algebra, Chap. IX, § 9. no. 5. Deduce that $ W^+(R)/\{1, -1\} $ is a simple non-abelian group.
