With the notation in no. 7, show that the automorphism of $ \mathbf{R}^{l+1} $ which transforms $ \varepsilon_1 $ to $ \varepsilon_2 $, $ \varepsilon_2 $ to $ \varepsilon_3 $, ..., $ \varepsilon_l $ to $ \varepsilon_{l+1} $ and $ \varepsilon_{l+1} $ to $ \varepsilon_1 $ induces a Coxeter transformation of the system R of type $ A_l $.
