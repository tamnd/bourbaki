Let $x \in V$ and let $W(x)$ be the subgroup of $W(R)$ consisting of the elements $w$ such that
$$
w(x) - x \in Q(R).
$$
Show that $W(x)$ is generated by reflections. (Use the affine Weyl group of $R^-$.)
