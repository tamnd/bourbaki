Assume that $ R $ is irreducible. Let $ \{ \alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_l \} $ be a basis of $ R $, let $ \tilde{\alpha} = \sum_i n_i \alpha_i $ be the highest root of $ R $ and let $ f $ be the connection index of $ R $. Show that $ f - 1 $ is equal to the number of indices $ i $ such that $ n_i = 1 $.
