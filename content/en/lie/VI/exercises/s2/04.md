Let $ C' $ be a chamber relative to $ W(R) $ in $ V^* $, and let $ C $ be the alcove with vertex 0 contained in $ C' $. Let $ S_a $ (resp. $ S $) be the set of reflections in the walls of $ C $ (resp. $ C' $). The pairs $ (W_a, S_a) $ and $ (W, S) $ are Coxeter systems, and $ S \subseteq S_a $. Show that an element $ w \in W_a $ is $ (S, \emptyset) $-reduced (Chap. IV, § 3, Exerc. 3) if and only if $ w(C) \subseteq C' $.
