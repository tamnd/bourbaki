Let $ R $ be a root system in $ V $ of rank $ \geq 3 $, $ (\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_l) $ a basis of $ R $. $ V' $ (resp. $ V'' $) the subspace of $ V $ generated by the $ \alpha_i $ for $ i \geq 2 $ (resp. $ i \geq 3 $). $ R' = R \cap V' $, $ R'' = R \cap V'' $. Let $ d $ (resp. $ d', d'' $) be the determinant of the Cartan matrix of $ R $ (resp. $ R', R'' $). Assume that $ \alpha_1 $ is orthogonal to all the $ \alpha_i $ except $ \alpha_2 $, and that $ \| \alpha_1 \| = \| \alpha_2 \| $. Show that $ d = 2d' - d'' $.
