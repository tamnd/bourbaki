With the assumptions and notations of Prop. 33 of no. 11, denote by $\omega_1, \ldots, \omega_l$ the fundamental weights of $R$.

a) Show that $c^{-1}(\omega_i) = \omega_i - \theta_i$. Deduce that $1-c$ maps $P(R)$ to $Q(R)$.

b) Let $f$ be the connection index of $R$, and let $m_1, \ldots, m_l$ be the exponents of $W(R)$. Show that

$$
f = \det(1-c) = \prod_{j=1}^l (1 - w^{m_j}) = 2^l \prod_{j=1}^l \sin(m_j \pi / h)
$$

with $w = e^{2 \pi i / h}$.

c) Let $p$ be a prime number. Write $h$ in the form $h = p^n H$, with $H$ not divisible by $p$. Show that $p$ divides $f$ if and only if $H$ divides one of the $m_j$.²
