Let R be an irreducible root system. For any $ \beta \in R $, 16\gamma(R) = (\sum_{\alpha \in R} n(\alpha, \beta)^2)(\sum_{\alpha \in R} n(\beta, \alpha)^2)

and consequently $ \gamma(R) = \gamma(\lambda R) $ for any non-zero scalar $ \lambda $. (Use formulas (17) and (19) of no. 12.)
