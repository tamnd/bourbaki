Let $ R $ be a reduced root system. $ (\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_l) $ a basis of $ R $ and $ \alpha = c_1 \alpha_1 + \cdots + c_l \alpha_l $ a root. Then, $ c_i (\alpha_i | \alpha_i) / (\alpha | \alpha) \in \mathbf{Z} $ for all $ i $. (Consider the inverse system.)
