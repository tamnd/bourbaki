a) Let $ A $ be an abelian group of finite type, and $ T $ a finite subset of $ A $ not containing 0. There exists a subgroup $ H $ of $ A $ of finite index such that $ H \cap T = \varnothing $. (Let $ t \in T $. By using the structure of abelian groups of finite type, construct a subgroup of $ A $ of finite index that does not contain $ t $. Now proceed by induction on $ \mathrm{Card}(T) $.)

b) Let $ R $ be a root system and $ P $ a closed symmetric subset of $ R $. There exists a subgroup $ H $ of $ Q(R) $ of finite index such that $ P = H \cap R $. (Pass to the quotient by the subgroup of $ Q(R) $ generated by $ P $, and use a) and Prop. 23.)
