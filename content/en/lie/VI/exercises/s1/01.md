Let R be a root system, $ R = R_1 \cup R_2 $ a partition of R. Assume that, if $ x, y $ are two elements of $ R_i $ and if $ x + y $ (resp. $ x - y $) is a root, then $ x + y \in R_i $ (resp. $ x - y \in R_i $), where $ i = 1, 2 $. Then, R is the direct sum of $ R_1 $ and $ R_2 $. (By using the Cor. of Th. 1, no. 3, show that if $ x \in R_1 $ and $ y \in R_2 $ then $ (x|y) = 0. $)
