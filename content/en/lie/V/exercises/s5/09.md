Let $ V $ be a vector space of dimension 4 over a finite field $ K $, of characteristic different from 2, and let $ Q $ be a non-degenerate quadratic form on $ V $ of index 2 (\emph{Algebra}, Chap. IX, § 4, no. 2). Let $ G = O(Q) $ be the orthogonal group of this form; this is a finite group generated by reflections (\emph{loc. cit.}, § 6, no. 4, Prop. 5).

a) Let $ E $ be a maximal totally isotropic subspace of $ V $, and let $ G_E $ be the subgroup of $ G $ consisting of the elements $ g $ such that $ g(x) = x $ for all $ x \in E $. Show that $ G_E $ is isomorphic to the additive group of $ K $, and contains no pseudo-reflection.

b) Show that the algebra of symmetric invariants of $ G $ is not a graded polynomial algebra (use the preceding exercise).
