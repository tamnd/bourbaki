Assume that (W, S) is of hyperbolic type, and that W leaves stable a lattice $ \Gamma $ of E. Let G be the orthogonal group of $ B_M $, and let $ G(\Gamma) $ be the subgroup of elements $ g \in G $ such that $ g\Gamma = \Gamma $. Show that $ G(\Gamma) $ is a discrete subgroup of G. Show that W is a subgroup of finite index of $ G(\Gamma) $ (use the fact that the measure of $ W \setminus G $ is finite).
