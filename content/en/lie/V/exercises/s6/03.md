Let K be a commutative ring, E a free K-module with basis $ (e_1, \ldots, e_l) $, and $ f_1, \ldots, f_l $ elements of the dual of E. Put $ a_{ij} = f_i(e_j) $. If $ 1 \leq i \leq l $, let $ s_i $ be the pseudo-reflection $ s_{e_i, f_i} $ (\S 2, Exerc. 1). Then

$$
s_i(e_j) = e_j - a_{ij} e_i.
$$

Put $ c = s_1 \ldots s_l $, and $ z_i = c(e_i) $.

a) For $ 1 \leq i, k \leq l $, put

$$
y_i^k = s_1 \ldots s_k(e_i) \quad \text{and} \quad y_i = s_1 \ldots s_{i-1}(e_i) = y_i^{i-1}.
$$

Thus $ y_i^0 = e_i $ and $ y_i^l = z_i $.

Show that

$$
y_i^{k-1} - y_i^k = a_{ki} y_k.
$$

Deduce the formulas

$$
c_i = y_i + \sum_{k < i} a_{ki} y_k
$$
$$
z_i = y_i - \sum_{k \geq i} a_{ki} y_k.
$$

b) Let C be the matrix of $ c $ with respect to the basis $ (e_i) $. Let $ U = (u_{ij}) $ and $ V = (v_{ij}) $ be the matrices defined by u_{ij} = \begin{cases} a_{ij} & \text{if } i < j \\ 0 & \text{otherwise,} \end{cases} \quad v_{ij} = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } i < j \\ a_{ij} & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}

The matrix $ I + U $ is invertible with determinant 1. Show that

$$
C = (I - V)(I + U)^{-1}.
$$

Deduce that

$$
\det(\lambda I - C) = \det((\lambda - 1)I + V + \lambda U),
$$

in other words

$$
\det(\lambda I - C) =
\left| \begin{array}{cccccc}
(\lambda - 1) + a_{11} & \lambda a_{12} & \lambda a_{13} & \cdots & \lambda a_{1l} \\
a_{21} & \lambda(\lambda - 1) + a_{22} & \lambda a_{23} & \cdots & \lambda a_{2l} \\
a_{31} & a_{32} & (\lambda - 1) + a_{33} & \cdots & \lambda a_{3l} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
a_{l1} & a_{l2} & a_{l3} & \cdots & (\lambda - 1) + a_{ll}
\end{array} \right|.
$$

c) Let $ \Gamma = (I, S) $ be the graph whose set of vertices is $ I = [1, l] $ and whose set $ S $ of arrows is the set of subsets $ \{i, j\} $ of $ I $ with 2 elements such that $ a_{ij} \neq 0 $ or $ a_{ji} \neq 0 $. If $ \alpha = \{i, j\} $ belongs to $ S $, denote by $ \sigma_\alpha $ the transposition of $ i $ and $ j $ (considered as an element of the symmetric group $ S_I $), and put $ a_\alpha = -a_{ij}a_{ji} $.

Let $ \mathcal{B} $ be the set of subsets of $ S $ consisting of the arrows whose vertices are disjoint. If $ X \in \mathcal{B} $, denote by $ C(X) $ the set of $ i \in I $ that are not vertices of any arrow of $ X $, and put $ \sigma_X = \prod_{\alpha \in X} \sigma_\alpha, a_X = \prod_{\alpha \in X} a_\alpha $.

Let $ \sigma \in S_I $, and let $ d_\sigma $ be the term corresponding to $ \sigma $ in the expansion of the determinant of $ (\lambda - 1)I + V + \lambda U $. Show that, if $ \sigma $ is of the form $ \sigma_X $, with $ X \in \mathcal{B} $, then

$$
d_\sigma = a_X \lambda^{\operatorname{Card}(X)} \prod_{i \in C(X)} (\lambda - 1 + a_{ii}).
$$

Assume now that $ \Gamma $ is a forest (Chap. IV, Appendix, no. 3). Show that, if $ \sigma \in S_l $ is not of the form $ \sigma_X $ with $ X \in \mathcal{B} $, then $ d_\sigma = 0 $. Deduce the formula

$$
\det(\lambda - c) = \sum_{X \in \mathcal{B}} a_X \lambda^{\operatorname{Card}(X)} \prod_{i \in C(X)} (\lambda - 1 + a_{ii}).
$$

d) Consider the polynomial

$$
P(X) = \left| \begin{array}{cccc}
X & a_{12} & \ldots & a_{1l} \\
a_{21} & X & \ldots & a_{2l} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
a_{l1} & a_{l2} & \ldots & X
\end{array} \right|.
$$

To the assumptions in c), we add that $ a_{ii} = 1 $ for all $ i $. Show that in that case

$$
\det(\lambda^2 - c) = \lambda^l P(\lambda + \lambda^{-1}).
$$
