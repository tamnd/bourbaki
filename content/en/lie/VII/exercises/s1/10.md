Assume that $k$ is of characteristic $p >0$. Denote by $\mathfrak{g}$ a Lie algebra with basis $\{e_1, e_2, e_3\}$, with $[e_1, e_2] =e_3,[e_1, e_3] = [e_2, e_3] = 0$.

a) Show that the centre of $U\mathfrak{g}$ is $k[e^p_1, e^p_2, e_3]$.

b) Assume that $k$ is algebraically closed. Show that, for all $(\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \lambda_3)\in k^3$, there exists (up to equivalence) a unique irreducible representation $\rho$ of $\mathfrak{g}$
