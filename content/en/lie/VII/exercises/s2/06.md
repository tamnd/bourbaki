Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a Lie algebra, $\mathfrak{h}$ a nilpotent subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}$. If $\mathfrak{g}^0(\mathfrak{h})$ is nilpotent, $\mathfrak{g}^0(\mathfrak{h})$ is a Cartan subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}$.
