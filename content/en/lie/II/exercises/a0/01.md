Let $\Phi_n$ be the cyclotomic polynomial of index $n$ (Algebra, Chapter V, § 11, no. 2). Using the formula
$$
X^n - 1 = \prod_{d|n} \Phi_d(X),
$$
show that
$$
\Phi_n(X) = \prod_{d|n} (X^{n/d} - 1)^{\mu(d)}.
$$
(Apply the Möbius inversion formula to the multiplicative group of the field $\mathbf{Q}(X)$.)
