Let $ G $ be given the filtration $ (C^n G) $ and suppose that $ \mathrm{gr}_1(G) = G/(G, G) $ is cyclic. Show that $ \mathrm{gr}_n(G) = \{0\} $ for $ n \geqslant 2 $ (use Proposition 5) and deduce that $ C^n G = (G, G) $ for $ n \geqslant 2 $.
