We preserve the notation of Exercise 6.

(a) Let G be a nilpotent group of class $ <c $ and let $ (i, \overline{G}) $ be a P-envelope of G. Let $ \rho : F^c \to G $ be a surjective homomorphism (such a homomorphism exists if X is suitably chosen) and let $ \bar{\rho} $ be the corresponding homomorphism of $ F_P^c $ into $ \overline{G} $ (\S 4, Exercise 15). The homomorphism $ \bar{\rho} $ is surjective and maps $ C^n F_P^c = F_{P,n}^c $ onto $ C^n \overline{G} $. Show that $ C^n \overline{G} $ is the P-envelope of $ i(C^n G) $ in $ \overline{G} $ and that the Lie algebra $ \mathrm{gr}(\overline{G}) $ is identified with $ \mathbf{Z}_P \otimes \mathrm{gr}(G) $.

(b) Deduce that G is P-divisible and P-torsion-free if and only if the $ C^n G/C^{n+1} G $ are.
