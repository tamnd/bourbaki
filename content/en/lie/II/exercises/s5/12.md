Let $ g $ be a Lie algebra over a field $ k $ of characteristic $ p > 0 $.
(a) Let $ h $ be an integer $ \leq p - 1 $. $ g $ is said to satisfy *Engel's h-th condition* if $ (\mathrm{ad}\ x)^h(y) = 0 $ for every ordered pair $ (x, y) $ of elements of $ g $. Show that this is equivalent to
$$
\sum_{\sigma \in S_h} \mathrm{ad}(x_{\sigma(1)}) \circ \cdots \circ \mathrm{ad}(x_{\sigma(h)}) = 0
$$
for $ x_1, \ldots, x_h $ in $ g $ (apply the formula $ (\mathrm{ad}\ x)^h = 0 $ to the linear combinations of the $ x_i $).

(b) Let $ x, y $ be elements of $ g $ such that $ \Lambda_p(ax, by) = 0 $ for all $ a, b $ in $ k $. Show that, if $ \Lambda_p^{r,s} $ is the bihomogeneous component of $ \Lambda_p $ of bidegree $(r, s)$, then $ \Lambda_p^{r,s}(x, y) = 0 $ for every ordered pair $(r, s)$. Deduce (taking $ r = p - 1, s = 1 $) that $ (\mathrm{ad}\, x)^{p-1}(y) = 0 $. In particular, if $ \Lambda_p(x, y) = 0 $ for $ x, y $ in $ g $, the Lie algebra $ g $ satisfies Engel’s $(p-1)$-th condition.

(c) Let $ c $ be the centre of $ g $. Suppose that $ (\mathrm{ad}\, x)^p = 0 $ for all $ x \in g $. Show that $ g/c $ satisfies Engel’s $(p-1)$-th condition. (Show that $ \Lambda_p(\mathrm{ad}\, x, \mathrm{ad}\, y) = 0 $ for $ x, y $ in $ g $ and apply (b) to the Lie algebra $ \mathrm{ad}\, g = g/c $.)

(d) Let $ G $ be a group such that $ x^p = 1 $ for all $ x \in G $ and let $ (G_\alpha) $ be a real central filtration of $ G $. The filtration $ (G_\alpha) $ is *restricted* (Exercise 10) and $ \mathrm{gr}(G) $ is a Lie $ p $-algebra whose $ p $-mapping is zero. Deduce that $ \mathrm{gr}(G) $ satisfies Engel’s $(p-1)$-th condition.\footnote{For properties of Lie algebras satisfying an Engel condition and their applications to the “restricted Burnside problem”, see A. Kostrikin, *Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR*, **23** (1959), pp. 3–34.}
