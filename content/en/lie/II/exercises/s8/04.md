Let $ c $ be an integer $ \geq 1 $ and let $ P $ be a set of prime numbers containing all prime numbers $ \leq c $. Let $ \mathbf{Z}_P = S_P^{-1}\mathbf{Z} $ (\S 4, Exercise 16). Show that the terms of degree $ \leq c $ in the Hausdorff series $ H(U, V) $ belong to $ L_{\mathbf{Z}_P}(\{U, V\}) $. Deduce

† For more details on this exercise, see M. Lazard, *Bull. Soc. Math. France*, **91** (1963), pp. 435–451.

that, if $ g $ is a nilpotent Lie $ \mathbf{Z}_p $-algebra of class $ \leq c $, the law of composition $ (u, v) \mapsto H(u, v) $ makes $ g $ into a P-torsion-free P-divisible nilpotent group of class $ \leq c $. Show that conversely every group with these properties can be obtained by this process. (Use "the inversion of the Hausdorff formula", cf. § 6, Exercise 4, and show that the exponents $ \alpha(m), \beta(m) $ which appear there belong to $ \mathbf{Z}_p $ when $ l(m) \leq c $.)

In particular, every $ p $-group of order $ p^n $ and class $ <p $ can be obtained by means of the Hausdorff law from a nilpotent Lie $ \mathbf{Z} $-algebra of class $ <p $ with $ p^n $ elements. (Take P to be the set of prime numbers distinct from $ p $.)
