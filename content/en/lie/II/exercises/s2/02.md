Let $X$ be a countable set with at least two elements and let $\mathfrak{H}$ be the set of subsets of $M(X)$ which are Hall sets (cf. Definition 2). Show that $\operatorname{Card}(\mathfrak{H}) = 2^{\aleph_0}$.
