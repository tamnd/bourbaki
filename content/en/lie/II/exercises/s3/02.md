Let $ g $ be a Lie algebra which is a free module; it is identified by means of $ \sigma : g \to Ug $ with its image in the enveloping algebra $ Ug $. Let $ U^+g $ be the kernel of the canonical homomorphism $ Ug \to K $. Let $ i $ be a mapping of $ X $ into $ g $ such that $ i(X) $ generates $ g $ as a Lie algebra.
(a) Show that $ U^+g $ is generated by $ i(X) $ as a left $ Ug $-module.
(b) Show the equivalence of the following properties:
(i) $ g $ is free with basic family $ i : X \to g $.
(ii) The family $ i $ is a basis of the left $ Ug $-module $ U^+g $.
(The implication (i) $ \Rightarrow $ (ii) follows from Exercise 1 (a) using the isomorphism $ Ug \to A(X) $. To show (ii) $ \Rightarrow $ (i), apply Exercise 1 (b) to the kernel of the homomorphism $ A(X) \to Ug $ defined by $ i $.)
