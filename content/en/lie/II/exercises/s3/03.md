Let $ x \in X $. Show that the centralizer of $ x $ in $ A(X) $ is the subalgebra generated by $ x $. Deduce that the only elements of $ L(X) $ which commute with $ x $ are the multiples of $ x $. In particular, the centre of $ L(X) $ is $ \{0\} $ if $ \operatorname{Card}(X) \geq 2 $ and the centre of $ L(X)/\left( \sum_{n > p} L^n(X) \right) $ reduces to the canonical image of $ L^p(X) $.
