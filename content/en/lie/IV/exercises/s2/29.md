Let $ H $ be a simple non-abelian group. Let $ \theta $ be an automorphism of $ H $ of prime order $ p $ and let $ U $ be the semi-direct product of $ \mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z} $ by $ H $ corresponding to $ \theta $. Show that, if $ \theta $ is not an inner automorphism, the only normal subgroups of $ U $ are $ \{1\} $, $ H $ and $ U $. Deduce that $ U $ is not soluble, but that it satisfies condition (R) of no. 7. Apply this to show that the symmetric group $ \mathfrak{S}_n $ satisfies condition (R).
