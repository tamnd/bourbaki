Let $ k $ be a commutative field of characteristic $ \neq 2 $ and let Q be the quadratic form $ x_1x_3 + x_2^2 $ on $ k^3 $. Show that the group $ \mathbf{SO}(Q) $ acts doubly transitively on the set of isotropic lines in $ k^3 $. Compare the corresponding Tits system (Exerc. 7) with that obtained in no. 2 for $ n = 2 $ (cf. Algebra Chap. IX, § 9, Exerc. 15).
