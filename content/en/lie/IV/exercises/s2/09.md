Let $(G, B, N, S)$ be a Tits system and $X, Y, Z$ three subsets of $S$. Show that
$$
G_X \cap (G_Y . G_Z) = (G_X \cap G_Y) . (G_X \cap G_Z)
$$
(use Exerc. 1 of § 1 and Prop. 2 of no. 3).
