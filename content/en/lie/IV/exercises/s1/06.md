Let $ E $ be a set with 6 elements and $ F $ the set of structures of the projective line over the field $ \mathbf{F}_5 $ on $ E $. Denote by $ S_E $ the group of permutations of $ E $; for any $ \sigma \in S_E $ denote by $ \tilde{\sigma} $ the permutation of $ F $ induced by $ \sigma $ by transport of structure. Show that there exists a bijection $ u $ from $ E $ to $ F $, and that the map $ \sigma \mapsto u^{-1} \tilde{\sigma} u $ is an outer automorphism of $ S_E $ (if $ s $ is a transposition, $ u^{-1} \tilde{s} u $ has three orbits of two elements).
