We use the notations of Chap. VI, Plate II, in the case of an algebra of type $B_2$. Let $n, p$ be integers $\geq 0$.

$a)$ We have

1

$$
\mathfrak{P}(n\alpha_1+p(\alpha_1+ 2\alpha_2)) = 1 +p(p+ 3)
$$

2

$$
\mathfrak{P}(n\alpha_2+p(\alpha_1+\alpha_2)) = [p^2/4] +p+ 1
$$

$_2$ 1

$$
\mathfrak{P}(n(\alpha_1+\alpha_2) +p(\alpha_1+ 2\alpha_2)) = [n/4] +n+ 1 +np+p(p+ 3)
$$

2

$b)$ Let $\lambda =n(\alpha_1+\alpha_2) +p(\alpha_1+ 2\alpha_2)$. The multiplicity of the weight 0 in $E(\lambda )$ is

$$
[n/2] + 1 +np+p
$$
