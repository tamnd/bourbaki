Let $(\mathfrak{g},\mathfrak{h})$ be a split semi-simple Lie algebra, and $(X_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in R}$ a family of elements satisfying the conditions of Lemma 2. If $\alpha , \beta \in R$ and $\alpha +\beta \in R$, define $N_{\alpha \beta}$ by the formula $[X_{\alpha}, X_{\beta}] = N_{\alpha \beta}X_{\alpha+\beta}$; if $\alpha +\beta  /\in R$, put $N_{\alpha \beta}= 0$. Prove the following formulas:

$$
a) N_{\alpha \beta}=-N_{\beta \alpha}
$$

$b)$ If $\alpha , \beta , \gamma \in R$ are such that $\alpha +\beta +\gamma = 0$, then

$$
N_{\alpha \beta}N_{\beta \gamma}N_{\gamma \alpha}
$$

$$
\overline{\langle\gamma , \gamma\rangle}=\overline{\langle\alpha , \alpha\rangle}=\overline{\langle\beta , \beta\rangle}
$$

$c)$ If $\alpha , \beta , \gamma , \delta \in R$ are such that $\alpha +\beta +\gamma +\delta = 0$, and if no pair has sum zero, we have:

$$
N_{\alpha \beta}N_{\gamma \delta}N_{\beta \gamma}N_{\alpha \delta}N_{\gamma \alpha}N_{\beta \delta}
$$

+ + $= 0$.

$$
\overline{\langle\gamma + \delta , \gamma + \delta\rangle}\langle \alpha +\delta , \alpha +\delta \rangle \langle \beta +\delta , \beta +\delta \rangle
$$
