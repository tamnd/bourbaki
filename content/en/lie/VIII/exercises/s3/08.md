Choose a basis of R, and hence a set of positive roots $R_+$. Assume that R is irreducible. Let $\widetilde{\alpha}$ be the highest root, S the set of roots orthogonal to $\widetilde{\alpha}$, and $S_+= S\cap R_+$.

$a)$ Let

$$
\mathfrak{g}'=\mathfrak{g}^S\oplus \mathfrak{h}_S,\mathfrak{m}_+=\sum_{\alpha\in S_+}\mathfrak{g}^{\alpha},\mathfrak{m}_-=\sum_{\alpha\in S_+}\mathfrak{g}^{-\alpha}
$$

Then $\mathfrak{g}'$ is semi-simple and $\mathfrak{g}'=\mathfrak{h}_S\oplus \mathfrak{m}_+\oplus \mathfrak{m}_-$.

$b)$ Put:

$$
\mathfrak{n}_+=\sum\mathfrak{g}^{\alpha},\mathfrak{p}=\sum\mathfrak{g}^{\alpha},\mathfrak{p}_0=\sum\mathfrak{g}^{\alpha}
$$

$\alpha \in R_+\alpha \in R_+-S_+\alpha \in R_+-S_+-\{\widetilde{\alpha}\}$

Then

$$
\mathfrak{n}_+=\mathfrak{m}_+\oplus \mathfrak{p},[\mathfrak{m}_+,\mathfrak{p}_0]\subset \mathfrak{p}_0,[\mathfrak{n}_+,\mathfrak{g}^{\widetilde{\alpha}}] = 0
$$

In particular, $\mathfrak{p}$ is an ideal of $\mathfrak{n}_+$.

$c)$ For all $\alpha \in R_+$ **--** $S_+$ **--** $\{\widetilde{\alpha}\}$, there exists a unique $\alpha '\in R_+$ **--** $S_+$ **--** $\{\widetilde{\alpha}\}$ such that $\alpha +\alpha '= \widetilde{\alpha}$. For all $\alpha \in R_+$ **--** $S_+$ **--** $\{\widetilde{\alpha}\}$, a non-zero element $X_{\alpha}$ of $\mathfrak{g}^{\alpha}$ can be chosen so that

$[X_{\alpha}, X_{\alpha'}] =\pm X_{\widetilde{\alpha}}$ if $\alpha \in R_+-S_+- \{\widetilde{\alpha}\}$

$[X_{\alpha}, X_{\beta}] = 0$ if $\alpha , \beta \in R_+-S_+, \beta \not=\alpha '.^{11}$
