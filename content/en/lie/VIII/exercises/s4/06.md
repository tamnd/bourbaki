Let A be a commutative ring, and let $\mathfrak{u}$ be the A-Lie algebra defined by the family of generators $\{x, y\}$ and the relations

$$
[x,[x, y]] = 0,[y,[y,[y, x]]] = 0
$$

Show that $\mathfrak{u}$ is a free A-module with basis

$$
\{x, y,[x, y],[y,[x, y]]\}
$$

Show that, when $A =k,\mathfrak{u}$ is isomorphic to the algebra $\mathfrak{a}_+/\mathfrak{n}$ corresponding to a root system of type $B_2$.
