Retain the notations of no. 2. Let $\lambda \in k^B$. Associate to any $\alpha \in B$ the endomorphisms $X_{-\alpha}^{\lambda}, H_{\alpha}^{\lambda}, X_{\alpha}^{\lambda}$ of the vector space E such that

$$
X_{-\alpha}^{\lambda}(\alpha_1, . . . , \alpha_n) = (\alpha , \alpha_1, . . . , \alpha_n)
$$

$$
H_{\alpha}^{\lambda}(\alpha_1, . . . , \alpha_n) = (\lambda (\alpha )-\sum_{i=1}^nn(\alpha_i, \alpha ))(\alpha_1, . . . , \alpha_n)
$$

The vector $X_{\alpha}^{\lambda}(\alpha_1, . . . , \alpha_n)$ is defined by induction on $n$ by the formula

$$
X_{\alpha}^{\lambda}(\alpha_1, . . . , \alpha_n) = (X_{-\alpha_1}^{\alpha}X_{\alpha}^{\lambda}-\delta_{\alpha ,\alpha_1}H_{\alpha}^{\lambda})(\alpha_2, . . . , \alpha_n)
$$

where $\delta_{\alpha ,\alpha_1}$ is the Kronecker symbol; we agree that, if $(\alpha_1, . . . , \alpha_n)$ is the empty word, then $X_{\alpha}^{\lambda}(\alpha_1, . . . , \alpha_n)$ is zero.

Show that Lemmas 1 and 2 remain true for these endomorphisms. We thus obtain a representation $\rho_{\lambda}:\mathfrak{a}\rightarrow \mathfrak{g}\mathfrak{l}(E)$ such that

$$
\rho_{\lambda}(x_{\alpha}) =X_{\alpha}^{\lambda}, \rho_{\lambda}(h_{\alpha}) =H_{\alpha}^{\lambda}, \rho_{\lambda}(x_{-\alpha}) =X_{-\alpha}^{\lambda}
$$
