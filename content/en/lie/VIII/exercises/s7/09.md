Let $\mathfrak{a}_1$ and $\mathfrak{a}_2$ be Lie algebras. Show that there exists a unique homomorphism

$$
f: R(\mathfrak{a}_1)\otimes_{\mathbf{Z}}R(\mathfrak{a}_2)\rightarrow R(\mathfrak{a}_1\times \mathfrak{a}_2)
$$

such that $f([E_1]\otimes [E_2]) = [E_1\otimes E_2]$ if $E_i$ is an $\mathfrak{a}_i$-module $(i= 1,2)$. Show that $f$ is injective, and that it is bijective if $\mathfrak{a}_1$ and $\mathfrak{a}_2$ are splittable semi-simple.
