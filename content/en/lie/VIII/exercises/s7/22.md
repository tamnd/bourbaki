Assume that $\mathfrak{g}$ is simple, in other words that R is irreducible. Show that there exists a unique dominant weight $\lambda \not= 0$ such that the set of weights of $E(\lambda )$ is $W.\lambda \cup  \{0\}:$ we have $\lambda =\alpha$, where $\alpha \in R$ is such that $H_{\alpha}$ is the highest root of $R^{\vee}$.
