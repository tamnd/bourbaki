Let $k'$ be an extension of $k$, and $\mathfrak{g}'=\mathfrak{g}_{(k')}$. Show that every $\mathfrak{g}'$-module arises, by extension of scalars, from a $\mathfrak{g}$-module that is unique up to isomorphism.
