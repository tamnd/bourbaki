Let $\lambda \in P_{++}$ and let $\mathscr{X}_{\lambda}$ be the set of weights of $E(\lambda )$. Show that

$$
\mathscr{X}_{\lambda}\subset \lambda -P_{++}
$$

does not hold in general. (Consider, for example, the adjoint representation of $\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{l}(3, k).)$
