Let $\lambda \in P_{++}, F_{\lambda}$ be the largest proper submodule of $Z(\lambda )$ and $v$ a primitive element of $Z(\lambda )$ of weight $\lambda$, cf. §6, no. 3. Show that

$$
F_{\lambda}=\sum_{\alpha\in B}U(\mathfrak{g})X_{-\alpha}^{\lambda(H_{\alpha})+1}v=\sum_{\alpha\in B}U(\mathfrak{n}_-)X_{-\alpha}^{\lambda(H_{\alpha})+1}v
$$
