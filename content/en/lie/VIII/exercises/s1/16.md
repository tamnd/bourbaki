Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a simple Lie algebra of dimension 3, and let $\Phi$ be its Killing form. Denote by $\mathfrak{o}(\Phi )$ the orthogonal algebra of $\Phi$, i.e. the subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}\mathfrak{l}(\mathfrak{g})$ consisting of the elements leaving $\Phi$ invariant.

$a)$ Show that ad$:\mathfrak{g}\rightarrow \mathfrak{o}(\Phi )$ is an isomorphism.

$b)$ Prove the equivalence of the following properties:

(i) $\mathfrak{g}$ contains a non-zero isotropic vector (for $\Phi )$.

(ii) $\mathfrak{g}$ contains a non-zero nilpotent element.

(iii) $\mathfrak{g}$ is isomorphic to $\mathfrak{s}$.

$c)$ Show that there exists an extension $k_1$ of $k$, of degree $\leq 2$, such that $\mathfrak{g}_{(k_1)}=k_1\otimes_k\mathfrak{g}$ is isomorphic to $\mathfrak{s}_{(k_1)}$.

$f)$ Show that the discriminant of $\Phi$ (with respect to any basis of $\mathfrak{g})$ is of the form $-2\lambda^2$, with $\lambda \in k^*$.

$g)$ Let $n$ be an integer $\geq 0$. Show that the $\mathfrak{g}$-module $\mathbf{S}^n(\mathfrak{g})$ has a unique simple submodule of dimension $2n+ 1$, and that this module is absolutely simple (reduce to the case where $\mathfrak{g}=\mathfrak{s}$, and use Exerc. 9).
