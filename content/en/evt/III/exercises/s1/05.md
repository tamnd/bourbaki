Let E be a metrizable vector space over a non-discrete valued field K. Prove that if $ (B_n) $ is an arbitrary sequence of bounded subsets of E (III, p. 37, exerc. 1), then there exists a sequence $ (\lambda_n) $ of scalars $ \neq 0 $, such that the union of the sets $ \lambda_n B_n $ is bounded.
