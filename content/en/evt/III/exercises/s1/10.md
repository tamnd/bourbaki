Let $ (E_i)_{i \in I} $ be an infinite family of Hausdorff topological vector spaces, none just the point 0, over a non-discrete topological field K. Let E be the direct sum vector space of the $ E_i $, and $ \mathcal{T}_0 $ the topology on E defined in I, p. 24, exerc. 14. Then a subset B of E is bounded for $ \mathcal{T}_0 $ (III, p. 37, exerc. 1) if and only if B is contained in a product subspace $ \prod_{i \in H} E_i $, where H is a finite subset of I and the projections of B on each $ E_i $ for $ i \in H $ are bounded. Deduce (for $ K = \mathbf{R} $ or $ \mathbf{C} $) that, if each $ E_i $ is a quasi-complete space, then E with $ \mathcal{T}_0 $ is quasi-complete.
