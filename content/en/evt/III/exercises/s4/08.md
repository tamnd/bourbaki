Let $ (E_i)_{i \in I} $ be a family of infrabarrelled spaces, and for every $ i \in I $, let $ f_i $ be a linear mapping from $ E_i $ into a vector space E. Show that the space E, with the finest locally convex topology for which the $ f_i $ are continuous, is infrabarrelled. In particular, every quotient space of an infrabarrelled space is infrabarrelled; every topological direct sum of infrabarrelled spaces is infrabarrelled.
