Let E be a strict inductive limit space of an increasing sequence $ (E_n) $ of locally convex metrizable subspaces (II, p. 33), and let F be a vector subspace of E such that every point of E is a limit point of a sequence of points of F.
a) If $ \overline{E}_n $ is the closure of $ E_n $ in E, then E is the strict inductive limit of the sequence $ (\overline{E}_n) $. Let $ F_n $ be the closure of $ F \cap \overline{E}_n $ in E. Show that E is the union of the increasing sequence of sub-spaces $ F_n $.
b) Suppose E is barrelled. Show that F is bornological. (Let $ u $ be a linear mapping from F into a Banach space G which transforms every bounded subset of F into a bounded subset of G. Show that there exists a linear mapping from E into G, whose restriction to F is equal to $ u $, and whose restriction to each $ F_n $ is continuous. Finally use exerc. 13, b) of III, p. 44.)
