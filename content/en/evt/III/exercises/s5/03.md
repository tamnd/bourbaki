Let E be the space $ \mathbf{R}^{(\mathbf{N})} $ with the finest locally convex topology (II, p. 26); let F be the space $ \mathbf{R}^{\mathbf{N}} $; the space E is ultrabornological (III, p. 45, exerc. 19) and complete, whilst F is metrizable and complete. Let $ \mathcal{S} $ (resp. $ \mathfrak{T} $) be the set of all bounded subsets of E (resp. F). Show that the bilinear form $ ((x_n), (y_n)) \mapsto \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} x_n y_n $ on $ E \times F $ is $ (\mathcal{S}, \mathfrak{T}) $-hypocontinuous, but is not continuous ($ cf. $ IV, p. 48, exerc. 11).
