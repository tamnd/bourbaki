Let H be a $ \mathcal{S} $-equihypocontinuous set of bilinear mappings from $ E \times F $ into G (exerc. 7). For every subset $ M \in \mathcal{S} $, show that H is equicontinuous in $ M \times F $; moreover, for every bounded subset Q of F, the union of the sets $ u(M \times Q) $, where $ u $ runs through H, is bounded in G.
