Let H be a $ (\mathcal{S}, \mathfrak{T}) $-equihypocontinuous set of bilinear mappings from $ E \times F $ into G (exerc. 7); show that for every pair of sets $ M \in \mathcal{S} $, $ N \in \mathfrak{T} $, H is uniformly equicontinuous in $ M \times N $.
