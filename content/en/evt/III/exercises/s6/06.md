Show that an inductive limit (II, p. 31) of an increasing sequence of subspaces $E_n$ of a vector space E, with topologies $\mathcal{T}_n$, such that $E_n$ endowed with $\mathcal{T}_n$ is exhaustible, is an exhaustible locally convex space, if it is Hausdorff.
