**Let** $X$ **be a Hausdorff topological space,** and $F$ **a topological vector space (over** $\mathbf{R}$ **or** $\mathbf{C}$). **Show that on the space** $\mathcal{C}(X ; F)$ **of all continuous maps from** $X$ **into** $F$, **the topology of compact convergence is compatible with the vector space structure.**
