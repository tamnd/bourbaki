**Let** $E$ **and** $F$ **be two Hausdorff locally convex spaces,** and $\mathfrak{S}$ **a family of bounded subsets of** $E$.
$a)$ **Show that if** $F$ **is not just 0,** then a necessary (and sufficient) condition for $\mathcal{L}_{\mathfrak{S}} (E ; F)$ **to be Hausdorff is that the union of the sets of** $\mathfrak{S}$ **is total in** $E$ (**use Hahn-Banach th.**).
$b)$ **Suppose that** $\mathfrak{S}$ **is a cover for** $E$. **Show that there exists an isomorphism from** $F$ **onto a closed subspace of** $\mathcal{L}_{\mathfrak{S}} (E ; F)$. **Deduce that if** $\mathcal{L}_{\mathfrak{S}} (E ; F)$ **is quasi-complete,** then $F$ **is necessarily quasi-complete.**
$c)$ **Suppose that** $\mathfrak{S}$ **is a bornology adapted to** $E$ (**III, p. 3, def. 4**). **In order that** $\mathcal{L}_{\mathfrak{S}} (E ; F)$ **be metrizable,** it is necessary and sufficient that $F$ **is metrizable and that there exists a countable base (III, p. 1) for the bornology** $\mathfrak{S}$. **In order that the** $\mathfrak{S}$-**topology on** $\mathcal{L}(E ; F)$ **be defined by a single norm it is necessary and sufficient that the topology of** $F$ **can be defined by a single norm and that there exists a set** $M \in \mathfrak{S}$ **which absorbs every set of** $\mathfrak{S}$.
