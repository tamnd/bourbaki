Let E and F be two locally convex Hausdorff spaces, $\mathcal{S}$ a family of subsets of E. In order that the $\mathcal{S}$-topology on the space $\mathcal{L}(E; F)$ be compatible with the vector space structure, it is necessary (and sufficient, cf. III, p. 13, corollary) that every set of $\mathcal{S}$ is bounded in E.
