a) Let E be a locally convex Hausdorff space. For every ultrafilter $ \mathcal{U} $ on E, let $ \mathcal{U}' $ be the filter for which the convex envelopes of sets of $ \mathcal{U} $ form a base. Show that, in order that a point of E be a limit of $ \mathcal{U} $ for the weakened topology, it is necessary and sufficient that it is a limit point of $ \mathcal{U}' $ for the initial topology (use exerc. 11 of II, p. 84).
b) Let A be a convex subset of a vector space E and let $ \mathcal{T}_1', \mathcal{T}_2' $ be two locally convex Hausdorff topologies on E, and $ \mathcal{T}_1', \mathcal{T}_2' $ the corresponding weakened topologies. Show that if the topology induced on A by $ \mathcal{T}_1' $ is finer than the topology induced by $ \mathcal{T}_2' $, then the topology induced on A by $ \mathcal{T}_1' $ is finer than the topology induced on A by $ \mathcal{T}_2' $.
