Give an example of a family $ (E_i)_{i \in I} $ of Hausdorff locally convex spaces such that the canonical mapping from $ \bigoplus_{i \in I} E'_i $ onto the dual of $ P = \prod_{i \in I} E_i $ is not an isomorphism from the topological direct sum of the $ E'_i $ endowed with the weak topologies $ \sigma(E'_i, E_i) $ onto the dual $ P' $ endowed with $ \sigma(P', P) $.
