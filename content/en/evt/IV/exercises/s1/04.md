Let F and G be two vector spaces in separating duality, G (resp. F) being identified with the dual of F (resp. G) when the latter is assigned the topology σ(F, G) (resp. σ(G, F)). Let $\mathcal{S}$ (resp. $\mathcal{T}$) be a covering of F (resp. G) consisting of convex, balanced and bounded subsets for σ(F, G) (resp. σ(G, F)). Show that the following propositions are equivalent :
α) Every set M ∈ $\mathcal{S}$ is precompact for the $\mathcal{T}$-topology.
β) Every set N ∈ $\mathcal{T}$ is precompact for the $\mathcal{S}$-topology.
γ) On every set M ∈ $\mathcal{S}$, the topology induced by the $\mathcal{T}$-topology is identical with the topology induced by σ(F, G).
δ) On every set N ∈ $\mathcal{T}$, the topology induced by the $\mathcal{S}$-topology is identical with the topology induced by σ(G, F).
(Use prop. 5 of III, p. 17 to show that α) implies δ) and exerc. 1 of II, p. 74 to show that δ) implies β).)
