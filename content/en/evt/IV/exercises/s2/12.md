Let E be a Banach space and E' its dual.
a) Show that the distance $ x \mapsto d(x, A) $ from a point $ x \in E $ to a closed convex set A is a lower semi-continuous function on E for the topology $ \sigma(E, E') $.
b) Show that if E is reflexive, then for every closed convex subset A of E, there exists a point $ x_0 \in A $ such that $ \|x_0\| $ is equal to the distance of 0 to A (use a)). This point is unique if every boundary point of the unit ball of E is extremal (II, p. 54, def. 1).
c) Suppose E is reflexive and let B be a closed convex and bounded subset in E; deduce from a) and b) that there exist two points $ x \in A, y \in B $ such that $ \|x - y\| = d(A, B) $ (cf. V, p. 71, exerc. 8).
