Let $F$ and $G$ be two vector spaces in separating duality.
$a$) Show that the following properties are equivalent :
   $ \alpha $ the space $F$ with a topology compatible with the duality between $F$ and $G$, is semi-reflexive;
   $ \beta $ the space $G$, with $\tau(G, F)$, is barrelled.
$b$) Show that the following properties are equivalent :
   $ \alpha $ the space $F$, with $\tau(F, G)$, is reflexive;

β) the space G, with τ(G, F), is reflexive;
γ) F and G are barrelled for τ(F, G) and τ(G, F) respectively.
c) Show that every locally convex Hausdorff space E, with the topology τ(E, E') is isomorphic to the quotient of a semi-reflexive space F by a closed subspace. (By III, p. 44, exerc. 14, c), E' endowed with τ(E', E) is isomorphic to a closed vector subspace M of a Hausdorff barrelled space G; take F = G' endowed with τ(G', G) and use prop. 11 of IV, p. 10.)
d) Let A be an infinite set with Card(A) > $ \aleph_1 $ (S, III, § 6, exerc. 10). In the product space P = $ \mathbf{R}^A $, let E_0 denote the everywhere dense subspace consisting of all x = $ (x_\alpha)_{\alpha \in A} $ such that $ x_\alpha = 0 $ except for a countable set of indices; the space E_0 is barrelled and so is the subspace E of P generated by E_0 and the point 1_A in P all whose coordinates are equal to 1 (III, p. 45, exerc. 16). Let B be a bounded subset in E_0, whose closure in P contains 1_A, and let J be a subset of A with cardinality $ \aleph_1 $; let pr_J denote the projection from P onto $ \mathbf{R}^J $; show that there exists a subset B_J in B, with cardinality $ \leq \aleph_1 $, such that the closure of pr_J(B_J) in $ \mathbf{R}^J $ contains 1_J; then the set J' ⊃ J of all indices $ \alpha \in A $ such that for at least one point of B_J the coordinate with index $ \alpha $ is $ \neq 0 $, has a cardinality equal to $ \aleph_1 $. We define J_0 = J and by induction $ J_{n+1} = J'_n $, and put H = $ \bigcup_n J_n $, whose cardinality is $ \aleph_1 $, and let $ B_H = \bigcup B_{J_n} $; show that the closure of $ B_H $ (and hence also that of B) contains the point $ (1_H, 0) \in \prod^n \mathbf{R}^{H} \times \mathbf{R}^{A-H} = P $, which does not belong to E_0. Conclude that for every bounded and compact set C in E, C ∩ E_0 is again closed in E, and that E is not semi-reflexive.
e) Deduce from d) that the dual E' of E (which can be identified with the dual P' = $ \mathbf{R}^{(A)} $ of P) is not complete for the topology τ(E', E), inspite of being semi-reflexive (hence quasi-complete) for this topology (consider the linear form on E which is equal to 0 on E_0 and equal to 1 at the point 1_A, and use III, p. 21, th. 2).
