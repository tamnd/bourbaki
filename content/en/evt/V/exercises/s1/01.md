Let E be a complex normed space and $ f $ a symmetric bilinear form on the underlying real vector space $ E_0 $, such that $ f(x, x) = \|x\|^2 $ for all $ x \in E $. Show that there exists one and only one hermitian sesquilinear form $ g $ on E such that $ f(x, y) = \Re g(x, y) $ (prove that $ f(x, iy) = -f(ix, y) $ by using formula (5) of V, p. 2), hence $ g(x, x) = \|x\|^2 $.
