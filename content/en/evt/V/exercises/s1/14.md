Let E be an infinite dimensional Banach space.
a) Show that for every integer N, there exists a sequence $(b_j)_{1 \leq j \leq N}$ of N vectors in E, of norm 1, such that, for every sequence $(\xi_j)_{1 \leq j \leq N}$ of N scalars, we have
$$
\left\| \sum_{j=1}^N \xi_j b_j \right\|^2 \leq 4 \sum_{j=1}^N |\xi_j|^2
$$
(use exerc. 12, b)).
b) For every sequence $(\lambda_n)_{n \geq 1}$ of numbers $\geq 0$ such that $\sum \lambda_n^2 < +\infty$, show that there exists a sequence $(x_n)_{n \geq 1}$ of points of E such that $\|x_n\| = \lambda_n$ for all $n$, and that the series $(x_n)$ is summable. (Use a) of exerc. 13, a).
c) Deduce from b) that in every infinite dimensional Banach space, there exists a commutatively convergent series, that is not absolutely convergent (Dvoretzky-Rogers th.).
