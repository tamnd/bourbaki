Let $ B $ be an orthonormal basis in an infinite dimensional hilbertian space $ E $.
a) Show that every everywhere dense subset in $ E $ has a cardinality at least equal to that of $ B $, and that there exists an everywhere dense set in $ E $ which is equipotent to $ B $.
b) Show that $ \mathrm{Card}(E) = \mathrm{Card}(\mathbf{B}^{\mathbf{N}}) $ (to see that $ \mathrm{Card}(E) \leq \mathrm{Card}(\mathbf{B}^{\mathbf{N}}) $ use $ a$) ).
c) Show that if $ \mathrm{Card}(B) \leq \mathrm{Card}(\mathbf{R}) $, then the cardinality of every algebraic basis of $ E $ is equal to $ \mathrm{Card}(\mathbf{R}) = 2^{\aleph_0} $ (use II, p. 80, exerc. 24, $ c$) ); however, if $ \mathrm{Card}(B) > \mathrm{Card}(\mathbf{R}) $, then every algebraic basis of $ E $ is equipotent to $ \mathbf{B}^{\mathbf{N}} $ (use $ b$)  and A, II, § 7, exerc. 3, $ d) $).
