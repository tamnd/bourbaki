Let E be a hilbertian space. Show that the bilinear mapping $ (u, v) \mapsto uv $ from $ \hat{\mathbf{S}}^m(E) \times \hat{\mathbf{S}}^n(E) $ into $ \hat{\mathbf{S}}^{m+n}(E) $ is continuous and that its norm is equal to $ \left( \frac{(m+n)!}{m! \ n!} \right)^{1/2} $. (To see that this norm is bounded by $ \left( \frac{(m+n)!}{m! \ n!} \right)^{1/2} $, argue as in the case of the exterior algebra (V, p. 35). Deduce that the multiplication in $ \mathbf{S}(E) $ cannot be extended to $ \hat{\mathbf{S}}(E) $ by continuity when E is not simply 0.
