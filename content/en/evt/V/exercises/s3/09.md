Let E be a hilbertian space and $ (P_j)_{j \in \mathbf{N}} $ be a sequence of orthoprojectors an E, such that, for all $ j \in \mathbf{N} $, there exists an $ n_j \in \mathbf{N} $ such that for all $ k \in \mathbf{N} $, at least one of the orthoprojectors $ P_k, P_{k+1}, ..., P_{k+n_j} $ is equal to $ P_j $. Put $ R_s = P_sP_{s-1} ... P_0 $ for all $ s \in \mathbf{N} $.
a) For every $ x \in E $, let $ x_s = R_s.x $. Show that $ \sum_s \|x_{s-1} - x_s\|^2 \leq \|x\|^2 $, and deduce that for every integer $ r \geq 1 $, $ x_{s+r} - x_s $ tends to 0 as s tends to $ +\infty $.
b) Let $ (x_{s_k}) $ be a sequence extracted from $ (x_s) $ which tends to a limit $ y $ weakly; then every sequence $ (x_{s_k+r_k}) $ also tends to $ y $ weakly. Deduce that $ y $ belongs to each of the subspaces $ M_j = P_j(E) $. (For each $ j $, there exists $ r_k $ such that $ 0 \leq r_k \leq n_j $ and $ s_k + r_k = j $; show that the sequence $ (x_{s_k+r_k}) $ tends to $ y $ weakly.)
c) Show that the sequence $ (x_s) $ converges weakly to the orthogonal projection from x onto the intersection M of the $ M_j $. (Reduce to the case where $ M = \{0\} $, and use b) and the weak compactness of every closed ball in E.)
