Let E and F be two Banach spaces and let $ (u_n)_{n \geq 0} $ be a sequence of elements of $ \mathcal{L}(E; F) $. Suppose that the limit $ u(x) = \lim_{n \to \infty} u_n(x) $ exists for all $ x \in E $. Then $ \sup_n \|u_n\| < + \infty $, $ u $ is continuous and the sequence $ (u_n) $ tends to $ u $ uniformly on every compact subset of E.
