(« The Banach-Steinhaus Theorem »). Let E be a Banach space; F a normed space and let $ (u_i)_{i \in I} $ be a family of elements of $ \mathcal{L}(E; F) $. Let A be the subset of $ x \in E $ such that $ \sup_{i \in I} \|u_i(x)\| < + \infty $. Then either A is meagre and its complement is dense in E, or alternatively $ \sup_{i \in I} \|u_i\| < + \infty $. In particular, if $ A = E $, then $ \sup_{i \in I} \|u_i\| < + \infty $.
