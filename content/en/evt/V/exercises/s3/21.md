Let E be a Banach space, $ B' $ be the unit ball of $ E' $ and C be a convex subset of $ E' $ (in particular a vector subspace). In order that C be closed for $ \sigma(E', E) $, it is necessary and sufficient that the intersection $ C \cap rB' $ be closed for $ \sigma(E', E) $ for every real number $ r > 0 $.
