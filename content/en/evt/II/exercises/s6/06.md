Let F, G be two vector spaces in separating duality, and A a weakly compact convex set in F. Let C be a convex cone with vertex 0, that is weakly closed in G. Suppose that, for all $ y \in C $, there exists $ x \in A $ such that $ \langle x, y \rangle \geq 0 $. Show that there exists $ x_0 \in A $ such that $ \langle x_0, y \rangle \geq 0 $ for all $ y \in C $ (apply prop. 4 of II, p. 38, to A and $ C^\circ $).
