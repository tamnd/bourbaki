Let $ E $ be a Hausdorff locally convex space and $ F $ a locally convex space of minimal type (exerc. 13).
*a) Show that if $ M $ is a closed vector subspace of the product space $ E \times F $, its projection on $ E $ is closed in $ E $ (use exerc. 13, *e*)).
*b) Let $ u $ be a linear mapping of $ E $ in $ F $. Show that if the graph of $ u $ is closed in $ E \times F $, then $ u $ is continuous (use *a*).
*c) Suppose that, in $ E $, every closed vector subspace has a topological complement (*cf.* V, p. 13). Show that, in $ E \times F $, every closed vector subspace $ M $ has a topological complement. (If $ N_1 $ is the projection of $ M $ on $ E $ and $ N_2 $ a topological complement of $ N_1 $ in $ E $, if $ P_1 = M \cap F $, and $ P_2 $ is a topological complement of $ P_1 $ in $ F $, show that $ N_2 + P_2 $ is a topological complement of $ M $ in $ E \times F $, using *b*).)
