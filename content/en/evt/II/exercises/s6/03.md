In $ \mathbf{R}^n $ let $ (D_i)_{1 \leq i \leq m} $ be a family of $ m \geq 2n $ closed half-spaces determined by hyperplanes passing through 0. Show that if, for any $ 2n $ of these half spaces, there exists a point $ \neq 0 $ in their intersection, then there exists a point $ \neq 0 $ in the intersection of all the $ D_i (1 \leq i \leq m) $ (*cf.* II, p. 66, exerc. 10).
