Let E be an infinite dimensional vector space.
$ a) $ Show that there exist hyperplanes in $ E^* $ that are everywhere dense for the topology $ \sigma(E^*, E) $.
$ b) $ If $ H' $ is such a hyperplane, show that, in E, the only linear subvarieties $ \neq E $ that are everywhere dense for the topology $ \sigma(E, H') $ are the hyperplanes.
