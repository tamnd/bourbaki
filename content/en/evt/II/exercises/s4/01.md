Let $E$ be a vector space and $A$ a convex symmetric convex subset of $E$. Let $\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{T}'$ be two locally convex topologies on $E$ and $\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{U}'$ be the uniform structures defined by $\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{T}'$ on $E$. In order that the uniform structure induced on $A$ by $\mathcal{U}'$ should be finer than that induced by $\mathcal{U}$, it is necessary and sufficient that every neighbourhood of 0 for the topology induced on $A$ by $\mathcal{T}$ should be a neighbourhood of 0 for the topology induced on $A$ by $\mathcal{T}'$.
