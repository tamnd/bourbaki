Let I be an infinite non-enumerable set. On the vector space $ E = \mathbf{R}^{(I)} $, show that the finest locally convex topology is distinct from the topology $ \mathcal{T}_0 $ defined in I, p. 24, exerc. 14; for this prove that the set of the $ x = (\xi_i) \in E $ such that $ \left| \sum_{i \in I} \xi_i \right| < 1 $, is open in $ \mathcal{T} $ but not in $ \mathcal{T}_0 $.
