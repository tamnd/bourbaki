Let C, $ C' $ be two closed convex sets in a Hausdorff topological vector space E that are strictly separated by a closed hyperplane H. Let $ H' $ be a closed hyperplane of support to both C and $ C' $ such that C and $ C' $ lie on the same side of $ H' $. Show that $ H' $ is the only hyperplane with these properties which contains $ H \cap H' $ and that $ H \cap H' $ is a support hyperplane of the trace P on H of the convex envelope of $ C \cup C' $. Conversely, if C and $ C' $ are compact, then for every support hyperplane D of P in H, there exists a hyperplane $ H' $ that supports both C and $ C' $, which contains D and such that C and $ C' $ lie on the same side of $ H' $.
