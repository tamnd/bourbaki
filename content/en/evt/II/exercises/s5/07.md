In a Hausdorff locally convex space $ E $, let $ A $ be a complete, convex set and let $ B $ be a precompact closed convex set such that $ A \cap B = \varnothing $. Show that there exists a closed hyperplane separating A from B (argue in the completion $ \hat{E} $). Consider the case when A is finite dimensional.
