Let A be a compact convex set in $ \mathbf{R}^n $ and B its projection on the subspace $ \mathbf{R}^{n-1} $ (identified as the hyperplane with equation $ \xi_n = 0 $). Show that there exist two convex functions $ f_1, f_2 $ defined over B, such that A is identical with the set of points $ (x, \zeta) $ of $ \mathbf{R}^n $ where $ x \in B, y \in \mathbf{R} $ and $ f_1(x) \leq \zeta \leq -f_2(x) $.
