a) Give an example of a convex function that is defined over a compact convex subset K of $ \mathbf{R}^2 $, that is bounded and lower semi-continuous in K, but is not continuous at a point of the frontier of K (consider the gauge of a disc of which 0 is a frontier point).
b) Deduce from a) an example of a convex function defined in an open half-plane D of $ \mathbf{R}^2 $, not bounded above in D and not tending to a limit at a frontier point of D.
c) Deduce from a) an example of a convex lower semi-continuous function defined over a compact convex subset A of $ \mathbf{R}^2 $ but not bounded above in A. (Take for A the set of points $ (\xi, \eta) $ such that $ \xi^4 \leq \eta \leq 1 $ in $ \mathbf{R}^2 $.)
