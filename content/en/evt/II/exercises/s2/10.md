Let C be the convex envelope of a subset A of $ \mathbf{R}^n $, and let $ a $ be an interior point of C. Show that there exist $ 2n $ points $ x_i \in A $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq 2n $) such that $ a $ is interior to $ C_0 $, the convex envelope of the $ x_i $. (Suppose $ a = 0 $, and argue by induction on $ n $, noting that by exerc. 9a) there exists a set of $ k + 1 $ points $ y_j $ of A ($ 0 \leq j \leq k, 1 \leq k \leq n $), affinely independent and such that, if V is the affine linear variety generated by the $ y_j $, then $ 0 \in V $ and, relative to V, 0 is interior to the convex envelope of the set of the $ y_j $. Then project C on E/V and show that 0 is interior to this projection relative to E/V). Show that in the above statement $ 2n $ cannot be replaced by $ 2n - 1 $.
