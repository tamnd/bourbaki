Let U, V be two open convex sets in a Hausdorff topological vector space E such that $ \overline{V} \subset U $ and that U does not contain any half line. Let $ \mathcal{F} $ be a set of convex functions defined in $ \overline{U} $, uniformly bounded above on the frontier of U and uniformly bounded below on the frontier of V. Show that $ \mathcal{F} $ is equicontinuous.
