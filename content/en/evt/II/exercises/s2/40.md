In a topological vector space E, let U be a convex neighbourhood of $ x_0 $ and let $ f $ be a real-valued continuous convex function in U. Show that there exists a convex neighbourhood $ V \subset U $ of $ x_0 $ and a convex continuous function $ f_1 $ in E such that $ f_1|V = f|V $.
