a) Let A be an unbounded closed convex set in $ \mathbf{R}^n $, that does not contain any line, but does contain an interior point. Show that the frontier of A is homeomorphic to $ \mathbf{R}^{n-1} $ (use exercs. 15, b) and 18).
b) In a Hausdorff topological vector space E, let A be a closed convex set that does not contain any line and is of dimension $ \geq 2 $. Show that the frontier of A is connected (use a) and GT, VI, § 2, exerc. 12).
