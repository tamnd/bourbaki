Let $ (A_i)_{1 \leq i \leq p} $ be a finite family of convex subsets of a vector space E; let $ W_i $ be the subspace obtained by a translation from the affine linear variety generated by $ A_i $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq p $). If $ W = \sum_{i=1}^p W_i $, show that the affine linear variety generated by the convex set $ \sum_{i=1}^p \lambda_i A_i $ (where $ \lambda_i $ are non-zero numbers) is obtained from W by a translation.
