Let C be a convex cone with vertex 0 in a vector space E; if $ (x_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n} $ is a finite family of points of C such that $ \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i x_i = 0 $ for a family of numbers $ \lambda_i > 0 $, then C contains the vector subspace of E generated by the $ x_i $.
