Let X be a convex set of an affine space E and $ p $ an affine linear mapping of E in a second affine linear space $ E_1 $. Write $ X_1 = p(X) $. For every real-valued function $ f $ defined in X and every $ x_1 \in X_1 $, let
$$
f_1(x_1) = \inf_{p(x) = x_1} f(x) .
$$
Show that if $ f $ is convex and if $ f_1(x_1) > -\infty $ for all $ x_1 \in X_1 $, then $ f_1 $ is a convex function.
