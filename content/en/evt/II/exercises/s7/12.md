In the Banach space $ l^\infty(\mathbf{N}) $, let $ e_n $ be the sequence all of whose terms are zero except the n-th which is 1. Let A be the convex closed envelope of the set formed from 0 and the points $ e_n/(n + 1) $ ($ n \geq 0 $). Show that A is compact but that it is not identical with the convex envelope of the set of its extremal points.
