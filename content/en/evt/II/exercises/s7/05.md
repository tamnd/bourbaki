In the Banach space $ l^\infty(\mathbf{N}) $ of bounded sequences $ x = (\xi_n) $ of real numbers, let A be the closed convex set defined by the inequalities $ -1/n \leq \xi_n \leq 1 $ for $ n \geq 1 $ and $ -1 \leq \xi_0 \leq 1 $. Show that A has a non-empty interior, that the origin is a frontier point of A and that the facet of 0 in A is not closed. If we give to A the topology induced by that of the product space $ \mathbf{R}^N $, show that A is compact but that the facet of 0 in A is not closed in A.
