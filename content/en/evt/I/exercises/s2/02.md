In the product topological vector space E = $ \mathbf{R}^N $ over the field $ \mathbf{R} $, denote by $ e_n $ ($ n \in \mathbf{N} $) the elements of the canonical basis of the direct sum $ \mathbf{R}^{(N)} $. Write $ a_0 = e_0 $, $ a_n = e_0 + (1/n)e_n $ for $ n \geq 1 $. Show that, for every integer $ n \geq 0 $, the $ a_i $ such that $ 0 \leq i \leq n $ form a topologically independent family in E, but that the infinite family $ (a_n)_{n \geq 0} $ is not topologically independent. If M is the closed vector subspace $ Ra_0 $, the classes $ \dot{a}_n $ of the $ a_n $ in E/M form a topologically independent family (for $ n \geq 1 $), but the closed vector subspace N generated by the $ a_n $, with index $ n \geq 1 $, in E contains M.
