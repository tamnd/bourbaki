Let S be an arbitrary infinite set.
a) Show that the smallest cardinal of any total set in the normed space $ \mathscr{B}(S) $ of bounded mappings of S in $ \mathbf{R} $ (I, p. 4, Example) is equal to $ 2^{\mathrm{Card}(S)} $ (consider the set of characteristic functions of subsets of S and note that there exists an enumerable set everywhere dense in $ \mathbf{R} $).
b) Show that the smallest cardinal of any total set in the normed space $ \ell^1(S) $ (I, p. 4, Example) is equal to Card(S).
