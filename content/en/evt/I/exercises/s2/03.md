Let E be a topological vector space over $ \mathbf{R} $, and $ f $ a homomorphism of the additive group of E in $ \mathbf{R} $. Show that if there exists a neighbourhood of 0 in E in which $ f $ is bounded, then $ f $ is a continuous linear form in E. This is so in particular when $ f $ is semi-continuous (lower or upper).
