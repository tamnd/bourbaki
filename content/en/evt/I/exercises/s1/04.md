Let $ E $ be a vector space over a division ring $ K $ with a valuation; we suppose that $ E $ carries a *metrisable* topology compatible with its additive group structure. Suppose further that this topology satisfies axioms (EVT'_I) and (EVT'_II); show that if one of the two metrisable groups $ K, E $ is *complete* then the topology also satisfies (EVT'_III) and is, in consequence, compatible with the vector space structure of $ E $ (*cf.* GT, IX, § 5, exerc. 23).
