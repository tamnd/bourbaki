Let $A$ and $B$ be relations in $\mathscr{T}$, and let $x$ and $y$ be letters. If $x$ does not appear in $B$, nor $y$ in $A$, then
$$(\forall x)(\forall y)(A \text{ and } B) \Leftrightarrow ((\forall x)A \text{ and } (\forall y)B)$$
is a theorem in $\mathscr{T}$.
