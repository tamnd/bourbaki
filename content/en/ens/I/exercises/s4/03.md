Let $A$ be a relation in $\mathscr{T}$ and let $x$ and $y$ be letters. The relations
$$(\forall x)(\forall y)A \Rightarrow (\forall x)((x|y)A), \qquad (\exists x)((x|y)A) \Rightarrow (\exists x)(\exists y)A$$
are then theorems in $\mathscr{T}$.
