Let $A$ and $B$ be relations in $\mathscr{T}$, and let $x$ be a letter which does not appear in $A$. Then $(\forall x)(A \Rightarrow B) \Leftrightarrow (A \Rightarrow (\forall x)B)$ is a theorem in $\mathscr{T}$.
