Let $A$ be a term or a relation in a theory $\mathscr{T}$. Show that every sign $\square$ in $A$ (if there is any) is linked to a single sign $\tau$ situated to its left. Show that every sign $\tau$ in $A$ (if there is any) is either not linked at all or else is linked to certain signs $\square$ situated to its right. Show also that no other sign is linked.
