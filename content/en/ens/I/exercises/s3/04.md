Let $A$ and $B$ be relations in a logical theory $\mathscr{T}$. Let $A|B$ denote the relation "(not $A$) or (not $B$)". Prove the following theorems in $\mathscr{T}$ :
$$(\text{not } A) \Leftrightarrow (A|A),$$
$$(A \text{ or } B) \Leftrightarrow ((A|A)|(B|B)),$$
$$(A \text{ and } B) \Leftrightarrow ((A|B)|(A|B)),$$
$$(A \Rightarrow B) \Leftrightarrow (A|(B|B)).$$
