Let $(\mathrm{E}_\alpha, f_{\alpha\beta})$ be an inverse system of sets relative to a directed index set, let $\mathrm{E} = \varprojlim \mathrm{E}_\alpha$, and let $f_\alpha : \mathrm{E} \to \mathrm{E}_\alpha$ be the canonical mapping for each $\alpha$. Show that, if all the $f_{\alpha\beta}$ are injective, then $f_\alpha$ is injective.
