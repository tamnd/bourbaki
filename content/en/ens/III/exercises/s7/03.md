Let $(\mathrm{E}_\alpha, f_{\alpha\beta})$ and $(\mathrm{F}_\alpha, g_{\alpha\beta})$ be two inverse systems of sets relative to the same index set I. For each $\alpha \in \mathrm{I}$, let $u_\alpha$ be a mapping of $\mathrm{E}_\alpha$ into $\mathrm{F}_\alpha$, such that the $u_\alpha$ form an inverse system of mappings. Let $\mathrm{G}_\alpha \subset \mathrm{E}_\alpha \times \mathrm{F}_\alpha$ be the graph of $u_\alpha$. Show that $(\mathrm{G}_\alpha)$ is an inverse system of subsets of $\mathrm{E}_\alpha \times \mathrm{F}_\alpha$ and that its inverse limit may be canonically identified with the graph of $u = \varprojlim u_\alpha$.
