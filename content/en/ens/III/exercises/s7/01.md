Let I be a directed set, let $(\mathrm{J}_\lambda)_{\lambda \in \mathrm{L}}$ be a family of subsets of I, indexed by a directed set L, such that (i) for each $\lambda \in \mathrm{L}$, $\mathrm{J}_\lambda$ is directed with respect to the induced ordering; (ii) the relation $\lambda \leqslant \mu$ implies $\mathrm{J}_\lambda \subset \mathrm{J}_\mu$; (iii) I is the union of the family $(\mathrm{J}_\lambda)$. Let $(\mathrm{E}_\alpha, f_{\alpha\beta})$ be an inverse system of sets relative to I, let E be its inverse limit, and for each $\lambda \in \mathrm{L}$ let $\mathrm{F}_\lambda$ be the inverse limit of the system obtained from $(\mathrm{E}_\alpha, f_{\alpha\beta})$ by restricting the index set to $\mathrm{J}_\lambda$. For $\lambda \leqslant \mu$ let $g_{\lambda\mu}$ be the canonical mapping of $\mathrm{F}_\mu$ into $\mathrm{F}_\lambda$ (no. 1). Show that $(\mathrm{F}_\lambda, g_{\lambda\mu})$ is an inverse system of sets relative to L, and define a canonical bijection of $\mathrm{F} = \varprojlim \mathrm{F}_\lambda$ onto E.
