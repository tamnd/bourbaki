Let $p_n$ be the number of permutations $u$ of a set E with $n$ elements such that $u(x) \neq x$ for all $x \in \mathrm{E}$.  Show that

$$p_n = n! - \binom{n}{1}(n-1)! + \binom{n}{2}(n-2)! - \cdots + (-1)^n$$

\* and   hence   that   $p_n \sim n!/e$   as   $n \to +\infty$ \*   (same   method   as   in Exercise 7 (a)).
