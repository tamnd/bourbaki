Prove Proposition 15 of no. 8 by defining a bijection of the set of mappings $u$ of $[1, h]$ into $[0, n]$ such that

$$\sum_{i=1}^{h} u(x) \leqslant n$$

onto the set of strictly increasing mappings of $[1, h]$ into $[1, n+h]$.
