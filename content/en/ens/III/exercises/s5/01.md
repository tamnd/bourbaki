Prove the formula

$$\sum_{k=q+1}^{n-p+q+1} \binom{n-k}{p-q-1}\binom{k-1}{q} = \binom{n}{p},$$

where $p \leqslant n$ and $q < p$ (generalize the argument of no. 8, Corollary to Proposition 14).
