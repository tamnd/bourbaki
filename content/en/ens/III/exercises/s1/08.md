Let E and F be two ordered sets, let $f$ be an increasing mapping of E into F, and $g$ an increasing mapping of F into E. Let A (resp. B) be the set of all $x \in \mathrm{E}$ (resp. $y \in \mathrm{F}$) such that $g(f(x)) = x$ (resp. $f(g(y)) = y$). Show that the two ordered sets A and B are canonically isomorphic.
