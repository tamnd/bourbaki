Let $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ be sets, $f$ a mapping of $A$ into $B$, $g$ a mapping of $B$ into $C$, $h$ a mapping of $C$ into $D$. If $g\circ f$ and $h\circ g$ are bijections, show that all of $f$, $g$, $h$ are bijections.
