Let G be a graph. Show that the following three propositions are equivalent :

(a) G is a functional graph.

(b) If X, Y are any two sets, then

$$\overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}}(\mathrm{X} \cap \mathrm{Y}) = \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}}(\mathrm{X}) \cap \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}}(\mathrm{Y}).$$

(c) The relation $\mathrm{X} \cap \mathrm{Y} = \emptyset$ implies

$$\overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}}(\mathrm{X}) \cap \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}}(\mathrm{Y}) = \emptyset.$$
