Let G be a graph. Show that for each set X we have

$$\mathrm{G}(\mathrm{X}) = \mathrm{pr}_2(\mathrm{G} \cap (\mathrm{X} \times \mathrm{pr}_2\mathrm{G})) \qquad \text{and} \qquad \mathrm{G}(\mathrm{X}) = \mathrm{G}(\mathrm{X} \cap \mathrm{pr}_1\mathrm{G}).$$
