Let $(\mathrm{X}_\iota)_{\iota \in \mathbf{I}}$ be a family of sets. Show that if $(\mathrm{Y}_\iota)_{\iota \in \mathbf{I}}$ is a family of sets such that $\mathrm{Y}_\iota \subset \mathrm{X}_\iota$ for each $\iota \in \mathrm{I}$, then

$$\prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{Y}_\iota = \bigcap_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \overset{-1}{\mathrm{pr}_\iota}(\mathrm{Y}_\iota).$$
