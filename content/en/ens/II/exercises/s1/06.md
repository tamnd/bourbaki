Let $\mathscr{T}$ be an equalitarian theory which contains the sign $\in$ and the following axiom :

A1'. $$(\forall y)(y = \tau_x((\forall z)(z \in x \Leftrightarrow z \in y)))$$

(in other words, every term is equal to the set of its elements). Show that the axiom of extent A1 is a theorem in $\mathscr{T}$ (use the scheme S7).
