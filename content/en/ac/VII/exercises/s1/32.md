Let $ A $ be an integral domain $ (a_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n} $ a finite family of elements of $ A $, $ a $ the ideal $ \sum_i A a_i $ and $ R $ the submodule of $ A'' $ generated by the element $ (a_1, \ldots, a_n) $. Show that, for the torsion submodule of the $ A $-module $ M = A^n/R $ to be a direct factor of $ M $, it is necessary and sufficient that

$$
a(A : a) + (A : (A : a)) = A.
$$
