(a) In the factorial polynomial domain $ K[X, Y] $ in two indeterminates over a field $ K $, give an example of two principal ideals $ a, b $ such that $ a + b $ is not divisorial.

(b) Let $ K $ be the quadratic extension of the field $ \mathbf{Q}(X) $ of rational functions in one indeterminate over $ \mathbf{Q} $, obtained by adjoining to $ \mathbf{Q}(X) $ a root $ y $ of the polynomial $ Y^2 - 2X \in \mathbf{Q}(X)[Y] $. Let $ A $ be the subring of $ K $ generated by $ Z, X $ and $ y $; $ A $ is an integrally closed Noetherian domain. Show that $ p = AX + Ay $ is a prime ideal of height 1 but that $ p^2 $ is not divisorial (show that $ X^{-1}p^2 $ is a prime ideal strictly containing $ p $). Moreover $ p . (A : p) \neq A $.

(c) Let $ A $ be an integrally closed Noetherian local integral domain, whose maximal ideal $ m $ is not of height $ \leq 1 $; let $ p $ be a prime ideal of $ A $ of height 1; for every integer $ i > 0 $ let $ a_i = p + m^i $; show that $ \operatorname{div}(\bigcap_i a_i) $ is distinct from $ \sup(\operatorname{div}(a_i)) $.
