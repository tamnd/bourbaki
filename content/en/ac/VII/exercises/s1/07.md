Let $ K $ be a field and $ (v_i)_{i \in I} $ a family of discrete valuations on $ K $ satisfying conditions (AK,) and (AK,,) of no. 3 and such that, for every integer $ r $, every family $ (n_h)_{1 \leq h \leq r} $ of rational integers and every family $ (t_h)_{1 \leq h \leq r} $ of distinct elements of $ I $, there exists $ x \in K $ such that $ v_{t_h}(x) = n_h $ for $ 1 \leq h \leq r $ and $ v_i(x) \geq 0 $ for $ i $ distinct from the $ t_h $. Show that the intersection of the valuation rings of the $ v_i $ is a Krull domain $ A $ whose field of fractions is $ K $ and that $ (v_i)_{i \in I} $ is the family of essential valuations of $ A $. (Show first that $ K $ is the field of fractions of $ A $ and hence that $ A $ is a Krull domain; then prove that for all $ i \in I $ the prime ideal of $ A $ consisting of those $ x $ such that $ v_i(x) > 0 $ is necessarily of height 1, arguing by *reductio ad absurdum* and using Corollary 2 to Proposition 6 of no. 4.)
