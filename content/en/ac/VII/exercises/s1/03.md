Let $ A $ be a Noetherian integral domain and $ p $ a prime ideal of height 1.

Show that $ \mathbf{A}: p \neq \mathbf{A} $ (in the field of fractionsof $ \mathbf{A} $). (Ifc $ \neq 0 $ is an element of $ p $, note that $ p \in \mathrm{Ass}(A/Ac) $ and deduce that $ Ac: p \# Ac $ by virtue of Chapter IV, § 2, Exercise 30). Is the result still valid for a non-Noetherian integral domain (consider a non-discrete valuation ring of height 1)?
