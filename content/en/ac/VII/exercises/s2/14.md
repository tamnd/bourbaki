Let $ A $ be a complete Noetherian local ring whose residue field is of characteristic $ p > 0 $. In the ring of formal power series $ A[[T]] $ consider the elements $ \omega_n = (1 - T)^{p^n} $ and $ \gamma_n = 1 - \omega_n $ for every integer $ n > 0 $. Show that $ \gamma_n $ is, to within a sign, a distinguished polynomial (no. 8); deduce that $ A_* = A[[T]]/(\gamma_n) $ is identified with the algebra over $ A $ of the group

$ G_n = \mathbf{Z}/p^n\mathbf{Z} $. Show that the intersection of the principal ideals $ (\gamma_n) $ is reduced to 0; deduce that $ A[[T]] $ is identified with the inverse limit $ \varprojlim A_*. $
