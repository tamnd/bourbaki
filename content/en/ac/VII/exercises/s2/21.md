Show that in $ \mathbf{Z}[X] $ the following polynomials are irreducible:
$ X^n - a $, where one of the prime factors of $ a $ has exponent 1 ;
$ X^{2^k} + 1 $, (replace $ X $ by $ X + 1 $);
$ x^4 + 3x^3 + 3x^2 - 5 $;
$ 5X^4 - 6X^3 - aX^2 - 4X + 2 $.
(Use Exercise 20.)
