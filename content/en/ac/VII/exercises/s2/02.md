(a) Let $ a, b $ be two integral ideals of a Dedekind domain $ A $; show that there exists an integral ideal $ c $ which is prime to $ b $ and such that $ ac $ is principal (use Proposition 9 of § 1, no. 5).

(b) Let $ a, b $ be two integral ideals of $ A $; show that there exists $ x \neq 0 $ in the field of fractions of $ A $ such that $ xa $ is an integral ideal of $ A $ which is prime to $ b $ (same method). Deduce that the module $ a/ab $ is isomorphic to $ A/b $.
