Let $ A $ be a Dedekind domain, $ K $ its field of fractions, $ P $ the set of prime ideals $ \neq 0 $ of $ A $ and for all $ \varrho \in P $ let $ v_\varrho $ be the corresponding essential valuation on $ K $. For every sub-A-module $ M $ of $ K $ and all $ \varrho \in P $ we write $ v_\varrho(M) = \inf v_\varrho(x) $ (taken in $ \overline{\mathbf{R}} $); if $ M \neq 0 $, $ v_\varrho(M) < +\infty $ for all $ \varrho \in P $ and $ v_\varrho(M) \leq 0 $ for almost all $ \varrho \in P $. If $ M, N $ are two sub-A-modules of $ K $, show that the relation $ M \subset N $ is equivalent to $ v_\varrho(N) \leq v_\varrho(M) $ for all $ \varrho \in P $ (use Proposition 9 of § 1, no. 5). Conversely, for every family $ (v_\varrho)_{\varrho \in P} $ of elements equal to an integer or $ -\infty $ and such that $ v_\varrho \leq 0 $ for almost all $ \varrho \in P $, there exists a unique sub-A-module $ M $ of $ K $ such that $ v_\varrho(M) = v_\varrho $ for all $ \varrho \in P $.
