If $U$ is a square matrix of order $n$, A its determinant and $\Delta_p$ the determinant of the $p$-th exterior power of $U$ (Algebra, Chapter 111, § 6, no. 3), show that:
$$
\Delta_p = \Delta^{(n-1)}_{p-1}
$$
(use Exercise 11 of Algebra, Chapter 111, § 6, and Exercise 17 (a) above).
