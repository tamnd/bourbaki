Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain and $K$ its field of fractions. If $L$ is a sub-field of $K$ such that $A$ is integral over $A \cap L$, then $A \cap L$ is a Dedekind domain (show first that $A \cap L$ is a Krull domain and then that every prime ideal of $A \cap L$ is maximal, using Chapter V, § 2, no. 1, Theorem 1).
