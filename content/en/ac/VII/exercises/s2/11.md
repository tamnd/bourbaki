Let $ K $ be a field and $ (v_i)_{i \in I} $ a family of discrete valuations on $ K $ satisfying conditions (AK_I) and (AK_{III}) of § 1, no. 3 and such that, for every integer $ r $, every family $ (n_h)_{1 \leq h \leq r} $ of integers $ \geq 0 $, every family $ (t_h)_{1 \leq h \leq r} $ of distinct elements of $ I $ and every family $ (a_h)_{1 \leq h \leq r} $ of elements of $ K $, there exists $ x \in K $ such that $ v_{t_h}(x - a_h) \geq n_h $ for $ 1 \leq h \leq r $ and $ v_i(x) = 0 $ for $ i $ distinct from the $ t_h $. Show that the intersection $ A $ of the valuation rings of the $ v_i $, is a Dedekind domain whose field of fractions is $ K $ and that $ (v_i)_{i \in I} $ is the family of essential valuations of $ A $. (Use Exercise 7 of § 1; then prove that two distinct prime ideals of height 1 are relatively prime and deduce that every prime ideal $ \neq 0 $ of $ A $ is maximal.)
