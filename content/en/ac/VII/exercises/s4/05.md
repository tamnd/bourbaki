Let $A$ be an integrally closed Noetherian domain and $M$ a finitely generated $A$-module. Show that, if $a, b$ are two ideals of $A$, the $A$-module
$$
((aM) \cap (bM))/(a \cap b)M
$$
is pseudo-zero; give an example where it is not zero.
