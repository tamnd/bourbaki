Let $ k $ be a field and $ A $ the polynomial ring $ k[X, Y] $. On the $ A $-module $ M = A^2 $ consider the linear form $ f $ such that $ f(e_1) = X, f(e_2) = Y((e_1, e_2)) $ being the canonical basis). Show that the kernel $ L $ off is a monogenous free $ A $-module, but that the quotient $ M/L $ (which is isomorphic to an ideal of $ A $) is not reflexive.
