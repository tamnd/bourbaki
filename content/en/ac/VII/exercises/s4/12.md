Let $ A $ be an integrally closed Noetherian domain and $ M, N $ two torsion-free finitely generated $ A $-modules of the same rank $ r $ such that $ N \subset M $; let $ j : N \to M $ be the canonical injection.
(a) Show that, for all $ k $, $ \bigwedge^k j : \bigwedge^k N \to \bigwedge^k M $ is pseudo-injective, that $ \operatorname{Coker}\left( \bigwedge^k j \right) $ is a torsion $ A $-module and that:
$$
\chi\left( \operatorname{Coker}\left( \bigwedge^k j \right) \right) = \binom{r-1}{k-1} \chi(\operatorname{Coker} j).
$$
(b) Using (a), show that, if $ M $ is a torsion-free finitely generated $ A $-module, the torsion submodule of $ \bigwedge^k M $ is pseudo-zero for all $ k $ and
$$
c\left( \bigwedge^k M \right) = \binom{r-1}{k-1} c(M),
$$
where $ M $ is of rank $ r $; in particular, $ c\left( \bigwedge^r M \right) = c(M) $.
