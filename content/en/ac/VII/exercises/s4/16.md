(a) Let $A$ be a Prüferian domain and $M$ a finitely generated lattice in a vector space $V$ of finite rank $n$ over the field of fractions $K$ of $A$. Show that there exists a basis $(e_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n}$ of $V$ and $n$ fractional ideals $a, (1 \leq i \leq n)$ such that M is equal to the direct sum of the $a_i e_i$. (Argue by induction on $n$: if $(u_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n}$ is a basis of V, consider the finitely generated fractional ideal $b_1$ generated by the $u_1$-coordinates of the elements of M; by considering the A-module $b_1^{-1}M$, reduce it the case where $b_1 = A$.)

Deduce that, if W is a subspace of V, $M \cap W$ is a direct factor of M.

(b) Suppose that M is a submodule of A" equal to the direct sum of the $a_i e_i$, where the $a_i$ are finitely generated (integral) ideals of A and $(e_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n}$ the canonical basis of $A^n$. Show that there exists a basis $(u_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n}$ of A" and ideals $b_i$ of A such that M is the direct sum of the $b_i u_i$ and $b_i$ divides $b_{i+1}$ for $1 \leq i \leq n - 1$. (Reduce it to the case where $n = 2$ and $a_1 + a_2 = A$.)
