Let $ A $ be an integrally closed Noetherian domain and $ E, F $ two finitely generated $ A $-modules. Suppose that $ E $ is torsion-free, that $ \mathrm{Hom}_A(E, F) $ is reflexive and that $ \mathrm{Ext}_A^1(E, F) = 0 $. Show then that $ F $ is reflexive. (Prove first that $ F $ is torsion-free; if $ T = F^{**}/c_F(F) $, calculate $ \mathrm{Ass}(\mathrm{Hom}_A(E, T)) $ in two ways using the exact sequence:
$$
0 \to \mathrm{Hom}(E, F) \to \mathrm{Hom}(E, F^{**}) \to \mathrm{Hom}(E, T) \to 0
$$
and Chapter IV, § 1, no. 4, Proposition 10.)
