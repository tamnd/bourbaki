Consider a commutative diagram of commutative groups

$$
\begin{array}{cccccc}
A & \longrightarrow & B & \longrightarrow & C & \longrightarrow & D \\
a \downarrow & & b \downarrow & & c \downarrow & & d \downarrow \\
A' & \longrightarrow & B' & \longrightarrow & C' & \longrightarrow & D'
\end{array}
$$

where the two rows are assumed to be exact.

(a) Show that, if $a$ is surjective and $b$ and $d$ injective, then $c$ is injective.
(b) Show that, if $d$ is injective and $a$ and $c$ surjective, then $b$ is surjective.
