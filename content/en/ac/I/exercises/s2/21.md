Let S be a commutative ring, R a commutative S-algebra, B an S-algebra (commutative or otherwise) and $ B_{(R)} $ the R-algebra obtained from B by extension of scalars. Suppose that R is a flat S-module and that B is a finitely generated S-module. If Z is the centre of B, show that the canonical homomorphism of $ Z_{(R)} = Z \otimes_S R $ to $ B_{(R)} $ is an isomorphism of $ Z_{(R)} $ onto the centre of $ B_{(R)} $. (Use the exact sequence

$$
0 \longrightarrow Z \longrightarrow B \xrightarrow{\theta} \operatorname{Hom}_S(B, B)
$$

where $ \theta(x)(y) = xy - yx $, and Proposition 11 of no. 10.)
