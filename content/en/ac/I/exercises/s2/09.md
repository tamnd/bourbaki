Let $ E $ be a finitely presented left $ A $-module.
(a) Show that for every family $ (F_i)_{i \in I} $ of right $ A $-modules, the canonical homomorphism $ E \otimes_A \left( \prod_{i \in I} F_i \right) \to \prod_{L \in I} (E \otimes_A F_i) $ (Algebra, Chapter II, § 3, no. 7) is bijective.
(b) Let $ (G_\alpha, \phi_{\beta\alpha}) $ be a direct system of left $ A $-modules; show that the Canonical homomorphism

$$
\lim_{\longrightarrow} \operatorname{Hom}_A(E, G_\alpha) \to \operatorname{Hom}_A(E, \lim_{\longrightarrow} G_\alpha)
$$

is bijective.
