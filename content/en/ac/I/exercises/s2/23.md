(a) Let $ 0 \to R \to L \to E \to 0 $ be an exact sequence of left A-modules, where L is a free A-module; let $ (e_i) $ be a basis of L. Show that the following conditions are equivalent:

(a) E is flat.

(b) For all $ x \in R $, if $ a $ is the right ideal generated by the components of $ x $ with respect to the basis $ (e_\alpha) $, then $ x \in Ra $.

(y) For all $ x \in R $, there exists a homomorphism $ u_x : L \to R $ such that $ u_x(x) = x $.

(8) For every finite sequence $ (x_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n} $ of elements of R, there exists a homomorphism $ u : L \to R $ such that $ u(x_i) = x_i $ for $ 1 \leq i \leq n $. (Use the Corollary to Proposition 7 of no. 6.)

(b) Let a be a left ideal of A such that $ A/a $ is a flat A-module. Show that, for every finitely generated left ideal $ b \subset a $, there exists $ x \in A $ such that

$$
b \subset Ax \subset a
$$

(use condition (8) of (a)).

(c) Derive from (a) a new proof of the result of Exercise 15.

(d) Let $ r $ be the Jacobson radical of $ A $ and $ 0 \to R \to L \to E \to 0 $ an exact sequence of left $ A $-modules such that $ L $ is free. Suppose that $ E $ is flat and $ R $ is contained in $ rL $. Show that $ R = 0 $ (in the notation of (a) observe that $ a_r $ is a finitely generated ideal and that $ a_r = a_{x,r} $).

(e) Let $ E $ be a finitely generated flat $ A $-module; suppose that there exists a two-sided ideal $ b $ of $ A $ contained in the Jacobson radical of $ A $ such that $ E/bE $ is a free $ (A/b) $-module. Show that $ E $ is then a free $ A $-module (observethat there exists a finitely generated free $ A $-module $ L $ such that $ L/bL $ is isomorphic to $ E/bE $ and use Algebra, Chapter VIII, § 6, no. 3, Corollary 4 to Proposition 6; then apply (d)).
