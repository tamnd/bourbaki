Give an example of an integral domain $A$ and a ring $B$ of which $A$ is a subring, such that $B$ is a flat $A$-module but there exists an $A$-module $E$ which is neither projective nor finitely generated, for which $B \otimes_A E$ is a finitely generated free $B$-module.
