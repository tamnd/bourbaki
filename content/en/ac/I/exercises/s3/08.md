Let $ p $ be a prime number and $ A $ the subring of $ \mathbf{Q} $ consisting of the fractions $ k/p^n $, where $ k \in \mathbf{Z}, n \geq 0 $. Show that $ A $ is a flat $ \mathbf{Z} $-module and that there exists a $ \mathbf{Z} $-module $ E $ which is not flat but where $ A \otimes_{\mathbf{Z}} E $ is a flat $ A $-module.
