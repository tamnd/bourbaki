(a) Let $ p : A \to B $ be a ring homomorphism. Show that, for every left ideal $ a $ of $ A $ which is a left annihilator of a subset $ M $ of $ A $, $ \bar{\rho}^{-1}(Ba) = a $

(b) Deduce from (a) an example of a homomorphism $ \rho : A \to B $ such that right $ A $-module $ B $ is not flat but $ \bar{\rho}^{-1}(Ba) = a $ holds for every left ideal $ a $ of $ A $ (cf. § 2, Exercise 17 and Algebra, Chapter VIII, § 3, Exercise 11 and § 2, Exercise 6 and Chapter IX, § 2, Exercise 4).
