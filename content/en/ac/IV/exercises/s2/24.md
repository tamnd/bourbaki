In a Laskerian ring A (Exercise 23), let $ p_1, p_2, p_3 $ be three distinct prime ideals such that $ p_1 \subset p_2 \subset p_3 $; let $ x_2 \in p_2 - p_1, x_3 \in p_3 - p_2 $. Replacing if need be $ p_2 $ and $ p_3 $ by prime ideals contained in $ p_2, p_3 $ respectively and containing respectively $ x_2 $ and $ x_3 $, we may suppose that $ p_2 $ is minimal in $ \operatorname{Ass}_f(A/(p_1 + Ax_1)) $ and $ p_3 $ is minimal in $ \operatorname{Ass}_f(A/p_2 + Ax_3)) $. Consider the ideal $ a = p_1 + x_2 p_3 $.

(a) Show that $ x_2 \notin a $ and $ x_3^k \notin a $ for every integer $ k > 0 $; deduce that a is not primary in A. Show that $ p_2 $ is a minimal element of $ \operatorname{Ass}_f(A/a) $.

(b) Let $ a = \bigcap_{i=1}^n q_i $ be a reduced primary decomposition of a in A and let $ p_i' $ be the radical of $ q_i $; suppose that $ p_3 \notin p_i' $ for $ 1 \leq i \leq s, p_3 \subset p_i' $ for $ s+1 \leq i \leq n $; show that of necessity $ s < n $ and $ x_2 \in q_i $ for $ 1 \leq i \leq s $. Show that there exists an index $ i \geq s+1 $ such that $ p_3 = p_i' $. (Argue by *reductio ad absurdum*: in the opposite case, there would exist $ y \notin p_3 $ such that $ y \in q_i $ for all $ i \geq s+1 $; then $ x_2 y \in a $ and prove that this relation contradicts $ y \notin p_3 $.)

Conclude that $ p_3 $ is a non-minimal element of $ \mathrm{Ass}_f(A/a) $; we denote by $ q'_3 $ the primary component of $ a $ in $ A $ which corresponds to it in the above decomposition.

(c) Let $ b = p_2 \cap q'_3 $; on the other hand let $ m > 0 $ be such that $ x_3^m \in q'_3 $ and let $ c = b + Ax_3^{2m} $. Show that the saturation of $ c $ with respect to $ p_3 $ is a $ p_3 $-primary ideal $ q''_3 $ (show that every element of $ p_3 $ is a power in $ q''_3 $ by using the fact that the saturation of $ p_2 + Ax_3 $ with respect to $ p_3 $ is primary). Prove on the other hand that $ x_3^m \notin q''_3 $ and that $ b = p_2 \cap q''_3 $; then there are two distinct reduced primary decompositions of the ideal $ b $.
