Let q be a primary ideal in a ring A and p its radical. Show that in the polynomial ring $ B = A[X] $ the ideal $ Bq $ is primary and has radical the ideal $ Bp $. (Let $ f(X) = \sum_j a_j X^j $ and $ g(X) = \sum_j b_j X^j $ be two non-constant polynomials such that $ fg \in Bq $ and $ f \notin Bp $. Let $ a_1 $ be the coefficient of smallest index not belonging to p; let a be the ideal of A generated by $ a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{m-1} $; there exists an integer k such that $ a^k \subset q $. Let $ q_i $ be the transporter of $ a^{k-i} $ in q for $ i \leq k $. Show by induction on i that $ g \in Bq_i $, arguing by reductio ad absurdum.)
