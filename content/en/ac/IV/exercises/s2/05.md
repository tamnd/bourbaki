Let L be a finitely generated free $ \mathbf{Z} $-module and T a finite commutative group whose order is a power of a prime number p. Show that the canonical primary decomposition (Exercise 4) of {0} in $ M = L \oplus T $ is $ \{0\} = p^n L \cap T $, where n is the least integer $ \geq 0 $ such that $ p^n T = 0 $ (note that $ p^n M = p^n L $).
