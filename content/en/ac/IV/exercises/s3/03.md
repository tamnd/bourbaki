Let $ A $ be a graded ring of type $ A $ (where $ A $ is torsion-free) and $ M $ a strongly Laskerian finitely generated graded $ A $-module (§ 2, Exercise 28). Show that the submodules $ Q(p) = Q(p, e_p(M)) $ (where $ p $ runs through $ \operatorname{Ass}_f(M) $) defined in Exercise 4 of § 2 are graded submodules.
