Let A be a Noetherian ring. For A to be isomorphic to a finite product of integral domains, it is necessary and sufficient that for every prime ideal p of A the local ring A, be an integral domain. (To see that the condition is sufficient, note first that it implies that that ring A is reduced; deduce that $ \{0\} = \bigcap_i p_i $, where the $ p_i $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq n $) are the minimal prime ideals of A. Show that, for $ i \neq j $, of necessity $ p_i + p_j = A $; for this, note that, if there existed a maximal ideal m containing $ p_i + p_j $, the ring $ A_m $ would not be an integral domain, using Corollary 3 to Proposition 2 of no. 1, and the Corollary to Proposition 5 of no. 2.)
