Let A be the ring of a valuation of height 2, $ \Gamma $ its order group, $ \Gamma_1 $ the unique isolated subgroup of $ \Gamma $ distinct from $ \{0\} $ and $ \Gamma $ and $ y > 0 $ an element of $ \Gamma $ not belonging to $ \Gamma_1 $. Let $ a $ be the ideal of those $ x \in A $ such that $ v(x) \geq y $, $ b $ the ideal of those $ x \in A $ such that $ v(x) > y $, $ E = A/a $ and $ F = A/b $. Show that $ \operatorname{Ass}_f(\operatorname{Hom}_A(E, F)) $ is distinct from $ \operatorname{Ass}_f(F) \cap \operatorname{Supp}(E) $. \*
