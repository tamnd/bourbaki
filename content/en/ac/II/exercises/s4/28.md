Give an example of a $ \mathbf{Z} $-module $ M $ such that, for some prime number $ p $ such that $ (p) \in \operatorname{Supp}(M) $, there exists no $ \mathbf{Z} $-homomorphism $ \neq 0 $ from $ M $ to $ \mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z} $.
