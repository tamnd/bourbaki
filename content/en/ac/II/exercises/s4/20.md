Give an example of a ring homomorphism $ \phi : A \to B $ such that every maximal ideal of $ A $ is of the form $ \phi(m) $, where $ m $ is a maximal ideal of $ B $, but there exist prime ideals of $ A $ which are not inverse images under $ \phi $ of ideals of $ B $ (take $ B $ to be a field).
