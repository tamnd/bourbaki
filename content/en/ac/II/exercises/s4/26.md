Let $ \phi : A \to B $ be a ring homomorphism such that the mapping
$$
{}^a\phi : \operatorname{Spec}(B) \to \operatorname{Spec}(A)
$$
is surjective. Let $ N $ be a finitely generated $ A $-module; show that, if $ u : M \to N $ is an $ A $-module homomorphism such that $ u \otimes 1 : M_{(B)} \to N_{(B)} $ is surjective, then $ u $ is surjective (apply Proposition 19 of no. 4 to $ \operatorname{Coker}(u) $).
