Give an example of a local homomorphism $ \rho : A \to B $ and an $ A $-module $ M $ (not finitely generated) such that $ \operatorname{Supp}(M_{(B)}) $ is not equal to $ {}^a\rho^{-1}(\operatorname{Supp}(M)) $ (take $ A = \mathbf{Z}_{(p)} $ and $ B = \mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z} $ for some prime number $ p $).
