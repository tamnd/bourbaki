Let $ A $ be a Noetherian ring, $ X = \operatorname{Spec}(A) $ its spectrum, $ M $ a finitely generated $ A $-module and $ Y = \operatorname{Supp}(M) $. Let $ Y_k $ ($ 1 \leq k \leq n $) be the distinct connected components of $ Y $.

(a) Show that there exists a unique decomposition of $ M $ as a direct sum $ M = M_1 \oplus M_2 \oplus \ldots \oplus M_n $ such that $ \operatorname{Supp}(M_k) = Y_k $ for all $ k $.

(b) If $ a = \operatorname{Ann}(M) $ and $ a_k = \operatorname{Ann}(M_k) $, show that the $ a_k $ are relatively prime in pairs and that $ \prod_{k=1}^n a_k = a $.

(Using Proposition 17 of no. 4, reduce it to the case where $ a = 0 $ and $ Y = X $ and apply Proposition 15 of no. 3.)
