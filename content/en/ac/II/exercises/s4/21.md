Let $ (A_\alpha, \phi_{\beta\alpha}) $ be a direct system of rings whose indexing set is directed, $ A = \varprojlim A_\alpha $, and $ \phi_\alpha : A_\alpha \to A $ the canonical homomorphism. If we write $ X_\alpha = \operatorname{Spec}(A_\alpha) $, $ (X_\alpha, {}^a\phi_{\beta\alpha}) $ is an inverse system of topological spaces and, if $ X = \operatorname{Spec}(A) $, the $ {}^a\phi_\alpha : X \to X_\alpha $, form an inverse system of continuous mappings. Show that $ u = \varprojlim {}^a\phi_\alpha $ is a homeomorphism of $ X $ onto $ \varprojlim X_\alpha $. (Prove first that, if, for all $ a $, $ p_\alpha $ is a prime ideal of $ A_\alpha $, such that $ p_\alpha = \phi_{\beta\alpha}^{-1}(p_\beta) $ for all $ a \leq \beta $, the union $ p $ of the $ \phi_\alpha(p_\alpha) $ is a prime ideal of $ A $; conversely, every prime ideal $ p $ of $ A $ is the union of the $ \phi_\alpha(p_\alpha) $, where $ p_\alpha = \phi_\alpha(p) $. Finally, if $ f_\alpha \in A_\alpha $ and $ f = \phi_\alpha(f_\alpha) $, note that the relation $ p \in X $, is equivalent to
$$
u(p) \in \overline{\operatorname{pr}_\alpha((X_\alpha)_{f_\alpha})}.
$$
