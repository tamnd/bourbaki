Let $ S = \mathbf{Z}^* $ (the complement of $ \{0\} $ in $ \mathbf{Z} $), $ p $ an integer $ > 1 $ and $ M $ the $ \mathbf{Z} $-module the direct sum of the modules $ \mathbf{Z}/p^n\mathbf{Z} $ for $ n \in \mathbf{N} $. Show that $ S^{-1}M = 0 $ although $ M $ is a faithful $ \mathbf{Z} $-module and that the canonical mapping
$$
S^{-1}\operatorname{End}_\mathbf{Z}(M) \to \operatorname{End}_{S^{-1}\mathbf{Z}}(S^{-1}M)
$$
is not injective.
