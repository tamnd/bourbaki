Let $ A $ be a commutative ring and $ \mathcal{F} $ a topologizing and idempotent set of ideals of $ A $.
(a) Then the ring $ A_{\mathcal{F}} $ is commutative (note that, if $ m \in \mathcal{F} $ and
$$
u \in \operatorname{Hom}_A(m, A),
$$
then $ u(m^2) \subset m $; if $ v $ is another element of $ \operatorname{Hom}_A(m, A) $, show that
$$
v(u(xy)) = u(v(xy))
$$
for $ x \in m, y \in m $.
(b) Let $ \mathcal{F}' $ be the family of ideals $ l' $ of $ A_{\mathcal{F}} $ such that $ A_{\mathcal{F}}/l' $ is 9-negligible (exercise 19 (h)). Show that the mapping $ p \mapsto p_{\mathcal{F}} $ is a bijection of the set of ideals $ p $ of $ A $ not belonging to $ \mathcal{F} $ onto the set of prime ideals of $ A_{\mathcal{F}} $ not belonging to $ \mathcal{F}' $. (Show first that, if $ A $ is an integral domain, so is $ A_{\mathcal{F}} $, making the same note as in (a); then use the exact sequence of Exercise 19 (d) to show that $\mathfrak p_{\mathscr F}$ is prime. If $\mathfrak p'$ is a prime ideal of $A_{\mathscr F}$ not belonging to $\mathscr F'$ and $\mathfrak p=\bar j_A^{-1}(\mathfrak p')$, recall that $\mathfrak p'=\mathfrak p_{\mathscr F}$.)

(c) Let B be an A-algebra and $\mathscr G$ the set of left ideals I of B such that $B_s/I$ is $\mathscr F$-negligible. Show that $\mathscr G$ is the set of left ideals of B containing an ideal of the form $B\mathfrak m$, where $\mathfrak m\in\mathscr F$; deduce that $\mathscr G$ is topologizing and idempotent and that, for every B-module N, $\mathscr F N=\mathscr G N$ and $N_{\mathscr F}$ is canonically identified with $N_{\mathscr G}$.

(d) Let S be a multiplicative subset of A and $\mathscr F$ the set of ideals of A meeting S. Show that $\mathscr F$ is topologizing and idempotent and that, for every A-module M, $M_{(\mathscr F)}$ and $M_{\mathscr F}$ are canonically identified with $S^{-1}M$ (note that every ideal of $\mathscr F$ contains a principal ideal $As$, where $s\in S$).
