In a ring A let $ r(a) $ denote the radical of an ideal a.
(a) Show that, for three ideals $ a, b, c $ of A,
$$
r(a + bc) = r(a + (b \cap c)) = t(a + b) \cap r(a + c).
$$
(b) Give an example of an infinite sequence of ideals $ (a_n) $ of A such that $ r(\bigcap_n a_n) \neq \bigcap_n r(a_n) $.
*(c) Give an example of a principal ideal $ a $ such that $ r(a) $ is not finitely generated and there exists no integer $ n > 0 $ such that $ (r(a))^n \subset a $. (Consider the ring of a non-discrete valuation of height 1.)*
