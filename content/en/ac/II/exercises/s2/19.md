Let $ \mathcal{F} $ be a set of left ideals of a ring $ A $; suppose that $ \mathcal{F} $ is topologizing and idempotent (Exercises 16 and 18).

(a) Show that, if $ m \in \mathcal{F} $, $ n \in \mathcal{F} $ and $ u \in \mathrm{Hom}_A(n, A_s) $, then $ \overline{u}^1(m) \in \mathcal{F} $ (consider the exact sequence $ 0 \to n/\overline{u}^1(m) \to A_s/\overline{u}^1(m) \to A_s/n \to 0 $). For every $ A $-module $ M $ and all $ v \in \mathrm{Hom}_A(m, M) $, let $ u.v $ denote the canonical image in $ M_{(\mathcal{F})} $ (Exercise 17 (c)) of the composite homomorphism $ \overline{u}^1(m) \xrightarrow{u} m \xrightarrow{v} M $. Show that the mapping $ (u, v) \mapsto u.v $ is $ \mathbf{Z} $-bilinear; there corresponds to it a $ \mathbf{Z} $-linear mapping

$$
\psi_{m,n} : \mathrm{Hom}_A(n, A_s) \otimes_{\mathbf{Z}} \mathrm{Hom}_A(m, M) \to M_{(\mathcal{F})}.
$$

These mappings form a direct system and their direct limit therefore corresponds canonically to a $ \mathbf{Z} $-bilinear mapping

$$
\psi : A_{(\mathcal{F})} \times M_{(\mathcal{F})} \to M_{(\mathcal{F})}.
$$

Show that, if $ M = A_0 $, $ \psi $ is an internal law of composition which makes $ A_{(\mathcal{F})} $ into a ring and the canonical mapping $ A \to A_{(\mathcal{F})} $ is a ring homomorphism for this structure. For any left $ A $-module $ M $, $ \psi $ is an external law defining on $ M_{(\mathcal{F})} $ a left $ A_{(\mathcal{F})} $-module structure; with this structure (and the canonical mapping $ A \to A_{(\mathcal{F})} $) the canonical mapping $ M \to M_{(\mathcal{F})} $ is an $ A $-module homomorphism.

(b) If $ i : M \to M_{(\mathcal{F})} $ is the canonical homomorphism, show that

$$
\operatorname{Ker}(i) = \mathcal{F}M
$$

and that the $ A $-module $ \operatorname{Coker}\,(i) = M_{(\mathcal{F})}/i(M) $ is 9-negligible.

(c) For every left $ A $-module $ M $ let $ M_{\mathcal{F}} $ denote the left $ A $-module $ (M/\mathcal{F}M)_{(\mathcal{F})} $ and $ j_M $ the composite canonical mapping

$$
M \to M/\mathcal{F}M \to M_{\mathcal{F}};
$$

then $ \operatorname{Ker}(j_M) = \mathcal{F}M $ and the $ A $-module $ \operatorname{Coker}(j_M) = M_{\mathcal{F}}/j_M(M) $ is $ \mathcal{F} $-negligible.

Let $ u : P \to M $, $ h : P \to Q $ be $ A $-module homomorphisms such that $ \operatorname{Ker}(h) $ and $ \operatorname{Coker}(h) $ are $ \mathcal{F} $-negligible; show that there exists a unique $ A $-homomorphism $ v : Q \to M_{\mathcal{F}} $ which makes the following diagram commutative

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
P & \xrightarrow{u} & M \\
| & & | \\
Q & \xrightarrow{v} & M_{\mathcal{F}}
\end{array}
$$

(First reduce it to the case where $ h $ is injective; then, identifying $ P $ with a submodule of $ Q $, note that, for $ x \in Q $, there exists $ m \in \mathcal{F} $ such that $ mx \in P $ and map $ x $ to the canonical image in $ M_{\mathcal{F}} $ of the composite homomorphism

$$
m \xrightarrow{x} mx \to P \xrightarrow{u} M \to M/\mathcal{F}M.
$$

(d) For every $ A $-module homomorphism $ u : M \to N $ show that there exists a unique homomorphism $ u_{\mathcal{F}} : M_{\mathcal{F}} \to N_{\mathcal{F}} $ which makes the following diagram commutative

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
M & \xrightarrow{u} & N \\
| & & | \\
M_{\mathcal{F}} & \xrightarrow{u_{\mathcal{F}}} & N_{\mathcal{F}}
\end{array}
$$

(use (a), (b) and (c)). Show that, if

$$
0 \longrightarrow M \xrightarrow{u} N \xrightarrow{v} P
$$

is an exact sequence of A-modules, the sequence

$$
0 \longrightarrow M_{\mathcal{F}} \xrightarrow{u_{\mathcal{F}}} N_{\mathcal{F}} \xrightarrow{v_{\mathcal{F}}} P_{\mathcal{F}}
$$

is exact (note that the mapping $M/\mathcal{F}M \to N/\mathcal{F}N$ derived from $u$ by taking quotients is injective).

Show that the mappings $j_{M_{\mathcal{F}}}$ and $(j_M)_{\mathcal{F}}$ coincide and are isomorphisms (and therefore $\mathcal{F}M_{\mathcal{F}} = 0$). If $N'$ is a sub-A-module of $M_{\mathcal{F}}$, $(j_{M_{\mathcal{F}}}^{-1}(N'))_{\mathcal{F}}$ is canonically identified with $N'_{\mathcal{F}}$ (observe that, if we write $N = j_M(N')$, $N'/j_M(N)$ is 9-negligible).

(e) Let M be a left A-module and $\phi : A \times M \to M$ the Z-bilinear mapping $(a, m) \mapsto am$. Show that there exists a unique Z-bilinear mapping $\phi_{\mathcal{F}} : A_{\mathcal{F}} \times M_{\mathcal{F}} \to M_{\mathcal{F}}$ which makes the following diagram commutative

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
A \times M & \xrightarrow{b} & M \\
j_A \times j_M \downarrow & & \downarrow j_M \\
A_{\mathcal{F}} \times M_{\mathcal{F}} & \xrightarrow{\phi_{\mathcal{F}}} & M_{\mathcal{F}}
\end{array}
$$

We also define on $A_{\mathcal{F}}$ a ring structure and on $M_{\mathcal{F}}$ a left $A_{\mathcal{F}}$-module structure. If $u : M \to N$ is an A-module homomorphism, $u_{\mathcal{F}} : M_{\mathcal{F}} \to N_{\mathcal{F}}$ is an $A_{\mathcal{F}}$-module homomorphism. In order that, for every A-module M, the mapping $u \mapsto u_{\mathcal{F}}$ of $\mathrm{Hom}_A(M, N)$ to $\mathrm{Hom}_{A_{\mathcal{F}}}(M_{\mathcal{F}}, N_{\mathcal{F}})$ be bijective, it is necessary and sufficient that $j_N$ be bijective.

(f) Take A to be the polynomial ring $K[X, Y]$ over a commutative field K and $\mathcal{F}$ to be the (topologizing and idempotent) set of ideals of A containing a power of the ideal $m = (X) + (Y)$. If $M = A/AX$ and $u : A \to M$ is the canonical mapping, show that $u_{\mathcal{F}} : A_{\mathcal{F}} \to M_{\mathcal{F}}$ is not surjective. (Show that $A_{\mathcal{F}}$ is identified with A and $M_{\mathcal{F}}$ with $K[\overline{Y}, 1/\overline{Y}]$, where $\overline{Y}$ is the class of Y in M, so that $X\overline{Y} = 0$ and $X(1/\overline{Y}) = 0$.)

(g) If we set $\mathcal{F}^1M = M_{\mathcal{F}}/j_M(M)$, show that, for every exact sequence $0 \to M \to N \to P \to 0$ of A-modules, there is an exact sequence

$$
0 \to \mathcal{F}M \to \mathcal{F}N \to \mathcal{F}P \to \mathcal{F}^1M \to \mathcal{F}^1N \to \mathcal{F}^1P
$$

(use the snake diagram).

(h) Let $\mathcal{F}'$ be the set of left ideals $l'$ of $A_{\mathcal{F}}$ such that $(A_{\mathcal{F}})_s/l'$ is an $\mathcal{F}$-negligible A-module: show that $\mathcal{F}'$ is the set of left ideals of $A_{\mathcal{F}}$ containing the $j_A(m)$, where $m \in \mathcal{F}$ (observe that $m_{\mathcal{F}} = A_{\mathcal{F}}$ for all $m \in \mathcal{F}$, using the exact sequence of (d); consequently $ A_{\mathcal{F}}/j_A(m) $ is 9-negligible). For every $ A_{\mathcal{F}} $-module $ M' $, $ \mathcal{F}M' = \mathcal{F}'M' $ ($ M' $ being considered as an $ A $-module by means of $ j_A $), $ \mathcal{F}' $ is topologizing and idempotent and $ M'_{\mathcal{F}'} $ is canonically identified with $ M'_{\mathcal{F}} $.
