Give an example of a sequence $ (P_n) $ of sub-$ \mathbf{Z} $-modules of $ \mathbf{Z} $ such that, for $ S = \mathbf{Z}^* $, the submodule $ S^{-1} \left( \bigcap_n P_n \right) $ is distinct from $ \bigcap (S^{-1}P_n) $.
