(a) Let M be the Z-module defined in Algebra, Chapter VII, § 3, Exercise 22 (h); let T be the torsion submodule of M. Show that, for every prime number $ p $, the submodule $ T_{(p)} $ of $ M_{(p)} $ is a direct factor of $ M_{(p)} $, although T is not a direct factor of M.

(b) Let M be the Z-module defined in Algebra, Chapter VII, § 3, Exercise 5; show that, for every prime number $ p $, $ M_{(p)} $ is a free $ \mathbf{Z}_{(p)} $-module, although M is not a free Z-module.
