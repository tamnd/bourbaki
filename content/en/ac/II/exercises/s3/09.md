Show that, for a (commutative) ring A to be absolutely flat (Chapter I, § 2, Exercise 17), it is necessary and sufficient that, for every maximal ideal m of A, $ A_m $ be a field (note that an absolutely flat local ring is necessarily a field and use the Corollary to Proposition 15).
