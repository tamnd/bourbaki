Give an example of a ring $A$ and an infinite family of distinct maximal ideals $(\mathfrak{m}_n)_{n \in \mathbf{N}}$ of $A$ such that $\bigcap_n \mathfrak{m}_n = 0$ but the canonical mapping
$$
A \to \prod_n (A/\mathfrak{m}_n)
$$
is not surjective.
