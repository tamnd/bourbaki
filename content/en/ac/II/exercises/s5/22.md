Let $A$ be a ring and $M$ a finitely generated projective $A$-module. For a finitely presented submodule $M'$ of $M$ to be a direct factor of $M$, it is necessary and sufficient that it be a pure submodule of $M$ (use Theorem 1 and § 3, Exercise 15).
