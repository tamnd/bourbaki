Define an endomorphism $ u $ of the $ \mathbf{Z} $-module $ M = \mathbf{Z}^{(\mathbf{N})} $ such that, for the prime ideal $ p = (0) $ of $ \mathbf{Z} $, $ u_p $ is an automorphism of $ M $, but that there exists no $ f \neq 0 $ in $ \mathbf{Z} $ such that $ u_f $ is a surjective endomorphism of $ M_f $.
