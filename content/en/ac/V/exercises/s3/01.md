Let $ A $ be a ring, $ \mathfrak{p} $ a prime ideal of $ A $, $ B $ a finitely generated $ A $-algebra and let $ r_1 \subset \ldots \subset r_m $ be a non-empty increasing sequence of ideals of $ B $ lying above $ \mathfrak{p} $. Show that there exist an element $ a \in A - \mathfrak{p} $ and a finite family $ (y_k)_{1 \leq k \leq q} $ of elements of $ B $ with the following properties:

(1) If we write $ B' = A[y_1, \ldots, y_q] $ and if $ S $ is the set of powers of $ a $, the ring $ S^{-1}B $ is integral over $ S^{-1}B' $.

(2) For all $ i $ such that $ 1 \leq i \leq m $ there exists an integer $ h(i) \geq 0 $ such that $ r_i $ contains $ y_1, \ldots, y_{h(i)} $ and for every polynomial $ P \in A[X_{h(i)+1}, \ldots, X_q] $ the relation $ P(y_{h(i)+1}, \ldots, y_r) \in r_i $ implies that all the coefficients of $ P $ belong to $ \mathfrak{p} $.

The ideal $ r_i \cap B' $ is then generated by $ \mathfrak{p} $ and the $ y_k $ such that $ k \leq h(i) $. (Argue by induction on $ m $, thus reducing it to the case $ m = 1 $. Then let $ c = r_1a $; argue by induction on the number $ n $ of generators of the $ A $-algebra $ B $. Let $ (x_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n} $ be such a system of generators and let $ s $ be the ideal of $ A[X_1, \ldots, X_n] $ consisting of those $ P $ such that $ P(x_1, \ldots, x_i) \in c $; then $ s \supseteq \mathfrak{p}A[X_1, \ldots, X_n] $; distinguish two cases according to whether $ s $ is equal or not to $ \mathfrak{p}A[X_1, \ldots, X_n] $. In the second case, there is a polynomial $ Q \in A[X_1, \ldots, X_n] $ none of whose coefficients is in $ \mathfrak{p} $ and which belongs to $ B $. Show that it is possible to determine integers $ m_i $ ($ 2 \leq i \leq n $) such that, if we write $ x'_i = Q(x_1, \ldots, x_n), x'_i = x_i - x_1^{m_i} $ ($ 2 \leq i \leq n $), there exists an element $ b \in A - \mathfrak{p} $ for which $ bx_1 $ is integral over $ C = A[x'_2, \ldots, x'_n] $; apply the induction hypothesis to the $ A $-algebra
$$
B_1 = A[x'_2, \ldots, x'_n]
$$
and the ideal $ c \cap B_1 $.
