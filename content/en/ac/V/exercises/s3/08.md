Let $ A $ be a ring. For $ A $ to be a Jacobson ring, it is necessary and sufficient that, for every maximal ideal $ m' $ of the polynomial ring $ A[X] $, $ m' \cap A $ is a maximal ideal of $ A $. (Note that if an integral domain $ B $ admits a Jacobson radical $ \mathfrak{R} \neq 0 $ and if $ b \in \mathfrak{R} $, $ b \neq 0 $, the intersection of $ B $ and a maximal ideal of $ B[X] $ containing $ 1 + bX $ cannot contain $ b $.)
