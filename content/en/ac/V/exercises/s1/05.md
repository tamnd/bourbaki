Let $ A $ be a commutative ring, $ B $ a commutative ring containing $ A $ and $ x $ an invertible element of $ B $. Show that every element of $ A[x] \cap A[x^{-1}] $ is integral over $ A $. (If $ y = a_0 + \ldots + a_p x^{-p} = b_0 + \ldots + b_q x^q $, where the $ a_i $ and $ b_j $ are in $ A $, show that the sub-A-module of $ B $ generated by $ 1,\ x,\ \ldots,\ x^{p+q+1} $ is a faithful $ A[y] $-module.)
