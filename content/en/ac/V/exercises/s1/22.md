Let $ A $ be an integrally closed domain and $ K $ its field of fractions; show that for every extension L of K there exists an integrally closed subdomain B of L whose field of fractions is L and such that $ B \cap K = A $ (consider L as an algebraic extension of a pure transcendental extension $ E = K(X_i)_{i \in I} $ of K and thus reduce it to the case where L is an algebraic extension of K, using Exercise 11 (a)).
