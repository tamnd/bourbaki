(a) Let $ A' $ be the polynomial ring $ \mathbf{Z}[X, Y] $ in two indeterminates and let $ \sigma $ be the automorphism of $ A' $ leaving $ Y $ invariant and such that $ \sigma(X) = -X $; $ \sigma $ and the identity automorphism form a group $ \mathcal{G} $ of automorphisms of $ A' $ and the ring of invariants of $ \mathcal{G} $ is $ A = \mathbf{Z}[X^2, Y] $. Let $ p' $ be the prime ideal $ A'(Y - 2X) $, $ p = A \cap p' $; the decomposition group $ \mathcal{G}^{\mathbf{Z}}(p') $ is reduced to the identity element. Show that $ A/p $ and $ A'/p' $ are not isomorphic (consider the tensor products of these rings with $ 2/22 $).

(b) Let $ K $ be a field of characteristic $ \neq 2 $, $ A' $ the polynomial ring $ K[X, Y] $ and $ \mathcal{G} $ the automorphism group of $ A' $ consisting of the identity and the $ K[Y] $-automorphism $ \sigma $ of $ A' $ such that $ \sigma(X) = -X $; the ring of invariants $ A $ of $ \mathcal{G} $ is $ K[X^2, Y] $. Let $ p' $ be the prime ideal $ A'(X^3 - Y) $ of $ A' $ and $ p = A \cap p' $; the decomposition group $ \mathcal{G}^{\mathbf{Z}}(p') $ is reduced to the identity element but $ A/p $ and $ A'/p' $ are not isomorphic.
