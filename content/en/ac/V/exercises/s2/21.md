Let $ A $ be an integrally closed domain containing the field $ \mathbf{Q} $, $ K $ its field of fractions, $ L $ an algebraic extension of $ K $ and $ B $ the integral closure of $ A $ in $ L $. Show that, for every ideal $ a $ of $ A $, $ A \cap_a B = a $.
