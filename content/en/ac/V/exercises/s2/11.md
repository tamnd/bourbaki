(a) Let $ A $ be a local integrally closed domain, $ m $ its maximal ideal, $ K $ its field of fractions, $ K' $ a finite Galois extension of $ K $, $ A' $ the integral closure of $ A $ in $ K' $, $ m' $ a maximal ideal of $ A' $ lying above $ m $, $ B = A^z(m') $ its decomposition ring and $ n = m' \cap B $. For every integer $ k > 0 $, $ B = A + n^k $ (argue as in Proposition 4). Moreover there exists a primitive element $ z $ of $ K^z $ (field of fractions of $ B $) such that $ z \in n $; deduce that $ n \cap A[z] = mA[z] $.

(b) Show that, for every integer $ k $, $ m^kB_n \cap A = m^k $. (If $ x \in m^kB_n \cap A $, note that there exists $ t \in A[z] $ (where $ z $ is defined in (a)) such that $ t \notin n $ and $ tx \in m^kA[z] $; writing $ t = t_0 + t_1z + \cdots + t_{r-1}z^{r-1} $ (where $ r $ is the degree $[K^z : K]$), deduce that $ t_0x \in m^k $ and $ t_0 \notin m $.)

(c) Suppose now that $ K $ is any Galois extension of $ K $. Keeping the same notation as above, show that $ B = A + n^k $ and $ m^kB_n \cap A = m^k $ still (note that every element of $ A^z(m') $ is contained in the decomposition ring of $ m' \cap C $, where $ C $ is the integral closure of $ A $ in a finite Galois sub-extension of $ K' $).

(d) Under the hypotheses of (c), show that, if $ A $ is Noetherian, so is $ B $. (Observe that the Hausdorff completion of $ B_n $ with respect to the $ m $-adic topology is identified with the completion $ \hat{A} $ of $ A $ by (c); if $ \phi : B_n \to \hat{A} $ is the canonical homomorphism, show that, for every ideal $ a $ of $ B_n $, $ \phi(a\hat{A}) = a $, using the fact that in $ \hat{A} $ every ideal is finitely generated and hence that $ a\hat{A} $ is generated by a finite number of elements of $ a $ and thus reduce it to the case where $ K' $ is a finite extension of $ K $.)
