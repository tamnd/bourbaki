Let $ K $ be a field, $ v $ a valuation on $ K $, $ L $ an extension of $ K $ and $ v' $ a valuation on $ L $ extending $ v $; $ L $ is called an *immediate extension* (with respect to $ u' $) if $ e(v'/v) = f(v'/v) = 1 $. The completion $ \hat{K} $ of $ K $ is an immediate extension of $ K $.

(a) For $ L $ to be an immediate extension of $ K $, it is necessary and sufficient that, for all $ x \in L - K $, there exist $ y \in K $ such that $ v'(x - y) > v'(x) $.

(b) For every field $ K $, show that there exists a *maximal* immediate extension $ L $ of $ K $, that is, not admitting an immediate extension distinct from itself (use § 3, Exercise 3 (b)).

(c) Show that, if $ K $ is linearly compact with the topology defined by $ v $, it admits no immediate extension other than itself (use (a), noting that, in the notation of (a), the set of $ v'(x - y) $ for $ y \in K $ has no greatest element in the value group of $ v' $).

(d) Suppose that $ K $ is not linearly compact; let $ B $ be a well-ordered subset of the order group of $ v $ and $ (a_\beta)_{\beta \in B} $ a family of elements of $ K $ such that, for $ \lambda < \mu < v $,
$$
v(a_\lambda - a_\mu) < v(a_\mu - a_v),
$$
but that there exists no $ x \in K $ such that $ v(x - a_\lambda) = v(a_\lambda - a_\gamma) $ for every ordered pair $ (\lambda, \mu) $ such that $ \lambda < \mu $. For every extension $ E $ of $ K $ and every extension $ w $ of $ v $ to $ E $, let $ D_E(\lambda) $ denote the set of $ z \in E $ such that $ v(z - a_\lambda) \geq \gamma_\lambda $, where $ \gamma_\lambda $ is the common value of the $ v(a_\lambda - a_\gamma) $ for $ \lambda < \mu $; the hypothesis is therefore that $ \bigcap_{\beta \in B} D_K(\beta) = \varnothing $ (§ 5, Exercise 5 (a)). Let $ \Omega $ be an algebraic closure of $ K $ and let $ v_0 $ be an extension of $ v $ to $ \Omega $; show that, if $ P $ is a polynomial in $ K[X] $, for there to exist $ A \in B $ such that $ v(P(a_\mu)) = v(P(a_\lambda)) $ for all $ \lambda \leq \mu $, it is necessary and sufficient that no zero of $ P $ in $ \Omega $ belong to $ \bigcap_{\beta \in B} D_\Omega(\beta) $. If $ P $ has this property and $ E $ and $ w $ have the same meaning as above, show that, for all $ z \in \bigcap_{\beta \in B} D_E(\beta) $, $ w(P(z) - P(a_\mu)) > w(P(z)) = v(P(a_\mu)) $ so long as $ \mu $ is large enough (decompose $ P(X) $ into factors in $ \Omega[X] $). Deduce that one of the following situations holds:

(1) Either $ \bigcap_{\beta \in B} D_\Omega(\beta) \neq \varnothing $ and, if $ \theta $ is an element of this intersection whose degree over $ K $ is the least possible, $ K(\theta) $ is an immediate extension of $ K $ distinct from $ K $.
