Let K be a field, A a integrally closed subdomain of K with K as a field of fractions and $(A_\alpha)$ a family of valuation rings of K whose intersection is A. Then if L is an extension of K, the integral closure of A in L is the intersection of the valuation rings of L which dominate one of the $A_\alpha$.
