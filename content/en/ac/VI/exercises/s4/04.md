Let $A$ be a valuation ring which is not a field.
(a) Show that for $A$ to be a discrete valuation ring, it is necessary and sufficient that every prime ideal of $A$ be principal,
(b) Show that, if $A$ is the ring of a valuation of height one, the field of fractions of $A$ is a finitely generated $A$-algebra.
