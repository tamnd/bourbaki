Let $ A_i \ (1 \leq i \leq n) $ be pairwise independent valuation rings of a field $ K $ and let $ A = \bigcap_i A_i $. For all $ i $, let $ a_i $ be an ideal $ \neq 0 $ of $ A $, and write $ a = \bigcap_i a_i; $ show that, for all $ i, a_i = A_i a $. Conversely, if $ b $ is an ideal $ \neq 0 $ of $ A $ and $ b_i = A_i b $, then $ b = \bigcap_i b_i $ (use Theorem 1 of no. 2).
