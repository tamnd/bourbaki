Let $ (\phi_i)_{i \in I} $ be a family of places of a field $ K $ taking their values in a finite number of fields; suppose further that for some $ x \in K $, the set of $ \phi_i(x) $ is finite. Show that there exists a polynomial $ f(X) $ of the form (1) of no. 1 such that $ f(x) \neq 0 $ and that the element $ z = f(x)^{-1} $ enjoys the following properties:
(i) $ \phi_i(x) = \infty $ implies $ \phi_i(xz) = 0 $ and $ \phi_i(z) = 0 $;
(ii) $ \phi_i(x) \neq \infty $ implies $ \phi_i(xz) \neq \infty $ and $ \phi_i(z) \neq 0 $. (Same proof as for Lemma 1).
