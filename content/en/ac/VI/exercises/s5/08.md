(a) Let $ K $ be a field, $ v $ a valuation on $ K $ of height 1 and $ A $ its ring. Let $ H $ be a compact subset of $ K $ (with the topology $ \mathcal{T}_v $) and $ a \neq 0 $ a point of $ K $. Show that there exists a polynomial $ f \in K[X] $ without constant term and such that $ f(a) = 1, f(H) \subset A $. (Prove that $ f $ may be taken to be a polynomial of the form
$$
1 - (1 - a^{-1}X)(1 - c_1^{-1}X)^{n(1)} \ldots (1 - c_r^{-1}X)^{n(r)}
$$
where the $ c_i $ are suitably chosen elements of $ H $ such that $ v(c_i) < v(a) $ and the $ n(i) $ are sufficiently large integers $ > 0 $.)

(b) Let $ X $ be a totally disconnected compact space; let the ring $ C(X; K) $ of continuous mappings from $ X $ to $ K $ be given the uniform convergence topology. Let $ B $ be a subring of $ C(X; K) $ containing the constants and separating the points of $ X $; show that $ B $ is dense in $ C(X; K) $ (Use (a) and General Topology, Chapter X, § 4, Exercise 21 (b).)
