Let w be the 2-adic valuation on the 2-adic field $ \mathbf{Q}_2 $; on the field of rational functions $ \mathbf{Q}_2(X) $ consider the discrete valuation $ v $ extending $ w $ and such that
$$
v(a_0 + a_1 X + \cdots + a_n X^n) = \inf_i (w(a_i))
$$
(§ 10, no. 1, Lemma 1); let K be the completion of $ \mathbf{Q}_2(X) $ with respect to this valuation and let its valuation also be denoted by $ v $. Let L be the splitting field of the polynomial $ (Y^2 - X)^2 - 2 $ in $ K[Y] $ and let $ v' $ be the unique valuation on L extending $ v $. Show that $ [L : K] = 8, e(v'/v) = 4, f(v'/v) = 2 $; if k (resp. $ k' $) is the residue field of $ v $ (resp. $ v' $), $ k' $ is a radicial extension of k of degree 2; show that there exists no sub-extension E of L such that $ [E : K] = 2 $ for which the residue field of the restriction of $ v' $ to E is equal to $ k' $. (Of necessity $ E = K(\sqrt{\alpha}) $, where $ \alpha \in K $ would not be a square in K but such that $ \alpha \equiv X \pmod{2} $; express &with the aid of a suitable basis of L over $ K(\sqrt{2}) $ and observe that there exists in $ K(\sqrt{2}) $ no element whose square is congruent to X mod. 2.)
