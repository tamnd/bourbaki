Let K be a totally disconnected non-discrete locally compact commutative field; let A be the ring of the absolute value $ \mathrm{mod}_K $ on K and let U be the group of units of A. For every integer $ n > 0 $, let $ _nU $ denote the group of n-th roots of unity in K and $ U^n $ the subgroup of U consisting of the n-th powers of the elements of U. Show that, if $ n $ is prime to the characteristic of K, $ U^n $ is an open subgroup of U and that

$$
\mathrm{Card}(U/U^n) = \mathrm{mod}_K(n) \cdot \mathrm{Card}(_nU)
$$

(use Exercise 14 of Integration, Chapter VII, § 2 and Commutative Algebra, Chapter III, § 4, no. 6, Corollary 1 to Theorem 2 to show that, if m is the maximal ideal of $ \mathbf{A} $, the image under $ x \mapsto x^n $ of $ 1 + m^k $ is $ 1 + n \cdot m^k $ for $ k $ sufficiently large).
