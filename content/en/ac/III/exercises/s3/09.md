(a) Let A be a Zariski ring, m a defining ideal of the topology on A and B a ring such that $A \subset B \subset \hat{A}$, which is a finitely generated A-module; show that, if mB is open in B under the topology induced by the topology on $\hat{A}$, of necessity $B = A$ (use Nakayama's Lemma).
   (b) Let A be a commutative Noetherian ring and m an ideal of A. Show that, if the Hausdorff completion $\hat{A}$ of A with respect to the m-adic topology is a finitely generated A-module, A is complete with the m-adic topology (reduce it to the case where A is Hausdorff; use Chapter V, § 2, no. 1, Proposition 1 and Theorem 1 to show that A is a Zariski ring with the m-adic topology and conclude with the aid of (a)). \*
