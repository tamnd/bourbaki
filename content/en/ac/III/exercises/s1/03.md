Let $ K $ be a field, $ B $ the polynomial ring $ K[X, Y] $ with the graduation defined by the total degree, $ C $ the graded subring of $ B $ generated by $ X $ and $ Y^2 $ and $ a $ the graded ideal of $ C $ generated by $ Y^4 $. Show that, in the graded ring $ A = C/a, A_{n,,} = A_1 A_n $ for $ n \geq 2 $ but $ A_n \neq (A_1)^n $ for $ n \geq 6 $.
