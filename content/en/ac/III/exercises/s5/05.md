Take $ A = \mathbf{Z} $ and $ 3 = p\mathbf{Z} $, where $ p $ is prime.
(a) Show that, if $ q $ is a prime number distinct from $ p $, the $ \mathbf{Z} $-module $ \mathbf{Z}/q\mathbf{Z} $ satisfies property (ii) of Theorem 1, but not property (i).
(b) Show that the $ \mathbf{Z} $-module $ \mathbf{Q}/\mathbf{Z} $ satisfies property (iv) of Theorem 1 but not property (ii).
