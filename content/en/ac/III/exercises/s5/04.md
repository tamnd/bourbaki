Let $ A $ be a commutative ring, $ 3 $ an ideal of $ A $ and $ S = 1 + 3 $. Show that, if $ M $ is an absolutely Hausdorff $ A $-module with respect to $ \mathfrak{g} $, then $ S^{-1}M = M $, in other words, for all $ a \in S $, $ x \mapsto ax $ is a bijection of $ M $ onto itself. (Show first that the hypothesis that $ M $ is Hausdorff with the $ 3 $-adic topology implies that $ x \mapsto ax $ is injective; then prove that the submodule $ aM $ of $ M $ is dense in $ M $ with the $ 8 $-adic topology and use Exercise 1 (a).)
