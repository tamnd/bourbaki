Let A be a filtered ring, (A,) its filtration, E a filtered A-module and $(E_n)$ its filtration. Show that if A, = A and E, = E, the mapping $(a, x) \mapsto ax$ of A $\times$ E to E is uniformly continuous with the topologies defined by the filtrations.
