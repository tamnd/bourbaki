Give an example of two filtered rings A, B whose filtrations are exhaustive and separated and a non-surjective homomorphism $ u : A \to B $ compatible with the filtrations and such that $ \mathrm{gr}(u) $ is bijective. From this deduce a counter-example to Corollary 1 to Proposition 12 when the ring A is not assumed to be complete and a counter-example to Proposition 13 when E is no longer assumed to be finitely generated (use Algebra, Chapter VIII, § 7, Exercise 3 (b)).
