Show that, if E is a vector space of dimension $ \geq 2 $ over a commutative field, the tensor algebra of E is a ring which is neither left nor right Noetherian (if $ a, b $ are two linearly independent vectors in E, consider the left ideal (or right ideal) generated by the elements $ a^n b^n $ for $ n \geq 1 $).
