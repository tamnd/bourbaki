Let T and X be two locally compact spaces, $ \lambda $ a positive measure on T, $ \mu $ a bounded positive measure on X such that $ \mu(X) = 1 $. Let $ f $ be a numerical function $ > 0 $ at every point of $ T \times X $, integrable along with $ \log f $ for the measure $ \lambda \otimes \mu $. Prove the inequality

$$
\log \left( \int \exp(\int \log f\, d\mu)\, d\lambda \right) \leq \int (\log \int f\, d\lambda)\, d\mu
$$

and show that equality cannot hold unless $ f $ is equivalent to a function of the form $ g \otimes h $ (apply the inequality of the geometric mean (Ch. IV, §6, Exer. 7 d)), for every $ t \in T $, to the function $ x \mapsto f(t, x)/(\int f(t, x)\, d\lambda(t)) $.
