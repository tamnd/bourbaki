Let $ p $ be a finite real number $ \geq 1 $, T and X two locally compact spaces, $ \lambda $ a positive measure on T, $ \mu $ a positive measure on X, and $ f $ a function $ \geq 0 $ defined on $ T \times X $, integrable along with $ f^p $ for the measure $ \lambda \otimes \mu $. Prove the inequality

$$
\left( \int^* \left( \int f\, d\mu \right)^p d\lambda \right)^{1/p} \leq \int^* \left( \int f^p\, d\lambda \right)^{1/p} d\mu.
$$

(For every $ t \in T $, apply Hölder’s inequality to the function $ x \mapsto f(t, x) $, put into the form

$$
f(t, x) = g(t, x) \left( \int f^p(t, x)\, d\lambda(t) \right)^{1/pq},
$$

where $ q $ is the exponent conjugate to $ p $.) Show that equality cannot hold unless $ f $ is equivalent to a function of the form $ g \otimes h $.
