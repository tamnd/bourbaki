Let $u$ and $v$ be two increasing and right-continuous numerical functions on $\mathbf{R}$, such that $u(x) = v(x) = 0$ for $x < 0$. Let $w$ be the increasing right-continuous function on $\mathbf{R}$ defined by $w(t) = u(t)v(t)$ for $t \geq 0$, $w(t) = 0$ for $t < 0$; let $\lambda, \mu, \nu$ be the Stieltjes measures associated with $u, v, w$ respectively (§6, Exer. 5).

To every function $f$ defined on $\mathbf{R}$, with values in $\overline{\mathbf{R}}$ or in a Banach space $F$, one makes correspond the function $\bar{f}$ defined on $\mathbf{R}^2$ by the conditions $\bar{f}(x, y) = f(x)$ if y < x , $ \bar{f}(x, y) = f(y) $ if $ y \geq x $. Show that, for $ f $ to be $ \nu $-integrable, it is necessary and sufficient that $ \bar{f} $ be integrable for the product measure $ \lambda \otimes \mu $, in which case $ \int f\, d\nu = \iint \bar{f}\, d\lambda\, d\mu $ (prove it first for the characteristic functions of intervals). From this, deduce the formula

$$
\int f(x)\, dw(x) = \int f(x)v(x-) du(x) + \int f(x)u(x+) dv(x).
$$

In particular, if $ u $ and $ v $ are continuous on $ \mathbf{R} $, one obtains *the formula for integration by parts*

$$
\int_a^b u(x)\, dv(x) = u(b)v(b) - u(a)v(a) - \int_a^b v(x)\, du(x).
$$
