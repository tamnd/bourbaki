Let $(\lambda_\alpha)$ be a family of complex point measures on T. For the family $(\lambda_\alpha)$ to be summable in the vector space $\mathcal{M}(T; \mathbf{C})$ equipped with the vague topology, it is necessary and sufficient that the family $(|\lambda_\alpha|)$ be so.
