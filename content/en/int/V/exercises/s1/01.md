Show that if $ f $ and $ g $ are two numerical functions $ \geqslant 0 $, then

$$
\mu^\bullet(\sup(f,g)) + \mu^\bullet(\inf(f,g)) \leqslant \mu^\bullet(f) + \mu^\bullet(g)
$$

(cf. Ch. IV, §1, Exer. 1).
