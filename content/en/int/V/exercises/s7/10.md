Let X, Y be two locally compact spaces. Show that every proper continuous mapping $ \pi $ of X into Y is conservative. (The hypothesis implies that the mapping $ f \mapsto f \circ \pi $ of $ \mathscr{H}(Y) $ into $ \mathscr{C}(X) $ has its image E contained in $ \mathscr{H}(X) $; then, for every positive measure $ \nu $ on Y, apply to the subspace E of $ \mathscr{H}(X) $ and to the positive linear form $ f \circ \pi \mapsto \nu(f) $ on E the Cor. of Prop. 1 of TVS, II, §3, No. 1.)
