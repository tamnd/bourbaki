Let X, Y be two locally compact spaces, $ \pi : X \to Y $ a continuous mapping, and M a subset of Y such that $ \pi^{-1}(M) $ is contained in a countable union of compact sets. Then, for every positive measure $ \nu $ on Y that is concentrated on M, there exists a positive measure $ \mu $ on X such that $ \pi(\mu) = \nu $ (use Exer. 10).
