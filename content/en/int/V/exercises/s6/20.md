Let T, X, Y be three locally compact spaces, $ \mu $ a positive measure on T, $ t \mapsto \lambda_t $ a $ \mu $-adequate mapping of T into $ \mathcal{M}_+(X) $, $ \nu = \int \lambda_t d\mu(t) $, and $ \pi $ a $ \nu $-proper mapping of X into Y. Assume *one* of the following hypotheses: *a*) Y is countable at infinity and $ \nu $ is moderated; *b*) Y is countable at infinity and $ \lambda_t $ is bounded locally almost everywhere in T. Then $ \pi $ is $ \lambda_t $-proper locally almost everywhere in T, the mapping $ t \mapsto \pi(\lambda_t) $ is $ \mu $-adequate, and its integral is equal to $ \pi(\mu) $.
