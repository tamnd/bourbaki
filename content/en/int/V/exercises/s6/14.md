Let $ f $ be a numerical function defined on a compact interval $ I = [a, b] $ of $ \mathbf{R} $, integrable on $ I $ for Lebesgue measure. If one sets $ F(x) = \int_a^x f(t)\,dt $, show that $ F $ admits almost everywhere in $ I $ a derivative equal to $ f $ (reduce to the case that $ f \geq 0 $; then, using Exer. 13, consider successively the case that $ f $ is lower semi-continuous then the general case (cf. Ch. IV, §4, No. 4, Cor. of Th. 3)).
