a) Show that in all of the results of §3 in which the concept of a vaguely continuous mapping $ t \mapsto \lambda_t $ intervenes, one can replace this hypothesis by the following: for every function $ g \in \mathcal{K}_+(X) $, the function $ t \mapsto \lambda_t(g) $ is lower semi-continuous on T.

b) Consider a scalarly essentially integrable mapping $ t \mapsto \lambda_t $ of T into $ \mathcal{M}_+(X) $ that satisfies the following condition:
For every compact subset K of T and every $ \varepsilon > 0 $, there exists a compact set $ K_1 \subset K $ such that $ \mu(K - K_1) \leq \varepsilon $ and such that the restrictions to $ K_1 $ of all the functions $ t \mapsto \langle f, \lambda_t \rangle $, where $ f $ runs over $ \mathcal{K}_+(X) $, are lower semi-continuous.
Show that the mapping $ t \mapsto \lambda_t $ is $ \mu $-adequate.
