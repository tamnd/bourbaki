Let X be a locally compact space, $ \Lambda : t \mapsto \lambda_t $ a $ \mu $-adequate mapping of T into $ \mathcal{M}_+(X) $. Let $ \mu' = g \cdot \mu $ be a positive measure with base $ \mu $, such that $ \Lambda $ is scalarly essentially $ \mu' $-integrable.

a) Show that $ \Lambda $ is $ \mu' $-integrable (use Exer. 27 b), or the relation $ \mu' = \sup_n (\inf(\mu', n\mu)) $ and Exer. 9 of §3).

b) Show that $ \nu' = \int \lambda_t \, d\mu'(t) $ is a measure with base $ \nu = \int \lambda_t \, d\mu(t) $.

c) Show that the mapping $ t \mapsto g(t)\lambda_t $ is $ \mu $-adequate, and that its integral is $ \nu' $.
