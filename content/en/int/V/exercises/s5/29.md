Let X be a locally compact space. Let $\Lambda : t \mapsto \lambda_t$ be a $\mu$-adequate mapping of T into $\mathcal{M}_+(X)$. Let g be a function on X, positive, universally measurable, and locally bounded. Show that the family $t \mapsto g \cdot \lambda_t$ is $\mu$-adequate, and that

$$
\int (g \cdot \lambda_t) d\mu(t) = g \cdot \int \lambda_t d\mu(t)
$$

under each of the following conditions: $a)$ g is moderated for the measure $\int \lambda_t d\mu(t)$; $b)$ the measures $\lambda_t$ are bounded.
