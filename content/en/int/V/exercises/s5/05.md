Let $ \rho, \sigma $ be two atomic measures on T, and let M, N be the smallest sets carrying $ |\rho| $ and $ |\sigma| $, respectively. For $ \rho $ and $ \sigma $ to be alien, it is necessary and sufficient that $ M \cap N = \varnothing $. From this, deduce an example of an atomic measure $ \nu $ on the interval $ I = [0,1] $ of $ \mathbf{R} $, such that I is the support of $ \nu^+ $ and of $ \nu^- $.
