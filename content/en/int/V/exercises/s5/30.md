$a)$ Let M be a bounded subset of $\mathbf{C}$. Show that the closed convex envelope of M is the intersection of the closed disks containing M.

$b)$ Let X be a locally compact space, $\mu$ a complex measure on X such that $\| \mu \| = \mu(1) = 1$, f a bounded, continuous complex function on X. Show that $\mu(f)$ belongs to the closed convex envelope of $f(X)$ in $\mathbf{C}$. (Use $a$).) Deduce from this a new proof of Prop. 9.
