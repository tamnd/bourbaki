Show that if E is a Fréchet space and X is a locally compact space, then the space $\mathcal{K}(X; E)$ is bornological.
From this, deduce that for every set $\mathcal{G}$ of bounded subsets of $\mathcal{K}(X; \mathbf{C})$ that is a covering of $\mathcal{K}(X; \mathbf{C})$ and is such that every set formed by the points of a convergent sequence in $\mathcal{K}(X; \mathbf{C})$ belongs to $\mathcal{G}$, the space $\mathcal{M}(X_\mathcal{G}; \mathbf{C})$ is complete.
