b) For every compact subset $ K $ of $ X $, show that the bilinear form $ (f, \mu) \mapsto \langle f, \mu \rangle $ is continuous on $ \mathcal{H}(X, K; \mathbf{C}) \times \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ when $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ is equipped with the quasi-strong topology; it is continuous on $ \mathcal{H}(X, K; \mathbf{C}) \times \mathcal{M}_+(X) $ when $ \mathcal{M}_+(X) $ is equipped with the vague topology (make use of Exer. 10).

c) Assume that $ X $ is paracompact and noncompact. Show that the bilinear form $ (f, \mu) \mapsto \langle f, \mu \rangle $ is not continuous on $ \mathcal{H}(X; \mathbf{C}) \times \mathcal{M}_+(X) $ when $ \mathcal{M}_+(X) $ is equipped with the strong topology (identical in this case to the quasi-strong topology).

d) Give an example where $ X $ is compact and the bilinear form $ (f, \mu) \mapsto \langle f, \mu \rangle $ is not continuous on $ \mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{C}) \times \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ when $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ is equipped with the topology of compact convergence (TVS, IV, §1, Exer. 11).

¶ 12) a) Show that the bilinear mapping $ (g, \mu) \mapsto g \cdot \mu $ of $ \mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{C}) \times \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ into $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ is continuous when $ \mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ is equipped with the topology of compact convergence and $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ with the quasi-strong topology (Exer. 8) or with the topology of strictly compact convergence.

$ b) $ Show that the bilinear mapping $ (g, \mu) \mapsto g \cdot \mu $ of $ \mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{C}) \times \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ into $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ is $ (\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{T}) $-hypocontinuous when $ \mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ is equipped with the topology of compact convergence and $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ with the vague topology, where $ \mathcal{S} $ denotes the set of finite subsets of $ \mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ and $ \mathcal{T} $ the set of vaguely bounded subsets of $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $.

$ c) $ Show that the mapping $ (g, \mu) \mapsto g \cdot \mu $ of $ \mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{C}) \times \mathcal{M}_+(X) $ into $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ is continuous when $ \mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ is equipped with the topology of compact convergence, $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ and $ \mathcal{M}_+(X) $ with the vague topology.

$ d) $ Give an example of a compact space $ X $ such that the bilinear mapping $ (g, \mu) \mapsto g \cdot \mu $ of $ \mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{C}) \times \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ into $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ is not continuous when $ \mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ is equipped with the topology of uniform convergence and $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ with the vague topology.
