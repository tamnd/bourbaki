Let E be a Hilbert space with a countable orthonormal basis $ (\mathbf{e}_n ) $ (TVS, V, §2, No.3), $ E_0 $ the dense linear subspace of E formed by the linear combinations of finitely many vectors $ \mathbf{e}_n $. Let X be the compact subspace of $ \mathbf{R} $ formed by 0 and the points $ 1/n $ ($ n $ an integer $ \geqslant 1 $); let $ \mu $ be the positive measure on X defined by the mass $ 1/n^2 $ at each point $ 1/n $ and the mass 0 at the point 0 ($ \S 1 $, No. 3, Example I). Consider the continuous mapping f of X into $ E_0 $ defined by $ f(0) = 0,\ f(1/n) = \mathbf{e}_n / n $ for $ n \geqslant 1 $; show that the integral $ \int f\ d\mu $ does not belong to $ E_0 $.
