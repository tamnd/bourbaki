The spaces X and E and the mapping f being defined as in Exer. 1, show that the mapping $ \lambda \mapsto \lambda(f) $ of $ \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C}) $ into E is not continuous for the vague topology. (If $ g_k $ ($ 1 \leqslant k \leqslant n $) are n continuous scalar functions on X, show that there exists a discrete measure $ \lambda $ on X such that $ \int g_k\ d\lambda = 0 $ for $ 1 \leqslant k \leqslant n $ but $ \| \int f\ d\lambda \| $ is arbitrarily large.)
