a) Let E be a quasi-complete Hausdorff locally convex space. Show that when $\mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C})$ is equipped with the quasi-strong topology ($\S 1$, Exer. 8) (resp. the topology of strictly compact convergence), the mapping $(f, \mu) \mapsto \int f\ d\mu$ is $(\mathcal{G}, \mathcal{T})$-hypocontinuous, $\mathcal{T}$ being the set of vaguely bounded subsets of $\mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C})$, $\mathcal{G}$ the set of bounded (resp. compact) subsets of $\mathcal{K}(X; E)$ that are contained in $\mathcal{K}(X, K; E)$ for a suitable compact set K.

b) For every compact subset K of X, the mapping $(f, \mu) \mapsto \int f\ d\mu$ is continuous on $\mathcal{K}(X, K; E) \times \mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C})$ when $\mathcal{M}(X; \mathbf{C})$ is equipped with the quasi-strong topology, and on $\mathcal{K}(X, K; E) \times \mathcal{M}_+(X)$ when $\mathcal{M}_+(X)$ is equipped with the vague topology.
