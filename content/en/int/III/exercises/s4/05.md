Let $ (X_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L} $ be an infinite family of compact spaces; for each $ \lambda \in L $, let $ a_\lambda $ be a point of $ X_\lambda $ and let $ \mu_\lambda $ be a positive measure on $ X_\lambda $ of total mass 1. For every finite subset $ J $ of $ L $, let $ \nu(J) $ be the measure on $ X = \prod_{\lambda \in L} X_\lambda $ that is the product of the measures $ \mu_\lambda $ for $ \lambda \in J $ and the measures $ \varepsilon_{a_\lambda} $ for $ \lambda \in L - J $. Show that, with respect to the directed set of finite subsets of $ L $, the measure $ \nu(J) $ tends vaguely to $ \mu = \bigotimes_{\lambda \in L} \mu_\lambda $ but does not tend strongly to $ \mu $.
