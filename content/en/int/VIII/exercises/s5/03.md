Let $ G $ be a locally compact group, $ \chi $ a continuous representation of $ G $ in $ \mathbf{R}_+^* $. Generalize Props. 4, 5 and 6 to the set $ \Gamma_\chi $ of measures $ \alpha \in \Gamma $ such that the restriction of $ \chi $ to $ H_\alpha $ is the modulus of $ \alpha $. (Replace the measure $ \mu $ by $ \chi \cdot \mu $ and consider the quotient measures $ (\chi \cdot \mu)/\check{\alpha} $.)
