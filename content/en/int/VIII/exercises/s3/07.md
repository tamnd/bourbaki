Let G be a locally compact group.

a) Show that if a positive measure $ \mu \in \mathcal{C}'(G) $ admits an inverse in $ \mathcal{C}'(G) $ that is a positive measure, then $ \mu $ is necessarily a point measure.

b) Taking G to be the finite group $ \mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z} $, give an example of a non point measure $ \mu \in \mathcal{M}(G) $ that is invertible in $ \mathcal{M}(G) $.
