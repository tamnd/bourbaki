Let G be a locally compact group; denote by $ \mathcal{C}'_+(G) $ the set of positive measures on G with compact support. Show that the mapping $ (\mu, \nu) \mapsto \mu * \nu $ of $ \mathcal{M}_+(G) \times \mathcal{C}'_+(G) $ into $ \mathcal{M}_+(G) $ is continuous when $ \mathcal{C}'(G) $ is equipped with the weak topology $ \sigma(\mathcal{C}'(G), \mathcal{C}(G)) $, and $ \mathcal{M}(G) $ with the vague topology $ \sigma(\mathcal{M}(G), \mathcal{K}(G)) $. (Make use of Exer. 10 of Ch. III, §1 and the fact that G is paracompact.)
