Let $ (G_\ell)_{\ell \in I} $ be a family of locally compact groups, all but finitely many of them compact. Let $ U_\ell $ be a continuous linear representation of $ G_\ell $ in a locally convex space $ E_\ell $. For every $ s = (s_\ell) \in G = \prod_\ell G_\ell $, let $ U(s) $ be the endomorphism $ (x_\ell) \mapsto (U_\ell(s)x_\ell) $ of $ E = \prod_\ell E_\ell $. Show that $ U $ is a continuous linear representation of $ G $ in $ E $. Let $ E' $ be the topological direct sum of the $ E_\ell $. Let $ V(s) $ be the restriction of $ U(s) $ to $ E' $. Show that $ V $ is a continuous linear representation of $ G $ in $ E' $.
