Let $G$ be a locally compact group, $\beta$ a relatively invariant, nonzero positive measure on $G$, $\chi$ and $\chi'$ its left and right multipliers. For $f \in L^p_C(G, \beta)$ and $s \in G$, set
$$
(U(s)f)(x) = \chi(s)^{-1/p} f(s^{-1}x)
$$
$$
(V(s)f)(x) = \chi'(s)^{-1/p} f(xs)
$$
$$
(Sf)(x) = (\chi \chi')(x)^{-1/p} \overline{f(x^{-1})}.
$$
Then $U$ and $V$ are linear representations of $G$, and
$$
S^2 = 1,\quad \|U(s)\| = \|V(s)\| = 1
$$
$$
U(s)V(t) = V(t)U(s),\quad SU(s)S = V(s)
$$
for all $s, t$ in $G$.
