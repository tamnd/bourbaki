Let $ G $ be a locally compact group, $ H $ a closed subgroup of $ G $, and $ U $ a continuous linear representation of $ H $ in a locally convex space $ E $. Let $ K $ be a compact subset of $ G $. Let $ \mathcal{K}^U(K) $ be the space of continuous functions on $ G $, with values in $ E $, with support contained in $ KH $, and satisfying $ f(xh) = U(h)^{-1}f(x) $ ($ x \in G, h \in H $). Let $ \mathcal{K}^U $ be the union of the $ \mathcal{K}^U(K) $, equipped with the direct limit topology of the topologies of uniform convergence in $ K $ on each of the spaces $ \mathcal{K}^U(K) $. For $ f \in \mathcal{K}^U $ and $ s \in G $, define $ V(s)f \in \mathcal{K}^U $ by
$$
(V(s)f)(t) = f(s^{-1}t).
$$
Show that $ V $ is a continuous linear representation of $ G $ in $ \mathcal{K}^U $.
