Let G be a locally compact group, $ \beta $ a left Haar measure on G, A and B two $ \beta $-integrable sets such that $ \beta(A) \leq \beta(B) $ and $ \beta^*(A^{-1}) < +\infty $. Show that there exist
1) disjoint sets N, K_1, K_2, ... covering A, with N $ \beta $-negligible and the K_n compact;

2) disjoint sets $ N' , K'_1 , K'_2 , \ldots $ covering B, with the $ K'_n $ compact;
3) elements $ s_n \in G $ such that $ K'_n = s_n K_n $.
(Using the fact that $ \beta(xA \cap B) $ depends continuously on $ x $ and that
$$
\int \beta(xA \cap B)\, d\beta(x) = \beta(A^{-1})\beta(B),
$$
show that if $ \beta(A) \neq 0 $ then there exists an $ x \in G $ such that $ \beta(xA \cap B) \neq 0 .$)
