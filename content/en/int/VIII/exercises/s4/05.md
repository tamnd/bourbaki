Let G be a locally compact group countable at infinity, $ \beta $ a left Haar measure on G, $ a \in \mathbf{R}_+^* $, and $ (\nu_u)_{0 \leq u \leq a} $ a family of positive measures on G satisfying the following conditions:
(i) $ \nu_0 = \varepsilon_e $; $ \nu_{u+v} = \nu_u * \nu_v $ if $ u + v \leq a $; $ \nu_u = \nu_u^\vee $;
(ii) for $ 0 < u \leq a $, $ \nu_u = f_u \cdot \beta $ with $ f_u \geq 0 $ lower semi-continuous;
(iii) $ \nu_u $ is a vaguely continuous function of $ u $.
a) Let $ f \in \mathcal{K}_+(G) $. Show that, for $ 0 < u \leq a $,

$$
\int (f_{u/2} * f)(z)^2 d\beta(z) = \int f(x)(f_u * f)(x) d\beta(x).
$$

b) Let $ f \in \mathcal{K}(G) $. Show that $ f_{u/2} * f $ has $ \beta $-integrable square for $ 0 < u \leq a $, and that (1) again holds.

c) Let $ f \in \mathcal{K}(G) $ be such that $ \nu_a * f = 0 $. Show that $ f = 0 $. (One has $ \nu_{a/2^n} * f = 0 $ for every integer $ n > 0 $ by b), therefore $ f = 0 $ by §3, No. 3, Remark 1).)

d) Let $ \nu \in \mathcal{C}'(G) $ be such that $ \nu_a * \nu = 0 $. Show that $ \nu = 0 $. (Regularize $ \nu $ by functions in $ \mathcal{K}(G) $ and apply c).)
