For $ t > 0 $ and $ x \in \mathbf{R} $, let $ F_t(x) = te^{-\pi t^2 x^2} $. Let $ f \in \overline{\mathcal{K}(\mathbf{R})} $. Show that $ f * F_t $ tends to $ f $ uniformly as $ t $ tends to $ +\infty $. (Show that the measures $ F_t(x) dx $ satisfy the conditions of §2, No. 7, Lemma 4, with $ a = 0 .$)
