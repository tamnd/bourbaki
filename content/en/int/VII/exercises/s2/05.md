Let $ G $ be a locally compact group. For every $ s \in G $, let $ \psi_s $ be the automorphism of the additive group $ \mathbf{R} $ defined by $ \psi_s(x) = \Delta_G(s)x $. Let $ \Gamma $ be the topological semi-direct product of $ G $ and $ \mathbf{R} $ defined by $ s \mapsto \psi_s $ (GT, III, §2, No. 10). Show that $ \Gamma $ is unimodular.
