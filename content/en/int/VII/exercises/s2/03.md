Let $ B $ be a locally compact space, $ H $ a locally compact group. Set $ X = B \times H $, the group $ H $ operating in $ X $ by

$$
(b, \xi)\xi' = (b, \xi\xi') .
$$

Let $ \lambda $ be a measure on $ B = X/H $, and $ \beta $ a left Haar measure on $ H $. Then $ \lambda^# = \lambda \otimes \beta $.
