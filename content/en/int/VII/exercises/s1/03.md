Let G be a locally compact group, $ \mu $ a left Haar measure on G, and $ \nu = \Delta_G^{-1/2} \cdot \mu $. Show that

$$
\gamma(s)\nu = \Delta_G(s)^{1/2}\nu, \quad \delta(s)\nu = \Delta_G(s)^{1/2}\nu, \quad \dot{\nu} = \nu.
$$
