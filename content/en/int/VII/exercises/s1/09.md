Adopt the notations of the proof of Th. 1 of No. 2. Let $ \beta $ be the unique left Haar measure on $ G $ such that $ \beta(f_0) = 1 $. Show that, for every $ f \in \mathcal{K}(G) $, $ I_g(f) $ tends to $ \beta(f) $ with respect to $ \mathcal{B} $. (Let $ a $ be a cluster point of $ g \mapsto I_g(f) $ with respect to $ \mathcal{B} $. There exists an ultrafilter $ \mathcal{U} $ finer than $ \mathcal{B} $ such that $ I_g(f) $ tends to $ a $ with respect to $ \mathcal{U} $. On the other hand, $ I_g(f) $ tends to $ \beta(f) $ with respect to $ \mathcal{U} $.)
