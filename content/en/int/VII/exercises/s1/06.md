Let G be a locally compact group having an open and compact subgroup H. Show that for every automorphism $ \varphi $ of G, $ \mod \varphi \in \mathbf{Q}_+^* $. (Observe that $ \varphi(H) \cap H $ has finite index in H and in $ \varphi(H) $.) Show that the equality $ \Delta_G(s) = 1 $ defines an open subgroup of G containing H.
