Let $ G $ be a locally compact group, $ A $ a dense subset of $ G $, $ \alpha $ a left Haar measure on $ G $, and $ H $ an $ \alpha $-measurable subset of $ G $ having the following property: for every $ s \in A $, $ sH \cap (\mathbf{C}H) $ and $ H \cap (\mathbf{C}sH) $ are locally $ \alpha $-negligible. Show that either $ H $ is locally $ \alpha $-negligible or its complement is locally $ \alpha $-negligible. (Show that $ \varphi_H \cdot \alpha $ is left invariant.)
