Let G be a locally compact group, $ \beta $ a left Haar measure on G, A a subset of G, and B a relatively compact $ \beta $-integrable subset of G such that $ \beta(B) > 0 $. Show that if the compact subsets of $ A \cdot B $ have bounded measures for $ \beta $, then $ A $ is relatively compact. (Imitate the argument of Prop. 2.)
