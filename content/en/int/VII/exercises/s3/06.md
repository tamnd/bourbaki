Let $P$ be the set of complex numbers $z = x + iy$ whose imaginary part is $> 0$. Show that $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbf{R})$ operates transitively and continuously on the left in $P$ by

$$
\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \cdot z \mapsto \frac{az + b}{cz + d}
$$

and that $P$ thus becomes a topological homogeneous space for $\mathrm{SL}(2, \mathbf{R})$. Show that $y^{-2} dx dy$ is an invariant measure on $P$.
