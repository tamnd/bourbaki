Let E be a Riesz space of finite dimension $ n $.

a) If E is archimedean ($ \S 1 $, Exer. 5), show that the cone P of elements $ \geq 0 $ of E is closed and has an interior point. From this, deduce that the intersection of the support hyperplanes of P reduces to 0 (make use of the fact that $ P \cap (-P) = \{0\} $).

b) Using a) and Exer. 6, show that every archimedean Riesz space of dimension $ n $ is isomorphic to the product space $ \mathbf{R}^n $ (cf. $ \S 1 $, Exer. 13 e)).

c) Give an example of a totally ordered Riesz space of dimension 2.
