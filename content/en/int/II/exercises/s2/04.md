Let E be a fully lattice-ordered space.

a) Let $ L $ be a positive linear form on E, continuous for the topology $ \mathcal{T}_0(E) $ (§1, Exer. 10). Show that the set of $ x \in E $ such that $ L(|x|) = 0 $ is a band $ Z(L) $. Let $ S(L) $ be the band supplementary to $ Z(L) $ in E (§1, Exer. 6).

b) Let $ L $ and $ M $ be two positive linear forms on E, continuous for $ \mathcal{T}_0(E) $. In order that, in the space $ \Omega $ of relatively bounded linear forms on E, $ M $ belong to the band generated by $ L $, it is necessary and sufficient that $ S(M) \subset S(L) $. (To see that the condition is sufficient, argue by contradiction, on supposing that $ M $ does not verify the condition of Prop. 5; consider a sequence $ (y_n) $ of elements of E such that $ 0 \leq y_n \leq x $,

$ L(y_n) \leq 1/2^n $ and $ M(y_n) \geq \alpha > 0 $, and infer the existence of an element $ z \geq 0 $ in E such that $ L(z) = 0 $ and $ M(z) \geq \alpha .$

c) Let $ L $ and $ M $ be two positive linear forms on E, continuous for $ \mathcal{T}_0(E) $. In order that, in $ \Omega $, $ L $ and $ M $ be alien, it is necessary and sufficient that $ S(L) \cap S(M) = \{0\} $. (To see that the condition is necessary, prove that if $ S(L) \cap S(M) $ does not reduce to 0 then the bands in $ \Omega $ generated by $ L $ and $ M $ have an element $ \neq 0 $ in common, by using b.)
