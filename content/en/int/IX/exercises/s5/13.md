*Let G be a locally compact abelian group. One denotes by $ \widehat{G} $ the dual group of G and by $ \alpha $ a Haar measure on $ \widehat{G} $. For every bounded measure $ \mu $ on G, one denotes by $ \mathcal{F} \mu $ the Fourier transform of $ \mu $, which is a function on the group $ \widehat{G} $ (cf. Théor. spec., Ch. II, §1, No. 2).

a) The Fourier transformation $ \mathcal{F} $ is a continuous mapping of $ \mathcal{M}_+^b(G) $ equipped with the tight topology into $ \mathcal{C}^b(\widehat{G}) $ equipped with the topology of compact convergence. (Make use of the Cor. of Prop. 13 of No. 6.)

b) Let $ \mathcal{F} $ be a filter on $ \mathcal{M}_+^b(G) $ and $ \Phi $ a bounded continuous function on $ \widehat{G} $; assume that $ \lim_{\lambda, \mathcal{F}} (\mathcal{F} \lambda) \cdot \alpha = \Phi \cdot \alpha $ (vague convergence) and $ \lim_{\lambda, \mathcal{F}} (\mathcal{F} \lambda)(0) = \Phi(0) $. Show that the filter $ \mathcal{F} $ converges tightly to a measure $ \mu $ such that $ \mathcal{F} \mu = \Phi $ (show by application of the Stone–Weierstrass theorem that the set of Fourier transforms of continuous functions on $ G $ with compact support is a dense linear subspace of $ C^0(\widehat{G}) $ for uniform convergence; next observe that $ \lim_{\lambda, \mathcal{F}} \int (\mathcal{F} \lambda) \cdot u \, d\alpha = \int \Phi u \, d\alpha $ for every $ u \in E $ and that the filter $ \mathcal{F} $ contains a vaguely compact set of bounded measures; deduce from this that the filter $ \mathcal{F} $ has at most one cluster point, then that it converges tightly).

c) Let $ \mathcal{F} $ be a filter on $ \mathcal{M}_+^b(G) $ *having a countable base*. In order that $ \mathcal{F} $ converge tightly to a bounded positive measure $ \mu $, it is necessary and sufficient that there exist a continuous function $ \Phi $ on $ \widehat{G} $ such that $ \mathcal{F} \lambda $ converges pointwise to $ \Phi $ with respect to $ \mathcal{F} $, in which case $ \mathcal{F} \mu = \Phi $ (*P. Lévy’s theorem*).
