Let $ I $ be a countable set, and $ (T_i)_{i \in I} $ a family of Radon spaces. Assume that every compact subset of any of the spaces $ T_i $ is metrizable. Show that the product space $ T = \prod_{i \in I} T_i $ is Radon. Generalize to the case of inverse limits.
