Let $ T_1 $ and $ T_2 $ be two topological spaces and $ f $ a bijective continuous mapping of $ T_1 $ onto $ T_2 $. Assume that, for every bounded countably additive function $ m_1 $ on the Borel tribe $ \mathcal{B}(T_1) $, there exists a sequence of compact subsets $ K_n $ of $ T_1 $ such that $ m_1(T_1 - \bigcup_n K_n) = 0 $. Let $ m_2 $ be a bounded countably additive function on $ \mathcal{B}(T_2) $. In order that there exist a bounded countably additive function $ m_1 $ on $ \mathcal{B}(T_1) $ such that $ m_2(A) = m_1(f^{-1}(A)) $ for all $ A \in \mathcal{B}(T_2) $, it is necessary and sufficient that the following condition be fulfilled: there exists a sequence of compact subsets $ K_n $ of $ T_1 $ such that $ m_2(T_2 - \bigcup_n f(K_n)) = 0 $.
