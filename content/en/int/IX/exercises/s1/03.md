Let T be a set and p an encumbrance on T. For every sequence $(f_n)_{n \geq 1}$ of elements of $ \mathcal{F}_+(T) $, $ p(\liminf_{n \to \infty} f_n) \leq \liminf_{n \to \infty} p(f_n) $.
