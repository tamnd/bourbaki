Let T₁ and T₂ be two topological spaces, and f an injective continuous mapping of T₁ into T₂. Show that the mapping $ \mu \mapsto f(\mu) $ is a bijection of $ \mathcal{M}^b(T_1) $ onto the subspace of $ \mathcal{M}^b(T_2) $ consisting of the measures carried by $ f(T_1) $.
