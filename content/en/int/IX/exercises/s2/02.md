Give an example of two topological spaces T_1 and T_2 and a bijective continuous mapping f of T_1 onto T_2, such that the mapping $ \mu \mapsto f(\mu) $ of $ \mathcal{M}_+^b(T_1) $ into $ \mathcal{M}_+^b(T_2) $ is not surjective.
