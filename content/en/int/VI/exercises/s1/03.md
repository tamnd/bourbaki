Let S, T, Y be three locally compact spaces, countable at infinity, with Y having a countable base. Let $ \rho $ (resp. $ \sigma $) be a positive measure on S (resp. T), $ \nu = \rho \otimes \sigma $, and let $ (\lambda_{s,t})_{(s,t) \in S \times T} $ be a $ \nu $-adequate$ ^1 $ family of positive measures on Y. Then, there exists a $ \rho $-negligible set N such that, for every $ s \notin N $, the family $ (\lambda_{s,t})_{t \in T} $ is $ \sigma $-adequate; the family $ s \mapsto \int \lambda_{s,t} d\sigma(t) $ (defined almost everywhere) is $ \rho $-adequate, and

$$
\iint \lambda_{s,t} d\rho(s) d\sigma(t) = \int d\rho(s) \int \lambda_{s,t} d\sigma(t).
$$

(Apply Exer. 2 to the space $ X = S \times T $, on noting that $ \nu = \int (\varepsilon_s \otimes \sigma) d\rho(s). $)
