Let $ \mu $ be Lebesgue measure on $ T = [0,1] $, $ F $ a Hilbert space having a countable orthonormal basis $ (e_n) $. Let $ f $ be the mapping of $ T $ into $ F $ such that $ f(0) = 0 $ and $ f(t) = 2^n e_n / n $ on the interval $ ]2^{-n-1}, 2^{-n}] $ ($ n \geq 0 $). Show that $ f $ is $ \mu $-measurable, scalarly $ \mu $-integrable, and that $ \int f\,d\mu \in F $, but that $ \int^* |f|\,d\mu = +\infty $.
