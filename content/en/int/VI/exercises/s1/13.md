Let $ F $ be a Hausdorff locally convex space, $ F' $ its dual, $ q $ a lower semi-continuous semi-norm on $ F $. Show that if $ f $ is a mapping of $ T $ into $ F $, $ \mu $-measurable for the topology $ \sigma(F,F') $, then the numerical function $ q \circ f $ is $ \mu $-measurable.
