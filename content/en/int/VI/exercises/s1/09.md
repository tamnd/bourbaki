Let $ G, H $ be two Hausdorff locally convex spaces, $ U $ a mapping of $ T $ into $ F = \mathcal{L}_s(G;H) $. Assume that $ G $ is barreled, $ H $ is quasi-complete, $ U $ is $ \mu $-measurable and $ U(K) $ is bounded for every compact subset $ K $ of $ T $; assume, moreover, that for every continuous semi-norm $ q $ on $ H $ and every $ x \in G $, $ \int^* q(U(t)\cdot x)\,d\mu(t) < +\infty $. Under these conditions, show that $ U $ is scalarly essentially $ \mu $-integrable and that $ \int U(t)\,d\mu(t) \in F $ (make use of Exercise 8 b)).
