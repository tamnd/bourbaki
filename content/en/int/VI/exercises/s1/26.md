Let $ (f_n) $ be a sequence of $ \mu $-integrable functions defined on T, such that for almost every $ t \in T $, $ \sup_n |f_n(t)| < +\infty $ and $ \sup_n \int |f_n(t)|\, d\mu(t) = +\infty $. Show that there exists a sequence $ (c_n) $ of scalars such that $ \sum_n |c_n| < +\infty $ and such that $ \sum_n c_n f_n $ is not $ \mu $-integrable (apply the Gelfand–Dunford theorem to the space $ L^1(\mathbf{N}) = F $.)
