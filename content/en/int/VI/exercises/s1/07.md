a) Let $ F = \overline{\mathcal{K}(\mathbf{N})} $ be the Banach space of sequences of real numbers tending to 0, $ F' = L^1(\mathbf{N}) $ its dual (TVS, IV, §1, Exer. 1). Let $ f = (f_n) $ be the mapping of $ I = [0,1] $ into $ F $ such that $ f_n(t) = n \varphi_{I_n}(t) $, where $ I_n = [0,1/n] $. Show that $ f $ is scalarly integrable for the Lebesgue measure $ \mu $ on $ I $, but that $ \int f\,d\mu $ is an element of $ F'' $ not in $ F $.

b) Let $ g $ be the mapping of $ J = [-1,+1] $ into $ F $ defined by the conditions $ g(t) = f(t) $ for $ t \geq 0 $, $ g(t) = -f(-t) $ for $ t \leq 0 $. Then $ g $ is scalarly integrable for Lebesgue measure on $ J $, and $ \int g\,d\mu \in F $, but there exist functions $ h \in \mathcal{L}^\infty $ such that $ \int hg\,d\mu \notin F $.

c) Deduce from a) a new proof of the fact that the topological vector space $ F $ is not isomorphic to the dual of a normed space (TVS, IV, §2, Exer. 22 c)).
