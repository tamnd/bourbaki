Let $ \mu $ be Lebesgue measure on $ T = [0,1] $, and let $ F $ be a Hilbert space having an orthonormal basis $ (e_t)_{t \in T} $ equipotent to $ T $.

a) Let $ f $ be the mapping $ t \mapsto e_t $ of $ T $ into $ F $. Show that $ f $ is scalarly $ \mu $-negligible, but is not $ \mu $-measurable when $ F $ is equipped with the weak topology $ \sigma(F,F') $ (nor, *a fortiori*, when $ F $ is equipped with its original topology).

b) Let $ A $ be a non-measurable set in $ T $ (Ch. IV, §4, Exer. 8); show that the function $ g = f \varphi_A $ is scalarly $ \mu $-negligible, but that the numerical function $ |g| $ is not $ \mu $-measurable.
