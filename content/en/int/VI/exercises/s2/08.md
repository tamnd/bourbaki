Let m be a vectorial measure on T with values in F, q-majorizable for a lower semi-continuous semi-norm q on F. Show that for every function $ f \geq 0 $ of $ \mathcal{H}(T) $,

$$
\int f\, d(q \circ m) = \sup \sum_i q(m(f_i)),
$$

where $ (f_i) $ runs over the set of all finite sequences of elements of $ \mathcal{H}(T) $ such that $ \sum_i |f_i| \leq f $. (Argue as in Th. 1 of Ch. II, §2, No. 2.)
