Let F be a semi-reflexive locally convex space, m a measure on T with values in F. Show that for every numerical function g locally integrable for m, the mapping $ f \mapsto \int fg\, dm $ of $ \mathcal{H}(T) $ into F is a measure on T, denoted $ g \cdot m $; in order that a numerical function h be essentially integrable for $ g \cdot m $, it is necessary and sufficient that $ gh $ be essentially integrable for m, in which case $ \int h\, d(g \cdot m) = \int hg\, dm $.
