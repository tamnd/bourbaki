Let X be a locally compact space, $ f $ a numerical function $ \geqslant 0 $ defined on X. In order that the mapping $ \mu \mapsto \mu^*(f) $ of $ \mathcal{M}_+(X) \cap \mathcal{C}'(X; \mathbf{C}) $ into $ \overline{\mathbf{R}} $ be continuous for the topology of compact convergence, it is necessary and sufficient that $ f $ be continuous on X.
