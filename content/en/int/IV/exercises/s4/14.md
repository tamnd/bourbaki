Let $ (A_n) $ be a sequence of integrable sets such that $ \sum_n \mu(A_n) < +\infty $. For every integer $ k $, let $ G_k $ be the set of $ x \in X $ such that $ x \in A_n $ for at least $ k $ values of $ n $; show that $ G_k $ is integrable and that $ k \cdot \mu(G_k) \leqslant \sum_n \mu(A_n) $.
