a) For a subset H of 𝒮_F to be precompact, it is necessary and sufficient that, for every ε > 0 and every integrable set A ⊂ X, there exist a compact set M ⊂ F and a partition of A into a finite number of integrable sets A_i such that, for every f ∈ H, there exists an integrable set B ⊂ A of measure μ(B) ≤ ε and having the following properties: 1° every point of f(A − B) has distance ≤ ε from M; 2° in each of the sets A_i ∩ C B, the oscillation of f is ≤ ε.
   b) Show that for a subset H of ℒ_F^1 to be relatively quasi-compact, it is necessary and sufficient that it be precompact in 𝒮_F and equi-integrable.
   c) For a sequence (f_n) of functions in ℒ_F^1 to be a Cauchy sequence for the topology of convergence in mean, show that it is necessary and sufficient that (f_n) be a Cauchy sequence for the topology of convergence in measure and that the set of the f_n be equi-integrable.
   d) Extend these properties to the spaces ℒ_F^p for p > 1.
