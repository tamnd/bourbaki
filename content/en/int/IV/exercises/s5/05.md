Let $ (f_{mn}) $ be a double sequence of measurable mappings of X into a metrizable space F. Assume that for every m, the sequence $ (f_{mn})_{n \geq 1} $ converges locally almost everywhere to a function $ g_m $, and that the sequence $ (g_m) $ converges locally almost everywhere to a function h. Show that for every compact subset K of X, there exist two strictly increasing sequences $ (m_k), (n_k) $ of integers $ > 0 $, such that the sequence of functions $ f_{m_k, n_k} $ converges to h almost everywhere in K. (Note that for every $ \varepsilon > 0 $, there exists a compact set $ K_1 \subset K $ such that $ \mu(K - K_1) \leq \varepsilon $ and such that in $ K_1 $ the sequence $ (g_m) $ and each of the sequences $ (f_{mn})_{n \geq 1} $ are uniformly convergent.)
