Let X be the interval [0,1] of ℝ, μ the Lebesgue measure on X. Show that for every Banach space F, every continuous linear form on 𝒮_F is identically zero. (For every neighborhood V of 0 in 𝒮_F, show that there exists an integer n > 0 such that V + V + ... + V (n terms) contains a line.)
