Let $ f $ be a measurable mapping of $ X $ into a Banach space $ F $. For every rational integer $ n $ (positive or negative) let $ A_n $ be the set of $ x \in X $ such that $ 2^n \geqslant |f(x)| > 2^{n-1} $. For $ f $ to be integrable, it is necessary and sufficient that the series with general term $ 2^n \mu(A_n) $ ($ n \in \mathbf{Z} $) be convergent.
