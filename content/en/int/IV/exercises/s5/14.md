Let $ (A_n) $ be a sequence of subsets of $ X $ such that, for every index $ n $, there exists a measurable set $ B_n \supset A_n $, the $ B_n $ being pairwise disjoint. Show that

$$
\mu^*\left( \bigcup_n A_n \right) = \sum_n \mu^*(A_n) \quad \text{and} \quad \mu_*\left( \bigcup_n A_n \right) = \sum_n \mu_*(A_n).
$$
