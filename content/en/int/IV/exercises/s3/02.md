Show that every numerical function $ f $ belonging to $ \mathcal{L}^p(X; \mathbf{R}) $ is equal almost everywhere to the difference $ g_1 - g_2 $ of two lower semi-continuous positive functions belonging to $ \mathcal{L}^p(X; \mathbf{R}) $ (note that $ f(x) $ is equal almost everywhere to the sum of an absolutely convergent series $ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n(x) $, where the $ f_n $ are continuous with compact support).
