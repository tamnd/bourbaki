Let $(u_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L}$ be a family of elements of $A[[X_i]]_{i \in I}$. If the family $(1 + u_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L}$ is multipliable, the family $(u_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L}$ is summable.
