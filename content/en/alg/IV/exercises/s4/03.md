Let F be a finite set of integers > 0, and let $ \beta_n $ be the number of finite sequences $(x_i)$ of at most $ n $ terms, all of whose terms lie in F and such that $ \sum x_i = n $. Show that the formal power series $ \sum_{n=0}^m \beta_n X^n $ over $ Q $ is the expansion of the rational fraction
$$
\frac{1}{1 - \sum_{a \in F} X^a}
$$
