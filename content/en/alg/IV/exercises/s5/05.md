Let Γ(Z) be the gamma algebra of the Z-module Z (Exercise 2). For each p ∈ N put T^{(p)} = γ_p(1).
a) Show that (T^{(p)})_{p ∈ N} is a basis of the Z-module Γ(Z). Show that there exists a unique homomorphism θ of the algebra Γ(Z) into the algebra Γ(Z) ⊗ Γ(Z) such that

$$\theta(T^{(p)}) = p! \; T^{(p)} \otimes T^{(p)}$$

for all p ∈ N.
b) Let B be a commutative ring and H the set of homomorphisms of the ring Γ(Z) into the ring B. Show that for every f ∈ H the sequence f(T^{(p)}), p ∈ N is a sequence of exponential type of elements of B. Show that the mapping φ : f ↦ (f(T^{(p)}))_{p ∈ N} is a bijection of H onto 𝒟(B) (Exercise 1).
c) For f, g ∈ H denote by f \* g the homomorphism of Γ(Z) ⊗ Γ(Z) into B defined by (f \* g)(u ⊗ v) = f(u)g(v) for any u, v ∈ Γ(Z). Verify that for any f, g ∈ H we have

$$
\begin{align*}
φ(f) + φ(g) &= φ((f * g) \circ Δ), \\
φ(f) φ(g) &= φ((f * g) \circ θ).
\end{align*}
$$
