(Divided powers of a series.) The notation is as in Exercises 10 and 11. Let E and F be two k-modules and a a series over E with values in F. Denote by a the homogeneous polynomial law of degree 1 of $ \Gamma(E) $ into F defined by a. For any integers p, $ m \in \mathbf{N} $ we denote by $ \sigma_m^{(p)} $ the series associated with the polynomial law $ \gamma_p \circ a \circ (\gamma_0 + \gamma_1 + ... + \gamma_r) $ (cf. Exercise 10).
a) Show that as m tends to $ \infty $, the sequence $ \sigma_m^{(p)} $ converges in $ S(E, \Gamma_p(F)) $ to a series $ \sigma^{(p)} $. This series $ \sigma^{(p)} $ is called the divided p-th power of the series a.

b) Show that if $ (x_i)_i $, is a family of elements of E and $ \eta \in \mathbf{N}^{(1)} $, then
$$
\sigma^{(p)}(\gamma_\eta(x)) = \sum_{\xi \in \mathcal{L}} \prod_{a \in \mathbf{N}^{(1)}} \gamma_{\xi(a)}(\sigma(\gamma_a(x))) ,
$$
where $ \mathcal{L} $ is the set of all mappings with finite support of $ \mathbf{N}^{(1)} $ into $ \mathbf{N} $ satisfying the conditions $ \sum \xi(a) = p $ and $ \sum \xi(a) a = \eta $ (Apply Ex. 10, a)).

c) Show that if $ a, \tau \in S(E, F) $, then for each $ p \in \mathbf{N} $ we have
$$
(\sigma + \tau)^{(p)} = \sum_{r+s=p} \sigma^{(r)} \sigma^{(s)}
$$
(product calculated in the algebra $ S(E, \Gamma(E)) $, cf. Exercise 12).

d) Show that for every integer $ p \in \mathbf{N} $ we have $ \sigma^p = p! \, \sigma^{(p)} $, where $ \sigma^p $ is the p-th power of a in the algebra $ S(E, \Gamma(F)) $.

e) Let $ A_+ $ and $ A_- $ be the respective coproducts in $ \Gamma(E) $ and $ \Gamma(F) $. Show that for all $ p \in \mathbf{N} $ we have
$$
A_+ \circ \sigma^{(p)} = \sum_{r+s=p} (\sigma^{(r)} \otimes \sigma^{(s)}) \circ \Delta_E .
$$
