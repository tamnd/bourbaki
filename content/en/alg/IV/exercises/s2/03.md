Suppose that $A$ is an integral domain and let $B$ be an infinite subset of $A$. Show that if the polynomial $f \in A[X]$ has degree $> 0$, then the image of $B$ under the polynomial function $x \mapsto f(x)$ is equipotent with $B$.
