Let $M$ be an $A$-module, and let $\alpha_0, ..., \alpha_n \in A$ be such that for $i \neq j$ the homothety with respect to $\alpha_i - \alpha_j$ in $M$ is bijective. For any $m_0, ..., m_n \in M$ there exists one and only one $f \in M[X]$ such that $f(\alpha_i) = m_i$ for $0 \leq i \leq n$ and $\deg f \leq n$.
