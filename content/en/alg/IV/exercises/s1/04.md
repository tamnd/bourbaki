a) Suppose that for every non-zero $ n \in \mathbf{Z} $, the relation $ n\xi = 0 $ implies $ \xi = 0 $ in A. Show that the mapping $ f \mapsto f(D_1, ..., D_n) $ is an injective homomorphism of the polynomial algebra $ A[X_1, ..., X_n] = E $ into the algebra (over A) of endomorphisms of the A-module E (use induction on n).
b) Let m be an integer $ \geqslant 1 $ such that $ m \cdot 1 = 0 $ in A. Show that we have $ D_i^m = 0 $ for every partial derivation $ D_i $ ($ 1 \leqslant i \leqslant n $) in $ A[X_1, ..., X_n] $.
