Let K be a commutative field. Show that

$$
[K(T):K] = \operatorname{Sup}(\operatorname{Card}(K), \operatorname{Card}(N))
$$

(Use the linear independence of the $1/(T - a)$, $a \in K$, to show that
$$
[K(T) : K] \geq \operatorname{Card}(K)
$$)
