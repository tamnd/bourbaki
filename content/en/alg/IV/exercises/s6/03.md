Let $ f $ be a symmetric polynomial of $ K[X_1, \ldots, X_n] $ and $ \varphi $ the unique polynomial of $ K[Y_1, \ldots, Y_n] $ such that $ f(X_1, \ldots, X_n) = \varphi(s_1, \ldots, s_n) $. Show that the total degree of $ \varphi $ is equal to the degree of $ f $ in any one of the $ X_i $.
