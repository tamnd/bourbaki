There exists a unique polynomial $P_{p,q}(T_0, \ldots, T_p, U_0, \ldots, U_q)$ with integer coefficients such that for every ring A and any polynomials $f = t_p X^p + \ldots + t_0, g = u_q X^q + \ldots + u_0$ of $\mathbf{A}[X]$ we have

$$
\operatorname{res}_{p,q}(f, g) = P_{p,q}(t_0, \ldots, t_p, u_0, \ldots, u_q).
$$

If we assign the weight i to $T_i$ and $U_i$, then $P_{p,q}$ is isobaric of weight pq.
