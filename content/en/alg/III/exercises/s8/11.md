Let $\Delta$ be the determinant of a square matrix $X$ of order $n$ and $\Delta_p$ the determinant of the square matrix $\hat{\wedge}^p X$, of order $\binom{n}{p}$. Show that

$$
\Delta_p \Delta_{n-p} = \Delta^{(n)}_{(p)}
$$

(use formulae (21) and (22) of no. 6).
