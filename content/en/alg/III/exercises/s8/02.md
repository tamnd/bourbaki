Let $\Delta = \det(\alpha_{ij})$ be the determinant of a matrix of order n; for
$$
1 \leq i \leq n-1
$$
and $1 \leq j \leq n-1$, we write
$$
\beta_{ij} = \begin{vmatrix}
\alpha_{1j} & \alpha_{1,j+1} \\
\alpha_{i+1,j} & \alpha_{i+1,j+1}
\end{vmatrix}.
$$
Show that the determinant $\det(\beta_{ij})$ of order $n-1$ is equal to
$$
\alpha_{12}\alpha_{13}\cdots\alpha_{1,n-1}\Delta.
$$
