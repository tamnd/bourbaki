Show that, if $X$ is a matrix with $n$ rows and $m$ columns, $Y$ a matrix with $p$ rows and $n$ columns and $Z = YX$ the product matrix with $p$ rows and $m$ columns, the minors of $Z$ of order $q$ are zero if $n < q$; if $q \leq n$, they are given by

$$
\det(Z_{L,H}) = \sum_K \det(Y_{L,K}) \det(X_{K,H})
$$

where K runs through the set of subsets of $\{1, n\}$ with $q$ elements (use formula (3) of § 7, no. 2).
