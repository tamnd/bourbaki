In the notation of Exercise 11, let B be the quotient ring
$$
A/(AX^2 + AY^2 + AZ^2)
$$
and let G be the B-module $E \otimes_A B$. Show that in $T^2(G)$, the module $\mathfrak{g}_2''$ is distinct from the kernel of the endomorphism $z \mapsto az$ (method analogous to that of Exercise 11).
