If $ M $ is a monogenous $ A $-module, then $ S(M) = T(M) $. Deduce an example of an injective homomorphism $ j : N \to M $ such that $ S(j) : S(N) \to S(M) $ is not injective ($ \S 5 $, Exercise 1).
