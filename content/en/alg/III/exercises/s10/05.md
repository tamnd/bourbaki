Let K be a field of characteristic $ p > 0 $, B the field $ K(X) $ of rational functions in one indeterminate and A the subfield $ K(X^p) $ of B. Show that the canonical homomorphism $ \Omega_K(A) \otimes_A B \to \Omega_K(B) $ is not injective.*
