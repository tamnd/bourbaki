Show that there exists no polynomial identity in 6 indeterminates

$$
(X_1^2 + X_2^2 + X_3^2)(Y_2^2 + Y_2^2 + Y_3^2) = P^2 + Q^2 + R^2
$$

where $P, Q, R$ are three polynomials with coefficients in $\mathbf{Z}$ with respect to the indeterminates $X_i$ and $Y_i$. (Observe that $15 = 3.5$ cannot be written in the form $p^2 + q^2 + r^2$, where $p, q, r$ are integers.)
