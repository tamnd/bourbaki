Prove the polynomial identity in 8 indeterminates

$$
(X_1^2 + X_2^2 + X_3^2 + X_4^2)(Y_1^2 + Y_2^2 + Y_3^2 + Y_4^2)
= (X_1 Y_1 - X_2 Y_2 - X_3 Y_3 - X_4 Y_4)^2
+ (X_1 Y_2 + X_2 Y_1 + X_3 Y_4 - X_4 Y_3)^2
+ (X_1 Y_3 + X_3 Y_1 + X_4 Y_2 - X_2 Y_4)^2
+ (X_1 Y_4 + X_4 Y_1 + X_2 Y_3 - X_3 Y_2)^2.
$$

(Apply formula (32) of no. 5 to a suitable quaternion algebra.)
