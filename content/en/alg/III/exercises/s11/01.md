If V is a finitely generated projective A-module, so is End(V), canonically identified with $ V \otimes_A V^* $ (II, § 4, no. 2, Corollary to Proposition 2). If every element $ u \in \mathrm{End}(V) $ is associated with the A-linear form $ v \mapsto \mathrm{Tr}(uv) $ on $ \mathrm{End}(V) $, an A-linear bijection is defined of $ \mathrm{End}(V) $ onto its dual $ (\mathrm{End}(V))^* $. Identifying $ \mathrm{End}(V) $ and its dual A-module under this mapping, the A-algebra structure on $ \mathrm{End}(V) $ defines by duality (no. 1, Example 3) an A-cogebra structure on $ \mathrm{End}(V) $, which is coassociative and has as counit the form $ \mathrm{Tr}: V \to A $. In particular, for $ V = A^n $, a cogebra structure is defined on $ \mathbf{M}_n(A) $ for which the coproduct is given by $ c(E_{ij}) = \sum_k E_{kj} \otimes E_{ik} $. This cogebra structure and the algebra structure on $ \mathbf{M}_n(A) $ do not define a bigebra structure.
