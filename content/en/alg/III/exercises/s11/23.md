Let C be a cogebra over a ring A. A sub-A-module S of C is called a *subcogebra* of C if $ c(S) \subset \operatorname{Im}(S \otimes S) $.

(a) If C is coassociative (resp. cocommutative), so is every subcogebra. If $ \gamma $ is a counit of C, the restriction of $ \gamma $ to any subcogebra of C is a counit.

(b) Which are the subcogebras of the cogebra $ A^{(X)} $ (no. 1, *Example 4*)?

(c) If $ (S_\alpha) $ is a family of subcogebras of C, $ \sum_\alpha S_\alpha $ is a subcogebra of C (the smallest containing all the $ S_\alpha $).
