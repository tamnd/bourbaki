In a projective plane over a field K with at least three elements, let $s, a, b, c, a', b', c'$ be seven distinct points such that $\{s, a, b, c\}$ and $\{s, a', b', c'\}$ are projective bases (Exercise 10) and the lines $D_{sa}, D_{sb}, D_{sc}$ pass respectively through $a', b', c'$. Show that the intersection points $r$ of $D_{ab}$ and $D_{a'b'}, p$ of $D_{bc}$ and $D_{b'c'}$, $q$ of $D_{ca}$ and $D_{c'a'}$ lie on a straight line ("Desargues's theorem"; method analogous to that of Exercise 14).
