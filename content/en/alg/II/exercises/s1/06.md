Given an A-module E, let $ \gamma(E) $ denote the least of the cardinals of the generating systems of E.

(a) If $ 0 \to E \to F \to G \to 0 $ is an exact sequence of A-modules, then
$$
\gamma(G) \leq \gamma(F) \leq \gamma(E) + \gamma(G).
$$
Give an example of a product $ F = E \times G $ such that $ \gamma(E) = \gamma(F) = \gamma(G) = 1 $ (take E and G to be quotients of $ \mathbf{Z} $).

(b) If E is the sum of a family $ (F_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L} $ of submodules, then $ \gamma(E) \leq \sum_{\lambda \in L} \gamma(F_\lambda) $.

(c) If M and N are submodules of a module E, show that
$$
\sup(\gamma(M), \gamma(N)) \leq \gamma(M \cap N) + \gamma(M + N).
$$
