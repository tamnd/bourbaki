Let E be a $\mathbf{Z}$-module the direct sum of two submodules M, N respectively isomorphic to $\mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z}$ and $\mathbf{Z}/3\mathbf{Z}$; show that there exists no other decomposition of E as a direct sum of two non-zero submodules.
