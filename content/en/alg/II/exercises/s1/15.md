Let c be an infinite cardinal.

(a) Let I be a set and R $ \{a, b\} $ a relation between elements of I such that for all $ \alpha \in I $ the set of $ \beta \in I $ for which $ R \{ \alpha, \beta \} $ holds has cardinal $ \leq c $. Show that there exists a well-ordered set L and an increasing family $ (I_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L} $ of subsets of I such that: (1) there exists a $ \lambda \in L $ such that $ I_\lambda = \varnothing $; (2) I is the union of the $ I_\lambda $ for $ \lambda \in L $; (3) if $ \lambda \in L $ is such that the set of $ \mu < \lambda $ has a greatest element $ \lambda' $, $ \mathrm{Card}(I_\lambda - I_{\lambda'}) \leq c $; (4) if $ \lambda \in L $ is the least upper bound of the set of $ \mu < \lambda $, $ I_\lambda $ is the union of the $ I_\mu $ for $ \mu < \lambda $; (5) if $ \alpha \in I_\lambda $ and $ R \{ \alpha, \beta \} $ holds, then $ \beta \in I_\lambda $. (Note first that for all $ \alpha \in I $ the set of $ \beta \in I $ for which there exists an integer n and a sequence $ (\gamma_j)_{1 \leq j \leq n} $ of elements of I such that $ \gamma_1 = \alpha $, $ \gamma_n = \beta $ and $ R \{ \gamma_j, \gamma_{j+1} \} $ holds for $ 1 \leq j \leq n-1 $, has cardinal $ \leq c $. Then construct the $ I_\lambda $ by transfinite induction.)

(b) Let E be an A-module the direct sum of a family $ (M_\alpha)_{\alpha \in I} $ of submodules such that $ \gamma(M_\alpha) \leq c $ for all $ \alpha \in I $ (cf. Exercise 6). Let f be an endomorphism of E. Show that there exists a well-ordered set L and an increasing family $ (I_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L} $ of subsets of I satisfying properties (1), (2), (3) and (4) of (a) and such

(c) With the hypotheses and notation of (b), suppose further that $ f $ is a projector and write $ P = f(E) $. Let $ P_\lambda = P \cap E_\lambda $; show that if $ \lambda \in L $ is such that the set of $ \mu < \lambda $ has a greatest element $ \lambda' $, $ P_{\lambda'} $ is a direct factor of $ P_\lambda $ (cf. Exercise 12), that a supplementary submodule $ P'_\lambda $ of $ P_{\lambda'} $ in $ P_\lambda $ is isomorphic to a direct factor of $ F_\lambda $ and that $ \gamma(P'_\lambda) \leq c $. Show that $ P $ is the direct sum of the $ P'_\lambda $ (apply Exercise 14).

(d) Deduce from (c) that if an A-module E is the direct sum of a family $ (M_\alpha)_{\alpha \in I} $ of submodules such that $ \gamma(M_\alpha) \leq c $, then every direct factor P of E is also the direct sum of a family $ (N_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L} $ of submodules such that $ \gamma(N_\lambda) \leq c $.
