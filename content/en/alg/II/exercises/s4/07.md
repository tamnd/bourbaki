Show that, if $ E_1, E_2, F_1, F_2 $ are four modules over a commutative ring

C, the canonical homomorphism (21) (no. 4) is composed of the homomorphisms
$$
\operatorname{Hom}(E_1, F_1) \otimes \operatorname{Hom}(E_2, F_2) \to \operatorname{Hom}(E_1, F_2 \otimes \operatorname{Hom}(E_2, F_2)) \\
\to \operatorname{Hom}(E_1, \operatorname{Hom}(E_2, F_1 \otimes F_2)) \\
\to \operatorname{Hom}(E_1 \otimes E_2, F_1 \otimes F_2)
$$
where the first two homomorphisms come from the canonical homomorphism (7) (no. 2) and the last is the isomorphism of no. 1, Proposition 1.
