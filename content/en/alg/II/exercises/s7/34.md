Let $ A $ be an integral domain, $ P $ a projective $ A $-module, $ P' $ a projective submodule of $ P $ and $ j : P' \to P $ the canonical injection. Show that for every torsion-free $ A $-module $ E $, the homomorphism $ j \otimes 1 : P' \otimes_A E \to P \otimes_A E $ is injective (reduce it to the case where $ E $ is finitely generated and embed $ E $ in a free $ A $-module).
