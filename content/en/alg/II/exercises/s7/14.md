Let E, F, G be three vector spaces and $u : E \to F, w : E \to G$ two linear mappings. Show that if $u^{-1}(0) \subset w^{-1}(0)$, there exists a linear mapping v of F into G such that $w = v \circ u$ (cf. § 2, Exercise 8).
