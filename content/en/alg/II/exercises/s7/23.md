Show that for a linear mapping $u$ of a vector space $E$ into a vector space $F$ to be such that $\mathrm{rg}({}^t u) = \mathrm{rg}(u)$, it is necessary and sufficient that $\mathrm{rg}(u)$ be finite (cf. Exercise 3 (d)).
