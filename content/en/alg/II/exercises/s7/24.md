Let $ E $ be a vector space of finite dimension $ n > 1 $ over a commutative field $ K $; show that, unless $ n = 2 $ and $ K $ is a field with two elements, there exists no isomorphism $ \phi $ of $ E $ onto $ E^* $ depending only on the vector space structure of $ E $. (Note that if $ \phi $ is such an isomorphism, then necessarily $ \langle x, \phi(y) \rangle = \langle u(x), \phi(u(y)) \rangle $ for $ x, y $ in $ E $ and for every automorphism $ u $ of $ E $ (Set Theory, IV, § 1, no. 5).)
