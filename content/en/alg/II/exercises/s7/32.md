Extend the results of no. 10 to the case where $ A $ is a non-commutative ring admitting a *field of left fractions* $ K $ (I, § 9, Exercise 15). (Note that if $ \xi_i $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq n $) are elements of $ K $, there exists an $ \alpha \neq 0 $ in $ A $ such that all the $ \alpha \xi_i $ belong to $ A $.)
