Let E, F be two vector spaces over a field K and $u : E \to F$ a linear mapping. Show that for every vector subspace V of F,
$$
\dim(u^{-1}(V)) = \dim(V \cap \mathrm{Im}(u)) + \dim(\mathrm{Ker}(u)).
$$
