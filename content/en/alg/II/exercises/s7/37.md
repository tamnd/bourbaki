(a) Let $ E $ be a left vector space (finite-dimensional or otherwise) and $ A_i $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq n $) vector subspaces of $ E $. Suppose that, for every sequence $ (a_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n} $ of points of $ E $ such that $ a_i \in A_i $ for all $ i $, the vector subspace of $ E $ generated by the $ a_i $ is of dimension $ \leq m $ ($ m $ an integer $ \leq n $). Then prove that there exists a subspace $ W $ of $ E $ of dimension $ h \leq m $, containing $ h + (n - m) $ of the subspaces $ A_i $. (Argue by induction on $ n $. Prove first that (for fixed $ n $) attention may be confined to the case where $ m < n $ and $ \dim(A_i) \leq m $ for all $ i $; it may also be assumed that $ A_i \neq \{0\} $ for all $ i $ and that the $ A_i $ are not all of dimension 1. Then argue (for fixed $ n $) by induction on $ d = \sum_{i=1}^m \dim(A_i) $. If for example $ \dim(A_n) \geq 2 $, consider a 1-dimensional subspace $ B_n $ of $ A_n $ of dimension 1 and apply the induction hypothesis to $ A_1, \ldots, A_{n-1}, B_n $; conclude that there exists a number $ k $ such that $ 1 \leq k \leq m $ and a $ k $-dimensional subspace $ U $ of $ E $ containing $ k $ of the $ A_i $, for example $ A_1, \ldots, A_k $; replacing, if need be, $ k $ by an integer $ k' $ such that $ 1 \leq k' \leq k $, show also that it can be assumed that there exist vectors $ b_i \in A_i $ for $ 1 \leq i \leq k $ such that $ b_1, \ldots, b_k $ form a basis of $ U $; then project onto a supplementary subspace of $ U $ in $ E $ and use the induction hypothesis.)

(b) Let $ K $ be a finite field and let $ E $ be an $ N $-dimensional vector space over $ K $. Let $ U_i $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq n $) be vector subspaces of $ E $ such that, for every subset $ H $ of $ \{1, n\} $, the intersection of the $ U_i $ such that $ i \in H $ has dimension $ \leq N - \mathrm{Card}(H) $. Show that the union of the $ U_i $ cannot be equal to $ E $ (argue by duality using (a)).
