Show that if E is a vector space $ \neq \{0\} $ over a field K with an infinity of elements, the set of generating systems of E is not inductive for the order relation $ \supset $ (form a decreasing sequence $ (S_n) $ of generating systems of E such that the intersection of the $ S_n $ is empty).
