Let $ (F_n, f_{mn}) $ be the direct system of $ \mathbf{Z} $-modules such that $ F_n $ is equal to $ U_p $ (\S 1, Exercise 12 (d)) for all $ n \geq 0 $ and $ f_{mn} $ is the endomorphism $ x \mapsto p^{m-n}x $ of $ U_p $ for $ n \leq m $. Show that $ \lim \rightarrow F_n = \{0\} $ although the $ f_{mn} $ are surjective.
