Let $ (F_\alpha, f_{\beta\alpha}) $ be a direct system of A-modules.
(a) For every A-module E, define a canonical $ \mathbf{Z} $-module homomorphism
$$
\varepsilon : \lim \rightarrow \mathrm{Hom}_A(E, F_\alpha) \to \mathrm{Hom}_A(E, \lim \rightarrow F_\alpha).
$$
(b) Show that if E is finitely generated, $ \varepsilon $ is injective. Give an example where $ \varepsilon $ is not injective (cf. Exercise 3, taking $ E = \lim \rightarrow F_\alpha $).

(c) Show that if E is finitely generated and the $ f_{\beta \alpha} $ are injective, $ \varepsilon $ is surjective. Give an example where $ E = \lim_{\longrightarrow} F_\alpha $ is free, the $ f_{\beta \alpha} $ are injective and $ \varepsilon $ is not surjective.

(d) Show that if E is projective and finitely generated, $ \varepsilon $ is bijective.

*(e) Let A be an integral domain and $ a $ an ideal of A which is not finitely generated. If $ (a_\alpha) $ is the family of finitely generated ideals contained in $ a $, show that $ E = A/a $ is canonically isomorphic to the direct limit of the $ F_\alpha = A/a_\alpha $, but that the homomorphism $ \varepsilon $ is not surjective.*
