Let K be a field, A the product ring $ K^{\mathbf{N}} $, $ a $ the ideal $ K^{(\mathbf{N})} $ of A and B the ring $ A/a $. Show that $ a $ is a projective A-module which is not finitely generated, although $ a_{(B)} = \{0\} $.
