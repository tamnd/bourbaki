Let $\mathbf{A}$ be the quotient ring $\mathbf{Z}/30\mathbf{Z}$. Show that in the matrix
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & -1 \\
0 & 2 & 3
\end{pmatrix}
$$
over the ring $\mathbf{A}$, the two rows are linearly independent but any two columns are linearly dependent.
