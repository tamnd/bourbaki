Let $ \mathbf{A} $ be a ring, $ \mathbf{C} $ its centre, $ \mathbf{B} $ the ring of matrices $ \mathbf{M}_n(\mathbf{A}) $, $ \Delta $ the additive subgroup of $ \mathbf{A} $ generated by the elements $ \alpha \beta - \beta \alpha $ for $ \alpha \in \mathbf{A},\ \beta \in \mathbf{A} $, and $ \mathbf{D} $ the additive subgroup of $ \mathbf{B} $ generated by the matrices $ XY - YX $ for $ X \in \mathbf{B},\ Y \in \mathbf{B} $; $ \Delta $ and $ \mathbf{D} $ are $ \mathbf{C} $-modules. For every matrix $ X = (\xi_{ij}) \in \mathbf{B} $, let $ \theta(X) $ be the element $ \sum_{i=1}^n \bar{\xi}_{ij} $ of $ \mathbf{A}/\Delta $, where, for all $ \alpha \in \mathbf{A} $, $ \bar{\alpha} $ denotes the class of $ \alpha $ mod. $ \Delta $. Show that $ \theta $ is a surjective homomorphism of $ \mathbf{B} $ onto $ \mathbf{A}/\Delta $, whose kernel is equal to $ \mathbf{D} $, so that $ \mathbf{B}/\mathbf{D} $ is isomorphic to $ \mathbf{A}/\Delta $ as a $ \mathbf{C} $-module. (Observe that $ \mathbf{D} $ contains the matrices $ \alpha E_{ij} $ and the matrices $ \alpha(E_{ii} - E_{jj}) $ for $ \alpha \in \mathbf{A} $ and $ i \neq j $.)
