Let $ X $ be a matrix with $ m $ rows and $ n $ columns over a field $ K $; if $ r $ is the rank of $ X $, show that the rank of a submatrix with $ m $ rows and $ s $ columns, obtained by suppressing $ n - s $ columns of $ X $, is $ \geq r + s - n $.
