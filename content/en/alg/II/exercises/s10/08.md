Let $ X = (\alpha_{ij}) $ be a matrix with $ m $ rows and $ n $ columns over a field $ K $. For $ X $ to be of rank 1, it is necessary and sufficient that there exist in $ K $ a family $ (\lambda_i)_{1 \leq i \leq m} $ of $ m $ elements not all zero and a family $ (\mu_j)_{1 \leq j \leq n} $ of $ n $ elements not all zero such that $ \alpha_{ij} = \lambda_i \mu_j $ for every ordered pair of indices.
