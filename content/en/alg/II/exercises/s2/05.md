Let u be a linear mapping from an A-module E to an A-module F and $v = {}^t u$ its transpose. For every submodule N' of F*, the submodule of E orthogonal to $v(N')$ is ${}^{-1}u(N)$, where N is the submodule of F orthogonal to N'.
