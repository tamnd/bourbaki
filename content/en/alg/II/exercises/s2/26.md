For every $\mathbf{Z}$-module $G$ and every integer $n > 0$, let $_n G$ denote the kernel of the endomorphism $x \mapsto nx$ of $G$. If $0 \to G' \to G \to G'' \to 0$ is an exact sequence, define a canonical homomorphism $d : _n G'' \to G'/nG'$ such that the sequence

$$
0 \longrightarrow _n G' \longrightarrow _n G'' \xrightarrow{d} G'/nG' \longrightarrow G/nG \longrightarrow G''/nG'' \longrightarrow 0
$$

is exact. If $_n G$ and $G/nG$ are finite, we write

$$
h_n(G) = \operatorname{Card}(G/nG) - \operatorname{Card}(_n G);
$$

then show that $_n G'$, $_n G''$, $G'/nG'$, $G''/nG''$ are finite and that

$$
h_n(G) = h_n(G') + h_n(G'').
$$
