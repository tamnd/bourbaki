Let $ E $ be a right $ A $-module and $ F $ a free left $ A $-module. Show that if $ x $ is a free element of $ E $ and $ y $ an element $ \neq 0 $ of $ F $, then $ x \otimes y \neq 0 $; if also $ A $ is commutative and $ y $ is a free element of $ F $, then $ x \otimes y $ is free in the $ A $-module $ E \otimes_A F $.
