Let K be a field of finite rank over its centre Z and L a subfield of K containing Z.

(a) Show that the left and right vector space structures of K with respect to L have the same dimension.

(b) Show that properties (a), (b), (c) of Exercise 3 also hold in this case (if $ u_0 $ is a linear form $ \neq 0 $ on $ L_Z $ and $ v_0 $ a linear form $ \neq 0 $ on $ K_L $, note that $ \xi \cdot (u_0 \circ v_0) = u_0 \circ (\xi v_0) $ for $ \xi \in K $, that $ \xi \cdot (u_0 \circ v_0) $ runs through the dual $(K_Z)^*$ when $ \xi $ runs through K and use Exercise 3 (c)).
