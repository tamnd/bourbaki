In the notation of Exercise 7, suppose A is finite; show that A contains one and only one idempotent ($ \S 1 $, no. 4) (observe that if $ x^p $ and $ x^q $ are idempotents, then $ x^p = x^{pq}, x^q = x^{pq} $, hence $ x^p = x^q $); if $ h = x^p $, the set of $ x^n $ for $ n \geq p $ is a stable subset D of E such that under the law induced on D, D is a group.
