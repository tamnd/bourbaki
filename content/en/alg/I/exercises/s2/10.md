If $ a $ and $ b $ are two elements of E such that $ ba $ is left cancellable, show that $ a $ is left cancellable. Deduce that under an associative and commutative law on E the set S of non-cancellable elements of E is such that $ ES \subset S $ (and in particular is stable).
