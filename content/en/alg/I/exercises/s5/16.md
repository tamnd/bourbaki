(a) Show that the alternating group $ \mathfrak{A}_n $ is $ (n-2) $-ply transitive.

(b) Show that $ \mathfrak{A}_n $ is a non-commutative simple group for $ n \geq 5 $. (Use (a), the method of Exercise 15 and Exercise 12(d) to prove that $ \mathfrak{A}_n $ is simple if $ n > 6 $; examine analogously the cases $ n = 5 $ and $ n = 6 $.)

(c) Show that, for $ n \geq 5 $, the only normal subgroups of $ \mathfrak{S}_n $ are $ \{e\} $, $ \mathfrak{A}_n $ and $ \mathfrak{S}_n $.
