Let $ G $ be a simple group and $ H $ a subgroup of $ G $ of finite index $ n > 1 $.
(a) Show that $ G $ is finite and that its order divides $ n! $ (use Exercise 5).
(b) Show that $ G $ is commutative if $ n \leq 4 $ (use Exercise 10).
