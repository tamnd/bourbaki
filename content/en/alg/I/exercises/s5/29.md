Let $ X $ be an infinite set. Let $ G $ be the subgroup of $ \mathfrak{S}_X $ consisting of the permutations $ \sigma $ which enjoy the following property: there exists a finite subset $ Y_\sigma $ of $ X $ such that $ \sigma x = x $ if $ x \in X - Y_\sigma $ and the restriction of $ \sigma $ to $ Y_\sigma $ is even.

(a) Show that G is a non-commutative simple group. Show that every subset of G which generates G is equipotent to X.

(b) Show that every finite group is isomorphic to a subgroup of G (use the fact that $ \mathfrak{S}_n $ is isomorphic to a subgroup of $ \mathfrak{A}_{n+2} $).
