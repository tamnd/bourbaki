Let $ G $ be a group, $ A $ and $ B $ two subgroups of $ G $ and $ \phi $ an isomorphism of $ A $ onto $ B $. Show that there exists a group $ G_1 $ containing $ G $ such that $ \phi $ is the restriction of an *inner* automorphism of $ G_1 $ (use the permutation group of the set $ G $). Show that, if $ G $ is finite, $ G_1 $ can be chosen to be finite.
