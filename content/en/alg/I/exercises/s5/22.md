Let $ A = \mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z} $, $ B = \mathfrak{S}_3 $ and $ G = A \times B $. If $ s $ is an element of $ B $ of order 2, let $ \phi $ be the endomorphism of $ G $ whose kernel is equal to $ B $ and whose image is $ \{1, s\} $. Show that the centre of $ G $ is not stable under $ \phi $.
