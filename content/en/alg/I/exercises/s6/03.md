Define extensions (cf. § 5, Exercise 10)

$$
\mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z} \times \mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z} \to \mathfrak{A}_4 \to \mathfrak{A}_3
$$
$$
\mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z} \times \mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z} \to \mathfrak{S}_4 \to \mathfrak{S}_3.
$$

Show that these extensions are non-trivial, that the first admits a section and that the second does not.
