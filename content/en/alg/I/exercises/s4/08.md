Let $ A $ and $ A' $ be two groups and $ G $ a subgroup of $ A \times A' $. We write:

$$
N = G \cap (A \times \{e\}), \qquad H = \mathrm{pr}_1(G)
$$
$$
N' = G \cap (\{e\} \times A'), \qquad H' = \mathrm{pr}_2(G).
$$

(a) Show that $ N $ is normal in $ H $ and $ N' $ is normal in $ H' $; define isomorphisms

$$
H/N \to G/(N \times N') \to H'/N'.
$$

(b) Suppose that $ H = A, H' = A' $, that the groups $ A $ and $ A' $ are of finite length and that no quotient of a Jordan-Hölder series of $ A $ is isomorphic to a quotient of a Jordan-Hölder series of $ A' $. Show that $ G = A \times A' $.
