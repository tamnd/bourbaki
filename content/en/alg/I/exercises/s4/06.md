Let $(G_\alpha)$ be a family of normal subgroups of a group $G$ such that $\bigcap_\alpha G_\alpha = \{e\}$; show that $G$ is isomorphic to a subgroup of the product group $\prod_\alpha (G/G_\alpha)$.
