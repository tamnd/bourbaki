Let $ A $ and $ B $ be two subgroups of a group $ G $.

(a) Show that the least subgroup containing $ A $ and $ B $ (that is the subgroup generated by $ A \cup B $) is identical with the set of compositions of the sequences $ (x_i)_{1 \leq i \leq 2n+1} $ of an (arbitrary) odd number of elements such that $ x_i \in A $ for $ i $ odd and $ x_i \in B $ for $ i $ even.

(b) For $ AB $ to be a subgroup of $ G $ (in which case it is the subgroup generated by $ A \cup B $), it is necessary and sufficient that $ A $ and $ B $ be permutable, that is that $ AB = BA $.

(c) If $ A $ and $ B $ are permutable and $ C $ is a subgroup containing $ A $, $ A $ is permutable with $ B \cap C $ and $ A(B \cap C) = C \cap (AB) $.
