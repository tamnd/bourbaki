Let $ \top $ and $ \perp $ be two internal laws on $ E $, each with an identity element; if the left action of $ E $ on itself derived from each of the laws is distributive with respect to the other, every element of $ E $ is idempotent under both these laws (if $ e $ is the identity element for $ \top $, $ u $ the identity element for $ \perp $, prove first that $ e \perp e = e $, noting that $ e = e \perp (u \top e) $).
