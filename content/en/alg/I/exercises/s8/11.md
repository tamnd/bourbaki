In a ring $ A $, a two-sided ideal $ a $ is said to be *irreducible* if there exists no ordered pair of two-sided ideals $ b, c $ distinct from $ a $ and such that $ a = b \cap c $.
(a) Show that the intersection of all the irreducible two-sided ideals of $ A $ reduces to 0 (note that the set of two-sided ideals containing no element $ a \neq 0 $ is inductive and apply Zorn’s Lemma).
(b) Deduce that every two-sided ideal $ a $ of $ A $ is the intersection of all the irreducible ideals which contain it.
