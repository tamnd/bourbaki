Show that the group defined by the presentation

$$
\langle x, y ; xy^2 = y^3x, yx^2 = x^3y \rangle
$$

reduces to the identity element.

(The first relation implies $x^2y^8x^{-2} = y^{18}$ and $x^3y^8x^{-3} = y^{27}$; use the second relation to deduce that $y^{18} = y^{27}$, whence $y^9 = e$; the fact that $y^2$ is conjugate to $y^3$ then implies $y = e$, whence $x = e$.)
