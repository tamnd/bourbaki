Let $G$ be a group and $X$ a subset of $G$ disjoint from $X^{-1}$. Let $S = X \cup X^{-1}$. Show the equivalence of the two following properties:
(i) $X$ is a free family in $G$.
(ii) No product $s_1 \ldots s_n$, with $n \geq 1, s_i \in S$ and $s_i s_{i+1} \neq e$ for $1 \leq i \leq n-1$, is equal to $e$.
