Let $X$ be a set with one element. If $n$ is an integer $\geqslant 1$, let $u_n$ denote the number of elements in $M(X)$ of length $n$.

(a) For every set $Y$, show that
$$
\operatorname{Card}(M_n(Y)) = u_n \cdot \operatorname{Card}(Y)^n.
$$
(Use Exercise 3.)

(b) Establish the relation
$$
u_n = \sum_{p=1}^{n-1} u_p u_{n-p} \quad \text{for } n \geqslant 2.
$$

(c) *Let $f(T)$ be the formal power series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} u_n T^n$. Show that
$$
f(T) = T + f(T)^2.
$$
Deduce the formulae
$$
f(T) = \frac{1}{2}(1 - (1 - 4T)^{1/2}), \qquad u_n = \frac{2^{n-1}}{n!} 1 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \ldots (2n-3) \quad \text{for } n \geqslant 2.*
$$
Obtain the last result using Exercise 11 of Set Theory, III, § 5.
