The group defined by the presentation $\langle x, y; x^3, y^2, (xy)^3 \rangle$ is isomorphic to $\mathfrak{A}_4$. (Show that the conjugates of $y$ form, with the identity element, a normal subgroup of order $\leq 4$ and index $\leq 3$.)
