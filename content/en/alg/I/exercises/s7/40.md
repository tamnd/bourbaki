*Let $n$ be an integer $\geq 2$ and $T_n$ the group of upper triangular matrices of order $n$ over $\mathbf{Z}$ with all the diagonal terms equal to 1. Let $e(T_n)$ be the invariant of the group $T_n$ defined in Exercise 39. Show that
$$
e(T_n) \leq \sum_{i=1}^n i(n-i).*
$$
