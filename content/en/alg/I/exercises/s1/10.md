Let X and Y be any two subsets of a set E and let $X \tau Y = X \cup Y$ if $X \cap Y = \varnothing, X \tau Y = E$ if $X \cap Y \neq \varnothing$; show that the law of composition thus defined on $\mathcal{P}(E)$ is associative and commutative.
