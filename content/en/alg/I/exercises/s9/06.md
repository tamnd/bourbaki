Let K be a commutative field of characteristic $ \neq 2 $; let f be a mapping of K into K such that $ f(x + y) = f(x) + f(y) $ for all x and y and for all $ x \neq 0 $ $ f(x)f(1/x) = 1 $. Show that f is an isomorphism of K onto a subfield of K (prove that $ f(x^2) = (f(x))^2 $).
