Let $ A $ be a commutative ring and $ \mathcal{U} $ the intersection of its maximal ideals.

(a) Show that the canonical homomorphism $ A^* \to (A/\mathcal{U})^* $ is surjective and that its kernel is $ 1 + \mathcal{U} $.

(b) Suppose that the set $ M $ of maximal ideals of $ A $ is finite and that $ A^* $ is finite. Deduce that $ A $ is finite (apply Proposition 8 of § 8, no. 10 to $ A/\mathcal{U} $).

(c) Deduce that the set of prime numbers is infinite (note that $ \mathbf{Z}^* = \{1, -1\} $).

(d) Suppose that $ A^* $ is finite and that $ A/m $ is finite for every maximal ideal $ m $. Can it be deduced that $ A $ is finite, that $ A $ is countable?
