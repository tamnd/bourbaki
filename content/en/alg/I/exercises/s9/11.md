Let $ P $ be the set of prime numbers and $ A $ the product ring of the fields $ \mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z} $, $ p \in P $. Let $ a $ be the subset of $ A $ consisting of the elements $ (a_p)_{p \in P} $ such that $ a_p \neq 0 $ only for a finite number of indices $ p $.

(a) $ a $ is an ideal of $ A $.

(b) Let $ B = A/a $. For every integer $ n > 0 $ and every $ b \neq 0 $ in $ B $, there exists one and only one element $ b' $ of $ B $ such that $ nb' = b $ (note that if $ p \in P $ does not divide $ n $, multiplication by $ n $ in $ \mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z} $ is bijective). Deduce that B contains a subfield isomorphic to $ \mathbf{Q} $.
