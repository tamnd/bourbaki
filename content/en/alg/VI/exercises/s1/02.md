Let E be a lattice with a composition law (x, y) $\mathcal{H} xy$ (not necessarily associative), such that, for all a ∈ E, the maps x $\mathcal{H} ax$ and x $\mapsto xa$ are order-automorphisms of E. Let $x_a$ (resp. $_a x$) denote the element of E defined by (x,) $a = x$ (resp. $a(_a x) = x$) and suppose that for each a ∈ E the maps x $\mathcal{H} a_x$ and x $\mathcal{H} _x a$ are isomorphisms from the ordering of E to the opposite ordering. Show that, for arbitrary x, y, z,
$$
(z_{\inf(x, y)}) \cdot x = (z_y) \cdot \sup(x, y)
$$
