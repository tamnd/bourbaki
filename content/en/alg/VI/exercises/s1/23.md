Let G be the lattice ordered group $ \mathbf{Z} \times \mathbf{Z} $ (where $ \mathbf{Z} $ has the usual ordering) and H the convex subgroup of G generated by $ (2, -3) $; show that the ordered group $ G/H $ is not lattice orderable (cf. VI, p. 30, Ex. 4).
