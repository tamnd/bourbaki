Let $x, y, z, t$ be elements of a semi-lattice ordered monoid $M$ such that $z \geq 0$ and $t \geq 0$. Prove the inequality

$$
\inf(x + z, y + t) + \inf(x, y) \geq \inf(x + z, y) + \inf(x, y + t)
$$
