Let (x,) be a family of n elements of a lattice ordered group G; for each integer k (0 ≤ k ≤ n), let d_k (resp. m_k) denote the inf (resp. sup) of the $ \binom{n}{k} $ sums of k terms x, with distinct indices. Show that $ d_k + m_{n-k} = x_1 + x_2 + \ldots + x_n $.
