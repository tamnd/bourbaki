Show that, in a semi-lattice ordered monoid $ M $.

$$
\inf(x_i + y_i) \geq \inf(x_i) + \inf(y_i)
$$

for all finite sequences of n terms $ (x_i), (y_i) $. Deduce the inequalities

$$
(x + y)^+ \leq x^+ + y^+ , \quad |x^+ - y^+| \leq |x - y|
$$

in any lattice ordered group.
