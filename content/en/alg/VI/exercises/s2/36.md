Let K be an ordered field and let $ \tilde{K} $ be the set of major sets D such that the cut (S, D) is proper ; show that $ \tilde{K} $ is an ordered field, taking addition to be that of the monoid $ \mathfrak{M}(K) $. and the product of two elements $ D_1 $ and D, of $ \tilde{K} $ contained in K, to be the set $ \langle D_1 D_2 \rangle $ (VI, p. 35, Ex. 30), where $ D_1 D_2 $ denotes the set of $ x_1 x_2 $ for $ x_i \in D_i $ and $ x_2 \in D_2 $. Show that for any increasing homomorphism f from K onto a dense subfield F of an ordered field E, there exists an increasing homomorphism g from E into $ \tilde{K} $ such that $ g \circ f $ is the natural injection of K into $ \tilde{K} $.
