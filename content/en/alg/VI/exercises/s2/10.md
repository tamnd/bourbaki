a) Let K be an orderable field and E a Galois extension of K. Show that either E is orderable or there exists an orderable algebraic extension F of K, contained in E, such that E is a quadratic extension of F (use Th. 6 of V, p. 70).

b) Show that the polynomial $ X^4 + 2 $ is irreducible over $ \mathbf{Q} $, and that the extension $ K $ of $ \mathbf{Q} $ of degree 4, obtained by adjoining a root of this polynomial to $ \mathbf{Q} $, contains no orderable subfield other than $ \mathbf{Q} $ (find all the subfields of $ K $ by Galois theory).
