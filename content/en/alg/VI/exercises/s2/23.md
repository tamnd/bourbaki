Let K be a maximal ordered field and let $a_1, \ldots, a_n, b_1, \ldots, b_n$ be 2n elements of K such that $\sum a_i \neq 0$ and $b_1 < b_2 < \ldots < b_n$. Show that for any integer $m \geq 1$ the number $v$ of roots in K, counted with multiplicity, of the polynomial

$$
f(T) = a_1 (T - b_1)^m + a_2 (T - b_2)^m + \ldots + a_n (T - b_n)^m
$$

is at most equal to the number of variations w of the sequence

$$
(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n; (-1)^m a_i),
$$

and that the difference $w - v$ is even. (Argue by induction on $n$, applying Ex. 13 (VI, p. 40) to a rational function of the form $f(T)/(T - c)^m$.)
