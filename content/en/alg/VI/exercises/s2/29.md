Let K be a maximal ordered field and let G be a subfield of K. Show that the set of extensions of G contained in K, and which are comparable to G, is inductively ordered ; if E, is a maximal element of this set, show that $ E_0 $ is isomorphic to the field $ K(G) $ defined in Ex. 11, b) of VI, p. 40 (prove that the natural map from $ F(G) $ onto $ K(G) $ maps E, onto $ K(G) $, by first showing that $ E_0 $ is a maximal ordered field, using Ex. 14, a) of VI, p. 40, and then that no element of $ K(G) $ is transcendent! over the image of $ E_0 $, using Ex. 24).
