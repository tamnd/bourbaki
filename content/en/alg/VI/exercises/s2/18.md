Let K be a maximal ordered field and let f be a polynomial of degree n in K[T], all of whose roots are in K. Show that for all $c \neq 0$ in K the polynomial $f^2 + c f'$ has at least $n - 1$ and at most $n + 1$ roots in K, counted with multiplicity (use Ex. 13).
