Let $ E $ be an ordered field, let $ K $ be a subfield of $ E $ such that $ E $ is algebraic over $ K $, and let $ R $ be a maximal ordered extension of $ K $.

a) Among the elements of E – K, let x, be one whose minimal polynomial f over K has the least possible degree. Show that $ f'(T) $ is a product of factors in R[T] of the form $ (T - a_i)' + c_i^2 $ ($ a_i, c_i \in \mathbf{R} $), and first order factors T – b, with the b, pairwise distinct elements of K. Deduce that there exists an element $ y_0 $ in R such that $ f(y_0) = 0 $ and such that $ x_0 - z $ and $ y_0 - z $ have the same sign, in E and R respectively, for all $ z \in K $ (cf. VI, p. 43. Ex. 26, c)).

b) Deduce from a) that there exists a K-isomorphism from the ordered field E onto a subfield of R. (First show that there exists such an isomorphism for the subfield $ K(x_0) $ of E ; for any polynomial $ g \in K[T] $ such that $ \deg(g) < \deg(f) $, apply the same argument used for $ f' $ in a) to evaluate the signs of $ g(x_0) $ and $ g(y_0) $; then apply Zorn's Lemma).

c) Show that if R and R' are two maximal ordered algebraic extensions of K then there exists precisely one K-isomorphism from R onto R' (apply b)).
