Let K be a maximal ordered field and f a nonzero polynomial in K[T]. A necessary and sufficient condition that, for every polynomial $ g \in K[T] $, the number of roots of $ fg + g' $, counted with multiplicity, not belonging to K, be at most equal to the number of roots of $ g $, counted with multiplicity, not belonging to K, is that $ f(T) = a - bT $ with b \geq 0 \text{ in } K. \text{ (To see that the condition is sufficient use Ex. 12; to show that it is necessary, take } g = 1 \text{ and } g = f).
