Let $ K $ be a maximal ordered field, let $ f $ be a polynomial over $ K $ and let $ a $ and $ b $ be two roots of $ F $ such that $ a < b $ and such that $ F $ has no roots between $ a $ and $ b $. Show that if $ g $ is a rational function over $ K $ whose denominator does not vanish for $ a \leq x \leq b $ then the equation $ f(x)g(x) + f'(x) = 0 $ has an odd number of solutions in $ (a, b) $ (where each solution is counted with its multiplicity; use Prop. 5 of VI, p. 25). Deduce that if $ h $ is a rational function over $ K $ having $ a $ and $ b $ as roots and whose denominator does not vanish in $ ]a, b[ $, then the equation $ h'(x) = 0 $ has at least one root in $ ]a, b[ $.
