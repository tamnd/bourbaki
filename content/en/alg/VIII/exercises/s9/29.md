Let V be a vector space of countable dimension over a commutative field K, and let A = End$_K(V)$. Prove that there exists a unique maximal two-sided ideal that is End$^f_K$(V), the set of endomorphisms of finite rank (cf. Exercise 2 of VIII, p. 128), but that the radical of A is 0.
