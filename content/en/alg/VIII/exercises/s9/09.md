Let K be a commutative field, B the ring $K[(X_n)_{n\geqslant 1}],\mathfrak{I}$ the ideal of B generated by the sequence $(X^n_n)$, and A the ring $B/\mathfrak{I}$. Prove that the radical of A is a nil ideal but is not nilpotent.
