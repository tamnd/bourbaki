Let K be a commutative field, and let A be the set of matrices of the form $(^{a b}_{0c})$ with $a, b, c\in K$. Verify that A is a subring of the matrix ring and that its radical is determined by $a=c= 0$. Prove that the image of $(^{1 0}_{0 0})$ in $A/\mathfrak{R}(A)$ is an idempotent element of the center of $A/\mathfrak{R}(A)$.
