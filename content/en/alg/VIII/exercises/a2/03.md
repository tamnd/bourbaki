Let $A = A_0\oplus A_1$ be a graded ring of type $\mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z}$. Suppose that $A_0$ is contained in the center of A and that every element of $A_1$ has square zero. a) Let $A^2_1$ be the $\mathbf{Z}$-submodule of $A_0$ generated by the products of two elements of $A_1$; show that $A^2_1$ is a nil ideal of $A_0$ and that $A^2_1\oplus A_1$ is a two-sided nil ideal of A. b) Let $p, q$ be integers $\geqslant 0$. Denote by $A^{p|q}$ the free graded right A-module $A^p_d\oplus A_d(1)^q$ (II, §11, No. 2, p. 365, Example 3) and by $\mathbf{G}\mathbf{L}(p|q,A)$ the group of (graded) automorphisms (of degree 0) of $A^{p|q}$. Such an automorphism is represented in the canonical basis of $A^{p|q}$ by a matrix X $= (_{C D}^{A B})$ with $A\in \mathbf{M}_p(A_0)$, $B\in \mathbf{M}_{p,q}(A_1),C\in \mathbf{M}_{q,p}(A_1),D\in \mathbf{M}_q(A_0)$. Prove that the matrices A and D are invertible (use a) and Exercise 16 of VIII, p. 168). c) For X as above, set sdetX = det($A-$ BD$^{-1}C$) det $D^{-1}$.
