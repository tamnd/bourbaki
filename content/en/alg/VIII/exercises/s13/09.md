Let K be a commutative field and A a K-algebra. Suppose that for every Artinian K-algebra B, the ring $A\otimes_KB$ is Artinian. Prove that A is finite-dimensional over K (reduce to the case when A is semisimple by observing that the $A/<(A)$-modules $<(A)^k/<(A)^{k+1}$ have finite length; then use Exercises 6 and 7).
