Let H be the quaternion field of type $(-1,0,-1)$ over $\mathbf{Q}$. a) An extension K of $\mathbf{Q}$ splits H if and only if $-1$ is the sum of two squares in K (apply Propositions 3 of VIII, p. 363 and 4 of VIII, p. 364).

b) Let K be a cyclic extension of $\mathbf{Q}$ of degree $2^n$ that splits H. Prove that no proper subfield of K splits H (observe that the unique subfield of K of degree $2^{n-1}$ is the intersection of K and a maximal ordered field containing $\mathbf{Q})$.
