Let $n$ be an integer $\geqslant 1$. A ring A is left Artinian (resp. left Noetherian) if and only if the matrix ring $\mathbf{M}_n(A)$ is. If A is left Artinian, then the left length of $\mathbf{M}_n(A)$ is equal to $n$ times that of A.
