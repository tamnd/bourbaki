Let $\rho : A\rightarrow B$ be a ring homomorphism. a) If, as a right A-module, B is free and nonzero, and if the ring B is left Artinian (resp. left Noetherian), then the ring A is left Artinian (resp. left Noetherian). b) Give an example where A is a commutative field, where B, viewed as a right vector space over A, is finite-dimensional, and where B is neither left Artinian nor left Noetherian (use Exercise 3).
