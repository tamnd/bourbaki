Let $A,B$ be rings, M a finitely generated left A-module, P a finitely generated right A-module, and N an $(A$, B)-bimodule. If the right B-module N is Artinian (resp. Noetherian), then so are the right B-modules Hom$_A(M,N)$ and $P\otimes_AN$.
