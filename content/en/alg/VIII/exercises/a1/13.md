Suppose that $k$ is a field and that A admits a finite basis over $k$ consisting of nilpotent elements. Prove that A is nilpotent (Exercise 9; reduce to the case when the algebra A is semisimple (cf. Exercise 7), and then of the form $\mathbf{M}_n(K)$ by extending the base field).
