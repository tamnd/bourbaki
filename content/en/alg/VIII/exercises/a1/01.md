Let A be a $k$-algebra. a) Prove that the only regular (left or right) ideal containing AA is A. b) A two-sided ideal $\mathfrak{a}$ of A is regular as a left ideal and as a right ideal if and only if the $k$-algebra $A/\mathfrak{a}$ is unital.
