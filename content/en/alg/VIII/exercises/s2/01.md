a) Give an example of a Noetherian module M such that every nonzero endomorphism of M is injective and that there exist nonzero nonbijective endomorphisms of M. b) Give an example of an Artinian module M such that every nonzero endomorphism of M is surjective and that there exist nonzero nonbijective endomorphisms of M.
