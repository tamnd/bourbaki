Let D be a field of finite rank over K with center K. There exists a K-subalgebra of $\mathbf{M}_n(D)$ isomorphic to $\mathbf{M}_r(K)$ if and only if $r$ divides $n$ (use Theorem 6 of VIII, p. 260).
