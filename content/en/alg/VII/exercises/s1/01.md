Show that if $ A $ is an integral domain then the polynomial ring $ A[X_i]_{i \in I} $ is not a principal ideal domain when one of the following conditions holds : 1) $ \operatorname{Card}(I) \geq 2 $; 2) $ A $ is not a field (consider the ideals $ (X_i) + (X_j) $ for $ a \neq \beta $. and $ (a) + (X_i) $ where $ a \neq () $ is a noninvertible element of $ A $).
