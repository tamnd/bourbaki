Let $ E $ be a vector space of finite dimension $ n $ over a field $ K $, let $ u $ be an endomorphism of $ E $, let $ V $ be a subspace of $ E $ invariant under $ u $, and let $ u|V $ be the restriction of $ u $ to $ V $. If $ f_i $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq n $) (resp. $ g_j $, $ 1 \leq j \leq m $) are the similarity invariants of $ u $ (resp. $ u|V $), show that $ g_j $ divides $ f_{j + n - m} $ for $ 1 \leq j \leq m $ (cf. VII, p. 64, Ex. 8, d)). State and prove the analogous property of the endomorphism of $ E/V $ induced by $ u $.
