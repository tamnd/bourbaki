Let $ u $ be an endomorphism of a finite dimensional vector space $ E $, and let $ V $ be a subspace of $ E $ invariant under $ u $. Show that the characteristic polynomial of the restriction of $ u $ to $ V $ divides that of $ u $. Hence obtain an elementary proof of the Cayley-Hamilton theorem (reduce to the case where $ E_u $ is cyclic, and use formula (2) of VII, p. 30).
