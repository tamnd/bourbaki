a) Let $ M $ be a $ n $-primary module, and let $ M_1 $ be the submodule of $ M $ consisting of elements of infinite height in $ M $. Show that the $ n $-primary module $ M/M_1 $ contains no nonzero element of infinite height.

b) Let $ p $ be a prime number, and let $ E $ be the direct sum of the $ p $-groups $ E_n = \mathbf{Z}/(p^n) $ ($ n \geq 1 $); let $ e_n $ denote the residue class of 1 mod $ p^n $ in $ E_n $. Let $ H $ be the submodule of $ E $ generated by the elements $ e_1 - p^{n-1} e_n $ for all integers $ n \geq 2 $. Let $ M $ be the quotient module $ E/H $. Show that the submodule $ M_1 $ of elements of infinite height in $ M $ is the submodule $ (E, + H)/H $, which is isomorphic to $ E_1 $, and show that there is no nonzero element in $ M_1 $, which has infinite height in $ M $.
