b) Let M be a reduced τ-module. For all x ∈ M, show that there exists a greatest ordinal γ(x) ≤ τ such that x ∈ M^{(γ(x))} (in particular, γ(0) = τ). Generalising the definition in Ex. 8, we say that γ(x) is the height of x in M. Show that if γ(x) < γ(y), then γ(x + y) = γ(x), and that if γ(x) = γ(y), then γ(x + y) ≥ γ(x).
