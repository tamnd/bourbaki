Let M be a $ \tau $-primary module and let N be a submodule of M whose nonzero elements all have height $ \leq h $ in M.
a) Let P be a pure submodule of M such that $ P(\pi) \subset N(\pi) $ and $ P(\tau) \neq N(\pi) $. Let a be an element of $ N(\pi) $ not belonging to $ P(\pi) $, and let $ x_0 $ be an element of $ a + P(\pi) $ whose height k in M is as large as possible; let $ x_0 = \pi^k y_0 $. Show that the sum $ P + Ay $, is direct, and is a pure submodule of M.
b) Let B be a pseudofree subset of M such that, if P is the pure submodule generated by B, then $ P(\pi) \subset N(\pi) $. Show that there exists a pseudofree subset $ C \to B $ of M such that, if Q is the pure submodule of M generated by C, then $ Q(\pi) = N(\pi) $ (apply Zorn's Lemma, using a)).
