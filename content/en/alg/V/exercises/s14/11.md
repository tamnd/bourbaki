Let E be an extension of a field K and $\Omega$ an algebraically closed extension of E. Show that the following conditions are equivalent :
   a) E is a p-radical extension of K ;
   β) for every extension F of K, $E \otimes_K F$ has only a single prime ideal ;
   γ) for every extension F of K, any two composite extensions of E and F are isomorphic ;
   δ) $E \otimes_K \Omega$ has only a single prime ideal ;
   ε) any two composite extensions of E and $\Omega$ are isomorphic.
