Let $ E $ be the relative algebraic closure of $ \mathbf{Q} $ in $ R $ ("the field of real algebraic numbers"); if $ u $ is an automorphism of $ E $, then $ u(x) = x $ for all $ x \in \mathbf{Q} $; show that if $ y \in E $ is such that $ y > 0 $, then also $ u(y) > 0 $. Deduce that $ u(y) = y $ for all $ y \in E $. \*
