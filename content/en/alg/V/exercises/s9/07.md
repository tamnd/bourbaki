Let K be a field of characteristic 0, a, β, 5 three elements of an algebraic closure of K such that $ [K(\alpha):K] = 2,\ [K(\beta):K] = 3,\ [K(\xi):K] = n > 1 $; denote by a' (resp. $ \beta', \beta'' $) the unique conjugate $ \neq a $ of a (resp. the conjugates $ \neq \beta $ of β).
a) Show that $ [K(\alpha, \xi):K] = n $ if $ a \in K(\xi) $, $ [K(\alpha, 5):K] = 2n $ otherwise.
b) Show that $ [K(\beta, 5):K] = n $ if $ \beta \in K(\xi) $, $ [K(\beta, 5):K] = 2n $ if $ \beta \in K(\xi) $ and just one of $ \beta', \beta'' $ belongs to $ K(\xi) $ and finally $ [K(\beta, \xi):K] = 3n $ if none of the three elements $ \beta, \beta', \beta'' $ belongs to $ K(\xi) $.
c) If d is the discriminant of the minimal polynomial of β, show that $ K(\beta, \beta') = K(\beta, \sqrt{d}) $.
d) Show that $ K(\beta, \beta') = K(\beta - \beta') $.
e) If $ n = 2 $ and $ a \notin K(\xi) $, show that $ K(\alpha, \xi) = K(\alpha + \xi) $.
f) Show that $ K(\alpha, \beta) = K(a + \beta) $.
g) Show that $ K(\alpha, \beta) = K(\alpha \beta) $ except when $ a = aj $ and $ \beta^3 = b $, where a, b are elements of K and $ j^3 = 1 $.
