Let $ K $ be a commutative ring, $ f(X) $ a monic polynomial in $ K[X] $, $ A $ the $ K $-algebra $ K[X]/(f) $ and $ x $ the residue class of $ X $ in $ A $. For each $ a \in K $ we have $ f(a) = N_{A/K}(a - x) $.
