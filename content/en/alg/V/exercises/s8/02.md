Let $ K $ be a field and $ A $ a $ K $-algebra of finite degree $ n $. For each integer $ m $ relatively prime to $ n $ we have $ A^*'' \cap K^* = K^{*m} $; in other words, if $ x \in K^* $ and if there exists an element $ y $ of $ A $ such that $ x = y^m $, then there exists an element $ z $ in $ K $ such that $ x = z^m $.
