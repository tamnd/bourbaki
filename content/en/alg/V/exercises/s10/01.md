a) Let K be a field, f a separable polynomial in $ K[X] $ and N a splitting field of f. Show that for the group Gal(N/K) to operate transitively on the set of roots of f in N it is necessary and sufficient for f to be irreducible in $ K[X] $.
b) Suppose further that f is irreducible in $ K[X] $; let x be a root of f in N. For there to exist no field E such that $ K \subset E \subset K(x) $, distinct from K and $ K(x) $ it is necessary and sufficient that Gal(N/K), qua transitive permutation group of the set of roots of f in N, should be primitive (I, p. 142, Ex. 13).
