Let N be a Galois extension of a field K, of infinite degree, and let $ \rho, a $ be two distinct elements of $ \mathrm{Gal}(N/K) $. If J is the set of $ x \in N $ such that $ \rho(x) \neq \sigma(x) $, show that $ K(J) $ is an extension of K of infinite degree. (Observe that a field cannot be the union of two of its subfields unless it is equal to one of them.)
