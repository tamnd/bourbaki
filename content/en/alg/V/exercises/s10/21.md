Let $ f(X) = X^n + a_1 X^{n-1} + \ldots + a_n $ be a polynomial of Z[X] and p a prime number. Suppose that $ a_i \equiv 0 \pmod{p} $, $ 1 \leq i \leq n $ and that $ a_n \not\equiv 0 \pmod{p^2} $. Show that $ f(X) $ is irreducible in Q[X]. (Let $ f(X) = P(X) Q(X) $ be a decomposition in Q[X]. Show that if P and Q are monic, they have integer coefficients. Reduce modp.)
