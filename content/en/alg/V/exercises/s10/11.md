a) Let E be a separable extension of a field K of finite degree and N the Galois extension of K generated by E. If $ a $ is an element of N whose conjugates form a normal basis of N over K and if $ \beta = \mathrm{Tr}_{N/E}(a) $, show that $ E = K(\beta) $.
b) Let N be a Galois extension of K of finite degree and let $ a $ be an element of N whose conjugates form a normal basis over K. If L is a subextension of N which is Galois over K and if $ \beta = \mathrm{Tr}_{N/L}(a) $, show that the conjugates of $ \beta $ form a normal basis of L over K.
c) Let L, M be two Galois extensions of K of finite degree, such that $ L \cap M = K $; let $ \alpha $ (resp. $ \beta $) be an element of L (resp. M) whose conjugates over K form a normal basis of L (resp. M) over K ; show that the conjugates over K of $ \alpha \beta $ form a normal basis of $ K(L \cup M) $.
