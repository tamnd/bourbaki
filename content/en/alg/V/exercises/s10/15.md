Let E be a Galois extension of finite degree $ n $ of a field K whose Galois group G is nilpotent. Let P be the set of prime numbers dividing n. Then there exist Galois subextensions $ E_p $ of E ($ p \in P $) such that $ \mathrm{Gal}(E_p/K) $ is a p-group for each $ p \in P $, and such that the canonical homomorphism $ \bigotimes_{p \in P} E_p \to E $ is an isomorphism.
