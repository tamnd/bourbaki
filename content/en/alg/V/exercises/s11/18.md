Let $ p \in \mathbf{N} $ be a prime number, $ C_p $ the cyclotomic extension of level $ p $ of $ \mathbf{Q} $, $ \zeta \in C_p $ a primitive p-th root of unity and $ n $ an integer. Write

$$
X_n(\zeta) = \sum_{a \in \mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z}} \zeta^{a^n}
$$

a) Calculate $ d = [\mathbf{Q}(X_n(\zeta)) : \mathbf{Q}] $. Determine the Galois group of the extension $ \mathbf{Q}(X_n(\zeta)) $ of $ \mathbf{Q} $ and the conjugates of $ X_n(\zeta) $.
b) Let $ T^d + a_1 T^{d-1} + \ldots + a_d $ be the minimal polynomial of $ X_n(\zeta) $ over $ \mathbf{Q} $. Show that $ a_1 = 0 $.
c) Suppose that $ n > 1 $ and $ p \equiv 1 \pmod{n} $. Show that $ a_1 = -\frac{n(n-1)p}{2} $ if $ \frac{p-1}{n} $ is even and $ a_1 = \frac{np}{2} $ if $ \frac{p-1}{n} $ is odd.
d) Suppose that $ p \equiv 1 \pmod{n} $. Let $ \beta \in \mathbf{F}_p^* $ be an element whose residue class in $ \mathbf{F}_p^*/\mathbf{F}_p^{*n} $ is a generator. Let $ \mu $ be the number of solutions in $ \mathbf{F}_p^n $ of the equation $ \sum_{i=1}^n \beta^{i-1} X_i^n = 0 $. Show that $ a_1 = \frac{(-1)^n p (\mu - p^{n-1})}{p-1} $
e) Calculate $ a_1 $ when $ n = 3 $ and $ p = 7, 13, 19 $.

\* ^1 The largest totally ramified extension of the field $ \mathbf{Q}_p $ of p-adic numbers in its algebraic closure is a field of characteristic 0 with the same property. \*
