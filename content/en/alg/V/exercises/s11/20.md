Let $p$ be a prime number and $G$ a commutative $p$-torsion group (VII, p. 10).
a) Let $G_i \subset G$ be the set of elements $x \in G$ such that for each $I$ the equation $p^I y = x$ has a solution. Show that $G_i$ is 2 direct factor of $G$.
b) Suppose that the equation $px = 0$ has only a finite number of solutions in $G$. Show that for each integer $I$, the kernel $_iG$ of the multiplication by $p^I$ in $G$ is a finite group and that the rank $r(l)$ of the vector $\mathbf{F}_p$-space $_lG / _{(l-1)}G$ is a decreasing function of $I$, hence constant for $I > I_0$, $I_0$ large enough. (Use the theorem of elementary divisors (VII, p. 24). Show that $G/G_i$ is a finite group annihilated by $p^{l_0}$.)
c) Suppose further that $G = G_i$. Show that the mapping $l \mapsto r(l)$ is constant. Writing $H = (\mathbf{Z}[1/p]/\mathbf{Z})^{(1)}$, show that for each $l$ there exists an isomorphism $_lG \to _lH$ and that every isomorphism $_lG \to _lH$ extends to an isomorphism $_{(l+1)}G \to _{(l+1)}H$. Deduce that $G$ is isomorphic to $H$.
d) Under the hypotheses of b) show that there exists an integer $n$ and an application $i \to n_i$ of $\mathbf{N} - \{0\}$ into $\mathbf{N}$, with finite support, such that $G$ is isomorphic to $(\mathbf{Z}[1/p]/\mathbf{Z})^r \oplus \bigoplus_i (\mathbf{Z}/p^{n_i}\mathbf{Z})^{n_i}$. Show that the integer $n$ and the mapping $i \mapsto n_i$ are uniquely determined by the mapping $l \mapsto \mathrm{Card}(_lG)$.
