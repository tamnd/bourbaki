Let K be a field of characteristic p, n an integer not a multiple of p, $ R_n(K) $ the field of n-th roots of unity and E a cyclic extension of $ R_n(K) $ of degree n over K ; E is thus the root field of a polynomial $ X^n - a \in R_n(K)[X] $ (V, p. 89, Th. 4). For E to be a Galois extension of K it is necessary and sufficient that for every automorphism $ \tau \in \mathrm{Gal}(R_n(K)/K) $ there exists an integer $ r > 0 $ and an element $ \gamma \in R_n(K) $ such that $ \tau(\alpha) = \gamma^n \alpha' $.
