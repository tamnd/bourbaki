Let N be a Kummer extension of a field K.
a) Let E be a subextension of N such that [E : K] = q is a prime number. Show that there exists a decomposition $ N = N_1 \otimes N_2 \otimes \ldots \otimes N_r $ of N into cyclic extensions of K such that $ E \subset N_j $ for an index j.
b) Deduce from a) that if $ x \in N $ is such that the conjugates of x over K form a normal basis of N over K, then for each field E such that $ K \subset E \subset N $, the conjugates of x over E form a normal basis of N over E (reduce to the case where [E : K] is prime and use Ex. 7, b)). \*
