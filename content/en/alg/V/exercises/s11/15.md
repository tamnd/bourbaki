Let $ K $ be a Moriya field (Ex. 13) and $ P $ the set of prime numbers dividing the degree of an algebraic extension of finite degree of $ K $. Show that the set of degrees of algebraic extensions of finite degree of $ K $ is either equal to the set $ N $, of integers all of whose prime factors are in $ P $ or to the subset of $ N_P $ consisting of integers not divisible by 4.
