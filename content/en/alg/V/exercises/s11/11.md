Let K be a field whose algebraic closure $ \Omega $ is an extension of finite degree $ > 1 $ of K. Show that $ \Omega = K(i) $ where $ i^2 = -1 $. (If we had $ \Omega \neq K(i) $, show with the help of Galois theory and Sylow's theorem that there would then exist a field E such that $ K(i) \subset E \subset \Omega $ and $ \Omega $ is an extension of prime degree of E; now apply Ex. 10.)
