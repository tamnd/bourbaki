Let $ G $ and $ H $ be two commutative torsion groups such that for every integer $ n $, the equation $ nx = 0 $ has the same finite number of solutions in $ G $ and in $ H $. Show that $ G $ is isomorphic to $ H $. (Decompose $ G $ and $ H $ into direct sums of $ p $-torsion groups and apply Ex. 20.) Let $ G = \bigoplus_{i \in \mathbf{N}} \mathbf{Z}/p^i\mathbf{Z} $ and $ H = \bigoplus_{i \in \mathbf{N}} \mathbf{Z}/p^{2i}\mathbf{Z} $. Show that $ G $ and $ H $ are not isomorphic and that for each integer $ n $ the cardinal of the set of solutions of the equation $ nx = 0 $ is the same in $ G $ and in $ H $.
