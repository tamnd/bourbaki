Show that for every integer $n > 1$ there exists a cyclic extension of degree n of the field Q. (Reduce to the case where n is a prime number q and use the structure of the multiplicative group $(\mathbf{Z}/q^m\mathbf{Z})^*$ (VII, p. 13). *)
