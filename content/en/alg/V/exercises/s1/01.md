Let $ A $ be a not necessarily commutative ring, not zero and without divisors of zero (I, p. 98). Show that if there exists an integer $ m \geq 1 $ such that $ mA = 0 $, then the set of all integers having this property is an ideal $ p\mathbf{Z} $, where $ p $ is a prime number, and $ A $ is a ring of characteristic $ p $.
