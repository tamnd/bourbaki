With the notation of Ex. 8 let M ⊂ L be a subextension. Put m = tr. deg_kM, n = tr. deg_kL, r = n - m. Let x_1, ..., x_r in L be such that L is a separable algebraic extension of k(x_1, ..., x_r) = K.
a) Show that, after renumbering the x_i if necessary, we may suppose that L is algebraic over M(x,, ..., x_r).
b) Show that there exists a finitely generated subextension N ⊂ M such that:
   a) L is algebraic over N(x_1, ..., x_r);
   β) K' = K(N(x_1, ..., x_r)) is a finite extension of N(x_1, ..., x_r);
   γ) the canonical homomorphism K' $ \otimes_{N(x_1, ..., x_r)} M(x_1, ..., x_r) \to K(M(x_1, ..., x_r)) = K'' $ is bijective.
c) Let N be a subextension as in b). Show successively that K'' is a separable algebraic extension of K' (because L is separable over K), that M(x,, ..., x_r) is a separable algebraic extension of N(x_1, ..., x_r) (use b), γ)) and that M is a separable algebraic extension of N.
d) Show that M is a separable algebraic extension of a pure transcendental extension (use c) and the fact that N is a finitely generated separable extension of k).
