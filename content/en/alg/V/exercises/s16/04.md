Let K be a field of characteristic $ p > 0 $ whose absolute degree of imperfection (V, p. 170, Ex. 1) is equal to 1. Show that for an extension E of K to be separable over K it is necessary and sufficient that E should contain no element p-radical over K and not belonging to K (if $ a \in K $ forms ap-basis of K over $ K^p $ show that $ K^{p^{-1}} = K(a^{p^{-1}}) $ and E are linearly disjoint over K).
