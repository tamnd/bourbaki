Show that the number of monic polynomials of degree $ n $ of $ \mathbf{F}_q[X] $ which have no root in $ \mathbf{F}_q $ is $ q^{n-q}(q-1)^q $ for $ n \geq q $ (observe that every function $ \mathbf{F}_q \to \mathbf{F}_q $ is a polynomial function).
