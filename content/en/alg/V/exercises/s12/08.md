.
a) Show that if K has at least 3 elements, $ \tau $ is a K-automorphism of the field E (reduce to the case where $ \tau(\alpha_1) = \alpha_1 $ and consider $ \tau(a, + c\alpha_i) $ for $ c \in K $).

b) If $ K = F_2 $, show that for each element $ \rho \in \mathrm{Gal}(E/K) $, the relations $ \rho(\alpha_r) = \alpha_r $, and $ \rho(\alpha_s) = \alpha_s' $ imply $ r' - r \equiv s' - s \pmod{n} $. Deduce that if $ n > 5 $, $ \tau $ is a K-automorphism of the field E. (Note that if $ \tau(\alpha_1) = a $, and $ \tau(\alpha_1 + \alpha_h) = a_+ + \alpha_k $ with $ k \neq h $, we must have $ h + k = n + 2 $; deduce from this that necessarily $ \tau(\alpha_2) = \alpha_n $ and $ \tau(\alpha_4) = \alpha_4 $ or $ \tau(\alpha_4) = \alpha_{n-2} $ which leads to a contradiction.)
c) For $ K = F_2 $ and $ 3 \leq n \leq 5 $ the following automorphisms of the vector K-space E are not field automorphisms of E:

for $ n = 3, \tau : (\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3) \mapsto (\alpha_1, \alpha_3, \alpha_2) $
for $ n = 4, \tau : (\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4) \mapsto (\alpha_1, \alpha_4, \alpha_3, \alpha_2) $
for $ n = 5, \tau : (\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4, \alpha_5) \mapsto (\alpha_1, \alpha_5, \alpha_4, \alpha_3, \alpha_2) $

but are such that $ \tau(x) $ is conjugate to x for all $ x \in E $.

8) Let K be a finite field of q elements and

$$
P(X) = a_0 + a_1 X + \cdots + a_{q-2} X^{q-2}
$$

a polynomial of $ K[X] $ of degree $ q-2 $. Let r be the rank of the matrix

$$
A = \begin{pmatrix}
a_0 & a_1 & \cdots & a_{q-2} \\
a_1 & a_2 & \cdots & a_0 \\
\ldots & \ldots & \ldots & \ldots \\
a_{q-2} & a_0 & \cdots & a_{q-3}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Show that the number of distinct roots of P in K, other than 0, is $ q-1-r $. (Consider the product VA, where V is the Vandermonde matrix of elements $ x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{q-1} $ of $ K^* $.)
