Let $ K $ be a field, $ \Omega $ an extension of $ K $ and $ E, F $ linearly disjoint subextensions over $ K $. Let $ C $ be the $ K $-algebra $ K[E \cup F] $ isomorphic to $ E \otimes_K F $.
a) Let $ E' $ (resp. $ F' $) be a subextension of $ E $ (resp. $ F $) and put $ C' = K[E' \cup F'] $. If an element $ a $ of $ C' $ is invertible in $ C $ then $ a^{-1} \in C' $. (Reduce to the case $ F' = F $. Let

E" be a supplement of E' in the vector E'-space E, so that C is isomorphic to $ C' \oplus (E'' \otimes_K F) $. Express the matrix of multiplication by a in this decomposition.)
\* b) Show that if C is a field, then E or F is algebraic over K. (If $ x \in E $ and $ y \in F $ are transcendental over K, take $ E' = K(x) $, $ F' = K(y) $ and $ a = x + y $ in $ a $.)
