a) Show that for every integer $ a \neq 0 $ the polynomial $ X^4 - aX - 1 $ is irreducible in $ \mathbf{Q}[X] $.
b) Let $ a $ be a root of $ X^4 - aX - 1 $ in an extension of $ \mathbf{Q} $, and let $ K = \mathbf{Q}(\alpha) $, which is of degree 4 over $ \mathbf{Q} $. Show that there is an infinity of values of $ a \in \mathbf{Z} $ such that $ K $ contains no subfield $ F $ distinct from $ \mathbf{Q} $ and $ K $.
