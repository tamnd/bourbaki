Let K be a field of characteristic p > 0, E an extension of K and B a p-basis of E over $ K(E^p) $ (V, p. 98).
a) Assume that $ E \subset K^{p^{-1}} $; let $ K_0 $ be a subfield of K such that E is separable over $ K_0 $. Show that the set $ B^p $ is a p-independent subset over $ K_0(K^p) $ (observe that if (a,) is a basis of the vector $ K_0 $-space $ K $, then $ (a_\lambda^p) $ is a basis of $ K_0(K^p) $ over $ K_0 $. Let $ C $ be a subset of $ K $, disjoint from $ B^p $ and such that $ B^p \cup C $ is a p-basis of $ K $ over $ K,(K^p) $; show that $ B \cup C $ is a p-basis of $ E $ over $ K_0(E^p) $.

b) Assume that $ E \subset K^{p^{-n}} $ and that $ E $ is separable over a subfield $ K_0 $ of $ K $. Show that if the degree of imperfection of $ K $ over $ K_n $ is finite (V, p. 170. Ex. 1), then it is equal to the degree of imperfection of $ E $ over $ K_0 $ (use a)).
