Let $ K $ be a field of characteristic $ p > 0 $, $ E $ a separable extension of $ K $ and $ B $ ap-basis of $ E $ over $ K(E^p) $.
a) Show that $ B $ is algebraically free over $ K $ (consider an algebraic relation of least degree between the elements of $ B $ and put the degrees of the variables which occur in the form $ kp + h $ with $ 0 \leq h \leq p - 1 $). Deduce that if $ \operatorname{tr. deg}_K E < +\infty $, then the degree of imperfection of $ E $ over $ K $ is at most equal to $ \operatorname{tr. deg}_K E $.
b) Show that $ E $ is separable and relatively perfect over $ K(B) $.
