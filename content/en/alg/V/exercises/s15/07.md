Show that a relatively perfect transcendental extension $ E $ of a field $ K $ of characteristic $ p > 0 $ is not finitely generated over $ K $. (In the opposite case show that for every transcendence basis $ B $ of $ E $ over $ K $, $ E $ would be algebraic and separable over $ K(B) $.)
