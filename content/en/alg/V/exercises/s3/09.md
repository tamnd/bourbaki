Let K be a field, E an extension of K and S a subset of $ E - K $.
a) Show that the set of extensions $ L \subset E $ of K such that $ L \cap S = \varnothing $, ordered by inclusion, has a maximal element M.
b) Show that if S is finite, E is an algebraic extension of M.
