Let A be a commutative algebra and let S be a subset of A generating A as K-algebra. Suppose that $ \operatorname{Card}(S) < \operatorname{Card}(K) $. Show that if $ \mathfrak{M} $ is a maximal ideal of A, then the field $ A/\mathfrak{M} $ is an algebraic extension of K. (Use Ex. 15 of V, p. 174 when $ \operatorname{Card}(K) \leq \operatorname{Card}(N) $ and Ex. 4 when
$$
\operatorname{Card}(K) > \operatorname{Card}(N).
$$
*Deduce that $ A/\mathfrak{M} = K $ when K is algebraically closed.*
