Let $ E $ be a linearly ordered set with a smallest element $ \omega $, and let $ I $ be an interval of $ E $, containing $ \omega $, with $ \omega $ as its left-hand end-point. Suppose that $ E $ and $ I $ satisfy axioms (GR_I), (GR_{II}), (GR_{IV}) and the following axiom:

(GR_{IIIb}) *The set of elements $ x > \omega $ in $ I $ is not empty, and if $ x $ is any element $ > \omega $ in $ I $, there exists $ y > \omega $ in $ I $ such that $ y^2 \leq x $.*

Show that there exists an increasing mapping $ f $ of $ I $ into $ \mathbf{R} $, such that $ f(\omega) = 0 $ and $ f(xy) = f(x) + f(y) $ whenever $ x, y $ and $ xy $ are in $ I $; also that $ f(I) \cap [0, f(b)] $ is dense in $ [0, f(b)] $ for all $ b \in I $.
