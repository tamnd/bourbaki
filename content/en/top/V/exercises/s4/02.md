Show, by induction on $n$, that for all integers $n > 0$ we have
$$
2^n > \frac{1}{2} n(n+1).
$$
Deduce that, if $a > 1$ and $\alpha > 0$,
$$
\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{a^x}{x^\alpha} = +\infty, \quad \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\log_a x}{x^\alpha} = 0
$$
(begin by proving the first of these relations for $a = 2$ and $\alpha = 1$).
