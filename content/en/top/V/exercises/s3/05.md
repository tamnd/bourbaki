Give an example of a topology on $\mathbf{R}$, compatible with the group structure, for which $\mathbf{R}$ is connected, locally compact and locally connected, and for which the complement of $\{0\}$ in $\mathbf{R}$ is connected (use the fact that $\mathbf{R}$ and $\mathbf{R}^n$ are algebraically isomorphic). \*
