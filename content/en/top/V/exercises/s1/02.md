Let $ \mathcal{G} $ be a Hausdorff topology on $ \mathbf{R} $, compatible with the group structure of $ \mathbf{R} $ and strictly coarser than the usual topology $ \mathcal{T}_0 $.

a) Show that every open neighbourhood of $ o $ with respect to $ \mathcal{T} $ is unbounded in $ \mathbf{R} $ (note that a Hausdorff topology which is coarser than the topology of a compact space must coincide with the latter).

b) Let $ V \neq \mathbf{R} $ be a symmetric open neighbourhood of $ o $ with respect to $ \mathcal{G} $, and let $ W $ be a symmetric open neighbourhood of $ o $ with respect to $ \mathcal{G} $ such that $ W + W \subset V $. Show that if $ a $ is the length of the component of $ V $ (with respect to $ \mathcal{G}_0 $) which contains $ o $, then every component (with respect to $ \mathcal{G}_0 $) of $ W $ has length $ \leq a $. Moreover, the set of lengths of components of the interior of $ \mathbf{R} - W $ (with respect to $ \mathcal{G}_0 $) is bounded [use a) and the fact that there exists a symmetric open neighbourhood $ W_1 $ of $ o $ with respect to $ \mathcal{T} $ such that $ W_1 + W_1 \subset W $].

c) Deduce from b) that $ \mathbf{R} $ is precompact and not locally compact in the topology $ \mathcal{G} $.

d) Show likewise that $ \mathbf{Z} $ is precompact and not locally compact in any topology $ \mathcal{G} $ compatible with its group structure and distinct from the discrete topology.

e) Let $ f $ be a continuous homomorphism of a subgroup $ \Gamma $ of $ \mathbf{R} $, not consisting of $ o $ alone and endowed with the topology induced by $ \mathcal{G}_0 $, into a complete Hausdorff group $ G $. Show that if $ f $ is not an isomorphism of $ \Gamma $ onto the subgroup $ f(\Gamma') $ of $ G $, then $ f(\Gamma') $ is relatively compact in $ G $ [use c) and d)].

\* $ f $) For every integer $ n \geq 2 $, give examples of continuous injective homomorphisms $ f $ of $ \mathbf{R} $ into $ \mathbf{T}^n $ such that $ f(\mathbf{R}) $ is dense in $ \mathbf{T}^n $ (cf. Chapter VII, § 1, no. 4, Corollary 1 of Proposition 7). \*
