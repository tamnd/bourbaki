Let $ X $ be a Hausdorff uniform space and let $ (f_i) $ be a family of pseudometrics on $ X $ which define the uniformity of $ X $. Let $ X_i $ be the metric space associated with the uniform space obtained by endowing $ X $ with the structure defined by the single pseudometric $ f_i $ (no. 1). Show that $ X $ is isomorphic to a subspace of the product uniform space $ \prod_i X_i $.
