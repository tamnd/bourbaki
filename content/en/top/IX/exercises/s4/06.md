Show that, in a normal space $ X $, every subspace $ Y $ of $ X $ which is a countable union of closed sets is normal. [Let $ A, B $ be two disjoint closed subsets of $ Y $, and suppose that $ Y $ is the union of an ascending sequence $(Y_n)$ of closed subsets of $X$. Define by induction two sequences $(U_n), (V_n)$ of open subsets of $X$ such that: (i) $U_n \cap Y_n$ and $V_n \cap Y_n$ do not intersect; (ii) $U_n \cap Y_n$ contains $A \cap Y_n$ and all the sets $U_i \cap Y_i$ for $1 \leq i \leq n$; and $V_n \cap Y_n$ contains $B \cap Y_n$ and all $V_i \cap Y_i, 1 \leq i \leq n.$]
