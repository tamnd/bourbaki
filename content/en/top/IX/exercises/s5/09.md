Let $X$ be the subspace of $\mathbf{R}^2$ which is the union of $\mathbf{Q}^2$ and the line $y = 0$. Show that $X$ is meagre relative to itself. Deduce that a topological space can have non-meagre subspaces without being non-meagre itself.
