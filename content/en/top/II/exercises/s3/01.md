Let $ \mathcal{U} $ denote the additive uniformity on the real line $ \mathbf{R} $, and let $ \mathcal{U}' $ denote the inverse image of $ \mathcal{U} $ under the mapping $ x \to x^3 $ of $ \mathbf{R} $ onto itself. Show that $ \mathcal{U}' $ is strictly finer than $ \mathcal{U} $, but that the Cauchy filters are the same for both uniformities.*
