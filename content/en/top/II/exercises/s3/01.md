Let $\mathscr{U}$ denote the additive uniformity on the real line $\mathbf{R}$, and let $\mathscr{U}'$ denote the inverse image of $\mathscr{U}$ under the mapping $x \to x^3$ of $\mathbf{R}$ onto itself. Show that $\mathscr{U}'$ is strictly finer than $\mathscr{U}$, but that the Cauchy filters are the same for both uniformities.*
