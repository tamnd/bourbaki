For each family $ (V_i)_{i \in H} $, where card $ (H) < c $ and $ V_i $ is an entourage of $ X_i $ for each $ i \in H $, let $ U((V_i)) $ be the set of pairs $ (x, y) $ of points of $ X = \prod_{i \in I} X_i $ such that $ (\mathrm{pr}_i x, \mathrm{pr}_i y) \in V_i $ for each $ i \in H $. Show that the sets $ U((V_i)) $ form a fundamental system of entourages
