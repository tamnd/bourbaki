Let X be a Hausdorff uniform space which has an entourage V such that V(x) is precompact for each x ∈ X. Show that the completion $ \hat{X} $ is locally compact and satisfies the conditions of Exercise 9. [If W is a symmetric open entourage of X such that $ \hat{W} \subset V $, show that the closure $ \overline{W} $ of W in $ \hat{X} \times \hat{X} $ is such that $ \overline{W}(x) $ is compact for each x ∈ $ \hat{X} $].
