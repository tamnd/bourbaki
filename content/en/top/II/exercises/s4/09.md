Let X be a locally compact space which carries a uniformity compatible with its topology and is such that there is an entourage V with the property that V(x) is relatively compact in X for all x ∈ X.
a) Show that X is complete with respect to this uniformity.
b) Let U be a symmetric entourage of X such that $ \hat{U} \subset V $. Show that if A is relatively compact in X, then so is U(A).
c) Show that X is paracompact [use b), Exercise 7 and Chapter I, § 9, no. 10, Theorem 5].
d) Let W be a closed symmetric entourage of X such that $ \hat{W} \subset V $. Show that if A is closed in X then so is W(A) (use Exercise 8).
