Let f : X → Y be a continuous open mapping, X locally compact and Y Hausdorff.

a) If K is any compact connected subset of Y and C is any compact component of $ \overline{f}(K) $, show that $ f(C) = K $ (reduce to the case K = Y and use the Corollary to Proposition 6 of no. 4). *Give an example of a non-compact component C' of $ \overline{f}(K) $ such that $ f(C') \neq K $.*

b) Suppose in addition that Y is locally compact and locally connected. Show that if U is any connected open subset of Y and R is any relatively compact component of $ \overline{f}(U) $ in X, then $ f(R) = U $. [Observe that in a locally compact locally connected space, a connected open set U is the union of the compact connected sets contained in U and containing a given point $ y_0 \in U $; then apply a).]

c) Suppose in addition that X is locally connected. Show that if K is any connected subset of Y which is either open or compact with a non-empty interior, and if H is any compact subset of X, then there is only a finite number of components of $ \overline{f}(K) $ contained in H. [Use a) and b)].
