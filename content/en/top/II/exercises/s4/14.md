Let $ X $ be a compact space, and let $ \mathcal{B} $ be a filter base on $ X $ consisting of connected closed sets. Show that the (non-empty) intersection of the sets of $ \mathcal{B} $ is closed and connected (argue by reductio ad absurdum, using Proposition 4 of no. 3 and Chapter I, § 9, no. 1, Theorem 1).
