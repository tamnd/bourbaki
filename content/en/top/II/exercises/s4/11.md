Let X be a Hausdorff uniform space, $ \mathcal{U} $ its uniformity, $ \mathfrak{F}(X) $ the set of all non-empty closed subsets of X. Let $ \mathfrak{F}(X) $ carry the uniformity induced by the uniformity $ \tilde{\mathcal{U}} $ defined in § 1, Exercise 5 a).
a) Show that if X' is precompact then so is $ \mathfrak{F}(X) $. [If $ (A_i) $ is a finite covering of X by V-small sets (V symmetric), show that the sets $ \tilde{V}(B_k) $ cover $ \mathfrak{F}(X) $, where the B_k are all the unions of sets A_i].
b) A point A ∈ $ \mathfrak{F}(X) $ is such that every neighbourhood of A in the topology induced by $ \mathcal{T}(\tilde{\mathcal{U}}) $ on $ \mathfrak{F}(X) $ contains a neighbourhood of A in the topology induced by $ \mathcal{T}_\Theta $ (Chapter I, § 8, Exercise 12) on $ \mathfrak{F}(X) $, if and only if A is precompact.
c) Show that the topologies $ \mathcal{T}(\tilde{\mathcal{U}}) $ and $ \mathcal{T}_\Theta $ induce the same topology on the set $ \mathfrak{K}(X) $ of compact subsets of X [use b) and Exercise 5 of § 1].
