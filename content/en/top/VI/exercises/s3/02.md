Identify the projective space $ P_n $ with the quotient space of $ B_n $ defined in no. 1, Proposition 3, and let $ \varphi $ denote the canonical mapping of $ B_n $ onto $ P_n $. A continuous mapping $ f $ of a topological space E into $ P_n $ is said to be *inessential* if there exists a continuous mapping $ g $ of E into $ B_n $ such that $ f = \varphi \circ g $, and *essential* in the converse case (cf. § 2, Exercise 6). Show that there exists an entourage U of the uniformity of $ P_n $ such that, if $ f $ is an inessential mapping of a topological space E into $ P_n $, every continuous mapping $ f' $ of E into $ P_n $ such that $ (f(x), f'(x)) \in U $ for all $ x \in E $ is also inessential.
