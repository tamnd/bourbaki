If $ n > 1 $, there exists an essential mapping $ f $ of $ S_1 $ into $ P_n $ such that $ f(S_1) \neq P_n $ [take $ f(S_1) $ to be the image under $ \varphi $ of a diameter of $ B_n $; this image is a projective line]. Deduce that, if $ n > 1 $, $ P_n $ is homeomorphic to neither $ S_n $ nor $ B_n $ (use Exercise 3 of § 3 and Exercise 3 of § 2).
