Give a proof of the fact that $\mathbf{R}^n$ is equipotent with $\mathbf{R}$ by using Cantor’s theorem (Chapter IV, § 8, no. 6, Theorem 1) and the relation $2^\alpha \cdot 2^\alpha = 2^\alpha$, valid for all infinite cardinals $\alpha$.
