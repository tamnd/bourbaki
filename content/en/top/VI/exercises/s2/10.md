Let $ H_{n, p, q} $ denote the "quadric" in $ \mathbf{R}^n $ defined by the equation
$$
x_1^2 + x_1^2 + \cdots + x_p^2 - x_{p+1}^2 - \cdots - x_{p+q}^2 = 1 \quad (p + q \leq n).
$$
Show that $ H_{n, p, q} $ is homeomorphic to $ S_{p-1} \times \mathbf{R}^{n-p} $.
