Let $I$ be the closed cube of $\mathbf{R}^n$ which is the product of $n$ intervals identical with $[-\frac{1}{2}\pi, \frac{1}{2}\pi]$. To each point $x = (x_i)$ of $I$ we make correspond the point $y = (y_j)$ of $\mathbf{R}^{n+1}$ such that

$$
\begin{align*}
y_1 &= \sin x_1 \\
y_2 &= \cos x_1 \sin x_2 \\
&\cdots \\
y_p &= \cos x_1 \cos x_2 \ldots \cos x_{p-1} \sin x_p \quad (2 \leq p \leq n-1) \\
&\cdots \\
y_n &= \cos x_1 \cos x_2 \ldots \cos x_{n-1} \sin 2x_n \\
y_{n+1} &= \cos x_1 \cos x_2 \ldots \cos x_{n-1} \cos 2x_n.
\end{align*}
$$

Show that the image of I under this mapping is the sphere $S_n$, and that the restriction of this mapping to the interior of I is a homeomorphism onto a dense open set in $S_n$. \*
