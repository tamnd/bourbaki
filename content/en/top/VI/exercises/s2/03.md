a) Show that, if $ f $ is a continuous mapping of $ S_1 $ into $ \mathbf{R}^n $, $ f $ can be extended to a continuous mapping of $ B_2 $ into $ \mathbf{R}^n $ [map the point $ t x \in B_2 $ ($ t \geq 0, x \in S_1 $) to $ t f(x) $].

b) Deduce that, if $ f $ is a continuous mapping of $ S_1 $ into $ S_n $ such that $ f(S_1) \neq S_n $, then $ f $ can be extended to a continuous mapping of $ B_2 $ into $ S_n $ [use a stereographic projection whose vertex does not lie in $ f(S_1) $]. (*)
