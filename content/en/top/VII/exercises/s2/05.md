Let $ G $ be a connected topological group and let $ H $ be a compact normal subgroup of $ G $ with no arbitrarily small subgroups (Chapter III, § 2, Exercise 30). Show that $ H $ is contained in the centre of $ G $. (Observe that for $ s $ close to $ e $ in $ G $, the image of $ H $ under $ x \to sx s^{-1} $ is arbitrarily close to $ e $.)
