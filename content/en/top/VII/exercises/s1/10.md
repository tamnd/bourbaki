Let $ a $ be a real number and $ n $ an integer $ > 0 $, and consider the numbers $ x_k = ka - [ka] \in [0, 1[ \quad (1 \leq k \leq n + 1) $. If $ I_h $ denotes the interval
$$
\left[ \frac{h-1}{n}, \frac{h}{n} \right] \qquad (1 \leq h \leq n),
$$
show that there exist two distinct indices $ k, k' $ such that $ x_k $ and $ x_{k'} $ belong to the same interval $ I_h $ (for a suitable value of $ h $). Deduce that there exist two integers $ p, q $ such that $ 1 \leq p \leq n $ and $ |pa - q| \leq 1/n $.
