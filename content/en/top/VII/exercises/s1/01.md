Let G be a discrete subgroup of rank $ p $ in $ \mathbf{R}^n $ and let $ (\mathbf{a}_i)_{1 \leq i \leq p} $ be a free system of $ p $ points of G. The proof of Theorem 1 of no. 1 shows that G is a subgroup of the group generated by the $ p $ points $ d^{-1} \mathbf{a}_i $, where $ d $ is some integer. Show, without using Theorem 1, that there exists a free system of $ p $ points $ \mathbf{b}_i = \sum_{j=1}^p b_{ij} \mathbf{a}_j $ of G such that, if $ x_i = \sum_{j=1}^p x_{ij} \mathbf{a}_i \quad (1 \leq i \leq p) $ is any free system of $ p $ points of G, we have $ |\det(x_{ij})| \geq |\det(b_{ij})| > 0 $. Hence give a proof of Theorem 1 which does not rest on the theory of invariant factors, by showing that the $ \mathbf{b}_i $ generate G. (Argue by contradiction: if $ z = \sum_{i=1}^p z_i \mathbf{b}_i \in G $ and one of the $ z_i $ is not an integer, there exists a point $ u = \sum_{i=1}^p u_i \mathbf{b}_i $ of the group generated by $ z $ and the $ \mathbf{b}_i $ such that $ 0 < u_i < 1 $ for some index $ i $, and hence obtain a contradiction.)
