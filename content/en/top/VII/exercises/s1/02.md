Let G be a discrete subgroup of $ \mathbf{R}^n $. If G is the direct sum of two subgroups H and K, then the intersection of the vector subspaces generated by H and K consists only of 0, and the rank of G is therefore equal to the sum of the ranks of H and K (observe that, for every discrete subgroup G of $ \mathbf{R}^n $, the vector subspace of $ \mathbf{R}^n $ generated by G is canonically isomorphic to $ G \otimes_{\mathbf{Z}} \mathbf{R} $).
