Let $ G $ be a Hausdorff topological group, and let $ s \neq e $ be a point of $ G $. Show that there is a symmetric neighbourhood $ V $ of $ e $ such that $ s \notin V^2 $, and that $ G $ can be covered by at most 17 sets of the form $ a_k V b_k $ ($ 1 \leq k \leq 17 $). (Consider a maximal element in the set of symmetric neighbourhoods $ V $ of $ e $ such that $ s \notin V^2 $. We are thus led to consider the set $ S $ of $ x \in G $ such that $ x^2 = s $; observe that if $ x, y $ are two elements of $ S $ such that $ xy^{-1} \in S $, then we must have $ x^2 = e $, and if $ z $ is a third element of $ S $ such that $ xz^{-1} \in S $ and $ yz^{-1} \in S $, then $ z $ must commute with $ xy^{-1} $, and $ xy^{-1}z^{-1} \notin S $.) If $ G $ is commutative, the number 17 may be replaced by 5.
