Let $ G $ be a quasi-topological group in which every subset consisting of a single point is closed and whose identity component $ C $ is such that $ G/C $ is finite. Show that if $ G/C $ has $ k $ elements, then the set of conjugates $ xax^{-1} $ of an element $ a \in G $ is either infinite or else has at most $ k $ elements (consider the mapping $ x \to xax^{-1} $ of $ G $ onto this set). In particular, if $ G $ is connected, the set of conjugates of every element not in the centre of $ G $ is infinite.
