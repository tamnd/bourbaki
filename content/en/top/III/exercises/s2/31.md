Let $ G $ be a connected topological group, $ H $ a subgroup of $ G $, $ U $ an open subset of $ G $ such that $ H = UH $. Let $ V = H \cap (U^{-1}U) $; show that $ H = V^\infty $ [show that if $ A = H \cap C(V^\infty) $, then the sets $ U.V^\infty $ and $ U.A $ do not intersect].
