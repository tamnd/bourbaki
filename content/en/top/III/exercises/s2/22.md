Let $ G $ be the product of a family $ (G_i)_{i \in I} $ of topological groups, and let $ G' $ be a topological group such that there is a neighbourhood $ V' $ of the identity element $ e' $ in $ G' $ which contains no normal subgroup of $ G' $ other than $ \{e'\} $. If $ f $ is a continuous homomorphism of $ G $ into $ G' $, show that there is a subset $ J $ of $ I $ whose complement $ J' $ is finite, such that $ f $ is equal to $ e' $ on the subgroup $ \prod_{i \in I} G_i \times \prod_{i \in J'} \{e_i\} $ of $ G $.
