With the hypotheses and notation of Exercise 26 of § 2, suppose that each of the groups $ G_i $ is Hausdorff and has a completion. Show that the local product G of the $ G_i $, relative to a family $ (K_i) $ of open normal subgroups, has a completion $ \hat{G} $ which is isomorphic to the local product of the $ \hat{G}_i $, relative to the closures $ \overline{K}_i $ of the $ K_i $.
