Let $ G $ be a complete Hausdorff commutative group such that every neighbourhood of $ o $ in $ G $ contains an open subgroup of $ G $ (this is the case in particular if $ G $ is locally compact and totally disconnected; cf. \S 4, Exercise 18). A family $ (x_i)_{i \in I} $ of points of $ G $ is summable if and only if $ \lim x_i = o $ with respect to the filter of complements of finite subsets of $ I $. Hence show that every convergent series in $ G $ is commutatively convergent.
