With the notation of Exercise 3, take $ G $ to be the discrete additive group $ \mathbf{Z} $ of rational integers and $ I $ to be the set $ \mathbf{N} $. Give an example of a non-summable sequence $ (x_n) $ for which the set $ A $ consists of the single point $ o $ (choose the $ x_n $ so that every finite partial sum, all of whose terms have indices $ \geq m $, is an integral multiple of $ m $).
