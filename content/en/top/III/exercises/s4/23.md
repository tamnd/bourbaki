Let $p$ be a prime number, let $(G_n)$ be an infinite sequence of topological groups each identical with the discrete group $\mathbf{Z}/(p^2)$, and let $ H_n $ be the subgroup $ (p)/(p^2) $ of $ G_n $. Let $ G $ be the local direct product of the $ G_n $ relative to the $ H_n $ (\S 2, Exercise 26); then $ G $ is a locally compact group but is not compact. Show that the continuous homomorphism $ u : x \to px $ of $ G $ into itself is not a strict morphism of $ G $ onto $ u(G) $, and that $ u(G) $ is not closed in $ G $.
