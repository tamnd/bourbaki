a) Let $ a $ be a discrete left ideal in a topological ring $ A $. Show that, for every $ x \in a $, the left annihilator of $ x $ in $ A $ is open. Hence show that if $ A $ is a non-discrete ring with no zero-divisors, it contains no discrete (left or right) ideal other than $ \{0\} $.
b) Let $ A $ be a locally connected topological ring, and let $ a $ be a totally disconnected left ideal of $ A $. Show that, for each $ x \in a $, the left annihilator of $ x $ in $ A $ is open. Hence show that if $ A $ has no zero-divisors it contains no totally disconnected (left or right) ideal other than $ \{0\} $.
