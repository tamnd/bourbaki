a) Let $ a \neq 0 $ be a $ p $-adic number. Then the mapping $ n \to a^n $ is continuous on $ \mathbf{Z} $ (as a subspace of $ \mathbf{Q}_p $) if and only if $ a \in 1 + \mathfrak{p} $. If this condition is satisfied, show that $ n \to a^n $ is uniformly continuous on $ \mathbf{Z} $ and extends to a continuous homomorphism of $ \mathbf{Z}_p $ into $ U $, denoted by $ x \to a^x $, which is injective if $ a \neq 1 $. If $ p > 2 $ and if $ m $ is the largest integer such that $ a \in 1 + \mathfrak{p}^m $, then $ x \to a^x $ is an isomorphism of $ \mathbf{Z}_p $ onto $ 1 + \mathfrak{p}^m $. How must this statement be modified when $ p = 2 $?

b) Show that every continuous homomorphism of $ \mathbf{Z}_p $ into $ U $ is of the form $ x \to a^x $ for some $ a \in 1 + \mathfrak{p} $ [if $ f $ is such a homomorphism and $ f(1) = a $, then $ f(n) = a^n $ for $ n \in \mathbf{Z} $].

c) Show that the mapping $ (x, y) \to x^y $ is continuous on $ (1 + \mathfrak{p}) \times \mathbf{Z}_p $. If $ p > 2 $ and $ b \in \mathbf{Z}_p $, and if $ m $ is the largest integer such that $ b \in \mathfrak{p}^m $, then the mapping $ x \to x^b $ is an isomorphism of the multiplicative group $ 1 + \mathfrak{p} $ onto the multiplicative subgroup $ 1 + \mathfrak{p}^{m+1} $ [use Exercise 24 b)]. How must this statement be modified when $ p = 2 $?

d) If $ n $ is an integer prime to both $ p - 1 $ and $ p $, show that the mapping $ x = x^n $ is an automorphism of the multiplicative group $ U $ [use Exercise 24 c)].
