Let A be a topological ring with no identity element, and let B be the ring obtained by adjoining an identity element to A show that the topology on B which is the product of the topology of A and the discrete topology on $\mathbf{Z}$ is compatible with the ring structure of B, and induces the given topology on A (a two-sided ideal of B).
