a) Let $ p, q $ be two distinct prime numbers and let $ \mathcal{T} $ be the topology on $ \mathbf{Q} $ which is the least upper bound of the topologies $ \mathcal{T}_p $ and $ \mathcal{T}_q $. Then $ \mathcal{T} $ is locally bounded [Exercise 20 c)] and is compatible with the field structure of $ \mathbf{Q} $. With respect to this topology neither of the two sequences $ ((p/q)^n)_{n \geq 0} $, $ ((q/p)^n)_{n \geq 0} $ is bounded; the sequence $ (1/p^n)_{n \geq 0} $ is not bounded, but the sequence $ (p^n)_{n \geq 0} $, which is bounded, does not tend to 0 [cf. Exercise 22 d)]. Show that the completion of $ \mathbf{Q} $ with respect to the topology $ \mathcal{T} $ is isomorphic to the product of the topological fields $ \mathbf{Q}_p $ and $ \mathbf{Q}_q $.

b) If $ P $ is the set of all prime numbers then the least upper bound $ \mathcal{T}_0 $ of the topologies $ \mathcal{T}_p $ for $ p \in P $ is compatible with the field structure of $ \mathbf{Q} $ but is not locally bounded [Exercise 20 c)]. What is the completion of $ \mathbf{Q} $ with respect to the topology $ \mathcal{T}_0 $?
