Show that on a ring $ A $ (resp. a division ring $ K $), the least upper bound $ \mathcal{T} $ of a family $ (\mathcal{T}_i) $ of topologies compatible with the ring structure of $ A $ (resp. the division ring structure of $ K $) is compatible with this structure. A set $ M $ which is left (resp. right) bounded for each of the topologies $ \mathcal{T}_i $ is also left (resp. right) bounded for $ \mathcal{T} $.
