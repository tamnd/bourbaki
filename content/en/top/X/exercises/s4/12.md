b) Conversely, let $A$ be a closed subalgebra of $\mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{R})$ containing the identity element. Show that there exists a continuous surjection $f$ of $X$ onto a compact space $Y$ such that $af$ is an isomorphism of $\mathcal{C}(Y; \mathbf{R})$ onto $A$. {If $(u_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L}$ is a family which is dense in $A$, consider the continuous mapping $x \to (u_\lambda(x))$ of $X$ into $\mathbf{R}^L$ and use the Weierstrass-Stone theorem.}
