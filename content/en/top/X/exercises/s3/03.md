Let $ X $ be a metric space, $ d $ the metric on $ X $. For each $ x \in X $ let $ d_x $ be the real-valued function $ y \to d(x, y) $ which is continuous on $ X $. Show that the mapping $ x \to d_x $ is an *isometry* of $ X $ onto a subspace of $ \mathcal{C}_u(X; \mathbf{R}) $ [endowed with the pseudometric $ \delta(u, v) = \sup_{x \in X} |u(x) - v(x)| $].
