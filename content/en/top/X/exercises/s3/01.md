Let $ X $ be a topological space and $ Y $ a metric space. Show that the set of all mappings of $ X $ into $ Y $ whose oscillation (Chapter IX, § 2, no. 3) at every point of $ X $ is $ \leq \alpha $ (where $ \alpha $ is a given real number $ > 0 $) is closed in the space $ \mathcal{F}_u(X; Y) $.
