Let $\Gamma$ be the group of all homeomorphisms of the real plane $\mathbf{R}^2$, endowed with the topology of pointwise convergence. Show that the mapping $(u, v) \to v \circ u$ of $\Gamma \times \Gamma$ into $\Gamma$ is not continuous. $$
