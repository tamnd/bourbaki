Let $ X, Y $ be two Hausdorff uniform spaces. For each continuous mapping $ f : X \to Y $, let $ G(f) \subset X \times Y $ be the graph of $ f $; $ G(f) $ is closed in $ X \times Y $ (Chapter I, § 8, no. 1, Proposition 2, Corollary 2); hence $ f \to G(f) $ is an injective mapping of $ \mathcal{C}(X; Y) $ into the set $ \tilde{\mathfrak{F}}(X \times Y) $ of non-empty closed subsets of $ X \times Y $.

$ a) $ Show that the mapping $ f \to G(f) $ of $ \mathcal{C}_u(X; Y) $ into $ \tilde{\mathfrak{F}}(X \times Y) $ is uniformly continuous when $ \tilde{\mathfrak{F}}(X \times Y) $ carries the uniformity defined in Chapter II, § 1, Exercise 5.

$ b) $ Let $ \Gamma $ be the image of $ \mathcal{C}(X; Y) $ in $ \tilde{\mathfrak{F}}(X \times Y) $ under the mapping $ f \to G(f) $, and let $ \varphi : \Gamma \to \mathcal{C}_u(X; Y) $ be the inverse mapping. Show that if $ X $ is compact, then $ \varphi $ is continuous on $ \Gamma $ (argue by contradiction).

$ c) $ Take both $ X $ and $ Y $ to be the compact interval $[0, 1]$ of $ \mathbf{R} $. Show that $ \varphi $ is not uniformly continuous on $ \Gamma $.
