Let X be a topological space, Y a uniform space, H an equicontinuous subset of $\mathcal{C}(X; Y)$, and $\Phi$ a filter on H. Show that the set of all $x \in X$ such that $\Phi(x)$ is a Cauchy filter base on Y is closed in X.
