Let $ X $ be a topological space, $ Y $ a uniform space, $ H $ an equicontinuous subset of $ C(X; Y) $ and $ V $ a symmetric entourage of $ Y $. Given a point $ x \in X $, show that the set of points $ x' \in X $ for which there is an integer $ n $ (depending on $ x' $) such that $ H(x') \subset \overset{n}{V}(H(x)) $ is both open and closed in $ X $. Deduce that, if $ K $ is any compact connected subset of $ X $ and if $ x_0 $ is any point of $ K $, there is an integer $ n > 0 $ such that $ H(K) \subset \overset{n}{V}(H(x_0)) $.
