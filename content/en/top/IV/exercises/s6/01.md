Let $ f $ be a continuous mapping of an open interval $ I \subset \mathbf{R} $ into $ \mathbf{R} $. Show that if $ f(I) $ is open and if, for each $ y \in \mathbf{R} $, the set $ f^{-1}(y) $ has at most two distinct points, then $ f $ is monotone.
