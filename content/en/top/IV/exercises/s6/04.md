Let $f$ be a lower semi-continuous function on a topological space $X$, and let $\varphi$ be an increasing lower semi-continuous function on $f(E) \subset \overline{\mathbf{R}}$. Show that $\varphi \circ f$ is lower semi-continuous on $X$.
