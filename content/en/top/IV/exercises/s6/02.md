Let $ B $ be a base of $ \mathbf{R} $ considered as a vector space over the field $ \mathbf{Q} $ ("Hamel base"). $ B $ is uncountable ($ \S 2 $, Exercise 2). Let $ \varphi $ be a bijection of a subset $ C \neq B $ of $ B $ onto the set $ B $. Define a mapping $ f $ of $ \mathbf{R} $ into $ \mathbf{R} $ as follows: if $ x = \sum_{\xi \in B} \lambda(\xi) \xi $, where $ \lambda(\xi) \in \mathbf{Q} $ and $ \lambda(\xi) = 0 $ for all but a finite number of elements $ \xi \in B $, then $ f(x) = \sum_{\xi \in C} \lambda(\xi) \varphi(\xi) $. Show that $ f(x + y) = f(x) + f(y) $, but that, for each $ z \in \mathbf{R} $, $ f^{-1}(z) $ is a dense subset of $ \mathbf{R} $, which implies that $ f $ is not bounded above nor below in any interval of $ \mathbf{R} $.
