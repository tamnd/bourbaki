Let $X_1, X_2$ be two directed sets and let $f$ be a real-valued function defined on $X_1 \times X_2$ such that, for each $x_1 \in X_1$, the mapping $x_2 \to f(x_1, x_2)$ is increasing on $X_2$, and for each $x_2 \in X_2$, the mapping x_1 \to f(x_1, x_2) \text{ is increasing on } X_1. \text{ Show that } f \text{ has a limit in } \overline{\mathbf{R}} \text{ with respect to the product of the section filters of } X_1 \text{ and } X_2, \text{ and that this limit is the least upper bound of } f.
