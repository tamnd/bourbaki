Let $ (u_n) $ be a sequence of finite real numbers and let $ (\varepsilon_n) $ be a sequence of numbers $ \geq 0 $, such that $ \lim_{n \to \infty} \varepsilon_n = 0 $ and $ u_{n+1} \geq u_n - \varepsilon_n $ for all integers $ n \geq 0 $. Let $ a = \liminf_{n \to \infty} u_n $ and let $ b = \limsup_{n \to \infty} u_n $; show that the set of cluster points of the sequence $ (u_n) $ is the interval $[a, b]$.
