Let $ f $ be a real-valued function defined on an infinite set $ X $ and let $ \mathcal{G} $ be the filter of complements of finite subsets of $ X $. Show that $ \lim \sup_{\mathcal{G}} f $ is the least upper bound of the set of real numbers $ x $ such that the set $ f([x, +\infty]) $ is infinite.
