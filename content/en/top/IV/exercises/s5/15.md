Let $ (x_n) $ be a sequence of finite real numbers and let $ (p_n) $ be a sequence of finite real numbers $ \geq 0 $ such that $ \lim_{n \to \infty} \left( \sum_{i=0}^n p_i \right) = +\infty $. Let $ y_n = \left( \sum_{i=0}^n p_i x_i \right) / \left( \sum_{i=0}^n p_i \right) $ for those values of $ n $ such that $ \sum_{i=1}^n p_i \neq 0 $. Show that $ \liminf_{n \to \infty} x_n \leq \liminf_{n \to \infty} y_n \leq \limsup_{n \to \infty} y_n \leq \limsup_{n \to \infty} x_n $.

Let $ H $ be any non-empty subset of the set of cluster points of the sequence $ (x_n) $ in $ \overline{\mathbf{R}} $. Show that the sequence $ (p_n) $ of finite real numbers $ \geq 0 $ can be determined so that $ \lim_{n \to \infty} \left( \sum_{i=0}^n p_i \right) = +\infty $ and so that the set of cluster points of the corresponding sequence $ (y_n) $ contains $ H $. [Reduce to the case where $ H $ is countable, then define $ (p_n) $ by induction, taking its terms to be 0 or 1].

Deduce that the sequence $ (x_n) $ converges in $ \overline{\mathbf{R}} $ if and only if the sequence $ (y_n) $ converges in $ \overline{\mathbf{R}} $ for every sequence $ (p_n) $ of numbers $ \geq 0 $ such that $ \lim_{n \to \infty} \left( \sum_{i=0}^n p_i \right) = +\infty $.
