Let $ (r_n) $ be an increasing sequence of finite real numbers $ > 0 $, such that $ \lim r_n = +\infty $. For each finite real number $ r > 0 $, let $ N(r) $ denote the largest index $ n $ such that $ r_n \leq r $. Show that

$$
\limsup_{r \to \infty} \frac{N(r)}{r} = \limsup_{n \to \infty} \frac{n}{r_n}, \quad \liminf_{r \to \infty} \frac{N(r)}{r} = \liminf_{n \to \infty} \frac{n}{r_n}.
$$
