Let $ f, g $ be two real-valued functions defined on a set $ X $ filtered by a filter $ \mathfrak{F} $. Show that
$$
\lim \sup_{\mathfrak{F}} (\sup (f, g)) = \sup (\lim \sup_{\mathfrak{F}} f, \lim \sup_{\mathfrak{F}} g).
$$
Give an example of a set $ X $ filtered by a filter $ \mathfrak{F} $, and an infinite family $ (f_i) $ of real-valued functions defined on $ X $, such that
$$
\sup_l (\lim \sup_{\mathfrak{F}} f_i) < \lim \sup_{\mathfrak{F}} (\sup f_i).
$$
