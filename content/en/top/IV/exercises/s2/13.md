Let $ a $ be an irrational number $ > 0 $. For each rational number $ x $, let $ f_a(x) $ be the real number in the interval $[0, a[$ such that $ x - f_a(x) $ is an integral multiple of $ a $. Show that $ f_a $ is a continuous injective mapping of $ \mathbf{Q} $ into $[0, a[$. Deduce with the help of Exercise 9 that there exist continuous bijective mappings of $ \mathbf{Q} $ onto itself, whose inverse mapping is not continuous at any point.
