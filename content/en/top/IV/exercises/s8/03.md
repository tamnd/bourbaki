Let $ a $ be an integer > 1. A real number $ x $ is rational if and only if its expansion to base $ a $, say $ x = p_0 + \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} u_n a^{-n} $, is periodic, that is to say if and only if there exist two integers $ n_0 $ and $ r > 0 $ such that $ u_{n+r} = u_n $ for all $ n \geq n_0 $.
