Let $ (u_n) $ be a summable sequence of numbers > 0 in $ \mathbf{R} $ which satisfies the following conditions : $ u_{n+1} \leq u_n $ and $ u_n \leq \sum_{k=1}^{\infty} u_{n+k} $ for all $ n \geq 0 $. Show that for every number $ a $ such that $ 0 < a \leq s = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} u_n $, there exists a subset I of $ \mathbf{N} $ such that $ a = \sum_{n \in I} u_n $. These conditions are satisfied in particular if $ u_{n+1} \leq u_n \leq 2u_{n+1} $ for all $ n $. Consider the case of dyadic expansions.
