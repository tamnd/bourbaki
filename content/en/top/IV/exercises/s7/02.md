Let $ (x_i) $ be a family of real numbers all of which belong to the interval $ A' $ (resp. $ A'' $) and which satisfies condition b) of Exercise 1. Show that every subfamily of $ (x_i) $ is summable in $ \overline{\mathbf{R}} $, and that the sum of the $ x' $ is associative (cf. Chapter III, § 5, no. 3, Theorem 2).
