Let $(d_n)$ be a sequence of finite real numbers $\geq 0$ such that
$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} d_n = + \infty.
$$
What can be said about the convergence of the series whose general terms are
$$
\frac{d_n}{1 + d_n}, \quad \frac{d_n}{1 + nd_n}, \quad \frac{d_n}{1 + n^2 d_n}, \quad \frac{d_n}{1 + d_n}?
$$
