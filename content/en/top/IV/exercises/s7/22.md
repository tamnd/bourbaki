For $n \geqslant 2$, let $u_{2n-1} = -1/\sqrt{n}$ and $u_{2n} = (1 + \sqrt{n})/n$. The product whose general factor is $1 + u_n$ is convergent, but the series whose general term is $u_n$ is not convergent.
