Let $ (x_n)_{n \geq 0} $ be a decreasing sequence of finite real numbers $ \geq 0 $. This sequence is summable in $ \mathbf{R} $ if and only if the sequence $ (2^n x_{2^n})_{n \geq 0} $ is summable in $ \mathbf{R} $ ("Cauchy's condensation test"). \* Deduce that the series whose general term is $ 1/n^\alpha $ is convergent if $ \alpha > 1 $ and not convergent if $ 0 < \alpha \leq 1 $. \*
