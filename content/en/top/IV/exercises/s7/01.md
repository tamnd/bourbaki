Let $ (x_i) $ be a family of real numbers, all of which belong to the interval $ A' = ]-\infty, +\infty] $ (resp. $ A'' = [-\infty, +\infty[ $). The family $ (x_i) $ is summable in $ \overline{\mathbf{R}} $ if and only if one of the following conditions is satisfied:
a) At least one of the numbers $ x_i $ is equal to $ +\infty $ (resp. $ -\infty $).
b) At least one of the two sums $ \sum_i x_i^+ $, $ \sum_i x_i^- $ is finite.

In the first case we have $ \sum_i x_i = +\infty $ (resp. $ -\infty $); in the second case, $ \sum_i x_i = \sum_i x_i^+ - \sum_i x_i^- $.
