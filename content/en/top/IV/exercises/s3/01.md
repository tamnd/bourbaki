Let $x$ be a real number $\geqslant 0$ and let $p$ and $q$ be two integers $> 0$. Prove the relations

$$
(x^{1/p})^{1/q} = x^{1/pq}, \quad (x^p)^{1/q} = (x^{1/q})^p, \quad x^{1/p} x^{1/q} = (x^{p+q})^{1/pq}.
$$
