The sphere $ S_5 $ being considered as a subspace of $ \mathbf{C}^3 $, consider the mapping of $ S_5 $ into $ \mathbf{R}^7 $ which sends each point $ x = (x_1, x_2, x_3) $ of $ S_5 $ ($ x_1, x_2, x_3 $ complex) to the point $ y = (y_i)_{1 \leq i \leq 7} $ of $ \mathbf{R}^7 $, where
$$
y_1 = |x_1|^2 - |x_2|^2, \quad y_2 = \mathcal{R}(x_1 \overline{x}_2), \quad y_3 = \mathcal{J}(x_1 \overline{x}_2), \quad y_4 = \mathcal{R}(x_1 \overline{x}_3),
y_5 = \mathcal{J}(x_1 \overline{x}_3), \quad y_6 = \mathcal{R}(x_2 \overline{x}_3), \quad y_7 = \mathcal{J}(x_2 \overline{x}_3).
$$
This function has the same value at two points $ x, x' $ of $ S_5 $ such that $ x' = \zeta x $, where $ |\zeta| = 1 $. Show that, by passing to the quotient, it induces a homeomorphism of $ P_2(\mathbf{C}) $ onto a subspace of $ \mathbf{R}^7 $.
