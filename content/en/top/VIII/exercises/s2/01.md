Let $ a $ be a non-zero complex number, and $ n $ an integer > 0. For each real number $ r > 0 $ such that $ r^n \leq |a| $, show that there exists $ z \in \mathbf{C} $ such that $ |z| = r $ and $ |a + z^n| = |a| - r^n $. Deduce that, if $ f $ is a polynomial of degree > 0, with complex coefficients, we cannot have $ |f(z_0)| \leq |f(z)| $ at all points $ z $ of a neighbourhood of a point $ z_0 $, provided that $ f(z_0) \neq 0 $.
