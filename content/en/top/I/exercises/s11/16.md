Let $ X $ be the subspace of the real plane $ \mathbf{R}^2 $ which is the union of the line $ y = 0 $, the closed segments with end points $ (0, 1) $ and $ (n, 1/(1+n)) $, and the half-lines $ x \leq n, y = 1/(1+n) $ ($ n $ an integer $ > 0 $). Show that $ X $ is a connected space but that there is a component $ C $ of the complement of $ (0, 1) $ in $ X $ such that $ (0, 1) \notin \overline{C} $ [(compare with Exercises 4 a) and 15 b)].
