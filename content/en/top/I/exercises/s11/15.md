Let $ X $ be a connected, locally connected space.
a) Let $ M, N $ be two disjoint non-empty closed subsets of $ X $. Show that $ C(M \cup N) $ has a component whose closure meets both $ M $ and $ N $. [If $ C $ is a component of $ C(M \cup N) $ whose closure does not meet $ M $, observe that $ C $ is both open and closed in $ CN $].
b) If $ A $ is a closed subset of $ X $ distinct from $ X $, deduce from a) that every component of $ A $ meets the closure of $ CA $.
