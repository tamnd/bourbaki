Let $ X $ be a topological space.
a) Let the set $ \mathfrak{P}_0(X) $ carry one of the topologies $ \mathcal{T}_\Omega, \mathcal{T}_\Phi $ ($ \S 2 $, Exercise 7) or $ \mathcal{T}_\Theta $ ($ \S 8 $, Exercise 12). Show that if a subset $ \mathfrak{B} $ of $ \mathfrak{P}_0(X) $ is connected and if each $ M \in \mathfrak{B} $ is connected, then $ \bigcup_{M \in \mathfrak{B}} M $ is connected.
b) Let $ \mathfrak{S} $ be a subset of $ \mathfrak{P}_0(X) $ which contains the set of all finite non-empty subsets of $ X $. Show that if $ X $ is connected then so is $ \mathfrak{S} $ [use Exercise 7 b) of $ \S 2 $ and Proposition 8 of $ \S 4 $, considering the mappings $ (x_1, \ldots, x_n) \to \{ x_1, \ldots, x_n \} $].
