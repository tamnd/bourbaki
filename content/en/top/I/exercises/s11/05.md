a) Give an example, in the real plane $ \mathbf{R}^2 $, of a decreasing sequence $ (A_n) $ of connected sets whose intersection is not connected (cf. Chapter II, § 4, Exercise 14).

b) In $ \mathbf{R}^2 $, let $ X $ be the set which is the union of the open half-planes $ y > 0 $ and $ y < 0 $ and the points $ (n, 0) $ where $ n $ is an integer $ > 0 $.

X is connected with respect to the topology $ \mathcal{T}_0 $ induced by that of $ \mathbf{R}^2 $. Define a sequence $ (\mathcal{T}_n) $ of topologies on X such that $ \mathcal{T}_{n+1} $ is finer than $ \mathcal{T}_n $, and such that X is connected with respect to each $ \mathcal{T}_n $ but not connected with respect to the topology which is the least upper bound of the sequence $ (\mathcal{T}_n) $ [pass from $ \mathcal{T}_n $ to $ \mathcal{T}_{n+1} $ by introducing new neighbourhoods of the point $ (n + 1, 0) $].*
