Let $ X $ be a non-empty topological space and $ R $ an equivalence relation on $ X $. Show that if $ X/R $ is regarded as a subset of $ \mathfrak{P}_0(X) $, then the quotient topology on $ X/R $ is coarser than the topologies induced on $ X/R $ by the topologies $ \mathcal{T}_\Omega $ and $ \mathcal{T}_\Phi $ on $ \mathfrak{P}_0(X) $ (\$ 2, Exercise 7).
