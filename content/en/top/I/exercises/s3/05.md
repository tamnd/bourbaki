Let $ Y, Z $ be two subspaces of a topological space $ X $ such that $ X = Y \cup Z $. Show that if a subset $ M $ of $ Y \cap Z $ is open (resp. closed) in both $ Y $ and $ Z $, then $ M $ is open (resp. closed) in $ X $.
