Let $ X $ be the subspace $[0, 1]$ of the real line $ \mathbf{R} $, and let $ S $ be the equivalence relation on $ X $ whose equivalence classes are $\{0, 1\}$ and the sets $\{x\}$ for $0 < x < 1$. Show that $ X $ has no continuous section with respect to $ S $. \*
