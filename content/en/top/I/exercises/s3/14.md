Let $ X $ be a topological space which is the sum of two subspaces $ X_1 $ and $ X_2 $, and let $ X/R $ be the space obtained by pasting together $ X_1 $ and $ X_2 $ along a subspace $ A_1 $ of $ X_1 $ and a subspace $ A_2 $ of $ X_2 $ by means of a homeomorphism $ h $ of $ A_1 $ onto $ A_2 $. Show that the canonical images of $ X_1 $ and $ X_2 $ in $ X/R $ are homeomorphic to $ X_1 $ and $ X_2 $ respectively.
