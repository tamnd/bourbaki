Consider the following three subspaces of $ \mathbf{R} $: $ X_1 = ] - 1, 1[ $, $ X_2 = ] - 2, - 1[, X_3 = ] 1, 2[ $. Let $ X $ be their union; $ X $ is then the sum of these three subspaces. Let $ A_2 $ (resp. $ A_3 $) be the set of irrational numbers contained in $ X_2 $ (resp. $ X_3 $); let $ B_1 $ (resp. $ B_2 $) be the set of rational numbers contained in ]—$ 1, 0[ $ (resp. $ X_2 $); and let $ C_1 $ (resp. $ C_3 $) be the set of rational numbers contained in ]$ 0, 1[ $ (resp. $ X_3 $). Let $ X/R $ be the space obtained by pasting together the $ X_i $ (i) along $ A_2 $ and $ A_3 $ by means of the homeomorphism $ x \to x + 3 $; (ii) along $ B_1 $ and $ B_2 $ by means of the homeomorphism $ x \to x - 1 $; (iii) along $ C_1 $ and $ C_3 $ by means of the homeomorphism $ x \to x + 1 $. Show that the canonical image of $ X_1 $ in $ X/R $ is not homeomorphic to $ X_1 $. If $ Y = X_1 \cup B_2 \cup C_3 $ is the saturation of $ X_1 $ with respect to $ R $, show that the quotient space $ Y/R_Y $ is not homeomorphic to the canonical image of $ Y $ in $ X/R $. \*
