Let $ S $ be the equivalence relation on the rational line $ \mathbf{Q} $ which is obtained by identifying all the points of $ \mathbf{Z} $. Show that $ S $ is closed, and that if $ U $ is the relation of equality on $ \mathbf{Q} $, then the canonical bijection of $ (\mathbf{Q} \times \mathbf{Q})/(U \times S) $ onto $ \mathbf{Q} \times (\mathbf{Q}/S) $ is not a homeomorphism.
