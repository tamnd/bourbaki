Show that a well-ordered set $ X $ with the topology $ \mathcal{T}_-(X) $ ($ \S 2 $, Exercise 5) is locally compact, and is compact if and only if $ X $ has a greatest element. Deduce that every set can be topologized in such a way that it becomes a compact space.
