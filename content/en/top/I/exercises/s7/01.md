Let $\mathcal{T}_1, \mathcal{T}_2$ be two topologies on the same set $X$. Show that $\mathcal{T}_1$ is finer than $\mathcal{T}_2$ if and only if every filter on $X$ which is convergent in the topology $\mathcal{T}_1$ converges to the same point in the topology $\mathcal{T}_2$.
