Let $\mathscr{T}_1, \mathscr{T}_2$ be two topologies on the same set $X$. Show that $\mathscr{T}_1$ is finer than $\mathscr{T}_2$ if and only if every filter on $X$ which is convergent in the topology $\mathscr{T}_1$ converges to the same point in the topology $\mathscr{T}_2$.
