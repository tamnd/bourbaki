Give an example of a continuous mapping $f : X \to Y$ (where $X$ and $Y$ are Hausdorff spaces) such that the inverse image $\overline{f^{-1}(K)}$ of every compact subset $K$ of $Y$ is compact, but which is not proper (cf. § 9, Exercise 4).
