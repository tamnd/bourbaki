Let $ Y $ be a locally compact space, $ X $ a topological space. Show that a mapping $ f : X \to Y $ is continuous if and only if, for each compact space $ Y' $ which contains $ Y $ as a subspace, the graph of $ f $ is closed in $ X \times Y' $. [Use Exercises 13 and 10 $ a) $].
