Let $f : X \to Y$ be a proper mapping and let $A$ be a subspace of $X$. Show that if $A = \overline{f^{-1}(f(A))} \cap \overline{A}$ then the restriction $f|A$ is a proper mapping of $A$ into $f(A)$. Is this condition necessary?
