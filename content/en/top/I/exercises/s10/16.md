Let X be a locally compact space, R an equivalence relation on X, C the graph of R in $ X \times X $. Show that C is closed in $ X \times X $ if and only if, for each compact subset K of X, the saturation of K with respect to R is closed in X. (To prove necessity, use Corollary 5 of Theorem 1 of no. 2).
