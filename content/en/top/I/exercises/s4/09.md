Let $ (X_i)_{i \in I} $ be an infinite family of topological spaces. Study the topology on the product set
$$
X = \prod_{i \in I} X_i
$$
generated by subsets of the form $ \prod_{i \in I} U_i $,
where $ U_i $ is an open subset of $ X_i $ for each $ i \in I $ and the set of indices $ i $ for which $ U_i \neq X_i $ has cardinal less than $ c $, where $ c $ is a given infinite cardinal. Consider what becomes of the propositions of § 4 for this topology.
