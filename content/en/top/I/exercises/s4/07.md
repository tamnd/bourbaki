Let $ X $ be the sum of a family $ (X_\lambda) $ of topological spaces, $ Y $ the sum of a family $ (Y_\mu) $ of topological spaces. Show that the product space $ X \times Y $ is the sum of the $ X_\lambda \times Y_\mu $.
