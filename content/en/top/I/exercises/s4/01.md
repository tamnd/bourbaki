Let $X, Y$ be two topological spaces, $A$ a subset of $X$ and $B$ a subset of $Y$. Show that $\operatorname{Fr}(A \times B) = (\operatorname{Fr}(A) \times \overline{B}) \cup (\overline{A} \times \operatorname{Fr}(B))$.
