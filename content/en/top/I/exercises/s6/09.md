a) Show that every ultrafilter which is finer than the intersection of a finite number of filters is finer than at least one of them (use Proposition 5 of no. 4).

b) Give an example of an ultrafilter which is finer than the intersection of an infinite family of ultrafilters, but which is not equal to any of the ultrafilters of this family (consider the family of ultrafilters on an infinite set $X$ each of which has a base consisting of a single point).
