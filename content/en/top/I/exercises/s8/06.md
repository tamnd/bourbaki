a) Let $ X $ be a Hausdorff space and $ D $ a dense subset of $ X $. Show that $ \operatorname{card}(X) \leq 2^{2^{\operatorname{card}(D)}} $ (observe that every point of $ X $ is the limit of a filter base on $ D $). Show that this upper bound for $ \operatorname{card}(X) $ cannot be improved ($ \S 4 $, Exercise 5 b)) and deduce that on an infinite set $ A $ the set of ultrafilters and the set of filters are each equipotent to $ \mathfrak{P}(\mathfrak{P}(A)) $.

b) Let $ K $ be the discrete space consisting of the two numbers $ 0 $ and $ 1 $. Let $ A $ be an infinite set. Deduce from a) that if $ \operatorname{card}(A) \geq 2^{2^{\operatorname{card}(N)}} $, then the product space $ K^A $ satisfies the condition $ (D_{IV}) $ of Exercise 7 of $ \S 1 $, but satisfies neither $ (D_{II}) $ nor $ (D_{III}) $. ($ \S 4 $, Exercise 4).
