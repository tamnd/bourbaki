Let $ X $ be a topological space which has at least two points.
a) Show that the topologies $ \mathcal{T}_\Omega $ and $ \mathcal{T}_\Phi $ on $ \mathfrak{P}_0(X) $ (\S 2, Exercise 7) are not topologies of an accessible space.
b) Let $ \mathcal{T}_\Theta $ denote the least upper bound of the topologies $ \mathcal{T}_\Omega $ and $ \mathcal{T}_\Phi $. Show that the set $ \mathfrak{F}(X) $ of non-empty closed subsets of $ X $, with the topology induced by $ \mathcal{T}_\Theta $, is a Kolmogoroff space, and that if $ X $ is accessible, so is $ \mathfrak{F}(X) $.
c) Supposing that $ X $ is accessible, show that the space $ \mathfrak{F}(X) $ (with the topology induced by $ \mathcal{T}_\Theta $) is Hausdorff if and only if $ X $ is regular.
