a) If a topology on a set $ X $ has a finite subbase, and if $ X $ is accessible with respect to this topology, then $ X $ is finite and the topology is discrete.

b) If, in an accessible space $ X $, *every* intersection of open sets is open, then $ X $ is discrete (cf. § 7, Exercise 6).
