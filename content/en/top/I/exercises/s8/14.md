Show that the axiom $ (O_{III}) $ is equivalent to each of the following axioms:

(O''''III) If $ F $ is any closed subset of $ X $ then the intersection of all the closed neighbourhoods of $ F $ is equal to $ F $.

(O'''''III) If $ \mathfrak{F} $ is any filter on $ X $ which converges to a point $ a $, then the filter $ \mathfrak{F} $ on $ X $ which has the closures of the sets of $ \mathfrak{F} $ as a base also converges to $ a $.
