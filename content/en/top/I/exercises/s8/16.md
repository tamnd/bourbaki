Let $(X_i)$ be an infinite family of topological spaces, and give the product set $X = \prod X_i$ one of the topologies defined in Exercise 9 of § 4. Show that if the $X_i$ are Kolmogoroff (resp. accessible, Hausdorff, regular) spaces then so is $X$.
