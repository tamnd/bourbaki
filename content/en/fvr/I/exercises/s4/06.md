Let $ f $ be a convex function on an interval $ ]a,\ +\infty[ $; show that $ f(x)/x $ has a limit (finite or equal to $ +\infty $) as $ x $ tends to $ +\infty $; this limit is also that of $ f'_d(x) $ and of $ f'_g(x) $; it is $ > 0 $ if $ f(x) $ tends to $ +\infty $ as $ x $ tends to $ +\infty $.
