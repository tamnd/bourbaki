Let $f$ and $g$ be two positive convex functions on an interval $I = [a, b]$: suppose that there exists a number $c \in I$ such that in each of the intervals $[a, c]$ and $[c, b]$ the functions $f$ and $g$ vary in the same sense. Show that the product $fg$ is convex on $I$.
