For a function $ f $ to be convex on a compact interval $[a,\ b] \subset \mathbf{R}$ it is necessary and sufficient that it be convex on $[a,\ b[$ and that one has $ f(a) \geq f(a+) $ and $ f(b) \geq f(b-) $.
