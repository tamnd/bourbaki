Let $ f $ be a finite real function, upper semi-continuous on an open interval $ I \subset \mathbf{R} $. Then $ f $ is convex if and only if $ \limsup_{h \to 0, h \neq 0} \frac{f(x+h) + f(x-h) - 2f(x)}{h^2} \geq 0 $ for all $ x \in I $. (First show that, for all $ \varepsilon > 0 $ the function $ f(x) + \varepsilon x^2 $ is convex, using prop. 9 of I, p. 31.)
