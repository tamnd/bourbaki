With the same notation as in exerc. 8 suppose that $ f $ is $ n $ times differentiable on I but otherwise arbitrary. Let $ g $ be the polynomial of degree $ n - 1 $ (with real coefficients) such that at the point $ x_i $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq p $) both $ g $ and its first $ n_i - 1 $ derivatives are respectively equal to $ f $ and its first $ n_i - 1 $ derivatives. Show that we have
$$
f(x) = g(x) + \frac{(x - x_1)^{n_1}(x - x_2)^{n_2} \ldots (x - x_p)^{n_p}}{n!} f^{(n)}(\xi)
$$
where $ \xi $ is interior to the smallest interval containing the points $ x_i $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq p $) and $ x $. (Apply exerc. 8 to the function of $ t $)
$$
f(t) - g(t) - a \frac{(t - x_1)^{n_1}(t - x_2)^{n_2} \ldots (t - x_p)^{n_p}}{n!}
$$
where $ a $ is a suitably chosen constant.)
