Let $ \mathbf{f} $ be a continuous vector function defined on an open interval $ I \subset \mathbf{R} $, with values in a normed space $ E $ over $ \mathbf{R} $, and admitting a right derivative at every point of $ I $. Show that the set of points of $ I $ where $ \mathbf{f} $ admits a derivative is the complement of a meagre subset of $ I $ (use exerc. 5 b) of I, p. 36, and prop. 6 of I, p. 18).
