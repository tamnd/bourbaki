Let $ f $ and $ g $ be two finite real functions, continuous on $ [a,\ b] $, both having a finite derivative on $ ]a,\ b[ $; show that there exists a $ c $ such that $ a < c < b $ and that

$$
\begin{vmatrix}
f(b) - f(a) & g(b) - g(a) \\
f'(c) & g'(c)
\end{vmatrix} = 0.
$$
