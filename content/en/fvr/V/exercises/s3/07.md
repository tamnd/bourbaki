Let $g$ be a strictly positive real function, continuous on an interval $[x_0, +\infty[$, and such that, for every $\mu > 0$, one has $g(\mu x) \sim g(x)$. Deduce from exerc. 6 that $g$ is of order 0 relative to $x$.
