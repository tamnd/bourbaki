Let $ \varphi $ be a strictly positive function, defined and increasing for $ x > 0 $, and such that $ \varphi \gg 1 $.

a) Show that if the function $ \log \varphi(x)/\log x $ is increasing, then the relation $ f \ll g $ between functions $ > 0 $ implies that $ \varphi \circ f \ll \varphi \circ g $ if $ g \gg 1 $.

b) Give an example where $ \log \varphi(x)/\log x $ is decreasing, $ f $ and $ g $ are two functions $ > 0 $ such that $ g \gg 1 $ and $ f \sim g $, but $ \varphi \circ f $ is not equivalent to $ \varphi \circ g $.
