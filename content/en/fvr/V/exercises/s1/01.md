Show that for $x$ real tending to $+\infty$ the function
$$
f(x) = (x \cos^2 x + \sin^2 x) e^{x^2}
$$
is monotone, but is neither comparable to $e^{x^2}$ nor weakly comparable to $x^{1/2} e^{x^2}$.
