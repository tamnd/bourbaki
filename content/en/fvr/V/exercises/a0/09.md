Show that the series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{e_n(x)}{e_n(n)}$ converges uniformly on every compact subset of $\mathbf{R}$, and that its sum $f(x)$ is such that $f(x) \gg e_n(x)$ for every integer $n$.
