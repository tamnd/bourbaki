Deduce from exerc. 13 that if $ f $ is an (H) function such that $ \log f(e^x) $ is of order $ < 1 $ relative to $ x $, the function $ f(x f(x)) $ is equivalent to a function of the form $ e^{g(x)} $, where $ g $ is a rational function of $ x, \log f(x) $, and of a certain number of derivatives of this last function.
