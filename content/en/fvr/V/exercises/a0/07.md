Let $ f $ be an (H) function having a definition sequence of $ n $ terms. Show that if one has $ e_p((l_{q+1}(x))^\beta) \ll f(x) \ll e_p((l_q(x))^\alpha) $ (resp. $ e_p((l_q(x))^\beta) \ll f(x) \ll e_{p+1}((l_q(x))^\alpha) $) for every $ \beta > 0 $ and every $ \alpha $ such that $ 0 < \alpha < 1 $, then necessarily $ p + q + l < n $ (argue by induction on $ n $, using methods similar to those of exerc. 1 (V, p. 263) and 4 (V, p. 264)).
