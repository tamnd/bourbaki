Let $f$ be an (H) function such that $1 \ll f(x) \ll x$. Show that in the Taylor formula
$$
f(x + f(x)) = f(x) + f(x)f'(x) + \frac{1}{2!}(f(x))^2 f''(x) + \cdots +
$$
$$
+ \frac{1}{n!}(f(x))^n f^{(n)}(x) + \frac{1}{(n+1)!}(f(x))^{n+1} f^{(n+1)}(x + \theta f(x))
$$
with $0 < \theta < 1$ (I, p. 42, exerc. 9) each term is negligible relative to the previous one. If $f$ is of order $< 1$ relative to $x$, the last term in this sum tends to 0 with $1/x$ once $n$ is sufficiently large.
