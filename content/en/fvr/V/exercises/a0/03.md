Let $ f $ and $ g $ be two (H) functions such that $ f/g $ is of order 0 relative to $ l_m(x) $; show that if $ g $ is not of order 0 relative to $ l_m(x) $ one has $ f'/g' \sim f/g $ (compare $ \log |f| $ and $ \log |g| $).
