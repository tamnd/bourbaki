Prove *Wallis' Formula*
$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \cdot \frac{2.4.6 \ldots 2n}{1.3.5 \ldots (2n-1)} = \sqrt{\pi}
$$
by using exerc. 31 and the inequality $ \sin^{n+1} x \leq \sin^n x $ for $ 0 \leq x \leq \pi/2 $.
