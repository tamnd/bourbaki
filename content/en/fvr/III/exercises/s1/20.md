Let D be the subset of $ \mathbf{C} $ which is the union of the set defined by $ -\pi < \mathcal{R}(z) \leq \pi $, $ \mathcal{I}(z) > 0 $, and the segment $ \mathcal{I}(z) = 0,\ 0 \leq \mathcal{R}(z) \leq \pi $. Show that the restriction of the function $ \cos z $ to D is a bijection of D onto $ \mathbf{C} $; the restriction of $ \cos z $ to the interior of D is a homeomorphism of this open set onto the complement, in $ \mathbf{C} $, of the half-line $ y = 0,\ x \leq 1 $.
