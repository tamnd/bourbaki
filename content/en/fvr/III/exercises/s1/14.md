Let $a$ be a real number such that $0 < a < \pi/2$. Show that the function
$$
\frac{\tan x}{x} - \frac{\tan a}{a}
$$
is strictly increasing on the interval $]a, \pi/2[$ (cf. I, p. 39, exerc. 11).
