Show (by induction on $n$) that
$$
\mathrm{D}''\left( \operatorname{Arc tan} x \right) = (-1)^n \cdot \frac{(n-1)!}{(1+x^2)^{n/2}} \sin \left( n \operatorname{Arc tan} \frac{1}{x} \right).
$$
