If $l_{m,n}$ is a primitive of $\sin^m x \cos^n x$ ($m$ and $n$ are arbitrary real numbers), show that
$$
I_{m+2,n} = -\frac{\sin^{m+1} x \cos^{n+1} x}{m + n + 2} + \frac{m + 1}{m + n + 2} I_{m,n}
$$
is a primitive of $\sin^{m+2} x \cos^n x$ if $m + n + 2 \neq 0$.

With the aid of this formula recover the formula (29) of III, p. 100 and prove the formula
$$
\int_0^{\pi/2} \sin^{2n+1} x \, dx = \frac{2.4.6 \ldots 2n}{1.3.5 \ldots (2n+1)} \quad (n \text{ an integer } \geqslant 0).
$$
