a) Show that for $\alpha > 0$ the derivative of
$$
I(\alpha) = \int_0^{+\infty} e^{-\alpha x} \frac{\sin x}{x} dx
$$
is equal to $-\int_0^{+\infty} e^{-\alpha x} \sin x \, dx$.

b) Deduce that $\int_0^{+\infty} \frac{\sin x}{x} dx = \frac{\pi}{2}$.
