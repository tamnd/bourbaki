Denote by $ A_n $ and $ G_n $ the usual arithmetic and geometric means of the first $ n $ numbers of a sequence $ (a_k) $ ($ k \geq 1 $) of positive numbers. Show that
$$
n \left( A_n - G_n \right) \leq (n+1) \left( A_{n+1} - G_{n+1} \right)
$$
with equality only if $ a_{n+1} = G_n $ (put $ a_{n+1} = x^{n+1},\ G_n = y^{n+1} $).
