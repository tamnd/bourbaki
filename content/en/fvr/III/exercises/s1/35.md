By differentiating with respect to the parameter and using exerc. 33 c), prove the formulae
$$
\int_0^{+\infty} e^{-x^2} \cos \alpha x \, dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2} e^{-\alpha^2},
$$
$$
\int_0^{+\infty} \frac{1 - e^{-\alpha x^2}}{x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi \alpha}
$$
$$
\int_0^{+\infty} \exp \left( -x^2 - \frac{\alpha^2}{x^2} \right) dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2} e^{-2\alpha} \quad (\alpha > 0).
$$
