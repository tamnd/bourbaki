For every real $ x $ and every number $ p > 1 $ prove the inequality
$$
|1 + x|^p \leq 1 + px + \frac{p(p-1)}{2} x^2 + \cdots + \frac{p(p-1)\ldots(p-m+2)}{(m-1)!} x^{m-1} \\
+ \frac{p(p-1)\ldots(p-m+1)}{m!} |x|^m + h_p\ |x|^p
$$
where one puts $ m = [p] $ (the integer part of $ p $) and
$$
h_p = \frac{p(p-1)\ldots(p-m+1)}{(m-1)!} \int_0^1 z^{p-m}(1-z)^{m-1}\ dz.
$$
