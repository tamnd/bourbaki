Let $ f $ be a real function that is $ n $ times differentiable at a point $ x $, and $ g $ a vector function $ n $ times differentiable at the point $ f(x) $. If one puts $ D^n(g(f(x))) = \sum_{k=1}^n g^{(k)}(f(x)) u_k(x) $, then $ u_k $ depends on the function $ f $ alone; deduce from this that $ u_k(x) $ is the coefficient of $ t^k $ in the expansion of $ e^{-tf(x)} D^n(e^{tf(x)}) $ (in terms of $ t $).
