Let $ f(X) $ be a polynomial of degree $ n $ with complex coefficients, and let

$$
\frac{1}{f(X)} = \sum_{j=1}^{q} \sum_{h=1}^{n_j} \frac{\lambda_{jh}}{(X - r_j)^h}
$$

be the decomposition of the rational fraction $ 1/f(X) $ into simple elements (*Alg.*, VII. 7, n 2). Show that, for any regulated function $ b $, the function

$$
t \mapsto \sum_{j=1}^{q} \sum_{h=1}^{n_j} \lambda_{jh} \int_{t_0}^{t} \frac{(t-s)^{h-1}}{(h-1)!} e^{l_j(t-s)} b(s) ds
$$

is an integral of the equation $ f(D)x = b(t) $.
