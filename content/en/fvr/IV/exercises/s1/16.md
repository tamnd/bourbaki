Let E be a vector space of finite dimension, and $ f $ a bounded function on $ I \times \mathbf{H} $ satisfying the conditions of IV, p. 165, lemma 1, such that the equation $ x' = f(t, x) $ has a unique solution $ u $ defined on $ I $, with values in $ \mathbf{H} $, equal to $ x_0 $ at the point $ t_0 $. Suppose further that for sufficiently large $ n $ there is an approximate integral $ u_n $ of $ x' = f(t, x) $ to within $ 1/n $, defined on $ I $, with values in $ \mathbf{H} $, equal to $ x_0 $ at the point $ t_0 $. Show that the sequence $ (u_n) $ converges uniformly to $ u $ on every compact interval contained in $ I $ (use the fact that the sequence $ (u_n) $ is equicontinuous on $ I $).
