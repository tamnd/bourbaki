Let $ x_n $ be the root of the equation $ \Gamma'(x) = 0 $ in the interval $ ]-n, -n+1[ $. Show that
$$
x_n = -n + \frac{1}{\log n} + O \left( \frac{1}{(\log n)^2} \right)
$$
(use the complements' relation and formula (5) of VII, p. 316). Deduce that
$$
\Gamma(x_n) \sim \frac{(-1)^n}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \ n^{-n-1/2} \ e^{n+1} \ \log n .
$$
