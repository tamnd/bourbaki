Let $t$ be a fixed real number $\neq 0$; show that as $s$ tends to $+\infty$
$$
|\Gamma(-s + it)| \sim \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}} \left( s^{s+1/2} \ e^{-s} \ \sqrt{\sinh^2 \pi t + \sin^2 \pi s} \right)^j
$$
(use the complements' relation).
