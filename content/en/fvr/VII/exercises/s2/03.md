Let $ s $ be a fixed real number; show that when $ t $ tends to $ +\infty $ or to $ -\infty $ one has
$$
|\Gamma(s + it)| \sim \sqrt{2\pi} \ |t|^{s-1/2} \ e^{-(\pi/2)|t|}
$$
and
$$
\frac{\Gamma'(s + it)}{\Gamma(s + it)} \sim \log |t| .
$$
