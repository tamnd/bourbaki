For every integer $k > 1$ put $S_k = \sum_{n=1}^\infty n^{-k}$. Show that, for $-1 < x \leq 1$ one has
$$
\log \Gamma(1 + x) = -\gamma x + \sum_{k=2}^\infty (-1)^k \frac{S_k}{k} x^k
$$
the series on the right being uniformly convergent on every compact subset of ]$-1, 1$], and absolutely convergent for $|x| < 1$.
