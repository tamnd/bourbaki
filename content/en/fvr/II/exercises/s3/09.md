Deduce from exerc. 8 that the integrals
$$
\int_0^{\infty} dx \int_0^{\infty} e^{-x^2} \sin y \, dy \quad \text{and} \quad \int_0^{\infty} dy \int_0^{\infty} e^{-y^2} \sin y \, dx
$$
exist and are equal.*
