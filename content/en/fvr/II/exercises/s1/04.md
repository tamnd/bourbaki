Give an example of a continuous real function $f$ on a compact interval $I \subset \mathbf{R}$, such that the composite function $x \mapsto \operatorname{sgn}(f(x))$ is not regulated on $I$ (even though $\operatorname{sgn}$ is regulated on $\mathbf{R}$).
