Let $f$ be a real increasing convex continuous function on an interval $[0, a]$, and such that $f(0) = 0$. If $a_1 \geq a_2 \geq \cdots \geq a_n \geq 0$ is a finite decreasing sequence of points in $[0, a]$, show that one has
$$
f(a_1) - f(a_2) + \cdots + (-1)^n f(a_n) \geq f(a_1 - a_2 + \cdots + (-1)^{n-1} a_n ).
$$
(One can restrict oneself to the case where $n = 2m$ is even; remark that for $1 \leq j \leq m$ one has
$$
\int_\alpha^\beta f'(t)\,dt \leq \int_{a_{2j-1}}^{a_{2j}} f'(t)\,dt
$$
where $\alpha = a_{2j+1} - a_{2j-2} + \cdots + a_{2m-1} - a_{2m}$ and $\beta = \alpha + (a_{2j-1} - a_{2j})$.)
