Let $ \mathbf{f} $ be a regulated function on the interval ]$a, +\infty$*; suppose that there exists a real decreasing function $ g $ on ]$a, +\infty$* such that $ \| \mathbf{f}(x) \| \leqslant g(x) $ on this interval and that the integral $ \int_a^{+\infty} g(t)\,dt $ converges. Show that the series $ \sum_{n=1}^\infty \mathbf{f}(a + nh) $ is absolutely convergent for every $ h > 0 $, and that
$$
\lim_{h \to +\infty} h \sum_{n=1}^\infty \mathbf{f}(a + nh) = \int_a^{+\infty} \mathbf{f}(t)\,dt.
$$
