Let $f$ and $g$ be two regulated functions, and $> 0$ on an open interval ]$a, b$*. Show that the integrals of the functions $f/(1 + fg)$ and $\inf(f, 1/g)$ over ]$a, b$* are simultaneously either convergent or infinite.
