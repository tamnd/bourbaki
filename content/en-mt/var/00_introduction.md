## INTRODUCTION

This fascicle brings together the fundamental notions and the principal results of the theory of differentiable varieties (over the field of real numbers) and of analytic varieties (over a complete non-discrete valued field). It contains no proofs.
The definitions and results of the first six books are assumed to be known.
