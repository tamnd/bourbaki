Let $Q = (S,F, o, t)$ be a quiver. For every $s\in S$ one calls the outgoing (resp. incoming) degree of Q at $s$ and denotes it by $d_+(s)$ (resp. $d_-(s)$) the cardinal of the set $\{f\in F|o(f) =s\}$ (resp. $\{f\in F|t(f) =s\}$. One says that Q is locally finite if $d_+(s)$ and $d_-(s)$ are finite, for every $s\in S$, and that it is finite if the sets F and S are finite.

Prove the equalities $\sum_{s\in S}d_+(s) =\sum_{s\in S}d_-(s) =$ Card(F).
