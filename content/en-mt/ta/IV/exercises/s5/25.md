On pose $C =\mathbf{B}_2\times [-1,1]$. Let K be a closed subspace of $\mathbf{R}^3$ and let P be its image by the projection given by $(x, y, z)\mapsto (x, y)$. Suppose that there exists a finite set I, a family $(p_i)_{i\in I}$ of points of $\mathbf{R}^2$, a family $(r_i)_{i\in I}$ of strictly positive real numbers and a family $(m_i)_{i\in I}$ of natural integers satisfying the following properties, where $\varphi_i$ denotes the mapping from $\mathbf{R}^3$ into $\mathbf{R}^3$ given by $x\mapsto (p_i,0) +r_ix$ and $C_i=\varphi_i(C)$ :

– The sets $C_i$ are pairwise disjoint ;

– For all $i\in I$, the set $L_i=\overset{-1}{\varphi_{i}}(C_i\cap K)$ is the subspace L defined in exercise 23 if $m_i= 0$ and in exercise 24 (where one puts $m=m_i$) if $m_i\geqslant 1$.
