Let X be a separated topological space; suppose that every point of X possesses a neighbourhood U such that every pair of points of U is joined by an injective path in U. If X is connected, prove (without making use of proposition 18 of III, p. 282) that X is path-connected and that every pair of points of X is joined by an injective path.
