Let $K$ be a finite field with q elements, $V$ a vector space of dimension n over $K$.
a) Now prove that the set of sequences $(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_m)$ of $m \leq n$ vectors of $V$ forming a free system, has cardinal equal to
$$(q^n - 1)(q^n - q) \cdots (q^n - q^{m-1})$$
(reason by induction on n).
b) Deduce from a) that the cardinal of the set of linear varieties of dimension m in a projective space of dimension n over $K$ is equal to
$$\frac{(q^{n+1} - 1)(q^{n+1} - q) \cdots (q^{n+1} - q^m)}{(q^{m+1} - 1)(q^{m+1} - q) \cdots (q^{m+1} - q^m)}.$$
