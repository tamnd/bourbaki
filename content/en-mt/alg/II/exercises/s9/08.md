Let $E$ be a left affine space over a field $K$, $T$ its space of translations.
a) In order that a mapping $f$ of $E$ into a left vector space $L$ over $K$ be affine, it is necessary and sufficient that one have
$$
f(t + x) - f(x) = f(t + y) - f(y)
$$
$$
f(\lambda t + x) - f(x) = \lambda(f(t + x) - f(x))
$$
for all $\lambda \in K, t \in T, x, y$ in $E$. Let $x \mapsto [x]$ be the canonical injection of $E$ into the vector space $K_s^{(E)}$ of formal linear combinations of the elements of $E$, and let $N$ be the subspace of $K_s^{(E)}$ generated by the elements
$$
[t + x] - [x] - [t + y] + [y]
$$
$$
[\lambda t + x] - [x] - \lambda[t + x] + \lambda[x]
$$
for $\lambda \in K, t \in T, x, y$ in $K$; finally, let $V$ be the quotient space of $K_s^{(E)}$ by $N$, and let $\varphi$ be the mapping of $E$ into $V$ which associates to every $x \in E$ the class of $[x]$ modulo $N$. Then $\varphi$ is an affine mapping and for every affine mapping $f$ of $E$ into a left vector space $L$ over $K$, there exists one and only one linear mapping $g$ of $V$ into $L$ such that $f = g \circ \varphi$.
b) Let $\varphi_0 : T \to V$ be the linear mapping associated with $\varphi$, so that $\varphi(t + x) - \varphi(x) = \varphi_0(t)$. Now prove that $\varphi$ (and consequently also $\varphi_0$) is injective (consider the mapping $x \mapsto x - a$ of $E$ into $T$ for an $a \in E$); for every family $(\lambda_i)_{i \in I}$ of elements of $K$ with finite support, and every family $(x_i)_{i \in I}$ of elements of $E$, one has

$$
\sum_i \lambda_i \varphi(x_i) = \varphi_0 \left( \sum_i \lambda_i x_i \right) \quad \text{if } \sum_i \lambda_i = 0
$$
$$
\sum_i \lambda_i \varphi(x_i) = \mu \varphi \left( \sum_i \mu^{-1} \lambda_i x_i \right) \quad \text{if } \sum_i \lambda_i = \mu \neq 0.
$$

Deduce that $\varphi_0(T)$ is a hyperplane passing through 0 in $V$, and $\varphi(E)$ a hyperplane parallel to $\varphi_0(T)$.
