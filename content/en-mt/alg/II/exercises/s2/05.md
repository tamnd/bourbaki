Let u be an a-linear mapping of an A-module E into an A-module F, $v = {}^t u$ its transpose. For every submodule N’ of F*, the submodule of E orthogonal to $v(N')$ is ${}^t u^1(N)$, where N is the submodule of F orthogonal to N’.
