## CHAPTER IX

# SESQUILINEAR FORMS AND QUADRATIC FORMS

Unless expressly stated otherwise, all rings considered in this chapter are assumed to admit a unit element denoted by 1; all modules are assumed to be unitary; for every homomorphism $f$ of a ring $A$ into a ring $B$ we assume that $f(1) = 1$.
