Let $\alpha_i$ ($1 \leq i \leq n$) be $n$ distinct elements of a commutative field $K$, $\beta_i$ ($1 \leq i \leq n$) and $\gamma_i$ ($1 \leq i \leq n$) be $2n$ arbitrary elements of $K$. Now prove that there exists one and only one polynomial $f \in K[X]$, of degree $\leq 2n - 1$, such that $f(\alpha_i) = \beta_i$ and $f'(\alpha_i) = \gamma_i$ for $1 \leq i \leq n$ (Hermite interpolation formula) (begin by considering the case where $2n - 1$ of the $2n$ elements $\beta_i, \gamma_i$ are zero).
