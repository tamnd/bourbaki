Let $x, y$ be two elements of a group $G$.
(a) For there to exist $a, b$ in $G$ such that $bay = xab$, it is necessary and sufficient that $xy^{-1}$ be a commutator.
(b) For there to exist $2n + 1$ elements $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{2n+1}$ in $G$ such that
$$
x = a_1 a_2 \ldots a_{2n+1} \quad \text{and} \quad y = a_{2n+1} a_{2n} \ldots a_1
$$
it is necessary and sufficient that $xy^{-1}$ be a product of $n$ commutators (reason by induction on $n$).
