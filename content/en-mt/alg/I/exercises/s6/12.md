Let $G$ be a nilpotent group and let $H$ be the set of elements of $G$ of finite order.
a) Now prove that $H$ is a subgroup of $G$.
b) Now prove that every finite subset of $H$ generates a finite subgroup.
c) Now prove that two elements of $H$ of relatively prime orders commute.
