Let G be a finite commutative group. Now prove the equivalence of the following conditions:
(a) G is cyclic.
(b) Every Sylow subgroup of G is cyclic.
(c) For every prime number $p$, the number of elements $x \in G$ such that $x^p = e$ is $\leq p$.
