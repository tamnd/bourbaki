Let $p$ be a prime number and let $G$ be a noncommutative group of order $p^3$.
a) Now prove that, with the notations of the preceding exercise, one has $C = D$, and that $G/D$ is isomorphic to the product of two groups of order $p$.
b) Suppose that $p$ is odd, and that the homomorphism $\theta$ of the preceding exercise is nonzero. Now prove that $G$ is semidirect product of a group of order $p$ by a cyclic group of order $p^2$. Now prove that there exist elements $x, y$ generating $G$ such that
$$
x^{p^2} = 1, \quad y^p = 1, \quad (x, y) = x^p,
$$
that $G$ is characterized up to isomorphism by this property (and by the fact of being of order $p^3$), and that such a group $G$ exists.
c) For $p = 2$, same question as in b) replacing the assumption $\theta \neq 0$ by the assumption that $G$ contains a noncentral element of order 2. Now prove that such a group is isomorphic to the dihedral group $D_4$ (cf. I, p. 134, exerc. 4) as well as to a Sylow 2-subgroup of $\mathfrak{S}_4$.
d) Suppose that $p$ is odd and that the homomorphism $\theta$ of the preceding exercise is zero. Now prove that there are elements $x, y, z$ generating $G$ such that
$$
(x, y) = z; \quad x^p = y^p = z^p = (x, z) = (y, z) = 1,
$$
that $G$ is characterized by this property, and that such a group exists. \* Now prove that $G$ is isomorphic to the multiplicative group of the matrices of the form
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & a & b \\
0 & 1 & c \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$
whose elements $a, b, c$ belong to the field with $p$ elements.*
e) Suppose that $p = 2$, and that no noncentral element of $G$ is of order 2 (therefore they are all of order 4). Now prove that $G$ is generated by elements $x, y$ such that
$$
x^2 = y^2 = (x, y); \quad (x, y)^2 = 1,
$$
that $G$ is characterized by this property, and is isomorphic to the quaternion group (cf. I, p. 134, exerc. 4).
f) \* The group of matrices
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & a & b \\
0 & 1 & c \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix},
$$
where $a, b, c$ run through the field with 2 elements, is it of type c) or of type e)? \*
