*Let V be a vector space over a commutative field k and let

$$
W = \bigwedge^2 V
$$

(cf. III, p. 76). On $E = V \times W$, one defines a law of composition by the formula

$$
(v_1, w_1) \cdot (v_2, w_2) = (v_1 + v_2, w_1 + w_2 + v_1 \wedge v_2).
$$

a) Now prove that this law endows E with a group structure, a central extension of V by W.
b) Suppose that the characteristic of $k$ is different from 2. Now prove that the derived group D(E) is the set of $(0, w)$, for $w \in W$; it is an isomorphic group to W. Now prove that, if $\dim(V) \geq 4$, there exist elements of D(E) which are not commutators.*
