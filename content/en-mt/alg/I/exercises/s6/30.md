Let $p$ be a prime number, $r$ and $t$ integers $\geqslant 0$, S a cyclic group of order $p^{r+t}$ and T the subgroup of order $p^t$ of S.
a) If S operates on a finite set E, now prove that one has
$$
\operatorname{Card}(E) \equiv \operatorname{Card}(E^T) \pmod{p^{r+1}},
$$
where $E^T$ denotes the set of elements of E invariant under T.
b) Let $m, n$ be integers $\geqslant 0$. Now prove that one has
$$
\binom{p^{r+t}m}{p^tn} \equiv \binom{p^rm}{n} \pmod{p^{r+1}}.
$$
(Let M be a set with $m$ elements, and let E be the set of subsets of $S \times M$ having $p^t n$ elements. Define an operation of S on $S \times M$ such that there exists a bijection of $E^T$ onto the set of subsets of $(S/T) \times M$ having $n$ elements; apply a).)
