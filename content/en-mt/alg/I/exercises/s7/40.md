\* Let n be an integer $\geqslant 2$, and let $T_n$ be the group of upper triangular matrices of order n over $\mathbf{Z}$ having all their diagonal terms equal to 1. Let $e(T_n)$ be the invariant of the group $T_n$ defined in Exercise 39. Prove that one has
$$
e(T_n) \leqslant \sum_{i=1}^{i=n} i(n - i).$$
