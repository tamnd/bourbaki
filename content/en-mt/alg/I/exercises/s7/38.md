Let $X$ be a finite set, $F(X)$ the free group constructed on $X$, and $S = X \cup X^{-1}$.
a) Let $S_n$ be the set of products $s_1 \ldots s_m$ for $s_i \in S, m \leq n$. Now prove that every $S$-connected component (cf. I, p. 146, exer. 18) of $F(X) - S_n$ contains one and only one element of the form $s_1 \ldots s_{n+1}$, with $s_i \in S$ and $s_i s_{i+1} \neq e$ for $1 \leq i \leq n$.
b) Deduce from a) a bijection of the set $B$ of ends of $F(X)$ (cf. exer. 37) onto the set of infinite sequences $(s_1, \ldots, s_n, \ldots)$ of elements of $S$ such that $s_i s_{i+1} \neq e$ for every $i$.
In particular, the number of ends of $F(X)$ is equal to $2^{N_0}$ if $\mathrm{Card}(X) \geq 2$.
