The group defined by the presentation $\langle x, y ; x^3, y^2, (xy)^3 \rangle$ is isomorphic to $\mathfrak{A}_4$. (Now prove that the conjugates of y form, together with the identity element, a distinguished subgroup of order $\leq 4$ and of index $\leq 3$.)
