Let $G$ be a group. Now prove the equivalence of the following properties:
(a) $G$ is a free group.
(b) Whatever the group $H$ and the extension $E$ of $G$ by $H$, there exists a section $G \to E$. (To prove that $b$ implies $a$), take for $E$ a free group and use Exercise 20.)
