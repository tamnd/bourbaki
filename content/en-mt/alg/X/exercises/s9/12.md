Let $A$ be a Noetherian local ring, $m$ its maximal ideal.
a) Show that if $0 < \operatorname{dh}(A) < \infty$, there exists a non-zero divisor element in $m - m^2$ (show as in exercise 11, *a*) that $m$ would otherwise be associated with $A$, hence an exact sequence
$$
0 \to k \to A \to N \to 0 ;
$$
arrive at a contradiction using exercise 16, p. 203).
b) Let $n$ be an integer. Show that we have $\operatorname{dh}(A) = n$ if and only if there exists a completely secant sequence $(x_1, ..., x_n)$ for $A$, generating $m$ (reason by induction on $n$, using *a*) and exercise 14, p. 203).
We say that a local ring is *regular* if it is Noetherian and of finite homological dimension.
