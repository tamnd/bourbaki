Let A be a local Noetherian ring, m its maximal ideal, $k = A/m$. Let M be a non-zero A-module of finite type. We call depth of M, and we denote prof (M), the upper bound of the integers n for which there exists a sequence $(x_1, ..., x_n)$ of elements of m which is completely secant for M.
a) Show that we have $\operatorname{prof}(M) = 0$ if and only if m is associated with M (X, p. 172, exercise 28), in other words, if M contains a a-submodule isomorphic to k (observe that an ideal contained in a union of prime ideals is necessarily contained in one of them).

b) Show that prof (M) is the greatest lower bound of the integers $n$ for which $\operatorname{Ext}_A^n(k, M)$ is non-zero (reason by induction on $n$). If $\operatorname{prof}(M) = n$, any sequence $(x_1, ..., x_p)$ of elements of $m$ completely secant for $M$ ($p \leq n$) can be extended into a sequence $(x_1, ..., x_n)$ in $m$ completely secant for $M$.

c) If $(x_1, ..., x_k)$ is a sequence of elements of $m$ completely intersecting for $M$, show that
$$
\operatorname{prof}\left(M/(x_1 M + \cdots + x_k M)\right) = \operatorname{prof}(M) - k .
$$

d) We assume that $M$ is of *finite projective dimension*. Prove the equality $\mathrm{dp}_A(M) + \operatorname{prof}(M) = \operatorname{prof}(A)$ (reason by induction on $\mathrm{dp}_A(M)$).

e) Show that $\operatorname{prof}(A)$ is the upper bound of the projective dimensions of the $A$-modules which are of *finite type* and of *finite projective dimension*.
