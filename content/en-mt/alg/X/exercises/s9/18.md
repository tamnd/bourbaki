Let $A$ be a Noetherian ring, $\mathscr{C}$ a stable set of classes of $A$-modules (X, p. 40), $x = (x_1, ..., x_n)$ a sequence of elements of $A$, $x$ the ideal generated by $x$. We note $\mathscr{C}_x$ all the elements of $\mathscr{C}$ which are canceled by $x$. Let $M$ be a $A$-module of finite type.
a) Prove that for every $i \geqslant 0$, there exists an element $M_i$ of $\mathscr{C}_x$ such that we have
$$
\sum_{k \geqslant 0} (-1)^k [H_{i+k}(x, M)] = [M_i]
$$
in $K(\mathscr{C}_x)$ (reason by induction on $n$, using prop. 4, p. 157; in the case $n = 1$, consider the successive powers of the endomorphism $(x_1)_M$).
b) We assume that $A/x$ is of finite length. Show that there exist positive integers $m_i$ ($i \geqslant 0$) such that we have $\operatorname{long} H_i(x, M) = m_i + m_{i+1}$.
