Let $A$ be a ring, $a$ be an ideal of $A$. On considère la $(A/a)$-algèbre graduée alternée $\operatorname{Tor}^A(A/a, A/a)$ (*cf.* X, p. 201, exercice 9) et l’homomorphisme de $(A/a)$-algèbres graduées $\theta : \Lambda_{A/a}(a/a^2) \to \operatorname{Tor}^A(A/a, A/a)$ déduit de l’isomorphisme $a/a^2 \to \operatorname{Tor}_1^A(A/a, A/a)$ (X, p. 73). Show that if the ideal $a$ is generated by a completely secant sequence in $A$, $\theta$ is an isomorphism.
