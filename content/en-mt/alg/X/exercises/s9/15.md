Let $A$ be a *Noetherian* ring, $a$ be an ideal contained in the radical of $A$. We assume that the $(A/a)$-module $a/a^2$ is free and that the homomorphism br $\theta_2 : \Lambda_{A/a}^2(a/a^2) \to \operatorname{Tor}_2^A(A/a, A/a)$ (exercise 14) is surjective. Show that $a$ is generated by a completely secant sequence in $A$. (If $x$ is a minimal family of generators of $a$, show using exercise 7 (or directly) that $\operatorname{Coker}(\theta_2)$ is isomorphic to $H_1(x, A) \otimes_A A/a$.)
