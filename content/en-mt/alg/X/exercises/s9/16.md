Let $A$ be a *local Noetherian* ring, $m$ its maximal ideal, $k = A/m$. Show that the homomorphism br of $k$-graded algebras $\theta : \Lambda_k(m/m^2) \to \operatorname{Tor}^A(k, k)$ is injective: if $\theta_2$ is bijective, the ring $A$ is *regular* (exercise 12).
(If $x$ is a minimal system of generators of $m$, show that the complex $K(x, A)$ satisfies the conditions of exercise 14, p. 182; use exercises 4 and 15.)
