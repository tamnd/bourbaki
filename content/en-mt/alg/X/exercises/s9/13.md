Let $A$ be a regular local ring (exercise 14) of homological dimension $n$; we note $m$ its maximal ideal and $k = A/m$.
a) Show that we have $\dim_k(m/m^2) = n$.
b) Show that the $k$-algebra $\bigoplus_{r \geq 0} (m^r/m^{r+1})$ is isomorphic to an algebra of indeterminate $n$ polynomials.
c) If $n = 0$, $A$ is a field. If $n = 1$, show that $A$ is a principal ideal domain *and therefore a ring of discrete valuation* (show that $A$ is integral by observing that $m \cap m^n = \bigcap m^n$, hence $\bigcap m^n = 0$).
d) Show that the ring $A[[T]]$ is regular.
