Let $A$ be a ring such that the $A$-module $A_s$ is of *finite injective dimension* (X, p. 202, exercise 4; a *regular* local ring (exercise 12) satisfies this property).
a) Let $x$ be a non-zero divisor and non-invertible element in $A$. Show that we have
$$
\operatorname{di}_{A/Ax}(A/Ax) = \operatorname{di}_A(A) - 1 .
$$
(Let $0 \to A \to I^0 \to I^1 \to \cdots$ be a *minimal* injective resolution (X, p. 182, exercise 13) of $A$; show that the complex $\operatorname{Hom}_A(A/Ax, I^1) \to \operatorname{Hom}_A(A/Ax, I^2) \to \cdots$ defines a minimal injective resolution of the $(A/Ax)$-module $A/Ax$.)
b) We now assume that the ring $A$ is *local Noetherian*. Prove the equality $\operatorname{di}(A) = \operatorname{prof}(A)$ (exercise 11; reason by induction on $\operatorname{di}(A)$).
c) Let $(x_1, ..., x_n)$ be a completely secant sequence for $A$, contained in the maximal ideal of $A$, with $n = \operatorname{di}(A)$. Show that the ring $A$ is *auto-injective* (X, p. 171, exercise 26).
