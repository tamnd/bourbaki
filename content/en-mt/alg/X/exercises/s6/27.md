We call augmentation of the k-algebra A a uniferous homomorphism br of k-algebras $\pi : A \to k$; we say that the couple (A, $\pi$) is an augmented k-algebra. The augmentation provides k with an A-module structure on the left. Let M be an A-module on the left, N an A-module on the right; we pose

$$
H_n(A, \pi ; N) = \mathrm{Tor}_n^A(N, k) \quad \text{et} \quad H^n(A, \pi ; M) = \mathrm{Ext}_A^n(k, M) \quad \text{pour tout } n \geq 0.
$$

a) We note $M_{(\pi)}$ (resp. $N_{(\pi)}$) the group M (resp. N) provided with the structure of (A, A)-bimodule defined by:

$$
ama' = \pi(a') am \quad (\text{resp. } ana' = n\pi(a) a' \quad \text{pour} \quad a,\ a' \in A,\ m \in M,\ n \in N.
$$

Show that the k-module $H_0(A, \pi ; N)$ (resp. $H^0(A, \pi ; M)$, resp. $H^1(A, \pi ; M)$) is isomorphic to the k-module $H_0(A, N_{(\pi)})$ (resp. $H^0(A, M_{(\pi)})$, resp. $H^1(A, M_{(\pi)})$) defined in exercise 26.

b) We now assume that A is a projective k-module. Define using standard resolution (X, p. 58) the k-isomorphisms $\varphi_n : H_n(A, N_{(\pi)}) \to H_n(A, \pi ; N)$ and $\varphi^n : H^n(A, M_{(\pi)}) \to H^n(A, \pi ; M)$ for all $n \geq 0$.

c) We suppose that there exists a homomorphism br of k-algebras $\rho : A \to A^e$ which makes $A^e$ a projective right A-module and such that the left ideal of $A^e$ generated by $\rho(\mathrm{Ker}\ \pi)$ is the kernel of the homomorphism br $A \otimes_k A^e \to A$ defined by the multiplication in A. Show that it exists for all (A, A)-bimodule Q and any integer $n \geq 0$ of k-isomorphisms $H_n(A, \pi ; Q) \to H_n(A, Q)$ (resp. $H^n(A, \pi ; Q) \to H^n(A, Q)$), Q being considered as A-module on the right (resp. on the left) via the homomorphism br $\rho$. (Use the canonical homomorphisms of X, p. 109.)

d) Show that the hypotheses of c) are verified in the following cases:
α) A is the algebra (on k) of a group G, $\rho$ is defined by $\rho(e_g) = e_g \otimes e_{g^{-1}}$.
β) A is the tensor algebra (resp. symmetric) of a projective k-module V; $\rho$ is defined by

$$
\rho(v) = v \otimes 1 - 1 \otimes v.
$$

\* γ) A is the enveloping algebra of a Lie algebra g on k, free as a k-module; we have

$$
\rho(x) = x \otimes 1 - 1 \otimes x \quad \text{pour} \quad x \in g
$$

(use cor. 5 in th. 1 of LIE I, § 2, no 7).

δ) A is the ring of functions of a smooth algebraic group G on a field k; we take

$$
\rho(f) = \sum_{i=1}^k f_i' \otimes f_i'', \quad \text{où} \quad f(gh^{-1}) = \sum_{i=1}^k f_i'(g)\ f_i''(h) \quad \text{pour} \quad g,\ h \in G.\ *

$$
