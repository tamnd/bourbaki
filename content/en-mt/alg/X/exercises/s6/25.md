Let K be a commutative field, L a finite Galois extension of K, G its Galois group. We operate G on the group $\mathbf{GL}(n, L)$ by setting $\sigma(a_{ij}) = (\sigma(a_{ij}))$ for $\sigma \in G$, $(a_{ij}) \in \mathbf{GL}(n, L)$. Any stable $\mathbf{GL}(n, L)$ subgroup for this action is thus provided with a G-group structure (exercise 23).
a) Show that the sets $H^1(G, \mathbf{GL}(n, L))$ and $H^1(G, \mathbf{SL}(n, L))$ are reduced to one element (*cf.* V, § 10, no 5, prop. 9).
b) Show that the set $H^1(G, \mathbf{PGL}(n, L))$ is identified with the set of isomorphism classes of simple central algebras S such that the algebra $L \otimes_K S$ is isomorphic to $M_n(L)$ (use exercise 24). By associating with such an algebra its class in the group Br(K, L) (VIII, § 13) and by identifying this last group with $H^2(G, L^*)$ (*loc. cit.*), we obtain a map $H^1(G, \mathbf{PGL}(n, L)) \to H^2(G, L^*)$ which is the opposite of the map $\partial^1$ (exercise 23, f)) deduced from the extension $L^* \to \mathbf{GL}(n, L) \to \mathbf{PGL}(n, L)$. Show that the image of $\partial^1$ is formed of elements of order n in $H^2(G, L^*)$.
c) Show that the set $H^1(G, \mathbf{Sp}(2n, L))$ is reduced to one element (*cf.* IX, § 5, no 1, th. 1).
d) Let Q be a quadratic form on the K-vector space V, $Q_L$ the quadratic form which is deduced from it on $L \otimes_K V$. Show that $H^1(G, O(Q_L))$ is identified with the set of isomorphism classes of quadratic forms Q' on V such that $Q'_L$ is isomorphism to $Q_L$.
