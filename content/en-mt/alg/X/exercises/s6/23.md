Let G be a group, M a group with operators in G (I, p. 29, def. 2). We will note $^g m$ the compound of $g \in G$ and $m \in M$. We will say that M is a G-group if we have $^h(^g m) = ^{hg} m$ for $m \in M, g, h \in G$, in other words if the map $\varphi$ of G in Aut (M) such that $\varphi(g).m = ^g m$ for $g \in G, m \in M$ is a homomorphism br.
If M is a G-group, we note $H^0(G, M)$ the subgroup of M formed by the elements $m \in M$ such that $^g m = m$ for all $g \in G$, and $Z^1(G, M)$ the set of maps $h$ of G in M such that
$$
h(gg') = h(g)\ ^gh(g') \quad \text{pour} \quad g, g' \in G .
$$
a) We say that two elements $h_1, h_2$ of $Z^1(G, M)$ are cohomologues if there exists an element $m$ of M such that $h_2(g) = m^{-1}\ h_1(g)\ ^g m$ for all $g \in G$. Show that the relation resulting is an equivalence relation on $Z^1(G, M)$; the quotient set is denoted $H^1(G, M)$. The class in $H^1(G, M)$ of the application $i \in Z^1(G, M)$ such that $i(g) = 1_M$ for all $g \in G$ is denoted $e_{G, M}$ or simply $e_M$.
b) Let $f : M \to N$ be a homomorphism br of G-groups (I, p. 30, def. 3); show that $f$ induces a homomorphism br of groups $f^0 : H^0(G, M) \to H^0(G, N)$, and an application $f^1 : H^1(G, M) \to H^1(G, N)$ such that $f^1(e_M) = e_N$.
c) Let $M \xrightarrow{i} N \xrightarrow{p} Q$ be a sequence of G-groups and homomorphisms, with $i$ injective, $p$ surjective and
$$
\operatorname{Im}(i) = \operatorname{Ker}(p) .
$$
Show that the group $N' = p^{-1}(H^0(G, Q))$ acts on $Z^1(G, M)$ in such a way that $(n.h)(g) = n^{-1}\ h(g)\ ^gn$ for $n \in N', h \in Z^1(G, M), g \in G$; show that this action defines by passing to the quotient an action of $H^0(G, Q)$ on $H^1(G, M)$. The application $i^1 : H^1(G, M) \to H^1(G, N)$ passes to the quotient and defines a bijection of $H^1(G, M)/H^0(G, Q)$ on $\operatorname{Im}(i^1)$.
d) The action of $H^0(G, Q)$ on $H^1(G, M)$ allows you to define an application $\partial : H^0(G, Q) \to H^1(G, M)$ by $\partial(q) = q.e_M$ for $q \in H^0(G, Q)$. Show that we have
$$
\operatorname{Im}(i^0) = \operatorname{Ker}(p^0), \operatorname{Im}(p^0) = \partial^{-1}(e_M), \quad \operatorname{Im}(\partial) = (i^1)^{-1}(e_N), \quad \operatorname{Im}(i^1) = (p^1)^{-1}(e_Q) .
$$
e) We now assume that $i(M)$ is contained in the center of N. Show that $\partial$ is a homomorphism br of groups; construct an action of the group $H^1(G, M)$ on the set $H^1(G, N)$ such that the application $p^1$ passes to the quotient and defines a bijection of $H^1(G, N)/H^1(G, M)$ on $\operatorname{Im}(p^1)$.
f) Let E be the set of maps $k$ of G in N such that $p \circ k \in Z^1(G, Q)$. For $k \in E$, show that there exists an element $f_k$ of $Z^2(G, M)$ such that $i(f_k(x, y)) = k(x)\ ^xk(y)\ (k(xy))^{-1}$ for $x, y \in G$. Show that the application $k \mapsto f_k$ defines by passing to the quotient an application $\partial^1 : H^1(G, Q) \to H^2(G, M)$. Prove that we have $\operatorname{Im}(p^1) = (\partial^1)^{-1}(0)$.
