Let K be a commutative field, L a finite Galois extension of K, G its Galois group.
a) Show that the exact sequence of G-modules
$$
0 \to \mu_n(L) \to L^* \xrightarrow{x \mapsto x^n} L^{*n} \to 0
$$
allows you to define an isomorphism $k_L : (L^n \cap K^*)/K^{*n} \to H^1(G, \mu_n(L))$. When $\mu_n(K)$ has n elements and we identify $H^1(G, \mu_n(L))$ with Hom $(G, \mu_n(K))$, show that $k_L$ coincides with the homomorphism br defined in V, § 11, no 8.
b) Let p be a prime number; we assume that K has characteristic p. Show that the exact sequence of G-modules (V, § 11, no 9)
$$
0 \to F_p \to L \xrightarrow{\rho} p(L) \to 0
$$
allows us to define an isomorphism $a_L : (p(L) \cap K)/p(K) \to H^1(G, F_p)$ which coincides with the homomorphism br defined in loc. cit. if we identify $H^1(G, F_p)$ with Hom $(G, F_p)$.
