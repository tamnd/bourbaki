Let G be a cyclic group of finite order.
a) If M is a $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module and q an even (resp. odd) rational integer, the group $\hat{H}^q(G, M)$ is isomorphic to $\hat{H}^0(G, M)$ (resp. $\hat{H}^1(G, M)$) (cf. exercise 7).
b) We denote $\mathscr{C}$ the set of classes of $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-modules of finite type T such that the groups $\hat{H}^q(G, T)$ are finite for all $q \in \mathbf{Z}$; if the $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module M is of type $\mathscr{C}$, we put
$$
h(M) = \operatorname{Card}(H^0(G, M))/\operatorname{Card}(H^1(G, M)).
$$
Let $0 \to M' \to M \to M'' \to 0$ be an exact sequence of $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-modules. Show that if two of the modules M', M, M" are of type $\mathscr{C}$, it is the same for the third, and that we then have $h(M) = h(M')\ h(M'')$. In particular, the function h defines a homomorphism br of the Grothendieck group K($\mathscr{C}$) in the multiplicative group $\mathbf{Q}^*$.
c) Let M be a finite $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module. Show that M is of type $\mathscr{C}$ and that we have $h(M) = 1$.
d) We assume that the order of G is a prime number p. We note $\mathscr{C}'$ the set of classes of $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-modules of finite type in which the multiplication by p has a kernel and a finite cokernel; if M is of type $\mathscr{C}'$, we set $\varphi(M) = \operatorname{Card}(\operatorname{Coker} p_M)/\operatorname{Card}(\operatorname{Ker} p_M)$. Prove as in b) that $\varphi$ defines a homomorphism br of K($\mathscr{C}'$) in $\mathbf{Q}^*$.
e) Show that K($\mathscr{C}'$) is generated by the classes of $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-modules T having one of the following properties:
(i) T is finite.
(ii) Multiplication by p in T is one-to-one.
(iii) $T = \mathbf{Z}$ equipped with the trivial action of G.
(iv) $T = \mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$.
(v) $T = \mathbf{Q}_p/\mathbf{Z}_p$ (cf. TG, III, p. 84, exercise 23), equipped with the trivial action of G.
(vi) $T = \mathbf{Z}^{(G)} \otimes_\mathbf{Z} (\mathbf{Q}_p/\mathbf{Z}_p)$.
(First show that every $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module of type $\mathscr{C}'$ is written in K($\mathscr{C}'$) as the sum of a $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module of finite type on $\mathbf{Z}$ and of a $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module in which the multiplication by p is surjective. Then determine the structure of simple modules on the rings $\mathbf{Q}^{(G)}$ and $\mathbf{Q}_p^{(G)}$.)

g) Deduce from the above that we have $\mathscr{C}' \subset \mathscr{C}$ and that if M is a $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module of type $\mathscr{C}'$, we have
$$
h(M)^{p-1} = (\varphi(H^0(G, M)))^p / \varphi(M) .
$$
(Check the formula in the six cases considered in f).)
