Let p be a prime number, G a p-group, M a $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module. We put $A = F_p^{(G)}$.
a) We assume that $pM = 0$. Show that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) There exists a rational integer q such that $\hat{H}^q(G, M) = 0$.

EXERCISES

β) M is cohomologically trivial (exercise 18).
γ) M is an induced $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module.
δ) The A-module M is free.
(To proving that α) entails δ), reduce to the case where $q = -2$; show that we then have
$$
\operatorname{Tor}_1^A(F_p, M) = 0,
$$
and conclude with exercise 4, p. 185 and exercise 22 of VIII, § 8).
b) We assume that the multiplication by p is injective in M. Show that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) There exists an integer q such that the groups $\hat{H}^q(G, M)$ and $\hat{H}^{q+1}(G, M)$ are zero.
β) M is cohomologically trivial.
γ) The A-module $M/pM$ is free.
