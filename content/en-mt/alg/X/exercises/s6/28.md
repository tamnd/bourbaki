Let g be a Lie algebra on the commutative ring k, U its enveloping algebra. We provide k with the U-module structure on the left corresponding to the trivial representation of g. Let N be a right g-module, M a left g-module, n an integer $\geq 0$; we pose

$$
H_n(g, N) = \mathrm{Tor}_n^U(N, k) \quad \text{et} \quad H^n(g, M) = \mathrm{Ext}_U^n(k, M).
$$

a) The k-module $H_0(g, N)$ (resp. $H^0(g, M)$) is isomorphic to $N/Ng$ (resp. to the a-submodule of M formed of the elements canceled by g). We note $Z^1(g, M)$ (resp. $B^1(g, M)$) the sub-k-module of $\mathrm{Hom}_k(g, M)$ formed of elements f such as $f([x, y]) = xf(y) - yf(x)$ for $x, y \in g$ (resp. elements $f_m$, for $m \in M$, such as $f_m(x) = xm$ for $x \in g$). Show that $H^1(g, M)$ is identified with the quotient $Z^1(g, M)/B^1(g, M)$.

b) We now assume that g is a free k-module. Show that the k-modules $H^n(g, M)$ are identified with the homology modules of the complex $C(g, M)$, where for $p \geq 0$, $C^p(g, M)$ is the k-module of the alternating multilinear maps of $g^p$ in M, $C^p(g, M) = 0$ for $p < 0$, the differential being given by the formula

$$
df(x_1, ..., x_{p+1}) = \sum_{1 \leq i \leq p+1} (-1)^{i+1} x_i . f(x_1, ..., \hat{x}_i, ..., x_{p+1})
$$
$$
+ \sum_{1 \leq i < j \leq p+1} (-1)^{i+j} f([x_i, x_j], x_1, ..., \hat{x}_i, ..., \hat{x}_j, ..., x_{p+1})
$$

for $f \in C^p(g, M)$, $x_1, ..., x_{p+1} \in g$ (where the sign $\hat{}$ on a letter means that it should be omitted).

Give an analogous expression for the $k$-modules $H_p(\mathfrak{g},M)$. (Use X, p. 184, exercise 21.)

c) We assume that $\dim_k(\mathfrak{g})=n<\infty$. Show that $H^p(\mathfrak{g},M)=0$ for all $p>n+1$ and all $\mathfrak{g}$-module $M$, and that there exists a $\mathfrak{g}$-module $N$ such that $H^n(\mathfrak{g},N)\neq0$ (take $N=\Lambda^n\mathfrak{g}$, where $\mathfrak{g}$ operates by the formula

$$
x.(x_1\wedge\cdots\wedge x_n)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_1\wedge\cdots\wedge x_{i-1}\wedge[x,x_i]\wedge x_{i+1}\wedge\cdots\wedge x_n;
$$

show that $df=0$ for all $f\in C^{n-1}(\mathfrak{g},N)$.

d) The adjoint representation of $\mathfrak{g}$ and the representation of $\mathfrak{g}$ on $M$ define a representation $\theta$ of $\mathfrak{g}$ in $C(\mathfrak{g},M)$, such that

$$
(\theta(x)f)(x_1,\ldots,x_p)=x.f(x_1,\ldots,x_p)-\sum_{i=1}^{p}f(x_1,\ldots,[x,x_i],\ldots,x_p)
$$

for $f\in C^p(\mathfrak{g},M)$, $x,x_1,\ldots,x_p\in\mathfrak{g}$. Show that, for all $x\in\mathfrak{g}$, $\theta(x)$ is a homotopic zero endomorphism of the complex $C(\mathfrak{g},M)$ (define a homotopy $i(x)$ by setting $(i(x)f)(x_1,\ldots,x_p)=f(x,x_1,\ldots,x_p)$).
