Let $\rho : A \to B$ be a ring homomorphism, $M, N$ two left $B$-modules, $P$ a right $B$-module.
a) Let $L_B$ (resp. $L_A$) be a projective resolution of the $B$-module (resp. of the $A$-module) $M$. Prove that there exists an $A$-linear morphism of resolutions $L_A \to L_B$, unique up to homotopy; deduce graded $k$-homomorphisms of degree zero
$$
\alpha : \operatorname{Tor}^A(P, M) \to \operatorname{Tor}^B(P, M) \qquad \beta : \operatorname{Ext}_B(M, N) \to \operatorname{Ext}_A(M, N) .
$$
b) Consider the canonical homomorphisms (X, p. 109)
$$
\lambda : \operatorname{Tor}^A(P, M) \to \operatorname{Tor}^B(P \otimes_A B, M) \qquad \mu : \operatorname{Tor}^A(P, M) \to \operatorname{Tor}^B(P, B \otimes_A M)
$$
$$
\nu : \operatorname{Ext}_B(B \otimes_A M, N) \to \operatorname{Ext}_A(M, N) \qquad \rho : \operatorname{Ext}_B(M, \operatorname{Hom}_A(B, N)) \to \operatorname{Ext}_A(M, N) .
$$
Let $m : B \otimes_A M \to M, p : P \otimes_A B \to B$ and $n : N \to \operatorname{Hom}_A(B, N)$ be the $B$-homomorphisms defined by $m(b \otimes x) = bx, p(y \otimes b) = yb$ and $n(z)(b) = bz$ for $b \in B, x \in M, y \in P, z \in N$. Prove the equalities
$$
\alpha = \operatorname{Tor}(1_P, m) \circ \mu = \operatorname{Tor}(p, 1_M) \circ \lambda
$$
$$
\beta = \nu \circ \operatorname{Ext}(m, 1_N) = \rho \circ \operatorname{Ext}(1_M, n) .
$$
