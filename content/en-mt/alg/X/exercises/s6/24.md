Let K be a commutative field, L a finite Galois extension of K, of Galois group G.
Let V (resp. V’) be a K-vector space, t (resp. t’) a tensor of type $(p, q)$ on V (resp. V’) (III, p. 63). We call K-isomorphism (or simply isomorphism) of $(V, t)$ on $(V', t')$ a K-linear isomorphism $u : V \to V'$ such that $(\mathbf{T}^p(u) \otimes \mathbf{T}^q(\tilde{u}))\ (t) = t'$. We will note $V_L$ the vector L-space $L \otimes_K V$ and $t_L$ the tensor of type $(p, q)$ on $V_L$ deduced from $t$ by extension of the scalars. We denote by $F_{L/K}(V, t)$ the set of isomorphism classes of pairs $(W, \tau)$ such that $(W_L, \tau_L)$ is L-isomorphic to $(V_L, t_L)$.

EXERCISES

a) Let M be the group of L-automorphisms of $(V_L, t_L)$. For $m \in M$ and $\sigma \in G$, we set
$$
^{\circ} m = \pi_V(\sigma) \circ m \circ \pi_V(\sigma)^{-1},
$$
where $\pi_V : G \to \mathrm{Aut}_K(V_L)$ designates the action of G on $V_L = L \otimes_K V$ deduced from the action of G on L. Show that we thus define a structure of G-group on M.
b) Let $(W, \tau)$ be an element of $F_{L/K}(V, t)$, and let $u$ be an L-isomorphism of $(V_L, t_L)$ on $(W_L, \tau_L)$. For $\sigma \in G$, we note $f_u(\sigma)$ the element $u^{-1} \circ \pi_W(\sigma) \circ u \circ \pi_V(\sigma)^{-1}$ of M. Show that $f_u \in Z^1(G, M)$ (exercise 23), and that its class in $H^1(G, M)$ is independent of the choose of $u$; we note it $\theta(W, \tau)$.
c) Show that the application $\theta : F_{L/K}(V, t) \to H^1(G, M)$ is *bijective*, and that we have $\theta(V, t) = e_M$.
