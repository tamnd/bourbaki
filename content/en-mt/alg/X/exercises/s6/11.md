Let $G$ be a group, $H$ a normal subgroup of $G$, $M$ a $G$-module. According to Exercise 10, the group $G$ acts by conjugation on the $\mathbf Z$-modules $H^q(H,M)$; the action of the subgroup $H$ being trivial, these are therefore endowed with a structure of $\mathbf Z^{(G/H)}$-modules.

a) Now prove that there exists a spectral sequence $E$ (X, p. 175, exercise 14), converging to $\displaystyle\bigoplus_n H^n(G,M)$, such that

$$
E_2^{pq}=H^p(G/H,H^q(H,M))
$$

(consider the spectral sequence of X, p. 188, exercise 6, d), taking into account the canonical isomorphism (12), p. 109).

b) Now prove that there exists an exact sequence

$$
0\longrightarrow H^1(G/H,H^0(H,M))
\longrightarrow H^1(G,M)
\longrightarrow H^0(G/H,H^1(H,M))
\longrightarrow H^2(G/H,H^0(H,M))
\longrightarrow H^2(G,M).
$$

Describe the homomorphisms of this sequence by using the description of $H^q(G,M)$ in terms of $C^q(G,M)$.

c) Define a spectral sequence analogous to that of a) for homology.
