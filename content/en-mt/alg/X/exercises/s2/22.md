We keep the conventions of the previous exercise; if M is an A-module, $\nabla$ a connection on M and if $X\in D_k(A)$, we note $\nabla_X$ the $k$-endomorphism $(1_M\otimes X)\circ\nabla$ of M.

a) Let $M_1,M_2$ be two A-modules, provided with connections ${}^1\nabla,{}^2\nabla$; show that we define a connection $\nabla$ on $M_1\oplus M_2$ (resp. $M_1\otimes_A M_2$, resp. $\operatorname{Hom}_A(M_1,M_2)$) by setting $\nabla(m_1+m_2)={}^1\nabla(m_1)+{}^2\nabla(m_2)$ for $m_1\in M_1$, $m_2\in M_2$ (resp. $\nabla_X(m_1\otimes m_2)={}^1\nabla_X(m_1)\otimes m_2+m_1\otimes{}^2\nabla_X(m_2)$ for $X\in D_k(A)$, $m_1\in M_1$ and $m_2\in M_2$, resp. $\nabla_X(u)={}^2\nabla_X\circ u-u\circ{}^1\nabla_X$ for $X\in D_k(A)$, $u\in\operatorname{Hom}_A(M_1,M_2)$.

b) Let M be an A-module, $\nabla:M\to M\otimes_A\Omega^1_{A/k}$ a connection on M. If $X,Y\in D_k(A)$, we note $R(X,Y)$ the A-endomorphism of M such that $R(X,Y)(m)=\langle R(m),X\wedge Y\rangle$ for all $m\in M$. Prove the formula: $R(X,Y)=[\nabla_X,\nabla_Y]-\nabla_{[X,Y]}$ (use exercise 21).

c) We assume the projective A-module M, so that the curvature homomorphism br R is identified with an element of $\operatorname{End}_A(M)\otimes_A\Omega^2_{A/k}$. If we equip $\operatorname{End}_A(M)$ with the connection $\nabla$ defined in a), show that we have $\nabla^2R=0$.
