Let $C$ be a complex of A-modules; we consider $C$ as an A(\varepsilon)-module (X, p. 27). We denote
$$
i : A \to A(\varepsilon)
$$
the canonical injection.
Now prove that the following conditions are equivalent:
(i) $C$ is i-projective (X, p. 170, exercise 19).
(ii) $C$ is i-injective (loc. cit.).
(iii) The complex $C$ is homotopic to zero.
(iv) There exists an A-module graded $M$ and an isomorphism of graded A(\varepsilon)-modules $A(\varepsilon) \otimes_A M \to C$.
(v) There exists an A-module graded $N$ and an isomorphism of graded A(\varepsilon)-modules $C \to \mathrm{Hom}_A(A(\varepsilon), N)$.
