We assume that the ring A is a comm alg on a commutative ring $k$; the A-module $D_k(A)$ of the $k$-derivations of A is then identified with the dual of the A-module $\Omega^1_{A/k}$, which defines on $\Omega_{A/k}$ a structure of $\Lambda_A(D_k(A))$-module on the left (cf. III, p. 165). Exercise prove the formula

$$
(X_0\wedge\cdots\wedge X_p)\rfloor d\omega
=
\sum_{i=0}^{p}(-1)^iX_i\bigl((X_0\wedge\cdots\wedge\widehat{X_i}\wedge\cdots\wedge X_p)\rfloor\omega\bigr)
$$

$$
\qquad+\sum_{0\leq i<j\leq p}(-1)^{i+j}\bigl([X_i,X_j]\wedge X_0\wedge\cdots\wedge\widehat{X_i}\wedge\cdots\wedge\widehat{X_j}\wedge\cdots\wedge X_p\bigr)\rfloor\omega
$$

where $\omega\in\Omega^p_{A/k}$, $X_0,\ldots,X_p\in D_k(A)$, and where the sign $\widehat{\ }$ on a letter means that it should be omitted. (Return to case $p=1$.)
