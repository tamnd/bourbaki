Let $u : C' \to C$ be a morphism of A-complexes. We denote by $v : C \to \mathrm{Coker}\,(u)$ the passing map to the quotient. We consider the graded A-homomorphisms

$$
\alpha : (\mathrm{Ker}\,u)\ (-1) \to \mathrm{Con}\,(u) \quad \text{and} \quad \varphi : \mathrm{Con}\,(u) \to \mathrm{Coker}\,(u)
$$

defined by $\alpha(x') = (x', 0)$, $\varphi(x', x) = v(x)$ for $x' \in C'$, $x \in C$. Now prove that $\alpha, \varphi$ are morphisms of complexes and that they give rise to an exact sequence

$$
\cdots \to H_{n-1}(\mathrm{Ker}\,u) \xrightarrow{H(\alpha)} H_n(\mathrm{Con}\,(u)) \xrightarrow{H(\varphi)} H_n(\mathrm{Coker}\,u) \xrightarrow{\delta} H_{n-2}(\mathrm{Ker}\,u) \to \cdots
$$

where $\delta$ is the composite of the connecting homomorphisms relating to the two exact sequences

$$
0 \to \mathrm{Ker}\,u \to C' \to \mathrm{Im}\,u \to 0 \quad \text{and} \quad 0 \to \mathrm{Im}\,u \to C \to \mathrm{Coker}\,u \to 0 .
$$
