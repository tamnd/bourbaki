Let $A, B$ be two $k$-algebras (associative and with a unit), $M$ a left $A$-module, $P$ a left $B$-module, $N$ a $(B, A)$-bimodule.
(a) Prove that there exist two spectral sequences (X, pp. 175-177, exercises 14 to 17) of $k$-modules $^1E$ and $^2E$, converging to the same graded $k$-module, such that
$$
^1E_2^{pq} = \mathrm{Ext}_A^p(M, \mathrm{Ext}_B^q(N, P)) ; \quad ^2E_2^{pq} = \mathrm{Ext}_B^p(\mathrm{Tor}_q^A(N, M), P) .
$$
(b) By composition of the homomorphisms $\gamma$ and $\delta$ of exercise 15, p. 176, define a $k$-homomorphism
$$
\mu : \mathrm{Ext}_A(M, \mathrm{Hom}_B(N, P)) \to \mathrm{Hom}_B(\mathrm{Tor}^A(N, M), P) .
$$
If $P$ is injective, prove that $\mu$ is an isomorphism.
(c) If $N$ is a flat $A$-module, show that the sequence $^1E$ converges to $\mathrm{Ext}_B(N \otimes_A M, P)$; if $N$ is a projective $B$-module, the sequence $^2E$ converges to $\mathrm{Ext}_A(M, \mathrm{Hom}_B(N, P))$.
(d) Let $\rho : A \to B$ be a homomorphism of $k$-algebras. Show that there exists a spectral sequence $E$, such that $E_2^{pq} = \mathrm{Ext}_B^p(\mathrm{Tor}_q^A(B, M), P)$, converging to $\mathrm{Ext}_A(M, P)$, and a spectral sequence $'E$, such that
$$
'E_2^{pq} = \mathrm{Ext}_B^p(P, \mathrm{Ext}_A^q(B, M)) ,
$$
converging to $\mathrm{Ext}_A(P, M)$.
