Let $M$ be an a-module, $n$ an integer $\geqslant 1$. Now prove that the following conditions are equivalent:

α) $M$ admits a finite $n$-presentation (X, p. 180, exercise 6).

β) For every filtered inductive system $(N_\alpha)_{\alpha \in I}$, the canonical homomorphism
$$
\varprojlim \mathrm{Ext}_A^i(M, N_\alpha) \to \mathrm{Ext}_A^i(M, \varprojlim N_\alpha)
$$
is bijective for $i < n$.

γ) For every family $(P_\beta)_{\beta \in J}$ of right A-modules, the canonical homomorphism
$$
\mathrm{Tor}_i^A\left( \prod_{\beta \in J} P_\beta, M \right) \to \prod_{\beta \in J} \mathrm{Tor}_i^A(P_\beta, M)
$$
is bijective for $i < n$.

δ) For every set I, the canonical homomorphism $A_d^! \otimes_A M \to M^!$ is bijective, and one has
$$
\mathrm{Tor}_i^A(A_d^!, M) = 0 \quad \text{for } 0 < i < n .
$$
(Reason by induction on $n$, using exercise 18, p. 170.)
