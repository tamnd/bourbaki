We suppose that the ring A is integral. Now prove that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) $\mathrm{dh}(A) \leq 1$.
β) Every divisible a-module is injective.
γ) The ring A is prüferian (exercise 11) and noetherian.
(To show that α) implies β), use exercise 10.)
One then says that A is a Dedekind ring.
