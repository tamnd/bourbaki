Let $G$ be a group. We call *cohomological dimension* of $G$, and we note $\mathrm{dc}(G)$, the projective dimension of the $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module $\mathbf{Z}$. If $n$ is an integer, we therefore have $\mathrm{dc}(G) \leq n$ if and only if $H^q(G, M) = 0$ for all $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module $M$ and all $q > n$.

a) Prove that we have $\mathrm{da}_\mathbf{Z}(\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}) = \mathrm{dc}(G)$ (use exercise 26 and exercise 27, d), p. 196).

b) If $G$ is a free group not reduced to the identity element, we have $\mathrm{dc}(G) = 1$.

c) Let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$; prove the inequality $\mathrm{dc}(H) \leq \mathrm{dc}(G)$. If $\mathrm{dc}(G) < \infty$ and if $H$ has finite index in $G$, show that $\mathrm{dc}(H) = \mathrm{dc}(G)$ (show that for $q = \mathrm{dc}(G)$, the homomorphism br

$$
t^q : H^q(H, M) \to H^q(G, M)
$$

of exercise 14, p. 191, is surjective).

d) Show that the cohomological dimension of a finite group not reduced to the identity element is infinite. Deduce that if $\mathrm{dc}(G) < \infty$, any element of $G$ distinct from the identity element is of infinite order (we then say that $G$ is *without torsion*).

e) If $G$ is without torsion and if $H$ is a finite index subgroup of $G$, show that we have $\mathrm{dc}(H) = \mathrm{dc}(G)$. (If $(P, p)$ is a projective resolution of the $\mathbf{Z}^{(H)}$-module $\mathbf{Z}$ and if $(G : H) = n$, define on the $\mathbf{Z}^{(H)}$-graded module $T^n_\mathbf{Z}(P)$ a structure of $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-complex so that $(T^n_\mathbf{Z}(P), p^{\otimes n})$ is a projective resolution of the $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$-module $\mathbf{Z}$.)

f) If $H$ is a distinguished subgroup of $G$, show that we have

$$
\mathrm{dc}(G) \leq \mathrm{dc}(H) + \mathrm{dc}(G/H) .
$$
