Let $u : A \to B$ be a homomorphism of rings, M a B-module. Prove the inequality

$$
\mathrm{dp}_A(M) \leqslant \mathrm{dp}_B(M) + \mathrm{dp}_A(B_s)
$$

(Reason by induction on $\mathrm{dp}_B(M)$ or use the spectral sequence of Exercise 6, p. 188.)
