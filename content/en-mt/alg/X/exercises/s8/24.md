We note dhf (A) (resp. dif (A), resp. tdf (A)) the upper bound of the projective (resp. injective, resp. flat) dimensions of the A-modules for which this dimension is finite. If A is of finite homological dimension, we have dhf(A) = dif(A) = dh(A) and tdf(A) = td(A). If td(A) < ∞, we have tdf(A) = td(A).
a) Show that we have tdf (A) ≤ dif (A°); there is equality if A is Left noetherian (use X, p. 189, exercise 3).
b) We assume A Left noetherian. Show that we have dhf(A) ≤ di_A(A_s). If di_A(A_s) and dif(A) are finite, show that they are equal (use exercise 2, p. 202).
c) Let G be a finite group not reduced to the identity element, A to the ring $\mathbf{Z}^{(G)}$. Show that we have
$$
\mathrm{dh}(A) = + \infty , \quad \mathrm{dhf}(A) = \mathrm{dif}(A) = \mathrm{tdf}(A) = 1 , \quad \mathrm{di}_A(A_s) = + \infty
$$
(cf. exercise 20, p. 193).
d) We assume the self-injective ring A (cf. X, p. 171, exercise 26), not semisimple. Show that any non-projective A-module has infinite projective dimension and injective dimension. Deduce that we have $\mathrm{dh}(A) = + \infty , \mathrm{dhf}(A) = \mathrm{dif}(A) = \mathrm{tdf}(A) = \mathrm{di}_A(A_s) = 0$.
