Show that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) Every A-module of finite projective dimension is projective.
β) The ring A satisfies the equivalent conditions of exercise 21, and for any right ideal of finite type a distinct from A, there exists an element $x \neq 0$ of A such that $xa = 0$.
γ) The ring A satisfies the equivalent conditions of exercise 21, and every simple A-module is a quotient of an injective A-module.
(Show that α) entails that any decreasing sequence of monogeneous right ideals of A is stationary by adapting the proof of exercise 21. Then deduce from exercise 22 the equivalence of α) and β). To see that α) entails γ), consider a projective cover P of the simple module M, an injective module I containing P and a projective cover Q of I; define an A-homomorphism of Q into P, and deduce a surjection of I into M. To show that γ) entails α), show that an A-module M such that $\mathrm{dp}_A(M) \leq 1$ is projective by considering a projective cover $p : P \to M$ and a surjection of $\ker p$ on a simple module.)
