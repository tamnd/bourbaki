Let A be an augmented k-algebra (X, p. 196, exercise 27); we suppose that there exists a homomorphism br $\rho : A \to A^e$ which satisfies the conditions of X, p. 196, exercise 27, c). Show that $\mathrm{da}_k(A) = \mathrm{dp}_A(k)$; if moreover k is a field, we have $\mathrm{da}_k(A) = \mathrm{dp}_A(k) = \mathrm{dh}(A) = \mathrm{dh}(A^*)$. (Use exercise 27, b) and c), p. 196, and exercise 26, e), p. 196.) In particular, if V is a non-zero k-vector space, we have $\mathrm{dh}\ T_k(V) = 1$.
