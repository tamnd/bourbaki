a) Suppose that the ring A is integral. Now prove that the conditions α) to δ) of Exercise 9 are still equivalent to the following conditions:
ε) Every finitely generated torsion-free a-module is projective.
ζ) Every torsion-free a-module is flat.
η) Every submodule of a flat a-module is flat.
(Use Exercise 13, p. 169.)
An integral ring satisfying these conditions is said to be Prüferian.
b) Let B be an integral ring, union of a filtered increasing family of Prüferian subrings. Now prove that B is Prüferian.
*c) Now prove that the ring of algebraic integers is Prüferian.*
