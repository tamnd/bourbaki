We note $A^e$ the k-algebra $A \otimes_k A^*$, and we consider A as a $A^e$-module on the left. We pose
$$
\mathrm{da}_k(A) = \mathrm{dp}_{A^e}(A) .
$$
If n is an integer $\geq 0$, the inequality $\mathrm{da}_k(A) \leq n$ is therefore equivalent to the nullity of $H^{n+1}(A, M)$ for all (A, A)-bimodule M (X, p. 195, exercise 26). We have $\mathrm{da}_k(A) = \mathrm{da}_k(A^o)$.
a) Show that we have $\mathrm{da}_k(M_n(k)) = 0$ and $\mathrm{da}_k(k[X_1, ..., X_n]) = n$ for all $n \geq 0$.
b) If k is a field, we have $\mathrm{da}_k(A) = 0$ if and only if the k-algebra A is absolutely semisimple (cf. VIII, § 11, no 3, th. 2).
c) Let M be a free k-module. Proving that we have $\mathrm{da}_k(T_k(M)) = 1$ (if $(e_i)_{i \in I}$ is a base of M, proving that the elements $(e_i \otimes 1 - 1 \otimes e_i)$ form a base of the ideal to the left of $A^e$ kernel of the multiplication $A \otimes_k A \to A$, with $A = T_k(M)$).
d) We now assume the projective k-module A. Let $\rho : k \to k'$ be a homomorphism br of commutative rings; show that we have $\mathrm{da}_k(A \otimes_k k') \leq \mathrm{da}_k(A)$. If $\rho$ is injective and if $\rho(k)$ is a direct factor of the k-module $k'$, we have equality (use X, p. 195, exercise 26, c)).
e) Let B be a second k-algebra (associative and uniferous), projective on k. Show that
$$
\mathrm{da}_k(A \otimes_k B) \leq \mathrm{da}_k(A) + \mathrm{da}_k(B) .
$$
If, moreover, k is a field and A and B are of finite dimension over k, there is equality (use X, p. 195, exercise 26, d)).
f) We assume that k is a field. Proving the inequalities $\mathrm{dh}(A) \leq \mathrm{da}_k(A)$ and $\mathrm{dh}(A^*) \leq \mathrm{da}_k(A)$ (cf. X, p. 196, exercise 26, e)).
