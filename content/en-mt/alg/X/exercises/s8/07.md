Let M be an A-module. The *flat dimension* of M is called, and is denoted by $\mathrm{dpl}_A(M)$, the greatest lower bound in $\overline{\mathbf{Z}}$ of the lengths of the flat resolutions of M.
a) If n is an integer $\geqslant 0$, prove that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) $\mathrm{dpl}_A(M) \leqslant n$.
β) $\mathrm{Tor}_r^A(N, M) = 0$ for every right a-module N and every integer $r > n$.
γ) $\mathrm{Tor}_{n+1}^A(N, M) = 0$ for every right a-module N.
δ) $\mathrm{Tor}_{n+1}^A(N, M) = 0$ for every monogeneous right a-module N.
ε) For every exact sequence $0 \to K \to P_{n-1} \to \cdots \to P_0 \to M \to 0$ where the $P_k$ are flat, K is flat.
b) Prove that $\mathrm{dpl}_A(M) \leqslant \mathrm{dp}_A(M)$; there is equality if A is Noetherian and M is of finite type.
c) Let $(N_\alpha)_{\alpha \in I}$ be an inductive system of A-modules and N its inductive limit. Prove the inequality

$$
\mathrm{dpl}_A(N) \leqslant \sup_{\alpha \in I} \mathrm{dpl}_A(N_\alpha)
$$
