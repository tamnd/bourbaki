We assume that the ring A is an algebra on a commutative ring $k$. Let $\eta:k\to A$ be the homomorphism br defined by $\eta(\lambda)=\lambda1_A$ for $\lambda\in k$, and let $\bar A$ be its cokernel. If $a$ is an element of A, we will note $\bar a$ its class in $\bar A$. We pose
$$
N_n(A)=A\otimes_k\bar A^{\otimes n}\otimes_k A
$$
for $n\geq0$, $N_n(A)=0$ for $n<0$, and
$$
N(A)=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbf Z}N_n(A).
$$

a) Show that there exists a $(A,A)$-endomorphism $d$ of $N(A)$, graded by degree $(-1)$, such that
$$
d(a\otimes\bar a_1\otimes\cdots\otimes\bar a_n\otimes b)
=aa_1\otimes\bar a_2\otimes\cdots\otimes\bar a_n\otimes b
+\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(-1)^i a\otimes\cdots\otimes\bar a_i a_{i+1}\otimes\cdots\otimes\bar a_n\otimes b
+(-1)^n a\otimes\bar a_1\otimes\cdots\otimes\bar a_{n-1}\otimes a_nb
$$
for $a,a_1,\ldots,a_n,b\in A$.

b) Show that $d\mathbin{\circ}d=0$, so that $N(A)$ is a complex of $(A,A)$-bimodules. If M is an A-module on the left, we note $N(A,M)$ the A-complex $N(A)\otimes_A M$.

c) Let $B(A,M)$ be the standard resolution of M (X, p. 58), $p:B(A,M)\to N(A,M)$ the A-homomorphism defined by
$$
p(a_0\otimes\cdots\otimes a_n\otimes m)
=a_0\otimes\bar a_1\otimes\cdots\otimes\bar a_n\otimes m
$$
for $a_0,\ldots,a_n\in A$, $m\in M$. Show that $p$ is a homotopism of A-complexes (construct by induction an inverse map up to homotopy).
