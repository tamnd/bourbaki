Let $a,b$ be two elements of A, such that the left annihilator of $a$ (resp. $b$) is the ideal $Ab$ (resp. $Aa$). Prove that the sequence

$$
\cdots\longrightarrow A_s\xrightarrow{\delta_a}A_s\xrightarrow{\delta_b}A_s\xrightarrow{\delta_a}A_s\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow A_s\xrightarrow{\delta_a}A_s\longrightarrow A/Aa\longrightarrow0,
$$

where $\delta_a$ (resp. $\delta_b$) denotes right multiplication by $a$ (resp. $b$), defines a free resolution of the a-module $A/Aa$. Prove that this construction applies in the following two cases:

$\alpha)$ Let $A_0$ be a ring; one takes $A=A_0(\varepsilon)$ (X, p. 27), $a=b=\varepsilon$.

$\beta)$ Let $G$ be a finite cyclic group, $\sigma$ a generator of $G$, $k$ a commutative ring. One takes

$$
A=k^{(G)},\qquad a=1-e_\sigma,\qquad b=\sum_{g\in G}e_g.
$$
