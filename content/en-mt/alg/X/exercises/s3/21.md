Let $k$ be a commutative ring, $\mathfrak g$ be a $k$-Lie algebra, U be its enveloping algebra (cf. LIE, I, § 2). We assume that $\mathfrak g$ is a free $k$-module.

a) Show that there exists for all $n\geq0$ an injective U-homomorphism
$$
j_n:U\otimes_k\Lambda^n(\mathfrak g)\to U^{\otimes(n+1)}
$$
such as
$$
j_n\bigl(u\otimes(x_1\wedge\cdots\wedge x_n)\bigr)
=\sum_{\sigma\in\mathfrak S_n}\epsilon(\sigma)\,u\otimes x_{\sigma(1)}\otimes\cdots\otimes x_{\sigma(n)}
$$
for $u\in U,x_1,\ldots,x_n\in\mathfrak g$.

We thus define a graded U-homomorphism of degree 0 $j:U\otimes_k\Lambda(\mathfrak g)\to B(U,k)$, where $B(U,k)$ is the standard resolution of the U-module on the left $k$ (the structure of U-module of $k$ being deduced from the natural homomorphism br $U\to k$). Show that $U\otimes_k\Lambda(\mathfrak g)$ is identified by $j$ with a subcomplex of $B(U,k)$, the differential being given by the formula:
$$
d\bigl(u\otimes(x_1\wedge\cdots\wedge x_n)\bigr)
=
\sum_{1\leq i\leq n}(-1)^{i+1}ux_i\otimes(x_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\hat x_i\wedge\cdots\wedge x_n)
$$
$$
+\sum_{1\leq i<j\leq n}(-1)^{i+j}u\otimes\bigl([x_i,x_j]\wedge x_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\hat x_i\wedge\cdots\wedge\hat x_j\wedge\cdots\wedge x_n\bigr)
$$
for $u\in U,x_1,\ldots,x_n\in\mathfrak g$ (where the $\hat{\ }$ sign on a letter means it should be omitted). We note $V(\mathfrak g)$ the U-complex thus defined.

b) Show that $V(\mathfrak g)$ defines a free resolution of the U-module $k$ (let $(U_r)_{r\geq0}$ be the natural filtration of U, $F_rV(\mathfrak g)$ the sub-$k$-module of $V(\mathfrak g)$ generated by the elements $u_p\otimes x_q$ for $u_p\in U_p$, $x_q\in\Lambda^q(\mathfrak g)$, $p+q\leq r$). Show that $F_rV(\mathfrak g)$ is a subcomplex of $V(\mathfrak g)$, and that the complex
$$
\bigoplus_{r\geq0}F_rV(\mathfrak g)/F_{r-1}V(\mathfrak g)
$$
is isomorphic to the complex $S(\mathfrak g)\otimes\Lambda(\mathfrak g)$ defined in X, p. 151). \*
