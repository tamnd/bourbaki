a) Let $0 \to M' \to M \to M'' \to 0$ be an exact sequence of A-modules, (P', p') (resp. (P'', p'')) a projective resolution of M' (resp. M''). Now prove that there exists a projective resolution (P, p) of M and a commutative diagram:
$$
\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
0 & \to & P' & \to & P & \to & P'' & \to & 0 \\
   &     & \downarrow p' & & \downarrow p & & \downarrow p'' & & \\
0 & \to & M' & \to & M & \to & M'' & \to & 0
\end{array}
$$
where the horizontal lines are exact.
b) Let $0 \to N' \to N \to N'' \to 0$ be a second exact sequence of A-modules, (Q, q) (resp. (Q', q'), resp. (Q'', q'')) a projective resolution of N (resp. N', resp. N''), in such a way that there exists a commutative diagram with exact lines:
$$
\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
0 & \to & Q' & \to & Q & \to & Q'' & \to & 0 \\
   &     & \downarrow q' & & \downarrow q & & \downarrow q'' & & \\
0 & \to & N' & \to & N & \to & N'' & \to & 0
\end{array}
$$
Let on the other hand:
$$
\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
0 & \to & M' & \to & M & \to & M'' & \to & 0 \\
   &     & \downarrow u' & & \downarrow u & & \downarrow u'' & & \\
0 & \to & N' & \to & N & \to & N'' & \to & 0 .
\end{array}
$$
be a commutative diagram of exact sequences, and let $\tilde{u}' : P' \to Q'$ and $\tilde{u}'' : P'' \to Q''$ be morphisms of complexes such that $u' \circ p' = q' \circ \tilde{u}'$ and $u'' \circ p'' = q'' \circ \tilde{u}''$. Now prove that there exists a morphism $\tilde{u} : P \to Q$ such that $u \circ p = q \circ \tilde{u}$ and that the diagram:
$$
\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
0 & \to & P' & \to & P & \to & P'' & \to & 0 \\
   &     & \downarrow \tilde{u}' & & \downarrow \tilde{u} & & \downarrow \tilde{u}'' & & \\
0 & \to & Q' & \to & Q & \to & Q'' & \to & 0
\end{array}
$$
is commutative.

c) Let $\tilde{u}_1' : P' \to Q', \tilde{u}_1 : P \to Q$ and $\tilde{u}_1'' : P'' \to Q''$ be three morphisms of complexes such that
$$
u' \circ p' = q' \circ \tilde{u}_1', \quad u \circ p = q \circ \tilde{u}_1, \quad u'' \circ p'' = q'' \circ \tilde{u}_1''
$$
and making the diagram (*) commutative; let $s'$ (resp. $s''$) be a homotopy connecting $\tilde{u}'$ to $\tilde{u}_1'$ (resp. $\tilde{u}''$ to $\tilde{u}_1''$). Now prove that there exists a homotopy $s$ connecting $\tilde{u}$ to $\tilde{u}_1$, such that the diagram:

$$
\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
0 & \to & P' & \to & P & \to & P'' & \to & 0 \\
& & \downarrow^{s'} & & \downarrow^s & & \downarrow^{s''} & & \\
0 & \to & Q' & \to & Q & \to & Q'' & \to & 0
\end{array}
$$

is commutative.

d) State and prove the results corresponding to $a), b), c)$ for injective resolutions.
