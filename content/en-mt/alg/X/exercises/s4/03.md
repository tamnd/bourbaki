Assume that the ring A is commutative. Let l be a finite set and $(C^{(i)}, d^{(i)})_{i ∈ I}$ a family of complexes of A-modules.
(a) The tensor product $\otimes C^{(i)}$ is provided with its grading of type $\mathbf{Z}^l$ and with the A-endomorphisms
$$
d_i = \bigotimes_{j ∈ I} d_i^{(j)} \quad \text{with} \quad d_i^{(j)} = 1_{C^{(j)}} \quad \text{for} \quad j ≠ i \quad \text{and} \quad d_i^{(i)} = d^{(i)} .
$$
Now prove that one thus obtains an I-precomplex (X, exercise 13, p. 174); the associated I-complex is called the l-complex tensor product of the complexes C^{(i)}. The complex associated with this I-complex is called the tensor product complex of the C^{(i)}.
(b) Now prove that the choice of a total order on I allows one to define an isomorphism of the tensor product complex of the C^{(i)} onto the complex defined in X, p. 64.
