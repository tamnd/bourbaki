Let $C$ be a complex of right $A$-modules and $M$ a left $A$-module. The torsion product of $C$ and $M$ is defined to be the graded $k$-module $\mathcal{T}or^A(C,M)=H(C\otimes_A L(M))$. If $f:C\to C'$ is a morphism of complexes and $g:M\to M'$ a homomorphism of $A$-modules, we denote by $\mathcal{T}or^A(f,g)=H(f\otimes L(g))$.

a) If $f:C\to C'$ is a homomorphism, $\mathcal{T}or^A(f,1)$ is an isomorphism; if $C$ is flat and right bounded, the graded $k$-module $\mathcal{T}or^A(C,M)$ is isomorphic to $H(C\otimes_A M)$.

b) If $0\to C'\to C\to C''\to0$ is an exact sequence of complexes of right $A$-modules and if $M$ is an $A$-module, prove that there exists an exact sequence

$$
\cdots\longrightarrow\mathcal{T}or^A_n(C',M)\longrightarrow
\mathcal{T}or^A_n(C,M)\longrightarrow
\mathcal{T}or^A_n(C'',M)\longrightarrow
\mathcal{T}or^A_{n-1}(C',M)\longrightarrow\cdots.
$$

If $0\to M'\mathop{\longrightarrow}^{i}M\mathop{\longrightarrow}^{p}M''\to0$ is an exact sequence of $A$-modules, prove that one has an exact sequence:

$$
\cdots\longrightarrow\mathcal{T}or^A_n(C,M')\longrightarrow
\mathcal{T}or^A_n(C,M)\longrightarrow
\mathcal{T}or^A_n(C,M'')\longrightarrow
\mathcal{T}or^A_{n-1}(C,M')\longrightarrow\cdots.
$$

(Notice that there exists a homotopism of $L(M'')$ onto the cone of $L(i)$.)

c) Prove that there exists a spectral sequence $'E$ (X, pp. 175-177, exercises 14 to 17) converging to $\mathcal{T}or^A(C,M)$, such that

$$
{}'E^2_{pq}=\operatorname{Tor}^A_p(H_q(C),M).
$$

If moreover the complex $C$ is right bounded, prove that there exists a spectral sequence $''E$, converging to $\mathcal{T}or^A(C,M)$, such that

$$
{}''E^2_{pq}=H_p(\operatorname{Tor}^A_q(C,M))
$$

(consider the double complex $C\otimes_A L(M)$).
