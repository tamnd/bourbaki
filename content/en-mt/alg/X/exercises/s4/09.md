Let $P$ be a complex of right $A$-modules, $Q$ a complex of left $A$-modules. Suppose either that $P$ and $Q$ are bounded below, or that there exists an integer $d$ such that every left or right $A$-module admits a projective resolution of length $\leq d$.

a) Now prove that there exist two spectral sequences (X, p. 175, exercise 14) $'E$ and $''E$, converging to the same graded $k$-module, such that

$$
{}'E^2_{pq}=H_p(\operatorname{Tor}^A_q(P,Q));
\qquad
{}''E^2_{pq}=\bigoplus_{q'+q''=q}\operatorname{Tor}^A_p(H_{q'}(P),H_{q''}(Q)).
$$

(where $\operatorname{Tor}^A_q(P,Q)$ denotes the complex associated with the bicomplex
$$
\bigoplus_{r,s}\operatorname{Tor}^A_q(P_r,Q_s).
$$
)

(Consider projective resolutions (X, p. 183, exercise 17) $\mathcal P$ and $\mathcal Q$ of $P$ and $Q$, and the double complex $C$ such that

$$
C_{p,q}=\bigoplus_{\substack{p'+p''=p\\q'+q''=q}}
(\mathcal P_{p',q'}\otimes\mathcal Q_{p'',q''}).
$$
)

b) If $P$ is flat, now prove that the spectral sequence $''E$ converges to $H(P\otimes_A Q)$. If $H(P)$ is flat, the spectral sequence $'E$ converges to $H(P)\otimes_A H(Q)$.
