Suppose that there exists a homomorphism (unital) of $k$-algebras π : A → k, which gives k a structure of A-module. Consider the standard resolution B(A, k) (X, p. 58); for n ≥ 0, identify B_n(A, k) with A ⊗_k A^⊗n and denote by a[a_1, ..., a_n] the element a ⊗ a_1 ⊗ ... ⊗ a_n of A ⊗_k A^⊗n.
(a) Prove that the complex B(A, k) ⊗_k B(A, k) defines a resolution of the (A ⊗_k A)-module k. Prove that the (A ⊗_k A)-linear homomorphism
$$
g : B(A ⊗_k A, k) → B(A, k) ⊗_k B(A, k)
$$
such that
$$
g([x_1 ⊗ y_1, ..., x_n ⊗ y_n]) = \sum_{0 ≤ p ≤ n} π(x_{p+1} ... x_n) [x_1, ..., x_p] ⊗ y_1 ... y_p[y_{p+1}, ..., y_n]
$$
for x_1, ..., x_n, y_1, ..., y_n in A, is a morphism of resolutions.
(b) Suppose that A is commutative. Prove that there exists on B(A, k) a structure of graded A-algebra such that one has for x_1, ..., x_n ∈ A:
$$
[x_1, ..., x_p] [x_{p+1}, ..., x_n] = \sum_{σ ∈ M_{p,n}} ε(σ) [x_{σ(1)}, ..., x_{σ(n)}]
$$
where M_{p,n} is the subset of S_n formed by the permutations σ such that σ(1) < ... < σ(p) and σ(p + 1) < ... < σ(n).
Prove that B(A, k) is then a differential graded A-algebra (X, p. 183, exercise 18).
