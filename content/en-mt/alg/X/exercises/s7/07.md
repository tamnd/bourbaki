Soient $A'$ a $k$-algebra (associative and unital) and $B = A \otimes_k A'$.
a) Let M be a left A-module, Q a right A-module, $M'$ a left $A'$-module, Q’ a right $A'$-module. Let $(P, p)$ (resp. $(P', p')$, resp. $(R, r)$) be a projective resolution of the A-module M (resp. of the $A'$-module $M'$, resp. of the B-module $M \otimes_k M'$). Prove that there exists a morphism of B-complexes
$$
u : P \otimes_k P' \to R ,
$$

unique up to homotopy, such that $r\circ u=p\otimes p'$. Define using $u$ a $k$-homomorphism graded of degree zero

$$
\mathrm{T}:\operatorname{Tor}^{A}(Q,M)\otimes_k\operatorname{Tor}^{A'}(Q',M')\longrightarrow\operatorname{Tor}^{B}(Q\otimes_k Q',M\otimes_k M').
$$

Prove that $\mathrm{T}$ does not depend on the choice of the resolutions $P,P',R$ and of the morphism $u$. If $a\in\operatorname{Tor}^{A}(Q,M)$ and $a'\in\operatorname{Tor}^{A'}(Q',M')$, put $\mathrm{T}(a\otimes a')=a\mathrm{T}a'$.

b) Suppose in addition that the $k$-modules A and A’ are *projective* and that one has $\operatorname{Tor}^{k}_{n}(M,M')=0$ for $n>0$.
Prove that $P\otimes_k P'$ is then a projective resolution of the B-module $M\otimes_k M'$; deduce, for every A-module N and every A’-module N’, a $k$-homomorphism graded of degree zero

$$
\vee:\operatorname{Ext}_{A}(M,N)\otimes_k\operatorname{Ext}_{A'}(M',N')\longrightarrow\operatorname{Ext}_{B}(M\otimes_k M',N\otimes_k N').
$$

Prove that $\vee$ is independent of the choice of the resolutions $P$ and $P'$. If $b\in\operatorname{Ext}_{A}(M,N)$ and $b'\in\operatorname{Ext}_{A'}(M',N')$, we note $\vee(b\otimes b')=b\vee b'$.

c) Let A’’ be a third $k$-algebra (associative and unital), M’’ and N’’ left A’’-modules, Q’’ a right A’’-module. We identify the $k$-algebras $A\otimes_k(A'\otimes_k A'')$ and $(A\otimes_k A')\otimes_k A''$, as well as the $k$-modules $M\otimes_k(M'\otimes_k M'')$ and $(M\otimes_k M')\otimes_k M''$, $N\otimes_k(N'\otimes_k N'')$ and $(N\otimes_k N')\otimes_k N''$, $Q\otimes_k(Q'\otimes_k Q'')$ and $(Q\otimes_k Q')\otimes_k Q''$. Prove the formulas

$$
a\mathrm{T}(a'\mathrm{T}a'')=(a\mathrm{T}a')\mathrm{T}a''
\qquad\text{pour }a\in\operatorname{Tor}^{A}(Q,M),\quad a'\in\operatorname{Tor}^{A'}(Q',M'),
$$

$$
a''\in\operatorname{Tor}^{A''}(Q'',M'')
$$

$$
b\vee(b'\vee b'')=(b\vee b')\vee b''
\qquad\text{pour }b\in\operatorname{Ext}_{A}(M,N),\quad b'\in\operatorname{Ext}_{A'}(M',N'),
$$

$$
b''\in\operatorname{Ext}_{A''}(M'',N'').
$$

d) Let
$$
\sigma:\operatorname{Tor}^{A\otimes_k A'}(Q\otimes_k Q',M\otimes_k M')
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Tor}^{A'\otimes_k A}(Q'\otimes_k Q,M'\otimes_k M)
$$

and

$$
\tau:\operatorname{Ext}_{A\otimes_k A'}(M\otimes_k M',N\otimes_k N')
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{Ext}_{A'\otimes_k A}(M'\otimes_k M,N'\otimes_k N)
$$

the isomorphisms deduced from the commutation isomorphisms

$$
A\otimes_k A'\longrightarrow A'\otimes_k A,\quad
M\otimes_k M'\longrightarrow M'\otimes_k M,\quad
N\otimes_k N'\longrightarrow N'\otimes_k N,\quad
Q\otimes_k Q'\longrightarrow Q'\otimes_k Q.
$$

Prove the formulas

$$
\sigma(a\mathrm{T}a')=(-1)^{pq}a'\mathrm{T}a
\qquad\text{pour }a\in\operatorname{Tor}^{A}_{p}(Q,M),\quad
a'\in\operatorname{Tor}^{A'}_{q}(Q',M')
$$

$$
\tau(b\vee b')=(-1)^{pq}b'\vee b
\qquad\text{pour }b\in\operatorname{Ext}^{p}_{A}(M,N),\quad
b'\in\operatorname{Ext}^{q}_{A'}(M',N').
$$

e) Let $(\mathcal{E}):0\to M_2\to M_1\to M_0\to0$ be an exact sequence of A-modules, such that the sequence

$$
(\mathcal{E}\otimes M'):0\to M_2\otimes_k M'\to M_1\otimes_k M'\to M_0\otimes_k M'\to0
$$

be exact. We denote $\delta$ (resp. $\Delta$) the connecting homomorphism $\partial(Q,\mathcal{E})$ (resp. $\partial(Q\otimes_k Q',\mathcal{E}\otimes M')$. Now prove that one has $(\delta a)\mathrm{T}a'=\Delta(a\mathrm{T}a')$ for $a\in\operatorname{Tor}^{A}(Q,M_0)$, $a'\in\operatorname{Tor}^{A'}(Q',M')$. Exercise prove the analogous formulas for the product $\vee$.

f) If $k$ is a field, show that $\mathrm{T}$ is an isomorphism of graded $k$-modules; the same holds for $\vee$ if, in addition, A and A’ are left noetherian and if the A-module M and the A’-module M’ are of finite type.

g) If the modules $\operatorname{Tor}^{A}_{n}(Q,M)$ are identified with the sets $T_n(Q,M)$ defined in exercise 6, prove the formula

$$
(P,p,q)\mathrm{T}(P',p',q')=(P\otimes_k P',p\otimes p',q\otimes q').
$$
