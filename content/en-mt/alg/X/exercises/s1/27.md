We assume the Noetherian ring A on the left.
a) Let I be an indecomposable injective A-module. Show that the set of canceling ideals of a non-zero a-submodule of I admits a greatest element $\mathscr{U}(I)$, which is a prime two-sided ideal (VIII, § 8, exercise 19).
b) Show that for every prime two-sided ideal $p$ of A, there exists an indecomposable injective A-module I such that $\mathscr{U}(I) = p$ (consider a direct indecomposable factor of the injective envelope of $A/p$).
c) We assume A to be commutative. Show that every indecomposable injective A-module I is an injective envelope of $A/\mathscr{U}(I)$. Deduce that the map $I \mapsto \mathscr{U}(I)$ defines a bijection of the set $\mathscr{J}$ of classes of indecomposable injective A-modules on the set of prime ideals of A. The inverse bijection associates the injective envelope of $A/p$ with the ideal $p$.
d) Show that for the application $I \mapsto \mathscr{U}(I)$ of $\mathscr{J}$ on the set of prime two-sided ideals of A to be bijective, it suffices that A satisfies the following condition (H):

(H) For any left ideal $a$ of $A$, there exist elements $x_1, \ldots, x_k$ of $A/a$ such that
$$
\operatorname{Ann}(A/a) = \bigcap_i \operatorname{Ann}(x_i).
$$
e) Show that condition (H) is verified in the following cases:
$\alpha$ All ideals to the left of $A$ are two-sided,
$\beta$ $A$ is Artinian.
$\gamma$ The center $Z$ of $A$ is a Noetherian ring and $A$ is a $Z$-module of finite type.
f) Let $k$ be a field, $\mathbf{Q}^+$ the additive monoid of the rational numbers $\geqslant 0$, $A$ the algebra $k^{(\mathbf{Q}^+)}$, $a$ a nonzero element of $A$, $I$ the injective envelope of $A/Aa$. Show that the $A$-module $I$ is indecomposable, but does not contain a a-submodule isomorphic to $A/p$ for $p$ prime (note that the set of ideals of $A$ is totally ordered and that $A$ contains only two prime ideals).
