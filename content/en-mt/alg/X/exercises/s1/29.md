We assume that the ring $A$ is commutative and Noetherian. Let $a$ be an ideal of $A$.
a) Let $H$ be a $A$-module such that every element of $H$ is canceled by a power of $a$. Show that for $H$ to be injective, it is sufficient that for every integer $n \geqslant 1$, every $A$-module $M$ of finite type canceled by $a^n$ and every a-submodule $M'$ of $M$, the natural homomorphism br $\operatorname{Hom}_A(M, H) \to \operatorname{Hom}_A(M', H)$ is surjective.
(Use the fact that for every ideal $b$ of $A$, there exists an integer $k \geqslant 0$ such that $b \cap a^k \subset ba$ (AC, III, § 3, no 1, cor. 2).)
