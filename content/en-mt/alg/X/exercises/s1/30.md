We assume the ring $A$ local, commutative and Noetherian; we note $m$ its maximal ideal, $k = A/m$. Let $I$ be a $A$-module such that any element of $I$ is canceled by a power of $m$. For any $A$-module $M$, we note $T(M)$ the $A$-module $\operatorname{Hom}_A(M, I)$ and $c_M : M \to T(T(M))$ the homomorphism br defined by
$$
(c_M(m))(u) = u(m) \quad \text{pour} \quad m \in M, \quad u \in T(M).
$$
Show that the following properties are equivalent:
$\alpha$ For any $A$-module of finite length $M$, the $A$-module $T(M)$ is of finite length and the homomorphism br $c_M : M \to T(T(M))$ is bijective.
$\beta$ For every $A$-module of finite length $M$, we have $\operatorname{long} T(M) = \operatorname{long} M$.
$\gamma$ The $A$-module $I$ is injective and the $A$-modules $k$ and $T(k)$ are isomorphic.
$\delta$ The $A$-module $I$ is isomorphic to the injective envelope of $k$.
When these conditions are satisfied, we sometimes say that $I$ is a dualizing module for the ring $A$.
(Show that the property $\gamma$ is equivalent to each of the three others: to see that $\alpha$ or $\beta$ leads to $\gamma$, use exercise 29.)
