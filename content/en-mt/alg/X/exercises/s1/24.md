Let $i : A \to I$ be an injective envelope of the A-module $A_s$. We set $E = \mathrm{End}_A(I)$ and $B = \mathrm{End}_E(I)$, so that B is the bicommunant of A-module I (VIII, § 3, def. 1). We consider A as a subring of B. We note $j : B \to I$ and $p : E \to I$ the maps defined by $j(b) = b(i(1))$ and $p(u) = u(i(1))$ for $b \in B,\ u \in E$.
a) Show that p is a surjection and j an injection.

EXERCISES

b) Show that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) The application $p$ is bijective.
β) The application $j$ is bijective.
γ) The A-module B is injective.
If these conditions are met, the map $j^{-1} \circ p$ is an isomorphism of the ring E onto the ring opposite B.
c) Let $\mathfrak{F}$ be the set of left ideals $a$ of A such that $a \cap b \neq (0)$ for any non-zero left ideal $b$.
We note $s(A)$ the set of elements $a$ of A for which there exists $a \in \mathfrak{F}$ such that $aa = 0$. Show that $s(A)$ is a two-sided ideal of A.
d) Show that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) $s(A) = 0$.
β) The ring E is radical-free.
γ) Ring B is absolutely flat.
If these conditions are met, the equivalent properties of b) are satisfied.
(Let $s(I)$ be the set of elements $x$ of I for which there exists $a \in \mathfrak{F}$ such that $ax = 0$. Using exercise 23, proving that $p^{-1}(s(I))$ is equal to the radical r of E, hence the implication $\beta$) $\Rightarrow \alpha)$. Then show that $\alpha)$ implies $r = \mathrm{Ker}\,(p) = 0$, which entails $\beta)$ and $\gamma)$ (taking into account exercise 23) as well as the condition $\alpha)$ of b). Finally show that $\gamma)$ entails $\alpha)$.)
e) If A is left Noetherian, the ideal $s(A)$ is nilpotent (first proving that every element $a$ of $s(A)$ is nilpotent, by considering the left cancellers of $a^n$; then use exercise 8, b) of VIII, § 9).
