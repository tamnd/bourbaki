a) We keep the hypotheses of exercise 30; we further assume that A contains a field $k_0$ such that $k$ is a $k_0$-vector space of finite dimension. Show that the A-module $I = \lim_{\longrightarrow n} \mathrm{Hom}_{k_0}(A/m^n, k_0)$ is dualizing for A.
b) Let $k$ be a field of characteristic zero, B the ring of formal series $k[[T_1, ..., T_n]]$. We provide the group $I = k[X_1, ..., X_n]$ with a B-module structure by setting $T_i . P = \partial P / \partial X_i$ for all $P \in k[X_1, ..., X_n]$. Show that I is an injective envelope of $k$.
