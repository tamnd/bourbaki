With the hypotheses of the previous exercise, let $B$ be a local, commutative and Noetherian ring and let $\rho : A \to B$ be a ring homomorphism br which makes $B$ a $A$-module of finite type. If $I$ is a dualizing module for $A$, show that the $B$-module $\operatorname{Hom}_A(B, I)$ is dualizing for $B$. In particular for any ideal $a$ of $A$, the a-submodule of $I$ formed of the elements canceled by $a$ is a dualizing module for $A/a$.
