Let A be a ring, a an element of A. Now prove that the following properties are equivalent:
α) $a \in aAa$.
β) $Aa$ is a direct factor in the module $A_s$.
γ) $A_s/Aa$ is a flat a-module.
δ) For every right ideal b of A, one has $b \cap Aa = ba$.
