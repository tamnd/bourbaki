Let $A$ be a Noetherian ring, $M$ be a $A$-module, $I$ be an injective envelope of $M$, direct sum of a family $(I_\alpha)_{\alpha \in J}$ of indecomposable submodules. We note $\operatorname{Ass}(M)$ the set of first two-sided ideals $\mathfrak{U}(I_\alpha)$, for $\alpha \in J$ (exercise 27); if $p \in \operatorname{Ass}(M)$, we say that $p$ is associated with $M$.
a) We have $\operatorname{Ass}(M) \neq \varnothing$ if $M \neq 0$; if $M$ is of finite type, the set $\operatorname{Ass}(M)$ is finite.
b) Show that a prime two-sided ideal $p$ is associated with $M$ if and only if $M$ contains a a-submodule $N$ such that $\operatorname{Ann}(N') = p$ for every non-zero a-submodule $N'$ of $N$. If $A$ is commutative, $p$ is associated with $M$ if and only if $M$ contains a a-submodule isomorphic to $A/p$,
c) Let $M'$ be a a-submodule of $M$, Show that we have
$$
\operatorname{Ass}(M') \subset \operatorname{Ass}(M) \subset \operatorname{Ass}(M') \cup \operatorname{Ass}(M/M').
$$
d) We put $J_p = \sum_{\mathfrak{U}(I_\alpha) = p} I_\alpha$ and $Q(p) = \left( \sum_{q \neq p} J_q \right) \cap M$. Show that we have $\bigcap_{p \in \operatorname{Ass}(M)} Q(p) = 0$ and $\bigcap_{p \in E} Q(p) \neq 0$ for any subset $E$ of $\operatorname{Ass}(M)$ distinct from $\operatorname{Ass}(M)$. For every $p \in \operatorname{Ass}(M)$, we have $\operatorname{Ass}(M/Q(p)) = \{ p \}$.
e) Let $a$ be an element of the center of $A$. Show that for the homothety of relation $a$ to be injective in $M$, it is necessary and sufficient that $a$ does not belong to any of the primary ideals associated with $M$.
