Let I be an injective A-module, E the ring $\mathrm{End}_A(I)$, r the set of elements $u$ of E such that I is the injective envelope of $\mathrm{Ker}(u)$.
a) Show that r is a two-sided ideal and that the ring $E/r$ is absolutely flat (exercise 16). (Let $u$ be an element of E, N a a-submodule of I such that $N \cap \mathrm{Ker}\,u = 0$ and maximal for this property; show that there exists $v \in E$ such that $vu(x) = x$ for all $x \in N$, and deduce that $uvu - u \in r$.)
b) Show that r is the radical of E (the inclusion $r(E) \subset r$ results from a); show that for all $u \in r$, $1 - u$ is bijective).
c) We suppose that I is the direct sum of a finite family of indecomposable injectives. Show that the ring $E/r$ is semisimple.
