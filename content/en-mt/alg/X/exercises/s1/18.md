Let M be an A-module. Show that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) M is of finite type (resp. of finite presentation).
β) For any inductive system (N_i, u_{ij}) of A-modules relating to a filtering preordered set I, the canonical homomorphism br $\lim_{\longrightarrow i \in I} \mathrm{Hom}_A(M, N_i) \to \mathrm{Hom}_A(M, \lim_{\longrightarrow i \in I} N_i)$ is injective (resp. bijective).
γ) For any family $(P_j)_{j \in J}$ of A-modules on the right, the canonical homomorphism br
$$
(\prod_{j \in J} P_j) \otimes_A M \to \prod_{j \in J} (P_j \otimes_A M)
$$
(II, p. 61) is surjective (resp. bijective).
δ) For any set I, the canonical homomorphism br $A_d^1 \otimes_A M \to M^I$ is surjective (resp. bijective).
