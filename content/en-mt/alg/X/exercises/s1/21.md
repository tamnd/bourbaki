Show that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) A is Noetherian.
β) Every inductive limit A-module of injective A-modules is injective.
γ) Any direct sum A-module of injective A-modules is injective.
(To proving that γ) entails α), let $(a_n)_{n \geq 1}$ be an increasing sequence of ideals to the left of A, $a = \bigcup_n a_n$, $I_n$ an injective envelope of $a/a_n$. The natural homomorphism br of a in $\bigoplus I_n$ is the restriction of a homomorphism br $f : A \to \bigoplus I_n$; consider $f(1)$.)
