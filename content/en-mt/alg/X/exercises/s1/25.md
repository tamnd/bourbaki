We assume that ring A is left Noetherian and does not contain nilpotent two-sided ideals.
a) Proving that the ring B defined in exercise 24 is semisimple (“Goldie’s theorem”; use exercise 23, c) and exercise 24, d) and e)). If moreover (0) is a prime two-sided ideal in A (VIII, § 8, exercise 19), show that B is simple.
b) We identify A with a subring of B. Proving the following properties:
(i) Any element of A that is simplifiable on the left is invertible in B (therefore simplifiable in A).
(ii) Any element $b$ of B can be written $s^{-1} a$, with $s \in A$, $a \in A$ and $s$ simplification,
We sometimes say that B is the total ring of fractions to the left of A.
(To proving (ii), reduce to the case where B is simple, Show that then the ideal (0) of A is prime, Let $a$ be an ideal of $\mathfrak{F}$, $a$ be an element of $a$ for which the ideal on the right $aB$ is maximal among the ideals $a'$ B ($a' \in a$), $x$ an element of B such that $axa = a$ Proving that $B(1 - xa) \subset aB$, hence.
$$
(B(1 - ax) \cap a) \cdot (B(1 - xa) \cap a) = (0);
$$
deduce that $a$ is simplifiable. Conclude by taking for $a$ the set of $a \in A$ such as $ab \in A$.)
c) We assume that any nonzero element of A is simplifiable. Show that B is a field, which is a field of left fractions for A (cf. I, p. 155, exercise 15).
