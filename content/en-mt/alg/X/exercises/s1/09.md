Let P be an a-module. Prove that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) For a sequence M' $\twoheadrightarrow$ M $\twoheadrightarrow$ M'' of right a-modules to be exact, it is necessary and sufficient that the sequence
$$
M' \otimes_A P \xrightarrow{u \otimes 1} M \otimes_A P \xrightarrow{v \otimes 1} M'' \otimes_A P
$$
be exact.
β) P is flat, and for every nonzero right a-module M, one has $M \otimes_A P \neq (0)$.
γ) P is flat, and for all right a-modules M, N, the canonical homomorphism
$$
\operatorname{Hom}_A(M, N) \to \operatorname{Hom}_\mathbf{Z}(M \otimes_A P, N \otimes_A P)
$$
is injective.
δ) P is flat, and for every maximal right ideal m of A, one has $mP \neq P$.
P is then said to be a faithfully flat A-module.
