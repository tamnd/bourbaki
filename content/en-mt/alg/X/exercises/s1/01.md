Soit

$$
\begin{array}{ccccccc}
M &\longrightarrow& N &\xrightarrow{t}& P &\longrightarrow& Q\\
\downarrow u && \downarrow v && \downarrow w && \downarrow s\\
M'&\longrightarrow&N'&\xrightarrow{t'}&P'&\longrightarrow&Q'
\end{array}
$$

a commutative diagram of $A$-modules in which the rows are exact; suppose that $u$ is surjective and $s$ is injective. Now prove that:

$$
\operatorname{Ker}(w)=t(\operatorname{Ker}(v))\qquad
\operatorname{Im}(v)={t'}^{-1}(\operatorname{Im}(w)).
$$
