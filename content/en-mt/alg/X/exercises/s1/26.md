a) Show that the following conditions are equivalent:
α) The ring A is Right noetherian, and the A-module $A_s$ is injective.
$\alpha^0$ A is Noetherian on the left and the A-module $A_d$ is injective,
β) Ring A is auto-injective (VIII, § 2, exercise 11).
(Proving that $\alpha$ entails that every right ideal r satisfies $r = r^0$. Then show that $r(A)$ is nilpotent, and deduce that A is right artinian using exercise 23. Then proving that the dual of a simple left A-module is simple. Deduce that A is left Noetherian and finally auto-injective.
b) Show that the ring A is self-injective if and only if every injective A-module is projective and every projective A-module is injective.
