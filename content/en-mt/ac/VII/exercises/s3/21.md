Now prove that in $\mathbf{Z}[X]$ the following polynomials are irreducible:
$X^n - a$, where one of the prime factors of $a$ has exponent 1 ;
$X^{2^k} + 1$, (replace $X$ by $X + 1$);
$X^4 + 3X^3 + 3X^2 - 5$;
$5X^4 - 6X^3 - aX^2 - 4X + 2$.
(Use Exercise 20).
