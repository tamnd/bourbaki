Soit $K$ une extension de type fini d’un corps $k$ ; prove that the following conditions are equivalent:

(i) the $k$-algebra $K$ is formally smooth ;
(ii) the $k$-algebra $K$ is formally étale ;
(iii) $K$ is a finite separable extension of $k$.
