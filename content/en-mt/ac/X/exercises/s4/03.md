Let $A$ be a ring, $T = (T_i)_{i \in I}$ a finite family of indeterminates. Prove that the following conditions are equivalent:

(i) the ring $A$ is regular ;
(ii) the ring $A[T]$ is regular ;
(iii) the ring $A[[T]]$ is regular.
