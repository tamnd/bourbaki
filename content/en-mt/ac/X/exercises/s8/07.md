Let $A$ and $B$ be rings *not necessarily commutative*, $J$ an $(A, B)$-bimodule, $P$ a flat left $B$-module. Suppose that the ring $A$ is left noetherian. Prove that if $J$ is an injective $A$-module, then the same is true of $J \otimes_B P$.
