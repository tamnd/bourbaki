Let $A$ be a noetherian local ring.

a) Let $M$ and $N$ be $A$-modules such that $\mathrm{dpl}_A(M) < +\infty$ and $\mathrm{Tor}_i^A(M, N) = 0$ for $i > 0$. Define for every integer $n$ a canonical isomorphism

$$
\mathrm{Ext}_A^n(k_A, M \otimes_A N) \longrightarrow \bigoplus_{p \geqslant n} \left( \mathrm{Tor}_{p-n}^A(k_A, M) \otimes_k \mathrm{Ext}_A^p(k_A, N) \right)
$$

and prove that one has $\mathrm{di}_A(M \otimes_A N) \leqslant \mathrm{di}_A(N)$ (adapt the proof of Exercise 6 of § 3 with the aid of Exercise 7).

b) Let $N$, $P$ be $A$-modules such that $\mathrm{di}_A(P) < +\infty$ and $\mathrm{Ext}_A^i(N, P) = 0$ for $i > 0$. Define for every integer $n$ a canonical isomorphism

$$
\mathrm{Tor}_n^A(k_A, \mathrm{Hom}_A(N, P)) \longrightarrow \bigoplus_{p \geqslant n} \mathrm{Hom}_{k_A} \left( \mathrm{Ext}_A^{p-n}(k_A, P), \mathrm{Ext}_A^p(k_A, N) \right)
$$

(apply Matlis duality).

c) Prove that if there exists an $A$-module $M$ whose injective dimension and flat dimension are finite, $A$ is a Gorenstein ring (take $N = A$ in *a*)).

d) Conversely, if $A$ is a Gorenstein ring, prove that there is an identity between modules of finite injective dimension and modules of finite flat dimension (prove with the aid of $a$) that a module of finite flat dimension has finite injective dimension, and obtain the converse by Matlis duality).

e) Deduce from $d$ that the following conditions are equivalent:
    (i) $A$ is a Gorenstein ring ;
    (ii) the $A$-modules of finite type and of finite injective dimension coincide with the $A$-modules of finite type and of finite projective dimension ;
    (iii) there exists an $A$-module of finite type whose injective dimension and projective dimension are finite.
