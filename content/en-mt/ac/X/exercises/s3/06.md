Let $A$ be a noetherian local ring, $M$ a finitely generated $A$-module and of finite projective dimension, $N$ an $A$-module such that Tor_i^A(M, N) = 0 for i > 0.
(a) Define for every integer n a canonical isomorphism

$$
\operatorname{Ext}_A^n(\kappa_A, M \otimes_A N) \longrightarrow \bigoplus_{p \geq n} \operatorname{Tor}_{p-n}^A(\kappa_A, M) \otimes_k \operatorname{Ext}_A^p(\kappa_A, N),
$$

and prove that one has di_A(M \otimes_A N) \leq \operatorname{di}_A(N).
(Let (P, p) be a finite free resolution of M, (I, e) an injective resolution of N, and C the complex P \otimes_A I. Prove that the complex

$$
0 \to C^0/B^0(C) \to C^1 \to C^2 \to \ldots
$$

defines an injective resolution of M \otimes_A N.)
(b) Prove the inequality prof_A(N) = dp_A(M) + prof(M \otimes_A N). Deduce another proof of the theorem of Auslander-Buchsbaum.
(c) Suppose in addition that N is of finite projective dimension. Prove the equality dp_A(M \otimes_A N) = dp_A(M) + dp_A(N).
(d) Suppose that A is a Gorenstein ring, and denote its dimension by d. Let n be an integer; show that the $\kappa_A$-vector spaces $\operatorname{Ext}_A^n(\kappa_A, M)$, $\operatorname{Tor}_{d-n}^A(\kappa_A, M)$ and $\operatorname{Ext}_A^{d-n}(M, \kappa_A)$ have the same dimension.
