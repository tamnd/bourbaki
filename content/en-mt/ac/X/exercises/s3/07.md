Let $A$ be a local noetherian ring, $M$ a finitely generated a-module, $T$ a finitely generated a-module and of finite injective dimension. Exercise prove the equality

$$
\operatorname{prof}(A) = \operatorname{prof}_A(M) + \sup \{ i \mid \operatorname{Ext}_A^i(M, T) \neq 0 \}
$$

(reason by induction on \operatorname{prof}_A(M), using Prop. 9).
