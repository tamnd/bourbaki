Let k be a field, $P$ its prime subfield, $K$ an extension of $k$. Then the following conditions are equivalent:
a) Every derivation of $k$ into a $K$-module $M$ extends uniquely to a derivation of $K$ into $M$.
b) One has $\Omega_P(K) = \Omega_P(k) \otimes_k K$.
c) The field $K$ is separable over $k$ and $\Omega_k(K) = 0$.
If $k$ is of characteristic 0, these conditions are also equivalent to the condition:
d) The field $K$ is an algebraic extension of $k$.
If $k$ is of nonzero characteristic $p$, they are also equivalent to the conditions:
e) $K = k \otimes_{k^p} K^p$.
f) Every p-basis of $k$ is also a p-basis of $K$.
