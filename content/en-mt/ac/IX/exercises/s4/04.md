Let A be a ring. Consider on A the following two conditions:
(R1) For every prime ideal p of A of height $\leq 1$, the ring $A_p$ is regular.
(S2) The set $\mathrm{Ass}_A(A)$ and, for every cancellable element $a$ of A, the set $\mathrm{Ass}_A(A/aA)$ contain no embedded prime ideals.
A normal noetherian ring (Exercise 3) satisfies (R1) and (S2). (Use Exercises 2 and 3, c.).)
