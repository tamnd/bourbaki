Let $A$ be a ring of characteristic $p$.
a) Let $n \in \mathbf{N}$; prove that $p^{n-1}/n! \in \mathbf{Z}_{(p)}$. Prove that $(nm)!/n!(m!)^n$ is an integer for $n, m$ in $\mathbf{N}$.
b) For $n \in \mathbf{N}$, let $\gamma_n : V_1(A) \to W(A)$ be the mapping defined by
$$
\begin{align*}
\gamma_n(Vx) &= 1 & \text{if } n = 0, \\
\gamma_n(Vx) &= (p^{n-1}/n!) V(x^n) & \text{if } n \geqslant 1.
\end{align*}
$$
(i) Prove that one has $\gamma_n(x) \in V_1(A)$ if $n \geqslant 1$ and $x \in V_1(A)$.
(ii) For $x, y \in V_1(A)$ and $n \in \mathbf{N}$, one has
$$
\gamma_n(x + y) = \sum_{i=0}^n \gamma_i(x) \gamma_{n-i}(y).
$$
(iii) For $\lambda \in W(A), \ x \in V_1(A), \ n \in \mathbf{N}$, one has
$$
\gamma_n(\lambda x) = \lambda^n \gamma_n(x).
$$
(iv) For $x \in V_1(A)$ and $n, m$ in $\mathbf{N}$, one has
$$
\gamma_n(x) \gamma_m(x) = \binom{m+n}{n} \gamma_{n+m}(x)
$$
and
$$
\gamma_n(\gamma_m(x)) = \frac{(nm)!}{n!(m!)^n} \gamma_{nm}(x).
$$
(v) One has $F \gamma_n(Vx) = (p^n/n!) x^n$ and $\gamma_n(px) = (p^n/n!) x^n$, for $x \in W(A)$.
