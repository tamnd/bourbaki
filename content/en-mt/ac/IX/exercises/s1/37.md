Let A be a ring.
a) Let $m \in \mathbf{J}$. For every element $a = (a_n)_{n \in \mathbf{J}}$ of U(A), one has
$$
a = (a_1, ..., a_m, 0, ..., 0, ...) + (\underbrace{0, ..., 0}_{m \text{ terms}}, a_{m+1}, a_{m+2}, ...).
$$
b) We endow U(A) with the product topology on $A^\mathbf{J}$ of the discrete topology on each of the factors. It makes U(A) into a separated and complete topological ring.
c) We denote by $\tau_A$ (or $\tau$) the mapping from A into U(A) which associates to $a \in A$ the element $(a, 0, ..., 0) \in U(A)$.
Let $a, b$ be two elements of A, $x = (x_n)_{n \in \mathbf{J}}$ an element of U(A).
(i) One has the formulae
$$
\tau(ab) = \tau(a) \times \tau(b), \quad \tau(a) \times x = (a^n x_n)_{n \in \mathbf{J}},
$$
$$
\Phi(\tau(a)) = (a^n)_{n \in \mathbf{J}},
$$
$$
F_n(\tau(a)) = \tau(a^n) \quad \text{for every } n \in \mathbf{J}.
$$
(ii) The series with general term $V_n \tau(x_n)$ is convergent in U(A), with sum x.
