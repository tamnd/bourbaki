Let $A$ be a ring of characteristic $p$.
a) The ring $W(A)$ is an integral domain if and only if $A$ is an integral domain.
b) $W(A)$ is reduced if and only if $A$ is reduced.
c) The following conditions are equivalent:
   (i) $A$ is a perfect ring of characteristic $p$.
   (ii) $W(A)/pW(A)$ is reduced.
