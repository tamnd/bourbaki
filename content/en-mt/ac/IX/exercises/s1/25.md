Let A be a perfect ring of characteristic $p$.
a) Let B be an A-algebra and $\varphi : A \to B$ the structural homomorphism. For every integer $n \geqslant 1$, endow the group $W_n(B)$ with the $W(A)$-module structure defined by
$$
(a, b) \mapsto \varphi(F^{1-n}a).b \quad \text{for} \quad (a, b) \in W(A) \times W_n(B).
$$
Endowed with the action of F by the Frobenius homomorphism and of V by the shift homomorphism V, $W_n(B)$ is then a $\mathcal{D}_A$-module (Exercise 22). The mapping $V_n : W_n(B) \to W_{n+1}(B)$ induced by the shift is a homomorphism of $\mathcal{D}_A$-modules.
By transport of structure, the group $CW^n(B)$ is endowed with a structure of $\mathcal{D}_A$-module.
b) Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the polynomial ring $A[X]$ in a family $X = (X_i)_{i \in I}$ of indeterminates. For every integer $r \geqslant 0$, let $b_r$ be the ideal of $\mathcal{A}$ generated by the $X_{-n}$, for $n \geqslant r$. Let $r$ and $s$ be integers $\geqslant 1$. Then, for every element $a = (a_n)_{n \in \mathbf{N}}$ of $W(A)$, one has
$$
P_m(a_0^{p^{-m}}, ..., a_m^{p^{-m}}; X_{-m}, ..., X_0) \equiv P_{m+1}(a_0^{p^{-m-1}}, ..., a_{m+1}^{p^{-m-1}}; X_{-m-1}, ..., X_0) \mod. b_r^s,
$$
for every integer $m \geqslant r - 1$ if $s < p$, and for every integer $m \geqslant r - 1 + (s - p)/(p - 1)$ if $s \geqslant p$.
c) Let B be an A-algebra and $\varphi : A \to B$ the structural homomorphism. Let $a = (a_n)_{n \in \mathbf{N}}$ be an element of $W(A)$, and $b = (b_i)_{i \in I}$ an element of $CW(B)$. For every integer $n \geqslant 0$, the sequence of elements $P_m(\varphi(a_0^{p^{-n-m}}), ..., \varphi(a_m^{p^{-n-m}}); b_{-m-n}, ..., b_{-n})$ is stationary.
Denoting by $c_{-n}$ the limit of this sequence, the element $c = (c_i)_{i \in I}$ belongs to $CW(B)$. Put $c = a.b$.
The mapping from $W(A) \times CW(B)$ into $CW(B)$ which associates $c$ with $(a, b)$ endows $CW(B)$ with a structure of $W(A)$-module, extending the structure of $W(A)$-module of $CW^n(A)$.
For every $a \in A$ and every $b \in CW(B)$, one has
$$
\tau(a).b = (c_i)_{i \in I} \quad \text{with} \quad c_i = a^{p^i}b_i \quad \text{for every} \quad i \in I
$$
and
$$
p.b = (b_{i-1}^p)_{i \in I}.
$$
If, for every $b \in CW(B)$, one puts
$$
Fb = (b_i^p)_{i \in I}
$$
$$
Vb = (b_{i-1})_{i \in I},
$$
then F and V are continuous endomorphisms of $CW(B)$ and make it possible to endow $CW(B)$ with a structure of $\mathcal{D}_A$-module. The $\mathcal{D}_A$-module $CW^n(B)$ is a sub-$\mathcal{D}_A$-module of $CW(B)$.
