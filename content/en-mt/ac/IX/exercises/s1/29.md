Let $A$ be a ring. Let $n \in J$, $p$ a prime number, and $a \in A^J$. Establish the relation
$$
\Phi_{pn}(a) = \Phi_n(a^p) + p^w \Phi_m(f_{p^w}(a)), \text{ where } w = w_p(pn) \text{ and } pn = p^w m. \text{ We therefore have}
$$
$$
\Phi_{pn}(a) \equiv \Phi_n(a^p) \mod. p^{w_p(pn)}A, \quad \text{and in particular} \quad \Phi_{pn}(a) \equiv \Phi_n(a)^p \mod. pA .
$$
