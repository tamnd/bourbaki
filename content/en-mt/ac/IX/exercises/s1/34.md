Let $R = \mathbf{Z}[X, Y]$ be the $\mathbf{Z}$-algebra of polynomials in two families of indeterminates $X = (X_n)_{n \in J}$ and $Y = (Y_n)_{n \in J}$. For every prime number $p$, let $\sigma_p$ be the endomorphism of $R$ defined by $\sigma_p(X_n) = X_n^p$ and $\sigma_p(Y_n) = Y_n^p$ for every $n \in J$; one then has $\sigma_p(a) \equiv a^p \mod. pR$ for every $a \in R$. Moreover, $R$ is $\mathbf{Z}$-torsion-free.
a) There exist in $R^J$ elements $S = (S_n)_{n \in J}$, $P = (P_n)_{n \in J}$, $I = (I_n)_{n \in J}$, and, for every $q \in J$, $F_q = (F_{q,n})_{n \in J}$ and $V_q = (V_{q,n})_{n \in J}$, characterized respectively by the equalities
$$
\begin{align*}
\Phi(S) &= \Phi(X) + \Phi(Y), \\
\Phi(P) &= \Phi(X) \cdot \Phi(Y), \\
\Phi(I) &= -\Phi(X), \\
\Phi(F_q) &= f_q(\Phi(X)), \\
\Phi(V_q) &= v_q(\Phi(X)).
\end{align*}
$$

(On embedding R in Q $\otimes$ R, we have, with the notation of Exercise 33,
$$
S_n(X, Y) = \Psi_n(\Phi(X) + \Phi(Y)), \quad P_n(X, Y) = \Psi_n(\Phi(X).\Phi(Y)), \quad I_n(X) = \Psi_n(-\Phi(X)),
$$
$$
F_{q,n}(X) = \Psi_n(f_q(\Phi(X))), \quad \text{and} \quad V_{q,n}(X) = \Psi_n(v_q(\Phi(X))).
$$)
b) For every $n \in J$, assign weight $n$ to $X_n$ and $Y_n$. Then $S_n, P_n$ and $I_n$ depend only on the families $(X_d)_{d \in J_n}$ and $(Y_d)_{d \in J_n}$. Moreover:
$\alpha)$ $S_n$ is isobaric of weight $n$.
$\beta)$ $P_n$ is isobaric of weight $2n$, and isobaric of weight $n$ in each of the families $(X_d)_{d \in J_n}$ and $(Y_d)_{d \in J_n}$.
$\gamma)$ $I_n$ is isobaric of weight $n$.
c) $F_{q,n}$ is isobaric of weight $qn$, and depends only on the family $(X_d)_{d \in J_{qn}}$.
d) One has $V_{q,n}(X) = X_{n/q}$, where by convention $X_{n/q} = 0$ if $n/q \notin J$. (It is enough to verify that with this definition of $V_q$, one has $v_q(\Phi(X)) = \Phi(V_q(X))$.)
