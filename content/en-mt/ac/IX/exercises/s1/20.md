Retain the hypotheses and notation of the preceding exercise.

a) For each extension L of K in $\Omega$, consider the $\mathbf{Z}_p$-module

$$
\mathcal{W}(L) = (W(L)/\mathfrak{p} W(L)) \otimes_{\mathbf{Z}_p} (\mathbf{Q}_p/\mathbf{Z}_p)
$$

and, for each integer $n \geqslant 0$, its submodule

$$
\mathcal{W}_n(L) = (W(L)/\mathfrak{p} W(L)) \otimes_{\mathbf{Z}_p} (p^{-n}\mathbf{Z}_p/\mathbf{Z}_p).
$$

Prove that $\mathcal{W}(L)$ can be identified with the inductive limit of its submodules $\mathcal{W}_n(L)$ with respect to the inclusion mappings

$$
i_n : \mathcal{W}_n(L) \to \mathcal{W}_{n+1}(L).
$$

b) For every integer $n \geqslant 0$, let $\psi_n$ be the mapping of $\mathcal{W}_n(L)$ into $W_n(L)/\mathfrak{p} W_n(L)$ which associates to $x \otimes p^{-n}$ the class of $x$. Prove that $\psi_n$ is an isomorphism and that one has

$$
V_n \circ \psi_n = \psi_{n+1} \circ i_n,
$$

where $V_n$ is the mapping of $W_n(L)/\mathfrak{p} W_n(L)$ into $W_{n+1}(L)/\mathfrak{p} W_{n+1}(L)$ induced by the shift V. Deduce that $\mathcal{W}(L)$ can be identified with the inductive limit of the groups $W_n(L)/\mathfrak{p} W_n(L)$ with respect to the mappings $V_n$.

c) Let $a \in \mathcal{W}(K)$. If $n$ is an integer such that $a$ belongs to $\mathcal{W}_n(K)$, the extension $K(p^{-1}(\psi_n(a)))$ was constructed in Exercise 19, c). This extension does not depend on the choice of the integer $n$; it is denoted by $K(p^{-1}(a))$. If $A$ is a subset of $\mathcal{W}(K)$, we denote by $K(p^{-1}(A))$ the extension generated by the fields $K(p^{-1}(a))$ for $a$ running through $A$. Prove that $K(p^{-1}(A))$ is an abelian extension of $K$. Prove, in this situation, assertions analogous to assertions (i) to (iv) of Exercise 19, c).
