a) There exists a unique sequence of polynomials $(R_n)_{n \in \mathbf{N}}$ in $\mathbf{Z}[X, Y]$ such that, for every integer $n \geqslant 0$, one has

$$
X^{p^n} + Y^{p^n} = \sum_{i=0}^n p^i R_i(X, Y)^{p^{n-i}}.
$$

b) If $p$ is odd, one has, for every $n \in \mathbf{N}$,

$$
R_n(X, -X) = 0.
$$

If $p = 2$, one has

$$
R_1(X, Y) = -XY
$$

and

$$
R_n(X, X) \equiv 0 \mod 2 \quad \text{for } n \geqslant 2.
$$
