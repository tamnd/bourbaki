Let A be a ring of characteristic $p$. For every element $a = (a_n)_{n \in \mathbf{N}}$ of $W(A)$ and every integer $n \in \mathbf{N}$, put $a_+ = (a_{n+1})_{n \in \mathbf{N}}$.
a) We have $a = \tau(a_0) + Va_+$.
b) Let $\alpha : W(A) \to W(A)$ denote the mapping which associates to $a = (a_n)_{n \in \mathbf{N}}$
$$
\alpha(a) = a_+ - \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} \frac{1}{p} \binom{p}{i} \tau(\alpha_0)^{p-i}(Va_+)^i - (p-1)! \gamma_p(Va_+),
$$
where (cf. previous exercise) we have put $\gamma_p(Va_+) = (p^{p-1}/p!) V(a_+^p)$. Prove that, in $W(A)$, one has the equality $Fa = a^p + p \alpha(a)$.
c) Prove that, for every integer $n \geq 1$, $\alpha$ defines, by passing to quotients, a mapping $\alpha_n$ of $W_{n+1}(A)$ into $W_n(A)$.
