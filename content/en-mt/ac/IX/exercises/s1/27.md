Let $r_1, r_2, n$ be integers satisfying
$$
1 \leqslant r_1 \leqslant r_2 \leqslant n .
$$
Let $T_1, ..., T_n$ be indeterminates. By arguments analogous to those of the preceding exercise, give a description of the ring of Witt vectors over the ring
$$
k[T_1, ..., T_{r_1}, T_1^{-1}, ..., T_{r_1}^{-1}, T_{r_1+1}, ..., T_{r_2}] [[T_{r_2+1}, ..., T_n]] .
$$
