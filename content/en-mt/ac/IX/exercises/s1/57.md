Let $m, n$ be two integers $\geq 1$. For every ring A of characteristic $p$, let us denote by $_m W_n(A)$ the kernel of the endomorphism $F^m$ of $W_n(A)$.
a) For $m \geq 2$ and $n \geq 1$, the following diagram is commutative

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
_m W_n(A) & \xrightarrow{V} & _m W_{n+1}(A) \\
| & & | \\
pI & & F \\
m-1 W_n(A) & \xleftarrow{R} & m-1 W_{n+1}(A)
\end{array}
$$

where the mappings V, F are induced by the shift and Frobenius homomorphisms respectively, I is the natural injection and R the natural projection.
b) For $n \geq 1$ and $a = [a_0, ..., a_{n-1}]$ in $W_n(A)$, we denote by $\tilde{a}$ the element $(a_0, ..., a_{n-1}, 0, ...)$ of $W(A)$.

Let $a \in {}_m W_n(A)$, $b \in {}_n W_m(A)$. We then put
$$
\langle a, b \rangle = E(\omega_A(\tilde{a})\ \omega_A(\tilde{b}), 1)
$$
(cf. p. 57, Exercise 43 for the notation E, and p. 54, Exercise 40 for the notation $\omega_A$). Prove that the mapping $(a, b) \mapsto \langle a, b \rangle$ of ${}_m W_n(A) \times {}_n W_m(A)$ into $A^*$ is $\mathbf{Z}$-bilinear.
c) With the notation of $a$, one has
$$
\langle a, Vb \rangle = \langle Fa, b \rangle \quad \text{for} \quad a \in {}_m W_n(A) \quad \text{and} \quad b \in {}_n W_{m-1}(A)
$$
and
$$
\langle Ra, b \rangle = \langle a, Ib \rangle \quad \text{for} \quad a \in {}_m W_n(A) \quad \text{and} \quad b \in {}_{n-1} W_m(A).
$$
