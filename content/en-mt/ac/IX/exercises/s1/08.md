Let $A$ be a ring, and $\xi$ an element of $A$ such that $\sum_{i=0}^{p-1} \xi^i = 0$. Then, in $W(A)$, one has the equation
$$
V_A(1) = \sum_{i=0}^{p-1} \tau_A(\xi^i).
$$
Deduce that if $A$ is of characteristic $p$, one has
$$
V_A \circ F_A(a) = p.a \quad \text{for all } a \in W(A).
$$
