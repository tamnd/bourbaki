Let $A$ be a ring and $T$ an indeterminate. Denote by $\Lambda(A)$ (or $\Lambda_T(A)$) the set $1 + TA[[T]]$ of formal series with coefficients in $A$ whose constant term is equal to 1; this is a subgroup of the multiplicative group of $A[[T]]$. Define
$$
L : \Lambda(A) \to A^J , \quad L(f) = (L_n(f))_{n \in J} ,
$$
by
$$
- T \frac{df}{dT}/f = \sum_{n \in J} L_n(f)(-T)^n .
$$

a) Now prove that one has $L(fg) = L(f) + L(g)$ for any $f, g \in \Lambda(A)$.

b) If $A$ is a $\mathbf{Q}$-algebra, then $L$ is bijective, its inverse $\varepsilon$ being given by
$$
\varepsilon(a) = \exp(- \sum_{n \in J} \frac{a_n}{n} (-T)^n)
$$
for $a \in A^J$.
