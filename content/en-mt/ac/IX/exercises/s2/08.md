Let $C$ be a $p$-ring of infinite length, with residue field $k$. Every automorphism of $k$ extends to an automorphism of $C$. In order that every automorphism of $C$ inducing the identity on $k$ by passing to quotients should be the identity, it is necessary and sufficient that $k$ be perfect.
