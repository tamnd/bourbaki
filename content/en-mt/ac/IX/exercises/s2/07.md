a) Let $R$ be a perfect ring of characteristic $p$, and $R'$ a ring of characteristic $p$. Let $f$ be a homomorphism of $R$ into $R'$. Then the homomorphism $W(f) : W(R) \to W(R')$ is the unique homomorphism making the following diagram commutative

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
W(R) & \xrightarrow{W(f)} & W(R') \\
\downarrow \Phi_0 & & \downarrow \Phi_0 \\
R & \xrightarrow{f} & R' .
\end{array}
$$

(Use the preceding exercise.)
In particular, taking for $f$ the raising to the $p$-th power in $R$, prove that $F_R$ is the unique endomorphism of $W(R)$ such that $\Phi_0 \circ F_R = f \circ \Phi_0$. It is characterized by the equalities

$$
F_R(\tau(x)) = \tau(x^p) \quad \text{for every } x \in R .
$$

b) Let $A$ be a ring separated and complete for the $p$-adic topology. Suppose that multiplication by $p.1_A$ in $A$ is injective and that $A/pA$ is a perfect ring. Then there exists a unique endomorphism $\sigma$ of $A$ such that $\sigma(x) \equiv x^p \mod. pA$ for $x \in A$, and the inverse of the isomorphism $t_\sigma : A \to W(A/pA)$ described in Exercise 14, p. 44 is given by

$$
(a_i)_{i \in \mathbf{N}} \mapsto \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \varphi(a_i)^{p^{-i}} p^i ,
$$

where $\varphi : A/pA \to A$ is the unique mapping which yields the identity of $A/pA$ on passing to the quotient and satisfies $\varphi(x^p) = \varphi(x)^p$ for $x \in R$ (cf. Exercise 6, a)).
