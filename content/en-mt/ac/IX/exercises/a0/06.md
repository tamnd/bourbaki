Let $A$ be a local ring with residue field $k$. Let $I$ be an index set. Now prove that the ring $A\mathbf{[}(X_i)_{i\in I}\mathbf{]}$ (cf. App., Example 2), which is a local ring blowing-up of $A$, has as residue field the purely transcendental extension $k((X_i)_{i\in I})$ of $k$.

Theorem of Chevalley: VIII, p. 101, exerc. 10.
Theorem of Nagata: IX, p. 32.

Witt vector: IX, p. 7.
Finite length Witt vector: IX, p. 12.
