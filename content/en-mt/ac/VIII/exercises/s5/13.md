Let p be a prime number, f an integer > 0. Put L = Q(ζ) where ζ is a primitive p^{f}th root of 1, B = Z[ζ], the subring of L generated by ζ. Now prove that B is the integral closure of Z in L. (By a discriminant calculation, one will show that B\left[\frac{1}{p}\right] is the integral closure of Z\left[\frac{1}{p}\right] (V, § 1, No. 6, Lemma 3); denoting by \overline{B} the integral closure of B, one will deduce that \overline{B} ⊂ \bigcap_{q} Z_q[ζ] where q runs through the prime numbers, whence \overline{B} ⊂ Z[ζ].)
