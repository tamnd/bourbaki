Let k be a field, K an extension of finite type of k, n a positive integer, A and B two formally smooth k-algebras, of dimension n, such that the extensions $\kappa_A$ and $\kappa_B$ of k are isomorphic to K. We propose to show that A and B are isomorphic k-algebras.
a) Reduce to the case where K is a finite root extension of k (exercise 29, a)).
b) Consider the completed algebra $C = A \otimes_k B$ of $A \otimes_k B$ in relation to the ideal
$$
(A \otimes_k m_B) + (m_A \otimes_k B)
$$
Show that C is a local, complete and regular Noetherian k-algebra. (For this last point, we will use the canonical homomorphism br $\rho$ from A into C. We will show that it makes C a flat A-module and that $C \otimes_A \kappa_A$ is isomorphic to $\kappa_A \otimes_k B$. We will then use exerc. 14, p. 96.)
c) Show that $\rho$ induces an isomorphism on the residual bodies and an injection of $m_A / m_A^2$ into $m_C / m_C^2$. Then construct a retraction $\pi$ of $\rho$ which is a homomorphism br of k-algebras. Show that $\pi \circ \rho' : B \to A$ is an isomorphism of k-algebras. (We note $\rho'$ the canonical homomorphism br of B in C.)
