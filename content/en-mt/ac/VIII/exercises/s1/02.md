Let A be a ring and Specmax(A) the set of maximal ideals of A. For Specmax(A) to be very dense in Spec(A), it is necessary and sufficient that A be a Jacobson ring (V, § 3, No. 4). One then has $\dim \operatorname{Specmax}(A) = \dim(A)$.
