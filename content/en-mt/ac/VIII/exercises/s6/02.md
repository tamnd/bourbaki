Let $A$ be a ring, and $\mathcal{F} = (\mathcal{F}_i)_{i \in \mathbf{Z}}$ a decreasing filtration of A such that $\mathcal{F}_i = A$ for $i \leq 0$. Let $\mathcal{A}$ be the subring $\bigoplus_{i \in \mathbf{Z}} \mathcal{F}_i . v^{-i}$ of $A[v, v^{-1}]$ and let $\mathrm{gr}_{\mathcal{F}}(A)$ be the graded ring associated with the filtered ring (A, $\mathcal{F}$).
(a) Prove that the homomorphism $f : \mathcal{A} \to \mathrm{gr}_{\mathcal{F}}(A)$ defined by $f(\sum_i a_i v^{-i}) = \sum_i \overline{a}_i$, where $\overline{a}_i$ is the class of $a_i$ in $\mathcal{F}_i / \mathcal{F}_{i+1}$, induces an isomorphism of $\mathcal{A}/v.\mathcal{A}$ with $\mathrm{gr}_{\mathcal{F}}(A)$.
(b) Prove that for every invertible element $v_0$ in A, the homomorphism $e_{v_0} : \mathcal{A} \to A$ defined by
$$
e_{v_0}(\sum a_i v^{-i}) = \sum a_i v_0^{-i}
$$
induces an isomorphism of $\mathcal{A}/(v - v_0) \mathcal{A}$ with A.
(c) Prove that $\mathcal{A}/A[v]$ is a $A[v]$-module of torsion.
(d) Assuming now that A contains a field k, prove that the composed morphism
$$
k[v] \to A[v] \to \mathcal{A} \quad (\text{where } A[v] = \bigoplus_{i \leq 0} \mathcal{F}_i . v^{-i})
$$
makes $\mathcal{A}$ a $k[v]$-module without torsion. Deduce that $\mathcal{A}$ is a $k[v]$-module flat.
(e) Suppose that A is local, with maximal ideal $m$, and contains a field $k$ such that A is direct sum of $k$ and $m$ (as additive group). Prove that there exists an ideal S of $\mathcal{A}$ such that $\mathcal{A}$ is direct sum of $k[v]$ and S (we have set $\mathcal{F}_i = m^i$ for every $i \in \mathbf{Z}$).
