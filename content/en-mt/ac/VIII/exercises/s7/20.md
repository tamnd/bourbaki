Let $A$ be a noetherian ring, $M$ a finitely generated $A$-module, and $q_1, ..., q_k$ ideals of $A$ such that $M/q_i \cdot M$ is of finite length for every i. Suppose that $M \neq 0$ and put $d = \dim_A(M)$.
(a) Show that $M/q_1^{n_1} ... q_k^{n_k} \cdot M$ is of finite length for every k-tuple of positive integers $(n_1, ..., n_k)$.
(b) Consider the graded ring H of type $\mathbf{N}^k$ such that
$$ H_{n_1, ..., n_k} = q_1^{n_1} q_2^{n_2} ... q_k^{n_k}/q_1^{n_1+1} q_2^{n_2} ... q_k^{n_k} , $$
and the graded H-module N such that
$$ N_{n_1, ..., n_k} = q_1^{n_1} q_2^{n_2} ... q_k^{n_k} \cdot M/q_1^{n_1+1} q_2^{n_2} ... q_k^{n_k} \cdot M ; $$
show that N is a finitely generated graded H-module generated by $N_{0,...,0}$ and a finite number of elements whose degrees are vectors of the canonical basis of $\mathbf{Z}^k$.
Deduce that there exists a polynomial $Q(T) \in \mathbf{Z}[T_1, ..., T_k]$ and integers $s_1 \geqslant 1, ..., s_k \geqslant 1$ such that one has
$$ \sum_{n \in \mathbf{N}^r} \mathrm{long}_A(H_n) \cdot T^n = \frac{Q(T)}{(1 - T_1)^{s_1} ... (1 - T_k)^{s_k}} . $$
(Proceed by induction on d.)

(c) Deduce, by extending to $Z((T_1, ..., T_k))$ the results of § 6, No. 1, the existence of integers $c(\alpha_1, ..., \alpha_k)$ for the sequences $(\alpha_1, ..., \alpha_k)$ such that $|\alpha| = \sum_{i=1}^k \alpha_i = d$, such that one has

$$
e_{q_1^{v_1} ... q_k^{v_k}}(M) = \sum_{|\alpha|=d} \frac{d!}{\alpha_1! ... \alpha_k!} c(\alpha_1, ..., \alpha_k) v_1^{\alpha_1} ... v_k^{\alpha_k}
$$

for $(v_1, ..., v_k)$ in $\mathbf{N}^k$.

(d) Suppose now that A is local, with infinite residue field $\kappa$. Show, using the existence of superficial elements for the graded H-module N, that one has

$$
c(\alpha_1, ..., \alpha_k) = \inf_{q_\alpha} (e_{q_\alpha}(M))
$$

where $q_\alpha$ runs through the set of ideals of A generated by $\alpha_1$ elements of $q_1$, $\alpha_2$ elements of $q_2$, ..., $\alpha_k$ elements of $q_k$. One sometimes denotes the integer $c(\alpha_1, ..., \alpha_k)$ by $e(q_1^{[\alpha_1]} + \cdots + q_k^{[\alpha_k]}; M)$, which is abbreviated to $e(q_1^{[\alpha_1]} + \cdots + q_k^{[\alpha_k]})$ when $M = A$.

(e) Prove the equality

$$
e_{q_1.q_2}(M) = \sum_{i=1}^d \binom{d}{i} e(q_1^{[i]} + q_2^{[d-i]}; M).
$$

(f) Show that one has :

$$
e(\overline{q_1}^{[\alpha_1]} + \cdots + \overline{q_k}^{[\alpha_k]}; M) = e(q_1^{[\alpha_1]} + \cdots + q_k^{[\alpha_k]}; M)
$$

where $\overline{q_i}$ denotes the integral closure in A of the ideal $q_i$ (cf. exerc. 19).
