Let $k$ be a field. Put $R = k[[X, Y, Z]]$ and consider the local ring $A = R/(XY, XZ)$, of maximal ideal $m$. Now prove that one has

$$
H_A = (1 - T)(1 - T - T^2) \quad H_R = 1 + \sum_{n \geq 1} (n + 2) T^n.
$$

Deduce that one has $\dim(A) = 2, \dim_k(m/m^2) = 3$ and $e_m(A) = 1$. (One has $H_A = P_G$ where $G = \operatorname{gr}_m(A) = k[X, Y, Z]/(XY, XZ)$. We shall prove the existence of an exact sequence of graded $H$-modules

$$
0 \to H(-3) \to H(-2) \oplus H(-2) \to H \to G \to 0
$$

where $H = k[X, Y, Z].$)
