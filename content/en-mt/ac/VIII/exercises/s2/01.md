Let $V$ be a discrete valuation ring, with field of fractions $K$, and let $n$ be an integer. Let $A$ be the subring of the polynomial ring $K[X₁, ..., Xₙ]$ consisting of the polynomials whose constant term belongs to $V$. The canonical homomorphism from $V$ to $A$ satisfies property (PM), and the canonical homomorphism $V/mᵥ → A/mᵥA$ is an isomorphism. Moreover, we have $\dim(A) ≥ n + 1$ and $\dim(V) = 1$. As soon as $n ≥ 1$, we therefore have $\dim(A) > \dim(V) + \dim(A/mᵥA)$. Now prove that $A$ is not noetherian. Using formal series, construct an analogous example in which $A$ is local and non-noetherian.
