Let $A$ be a ring which is an integral domain, with field of fractions $K$, and let $m \in \mathbf{N}$. Now prove that one has
$$
\dim(A[X_1, ..., X_m]) = m + \sup \left\{ \dim(A[t_1, ..., t_m]) | t_i \in K, 1 \leq i \leq m \right\}.
$$
(One will use Exercise 10). More generally if $F$ is a field, extension of $K$, prove that one has
$$
\dim(A[X_1, ..., X_m]) = \sup \left\{ \dim(A[t_1, ..., t_m]) | t_i \in F, 1 \leq i \leq m \right\} + \sup(0, m - \deg. \operatorname{tr}_K(F)).
$$
