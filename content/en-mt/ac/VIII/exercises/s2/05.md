Let $A$ be an integrally closed ring with field of fractions $K$. Given a prime ideal $p$ of $A$, and an element $x$ of $K$ such that $x ∉ A_p$, and $x⁻¹ ∉ A_p$, the ideal $pA[x]$ of $A[x]$ is prime; one has: $pA[x] ∩ A = p$ and the canonical homomorphism $(A/p)[X] → A[x]/pA[x]$ is an isomorphism.
