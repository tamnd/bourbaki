Let $A$ be a ring, $M$ a finitely generated projective a-module. In order that a submodule $M'$ of $M$, of finite presentation, be a direct factor of $M$, it is necessary and sufficient that it be a pure submodule of $M$ (use Th. 1, as well as § 3, Exer. 15).
