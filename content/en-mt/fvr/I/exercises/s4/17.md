Let $f$ and $g$ be two positive convex functions in an interval $I = (a, b)$; suppose that there exists a number $c \in I$ such that, in each of the intervals $(a, c)$ and $(c, b)$, $f$ and $g$ vary in the same direction. Now prove that the product $fg$ is convex in $I$.
