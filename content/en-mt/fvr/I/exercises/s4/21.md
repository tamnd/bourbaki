Let $f$ be a finite numerical function, upper semicontinuous in an open interval $I \subset \mathbf{R}$. In order that $f$ be strictly convex in $I$, it is necessary and sufficient that there exist no right locally above the graph $G$ of $f$ at a point of $G$.
