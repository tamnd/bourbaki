Let $(u_n)$ be a series with terms $> 0$ from a certain rank on. Prove that if
$$
\limsup_{n \to \infty} \left( \frac{u_{n+1}}{u_n} \right)^n < \frac{1}{e},
$$
the series $(u_n)$ is convergent, and that if $\liminf_{n \to \infty} (u_{n+1}/u_n)^n \geq 1/e$, the series has an infinite sum.
