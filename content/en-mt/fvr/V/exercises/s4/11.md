For every number $a > 0$, prove the formula
$$
(1!)^{-a/n} + (2!)^{-a/n} + \cdots + (n!)^{-a/n} \sim \frac{1}{a \log n}
$$
when $n$ tends to $+\infty$ (compare each term $(p!)^{-a/n}$ with $n^{-a p/n}$).
