The hypotheses being the same as in Exercise 10, now prove that, for every $\varepsilon > 0$, there exists a number $\rho > 0$ such that for every subdivision of $[a, b]$ into intervals $[x_i, x_{i+1}]$ of length $\leq \rho$ ($0 \leq i \leq n - 1$), one has
$$
\left\| \int_a^b [f(t) \cdot g(t)] dt - \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} [f(u_i) \cdot g(v_i)] (x_{i+1} - x_i) \right\| \leq \varepsilon
$$
whatever the points $u_i, v_i$ in $[x_i, x_{i+1}]$ may be, for each index $i$ (reduce to the case where $f$ and $g$ are step functions).
