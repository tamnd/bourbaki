b) Prove that there exists a convex function h in (0, 1) such that $h \geq f$ and that
$$
\int_0^1 h(t)\,dt \leq 2 \int_0^1 f(t)\,dt.
$$

(For every $a$ such that $0 < a \leq 1$, let $f_a$ be the function equal to $f$ for $0 \leq t \leq a$ and to $f(a)$ for $a \leq t \leq 1$; let $A$ be the set of the $a \in ]0, 1[$ for which there exists a convex function $h_a$ in $(0, 1)$ such that $h_a \geq f_a$ and $\int_0^1 h_a(t) \, dt \leq 2 \int_0^1 f_a(t) \, dt$. Prove that the upper bound $b$ of $A$ still belongs to $A$, using exerc. 1 of I, p. 52. Proving subsequently that $f_b = f$ by reasoning by contradiction. For this, one reduces to proving the following result: if $\varphi$ is continuous and increasing in $(0, 1]$, nonconstant and such that $\varphi(0) = 0$, there is a point $c \in ]0, 1[$ such that $\varphi(c) > 0$ and that the linear function $\psi$ such that $\psi(c) = \varphi(c)$ and

$$
\psi(2c - 1) = 0
$$

verifies the relation $\psi(t) \geq \varphi(t)$ for $\max(0, 2c - 1) \leq t \leq c.$

23) Let $\mathbf{f}$ be a continuously differentiable vector function in an interval $(a, b) \subset \mathbf{R}$, with values in a complete normed space.
a) Prove that for $a \leq t \leq b$, one has

$$
f(t) = \frac{1}{b - a} \int_a^b \mathbf{f}(x) \, dx + \int_a^b \frac{x - a}{b - a} \mathbf{f}'(x) \, dx + \int_t^b \frac{x - b}{b - a} \mathbf{f}'(x) \, dx.
$$

b) Deduce the inequalities

$$
\| \mathbf{f}(t) \| \leq \frac{1}{b - a} \int_a^b \| \mathbf{f}(x) \| dx + \int_a^b \| \mathbf{f}'(x) \| dx
$$

and

$$
\| \mathbf{f}(\frac{1}{2}(a + b)) \| \leq \frac{1}{b - a} \int_a^b \| \mathbf{f}(x) \| dx + \frac{1}{2} \int_a^b \| \mathbf{f}'(x) \| dx.
$$
