Let f be a finite numerical function continuous in an open interval I. In order that f be convex in I, it is necessary and sufficient that, for every $x \in I$, one has
$$
\limsup_{h \to \infty} \left( \frac{1}{2h} \int_{x-h}^{x+h} f(t)\,dt - f(x) \right) \geq 0
$$
(reason as in Exercise 9 of I, p. 52).
