Let $G_1, G_2$ be two locally compact groups, $\beta_1, \beta_2$ left Haar measures on $G_1, G_2$ and $A_1$ (resp. $A_2$) the topological algebra (over $\mathbf{R}$) $L^1(G_1, \beta_1)$ (resp. $L^1(G_2, \beta_2)$). Let $T$ be an algebra isomorphism of $A_1$ onto $A_2$, such that the relation $f \geq 0$ almost everywhere is equivalent to $T(f) \geq 0$ almost everywhere.

a) Now prove that $T$ is a topological algebra isomorphism. (First note that if a decreasing sequence $(f_n)$ of elements of $A_1$ tends to $0$ almost everywhere, the same is true of the sequence of the $T(f_n)$; deduce that for every sequence $(f_n)$ bounded above in $A_1$, $T(\sup(f_n)) = \sup(T(f_n))$ and conclude with the aid of Fatou's lemma).

b) Now prove that $T$ extends in one and only one way to an isomorphism of the topological algebra $\mathcal{M}^1(G_1)$ onto the topological algebra $\mathcal{M}^1(G_2)$, and that there exist a topological isomorphism $u$ of $G_1$ onto $G_2$ and a continuous homomorphism $\chi$ of $G_1$ into $\mathbf{R}_+^*$ such that $T(\varepsilon_s) = \chi(s)\varepsilon_{u(s)}$ (use Exercise 15 a) and Exercise 19 b); to prove that $u$ and $\chi$ are continuous, note that $T$ defines an isomorphism of the algebra $\mathcal{L}(A_1)$ of continuous endomorphisms of the topological vector space $A_1$ onto the analogous algebra $\mathcal{L}(A_2)$ and that this isomorphism is bicontinuous for the topology of simple convergence; on the other hand, observe that $s \to \delta(s^{-1})$ is an isomorphism of $G$ onto a multiplicative subgroup of $\mathcal{L}(A_1)$.

c) Deduce from b) that one has, for every $f \in A_1$,

$$
(T(f))(t) = \chi(u^{-1}(t))f(u^{-1}(t))
$$

for every $t \in G_2$.

Recall (Alg., Chapter III, 3rd ed., § 1) that there are finite groups $G_1, G_2$ such that the algebras $L^1(G_1, \beta_1)$ and $L^1(G_2, \beta_2)$ are isomorphic without $G_1$ and $G_2$ being isomorphic.
