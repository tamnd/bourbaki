Let $n\geqslant 1$ be an integer and equip $\mathbf{R}^n$ with the euclidean norm. Let $\mu$ denote Lebesgue measure on $\mathbf{R}^n$.

Let $r >0$. A sphere packing of radius $r$ in $\mathbf{R}^n$ is a set $\mathscr{P}$ of disjoint parts of $\mathbf{R}^n$, such that every $S\in \mathscr{P}$ is a closed ball of radius $r$. One says that $\mathscr{P}$ is periodic if there exists a lattice $\Lambda \subset \mathbf{R}^n$ such that $S +h\in \mathscr{P}$ for every $S\in \mathscr{P}$ and every $h\in \Lambda$.

Let $A_{\mathscr{P}}$ be the union of the elements of $\mathscr{P}$. It is a measurable set. One calls the density of $\mathscr{P}$ the real number

$\Delta (\mathscr{P}) =$ lim sup$_{R\rightarrow+\infty}\frac{\mu(A_{\mathscr{P}}\cap B_R)}{\mu(B_R)}$ where $B_R$ is the closed ball of radius R in $\mathbf{R}^n$. Let $\Delta_n=$ sup$_{\mathscr{P}}\Delta (\mathscr{P})$. (a) One has
