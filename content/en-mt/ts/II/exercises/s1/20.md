Soit $n\geqslant 1$ un entier. We identify $\mathbf{R}^*_+\times \mathbf{S}_n$, and $\mathbf{R}^{n+1}-\{0\}$ by the mapping $(t, z)\mapsto tz$. Let $\omega$ be the unique measure on the sphere $\mathbf{S}_n\subset \mathbf{R}^{n+1}$ invariant under $\mathbf{O}(n+ 1,\mathbf{R})$ such that the measure $t^ndt\otimes \mu$ is identified with Lebesgue measure on $\mathbf{R}^{n+1}-\{0\}($cf. INT, VII, p. 116, exercise 8). Let $\mu$ denote the measure on $\mathbf{R}^{n+1}$ image of the measure $\omega$ by the injection of $\mathbf{S}_n$ into $\mathbf{R}^{n+1}$.

a) One has $\mu(\mathbf{S}_n) = 2\pi^{(n+1)/2}\Gamma (\frac{n+1}{2})^{-1}($cf. INT, V, §8, n$^o7)$.

b) For every real number $k\geqslant 0$, define the function $J_k:\mathbf{R}_+\rightarrow \mathbf{C}$ by

$$
J_k(t) =\frac{(t/2)^k}{\Gamma (k+\frac{1}{2})\Gamma (\frac{1}{2})}\int_{-1}^1e^{iut}(1-u^2)^{k-1/2}du
$$

for $t\in \mathbf{R}_+$ (« Bessel function of the first species »). For every $x\in \mathbf{R}^{n+1}$, one has

$$
\mathscr{F}(\mu)(x) =\frac{2\pi}{\|x\|^{(n-1)/2}}J_{(n-1)/2}(2\pi \|x\|)
$$

where $\|x\|$ is the euclidean norm. c) If $k\in \mathbf{N}$, one has

for every $t\in \mathbf{R}_+$.
