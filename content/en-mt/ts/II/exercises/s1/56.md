$k(z) =($ sin($\pi z\pi z$))$^2$

if $z\in \mathbf{C}-\mathbf{Z}$ and $k(z) = 1$ if $z\in \mathbf{Z}$. The function $k$ belongs to the space $\mathscr{E}_{1/2}$ (exercise 36).

$\sum\frac{1}{(z-n)^2}=($sin($\pi \pi z$))$^2$

$n\in \mathbf{Z}$

(apply the Poisson formula).

$h(z) =($sin($\pi \pi z$))$^2\{(\sum_{n\in\mathbf{Z}}(z$s($-nn$))$^2+\frac{2}{z}\}$,
