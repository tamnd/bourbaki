On retains the notations of the preceding exercise. Let M, $N\geqslant 1$ be integers and let $(a_n)_{M<n\leqslant M+N}$ be a family of complex numbers. Let $\delta  >0$ be a real number, $R\geqslant 1$ an integer and $(\xi_r)_{1\leqslant r\leqslant R}$ a family of real numbers such that inf$_{h\in\mathbf{Z}}|\xi_r-\xi_{r'}-h|\geqslant \delta$ for all $r\not=r'$.

For every $x\in \mathbf{R}$, put

$$
S(x) =\sum_{n=M+1}^{M+N}a_ne^{2i\pi nx}
$$

a) One has

$$
\sum_{r=1}^R|S(\xi_r)|^2\leqslant (N +\delta^{-1}-1)\sum_{n=M+1}^{M+N}|a_n|^2
$$

("large sieve inequality").

(Consider the function $\tau :z\mapsto \sigma (\delta z)$, where $\sigma$ is the function of questions f) and g) of the preceding exercise for the interval $[\alpha , \beta ] = [\delta (M + 1), \delta (M + N)]$; put

$$
S^*(x) =\sum_{n=M+1}^{M+N}a(n)\tau (n)^{-1}e^{2i\pi nx}
$$

and observe that

$$
S(x) =\int_{-\delta /2}^{\delta /2}\widehat{\tau}(t)S^*(t+x)dt
$$

Put $x=\xi_r$, apply the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality and sum over $r$.)

b) The constant $N +\delta^{-1}-1$ is optimal.
