Let $\nu \in \mathscr{P}(\mathbf{R})$ be a probability measure on $\mathbf{R}$. The characteristic function of $\nu$ is the function $\varphi_{\nu}$ from $\mathbf{R}$ into $\mathbf{C}$ such that

$$
\varphi_{\nu}(x) =\int_{\mathbf{R}}e^{itx}d\nu (t)
$$

for every $x\in \mathbf{R}$.

a) For $\nu_1,\nu_2$ in $\mathscr{P}(\mathbf{R})$, one has $\nu_1=\nu_2$ if and only if $\varphi_{\nu_1}=\varphi_{\nu_2}$.

b) For $\nu_1,\nu_2$ in $\mathscr{P}(\mathbf{R})$, one has $\nu_1*\nu_2\in \mathscr{P}(\mathbf{R})$ and $\varphi_{\nu_1*\nu_2}=\varphi_{\nu_1}\varphi_{\nu_2}$.

c) Let $\nu \in \mathscr{P}(\mathbf{R})$. The function $\varphi_{\nu}$ is bounded by 1 and uniformly continuous on $\mathbf{R}$.

e) Let $(\nu_n)_{n\in\mathbf{N}}$ be a sequence of probability measures on $\mathbf{R}$ converging narrowly (INT, IX, p. 59, déf. 1) to a measure $\nu$; then $\nu \in \mathscr{P}(\mathbf{R})$ and $\varphi_{\nu_n}(x)$ converges to $\varphi_{\nu}(x)$ for every $x\in \mathbf{R}$.

Let $(\nu_n)_{n\in\mathbf{N}}$ be a sequence of probability measures on $\mathbf{R}$. Suppose that for every $x\in \mathbf{R}$, the sequence $(\varphi_{\nu_n}(x))_n$ converges to a complex number $\varphi (x)$, and that the function $x\mapsto \varphi (x)$ is continuous at 0.

f) Let $\varepsilon  >0$. There exists $a >0$ such that

$$
\nu_n([-a, a])\geqslant 1-\varepsilon
$$

for every integer $n\in \mathbf{N}$.

g) The sequence $(\nu_n)$ is relatively compact for the narrow topology.
