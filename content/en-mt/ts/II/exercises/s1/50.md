Let $k$ be a finite field of characteristic $p\not= 2$ and of cardinal $q$. We equip $k$ with the counting measure and denote by $\widehat{f}$ the Fourier cotransformation of a function $f:k\rightarrow \mathbf{C}$. We equip $k^*$ with the counting measure and fix a nontrivial character $\psi$ of $k$. We denote by $\lambda$ the unique character of order 2 of $k^*$, extended to $k$ by setting $\lambda (0) = 0$.

For all $a\in k^*$, we set

$$
S(a) =\sum_{x\in k^*}\lambda (x)\psi (ax+x^{-1})
$$

(“Salié sums”). We denote by $\mathscr{F}(S)$ the Fourier transform of the function S on $k^*$.

a) Let $\chi \in \widehat{k}^*$ and set $\eta =\chi^2$. One has

$$
\mathscr{F}(S)(\chi ) =\overline{\widehat{\chi}}(\psi )\widehat{\chi \lambda}(\psi ) =\chi (4)\widehat{\lambda}(\psi )\overline{\widehat{\eta}}(\psi )
$$

(use the Hasse-Davenport formula).
