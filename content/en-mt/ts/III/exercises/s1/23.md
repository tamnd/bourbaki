Let $B$ be an infinite-dimensional Banach space. We denote by E the strong dual of B and by F the dual of B endowed with the topology $\sigma (B',B)$. Prove that the identity mapping of the dual of B is compact from E into F, but that its transpose is not so from $F'_b$ into $E'_b$.
