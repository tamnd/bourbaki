Let $I = [a, b]$ be a compact interval of $\mathbf{R}$. For every continuous function $f: I\rightarrow \mathbf{C}$ and every $y\in I$, put

$$
V(f)(y) =\int_a^yf(x)dx
$$

Now prove that the linear mapping V from $\mathscr{C}(I)$ into $\mathscr{C}(I)$ is compact (« Volterra operator »).
