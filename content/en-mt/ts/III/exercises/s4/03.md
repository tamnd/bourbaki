Let $p\in [1,+\infty ]$ and $E =\ell^p_K(\mathbf{N})$, and let $u$ be the shift endomorphism

$$
(x_0, x_1, . . . ,)\mapsto (0, x_0, x_1, . . .)
$$

of E. Now prove that $u$ is a Fredholm endomorphism of index 1 and that $((u)) = 1$.
