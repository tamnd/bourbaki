Let $E$ be the complex Hilbert space $\mathbf{C}^2$ and let $u\in \mathscr{L}(E)$ be the endomorphism such that $u(ae_1+be_2) =be_1$.

a) Now prove that the spectrum of $u$ consists of 0 alone and that, for every $\lambda \in \mathbf{C}^*$, one has the formula

$$
\|R(u, \lambda )\|^2=\frac{2}{1 + 2|\lambda|^2 -\surd\overline{1 + 4|\lambda|^2}}
$$

Deduce the form of the pseudospectrum PSp$_{\varepsilon}(u)$ for every $\varepsilon  >0$.
