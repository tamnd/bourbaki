Let $E =\ell^2_{\mathbf{C}}(\mathbf{N})$ and let $u$ be the endomorphism of E defined by

$$
u((x_i)_{i\in\mathbf{N}}) = (0, x_1, . . . , x_n, . . .)
$$

for all $(x_i)\in E$.

a) The spectrum of $u$ is the closed unit disk in $\mathbf{C}$.

b) The residual spectrum of $u$ is the open unit disk in $\mathbf{C}$.

c) The essential spectrum of $u$ is the unit circle in $\mathbf{C}$.
