a) Let $u$ and $v$ be positive continuous endomorphisms of E. Suppose that for all $t >0$, one has

$$
2tu^2\circ (u^2+t^21_E)^{-1}\leqslant v\leqslant \frac{1}{2t}(u^2+t^21_E)
$$

Prove that $u=v$.

b) For $u$ to be a normal endomorphism, it is necessary and sufficient that $u$ and $u^*$ be paranormal and have the same kernel.
