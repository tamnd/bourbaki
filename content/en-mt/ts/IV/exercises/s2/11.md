Let $u$ be a continuous endomorphism of E.

a) For $u$ to be paranormal, it is necessary and sufficient that

$$
(u^*)^2\circ u^2-2t(u^*\circ u) +t^21_E\geqslant 0
$$

for every real number $t >0$.

b) For $u$ to be paranormal, it is necessary and sufficient that

$$
p\circ q^2\circ p-2tp^2+t1_E\geqslant 0
$$

for every $t >0$, where $p= (u\circ u^*)^{1/2}$ and $q= (u^*\circ u)^{1/2}$.
