Let U be an open subset of $\mathbf{R}$. A universally measurable function $f: U\rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ is called a Loewner function if, for every complex Hilbert space E and for all Hermitian endomorphisms $u$ and $v$ of E such that Sp($u$)$\subset U$ and Sp($v$)$\subset U$, the condition $u\leqslant v$ in $\mathscr{L}(E)$ implies $f(u)\leqslant f(v)$. We denote by Loe(U) the set of Loewner functions on U. a) Let $f\in$ Loe(U). The function $f$ is increasing on U. The constant function 1 and the identity mapping of U belong to Loe(U).

d) The set Loe($\mathbf{R}$) is the set of functions of the form $f(x) =ax+b$ where $(a, b)\in \mathbf{R}_+\times \mathbf{R}$. (Apply the preceding question to $f$ and also to the function $x\mapsto  -f(-x).$)

e) Let $t\in \mathbf{R}_+^*$. The function $f:x\mapsto  -(t+x)^{-1}$ belongs to Loe($\mathbf{R}_+^*$).

f ) For every bounded positive measure $\nu$ on $\mathbf{R}^*_+$ such that the function $x\mapsto x^{-1}$ is $\nu$-integrable, the function $f:\mathbf{R}^*_+\rightarrow \mathbf{R}$ defined by
