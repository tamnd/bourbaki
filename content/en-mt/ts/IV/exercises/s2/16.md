e) A family $(G_j)_{j\in J}$ of finite graphs $G_j= (S_j,F_j, o_j, t_j, i_j)$ is said to be a family of expander graphs if the following conditions are satisfied:

(i) One has Card(S$_j$)$\rightarrow +\infty$ with respect to the filter of complements of finite subsets of J;

(ii) There exists $C\geqslant 0$ such that for every $j$ and every $x\in S_j$, the valency of $G_j$ at $x$ is $\leqslant C$;

(iii) There exists $\delta  >0$ such that $h_{G_j}\geqslant \delta$ for every $j\in J$.

Now prove that $(G_j)$ is a family of expander graphs if and only if (i), (ii) are satisfied, as well as the condition

(iii’) There exists $\delta  >0$ such that $\lambda_1(G_j)\geqslant \delta$ for every $j\in J$.
