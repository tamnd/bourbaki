Let $G = (S,F, o, t, \iota )$ be a graph (TA, II, p. 155, déf. 1). Suppose that G is connected, that S is countable and that S has at least two elements. For every $x\in S$, denote by $v(x)$ the number of edges of G of which $x$ is an endpoint; we have $v(x)\geqslant 1$ for every $x\in S$, and suppose that $v(x)$ is finite for every $x\in S$.
