Let G be a group. We say that G has the Powers property if, for every $n\in \mathbf{N}$ and every finite subset F of G$-\{e\}$, there exists a partition of G into two subsets A and B and a family $(g_i)_{1\leqslant i\leqslant n}$ of elements of G such that

(i) for every $g\in F$, the sets A and $gA$ are disjoint ;

(ii) for all distinct integers $j$ and $k$ between 1 and $n$, the sets $g_jB$ and $g_kB$ are disjoint.
