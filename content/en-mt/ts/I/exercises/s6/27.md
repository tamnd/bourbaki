Let A be a unital star algebra, $a,b$ and $c$ elements of A. Suppose that $b$ and $c$ are normal. If $ac=cb$, then one has $f(a)c=cf(b)$ for every function $f$ continuous on the union of the spectrum of $a$ and the spectrum of $b$.
