Let $A$ be a normed algebra and $x\in A$. We say that $x$ is topologically nilpotent if $x^n$ tends to 0 when $n$ tends to $+\infty$. Now prove that $x$ is topologically nilpotent if, and only if, $\varrho (x)<1$.
