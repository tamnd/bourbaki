Let $A$ be a normed algebra. For every $x\in A$, set:

$\lambda (x) =$ inf$_{y\not=0}\frac{\|xy\|}{\|y\|}\lambda '(x) =$ inf$_{y\not=0}\frac{\|yx\|}{\|y\|}$.

a) One has

$$
|\lambda (x)-\lambda (y)|\leqslant \|x-y\|,|\lambda '(x)-\lambda '(y)|\leqslant \|x-y\|
$$

$$
\lambda (x)\lambda (y)\leqslant \lambda (xy)\leqslant \|x\|\lambda (y),\lambda '(x)\lambda '(y)\leqslant \lambda '(xy)\leqslant \lambda '(x)\|y\|
$$

b) The set of topological zero divisors on the left (resp right) in $A$ is closed.
