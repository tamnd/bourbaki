a) The group G acts continuously on the left on $\mathbf{H}$ by

$$
(a bc d)\cdot z=\frac{az + b}{cz + d}
$$

The measure $\nu =y^{-2}\cdot \mu$ is G-invariant.

b) Let $n$ be an integer such that $n\geqslant 2$. Let $E_n$ denote the vector space of holomorphic functions $f:\mathbf{H}\rightarrow \mathbf{C}$ such that the function $z\mapsto  |f(z)|^2\mathscr{I}(z)^n$ belongs to $\mathscr{L}^2(\mathbf{H}, \nu )$. The space $E_n$ endowed with the sesquilinear form

$$
\langle f|g\rangle =\int_{\mathbf{H}}\overline{f(z)}g(z)\mathscr{I}(z)^nd\nu (z)
$$

is a Hilbert space of countable type and of infinite dimension.

c) For every $g\in G$ and every $f\in E_n$, put

$$
\pi_n(g)f(z) =j(g^{-1}, z)^{-n}f(g^{-1}\cdot z)
$$

The mapping $\pi_n$ defines a unitary representation of G in $E_n$.
