Suppose that G is a unimodular locally compact group. Let Γ be a discrete subgroup of G such that the quotient space $X = \Gamma \backslash G$ is compact. Let $\mu$ be a Haar measure on G and $\beta$ the counting measure on Γ; write $\widetilde{\mu}=\mu/\beta$.

Let $\varrho$ be the unitary representation of G on $L^2(X,\widetilde{\mu})$ defined in example 3 of V, p. 404.

For $\gamma \in \Gamma$, write $\Gamma_{\gamma}$ (resp. $G_{\gamma}$) for the centralizer of $\gamma$ in Γ (resp. G). a) The group $G_{\gamma}$ is unimodular.

b) Let $\varphi \in \mathscr{K}(G)$. For every Γ-automorphic unitary representation $\pi$, the endomorphism $\pi (\varphi )$ is of finite trace.

c) One has

Tr($\varrho (\varphi )$) $=\sum_{\pi\in\widehat{G}}m_{\pi}(\varrho )$ Tr($\pi (\varphi )$)

where $m_{\pi}(\varrho )$ is the multiplicity of $\pi$ in $\varrho$ ("spectral side of the trace formula").
