Let G be a compact group endowed with its normalized Haar measure $\mu$ and let $f\in \mathscr{C}(G)$ be a continuous function on G. There exists an irreducible representation $\pi \in \widehat{G}$ such that $f=$ dim($\pi$ )$^{-1}\chi_{\pi}$ if and only if

$$
\int_Gf(xgx^{-1}h)d\mu(x) =f(g)f(h)
$$

for all $(g, h)\in G^2$.
