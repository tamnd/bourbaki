Let $E$ be a real or complex normed space, having the following property: if $x, y, x', y'$ are four vectors of $E$ such that

$$
\|x\| = \|x'\|,\quad \|y\| = \|y'\|,\quad \|x + y\| = \|x' + y'\|,
$$

then we have $\|x - y\| = \|x' - y'\|$. Prove that the norm on $E$ is prehilbertian (use Exercise 7).
