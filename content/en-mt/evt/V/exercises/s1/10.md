Let $E$ be a real Hilbert space, $B$ a bilinear form on $E \times E$, $c_1, c_2$ two numbers $> 0$ such that

$$
\begin{align*}
|B(x, y)| &\leq c_1 \|x\| \cdot \|y\| \quad \text{for all } x, y \text{ in } E; \\
|B(x, x)| &\geq c_2 \|x\|^2 \quad \text{for all } x \in E.
\end{align*}
$$

Prove that for every continuous linear form $f$ on E, there exists a unique element $x_f \in E$ (resp. $y_f \in E$) such that $f(x) = B(x_f, x)$ (resp. $f(x) = B(x, y_f)$) for all $x \in E$.
