In the projective space $P_n$ let $C_{n,p}$ be the “second degree cone” defined by the equation
$$
x_1^2 + x_2^2 + \cdots + x_p^2 - x_{p+1}^2 - \cdots - x_n^2 = 0 \quad (1 \leq p \leq n-1).
$$
Now prove that the complement of $\{0\}$ in $C_{n,p}$ is homeomorphic to $\mathbf{R} \times H_{n-1,p}$ (with the notation of Exerc. 10).
