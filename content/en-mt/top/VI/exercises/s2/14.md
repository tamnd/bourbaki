Let $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ be a finite number of points in a Euclidean space $\mathbf{R}^m$. Put

$$
s = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=1}^n x_k, \qquad r^2 = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{k=1}^n \|x_k\|^2 \quad (r \geqslant 0)
$$

and one has $\|s\| \leqslant r$.

a) Now prove that for every index k, one has

$$
\|x_k - s\| \leqslant ((n-1)(r^2 - \|s\|^2))^{1/2}
$$

(observe that one has $\sum_{k=1}^n \|x_k - s\|^2 = n(r^2 - \|s\|^2)$ and order the $\|x_k - s\|$ in increasing order).

b) Now prove that one has $\sum_{j < i} \|x_i - x_j\|^2 = n \sum_{k=1}^n \|x_k - s\|^2$. Deduce that one has

$$
\sup_{i < j} \|x_i - x_j\| \leqslant (2n(r^2 - \|s\|^2))^{1/2}.
$$

(Number the $x_k$ so that the greatest value M of the $\|x_i - x_j\|$ is $\|x_{n-1} - x_n\|$. Put $t_k = (2x_k - x_{n-1} - x_n)/M$, and calculate the sum

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n-2} \|t_k - t_{n-1}\|^2 + \sum_{k=1}^{n-1} \|t_k - t_n\|^2
$$

by observing that $t_{n-1} + t_n = 0$.)
