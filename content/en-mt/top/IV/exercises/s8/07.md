Soit $(\varepsilon_n)_{n \geq 0}$ a sequence of numbers equal to $1$ or to $-1$; now prove that the real number
$$
x_n = \varepsilon_0 \sqrt{2 + \varepsilon_1 \sqrt{2 + \varepsilon_2 \sqrt{2 + \cdots + \varepsilon_n \sqrt{2}}}}
$$
is defined, and equal to
$$
2 \sin \left( \frac{\pi}{4} \sum_{k=0}^n \frac{\varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_1 \ldots \varepsilon_k}{2^k} \right).
$$

Deduce that $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n$ exists and that for every real number $x$ such that $-2 \leq x \leq 2$, there exists a sequence $(\varepsilon_n)$ such that $x$ is equal to the limit of the corresponding sequence $(x_n)$; for which values of $x$ is the sequence $(\varepsilon_n)$ unique? For which values of $x$ is it periodic (see IV, p. 62, exerc. 3)?*
