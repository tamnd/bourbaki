Let $X$ be a topological space, $(G_α)_{α ∈ A}$ a family of equivalence graphs in $X$, whose index set is increasing filtered preordered. Let $R_α$ be the relation $(x, y) ∈ G_α$ and let $φ_α$ be the canonical mapping $X → X/R_α$. We suppose that for $α ≤ β$, the relation $R_β$ implies $R_α$; let then $φ_{αβ}$ be the canonical mapping $X/R_β → X/R_α$; $(X/R_α, φ_{αβ})$ is a projective system of topological spaces; let $Y = \lim (X/R_α)$.

(a) Let $g : X → Y$ be the continuous mapping $\lim φ_α$. Now prove that $g(X)$ is everywhere dense in $Y$, and that for every $x ∈ X$, $g^{-1}(g(x))$ is the class of $x$ for the equivalence relation whose graph is the intersection of the $G_α$.

(b) We suppose that the equivalence relations $R_α$ are closed, and that for every $x ∈ X$ and every neighbourhood $V$ of $x$, there exists an index $α$ such that the class of $x$ for $R_α$ is contained in $V$. Now prove that under these conditions $g$ is an open mapping of $X$ onto $g(X)$.

(c) If we suppose that the classes for the relations $R_α$ are closed quasi-compact sets, then $g$ is surjective.
