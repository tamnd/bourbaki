Let X be a topological space, $\mathcal{F}(X)$ the set of non-empty closed parts of X, $\mathcal{R}(X)$ the set of non-empty quasi-compact parts of X; these sets are equipped with the topology induced by the topology $\mathcal{T}_\Omega$ on $\mathfrak{P}_0(X)$ (I, p. 91, exer. 7).

a) Prove that if $\mathcal{B} \subset \mathcal{F}(X)$ is quasi-compact for $\mathcal{T}_\Omega$ and if X is regular, the union in X of the sets $M \in \mathcal{B}$ is closed.

b) Prove that if $\mathcal{B} \subset \mathcal{R}(X)$ is quasi-compact for $\mathcal{T}_\Omega$, the union in X of the sets $M \in \mathcal{B}$ is quasi-compact.
