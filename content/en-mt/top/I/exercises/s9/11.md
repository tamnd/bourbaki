Let $X$ be a paracompact space. Now prove that if every open subspace of $X$ is paracompact, then the same is true of every subspace of $X$. Deduce that every subspace of a locally compact space whose topology has a countable basis is paracompact (cf. IX, § 2, Exer. 23 a)).
