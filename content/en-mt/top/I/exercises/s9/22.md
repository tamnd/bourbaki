(a) Let $X$ be a separated space, and let $\mathcal{T}$ be its topology. Show that for $X$ to be absolutely closed (Exercise 19), it is necessary and sufficient that the semi-regular topology $\mathcal{T}^*$ associated with $\mathcal{T}$ (I, p. 103, Exercise 20) be a topology of a minimal space (Exercise 20).

(b) If $\mathcal{T}$ is completely separated (Exercise 21), the same is true of $\mathcal{T}^*$ (use Exercise 20 of I, p. 103). Deduce that a regular absolutely closed space is compact.
