Let $X$ be a topological space having a countable basis of open sets $\mathcal{B}$. If $\mathcal{U}$ is an uncountable set of nonempty open sets of $X$, there exists a set $V \neq \varnothing$ in $\mathcal{B}$ contained in an uncountable infinity of sets of $\mathcal{U}$.
