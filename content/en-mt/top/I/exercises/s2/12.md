Let $f : X \to Y$ be a surjective continuous mapping. Prove that the following two properties are equivalent:
$\alpha)$ for every closed subset $F \neq X$ of $X$, one has $f(F) \neq Y$;
$\beta)$ for every open subset $G$ of $X$, one has $f(G) \subset Y - f(X - G)$.
A continuous mapping with this property is called *irreducible*.
