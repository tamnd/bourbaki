24) Let X be a topological space. A mapping f of X into a topological space Y is said to be almost continuous at a point x_0 ∈ X if, whatever the open neighbourhood V of f(x_0) in Y, the point x_0 is interior to the set D(f(V)) (IX, p. 111, exerc. 3).
