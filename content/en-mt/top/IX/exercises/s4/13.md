. Prove that the product $X \times Y$ is normal. (Let A, B be two closed parts of $X \times Y$ having no point in common; for every $x \in X$, prove that there exists a neighbourhood $U_x$ of x in X and two open sets $V_x, W_x$ in F, such that $\overline{V_x} \cap \overline{W_x} = \varnothing$ and that for every $z \in U_x$, one has $A(z) \subset V_x$ and $B(z) \subset W_x$; to see this one reasons by contradiction.
