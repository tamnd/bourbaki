Let $X$ be a completely regular space. Now prove that every subset of $\beta X$ which is a countable intersection of non-empty open sets meets $X$. Deduce that in order that $\beta X$ be meagre in itself (resp. a Baire space), it is necessary and sufficient that $X$ be meagre in itself (resp. a Baire space).
