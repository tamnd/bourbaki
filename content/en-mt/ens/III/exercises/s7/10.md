. One also says that $E = G / R'$ is the inductive limit of the family $(E_\alpha)$ for the family of mappings $(f_{\beta \alpha})$ and one writes $E = \lim_{\longrightarrow} E_\alpha$; when I is filtered, now prove that this definition coincides with that of III, p. 61. In the general case, one calls the canonical mapping of $E_\alpha$ into E and denotes by $f_\alpha$ the restriction to $E_\alpha$ of the canonical mapping of G into E. Suppose given, for every $\alpha \in I$, a mapping $u_\alpha$ of $E_\alpha$ into a set F such that $u_\beta \circ f_{\beta \alpha} = u_\alpha$ for $\alpha \leq \beta$; now prove that there exists one and only one mapping $u$ of E into F such that $u = u_\alpha \circ f_\alpha$ for every $\alpha \in I$.
