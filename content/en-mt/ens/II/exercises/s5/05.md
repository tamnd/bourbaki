Let $(X_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n}$ be a finite family of sets. For every subset $H$ of the set of indices $\{1, n\}$, let $P_H = \bigcup_{i \in H} X_i$ and $Q_H = \bigcap_{i \in H} X_i$. Let $\mathcal{F}_k$ be the set of subsets of $\{1, n\}$ having $k$ elements; now prove that one has:

$$
\bigcup_{H \in \mathcal{F}_k} Q_H \supset \bigcap_{H \in \mathcal{F}_k} P_H \quad \text{si} \quad k \leq \frac{1}{2}(n + 1)
$$

and

$$
\bigcup_{H \in \mathcal{F}_k} Q_H \subset \bigcap_{H \in \mathcal{F}_k} P_H \quad \text{si} \quad k \geq \frac{1}{2}(n + 1) \ .
$$
