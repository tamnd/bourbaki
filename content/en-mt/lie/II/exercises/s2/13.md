(In this exercise, it is assumed that every projective module over K is free; this is for example the case if K is a principal ideal domain.)

Let $l = \sum_{n=1}^\infty l_n$ be a graded Lie algebra admitting a basic family B formed of homogeneous elements, and let $h$ be a graded Lie subalgebra of $l$, which is a direct factor in $l$ as a module.
(a) For every $i \geq 0$, let $l^{(i)}$ be the graded subalgebra of $l$ such that
$$
l_j^{(i)} = h_j \quad \text{si} \quad j \leq i.
$$
$$
l_j^{(i)} = l_j \quad \text{si} \quad j > i.
$$
We have $l = l^{(0)} \supset l^{(1)} \supset \ldots \supset h$. Now prove, by reasoning by induction on $i$, the existence of a basic family $B^{(i)}$ of $l^{(i)}$, formed of homogeneous elements, and such that the elements of $B^{(i-1)}$ and of $B^{(i)}$ of degree $< i - 1$ are the same. (Suppose that $B^{(i-1)}$ has been constructed.
