Let Y and Z be two disjoint sets (Y being endowed with a total order structure), let $X = Y \cup Z$ and let $l = L(X)$. Let $\alpha_Y$ be the submodule of $l$ generated by Z and $[l, l]$; it is an ideal of the Lie algebra $l$. We shall make explicit a basic family of $\alpha_Y$.

Let M be the set of positive integer-valued functions on Y, with finite support. If $m \in M$, we denote by $\theta^m$ the endomorphism of $l$ given by
$$
\theta^m = \prod_{y \in Y} (\operatorname{ad} y)^{m(y)},
$$
the product being relative to the order relation given on Y. We denote by $S$ the subset of $Y \times M$ formed by the pairs $(u, m)$ such that there exists $y \in Y$ for which $y > u$ and $m(y) \geq 1$. Prove that the elements
$$
\theta^m(u) \text{ for } (u, m) \in S \quad \text{ and } \quad \theta^m(z) \text{ for } (z, m) \in Z \times M
$$
form a basic family of the Lie algebra $\alpha_Y$.

(Reduce to the case where Y is finite, and reason by induction on Card(Y). Use the cor. of prop. 10, applied to the greatest element $y$ of Y, and apply the induction hypothesis to $Y - \{y\}$.)
