令$K$为特征$\neq 2$的交换域和两个不定式中形式幂级数的$A$环$K[[X, Y]]$。
(a) 证明在 $A$ 中主理想 $p = (X^2 - Y^3)$ 是素数。 (If 乘积 $f(X, Y)g(X, Y)$ of two formal 幂级数 is divisible by $X^2 - Y^3$, 注 first that $f(T^3, T^2) = 0$ or $g(T^3, T^2) = 0$ in 环 of formal 幂级数 $K[[T]]$; assuming for example $f(T^3, T^2) = 0$, show first that $f(X, Y^2)$ is divisible by $X - Y^3$ and by $X + Y^3$ and, by considering $f(-Y^3 + X, Y^2)$, prove finally that $f(X, Y^2)$ is divisible by $X^2 - Y^6$.)
(b) 令$m$为$A$的最大理想$AX + AY$。 Show that $p$ is closed with respect to the $m$-adic 拓扑 on $A$ (with which $A$ is Hausdorff and complete) but that in 环 $\operatorname{gr}(A)$, $\operatorname{gr}(p)$ is not a prime ideal ($p$ being given the filtration由$A$引起）。
