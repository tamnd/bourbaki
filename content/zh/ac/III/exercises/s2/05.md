Let $A$ be a filtered 交换环 with filtration $(a)_m$ such that $a_0 = A$.
(a) For 环 $\operatorname{gr}(A)$ to be generated by a family of 元素 whose 次数 are bounded, it is necessary and sufficient that there exist 整数 $q$ such that, for all $n$, $a_n = a_{n+1} + b_n$ where $b_n$ is 和 of the ideals $a_1^{\alpha_1} a_2^{\alpha_2} \ldots a_q^{\alpha_q}$ for all 系统 of 整数 $\alpha_i \geq 0$ ($1 \leq i \leq q$) such that $\sum_{i=1}^q i \alpha_i = n$. Then $a_n = a_{n+k} + b_n$ for all $k > 0$.

(b) For $\mathrm{gr}(A)$ to be 诺特, it is necessary and sufficient that $A/a_1$ be 诺特, that 条件 of (a) hold and that for $i \leq q$ the $a_i/a_{i+1}$ be finitely generated $(A/a_1)$-modules (use 推论 4到定理 2（共 no. 10）。

(c) Let $K$ be 交换域 and $A$ 多项式环 in two indeterminates $K[X, Y]$. A total ordering is defined on 集合 of monomials $X^m Y^n$ by 设定 $X^m Y^n \leq X^p Y^q$ if $m + n \leq p + q$ or if $m + n = p + q$ and $m \leq p$. Let $(M_k)$ be 序列 of monomials in $X, Y$ thus arranged in ascending 阶 and let $a_n$ be the ideal of $A$ generated by the $M_k$ of 指标 $k \geq n$. Show that (a) is a filtration compatible with 环结构 on $A$; with this filtration $\mathrm{gr}(A)$ admits 除子 of 0 and is not 诺特, although $A$ is 诺特整环 (use the criterion in (b));特别是，$\mathrm{gr}(a_1)$（由$A$在$a_1$上引起的过滤）不是有限生成的$\mathrm{gr}(A)$-模块，尽管$a_n$是有限生成的$A$-模块（cf.§ 1, no. 2，推论到命题 1).
