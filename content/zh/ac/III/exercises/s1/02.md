Let $A$ be a graded 交换环 of 型 $\mathbf{Z}$ such that $A_n = 0$ for $n < 0$. For all $n \geq 0$, let $A_{[n]}$ denote the graded ideal $\bigoplus_{m \geq n} A_m$ of $A$; the $A$-algebra $A^h = \bigoplus_{n \geq 0} A_{[n]}$ is 环 graded by the $A_{[n]} = A_n^h$;对于所有$f \in A, (d > 0)$，让$f^h$表示$A^h$的元素，其分量是零，除了次数$d$，它等于$f$。

(a) Show that, iff $\in A_d$, where $d > 0$, 环 $A_{(f^h)}^h$ is isomorphic to $(A_f)^{\geq}$ (记号 of 习题 1).

(b) 对于$A^h$ 是有限生成的$A$-代数，$A$ 是有限生成的$A_{(f)}$-代数是充分必要的。

(c) In 阶 that $A_{n+1}^h = A_1^h A_n^h$ (resp. $A_n^h = (A_1^h)^n$) for $n \geq n_0$, it is necessary and sufficient that $A_{n+1} = A_1 A_n$ (resp. $A_n = (A_1)^n$) for $n \geq n_0$.
