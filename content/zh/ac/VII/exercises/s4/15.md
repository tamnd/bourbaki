Let $A$ be the polynomial ring $\mathbf{Z}[X, Y, T, U, V, W]$, $L$ the free $A$-module $A^4, (e_i)_{1 \leq i \leq 4}$ its canonical basis and $M$ the submodule of $L$ generated by the four vectors $Xe_1, Ye_2, Te_3 + Ue_4, Ve_3 + We_2$. 表明

$$
\delta_1(L/M)\delta_3(L/M) \not\subset (\delta_2(L/M))^2
$$
（与习题 14 (b) 比较）。
