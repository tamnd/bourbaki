Show that $\gamma_n$ is, to within a sign, a distinguished polynomial (no. 8); deduce that $A_* = A[[T]]/(\gamma_n)$ is identified with 代数 over $A$ of 群

$G_n = \mathbf{Z}/p^n\mathbf{Z}$。 Show that 交 of the principal ideals $(\gamma_n)$ is reduced to 0; deduce that $A[[T]]$ is identified with 逆极限 $\varprojlim A_*.$
