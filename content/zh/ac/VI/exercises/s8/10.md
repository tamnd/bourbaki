Let w be the 2-adic valuation on the 2-adic 域 $\mathbf{Q}_2$; on 域 of rational 函数 $\mathbf{Q}_2(X)$ consider the discrete valuation $v$ extending $w$ and such that
$$
v(a_0 + a_1 X + \cdots + a_n X^n) = \inf_i (w(a_i))
$$
（§ 10, no. 1，引理1）； let K be the completion of $\mathbf{Q}_2(X)$ with respect to this valuation and let its valuation also be denoted by $v$. Let L be 分裂域 of the polynomial $(Y^2 - X)^2 - 2$ in $K[Y]$ and let $v'$ be the unique valuation on L extending $v$.表明 $[L : K] = 8, e(v'/v) = 4, f(v'/v) = 2$； if k (resp. $k'$) is 剩余类域 of $v$ (resp. $v'$), $k'$ is a radicial 扩张 of k of 次数 2; show that there exists no sub-extension E of L such that $[E : K] = 2$ for which 剩余类域 of 限制 of $v'$ to E is equal to $k'$. （必然是$E = K(\sqrt{\alpha})$，其中$\alpha \in K$在K中不会是平方，而是$\alpha \equiv X \pmod{2}$；借助L在$K(\sqrt{2})$上的合适的基来表达，并观察$K(\sqrt{2})$中不存在元素，其平方是与 X 模 2 一致。）
