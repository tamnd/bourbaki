Let $\mathscr{T}$ be a logical 理论 with explicit axioms $A_1$, $A_2$, ..., $A_n$. Then $A_n$ is independent of the other axioms of $\mathscr{T}$ (§ 2, 习题) if and only if 理论 whose signs and 概形 are the same as those of $\mathscr{T}$, and whose explicit axioms are $A_1$, $A_2$, ..., $A_{n-1}$，“不是$A_n$”，并不矛盾。
