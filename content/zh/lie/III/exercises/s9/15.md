(a) 设$x \in \mathfrak{sl}(2, \mathbf{R})$和$\Delta = \det x$。表明
$$
e^x = \mathrm{ch}\, \sqrt{-\Delta}.I + \frac{\mathrm{sh}\, \sqrt{-\Delta}}{\sqrt{-\Delta}}.x \quad \text{if } \Delta < 0
$$
$$
e^x = \cos \sqrt{\Delta}.I + \frac{\sin \sqrt{\Delta}}{\sqrt{\Delta}}.x \qquad \text{if } \Delta > 0.
$$

(b)设$H = \mathbf{SL}(2, \mathbf{R})$。证明$g = \begin{pmatrix} \lambda & 0 \\ 0 & \lambda^{-1} \end{pmatrix} \in H$是$\exp_H$的像当且仅当$\lambda > 0$或$\lambda = -1$。如果$\lambda = 0$，则所有单参数子群包含 $g$ 都相等。
