令$g$为李代数，并且由$g$($n \geq 0,\ \mathscr{D}^{n-1}g \neq \mathscr{D}^n g$)导出代数的$(\mathscr{D}^0 g, \mathscr{D}^1 g, \ldots, \mathscr{D}^n g)$序列。然后$\dim \mathscr{D}^i g / \mathscr{D}^{i+1} g \geq 2^{i-1} + 1$换$1 \leq i \leq n-2$。 （将$\mathscr{D}^n g$、约化商到$g$可解的情况。然后使用$\mathscr{D}g$幂零和习题 7 (c) of § 4这一事实。）
