令$H$为类$c$的幂零群，并让$G$为$H$的自同构的群，它对$H/(H, H)$的作用微不足道。 Show that $G$ is nilpotent of class $\leq c - 1$. (Apply Exercise 3 to the lower central series of $H$ and note that $G$ operates trivially on $\operatorname{gr}(H)$.) Show that, if $H$ is a finite $p$-group ($p$ prime), so is $G$ (same method).
