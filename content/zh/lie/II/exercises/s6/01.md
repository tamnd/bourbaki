证明$\exp(U) \cdot \exp(V) \cdot \exp(-U) = \exp\left( \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{1}{n!} (\mathrm{ad}\, U)^n(V) \right)$。推断出
$$
H(U, H(V, -U)) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{1}{n!} (\mathrm{ad}\, U)^n(V) = (\exp(\mathrm{ad}\, U))(V).
$$
