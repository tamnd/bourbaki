令 K 为 q 元素的有限域 ； for every prime 数 l let N, be 并 of 扩张 of K (contained in 代数闭包 $\Omega$ of K) whose 次数 is 幂 of l. Show that N, is 阿贝尔的扩张 of K whose 伽罗瓦群 is isomorphic to 群 $\mathbf{Z}_l$ of l-adic 整数. Deduce that $\Omega$ is isomorphic to 张量积 $\bigotimes_l N_l$, and hence, that $\mathrm{Gal}(\Omega/K)$ is isomorphic to 乘积 $\prod_l \mathbf{Z}_l$.
