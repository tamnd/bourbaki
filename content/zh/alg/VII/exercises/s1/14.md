The set of prime numbers of the form $4n - 1$ (resp. $6n - 1$) is infinite (same method as Prop. 5 (VII, p. 5)); notice that every prime number except 2 (resp. except 2 and 3) has the form $4n \pm 1$ (resp. $6n \pm 1$).
