Let A be 主理想整环, let τ be 不可约元 of A, and let M be a π-primary 模 over A. Let I be 集合 of ordinals a (集合理论, III, p. 225, Ex. 14) such that the cardinality of 集合 of ordinals < a is at most equal to that of M. 定义子模 M^{(α)} of M for all a ∈ I by 超限归纳 as follows: take M^{(0)} = M;取 M^{(α + 1)} = πM^{(α)}; and if a is 极限 ordinal take M^{(α)} to be 交 of all the M^{(β)} for β < a.

a) Show that there exists a least ordinal τ ∈ I such that M^{(τ + 1)} = M^{(τ)} (the terminal ordinal of M). Show that M^{(τ)} admits a complement in M, and is 直和 of 子模 isomorphic to U_π (Ex. 3).如果 M^{(τ)} = 0，则称 M 减少。
b) 令 M 为简化的 τ 模。 For all x ∈ M, show that there exists 最大的 ordinal γ(x) ≤ τ such that x ∈ M^{(γ(x))} (in particular, γ(0) = τ).概括Ex中的定义。 8，我们说γ(x)是x在M中的高度。证明如果γ(x) < γ(y)，则γ(x + y) = γ(x)，如果γ(x) = γ(y)，则γ(x + y) ≥ γ(x)。 We say that x is proper with respect to 子模 N of M if for all y ∈ N the height γ(x + y) is at most equal to γ(x); show that then γ(x + y) = inf(γ(x), γ(y)), for all y ∈ N.
c) Let a < τ be an ordinal, and let M^{(α)} be 子模 of M^{(α)} 由……组成 of those x such that πx = πy for some y ∈ M^{(α + 1)}.对于那些 y ∈ M^{(α + 1)} 具有性质, 元素 of 形式 u = x − y 形式类 mod M(π) ∩ M^{(α − 1)} in M(τ) ∩ M^{(α)}; if φ is 典范同态 from M(π) ∩ M^{(α)} onto 商 of this 模 by M(π) ∩ M^{(α + 1)}, then show that 映射 ψ which sends x to φ(u) is 同态 with 核 M^{(α − 1)}, and consequently that M^{(α)}/M^{(α + 1)} is isomorphic to (M(π) ∩ M^{(α)})/(M(π) ∩ M^{(α + 1)}).
d) Let N be 子模 of M, and let x ∈ M^{(α)} be 元素 of height α; show that x is proper with respect to N if and only if 相应的元素 u = x − y of M(π) ∩ M^{(α)} (cf. c)) are proper with respect to N. There exists such 元素 x if and only if 像 of N ∩ M^{(α)} under 同态 ψ is distinct from (M(π) ∩ M^{(α)})/(M(π) ∩ M^{(α − 1)}).
