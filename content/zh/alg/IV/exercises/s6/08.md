放

$$
s_k = \sum A_{m_1 \ldots m_n} p_1^{m_1} \cdots p_n^{m_n},
$$

其中$A_{m_1 \ldots m_n}$是有理数数并且和扩展到所有族$(m_1, \ldots, m_n)$使得$\sum i \cdot m_i = k$。证明$\sum A_{m_1 \ldots m_n} = 0$代表$k > 1$，$\sum |A_{m_1 \ldots m_n}| = 1$代表$k \geq 1$。推断在写 $q_j = -p_j$ 时我们有

$$
(-1)^k s_k = \sum |A_{m_1 \ldots m_n}| q_1^{m_1} \cdots q_n^{m_n}.
$$
