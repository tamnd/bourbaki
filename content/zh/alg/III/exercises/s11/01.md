如果 V 是有限生成的射影 A-模，则 End(V) 也是有限生成的射影 $V \otimes_A V^*$（II, § 4, no. 2，命题 2 的推论）。 If every element $u \in \mathrm{End}(V)$ is associated with the A-linear form $v \mapsto \mathrm{Tr}(uv)$ on $\mathrm{End}(V)$, an A-linear bijection is defined of $\mathrm{End}(V)$ onto its dual $(\mathrm{End}(V))^*$. Identifying $\mathrm{End}(V)$ and its dual A-module under this mapping, the A-algebra structure on $\mathrm{End}(V)$ defines by duality (no. 1, Example 3) an A-cogebra structure on $\mathrm{End}(V)$, which is coassociative and has as counit the form $\mathrm{Tr}: V \to A$. 特别是，对于$V = A^n$，余代数结构是在$\mathbf{M}_n(A)$上定义的，其中余积由$c(E_{ij}) = \sum_k E_{kj} \otimes E_{ik}$给出。 $\mathbf{M}_n(A)$ 上的余代数结构和代数结构不定义结构。
