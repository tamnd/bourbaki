令$X_i$ ($1 \leq i \leq m$) 为阶 $n$ 在交换环 A 上的方阵。 证明如果 $m$ 是偶数则 (习题 23)

$$
\operatorname{Tr}[X_1X_2 \ldots X_m] = 0
$$

如果 $m$ 是奇数

$$
\operatorname{Tr}[X_1 X_2 \ldots X_m] = m \cdot \operatorname{Tr}(X_m[X_1 X_2 \ldots X_{m-1}]) .
$$

(If $\lambda$ is the cyclic permutation $(1, 2, \ldots, m)$, $C$ the cyclic subgroup of $S_m$ generated by $\lambda$ and $H$ the subgroup of $S_m$ consisting of the permutations leaving $m$ invariant, note that every permutation in $S_m$ can be written uniquely as $\sigma \tau$ with $\sigma \in H$ and $\tau \in C$; then use the fact that the trace of a product of matrices is invariant under cyclic permutation of the factors.)
