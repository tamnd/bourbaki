Let $S$ be the equivalence relation on the rational line $\mathbf{Q}$ which is obtained by identifying all the points of $\mathbf{Z}$. 证明$S$是闭集，并且如果$U$是$\mathbf{Q}$上等式的关系，则$(\mathbf{Q} \times \mathbf{Q})/(U \times S)$到$\mathbf{Q} \times (\mathbf{Q}/S)$的规范双射不是同胚。
