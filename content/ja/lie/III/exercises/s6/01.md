Let D be a set of elements of $\mathbf{SL}(2, \mathbf{C})$ of the form $
\begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
0 & a^{-1}
\end{pmatrix}
$ where $a > 0$ and $b \in \mathbf{C}$. Show that D is a Lie subgroup of the underlying real Lie group of $\mathbf{SL}(2, \mathbf{C})$ and that the mapping $(u, d) \mapsto ud$ of $\mathbf{SU}(2, \mathbf{C}) \times D$ into $\mathbf{SL}(2, \mathbf{C})$ is an isomorphism of real analytic manifolds. これと§ 3, 練習問題 7 (c) から、$\mathbf{SL}(2, \mathbf{C})$ は単純に接続されていると推測します。
