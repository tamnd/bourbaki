$\mathfrak{h}$ を $\mathfrak{h}^*$ 含む 0 の可換リー代数 $\not= 0, P$ 有限部分集合とします。 Show that there exists a Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ containing $\mathfrak{h}$ as a Cartan subalgebra, and such that the set of weights of $\mathfrak{h}$ in $\mathfrak{g}$ is P. (Construct $\mathfrak{g}$ as the semi-direct product of $\mathfrak{h}$ by the $\mathfrak{h}$-module V which is the direct sum of the 1-dimensional modules corresponding to the elements of P **--** $\{0\}$, cf. Exerc. 7.)

$\mathfrak{h}$ の元 $x$ は、$x$ が P**--** $\{0\}$ のいずれの元とも直交しない場合に限り、$\mathfrak{h}=\mathfrak{g}^0(x)$ となるものです。
