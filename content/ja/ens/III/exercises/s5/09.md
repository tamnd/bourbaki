(a) E を $qn$ 元で集合とします。 Show that the number of partitions of E into $n$ subsets each of $q$ elements is equal to

$$(qn)!/(n!(q!)^n).$$

(b) $\mathrm{E} = [1,\, qn]$ と仮定します。 Show that the number of partitions of E into $n$ subsets of $q$ elements, no one of which is an interval, is equal to

$$\frac{(qn)!}{n!(q!)^n} - \frac{(qn-q+1)!}{1!(n-1)!(q!)^{n-1}} + \frac{(qn-2q+2)!}{2!(n-2)!(q!)^{n-2}} - \cdots + (-1)^n$$

(練習問題 7 および 8 と同じ方法)。
