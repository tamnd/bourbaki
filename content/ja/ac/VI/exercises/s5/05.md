(a) Let K be 体, $v$ a valuation on K and $\Gamma$ 位数群 of $v$. For K to be linearly compact (章 III, § 2, 練習問題 15) with 位相 $\mathscr{T}_v$, it is necessary and sufficient that, for every well-ordered 部分集合 B of $\Gamma$ and every family $(a_\beta)_{\beta \in B}$ of 元 of K such that, for $A < \mu < v$,

$$
v(a_\lambda - a_\mu) < v(a_\mu - a_v),
$$

there exists 元 $a \in K$ such that $v(a - a_\lambda) = v(a_\lambda - a)$ for every ordered 対 of 添字 $A, \mu$ such that $\mu > A$. (*集合理論* の練習問題 4 を使用、

章 111, § 2). If this is so, 環 A of the valuation v is also linearly compact with the discrete 位相.

(b) Show that 体 S(\Gamma, k) = K defined in § 3, 練習問題 2 is linearly compact with $\mathscr{T}_v$. Take \Gamma to be the totally ordered 群 \mathbf{Q} of rational 数 and consider 部分環 K_0 of K からなる of the $x = (x_\alpha)$ such that 集合 of $a \in \mathbf{Q}$ for which $x, \# 0$ is finite or is 集合 of 点 of 真に増加する数列 tending to $+\infty$. Show that K_0 is 体 which is complete with respect to the valuation $v_0$ induced by the canonical valuation v on K and that $v_0$ has the same 値群 and 剰余体 as v but that K_0 is not linearly compact (cf. § 10, 練習問題 2).
