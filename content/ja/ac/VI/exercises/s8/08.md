Let $K'$ be a field, $B$ the ring of a valuation $v$ on $K'$, $G$ a finite group of automorphisms of $K'$, $K$ the subfield of $K'$ consisting of the elements invariant under $G$ and $A = K \cap B$, which is a valuation ring of $K$, corresponding to the valuation $w = v|K$.

B$の共通部分で、$\sigma$は$G$を通過します。 $p(B)$ が $A'$ の共通部分であり、最大理想値 $m(B)$ である場合、$\sigma . p(B) = p(\sigma . B)$ です。 Show that the decomposition group $G^Z(p(B))$ (Chapter V, § 2, no. 2) is the subgroup of $G$ consisting of the $\sigma$ such that $\sigma . B = B$; write $G^Z = G^Z(p(B))$ and denote by $K^Z$ the subfield of $K'$ consisting of the elements invariant under $G^Z$ and by $B^Z$ the ring of the valuation induced on $K^Z$ by $v$, which is equal to $B \cap K^Z$; recall that the residue fields of $B^Z$ and $A$ are the same and that the maximal ideal $m(B^Z)$ is generated by $m(A)B^Z$ (Chapter V, § 2, no. 2, Proposition 4).
