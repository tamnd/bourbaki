$A$ を絶対平坦環 (練習問題 17) とします。
(a) $P$ を射影右加群 $A$ とする。 有限に生成された $P$ のすべての部分加群 $E$ が $P$ の直因子であることを示してください。 (簡約するは$P$が自由で有限に生成される場合です。 Then note that $P/E$ is finitely presented and use Exercises 15.)
(b) Show that every projective right $A$-module $P$ is the direct sum of monogenous submodules isomorphic to monogenous right ideals of $A$. (Use Kaplansky’s Theorem (Algebra, Chapter II, § 2, Exercises 3) to reduce the problem to the case where $P$ is generated by a countable family of elements, then use (a).)
(c) Give an example of an absolutely flat ring $A$ and a non-projective finitely generated $A$-module (consider a quotient of $A$ by an ideal which is not finitely generated; cf. Algebra, Chapter VIII, § 6, Exercise 15(f) and *Commutative* Algebra, Chapter 11, § 4, Exercise 17).
