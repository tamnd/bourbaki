$A$を可換環、$3$を$A$の理想とします。 An $A$-module $M$ is called absolutely *Hausdorff* with respect to $3$ if, for every finitely generated $A$-module $N$, the $A$-module $N \otimes_A M$ is Hausdorff with the $\mathfrak{g}$-adic topology; an absolutely Hausdorff $A$-module is ideally Hausdorff.

(a) For $M$ to be absolutely Hausdorff with respect to $\mathfrak{g}$, it is necessary and sufficient that, for every finitely generated $A$-module $N$ and every submodule $N'$ of $N$, $\operatorname{Im}(N' \otimes_A M)$ be closed in $N \otimes_A M$ with the $3$-adic topology.

(b) $B$ を可換 $A$-代数、$\mathfrak{L}$ を $B$ 含む $\mathfrak{g}B$ のイデアルとする。 $M$ が $\mathfrak{L}$ に関して絶対ハウスドルフ $B$ 加群である場合、$M$ は $3$ に関して絶対ハウスドルフ $A$ 加群です。
