P を、$\mathbf{R}$ 上の E ベクトル空間における頂点 0 を持つ適切な尖った凸円錐とし、p を E および $p(x) < 1$ に対する点 $x \in E$ の V 集合上の準ノルムとします。 M を E のベクトル部分空間とし、f を M の線型形式とします。 There exists a linear form g on E, which extends f and is such that it is $\geq 0$ in P and $|g(x)| \leq p(x)$ for all $x \in E$, if and only if, for all $x \in M \cap (V + P)$, we have $f(x) > -1$. (To see that the condition is sufficient consider a point $x_0 \in M$ such that, $f(x_0) = 1$, the cone Q of vertex 0 generated by $x_0 + V$, and apply the cor. of the prop. 1 (II, p. 21) to the space E carrying the relation of preorder for which $P + Q$ is the cone of elements $\geq 0$.)
