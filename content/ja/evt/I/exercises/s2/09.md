Let X be a Hausdorff topological space, and let V be a vector subspace of finite dimension n of the space $\mathscr{C}(X; \mathbf{R})$.
a) Show that there exist n pair-wise disjoint, open sets $U_i$ ($1 \leq i \leq n$) in X, such that any function $f \in V$ which is identically zero in each of the $U_i$, is identically zero in X (use A, II, § 7.5, cor. 3).
b) Let $x_i \in U_i$ for $1 \leq i \leq n$. a) から、定数 $c > 0$ が存在し、写像 $f \in V$ ごとに、
$$
\sup_{x \in X} |f(x)| \leq c \sum_{i=1}^n |f(x_i)| .
$$
