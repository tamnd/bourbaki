Let $E$ be a topological vector space, which is a Baire space, and $F$ be a topological vector space.
a) Show that, if a subset $H$ of $\mathscr{L}(E; F)$ is bounded for the topology of simple convergence, then $H$ is equicontinuous (for every closed neighbourhood $V$ of 0 in $F$, consider the sets $M_n = \bigcap_{u \in H} u^{-1}(nV)$).
b) Show that, if a subset $H$ of $\mathscr{L}(E, F)$ is not equicontinuous, the set of all $x \in E$ such that $H(x)$ is not bounded in $F$ is the complement of a first category set. このことから、$(H_n)$ が等連続ではない $\mathscr{L}(E; F)$ の部分集合の数列である場合、集合 $H_n(x)$ のどれも $F$ で囲まれないような $x \in E$ が存在することが推測できます (*特異点の凝縮の原理*)。
