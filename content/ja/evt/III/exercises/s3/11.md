Let $T$ be a metrizable topological space, $E$ a topological vector space which is a Baire space, and $M$ a family of mappings from $E \times T$ into a topological vector space $F$, satisfying the following conditions :
1° for all $t_0 \in T$, the set of all mappings $x \mapsto f(x, t_0)$ where $f$ runs through $M$, is an equicontinuous set of linear mappings from $E$ into $F$;
2° for all $x_0 \in E$, the set of all mappings $t \mapsto f(x_0, t)$ from $T$ into $F$, where $f$ runs through $M$, is equicontinuous.
Show that $M$ is equicontinuous. (Given $t_0 \in T$ and a closed balanced neighbourhood $V$ of 0 in F, for every $x \in E$, let $d_x$ be the upper bound of the radii of all open balls with center $t_0$ in T such that, for an arbitrary point $t$ in one of these balls, we have $f(x, t) - f(x, t_0) \in V$ for all $f \in M$. $x \mapsto d_x$ が点 $x_0 \in E$ ごとに上部半連続であることを示します。このために、任意に $x_0$ に近い点に対して $d_x > \alpha > d_{x_0}$ があった場合、F の 0 の近傍 W ごとに、$f(x_0, t) - f(x_0, t_0)$ は $d(t, t_0) \leq \alpha$ と $f \in M$ に対して属するから $V + W$ になることを示してください。 最後にGT、IX, § 5, No. 4を使用します。 2.)
