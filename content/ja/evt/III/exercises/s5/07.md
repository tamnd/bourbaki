E、F、G を、E の有界部分集合の 3 つのトポロジー ベクトル空間、$\mathscr{S}$ 集合、および $E \times F$ から G への個別に連続する双線形写像の H 集合とします。 The following properties are equivalent :
$\alpha)$ For every neighbourhood W of 0 in G and every set $M \in \mathscr{S}$, there exists a neighbourhood V of 0 in F such that $u(M \times V) \subset W$ for all $u \in H$.
$\beta)$ For every set $M \in \mathscr{S}$, the image of $H \times M$ under the mapping $(u, x) \mapsto u(x, .)$ is an equicontinuous subset of $\mathscr{L}(F; G)$.
$\gamma)$ As $u$ runs through H, the set of mappings $y \mapsto u(., y)$ from F into $\mathscr{L}_{\mathscr{S}}(E; G)$ is equicontinuous.
We then say that H is a $\mathscr{S}$-equihypocontinuous set of bilinear mappings (separately continuous) from $E \times F$ into G. 同様に、F の有界部分集合の集合 $\mathfrak{T}$ については、$\mathfrak{T}$ 等次次連続集合と $(\mathscr{S}, \mathfrak{T})$ 等次次連続集合の定義する概念を計算します。
