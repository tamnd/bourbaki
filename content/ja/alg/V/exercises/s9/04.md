Let $K$ be a field, $f$ an irreducible polynomial in $K[X]$, separable over $K$ and of degree $n$ and let $\alpha_i \ (1 \leq i \leq n)$ be its roots in an algebraic closure $\Omega$ of $K$. $g$ を $K[X]$ の多項式、$h$ を多項式 $f(g(X))$ の $K[X]$ の既約因子とします。 Show that the degree of $h$ is a multiple $rn$ of $n$ and that $h$ has exactly $r$ roots in common with each of the polynomials $g(X) - \alpha_i \ (1 \leq i \leq n)$ of $\Omega[X]$ (consider the conjugates over $K$ of a root of $h$).
