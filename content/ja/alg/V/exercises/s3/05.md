Aを可換代数、Sを生成するAの部分集合をK-代数とします。 $\operatorname{Card}(S) < \operatorname{Card}(K)$ と仮定します。 Show that if $\mathfrak{M}$ is a maximal ideal of A, then the field $A/\mathfrak{M}$ is an algebraic extension of K. (Use Ex. 15 of V, p. 174 when $\operatorname{Card}(K) \leq \operatorname{Card}(N)$ and Ex. 4 when
$$
\operatorname{Card}(K) > \operatorname{Card}(N).
$$
*Deduce that $A/\mathfrak{M} = K$ when K is algebraically closed.*
