Let A be 可換環, E, F two A-代数, M a left E-module, N a left F-module and G 代数 $G = E \otimes_A F$. For every ordered 対 of 自己準同型 $u \in \mathrm{End}_E(M)$, $v \in \mathrm{End}_F(N)$, there exists one and only one G-endomorphism $w$ of $M \otimes_A N$ such that $w(x \otimes y) = u(x) \otimes v(y)$ for $x \in M, y \in N$ and we can write $w = \phi(u \otimes v)$, where $\phi$ is A-線型写像 (called *canonical*) of $\mathrm{End}_E(M) \otimes_A \mathrm{End}_F(N)$ into $\mathrm{End}_G(M \otimes_A N)$.以下では、A が *体* であると仮定します。

(a) Show that 標準写像 $\phi$ is injective (consider 元 $z = \sum_i u_i \otimes v_i$, where the $v_i \in \mathrm{End}_F(N)$ are linearly independent over A and write $(\phi(z))(x_\alpha \otimes y) = 0$ for every 元 $x_\alpha$ of 基底 of M over A and all $y \in N$）。

(b) Suppose that M is a *finitely generated free* E-module. Show that 写像 $\phi$ is bijective in each of the following cases: (1) E is of 有限階数 over 体 A; (2) N は有限生成された F モジュールです。

(c) Suppose that M is a *free* E-module and that there exist a $y \in N$ and an infinite 数列 $(v_n)$ of 自己準同型 of the F-module N such that ベクトル部分空間 (over A) of N generated by the $v_n(y)$ is of infinite rank over A. Show that, if 写像 $\phi$ is bijective, every 基底 of M over E is necessarily *finite*.

(d) さらに、M が *自由な* E モジュールであると仮定します。 Show that, for the G-module $M \otimes_A N$ to be faithful, it is necessary and sufficient that the F-module N be faithful (consider 基底 of E over A).
