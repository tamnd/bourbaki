Let $X = (\xi_{ij})$ be a square matrix of order $n$ over a commutative field $K$; let $H$ be a subset of $\{1, n\}$ with $p$ elements and $H'$ the complement of $H$ in $\{1, n\}$; suppose that, for every ordered pair of indices $h \in H, k \in H'$,
$$
\sum_i \xi_{ih} \xi_{ik} = 0.
$$
Show that, for all subsets $L, M$ of $\{1, n\}$ with $p$ elements,
$$
\rho_{M', M'} \det(X_{L, H}) \det(X_{M', H'}) - \rho_{L, L'} \det(X_{M, H}) \det(X_{L, H'}) = 0.
$$
(Consider the columns $x_h$ of index $h \in H$ in $X$ as vectors in $E = K^n$ and the columns $x_k^*$ of index $k \in H'$ as vectors in $E^*$ and show that the $(n - p)$-form $x_{H'}^*$ is proportional to $\phi_p(x_H)$.)
