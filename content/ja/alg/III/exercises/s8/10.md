Let $\Gamma, \Delta$ be the determinants of two matrices $U, V$ of order $n$; let H and K be any two subsets of $\{1, n\}$ with $p$ elements and $(i_k)$ (resp. $(j_k)$) the sequence obtained by arranging the elements of H (resp. K) in increasing order; let $\Gamma_{H, K}$ be the determinant obtained by replacing in $U$ the column of index $i_k$ by the column of $V$ of index $j_k$, for $1 \leq k \leq p$; similarly let $\Delta_{K, H}$ be the determinant obtained by replacing in $V$ the column of index $j_k$ by the column of $U$ of index $i_k$, for $1 \leq k \leq p$. $\{1, n\}$ と $p$ 元の部分集合 $H$ ごとに、

$$
\Gamma \Delta = \sum_K \Gamma_{H, K} \Delta_{K, H}
$$

ここで、$K$ は、$\{1, n\}$ の部分集合の集合を$p$ 元と実行します (no. 6 の式 (21) と (22) を使用します)。
