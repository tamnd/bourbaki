Kを可換体とし、$2 = 0$、A多項式環$K[X, Y, Z]$、E A-加群$A^3/M$とし、Mは$A^3$の元$(X, Y, Z)$によって生成された$A^3$の部分加群とする。最後に、理想$AX^2 + AY^2 + AZ^2$により、F を A の商 A-加群とします。 Show that there exists an alternating bilinear mapping of $E^2$ into F, which is the skewsymmetrization of no bilinear mapping of $E^2$ into F, but that in $T^2(E)$ the module $\mathfrak{g}_2''$ is equal to the kernel of the endomorphism $z \mapsto az$ (consider $T^2(E)$ as a quotient module of $T^2(A^3)$ and show that the kernel of $z \mapsto az$ is the canonical image of the module of symmetric tensors $S'_2(A^3)$).
