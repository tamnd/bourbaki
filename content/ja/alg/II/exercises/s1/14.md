Let L be a well-ordered set, E an A-module and $(E_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L}$ an increasing family of submodules of E such that: (1) there exists a $\lambda \in L$ such that $E = \{0\}$; (2) E is the union of the $E_\lambda$ for $\lambda \in L$; (3) if $\lambda \in L$ is such that the set of $\mu < \lambda$ admits a greatest element $\lambda'$, $E_\lambda$ is the direct sum $E_{\lambda'}$ and a submodule $F_\lambda$; (4) if $\lambda \in L$ is the least upper bound of the set of $\mu < \lambda$, then $E_\lambda$ is the union of the $E_\mu$ for $\mu < \lambda$. これらの条件の下で、E が $F_\lambda$ の *直和* であることを示してください。 (超有限帰納法によって、各 $E_\lambda$ が $\mu \leq \lambda$ となるように $F_\mu$ の直和であることを証明します。)
