$(X_i)_{i \in I}$ をトポロジー 空間の無限族とします。 Study the topology on the product set
$$
X = \prod_{i \in I} X_i
$$
generated by subsets of the form $\prod_{i \in I} U_i$,
where $U_i$ is an open subset of $X_i$ for each $i \in I$ and the set of indices $i$ for which $U_i \neq X_i$ has cardinal less than $c$, where $c$ is a given infinite cardinal. この位相に対して、§ 4の命題はどうなるかを考えてみましょう。
