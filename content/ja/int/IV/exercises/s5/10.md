$F$ をバナッハ空間、$f$ を $X$ の可測写像から $F$ にすると、$x \in X$ の集合 $A$ になります。ここで、$f(x) \neq 0$ は可算の和集合です集合。 Show that there exists a sequence $(f_n)$ of continuous functions with compact support, with values in $F$, such that the sequence $(f_n(x))$ converges to $f(x)$ almost everywhere in $X$ (note on the one hand that $A$ is the union of a negligible set $N$ and a sequence of pairwise disjoint compact sets $K_n$ such that the restriction of $f$ to each $K_n$ is continuous; and on the other hand, that there exists a decreasing sequence $(U_n)$ of open sets containing $A$ such that $\mu(U_n \cap \complement A)$ tends to 0 as $n$ tends to infinity).
