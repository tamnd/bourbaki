Let T be a locally compact space, X a locally compact subspace of T, and $\mu$ a positive measure on T; set $\nu = \varphi_X \cdot \mu = j(\mu_X)$ (*j* the canonical injection of X into T).
   *a*) Show that every $\mu_X$-negligible subset of X is $\nu$-negligible.
   *b*) For every numerical function $g \geq 0$ defined on X, show that $\int^* g \, d\mu_X = \int_X^* g \, d\nu$ (use *a*), as well as formula (7) of §3).
   *c*) Let g be a function defined on X, with values in $\overline{\mathbf{R}}$ or in a Banach space, $g'$ the extension of g to T equal to 0 on $T - X$. g が $\mu_X$ 積分可能であるためには、 $g'$ が $\nu$ 積分可能であることが必要かつ十分であり、その場合は $\int g \, d\mu_X = \int_X g \, d\nu$ (*b* を使用))。
