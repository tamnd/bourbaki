$(T_i)_{i \in I}$ をラドン トポロジカル空間の族とし、その族の $T$ 和空間を仮定します。 If Card(I) is an Ulam cardinal, then the topological space $T$ is a Radon space (show that for every positive and bounded countably additive function $m$ on the Borel tribe of $T$, there exists a countable subset $J$ of $I$ such that $m(\bigcup_{i \in I - J} T_i) = 0$).
