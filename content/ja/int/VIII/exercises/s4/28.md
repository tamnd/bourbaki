$G_1, G_2$ を 2 つのローカルコンパクト群、$\beta_1, \beta_2$ $G_1, G_2$ の左ハール測度、$A_1$ (それぞれ $A_2$) をトポロジカル代数 ($\mathbf{R}$ 以上) $L^1(G_1, \beta_1)$ (それぞれ $L^1(G_2, \beta_2)$) とします。 Let $T$ be an algebra isomorphism of $A_1$ onto $A_2$, such that the relation $f \geq 0$ almost everywhere is equivalent to $T(f) \geq 0$ almost everywhere.
a) Show that $T$ is an isomorphism of topological algebras. (First note that if a decreasing sequence $(f_n)$ of elements of $A_1$ tends to 0 almost everywhere, then the same is true of the sequence of the $T(f_n)$; deduce from this that for every sequence $(f_n)$ that is bounded above in $A_1$, $T(\sup(f_n)) = \sup(T(f_n))$, and conclude with the help of Fatou’s lemma.)
b) Show that $T$ may be extended, in only one way, to an isomorphism of the topological algebra $\mathscr{M}^1(G_1)$ onto the topological algebra $\mathscr{M}^1(G_2)$, and that there exist a topological isomorphism $u$ of $G_1$ onto $G_2$ and a continuous homomorphism $\chi$ of $G_1$ into $\mathbf{R}_+^*$ such that $T(\varepsilon_s) = \chi(s)\varepsilon_{u(s)}$ (make use of Exer. 15 a) and Exer. 19 b); to prove that $u$ and $\chi$ are continuous, observe that $T$ defines an isomorphism of the algebra $\mathscr{L}(A_1)$ of continuous endomorphisms of the topological vector space $A_1$ onto the analogous algebra $\mathscr{L}(A_2)$, and that this isomorphism is bicontinuous for the topology of pointwise convergence; on the other hand, observe that $s \mapsto \delta(s^{-1})$ is an isomorphism of $G_1$ onto a multiplicative subgroup of $\mathscr{L}(A_1)$.

c) b) から、$f \in A_1$ ごとに、

$$
(T(f))(t) = \chi(u^{-1}(t))f(u^{-1}(t))
$$

すべての$t \in G_2$に。

(A, III, §2, Exer. 9) を思い出してください。代数 $L^1(G_1, \beta_1)$ と $L^1(G_2, \beta_2)$ が同型であり、$G_1$ と $G_2$ は同型ではないような有限群 $G_1, G_2$ が存在します。
