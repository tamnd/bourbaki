PROPOSITION 3. *With the assumptions and notation of Th. 3, let H be the set of pseudo-reflections belonging to G and distinct from 1. Assume that K is of characteristic 0. Then* $ \operatorname{Card}(H) = \sum_{i=1}^{l} (k_i - 1) $.

By Prop. 3 of the Appendix, we can assume that K is algebraically closed. For any $ g \in G $, let $ \lambda_1(g), \ldots, \lambda_l(g) $ be its eigenvalues. Since every $ g \in G $ is diagonalizable (Appendix, Prop. 2), $ g = 1 $ if and only if all of the $ \lambda_i(g) $ are equal to 1, and $ g \in H $ if and only if the number of $ \lambda_i(g) $ equal to 1 is $ l - 1 $ (we then denote by $ \lambda(g) $ the eigenvalue distinct from 1). Prop. 1 of no. 1 and Lemma 3 prove that

$$
q \prod_{i=1}^{l} (1 - T^{k_i})^{-1} = \sum_{g \in G} (\det(1 - gT))^{-1}
$$

in $ K[[T]] $, and hence in $ K(T) $. Consequently, we have in $ K(T) $

$$
q \frac{(1 - T)^{l-1}}{\prod_{i=1}^{l} (1 - T^{k_i})} = \frac{1}{1 - T} + \sum_{g \in H} \frac{1}{1 - \lambda(g)T} + \sum_{g \neq 1, g \notin H} \frac{(1 - T)^{l-1}}{\det(1 - gT)},
$$

which can be written

$$
\frac{q - \prod_{i=1}^{l} (1 + T + \cdots + T^{k_i-1})}{(1 - T) \prod_{i=1}^{l} (1 + T + \cdots + T^{k_i-1})}
$$
$$
= \sum_{g \in H} \frac{1}{1 - \lambda(g)T} + \sum_{g \neq 1, g \notin H} \frac{(1 - T)^{l-1}}{\det(1 - gT)}.
$$

It follows that $ q - \prod_{i=1}^{l} (1 + T + \cdots + T^{k_i-1}) $ vanishes for $ T = 1 $, so $ q = k_1 k_2 \ldots k_l $, which we knew already by the Cor. of Th. 3. This granted, let Q(T) be the polynomial $ (1 - T)^{-1}(q - \prod_{i=1}^{l} (1 + T + \cdots + T^{k_i-1})) $. Differentiating the equality $ (1 - T)Q(T) = q - \prod_{i=1}^{l} (1 + T + \cdots + T^{k_i-1}) $ and putting $ T = 1 $, we see that $ -Q(1) $ is the value for $ T = 1 $ of

$$
-\frac{d}{dt} \left( \prod_{i=1}^{l} (1 + T + \cdots + T^{k_i-1}) \right)
$$
$$
= - \sum_{i=1}^{l} ((1 + 2T + \cdots + (k_i - 1)T^{k_i-2}) \prod_{j \neq i} (1 + T + \cdots + T^{k_j-1}))
$$
