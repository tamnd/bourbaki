SYSTEMS OF TYPE $ C_l $ ($ l \geq 2 $)

(I) $ V = E = \mathbf{R}^l $.
Roots: $ \pm 2\varepsilon_i,\ (1 \leq i \leq l),\ \pm \varepsilon_i \pm \varepsilon_j\ (1 \leq i < j \leq l) $.
Number of roots: $ n = 2l^2 $.

(II) Basis: $ \alpha_1 = \varepsilon_1 - \varepsilon_2, \alpha_2 = \varepsilon_2 - \varepsilon_3, \ldots, \alpha_{l-1} = \varepsilon_{l-1} - \varepsilon_l, \alpha_l = 2\varepsilon_l $
Positive roots:
$$
\varepsilon_i = \varepsilon_i - \varepsilon_j = \sum_{i \leq k < j} \alpha_k\ (1 \leq i < j \leq l),
$$
$$
\varepsilon_i + \varepsilon_j = \sum_{i \leq k < j} \alpha_k + 2 \sum_{j \leq k < l} \alpha_k + \alpha_l\ (1 \leq i < j \leq l),
$$
$$
2\varepsilon_i = \sum_{i \leq k < l} \alpha_k + \alpha_l\ (1 \leq i \leq l).
$$

(III) Coxeter number: $ h = 2l $.

(IV) Highest root:
$$
\tilde{\alpha} = 2\varepsilon_1 = 2\alpha_1 + 2\alpha_2 + \cdots + 2\alpha_{l-1} + \alpha_l.
$$
Completed Dynkin graph:

$$
\begin{array}{cccccc}
\circlearrowleft & \circ & \cdots & \circ & \circlearrowright \\
\alpha_1 & \alpha_2 & \cdots & \alpha_{l-2} & \alpha_{l-1} & \alpha_l
\end{array}
$$

(V) $ R^- $ is the set of vectors $ \pm \varepsilon_i,\ \pm \varepsilon_i \pm \varepsilon_j $.

$$
\Phi_R(x, y) = \frac{(x|y)}{4(l+1)},\quad \gamma(R) = (l+1)(4l-2).
$$

(VI) Fundamental weights:
$$
\omega_i = \varepsilon_1 + \varepsilon_2 + \cdots + \varepsilon_i\ (1 \leq i \leq l)
$$
$$
= \alpha_1 + 2\alpha_2 + \cdots + (i-1)\alpha_{i-1}
$$
$$
+ i(\alpha_i + \alpha_{i+1} + \cdots + \alpha_{l-1} + \frac{1}{2}\alpha_l).
$$
