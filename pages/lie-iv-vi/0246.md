### 14. IRREDUCIBLE NON-REDUCED ROOT SYSTEMS

The irreducible, non-reduced root systems can be obtained from the irreducible, reduced systems by using Props. 13 and 14 of § 1. no. 4. For each integer $l \geq 1$, there exists, up to isomorphism, a unique irreducible, non-reduced root system of rank $l$: let R be a root system of type B_l, A the set of shortest roots of R; take the union of R and 2A. With the notation of no. 5, we obtain the $2l(l+1)$ vectors

$$
\pm \varepsilon_i,\ \pm 2\varepsilon_i,\ \varepsilon_i \pm \varepsilon_j\ (i < j).
$$
