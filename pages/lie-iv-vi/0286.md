(XII) Action of $P(R^-)/Q(R^-) = P(R)/Q(R)$ on the completed Dynkin graph:
$l$ odd: $\omega_l$ transforms $\alpha_0$ into $\alpha_1$, $\alpha_l$ into $\alpha_1$, $\alpha_1$ into $\alpha_{l-1}$ and $\alpha_{l-1}$ into $\alpha_0$: it interchanges $\alpha_j$ and $\alpha_{l-j}$ for $2 \leq j \leq l-2$.
$l$ even: $\omega_l$ (resp. $\omega_{l-1}$) interchanges $\alpha_0$ and $\alpha_l$ (resp. $\alpha_0$ and $\alpha_{l-1}$), $\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_{l-1}$ (resp. $\alpha_1$ and $\alpha_l$) and interchanges $\alpha_j$ and $\alpha_{l-j}$ for $2 \leq j \leq l-2$.
