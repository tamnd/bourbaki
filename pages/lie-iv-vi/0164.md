b) Let $ s' = s_1 \ldots s_r $ and $ s'' = s_{r+1} \ldots s_l $, so that $ c = s's'' $ is the Coxeter transformation associated to the ordered chamber C. Let $ w_0 $ be the element of W that transforms C to $-C$ (cf. § 4, Exerc. 2). Show that, if $ h $ is odd,

$$
w_0 = \underbrace{s' s'' s' \ldots s'' s'}_{h \text{ terms}} = \underbrace{s'' s' s'' \ldots s' s''}_{h \text{ terms}} = s'' c^{(h-1)/2} = c^{(h-1)/2} s'.
$$

c) Put $ S = \{ s_1, \ldots, s_l \} $. The pair $(W, S)$ is a Coxeter system. If $ w \in W $, denote by $ l_S(w) $ the length of $ w $ with respect to S (Chap. IV, § 1, no. 1). Show that

$$
l_S(s') = r, \quad l_S(s'') = l - r, \quad l_S(c) = l.
$$

Deduce that, with the assumptions in b),

$$
l_S(w_0) \leq r + \frac{h-1}{2} l \quad \text{and} \quad l_S(w_0) \leq (l-r) + \frac{h-1}{2} l.
$$

Show on the other hand that $ l_S(w_0) = \operatorname{Card}(\mathfrak{H}) = hl/2 $ (use Exerc. 22 of Chap. IV, § 1). Deduce that $ r = l/2 $ and that $ (s_1, s_2, \ldots, s_{lh/2}) $ is a reduced decomposition of $ w_0 $.

d) Show that, if $ h $ is even, $ (s_1, \ldots, s_{lh/2}) $ is a reduced decomposition of $ w_0 = c^{h/2} $. (Same method.)
