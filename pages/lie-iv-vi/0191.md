PROPOSITION 24. *Let $R'$ be the intersection of $R$ with a vector subspace of $V$, so that $R'$ is a root system in the vector subspace $V'$ that it generates (cf. Cor. of Prop. 4, no. 1). Let $B'$ be a basis of $R'$.*

(i) *There exists a basis of $R$ containing $B'$.*
(ii) *$R'$ is the set of elements of $R$ that are linear combinations of elements of $B'$.*

Assertion (ii) is clear. We prove (i). Let $(\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2, \ldots, \varepsilon_l)$ be a basis of $V$ such that $B' = (\varepsilon_{p+1}, \varepsilon_{p+2}, \ldots, \varepsilon_l)$. The lexicographic order on $V$ corresponding to this basis defines a chamber $C$ of $R$. It is clear that every element of $B'$ is minimal in $R_+(C)$. Thus $B' \subseteq B(C)$.

PROPOSITION 25. *Assume that $R$ is irreducible. Let $C$ be a chamber of $R$, and let $B(C) = \{\alpha_1, \ldots, \alpha_l\}$ be the corresponding basis.*

(i) *There exists a root $\tilde{\alpha} = \sum_{i=1}^{l} n_i \alpha_i$ such that, for every root $\sum_{i=1}^{l} p_i \alpha_i$, we have $n_1 \geq p_1, n_2 \geq p_2, \ldots, n_l \geq p_l$. In other words, $R$ has a largest element for the ordering defined by $C$.*
(ii) *We have $\tilde{\alpha} \in \overline{C}$.*
(iii) *We have $(\tilde{\alpha}|\tilde{\alpha}) \geq (\alpha|\alpha)$ for every root $\alpha$.*
(iv) *For every positive root $\alpha'$ not proportional to $\tilde{\alpha}$, we have $n(\alpha', \tilde{\alpha}) = 0$ or $1$.*

1) Let $\alpha = \sum_{i=1}^{l} n_i \alpha_i$, $\beta = \sum_{i=1}^{l} p_i \alpha_i$ be two maximal roots for the ordering defined by $C$. We shall prove that $\alpha = \beta$, which will establish (i).

2) If $(\alpha|\alpha_i) < 0$ for some index $i$, it follows that either $\alpha + \alpha_i \in R$ or $\alpha = -\alpha_i$ (Cor. of Th. 1, no. 3), and both possibilities are absurd by the maximality of $\alpha$. Thus $(\alpha|\alpha_i) \geq 0$ for all $i$.
