f) If $ g \in G $, let $ \mathrm{Tr}_{n,m}(g) $ be the trace of the automorphism of $ E_{n,m} $ defined by $ g $; put
$$
\mathrm{Tr}(X, Y)(g) = \sum_{n,m \geq 0} \mathrm{Tr}_{n,m}(g) X^n Y^m.
$$
Show that
$$
\mathrm{Tr}(X, Y)(g) = \frac{\det(1 + Yg)}{\det(1 - Xg)}.
$$
g) Let $ q = \mathrm{Card}(G) $. Show that
$$
\frac{1}{q} \sum_{g \in G} \mathrm{Tr}(X, Y)(g) = a(X, Y),
$$
i.e.
$$
\frac{1}{q} \sum_{g \in G} \frac{\det(1 + Yg)}{\det(1 - Xg)} = \prod_{i=1}^l \frac{1 + Y.X^{p_i-1}}{1 - X^{p_i}}.
$$
