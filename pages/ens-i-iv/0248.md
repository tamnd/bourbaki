Show that, if $0 \leqslant \eta < \xi \leqslant \alpha$, then $\mathrm{Card}(f_\alpha(\eta)) < \mathrm{Card}(f_\alpha(\xi))$ and that, if $\xi \leqslant \alpha \leqslant \beta$, then $f_\alpha(\xi) = f_\beta(\xi)$.
