§ 3

1. Show that the relations $x \in y$, $x \subset y$, $x = \{y\}$ have no graph with respect to $x$ and $y$.

2. Let G be a graph. Show that the relation $\mathrm{X} \subset \mathrm{pr}_1\mathrm{G}$ is equivalent to $\mathrm{X} \subset \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}}\langle \mathrm{G}\langle \mathrm{X}\rangle\rangle$.

3. Let G, H be two graphs. Show that the the relation $\mathrm{pr}_1\mathrm{H} \subset \mathrm{pr}_1\mathrm{G}$ is equivalent to $\mathrm{H} \subset \mathrm{H} \circ \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}} \circ \mathrm{G}$. Deduce that $\mathrm{G} \subset \mathrm{G} \circ \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}} \circ \mathrm{G}$.

4. If G is a graph, show that $\emptyset \circ \mathrm{G} = \mathrm{G} \circ \emptyset = \emptyset$, and that $\overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}} \circ \mathrm{G} = \emptyset$ if and only if $\mathrm{G} = \emptyset$.

5. Let A, B be two sets and let G be a graph. Show that

$$(\mathrm{A} \times \mathrm{B}) \circ \mathrm{G} = \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}}\langle \mathrm{A}\rangle \times \mathrm{B} \qquad \text{and} \qquad \mathrm{G} \circ (\mathrm{A} \times \mathrm{B}) = \mathrm{A} \times \mathrm{G}\langle \mathrm{B}\rangle.$$

6. For each graph G, let G′ be the graph $((\mathrm{pr}_1\mathrm{G}) \times (\mathrm{pr}_2\mathrm{G})) - \mathrm{G}$. Show that

$$(\overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}})' = \overset{-1}{\overline{\mathrm{G}'}},$$

and that

$$\mathrm{G} \circ (\overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}})' \subset \Delta'_{\mathrm{B}}, \qquad (\overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}})' \circ \mathrm{G} \subset \Delta'_{\mathrm{A}}$$

if $\mathrm{A} \supset \mathrm{pr}_1\mathrm{G}$ and $\mathrm{B} \supset \mathrm{pr}_2\mathrm{G}$. Show that
