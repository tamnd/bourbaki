# EXERCISES

§ 5

1. Let $(\mathrm{X}_\iota)_{\iota \in \mathbf{I}}$ be a family of sets. Show that if $(\mathrm{Y}_\iota)_{\iota \in \mathbf{I}}$ is a family of sets such that $\mathrm{Y}_\iota \subset \mathrm{X}_\iota$ for each $\iota \in \mathrm{I}$, then

$$\prod_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{Y}_\iota = \bigcap_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \overset{-1}{\mathrm{pr}_\iota}(\mathrm{Y}_\iota).$$

2. Let A, B be two sets. For each subset G of $\mathrm{A} \times \mathrm{B}$ let $\tilde{\mathrm{G}}$ be the mapping $x \rightarrow \mathrm{G}\langle\{x\}\rangle$ of A into $\mathfrak{P}(\mathrm{B})$. Show that the mapping $\mathrm{G} \rightarrow \tilde{\mathrm{G}}$ is a bijection of $\mathfrak{P}(\mathrm{A} \times \mathrm{B})$ onto $(\mathfrak{P}(\mathrm{B}))^{\mathrm{A}}$.

*3. Let $(\mathrm{X}_i)_{1 \leqslant i \leqslant n}$ be a finite family of sets. For each subset H of the index set $[1, n]$ let

$$\mathrm{P_H} = \bigcup_{\iota \in \mathrm{H}} \mathrm{X}_i \quad \text{and} \quad \mathrm{Q_H} = \bigcap_{\iota \in \mathrm{H}} \mathrm{X}_i.$$

Let $\mathfrak{F}_k$ be the set of subsets of $[1, n]$ which have $k$ elements. Show that

$$\bigcup_{\mathrm{H} \in \mathfrak{F}_k} \mathrm{Q_H} \supset \bigcap_{\mathrm{H} \in \mathfrak{F}_k} \mathrm{P_H} \quad \text{if} \quad k \leqslant \frac{1}{2}(n+1),$$

and that

$$\bigcup_{\mathrm{H} \in \mathfrak{F}_k} \mathrm{Q_H} \subset \bigcap_{\mathrm{H} \in \mathfrak{F}_k} \mathrm{P_H} \quad \text{if} \quad k \geqslant \frac{1}{2}(n+1).*$$

§ 6

1. For a graph G to be the graph of an equivalence relation on a set E, it is necessary and sufficient that $\mathrm{pr_1G} = \mathrm{E}$, $\mathrm{G} \circ \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}} \circ \mathrm{G} = \mathrm{G}$, and $\Delta_{\mathrm{E}} \subset \mathrm{G}$ ($\Delta_{\mathrm{E}}$ being the diagonal of E).

2. If G is a graph such that

$$\mathrm{G} \circ \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}} \circ \mathrm{G} = \mathrm{G},$$

show that $\overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}} \circ \mathrm{G}$ and $\mathrm{G} \circ \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}}$ are graphs of equivalences on $\mathrm{pr_1G}$ and $\mathrm{pr_2G}$ respectively.

3. Let E be a set, A a subset of E, and R the equivalence relation associated with the mapping $\mathrm{X} \rightarrow \mathrm{X} \cap \mathrm{A}$ of $\mathfrak{P}(\mathrm{E})$ into $\mathfrak{P}(\mathrm{E})$. Show that there exists a bijection of $\mathfrak{P}(\mathrm{A})$ onto the quotient set $\mathfrak{P}(\mathrm{E})/\mathrm{R}$.

4. Let G be the graph of an equivalence on a set E. Show that if A is a graph such that $\mathrm{A} \subset \mathrm{G}$ and $\mathrm{pr_1A} = \mathrm{E}$ (resp. $\mathrm{pr_2A} = \mathrm{E}$), then

$$\mathrm{G} \circ \mathrm{A} = \mathrm{G} \quad (\text{resp.} \quad \mathrm{A} \circ \mathrm{G} = \mathrm{G});$$
