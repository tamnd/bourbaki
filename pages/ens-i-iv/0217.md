COROLLARY. Let $(\mathbf{F}_\alpha, g_{\beta\alpha})$ and $(\mathbf{F}'_\alpha, g'_{\beta\alpha})$ be two direct systems of sets relative to $I$, and for each $\alpha \in I$ let $u_\alpha : E_\alpha \to \mathbf{F}_\alpha$, $u'_\alpha : E'_\alpha \to \mathbf{F}'_\alpha$ be mappings such that $(u_\alpha)$ and $(u'_\alpha)$ are two direct systems of mappings. Then $(u_\alpha \times u'_\alpha)$ is a direct system of mappings, and (up to canonical bijections) we have

$$
\lim_{\longrightarrow} (u_\alpha \times u'_\alpha) = (\lim_{\longrightarrow} u_\alpha) \times (\lim_{\longrightarrow} u'_\alpha).
$$

We leave the verification to the reader.
