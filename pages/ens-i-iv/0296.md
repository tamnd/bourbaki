2. Let S be a signed echelon type on $n + m$ letters (Exercise 1). Let $\Sigma$ be a species of structures having $x_1$, ..., $x_n$ as principal base sets and $\mathrm{A}_1$, ..., $\mathrm{A}_m$ as auxiliary base sets, whose typical characterization is of the form $s \in \mathfrak{P}(\mathrm{S}(x_1, \ldots, x_n, \mathrm{A}_1, \ldots, \mathrm{A}_m))$. Show that we may define a notion of $\sigma$-morphism for this species of structures as follows: given $n$ sets $\mathrm{E}_1, \ldots, \mathrm{E}_n$ endowed with a structure U of species $\Sigma$, $n$ sets $\mathrm{E}'_1, \ldots, \mathrm{E}'_n$ endowed with a structure $\mathrm{U}'$ of species $\Sigma$, and mappings $f_i : \mathrm{E}_i \to \mathrm{E}'_i$ $(1 \leqslant i \leqslant n)$, then $(f_1, \ldots, f_n)$ is said to be a $\sigma$-morphism if the mappings $f_i$ satisfy the following conditions :

(i) If S is a covariant echelon type, then

$$\{f_1, \ \ldots, f_n, \mathrm{I}_1, \ \ldots, \mathrm{I}_m\}^{\mathrm{S}}\langle \mathrm{U} \rangle \subset \mathrm{U}'.$$

(ii) If S is a contravariant echelon type, then

$$\{f_1, \ \ldots, f_n, \mathrm{I}_1, \ \ldots, \mathrm{I}_m\}^{\mathrm{S}}\langle \mathrm{U}' \rangle \subset \mathrm{U}.$$

Show that by assigning the variances suitably we may obtain in this way the definition of the morphisms for order structures, algebraic structures, and topological structures.

3. Let A, B, C be three sets endowed with structures of the same species $\Sigma$, let $f$ be a morphism of A *onto* B, and let $g$ be a morphism of B into C. Show that if $g \circ f$ is an isomorphism of A onto C, then $g$ and $f$ are isomorphisms.

4. Let A, B, C, D be four sets endowed with structures of the same species $\Sigma$, let $f$ be a morphism of A into B, $g$ a morphism of B into C, and $h$ a morphism of C into D. Show that if $g \circ f$ and $h \circ g$ are isomorphisms, then $f$, $g$, and $h$ are isomorphisms (cf. Chapter II, §3, Exercise 9).

5. Let A, B be two sets endowed with structures $\mathscr{S}$, $\mathscr{S}'$ of the same species $\Sigma$. Let $f$ be a morphism of A into B, and let $g$ be a morphism of B into A. Let M (resp. N) be the set of all $x \in \mathrm{A}$ (resp. $y \in \mathrm{B}$) such that $g(f(x)) = x$ (resp. $f((g)y) = y$). Suppose that $\mathscr{S}$ (resp. $\mathscr{S}'$) induces on M (resp. N) a structure of species $\Sigma$. Show that M and N, endowed with these structures, are isomorphic.
