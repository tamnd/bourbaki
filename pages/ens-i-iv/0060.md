**Lemma 2.** — Every balanced word $A$ can be put in the form

$$
A=fA_1A_2\ldots A_p,
$$

where the $A_i$ are balanced and $n(f)=p$.

Let $f$ be the initial sign of $A$. By Lemma 1, $A$ can be written as

$$
fA_1A_2\ldots A_p,
$$

where the $A_i$ are balanced : we need only define $A_i$ inductively as the balanced segment of $A$ which begins at the $k(i)$th place, where

$$

$$
1+l(A_1)+\ldots+l(A_p)=l(A)=n(A)+1
