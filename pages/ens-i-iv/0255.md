¶ 27. Let L be an infinite set and let $(\mathrm{E}_\lambda)_{\lambda \in \mathbf{L}}$ be a family of sets indexed by L. Suppose that for each integer $n > 0$ the set of $\lambda \in \mathrm{L}$ such that Card $(\mathrm{E}_\lambda) > n$ is equipotent to L. Show that there exists a subset F of the product $\mathrm{E} = \prod_{\lambda \in \mathbf{L}} \mathrm{E}_\lambda$, such that Card $(\mathrm{F}) = 2^{\mathrm{Card\ (L)}}$, and such that F has the following property : for each finite sequence $(f_k)_{1 \leqslant k \leqslant n}$ of distinct elements of F there exists $\lambda \in \mathrm{L}$ such that the elements

$$f_k(\lambda) \in \mathrm{E}_\lambda \ (1 \leqslant k \leqslant n)$$

are all distinct.
