§ 27. Let L be an infinite set and let $ (E_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L} $ be a family of sets indexed by L. Suppose that for each integer $ n > 0 $ the set of $ \lambda \in L $ such that Card $ (E_\lambda) > n $ is equipotent to L. Show that there exists a subset F of the product $ E = \prod_{\lambda \in L} E_\lambda $, such that Card $ (F) = 2^{\text{Card}(L)} $, and such that F has the following property: for each finite sequence $ (f_k)_{1 \leq k \leq n} $ of distinct elements of F there exists $ \lambda \in L $ such that the elements

$$
f_k(\lambda) \in E_\lambda \quad (1 \leq k \leq n)
$$

are all distinct. (Show first that there exists a partition $ (L_j)_{j \in \mathbf{N}} $ of L such that Card $ (L_j) = \text{Card}(L) $ for all j, and such that Card $ (E_\lambda) \geq 2^j $ for each $ \lambda \in L_j $. Hence reduce to the case where L is the sum of the countable family of sets $ X^j (j \geq 1) $, where X is an infinite set, and $ E_\lambda = 2^j $ for each $ \lambda \in X^j $.
