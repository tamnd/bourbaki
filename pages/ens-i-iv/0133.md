3. Let X, Y, Y', Z be four sets. Show that

$$(\mathrm{Y}' \times \mathrm{Z}) \circ (\mathrm{X} \times \mathrm{Y}) = \emptyset \quad \text{if} \quad \mathrm{Y} \cap \mathrm{Y}' = \emptyset$$

and that

$$(\mathrm{Y}' \times \mathrm{Z}) \circ (\mathrm{X} \times \mathrm{Y}) = \mathrm{X} \times \mathrm{Z} \quad \text{if} \quad \mathrm{Y} \cap \mathrm{Y}' \neq \emptyset.$$

4. Let $(\mathrm{G}_\iota)_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}}$ be a family of graphs. Show that for every set X we have

$$\left( \bigcup_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{G}_\iota \right) \langle \mathrm{X} \rangle = \bigcup_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{G}_\iota \langle \mathrm{X} \rangle,$$

and that for every object $x$,

$$\left( \bigcap_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{G}_\iota \right) \langle \{x\} \rangle = \bigcap_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{G}_\iota \langle \{x\} \rangle.$$

Give an example of two graphs G, H and a set X such that

$$(\mathrm{G} \cap \mathrm{H}) \langle \mathrm{X} \rangle \neq \mathrm{G} \langle \mathrm{X} \rangle \cap \mathrm{H} \langle \mathrm{X} \rangle.$$

5. Let $(\mathrm{G}_\iota)_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}}$ be a family of graphs and let H be a graph. Show that

$$\left( \bigcup_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{G}_\iota \right) \circ \mathrm{H} = \bigcup_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} (\mathrm{G}_\iota \circ \mathrm{H}) \quad \text{and} \quad \mathrm{H} \circ \left( \bigcup_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} \mathrm{G}_\iota \right) = \bigcup_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}} (\mathrm{H} \circ \mathrm{G}_\iota).$$

6. A graph G is functional if and only if for each pair of graphs H, H' we have $(\mathrm{H} \cap \mathrm{H}') \circ \mathrm{G} = (\mathrm{H} \circ \mathrm{G}) \cap (\mathrm{H}' \circ \mathrm{G})$.

7. Let G, H, K be three graphs. Prove the relation

$$(\mathrm{H} \circ \mathrm{G}) \cap \mathrm{K} \subset (\mathrm{H} \cap (\mathrm{K} \circ \overset{-1}{\mathrm{G}})) \circ (\mathrm{G} \cap (\overset{-1}{\mathrm{H}} \circ \mathrm{K})).$$

8. Let $\mathfrak{R} = (\mathrm{X}_\iota)_{\iota \in \mathrm{I}}$ and $\mathfrak{S} = (\mathrm{Y}_\varkappa)_{\varkappa \in \mathrm{K}}$ be two coverings of a set E.

(a) Show that if $\mathfrak{R}$ and $\mathfrak{S}$ are partitions of E and if $\mathfrak{R}$ is finer than $\mathfrak{S}$, then for every $\varkappa \in \mathrm{K}$ there exists $\iota \in \mathrm{I}$ such that $\mathrm{X}_\iota \subset \mathrm{Y}_\varkappa$.

(b) Give an example of two coverings $\mathfrak{R}$, $\mathfrak{S}$ such that $\mathfrak{R}$ is finer than $\mathfrak{S}$ but such that the property stated in (a) is not satisfied.

(c) Give an example of two partitions $\mathfrak{R}$, $\mathfrak{S}$ of E such that for every $\varkappa \in \mathrm{K}$ there exists $\iota \in \mathrm{I}$ such that $\mathrm{X}_\iota \subset \mathrm{Y}_\varkappa$ but such that $\mathfrak{R}$ is not a refinement of $\mathfrak{S}$.
