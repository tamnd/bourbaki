**3. DEFINITIONS OF THE PRODUCT OF A FAMILY OF SETS**

Let $ (X_i)_{i \in I} $ be a family of sets and let $ F $ be a functional graph with domain $ I $ such that $ F(i) \in X_i $ for each $ i \in I $. Then for each $ i \in I $ we have $ F(i) \in A = \bigcup_{i \in I} X_i $, and therefore $ F $ is an element of $ \mathscr{P}(I \times A) $.

The functional graphs with the above property therefore form a subset of $ \mathscr{P}(I \times A) $.

**Definition 1.** *Let $ (X_i)_{i \in I} $ be a family of sets. The set of functional graphs $ F $ with domain $ I $ such that $ F(i) \in X_i $ for each $ i \in I $ is called the product of the family of sets $ (X_i)_{i \in I} $ and is denoted by $ \prod_{i \in I} X_i $. For each $ i \in I $, $ X_i $ is called the factor of index $ i $ in the product $ \prod_{i \in I} X_i $. The mapping $ F \to F(i) \left( F \in \prod_{i \in I} X_i, \ F(i) \in X_i \right) $ is called the coordinate function (or projection) of index $ i $, and is denoted by $ \mathrm{pr}_i $.*

$ F(i) $ is called the *coordinate of index $ i $* (or *projection of index $ i $*) of $ F $; the image $ \mathrm{pr}_i(A) $ of a subset $ A $ of $ \prod_{i \in I} X_i $ under the coordinate function of index $ i $ is called the *projection of index $ i $* of $ A $. It is easily verified that $ A \subset \prod_{i \in I} \mathrm{pr}_i(A) $.

We shall often use the notation $ (x_i)_{i \in I} $ to denote an element of $ \prod_{i \in I} X_i $ (\S 3, no. 6).

If $ I = \varnothing $, the set $ \prod_{i \in I} X_i $ has only one element, namely the empty set (\S 3, no. 4, Example 1).

If all the factors $ X_i $ of the product $ \prod_{i \in I} X_i $ are equal to the same set $ E $, we have $ \prod_{i \in I} X_i = E^I $; this follows immediately from the definitions.
