6. Let $ E $ and $ F $ be two ordered sets, and let $ \mathscr{A}_0(E, F) $ be the subset of the product ordered set $ F^E $ consisting of the increasing mappings of $ E $ into $ F $.

(a) Show that if $ E, F, G $ are there ordered sets, then the ordered set $ \mathscr{A}(E, F \times G) $ is isomorphic to the product ordered set $ \mathscr{A}(E, F) \times \mathscr{A}(E, G) $.
