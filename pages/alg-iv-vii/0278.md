18) Let $p \in \mathbf{N}$ be a prime number, $C_p$ the cyclotomic extension of level $p$ of $\mathbf{Q}$, $\zeta \in C_p$ a primitive p-th root of unity and $n$ an integer. Write

$$
X_n(\zeta) = \sum_{a \in \mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z}} \zeta^{a^n}
$$

a) Calculate $d = [\mathbf{Q}(X_n(\zeta)) : \mathbf{Q}]$. Determine the Galois group of the extension $\mathbf{Q}(X_n(\zeta))$ of $\mathbf{Q}$ and the conjugates of $X_n(\zeta)$.
b) Let $T^d + a_1 T^{d-1} + \ldots + a_d$ be the minimal polynomial of $X_n(\zeta)$ over $\mathbf{Q}$. Show that $a_1 = 0$.
c) Suppose that $n > 1$ and $p \equiv 1 \pmod{n}$. Show that $a_1 = -\frac{n(n-1)p}{2}$ if $\frac{p-1}{n}$ is even and $a_1 = \frac{np}{2}$ if $\frac{p-1}{n}$ is odd.
