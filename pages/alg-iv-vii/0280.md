23) Let $ n $ and $ p $ be odd and distinct prime numbers.
a) Let $ Q \in \mathbf{F}_p[X] $ be the image of the cyclotomic polynomial $ \Phi_n $. Show that the number $ i $ of irreducible factors of $ Q $ is even if and only if $ p $ is a square mod $ n $. (Let $ K $ be the splitting field of $ Q $. Show that $ \mathrm{Gal}(K/\mathbf{F}_p) $ may be identified with the subgroup of $ (\mathbf{F}_n)^* $ generated by the image of $ p $. Study the parity of the index $ ((\mathbf{F}_n)^*: \mathrm{Gal}(K/\mathbf{F}_p)) $.)
b) Show that the discriminant of $ X^n - 1 \in \mathbf{F}_p[X] $ is
$$
(-1)^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2} + n-1} n^n.
$$
c) Show that $ i $ is even if and only if $ (-1)^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}} n $ is a square modp (apply En. 22 to the polynomial $ X^n - 1 $).
d) Put $ \left( \frac{n}{p} \right) = 1 $ if $ n $ is a square modp and $ \left( \phantom{\frac{n}{p}} \right) = -1 $ otherwise. Deduce from c) that
$$
\left( \frac{n}{p} \right) \left( \frac{p}{n} \right) = (-1)^{\frac{(n-1)(p-1)}{4}} \quad \text{(a Law of quadratic reciprocity »)}.
$$

§ 12

1) a) Let $ q $ be a power of a prime number. Show that for every integer $ n > 1 $, if $ h_i $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq r $) are the distinct prime divisors of the integer $ n $, then the number of elements $ \zeta \in \mathbf{F}_{q^n} $ such that $ \mathbf{F}_{q^n} = \mathbf{F}_q(\zeta) $ is equal to
$$
v = q^n - \sum_i q^{n/h_i} + \sum_{i < j} q^{n/h_i h_j} - \sum_{i < j < k} q^{n/h_i h_j h_k} + \cdots + (-1)^r q^{n/h_1 h_2 \ldots h_r}
$$
(observe that such an element is characterized by the property of not belonging to any of the fields $ \mathbf{F}_{q^{n/h_i}} $). If $ h_1 \leq \ldots \leq h_r $, we have
$$
q^n - \sum_i q^{n/h_i} \leq v \leq q^n - q^{n/h_1}.
$$
Examine the case where $ n $ is a prime power.
b) Let $ b_n(q) $ be the number of monic irreducible polynomials of degree $ n $ over $ \mathbf{F}_q $. Deduce from a) an expression for $ b_n(q) $. In particular, if $ q \geq 3 $, show that the number of polynomials (not necessarily monic) of degree $ n $ in $ \mathbf{F}_q[X] $ which are irreducible is at least equal to $ q^n(q-2)/n \geq q^{n+1}/3n $.
c) Prove that $ \sum_{d|n} d b_d(q) = q^n $ (establish the identity $ 1/(1-qT) = \prod (1-T^n)^{-b_n(q)} $).
\* Using the Mobius inversion formula (Lie, II, p. 176, Appendix), show that
$$
nb_n(q) = \sum_{d|n} \mu(n/d) q^d.
$$
Compare this result with that obtained in b).
d) Let $ a_n(q) $ be the number of monic polynomials without multiple factors in $ \mathbf{F}_q[X] $. Show that $ a_0(q) = 1,\ a_1(q) = q,\ a_n(q) = q^n - q^{n-1} $ for $ n > 1 $ (establish the identity $ q^n = \sum a_{n-2i}(q)\ q^i $).
