6) a) Let K be a field of characteristic exponent p, L an extension of K, Ω an algebraic closure of L and $ \overline{K} $ the relative algebraic closure of K in R. Show that $ \overline{K} $ and $ L^{p^{-\infty}} $ are linearly disjoint over $ E = K \cap L^{p^{-\infty}} $. (Let (a,) be a basis of the vector space $ L^{p^{-\infty}} $ over E ; consider a relation $ \sum_{\lambda} c_{\lambda} a_{\lambda} = 0 $, where c, $ \in \overline{K} $ and the number of non-zero $ c_{\lambda} $ is as small as possible, and apply an L-automorphism of $ \Omega $ to this relation.)
b) Deduce from a) that if E and F are two extensions of K such that E is algebraic over K and K is relatively algebraically closed in F, then any two composite extensions of E and F are isomorphic.

7) Let K be a field of characteristic 0, a, β, 5 three elements of an algebraic closure of K such that $ [K(\alpha):K] = 2,\ [K(\beta):K] = 3,\ [K(\xi):K] = n > 1 $; denote by a' (resp. $ \beta', \beta'' $) the unique conjugate $ \neq a $ of a (resp. the conjugates $ \neq \beta $ of β).
a) Show that $ [K(\alpha, \xi):K] = n $ if $ a \in K(\xi) $, $ [K(\alpha, 5):K] = 2n $ otherwise.
b) Show that $ [K(\beta, 5):K] = n $ if $ \beta \in K(\xi) $, $ [K(\beta, 5):K] = 2n $ if $ \beta \in K(\xi) $ and just one of $ \beta', \beta'' $ belongs to $ K(\xi) $ and finally $ [K(\beta, \xi):K] = 3n $ if none of the three elements $ \beta, \beta', \beta'' $ belongs to $ K(\xi) $.
c) If d is the discriminant of the minimal polynomial of β, show that $ K(\beta, \beta') = K(\beta, \sqrt{d}) $.
d) Show that $ K(\beta, \beta') = K(\beta - \beta') $.
e) If $ n = 2 $ and $ a \notin K(\xi) $, show that $ K(\alpha, \xi) = K(\alpha + \xi) $.
f) Show that $ K(\alpha, \beta) = K(a + \beta) $.
g) Show that $ K(\alpha, \beta) = K(\alpha \beta) $ except when $ a = aj $ and $ \beta^3 = b $, where a, b are elements of K and $ j^3 = 1 $.

8) Let K be an infinite field, E an algebraic extension of K of finite degree and τ an automorphism of the vector K-space E such that for all x $ \in E $, $ \tau(x) $ is conjugate to x over K. Show that τ is a K-automorphism of the field E. (Let N be the quasi-Galois extension of K generated by E and let Γ be the group of K-automorphisms of N, A the subgroup of Γ consisting of all E-automorphisms and $ \sigma_j $ ($ 1 \leq j \leq r $) a system of representatives of the left cosets of A. If $ (u_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n} $ is a basis of the vector K-space E, consider the polynomial

$$
F(X_1, ..., X_n) = \prod_{j=1}^r \left( \sum_{i=1}^n (\tau(u_i) - \sigma_j(u_i)) X_i \right)

1) a) Let K be a field, f a separable polynomial in $ K[X] $ and N a splitting field of f. Show that for the group Gal(N/K) to operate transitively on the set of roots of f in N it is necessary and sufficient for f to be irreducible in $ K[X] $.
b) Suppose further that f is irreducible in $ K[X] $; let x be a root of f in N. For there to exist no field E such that $ K \subset E \subset K(x) $, distinct from K and $ K(x) $ it is necessary and sufficient that Gal(N/K), qua transitive permutation group of the set of roots of f in N, should be primitive (I, p. 142, Ex. 13).
