The following proposition generalizes part b) of Prop. 13 (V, p. 113).

PROPOSITION 16. — *Let* (E_i)_{i \in I} *be a family of subextensions of an extension of a field K*, and let E be the field generated by $ \bigcup_{i \in I} E_i $.

a) *We have* $ \operatorname{tr} \cdot \deg_K E \leq \sum_{i \in I} \operatorname{tr} \cdot \deg_K E_i $, *with equality when the family* (E_i)_{i \in I} *is algebraically free over K*.

b) *Conversely, assume that* $ \operatorname{tr} \cdot \deg_K E = \sum_{i \in I} \operatorname{tr} \cdot \deg_K E_i $ *and that* $ \operatorname{tr} \cdot \deg_K E $ *is finite; then the family* (E_i)_{i \in I} *, is algebraically free over K*.

For each $ i \in I $ let B_i be a transcendence basis of E_i over K and put $ B = \bigcup_{i \in I} B_i $. For each $ i \in I $, each element of E_i is algebraic over K(B_i), hence over K(B). Now Cor. 1 of V, p. 18 shows that E is algebraic over K(B); by V, p. 109, Th. 2, B thus contains a transcendence basis of E over K. If moreover the family (E_i)_{i \in I} is algebraically free over K, then the B_i are disjoint and the set B is algebraically free over K. This establishes a) (*Set Theory*, III, p. 160, Cor. of Prop. 4).

Under the hypotheses of b), E is algebraic over K(B) and of finite transcendence degree over K, and we have Card(B) \leq \operatorname{tr} \cdot \deg_K E. By Cor. 1 of V, p. 110, B is algebraically free over K and the B_i are disjoint. Now Prop. 15 shows that the family (E_i)_{i \in I} is algebraically free over K.
