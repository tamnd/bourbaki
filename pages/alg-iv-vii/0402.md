### 9. **Jordan decomposition

**Definition 8.** — *Let $ E $ be a finite dimensional vector space over a commutative field and let $ u $ be an endomorphism of $ E $. Then a Jordan decomposition of $ u $ is a pair* $ (u_s, u_n) $, *where $ u_s $ is a semi-simple endomorphism of $ E $ and $ u_n $ is a nilpotent endomorphism of $ E $, such that* $ u_s u_n = u_n u_s $ *and* $ u = u_s + u_n $.

**Theorem 1.** — *Let $ E $ be a finite dimensional vector space over a commutative field $ K $ and let $ u $ be an endomorphism of $ E $. Then $ u $ has a Jordan decomposition* $ (u_s, u_n) $ *if and only if the eigenvalues of $ u $ are separable over $ K $. Moreover the decomposition is unique, the characteristic polynomials of $ u $ and $ u_s $ coincide, and there exist polynomials* $ P, Q \in K[X] $, *with no constant terms, such that* $ u_s = P(u) $ *and* $ u_n = Q(u) $.
