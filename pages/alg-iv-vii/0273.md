e) Let S be the group of order 3 in PGL_2(K) generated by the image of the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$. We have $S(X) = (X - T) \left( X - \frac{1}{T-1} \right) \left( X - \frac{T+1}{T-1} \right)$ and $R^s(T) = \frac{T^3 - 3T + 1}{T^2 - 1}$

f) Suppose that K is a finite field with q elements. Let U be the image in PGL_2(K) of the group of all matrices $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ b & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $b \in K$. Then

$$
U(X) = (X - T)^q - (X - T) = X^q - X - (T^q - T) \quad \text{and} \quad R^U(T) = T^q - T .
$$

Let B be the group generated by $C_{q-1}$ and U; it is of order q(q - 1). We have

$$
B(X) = (X^q - X)^{q-1} - (T^q - T)^{q-1} \quad \text{and} \quad R^H(T) = (T^q - T)^{q-1}
$$

19) Let E be a Galois extension of a field K with Galois group G. For each $x \in E$ denote by Gx the set of conjugates of x in E; this is a finite G-set. Let x, y be two elements of E. For the G-sets Gx and Gy to be isomorphic (that is, for a bijection Gx → Gy to exist, compatible with the operation of G on the two sets), it is necessary and sufficient that the extensions K(x) and K(y) should be conjugate.

20) Let E be an algebraic extension of a field K such that every non-constant polynomial in K[X] has at least one root in E. Show that E is algebraically closed. (In an algebraic closure of E, hence of K, let F be an extension of finite degree of K. To show that F ⊂ E reduce to the case where F is quasi-Galois and then, by means of Prop. 13 of V, p. 76, to the case where F is either Galois or p-radical.)

21) Let $f(X) = X^n + a_1 X^{n-1} + \ldots + a_n$ be a polynomial of Z[X] and p a prime number. Suppose that $a_i \equiv 0 \pmod{p}$, $1 \leq i \leq n$ and that $a_n \not\equiv 0 \pmod{p^2}$. Show that $f(X)$ is irreducible in Q[X]. (Let $f(X) = P(X) Q(X)$ be a decomposition in Q[X]. Show that if P and Q are monic, they have integer coefficients. Reduce modp.)
