f) Let h : E → F be a surjective homomorphism of k-modules. Show that Γ(h) is surjective and that its kernel is the ideal of Γ(E) generated by the elements γ_p(x) with p > 0 and x ∈ Ker(h).

g) Let E and F be two k-modules. Show that there exists a unique homomorphism φ of the algebra T(E × F) into the algebra Γ(E) ⊗_k Γ(F) such that

$$(φ \circ γ_p)(x, y) = \sum_{r + s = p} γ_r(x) \otimes γ_s(y)$$

for any p ∈ N, x ∈ E and y ∈ F. Show that this homomorphism is an isomorphism compatible with the graduations.

3) Let k be a commutative ring and E a k-module. Show that there exists a unique homomorphism A of the algebra Γ(E) (Exercise 2) into the algebra Γ(E) ⊗_k Γ(E) such that

$$(Δ \circ γ_p)(x) = \sum_{r + s = p} γ_r(x) \otimes γ_s(x)$$

for all x ∈ E and all p ∈ N. Show that the coproduct A is coassociative, cocommutative (III, p. 580-581) and defines on Γ(E) a graded bigebra structure (III, p. 585). Show that the homomorphism ε defined in Exercise 2, c) is a counit.

4) Let E_1 and E_2 be two modules over a commutative ring k and let h be a homomorphism of E_1 into E_2. Verify that Γ(h) (Exercise 2, e)) is a homomorphism of graded bigebras (cf. Exercise 3).

5) Let Γ(Z) be the gamma algebra of the Z-module Z (Exercise 2). For each p ∈ N put T^{(p)} = γ_p(1).
a) Show that (T^{(p)})_{p ∈ N} is a basis of the Z-module Γ(Z). Show that there exists a unique homomorphism θ of the algebra Γ(Z) into the algebra Γ(Z) ⊗ Γ(Z) such that

$$\theta(T^{(p)}) = p! \; T^{(p)} \otimes T^{(p)}$$

for all p ∈ N.
b) Let B be a commutative ring and H the set of homomorphisms of the ring Γ(Z) into the ring B. Show that for every f ∈ H the sequence f(T^{(p)}), p ∈ N is a sequence of exponential type of elements of B. Show that the mapping φ : f ↦ (f(T^{(p)}))_{p ∈ N} is a bijection of H onto 𝒟(B) (Exercise 1).
c) For f, g ∈ H denote by f \* g the homomorphism of Γ(Z) ⊗ Γ(Z) into B defined by (f \* g)(u ⊗ v) = f(u)g(v) for any u, v ∈ Γ(Z). Verify that for any f, g ∈ H we have

$$
\begin{align*}
φ(f) + φ(g) &= φ((f * g) \circ Δ), \\
φ(f) φ(g) &= φ((f * g) \circ θ).
\end{align*}
$$

6) Let n be an integer > 0 and let Γ(Z/nZ) be the gamma algebra of the Z-module Z/nZ (Exercise 2). Show that for every p > 0, Γ_p(Z/nZ) is isomorphic to the quotient of Z by the subgroup generated by the integers n^k((k, p - k)), where 1 ≤ k ≤ p. Show that if p and n are distinct primes, then Γ_p(Z/nZ) is cyclic of order n. Show that if n is even, then Γ_2(Z/nZ) is cyclic of order 2n.
