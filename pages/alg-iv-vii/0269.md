EXERCISES

2) Let K be a field, $ f_0 $ an irreducible and separable polynomial in $ K[X] $, of degree n ; let $ \alpha_i $ ($ 1 \leq i \leq n $) be its roots in a splitting field N of $ f_0 $. Let F be the field of rational fractions $ N(X_1, X_2, ..., X_n) $, E the subfield $ K(X_1, X_2, ..., X_n) $ of F ; F is a Galois extension of E and $ \mathrm{Gal}(E/F) $ is canonically isomorphic to $ \mathrm{Gal}(N/K) $ (V, p. 71, Th. 5 ; V, p. 154, Ex. 8). Show that if $ 8 = \alpha_1 X_1 + \alpha_2 X_2 + ... + \alpha_n X_n $ then $ F = E(8) $ and the minimal polynomial $ g_1 $ of 8 in $ E[T] $ is an irreducible factor of the polynomial

$$
f(T) = \prod_{\sigma \in S_n} (T - \alpha_1 X_{\sigma(1)} - \alpha_2 X_{\sigma(2)} - \cdots - \alpha_n X_{\sigma(n)})
$$

Each permutation $ \sigma \in S_n $, defines a K-automorphism of E, again denoted by a, by the condition $ \sigma(X_i) = X_{\sigma(i)} $ for $ 1 \leq i \leq n $; show that $ \mathrm{Gal}(N/K) $ is isomorphic to the subgroup of $ S_n $, consisting of permutations a such that $ \sigma(g_1) = g_1 $. Further, if $ f = g_1 g_2 ... g_r $ is a decomposition of f into irreducible factors in $ E[T] $, show that for every index j such that $ 1 \leq j \leq r $ there exists a permutation $ \sigma_j \in S_n $, such that $ \sigma_j(g_1) = g_j $.

3) Let N be a Galois extension of a field K of infinite degree over K. Show that the cardinal of the Galois group $ \mathrm{Gal}(N/K) $ is greater than that of the set of subsets of N (use V, p. 61 and 62). Deduce that there exist non-closed subgroups of $ \mathrm{Gal}(N/K) $.

4) Let N be a Galois extension of a field K, $ (E_i)_{i \in I} $ a family of subextensions of N, Galois over K, such that : 1" for each index i, if $ F_i $ is the field generated by the $ E_i $, for $ j \neq i $, then $ E_i \cap F_i = K $; 2" N is generated by the union of the $ E_i $.
a) Show that N is isomorphic to the tensor product $ \bigotimes_{i \in I} E_i $ (III, p. 470) (define an isomorphism of this tensor product onto N, using Th. 5 of V, p. 71).
b) Show that $ \mathrm{Gal}(N/K) $ is isomorphic to the product of the topological groups $ \mathrm{Gal}(E_i/K) $.

5) Show that every compact totally disconnected group is isomorphic to the group $ \mathrm{Gal}(N/K) $ of a Galois extension of a field (using Gen. Top. III, p. 325, Ex. 3, reduce to the case where the group is a product of finite groups and use Ex. 4 as well as V, p. 59, Example 5).

6) Let N be a Galois extension of a field K, of infinite degree, and let $ \rho, a $ be two distinct elements of $ \mathrm{Gal}(N/K) $. If J is the set of $ x \in N $ such that $ \rho(x) \neq \sigma(x) $, show that $ K(J) $ is an extension of K of infinite degree. (Observe that a field cannot be the union of two of its subfields unless it is equal to one of them.)

7) Let N be a Galois extension of a field K such that $ \mathrm{Gal}(N/K) $ is isomorphic to the symmetric group $ S_n $ (V, p. 59, Example 5), n being a non-prime integer ; identify $ \mathrm{Gal}(N/K) $ and $ S_n $. Let $ \Gamma_1 $ be the subgroup of $ S_n $ of order $ (n-1)! $ leaving the number 1 invariant and let $ \Gamma_2 $ be the cyclic subgroup of $ S_n $ of order n generated by the cycle $ (1, 2, ..., n) $ (I, p. 142, Ex. 12). Let $ E_1, E_2 $ be the field of invariants of the subgroups $ \Gamma_1, \Gamma_2 $ respectively. Show that $ E_1 $ and $ E_2 $ are linearly disjoint over K, that there exists no field F other than K and $ E_1 $ such that $ K \subset F \subset E_1 $ but that there exist fields $ F' $ distinct from $ E_2 $ and N such that $ E_2 \subset F' \subset N $ (use Ex. 13 of I, p. 142 and Ex. 14, I, p. 143).
