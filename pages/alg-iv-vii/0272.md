16) Let a be an integer and let x₁, x₂, x₃, x₄ be the roots of P(X) = X⁴ − aX − 1 in an algebraic closure of Q. We identify the Galois group G of P with a group of permutations of {x₁, ..., x₄}. Suppose that Q(x₁) contains no quadratic subextension (cf. Ex. 1 of V, p. 154).
a) Show that G = S₄ or A₄. (Use Ex. 1 of V, p. 154: P is irreducible, hence G acts transitively; further Q(x₁) contains no quadratic subextensions, hence the stabilizer H of x₁ is not contained in a subgroup of index 2 of G, whereas [G : H] = 4; deduce that G is not a 2-group, hence its order is divisible by 3.)
b) Let 6 = $\prod_{1 \leq i < j \leq 4} (x_i - x_j)$ and d = δ². Show that d = 4⁴ − 3³ a⁴; further s(δ) = εₛδ for all s ∈ G. Deduce that G = A₄ if δ ∈ Q, and G = G, otherwise. Show that 6 is not rational. (We have thus constructed an infinite family of equations of degree 4 over Q with Galois group S₄.)

18) Let K be a field and F the field of rational fractions K(T). The group GL₂(K) operates on F by the law (yf)(T) = f $\left( \frac{aT + b}{cT + d} \right)$, for y = $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$ ∈ GL₂(K) and f ∈ F. The centre K* . I of GL₂(K) operates trivially, whence we obtain, by passage to quotients, a homomorphism of PGL₂(K) = GL₂(K)/K* . I into the automorphism group of the extension F of K. This homomorphism is bijective (V, p. 148, Ex. 11).
a) Let H be a subgroup of PGL₂(K) of finite order h, and let E be the field of invariants of H, so that F = E(T) is a Galois extension of E with Galois group H (V, p. 66, Th. 3). Let
$$
H(X) = \prod_{s \in H} (X - s(T)) = \sum_{i=0}^{h} (-1)^i S_i(T) X^{h-i}
$$
be the minimal polynomial of T over E. For each i such that 0 ≤ i ≤ h, either S_i(T) ∈ K or E = K(S_i(T)). (Use Ex. 11, a) of V, p. 157.)
b) For each s ∈ H let $\begin{pmatrix} a_s & b_s \\ c_s & d_s \end{pmatrix}$ be a representative in GL₂(K) and let H₀ be the subgroup of H consisting of those s for which cₛ = 0; let h₀ = Card H₀ and put R^H(T) = S_{h₀}(T). Show that E = K(R^H(T)) and that R^H(T) = P(T)/Q(T)^{h₀} where P is a polynomial of degree h and Q(T) = $\prod_s (c_s T + d_s)$ where s runs over a system of representatives of the cosets H₀s different from H₀; the degree of Q is thus (h/h₀) − 1.
c) Suppose that K* contains a subgroup μₙ of finite order n. Let C, be the image in PGL₂(K) of the subgroup of GL₂(K) consisting of the matrices $\begin{pmatrix} a & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, a ∈ μₙ. Then C, (X) = Xⁿ − Tⁿ and R^{Cₙ}(T) = (−1)^{n+1} Tⁿ.
d) Let w be the element of PGL₂(K) represented by $\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. Then w² = 1 and wsw⁻¹ = s⁻¹ if s is represented by a diagonal matrix. The subgroup Dₙ of PGL₂(K) generated by Cₙ and w is therefore of order 2n.
