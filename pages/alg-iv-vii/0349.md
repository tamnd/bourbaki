Deduce that $ A $ is isomorphic to a subring $ A' $ of a product $ \prod A_i $, such that each $ A_i $ is lattice ordered, $ \operatorname{pr}_i(A') = A_i $, for all $ i $ and the ideal $ \{0\} $ is irreducible in $ A $, (as a convex ideal) for all $ i $.
b) Show that the following conditions are equivalent in $ A $:
α) $ |xy| = |x| \cdot |y| $ for all $ x, y $;
β) $ x \cdot \sup(y, z) = \sup(xy, xz) $ for all $ x \geqslant 0, y, z $;
γ) $ x \cdot \inf(y, z) = \inf(xy, xz) $ for all $ x \geqslant 0, y, z $;
δ) $ \inf(y, z) = 0 $ implies $ \inf(xy, z) = 0 $ for all $ x \geqslant 0 $.
(To see that γ) implies δ), observe that $ xy \leqslant y \cdot \sup(x, 1) $).
When these conditions are satisfied, then $ A $ is said to be strongly *lattice* ordered.
