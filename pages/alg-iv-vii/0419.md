$$
x_0 = (1, 0), \quad x_1 = (0, 1), \quad x_{n+1} = p_n^{-1}(x_0 + x_n) \quad \text{for } n \geq 1.
$$

Show that $M$ has rank 2, and that every rank 1 submodule of $M$ is cyclic (notice that $x_0$ and $x_n$ form a basis for the submodule of $M$ generated by the $x_i$ with $i \leq n$).

4) Let $E$ be a torsion-free module over a principal ideal domain $A$.
a) Show that, if $F$ is a pure submodule of $E$ (VII, p. 55, Ex. 7), then $E/F$ is torsion-free, and conversely.
b) Let $F$ be a submodule of $E$ generated by a maximal pseudofree family (VII, p. 55, Ex. 4); then $F$ is a pure, free submodule of $E$. Show that, for all $1 \in E/F$, there exist a noninvertible element $a$ of $A$ and an element $y' \in E/F$ such that $x = a y'$, but that $E/F$ is not necessarily a divisible module (cf. Ex. 3).

5) Let $A$ be a principal ideal domain having a unique maximal ideal $A \pi$ (cf. VII, p. 48, Ex. 4). Let $E$ be a torsion-free $A$-module, and $M$ a free submodule of $E$, such that $E/M$ is torsion-free, divisible and of rank 1. Let $(e_\alpha)$ be a basis of $M$, let $u$ be a nonzero element of $E/M$, and $u$ an element of the coset $u$; for each integer $n > 0$, let $u_n$ be an element of $E$ such that $u \equiv \pi^n u_n \pmod{M}$. Put $u = \pi^n u_n + \sum \lambda_{n\alpha} e_\alpha$.

a) Show that $M$ is generated by $M$ and the $u_n$, and that $A_\alpha - A_{\alpha-1,\alpha} \in A n^n$ for all $\alpha$ and for every integer $n > 0$ (cf. Ex. 4, a)).
b) Suppose in addition that $A$ is complete with respect to the topology $\mathcal{T}$ defined by taking the ideals $A n^n$ as a basis of open neighbourhoods of $0$ in $A$ (Gen. Top., III, p. 5); put $A_\alpha = \lim_{n \to \infty} A_{\alpha-1,\alpha}$. Show that, if $\mu$ and $\mu_\alpha$ are such that $\mu u - \sum \mu_\alpha e_\alpha$ belongs to all the submodules $\pi^n E$, then necessarily $\mu_\alpha = \lambda_\alpha \mu$ for all $\alpha$.
