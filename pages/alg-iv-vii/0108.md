6) Let $n$ be an integer $\geq 1$, and let $X_1, \ldots, X_n$ be indeterminates. For $1 \leq j \leq n$ let $s_j$ be the elementary symmetric polynomial of degree $j$ in the $X_i$ and $u_j = (-1)^{j+1} s_j$. Put

$$
