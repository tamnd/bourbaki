6) Let G be an ordered group, let P be its set of positive elements. Show that P – P is the largest filtered subgroup of G, and that it is convex. What is the order relation on the quotient?

7) In the group Z, if P is taken to be the set consisting of 0 and the integers ≥ 2, then the resulting ordered group is filtered but not lattice ordered (show that the set of elements x such that x ≥ 0 and x ≥ 1 has two distinct minimal elements).

8) Let x be an element of an ordered group such that y = inf(x, 0) is defined; if n > 0 is an integer, then nx ≥ 0 implies x ≥ 0 (we' have

$$
ny = \inf(nx, (n-1)x, ..., 0) \geq \inf((n-1)x, ..., 0) = (n-1)y;
$$

and hence nx = 0 implies x = 0.

9) In a lattice ordered group G, show that the sum of a family (H,) of filtered convex subgroups is a filtered convex subgroup (use VI, p. 12, Cor.).

10) Show that, for every finite sequence (x,) (1 ≤ i ≤ n) of elements of a lattice ordered group G,

$$
\sup(x_i) = \sum_i x_i - \sum_{i < j} \inf(x_i, x_j) + \cdots + (-1)^{p+1} \sum_{i_1 < i_2 < ... < i_p} \inf(x_{i_1}, ..., x_{i_p}) + \cdots + (-1)^{n+1}\inf(x_1, ..., x_n).
$$

(Argue by induction based on Prop. 7 (VI, p. 10), using the distributivity of sup over inf.)

11) Let (x,) be a family of n elements of a lattice ordered group G; for each integer k (0 ≤ k ≤ n), let d_k (resp. m_k) denote the inf (resp. sup) of the $\binom{n}{k}$ sums of k terms x, with distinct indices. Show that $d_k + m_{n-k} = x_1 + x_2 + \ldots + x_n$.

12) Given a lattice ordered group G, a subgroup H of G is called a colattice if for each pair x, y of elements of H, $\sup_G(x, y) \in H$ (and so equals $\sup_H(x, y)$).
a) If $G = \mathbf{Q} \times \mathbf{Q} \times \mathbf{Q}$ (where Q has the usual ordering), then the subgroup H of (x, y, z) such that z = x + y is a lattice but not a colattice.
b) Every filtered convex (Ex. 4) subgroup H of a lattice ordered group G is a colattice.
c) Let G be a lattice ordered group and H an arbitrary subgroup of G. Define H' to be the set of infima of finite subsets of H, and H" to be the set of suprema of finite subsets of H'. Show that H" is the smallest colattice of G containing H (use the remark on VI, p. 16).

13) a) A monoid M is said to be lower semi-lattice ordered (or simply semi-lattice ordered) if it is an ordered monoid, if inf(x, y) exists for all x, y ∈ M, and if

$$
\inf(x + z, y + z) = \inf(x, y) + z
$$

for all x, y, z ∈ M. Prove the identities:

$$
\inf(x, z) + \inf(y, z) = \inf(x + y, z + \inf(x, y, z))
$$
$$
\inf(x, y, z) + \inf(x + y, y + z, z + x) = \inf(x, y) + \inf(y, z) + \inf(z, x).
$$
