2) Show that the number of monic polynomials of degree $ n $ of $ \mathbf{F}_q[X] $ which have no root in $ \mathbf{F}_q $ is $ q^{n-q}(q-1)^q $ for $ n \geq q $ (observe that every function $ \mathbf{F}_q \to \mathbf{F}_q $ is a polynomial function).

3) Let K be a finite field of q elements ; for every prime number l let N, be the union of the extensions of K (contained in an algebraic closure $ \Omega $ of K) whose degree is a power of l. Show that N, is an abelian extension of K whose Galois group is isomorphic to the group $ \mathbf{Z}_l $ of l-adic integers. Deduce that $ \Omega $ is isomorphic to the tensor product $ \bigotimes_l N_l $, and hence, that $ \mathrm{Gal}(\Omega/K) $ is isomorphic to the product $ \prod_l \mathbf{Z}_l $.

5) Let K be a finite field of q elements where q is not a power of 2, and let a,, a,, b be three elements of K such that $ a_1a_2 \neq 0 $. Show that the number $ \nu $ of solutions $ (x,, x_2) \in K^2 $ of the equation $ a_1x_1^2 + a_2x_2^2 = b $ is given by the following formulae :
1" if $ b = 0 $ and $ -a_1a_2 $ is not a square in K, $ \nu = 1 $;
2" if $ b \neq 0 $ and $ -a_1a_2 $ is not a square in K, $ \nu = q + 1 $;
3" if $ b = 0 $ and $ -a_1a_2 $ is a square in K, $ \nu = 2q - 1 $;
4" if $ b \neq 0 $ and $ -a_1a_2 $ is a square in K, $ \nu = q - 1 $.
(When $ -a_1a_2 $ is not a square, adjoin to K a root of $ X^2 + a_1a_2 $ and use the fact that the multiplicative group of a finite field is cyclic.)

6) Let K be a finite field of q elements where q is not a power of 2.
a) Let a,, a,, ..., a,, b be elements of K such that $ a_1a_2 ... a_n \neq 0 $. Show that the number $ \nu $ of solutions $ (x,, ..., x_n) \in K^{2m} $ of the equation $ a_1x_1^2 + \cdots + a_{2m}x_{2m}^2 = b $ is given by the following formulae :
1" if $ b = 0 $ and $ (-1)^ma_1a_2 ... a_n $ is not a square in K, $ \nu = q^{2m-1} - q^m + q^{m-1} $;
2" if $ b \neq 0 $ and $ (-1)^ma_1a_2 ... a_n $ is not a square in K, $ \nu = q^{2m-1} + q^{m-1} $;
3" if $ b = 0 $ and $ (-1)^ma_1a_2 ... a_n $ is a square in K, $ \nu = q^{2m-1} + q^m - q^m $;
4" if $ b \neq 0 $ and $ (-1)^ma_1a_2 ... a_n $ is a square in K, $ \nu = q^{2m-1} - q^{m-1} $.
(Argue by induction on m, using Ex. 5.)
b) Let a,, a,, ..., a,+,, b be elements of K such that $ a_1a_2 ... a_{+} \neq 0 $. Show that the number $ \nu $ of solutions $ (x,, x_2, ..., x_{2m-1}) \in K^{2m+1} $ of the equation
$$
a_1x_1^2 + \cdots + a_{2m+1}x_{2m+1}^2 = b
$$
is given by the following formulae :
1" if $ b = 0 $, $ \nu = q^{2m} $;
2" if $ b \neq 0 $ and $ (-1)^ma_1a_2 ... a_{2m-1}b $ is not a square in K, $ \nu = q^{2m} - q^m $;
3" if $ b \neq 0 $ and $ (-1)^ma_1a_2 ... a_{2m+1}b $ is a square in K, $ \nu = q^{2m} + q^m $. (Reduce to the case a.).

Let $ \tau $ be an automorphism of the vector K-space E such that for each $ x \in E $, $ \tau(x) $ is conjugate to x over E (cf. V, p. 154, Ex. 8).
a) Show that if K has at least 3 elements, $ \tau $ is a K-automorphism of the field E (reduce to the case where $ \tau(\alpha_1) = \alpha_1 $ and consider $ \tau(a, + c\alpha_i) $ for $ c \in K $).
