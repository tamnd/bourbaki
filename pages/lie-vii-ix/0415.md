$a)$ Show that the interval $1, \nu (R)$ in $\mathbf{N}$ is the union of 1 and the $n^{\vee}_{\beta}$ for $\beta \in B$ (put $\alpha_0=\alpha$; let $(\alpha_1, . . . , \alpha_q)$ be a sequence of distinct elements of B such that $\alpha_i$ is not orthogonal to $\alpha_{i-1}$ for $i= 1, . . . , q$, such that $n^{\vee}_{\alpha}=\nu (R)$ and such that $q$ is maximal with these properties; prove that $n^{\vee}_{\alpha_i}=^{^q}i+ 1$ for $i= 1, . . . , q)$.

$b)$ Let $p$ be a prime number. Show that the following three properties are equivalent:

(i) $p\leq \nu (R)$;

(ii) there exists $\beta \in B$ such that $p=n^{\vee}_{\beta}$;

(iii) there exists $\beta \in B$ such that $p$ divides $n^{\vee}_{\beta}$.

In this case $p$ is called a torsion prime number of R.

$c)$ For each type of irreducible root system, give the value of $\nu (R)$ and the torsion prime numbers. Show that the set of the $n_{\beta}$ and that of the $n^{\vee}_{\beta}$ coincide except for type $G_2$.

$d)$ Let $R'$ be a symmetric closed subset of R that is irreducible as a root system. Show that $\nu (R')\leq \nu (R)$.

10) Let R be a root system and $p$ a prime number. Show that the following properties are equivalent:

(i) $p$ is a torsion prime number of an irreducible component of R (Exerc. $9b))$; (ii) there exists a symmetric closed subset $R_1$ of R, distinct from R and maximal for these properties, such that $(Q(R^{\vee}) : Q(R^{\vee}_1)) =p(Q(R^{\vee})$ denotes the $\mathbf{Z}$-module generated by the inverse roots of R and $Q(R^{\vee}_1)$ the submodule generated by the $\alpha^{\vee}$ for $\alpha \in R_1)$;

(iii) there exists a symmetric closed subset $R_1$ of R such that the $p$-torsion submodule of $Q(R^{\vee})/Q(R^{\vee}_1)$ is non-zero.

(To prove that (i) $=\Rightarrow$ (ii), use Exerc. 4 of Chap. VI, $§4$; to prove that (iii) $=\Rightarrow$ (i), use Exerc. $9d).)$

In this case $p$ is said to be a torsion prime number of R.

11) Let $p$ be a prime number. Then $p$ is said to be a torsion prime number of G if there exists a connected closed subgroup H of G, of maximum rank, such that the $p$-torsion subgroup of $\pi_1(H)$ is non-zero. Put $R = R(G,T)$.

$a)$ The torsion prime numbers of G are the torsion prime numbers of R (Exerc. 10) and the prime numbers that divide the order of the torsion group of $\pi_1(G)$.

$b)$ Show that every torsion prime number of G divides $w/l$!, where $w$ is the order of W and $l$ the rank of the semi-simple group D(G) (use Chap. VI, $§2$, no. 4, Prop. 7).

$c)$ Let $t\in T$ and $n\in \mathbf{N}$ be such that $t^n\in C(G)$. Let $R_1$ be the set of $\alpha \in R$ such that $\alpha (t) = 1$, and $m$ the order of the torsion group of $Q(R^{\vee})/Q(R^{\vee}_1)$ (cf. Exerc. 10). Show that every prime factor of $m$ divides $n$ (reduce to the case in which G is simply-connected and almost simple).
