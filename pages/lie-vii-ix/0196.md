DEFINITION 3. Let E be a $\mathfrak{g}$-module. A lattice $\mathscr{E}$ in E is said to be admissible (relative to $\mathscr{G})$ if the following conditions are satisfied:

(i) $\mathscr{U}$ maps $\mathscr{E}$ to $\mathscr{E}$;

(ii) $\mathscr{E}$ is stable under $(^h_n)$ and $x^{(n)}$ for all $\alpha \in R, x\in \mathscr{G}^{\alpha}, n\in \mathbf{N}, h\in \mathscr{H}$.

3) Let E be a finite dimensional $\mathfrak{g}$-module, $\mathscr{E}$ an admissible lattice in E, $\mathfrak{c}$ the centre of $\mathfrak{g}$.
