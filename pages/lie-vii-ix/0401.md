1) Let G be a compact Lie group of dimension $>0$. Then every finite commutative subgroup of G is cyclic if and only if G is isomorphic to $\mathbf{U},\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}(2,\mathbf{C})$ or the normalizer of a maximal torus in $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}(2,\mathbf{C})$.

2) Denote by K one of the fields $\mathbf{R},\mathbf{C}$ or $\mathbf{H}$, and by $n$ an integer $\geq 1$. Give the space $K^n_s$ the usual hermitian form.

a) Show that $\mathbf{U}(n,K)$ is a compact real Lie group.

b) Show that the sphere of radius 1 in $K^n_s$ is a (real) Lie homogeneous space for $\mathbf{U}(n,K)$; the fixer of a point is isomorphic to $\mathbf{U}(n-1,K)$.

c) Deduce that the groups $\mathbf{U}(n,\mathbf{C})$ and $\mathbf{U}(n,\mathbf{H})$ are connected, and that $\mathbf{O}(n,\mathbf{R})$ has two connected components.

d) Show that the groups $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}(n,\mathbf{R})$ and $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}(n,\mathbf{C})$ are connected.

e) Show that the group $\mathbf{O}(n,\mathbf{R})$ (resp. $\mathbf{U}(n,\mathbf{C})$) is the semi-direct product of $\mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z}$ (resp. $\mathbf{T}$) by $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}(n,\mathbf{R})$ (resp. $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}(n,\mathbf{C})$).

$3)a)$ Show that the Lie algebra of the real Lie group $\mathbf{U}(n,\mathbf{H})$ is the set of matrices $x\in M_n(\mathbf{H})$ such that $^t\overline{x}=-x$, with the bracket $[x, y] =xy-yx$. Denote it by $\mathfrak{u}(n,\mathbf{H})$.

b) Identify $\mathbf{C}$ with the subfield $\mathbf{R}(i)$ of $\mathbf{H}$, and $\mathbf{C}^{2n}$ with $\mathbf{H}^n$ by the isomorphism $(z_1, . . . , z_{2n}) \rightarrow (z_1+jz_{n+1}, . . . , z_n+jz_{2n})$. Prove the equality $\mathbf{U}(n,\mathbf{H}) =\mathbf{U}(2n,\mathbf{C})\cap \mathbf{S}\mathbf{p}(2n,\mathbf{C})$.

c) Deduce from b) that every compact simple (real) Lie algebra of type $C_n$ is isomorphic to $\mathfrak{u}(n,\mathbf{H})$.
