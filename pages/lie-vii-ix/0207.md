We now argue by induction on $l$, and assume that $l\geq r\geq 3$. We remark first of all that if W is a non-isotropic subspace of V of odd dimension, with orthogonal complement $W'$, the restriction $\Psi_W$ of $\Psi$ to W is non-degenerate and $\mathfrak{o}(\Psi_W)$ can be identified with the subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}$ consisting of the elements vanishing on $W'$. If dim $W<$ dim V, and if $\Psi_W$ is of maximal index, the induction hypothesis implies that if $T\bigwedge_r$ contains a non-zero element of$\bigwedge$ the form $w'\wedge \bigwedge w$, with $w'\in^{r-k}W'$ and $w\in^kW (0\leq k\leq r)$, then $T_r$ contains $w'\wedge^kW:$ indeed, we have $a.(w'\wedge w) =w'\wedge a.w$ for all $a\in \mathfrak{o}(\Psi_W)$.
