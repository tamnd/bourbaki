6) Assume that $k=\mathbf{R}$ or $\mathbf{C}$. Let G be the Lie group Aut$_0(\mathfrak{g})$. Show that an element $a\in G$ is regular (in the sense of Chap. VII, $§4$, no. 2) if and only if it satisfies the conditions of Exerc. $5a)$.

7) Assume that $\mathfrak{g}$ is splittable. Let $B(\mathfrak{g})$ be the canonical basis of the canonical Cartan subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}$ (no. 3, Remark 2). If $s\in$ Aut($\mathfrak{g}$)$,s$ induces a permutation of $B(\mathfrak{g}\bigwedge)$; denote by sgn($s$) the sign of this permutation. Show that $s$ operates on $^n\mathfrak{g}$ (with $n=$ dim $\mathfrak{g})$ by

$x \rightarrow$ sgn($s$)$.x$.

$\P 8)$ Let ¯$k$ be an algebraic closure of $k$, and ¯$\mathfrak{g}=$ ¯$k\otimes_k\mathfrak{g}$. The Galois group Gal(¯$k/k)$ operates naturally on ¯$\mathfrak{g}$, on the canonical Cartan subalgebra of ¯$\mathfrak{g}$ (no. 3, Remark 2), as well as on its root system ¯R and its canonical basis ¯B. We thus obtain a continuous homomorphism (i.e. with open kernel)

$\pi :$ Gal(¯$k/k)\rightarrow$ Aut( ¯$R,B)$¯.

Show that $\mathfrak{g}$ is splittable if and only if the following two conditions are satisfied:

(i) The homomorphism $\pi$ is trivial.

(ii) $\mathfrak{g}$ has a Borel subalgebra $\mathfrak{b}$.

(Show that a Cartan subalgebra $\mathfrak{h}$ contained in $\mathfrak{b}$ is splitting if and only if $\pi$ is trivial.)

9) Let R be a reduced root system, B a basis of R, and $(\mathfrak{g}_0,\mathfrak{h}_0,B,(X_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in B})$ a corresponding framed semi-simple Lie algebra $(§4)$. Let ¯$k$ be an algebraic closure of $k$, and $\rho :$ Gal(¯$k/k)\rightarrow$ Aut(R$,B)$ a continuous homomorphism (cf. Exerc. 8); if $\sigma \in$ Gal(¯$k/k)$, denote by $\rho_{\sigma}$ the $k$-linear automorphism of $\mathfrak{g}$¯$_0=$ ¯$k\otimes_k\mathfrak{g}_0$ such that $\rho_{\sigma}(X_{\alpha}) =X_{\rho(\sigma)\alpha}$. On the other hand, the natural operation of Gal(¯$k/k)$ on ¯$k$ can be extended to an operation on ¯$\mathfrak{g}_0$. Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be the subset of ¯$\mathfrak{g}_0$ consisting of the elements $x$ such that $\rho_{\sigma}(x) =\sigma^{-1}.x$ for all $\sigma \in$ Gal(¯$k/k)$.

$a)$ Show that $\mathfrak{g}$ is a $k$-Lie subalgebra of ¯$\mathfrak{g}_0$, and that the injection of $\mathfrak{g}$ into ¯$\mathfrak{g}_0$ extends to an isomorphism from ¯$k\otimes_k\mathfrak{g}$ to ¯$\mathfrak{g}_0$. In particular, $\mathfrak{g}$ is semi-simple.

$b)$ Let $\mathfrak{b}_0$ be the subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}_0$ generated by $\mathfrak{h}_0$ and the $X_{\alpha}$. Put

¯$\mathfrak{b}_0=$ ¯$k\otimes_k\mathfrak{b}_0,\mathfrak{h}=\mathfrak{g}\cap$ ¯$\mathfrak{b}_0$, ¯$\mathfrak{h}_0=$ ¯$k\otimes_k\mathfrak{h}_0,\mathfrak{h}=\mathfrak{g}\cap$ ¯$\mathfrak{h}_0$.

Show that ¯$k\otimes_k\mathfrak{b}=$ ¯$\mathfrak{b}_0$ and that ¯$k\otimes_k\mathfrak{h}=$ ¯$\mathfrak{h}_0$, so that $\mathfrak{b}$ is a Borel subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}$ and $\mathfrak{h}$ is a Cartan subalgebra contained in it.
