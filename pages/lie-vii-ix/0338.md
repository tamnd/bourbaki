### 4. THE MAPS (G$\boldsymbol{/}$T) $\times \mathbf{T}\rightarrow$ G AND (G$\boldsymbol{/}$T) $\times \mathbf{A}\rightarrow \mathbf{G}_{\boldsymbol{r}}$

The map $(g, t) \rightarrow gtg^{-1}$ from $G\times T$ to G induces by passage to the quotient a morphism of analytic manifolds

$$
f: (G/T)\times T\rightarrow G
$$

which is surjective (§2, no. 2, Th. 2). By restriction, $f$ induces a surjective morphism

$$
f_r: (G/T)\times T_r\rightarrow G_r
$$

By composition with Id$_{G/T}\times$ exp$_T$, we obtain morphisms, also surjective,

$$
\varphi : (G/T)\times \mathfrak{t}\rightarrow G
$$

$$
\varphi_r: (G/T)\times \mathfrak{t}_r\rightarrow G_r
$$

finally, if A is an alcove of $\mathfrak{t},\varphi_r$ induces a surjective morphism

$$
\varphi_A: (G/T)\times A\rightarrow G_r
$$

We define a right operation of W on $G/T$ as follows: let $w\in W$ and $u\in G/T$; lift $w$ to an element $n$ of $N_G(T)$ and $u$ to an element $g$ of G. Then the image of $gn$ in $G/T$ does not depend on the choice of $n$ and $g$; we denote it by $u.w$.

For this operation, W operates freely on $G/T:$ indeed, with the preceding notations, assume that $u.w=u$; then $gn\in gT$, so $n\in T$ and $w= 1$.

We define a right operation of W on $(G/T)\times T$ by

$$
(u, t).w= (u.w, w^{-1}(t)),u\in G/T, t\in T, w\in W
$$

and a right operation of $W'_a$ on $(G/T)\times \mathfrak{t}$ by

$$
(u, x).\omega = (u.\omega , \omega^{-1}(x)),u\in G/T, x\in \mathfrak{t}, \omega \in W'_a
$$

where $\overline{\omega}$ is the image of $\omega$ in the quotient $W'_a/\Gamma (T) = W$.

If A is an alcove of $\mathfrak{t}$, and if $H_A$ is the subgroup of $W'_a$ that stabilizes A, we obtain by restriction an operation of $H_A$ on $(G/T)\times A$.

These different operations are compatible with the morphisms $f, \varphi$ and $\varphi_A:$ for $u\in G/T, t\in T, x\in \mathfrak{t}, y\in A, w\in W, \omega \in W'_a, h\in H_A$, we have

$$
f((u, t).w) =f(u, t), \varphi ((u, x).\omega ) =\varphi (u, x), \varphi_A((u, y).h) =\varphi_A(u, y)
$$
