PROPOSITION 1. Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a Lie algebra, $\mathfrak{n}$ a subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}$ such that ad$_{\mathfrak{g}}x$ is nilpotent for all $x\in \mathfrak{n}$. Then $e^{ad_{\mathfrak{g}}\mathfrak{n}}$ is a subgroup of Aut$_e(\mathfrak{g})$.

This follows immediately from Lemma 1 (i).

In particular, if $\mathfrak{n}$ is the nilpotent radical of $\mathfrak{g},e^{ad_{\mathfrak{g}}\mathfrak{n}}$ is the group of special automorphisms of $\mathfrak{g}$ (Chap. $I,§6$, no. 8, Def. 6).
