a) Denote by $a(\mathfrak{g})$ the smallest algebraic subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}\mathfrak{l}(V)$ containing $\mathfrak{g}$. Then

$$
a(\mathfrak{g})\supset e(\mathfrak{g})\supset \mathfrak{g}
$$

Give an example where $a(\mathfrak{g})$ and $e(\mathfrak{g})$ are distinct (take V of dimension 2 and $\mathfrak{g}$ of dimension 1).

b) Show that, if $\mathfrak{n}$ is an ideal of $\mathfrak{g},\mathfrak{n}$ and $a(\mathfrak{n})$ are ideals of $a(\mathfrak{g})$, and $[a(\mathfrak{g}), a(\mathfrak{n})] = [\mathfrak{g},\mathfrak{n}]$ (imitate the proof of Prop. 4). Deduce that $\mathscr{D}^ia(\mathfrak{g}) =\mathscr{D}^i\mathfrak{g}$ for $i\geq 1$ and that $\mathscr{C}^ia(\mathfrak{g}) =\mathscr{C}^i\mathfrak{g}$ for $i\geq 2$.

c) Show that every Lie algebra consisting of nilpotent elements is algebraic.$^8$

$\P 6)$ Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a semi-simple subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}\mathfrak{l}$(V), $\mathbf{T}(V) =\bigoplus_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathbf{T}^n(V)$ the

tensor algebra of $V,\mathbf{T}(V)^{\mathfrak{g}}$ the set of elements of $\mathbf{T}(V)$ invariant under $\mathfrak{g}$ (cf. Chap. III, App.) and $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}$ the set of $u\in \mathfrak{g}\mathfrak{l}(V)$ such that $u.x= 0$ for all $x\in \mathbf{T}(V)^{\mathfrak{g}}$. We are going to show that $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}=\mathfrak{g}$.

a) Show that the representation of $\mathfrak{g}$ on the dual $V^*$ of V is isomorphic to its representation on $\bigwedge^{p-1}V$, where $p=$ dim V (use the fact that $\mathfrak{g}$ is contained in $\mathfrak{s}\mathfrak{l}(V)$). Deduce that every element of $\mathbf{T}_{n,m}=\mathbf{T}^n(V)\otimes \mathbf{T}^m(V^*)$ invariant under $\mathfrak{g}$ is invariant under $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}$, and that $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}$ is algebraic (Exerc. 5).

b) Let W be a vector subspace of one of the $\mathbf{T}_{n,m}$. Assume that W is stable under $\mathfrak{g}$. Show that W is stable under $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}$ (if $e_1, . . . , e_r$ is a basis of W, remark that $e_1\wedge  \cdots  \wedge e_r$ is invariant under $\mathfrak{g}$, and hence under $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}$).

Deduce that $\mathfrak{g}$ is an ideal of $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}$, and that $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}/\mathfrak{g}$ is commutative (cf. proof of Prop. 4). We have $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}=\mathfrak{g}\times \mathfrak{c}$, where $\mathfrak{c}$ is the centre of $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}$.

c) Let R be the associative subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}\mathfrak{l}(V)$ generated by 1 and $\mathfrak{g}$. Show that $\mathfrak{c}$ is contained in the centre of R (remark that $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}$ is contained in the bicommutant of R, which is equal to R). Deduce that the elements of $\mathfrak{c}$ are semi-simple.

d) Let $x\in \mathfrak{c}$. Show that the replicas of $x$ belong to $\mathfrak{c}$ (Chap. I, §5, Exerc. 14). Show that Tr($sx$) $= 0$ for all $s\in \mathfrak{g}$; deduce that Tr($sx$) $= 0$ for all $s\in \overline{\mathfrak{g}}$, and hence that $x$ is nilpotent (loc. cit).

e) Show that $\mathfrak{c}= 0$ and $\overline{\mathfrak{g}}=\mathfrak{g}$ by combining c) and d).

7) Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a Lie subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}\mathfrak{l}(V)$. Let $m, n$ be two integers $\geq 0, W$ and $W'$ two vector subspaces of $\mathbf{T}^m(V)\otimes \mathbf{T}^n(V^*)$ where $V^*$ is the dual of V. Assume that $W'\subset W$ and that W and $W'$ are stable under the natural representation of $\mathfrak{g}$ on $\mathbf{T}^m(V)\otimes \mathbf{T}^n(V^*)$. Show that W and $W'$ are then stable under $e(\mathfrak{g})$. If $\pi$ denotes the representation of $e(\mathfrak{g})$ on $W/W'$ thus obtained, show that $\pi e(\mathfrak{g})$ is the decomposable envelope of $\pi (\mathfrak{g})$ (use Th. 1).

Deduce that ad $e(\mathfrak{g})$ is the decomposable envelope of ad$\mathfrak{g}$ in $\mathfrak{g}\mathfrak{l}(\mathfrak{g})$.

$^8$ For more details, cf. C. CHEVALLEY, Théorie des groupes de Lie, II, Groupes algébriques, Chap. II, §14, Paris, Hermann, 1951.
