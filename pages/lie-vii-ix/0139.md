Let $H$ = $n_1H_{\alpha_1}+\cdots +n_lH_{\alpha_l}$ be the highest root of $R^{\vee}$, and J the set of $i\in  \{1, . . . , l\}$ such that $n_i= 1$. Let $\lambda \in P_{++}$ **--** $\{0\}$. Then conditions (i), (ii) and (iii) of Prop. 6 are equivalent to each of the following conditions:

(v) $\lambda (H) = 1$;

(vi) there exists $i\in J$ such that $\lambda =_i$.
