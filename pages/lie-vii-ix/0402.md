5) For $n$ an integer $\geq 1$, denote by **Spin**$(n,\mathbf{R})$ the reduced Clifford group associated to the usual quadratic form on $\mathbf{R}^n($Algebra, Chap. IX, §9, no. 5).

a) Show that **Spin**$(n,\mathbf{R})$ is a compact Lie group and that the surjective homomorphism $\varphi :$ **Spin**$(n,\mathbf{R})\rightarrow \mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}(n,\mathbf{R}) ($loc. cit.) is analytic, with kernel $\{+1,-1\}$.

b) Show that, for $n\geq 2$, **Spin**$(n,\mathbf{R})$ is connected and simply-connected (use Exerc. 2). The group $\pi_1(\mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}(n,\mathbf{R}))$ is cyclic of order 2.

c) Let $Z_n$ be the centre of **Spin**$(n,\mathbf{R})$. Show that $Z_n=\{+1,-1\}$ if $n$ is odd, and $Z_n=\{+1,-1, \varepsilon ,-\varepsilon \}$ if $n$ is even, where $\varepsilon =e_1. . . e_n$ is the product of the elements of the canonical basis of $\mathbf{R}^n$; the group $Z_{2r}$ is isomorphic to $(\mathbf{Z}/2\mathbf{Z})^2$ (resp. to $\mathbf{Z}/4\mathbf{Z}$) if $r$ is even (resp. odd).

d) Prove that every almost simple connected compact Lie group of type $B_n$ $(n\geq 2)$ is isomorphic to **Spin**$(2n+ 1,\mathbf{R})$ or $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}(2n+ 1,\mathbf{R})$.

e) If $r$ is odd (resp. even) and $\geq 2$, every almost simple connected compact Lie group of type $D_r$ is isomorphic to **Spin**$(2r,\mathbf{R}),\mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}(2r,\mathbf{R})$ or $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}(2r,\mathbf{R})/\{\pm I_{2r}\}$ (resp. to one of the preceding groups or **Spin**$(2r,\mathbf{R})/\{1, \varepsilon \}$).

b) Every almost simple connected compact Lie group of type $C_n(n\geq 3)$ is isomorphic to $\mathbf{U}(n,\mathbf{H})$ or $\mathbf{U}(n,\mathbf{H})/\{\pm I_n\}$.

7) Let A be the algebra of Cayley octonions (Algebra, Chap. III, App., no. 3), with the basis $(e_i)_{0\leq i\leq 7}$ of loc. cit. Denote by V the subspace of pure octonions generated by $e_1, . . . , e_7$ and by E the subspace of V generated by $e_1, e_2, e_3, e_5, e_6, e_7$. Identify the subalgebra of A generated by $e_0, e_4$ with the field $\mathbf{C}$ of complex numbers, and denote by G the topological group of automorphisms of the unital algebra A.

a) Denote by Q the quadratic form on V induced by the Cayley norm, so that $(e_i)_{1\leq i\leq 7}$ is an orthonormal basis of V. Prove that the map $\sigma  \rightarrow \sigma |V$ is an injective homomorphism from G to the group $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}$(Q), which is isomorphic to $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{O}(7,\mathbf{R})$.

b) Show that the multiplication of A gives E the structure of a $\mathbf{C}$-vector space of dimension 3, of which $\{e_1, e_2, e_3\}$ is a basis. Denote by $\Phi$ the hermitian form on E for which this basis is orthonormal. Let H be the fixer of $e_4$ in G. Show that the map $\sigma  \rightarrow \sigma |E$ is an isomorphism from H to the group $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}(\Phi )$, which is isomorphic to $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}(3,\mathbf{C})$. The map $\sigma  \rightarrow \sigma (e_4)$ induces an embedding of $G/H$ in the sphere of V, which is isomorphic to $\mathbf{S}_6$.
