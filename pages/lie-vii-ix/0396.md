# EXERCISES

§**1**

1) Let G be a finite dimensional, connected, commutative complex Lie group, and let V be its Lie algebra.

$a)$ The map exp$_G: V\rightarrow G$ is a surjective homomorphism; its kernel $\Gamma$ is a discrete subgroup of V.

$b) G$ is compact if and only if $\Gamma$ is a lattice in V; then G is said to be a complex torus.

$c)$ Let $\Gamma$ be the discrete subgroup of$\surd \mathbf{C}^2_2$ generated by the elements $e^1=$ $(1,0), e_2= (0,1), e_3= (2, i)$; put $G =\mathbf{C}/\Gamma , H = (\Gamma +\mathbf{C}e_1)/\Gamma$. Show that H is isomorphic to $\mathbf{C}^*$, that $G/H$ is a complex torus of dimension 1, but that G contains no non-zero complex torus.

$d)$ Every finite dimensional connected compact complex Lie group is a complex torus (cf. Chap. III, §6, no. 3, Prop. 6).

$a)$ Show that an analytic law of left operation of the discrete group $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{L}(2,\mathbf{Z})$ on H can be defined by putting $\gamma \tau =^{a\tau+b}_{c\tau+d}$ for all $\gamma =(ab)\in \mathbf{S}\mathbf{L}(2,\mathbf{Z})$

$b)$ For $\tau \in H$, denote by $T_{\tau}$ the complex torus $\mathbf{C}/(\mathbf{Z}+\mathbf{Z}\tau )$. Show that the map $\tau  \rightarrow T_{\tau}$ induces by passage to the quotient a bijective map from the set $H/\mathbf{S}\mathbf{L}(2,\mathbf{Z})$ to the set of isomorphism classes of complex tori of dimension one.

3) Let G be an integral subgroup of $\mathbf{O}(n)$ such that the identity representation of G on $\mathbf{R}^n$ is irreducible. Show that G is closed (write G in the form $K\times N$, where K is compact and N is commutative, and prove that dim $N\leq 1)$.

4) Show that the conditions in Prop. 3 (no. 3) are equivalent to each of the following conditions:

(ii$')$ The group Ad(G) is relatively compact in Aut(L(G)).

(ii$'')$ The group Ad(G) is relatively compact in End(L(G)).
