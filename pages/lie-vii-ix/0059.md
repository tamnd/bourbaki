$§11$, no. 1, Prop. 1). For any $x\in X$, there exists an open neighbourhood V of $x$ and an isomorphism $c$ from V to an open ball in $\mathbf{C}^n$ such that $c(A\cap V)$ is contained in the set of zeros of a holomorphic function not identically zero on $c(V)$. Then, by Lemma $2, V\cap (X$ **--** A) is connected. In view of Lemma 1, this proves that X **--** A is connected.
