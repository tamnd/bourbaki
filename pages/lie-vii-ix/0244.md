$\P 10)$ Let $\mathfrak{h}$ be a splitting Cartan subalgebra of $\mathfrak{g}$, and $(X_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in R}$ a Chevalley system in $(\mathfrak{g},\mathfrak{h})$, cf. $§2$, no. 4. If $\alpha \in R$, put

$$
\theta_{\alpha}=e^{adX_{\alpha}}e^{adX_{-\alpha}}e^{adX_{\alpha}}
$$

$a)$ Show that $\varepsilon (W) = W(R)$.

$b)$ Let $s\in W$, and let $w=\varepsilon (s)$. Show that

$s(X_{\alpha}) =\pm X_{w(\alpha)}$ for all $\alpha \in R$.
