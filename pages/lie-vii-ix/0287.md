37) A nilpotent element $x$ of $\mathfrak{g}$ is called principal if the dimension of the centralizer of $x$ is equal to the rank of $\mathfrak{g}$. There exist principal nilpotent elements in $\mathfrak{g}$. If $k$ is algebraically closed, the principal nilpotent elements of $\mathfrak{g}$ are conjugate under Aut$_e(\mathfrak{g})$.
