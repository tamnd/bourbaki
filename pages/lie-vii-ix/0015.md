COROLLARY 2. Assume that V is finite dimensional and that condition (AC) is satisfied. Denote by $V^+(S)$ the vector subspace $\sum_{s\in S}((\bigcap_{i\geq 1}r(s)^iV)$. Then:

(i) $V^0(S)$ and $V^+(S)$ are stable under $r(S)$;

(ii) $V = V^0(S)\oplus V^+(S)$;
