# $§$ 4. SPLIT SEMI-SIMPLE LIE ALGEBRA DEFINED BY A REDUCED ROOT SYSTEM

### 1. FRAMED SEMI-SIMPLE LIE ALGEBRAS

PROPOSITION 1. Let $(\mathfrak{g},\mathfrak{h})$ be a split semi-simple Lie algebra, R its root system, B a basis of R, and $(n(\alpha , \beta ))_{\alpha ,\beta\in B}$ the corresponding Cartan matrix. For all $\alpha \in B$, let $X_{\alpha}\in \mathfrak{g}^{\alpha}, X_{-\alpha}\in \mathfrak{g}^{-\alpha}$. Then, for $\alpha , \beta \in B$,

$[H_{\alpha}, H_{\beta}] = 0$ (1)

$[H_{\alpha}, X_{\beta}] =n(\beta , \alpha )X_{\beta}$ (2)

$[H_{\alpha}, X_{-\beta}] =-n(\beta , \alpha )X_{-\beta}$ (3)

$[X_{-\alpha}, X_{\beta}] = 0$ if $\alpha \not=\beta$ (4)

(ad $X_{\alpha})^{1-n(\beta ,\alpha)}X_{\beta}= 0$ if $\alpha \not=\beta$ (5)

(ad $X_{-\alpha})^{1-n(\beta ,\alpha)}X_{-\beta}= 0$ if $\alpha \not=\beta$. (6)

The family $(H_{\alpha})_{\alpha\in B}$ is a basis of $\mathfrak{h}$. If $X_{\alpha}\not= 0$ and $X_{-\alpha}\not= 0$ for all $\alpha \in B$, the Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$ is generated by the $X_{\alpha}$ and the $X_{-\alpha}(\alpha \in B)$.

(Recall that, if $\alpha , \beta \in B$ and $\alpha \not=\beta ,n(\beta , \alpha )$ is an integer $\leq 0$, so formulas (5) and (6) make sense.)

Formulas (1), (2) and (3) are clear.
