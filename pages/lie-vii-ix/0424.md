8) Let G be a Lie group whose Lie algebra is compact, and which has a finite number of connected components. Show that G has a faithful linear representation on a finite dimensional vector space (write G as the semi-direct product of a compact group K by a vector group N; choose a faithful representation of K on a finite dimensional vector space W and represent G as a subgroup of the affine group of $W\oplus N)$.

§**8**

1) Let $G =\mathbf{S}\mathbf{U}(2,\mathbf{C})$, and let $\sigma$ be the identity representation of G on $\mathbf{C}^2$. $a)$ The irreducible representations of G are the representations $\tau^n=\mathbf{S}^n\sigma$ for $n\geq 0$.

$b)$ Let $e_1, e_2$ be the canonical basis of $\mathbf{C}^2$; show that the coefficients of $\tau^n$ in the basis $(e^i_1e^{n-i}_2)_{0\leq i\leq n}$ are the functions $\tau_{ij}^n$ such that, for $g=(-\alpha \beta )\in G$,

$$
\beta \overline{\alpha}
$$

we have $\tau_{ij}^n(g) =\frac{(-1)^i}{j!}\alpha^{i+j-n}\beta^{j-i}P^n_{ij}(|\alpha |^2)$, with $P^n_{ij}(t) =\frac{d^j}{dt^j}[t^{n-i}(1-t)^i]$

(“Jacobi polynomials”).

$c)$ Deduce from $b)$ that the functions $(n+ 1)^{1/2}(\frac{j!(n-j)!}{i!(n-i)!})^{1/2}\tau_{ij}^n$, for $i, j, n$ integers with $0\leq i\leq n, 0\leq j\leq n$, form an orthonormal basis of $L^2(G)$.

$d)$ For $g=(-\alpha \beta )\in G$, put $\alpha =t^{1/2}e^{i(\varphi-\psi)/2},\beta = (1-t)^{1/2}e^{i(\varphi+\psi)/2}$,

$$
\beta \overline{\alpha}
$$

with $0\leq t\leq 1, 0\leq \varphi  <2\pi ,-2\pi \leq \psi  <2\pi$. Show that the normalized Haar measure $dg$ on G is equal to $(8\pi^2)^{-1}dtd\varphi d\psi$.

$e)$ Let $a, b$ in $\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{Z}$ be such that $a-b\in \mathbf{Z}$. Deduce from $d)$ that the polynomials $P^n_{n/2-a,n/2-b}(t)$, for $n$ an integer of the same parity as $2a,n\geq$ max(2$a,2b)$, form an orthogonal basis of $L^2([0,1])$ for the measure $t^{-a-b}(1-t)^{a-b}dt$.

2) Let $f$ be a complex-valued function on G of class $C^{\infty}$. Prove that there exist two complex-valued functions $g$ and $\varphi$ on G, of class $C^{\infty}$, such that $\varphi$ is central and $f=g*\varphi$.

3) Let $u$ be an irreducible representation of G, and let $\lambda$ be its highest weight. For $x\in \mathfrak{g}$, denote by $\overline{x}$ the unique element of $\overline{\mathbf{C}}$ that is conjugate to $x$ under Ad(G). Prove the equality $u(x)_{\infty}=|\delta (\lambda )(\overline{x})|$.

4) Choose a separating positive quadratic form Q on $\mathfrak{g}$ invariant under Ad(G). For $x\in \mathfrak{t}$, put $\vartheta_0(x) =\sum_{u\in\Gamma(T)}e^{-Q(x+u)}$.

$a)$ Show that $\vartheta_0$ is a function of class $C^{\infty}$ on $\mathfrak{t}$, and that there exists a function $\vartheta_1$ of class $C^{\infty}$ on T such that $\vartheta_1\circ$ exp$_T=\vartheta_0$.
