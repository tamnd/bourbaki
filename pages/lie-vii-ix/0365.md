### 6. CASIMIR ELEMENTS

By Prop. 3 of $§1$, no. 3, there exist negative symmetric bilinear forms on $\mathfrak{g}$, separating and invariant under Ad(G) (if G is semi-simple, we can take the Killing form of $\mathfrak{g}$, for example). Let F be such a form. Recall (Chap. $I,§3$, no. 7) that the Casimir element associated to F is the element $\Gamma$ of the centre of the enveloping algebra $U(\mathfrak{g})$ such that, for any basis $(\sum e_i)$ of $\mathfrak{g}$ satisfying $F(e_i, e_j) =-\delta_{ij}$, we have $\Gamma =-e^2_i$.

In the remainder of this chapter we shall call the Casimir elements of G the elements of $U(\mathfrak{g})$ obtained in this way from negative separating invariant symmetric bilinear forms on $\mathfrak{g}$. If $\Gamma$ is a Casimir element of G and if $\tau : G\rightarrow \mathbf{G}\mathbf{L}(V)$ is an irreducible representation of G, the endomorphism $\Gamma_V$ of V is a homothety (Algebra, Chap. VIII, $§3$, no. 2, Th. 1), whose ratio we shall denote by ˜$\Gamma (\tau )$.
