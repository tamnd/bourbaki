$$
\| f(x) - f(a) \| \leq g(c) - g(a) + \varepsilon (c - a) + \varepsilon \sum_{a_n < c} \frac{1}{2^n}
$$

from which, letting $ x $ tend to $ c $ in this inequality, it follows from the continuity of $ f $ that $ c $ satisfies (8).
