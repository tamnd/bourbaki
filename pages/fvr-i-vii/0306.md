# EXERCISES

§ 1.

1) Let K be a field of characteristic 0, let U and V two composition operators in K[X], and W = VU = UV; let (u_n), (v_n) (w_n) be the sequences of Appell polynomials corresponding to U, V, W respectively. Show that if p is the order of U then

$$
w_n^{(p)}(X) = \sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k} v_{n-k}(X)V_0(u_k)
$$

$$
w_n(X+Y) = \sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k} u_k(X)v_{n-k}(Y).
$$

2) Let E be the vector space over C generated by the functions $ x^n \ (n \in \mathbf{N}) $, $ e^{\lambda x} \ (\lambda \in \mathbf{C}, \lambda \neq 0) $ and $ |x+\mu| \ (\mu \in \mathbf{R}) $.

a) Show that the preceding functions form a basis for E.

b) Let U be the composition operator defined on E by the conditions: $ U_0^\xi(\xi^n) = (n!)^2 $, $ U(e^{\lambda x}) = e^{\lambda x} $ for $ \lambda \neq 0 $, $ U(|x+\mu|) = |x+\mu| $ for $ \mu \in \mathbf{R} $. The indicatrix $ u(\lambda) $ is the constant 1 but the series with general term $ n! \lambda^n $ does not converge for any value $ \lambda \neq 0 $.

c) Let V be the composition operator defined on E by the conditions $ V(x^n) = x^n $, $ V(|x+\mu|) = |x+\mu| $, $ V(e^{\lambda x}) = 0 $ for $ \lambda \neq 0 $; the indicatrix $ v(\lambda) $ is equal to 1 for $ \lambda = 0 $, to 0 for $ \lambda \neq 0 $, so is different from the sum of the series $ \sum_{n=0}^\infty \alpha_n \lambda^n $ where $ \alpha_n = V_0^\xi(\xi^n)/n! $.

d) Let W be the composition operator defined on E by

$$
W(x^n) = x^n, \qquad W(e^{\lambda x}) = e^{\lambda x}, \qquad W(|x+\mu|) = e^{\lambda+\mu};
$$

show that $ VW \neq WV $.

3) Let K be a field of characteristic 0. One says that an endomorphism U of the algebra K[X,Y] of polynomials in two indeterminates X, Y over K is a composition operator if, for every polynomial $ f \in K[X, Y] $, one has, putting $ g = U(f) $, that $ g(X+S, Y+T) = U_{X,Y}(f(X+S, Y+T)) $, where S and T are two other indeterminates.

a) Generalize prop. 1 and th. 1 to these operators. Derive a new proof of the formula $ e^{S+T} = e^S e^T $ from this.
