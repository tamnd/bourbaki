§ 1.

1) a) With the notation of IV, p. 165, suppose that $ f $ is uniformly continuous on $ J \times S $. Prove prop. 3 of IV, p. 166 without using the axiom of choice. (Let $ \delta $ be such that the relations $ |t_2 - t_1| \leq \delta, \|x_2 - x_1\| \leq \delta $ imply $ \|f(t_2, x_2) - f(t_1, x_1)\| \leq \varepsilon $; consider a subdivision of $ J $ into intervals of length $ \leq \inf(\delta, \delta/M) $ and define the approximate solution by induction.)

b) When $ E $ is finite dimensional and $ f $ is Lipschitz on $ I \times H $ prove prop. 3 of IV, p. 166 without using the axiom of choice. (Remark that for every $ \delta > 0 $ there exists a finite number of points $ x_i $ of $ S $ such that every point of $ S $ is at a distance $ \leq \delta $ from one of the $ x_i $; then proceed as in $ a $), considering the regulated functions $ t \mapsto f(t, x_i) $, of which there are only a finite number.)

2) Given two numbers $ b > 0, M > 0 $ and an arbitrary number $ \varepsilon > 0 $, give an example of a scalar differential equation $ x' = g(x) $ such that $ |g(x)| \leq M $ for $ |x| \leq b $ and which admits an integral $ x = u(t) $ continuous on the interval $ ] - b/M - \varepsilon, b/M + \varepsilon[ $, but which does not have a finite limit at the point $ x = \frac{b}{M} + \varepsilon $ (define $ g $ so that the integral considered has a continuous derivative on $ ] - b/M - \varepsilon, b/M + \varepsilon[ $, this derivative being equal to the constant $ M $ on $ ] - b/M, b/M[ $).

3) Let $ S \subset H $ be an open ball with centre $ x_0 $ and radius $ r $, and $ f $ a Lipschitz function for the constant $ k > 0 $ on $ I \times S $; suppose further that $ t \mapsto f(t, x_0) $ is bounded on $ I $, and denote by $ M_0 $ the supremum of $ \|f(t, x_0)\| $ over $ t \in I $. Show that for all $ t_0 \in I $ there is an integral $ u $ of $ x' = f(t, x) $, with values in $ S $, equal to $ x_0 $ at the point $ t_0 $ and defined on the intersection of $ I $ and the interval $ ]t_0 - \lambda, t_0 + \lambda[ $, where
$$
\lambda = \frac{1}{k} \log \left( 1 + \frac{kz}{M_0} \right)
$$
(note that one has $ \|u(t) - x_0\| \leq M(t - t_0) + k \int_{t_0}^t \|u(s) - x_0\| \, ds $ for $ t > t_0 $).

4) a) Let $ I = ]t_0 - a, t_0 + a[ $ be an open interval in $ \mathbf{R} $, let $ S $ be an open ball with centre $ x_0 $ and radius $ r $ in $ E $, and $ f $ a locally Lipschitz function on $ I \times S $. Let $ (s, z) \mapsto h(s, z) $ be a function $ \geq 0 $ defined and continuous for $ 0 \leq s \leq a $ and $ 0 \leq z \leq r $, such that for every $ s \in [0, a] $ the map $ z \mapsto h(s, z) $ is increasing; suppose that $ \|f(t, x)\| \leq h(|t - t_0|, \|x - x_0\|) $ on $ I \times S $. Let $ \varphi $ be a primitive of a regulated function on an interval $ [0, \alpha] $ (with $ \alpha < a $), with values in $ [0, r[ $, such that $ \varphi(0) = 0 $ and $ \varphi'(s) > h(s, \varphi(s)) $ on $ [0, \alpha] $ except on a countable set. Show that the integral $ u $ of $ x' = f(t, x) $, equal to $ x_0 $ at the point $ t_0 $, is defined on $ ]t_0 - \alpha, t_0 + \alpha[ $, and that $ \|u(t) - x_0\| \leq \varphi(|t - t_0|) $ on this interval.
