1. DEFINITION OF A CONVEX FUNCTION

DEFINITION 1. We say that a finite numerical function $ f $, defined on an interval $ I \subset \mathbf{R} $, is convex on $ I $ if, no matter what the points $ x, x' $ of $ I $, ($ x < x' $), every point $ M_z $ of the graph $ G $ of $ f $ such that $ x \leq z \leq x' $ lies below the segment $ M_xM_{x'} $ (or, what comes to the same, if every point of this segment lies above $ G $) (fig. 2).
