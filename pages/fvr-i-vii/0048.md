COROLLARY 2. *Let $ f $ be a finite real function defined on an open interval $ I \subset \mathbf{R} $. If for every point $ x \in I $ there is an open interval $ J_x \subset I $ containing $ x $ and such that the restriction of $ f $ to $ J_x $ is convex on $ J_x $, then $ f $ is convex on $ I $.*

It is clear that $ f $ satisfies the criterion of prop. 9.
