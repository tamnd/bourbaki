b) Deduce that if $ (a_{ij}) $ is a square matrix of order $ n $ with arbitrary complex elements and $ \Delta $ is its determinant, one has ("Hadamard's inequality")
$$
|\Delta|^2 \leq \left( \sum_{j=1}^n |a_{1j}|^2 \right) \left( \sum_{j=1}^n |a_{2j}|^2 \right) \cdots \left( \sum_{j=1}^n |a_{nj}|^2 \right)
$$
with equality only if one of the terms on the right-hand side is zero or if, for all distinct indices $ h, k $
$$
a_{h1}\bar{a}_{k1} + a_{h2}\bar{a}_{k2} + \cdots + a_{hn}\bar{a}_{kn} = 0
$$
(multiply the matrix $ (a_{ij}) $ by the conjugate of its transpose).

13) If $ x, y, a, b $ are > 0, show that
