c) (Gevrey’s spaces.) Let I be a compact interval in $\mathbf{R}$. For every integer $n \geqslant 0$, $D^n f$ denotes the $n$th derivative of a numerical function $f$ defined on I (whenever this derivative exists). Let $s \geqslant 1$ and $M \geqslant 0$ be two real numbers. Let $\mathscr{G}_{s,M}(I)$ denote the vector space of those infinitely differentiable functions $f$ on I (FVR, I, p. 28) for which the sequence $(|D^n f|/M^n(n!)^s)_{n \geqslant 0}$ is bounded in the space $\mathscr{C}(I)$ of all continuous functions on I (with the topology of uniform convergence). The space $\mathscr{G}_{s,M}(I)$ is a Banach space with the norm

$$
\|f\|_{s,M} = \sup_{n \geqslant 0, x \in I} |D^n f(x)|/M^n(n!)^s .
$$

For $M \leqslant M'$, we have $\mathscr{G}_{s,M}(I) \subset \mathscr{G}_{s,M'}(I)$, and

$$
\|f\|_{s,M'} \leqslant \|f\|_{s,M}
$$

for every $f \in \mathscr{G}_{s,M}(I)$. Let $\mathscr{G}_s(I)$ denote the union of the spaces $\mathscr{G}_{s,M}(I)$ and endow it with the inductive limit topology of the topologies of $\mathscr{G}_{s,M}(I)$.

Let $M < M'$ and let B be the unit ball (closed) in $\mathscr{G}_{s,M}(I)$. We will prove that B is a compact subset of the Banach space $\mathscr{G}_{s,M'}(I)$. It is clear that B is closed in $\mathscr{G}_{s,M'}(I)$ and so it is enough to prove that B is precompact in $\mathscr{G}_{s,M'}(I)$. Let $\varepsilon > 0$ and let N be a positive integer such that $(M/M')^N \leqslant \varepsilon/2$. Let $k$ be a positive integer; the set of all functions $D^{k+1} f$, as $f$ ranges over B, is bounded in $\mathscr{C}(I)$, hence the set of all functions $D^k f$, as $f$ ranges over B, is relatively compact in $\mathscr{C}(I)$: this follows from the theorem of finite increments (FVR, I, p. 23, cor. 1) and from Ascoli’s theorem (GT, X, § 2, No. 5). We define a norm on $\mathscr{G}_{s,M}(I)$ by

$$
q(f) = \sup_{0 \leqslant n \leqslant N, x \in I} |D^n f(x)|/M^n(n!)^s .
$$

The above argument shows that B is precompact for the topology associated with the norm $q$; in other words, there exists a finite subset C of B such that for every $f \in B$, there exists $g \in C$ such that $q(f - g) \leqslant \varepsilon$. Finally, for every $n > N$, we have

$$
|D^n f(x) - D^n g(x)|/M^n(n!)^s \leqslant 2(M/M')^n \leqslant \varepsilon ,
$$

from which we get $\|f - g\| \leqslant \varepsilon$. This proves that B is precompact in $\mathscr{G}_{s,M'}(I)$.

The space $\mathscr{G}_s(I)$ is the inductive limit of the spaces $\mathscr{G}_{s,k}(I)$ as $k$ ranges over $\mathbf{N}$; by prop. 7 (III, p. 6) every bounded subset of $\mathscr{G}_s(I)$ is contained in one of the spaces $\mathscr{G}_{s,k}(I)$ and is relatively compact in this space.
