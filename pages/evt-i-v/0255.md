\textbf{T \* 5) Let } $ G $ \textbf{be a discrete group.}
a) Show that a mean can be defined on $ G $ (exerc. 4) if and only if the closed vector subspace $ N $ of $ \ell^\infty_\mathbf{R}(G) $ generated by the functions $ \gamma(s)\ x - x $, where $ s \in G $ and $ x \in \ell^\infty_\mathbf{R}(G) $, is distinct from $ \ell^\infty_\mathbf{R}(G) $ (use the Hahn-Banach theorem).
b) Suppose that for every $ \varepsilon > 0 $ and for every finite sequence $ s_1, ..., s_k $ of elements of $ G $, there exists a non-empty finite subset $ F $ of $ G $ such that
$$
\operatorname{Card}(F \cap s_j F) \geq (1 - \varepsilon) \operatorname{Card}(F) \quad \text{for} \quad 1 \leq j \leq k .
$$
Show that a mean can be defined on $ G $ (use $ a $) and show that $ 1 \notin N $.
c) Suppose a mean can be defined on $ G $. Let $ \varepsilon > 0 $ and let $ s_1, ..., s_k $ be elements of $ G $. Show that there exists a vector $ x \geq 0 $ in the space $ \ell^1_\mathbf{R}(G) $ such that $ \|x\| = 1 $ and that
$$
\sum_{j=1}^k \| \gamma(s_j)\ x - x \| \leq \varepsilon . $$
(In the space $ E = (\ell^1_\mathbf{R}(G))^k $, consider the set $ C $ of points $ (\gamma(s_j)\ x - x)_{1 \leq j \leq k} $,
where $ x $ ranges over the set of all vectors of $ \ell^1_\mathbf{R}(G) $ such that $ x \geq 0 $ and $ \|x\| = 1 $. Show that 0 belongs to the closure of the convex set $ C $ for the topology $ \sigma(E, E') $; for this, use $ a $), observing that the unit ball of $ E $ is dense in the unit ball of $ E'' $ for $ \sigma(E'', E') $.
d) For every $ x \geq 0 $ in $ \ell^1_\mathbf{R}(G) $, and for every $ a > 0 $, let $ x_a $ denote the characteristic function of the set of all $ s \in G $ such that $ x(s) \geq a $ (*i.e.* $ x_a(s) = 1 $ if $ x(s) \geq a $, $ x_a(s) = 0 $ if $ x(s) < a $). Then for every $ s \in G $,
$$
\int_0^\infty x_r(s)\ dr = x(s) \text{ and, for two elements } x \geq 0, y \geq 0 \text{ of } \ell^1_\mathbf{R}(G),
$$
$$
\int_0^\infty |x_r(s) - y_r(s)|\ dr = |x(s) - y(s)| .
$$
e) Show that, if a mean can be defined on $ G $, then for every $ \varepsilon > 0 $, and for every finite sequence of elements $ s_1, ..., s_k $ of $ G $, there exists a non-empty finite subset $ F $ of $ G $ such that
$$
\operatorname{Card}(F \cap s_j F) \geq (1 - \varepsilon) \operatorname{Card}(F) \quad \text{for} \quad 1 \leq j \leq k .
$$
(Show that, having chosen $ x $ as in $ c $), there exists an $ a > 0 $ such that $ \sum_{j=1}^k \| \gamma(s_j)\ x_a - x_a \| \leq \varepsilon $; use $ d $). \*
