Show that if the measure $ \mu' $ is bounded, then the projection $ \mathrm{pr}_1 $ of $ T \times T' $ onto T is a $ (\mu \otimes \mu') $-proper mapping and $ \mathrm{pr}_1(\mu \otimes \mu') = a \cdot \mu $, where $ a = \mu'(T') $.
