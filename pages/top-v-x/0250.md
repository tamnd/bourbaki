16) For each covering $ \mathcal{H} $ of a topological space $ X $, let $ V_{\mathcal{H}} $ denote the union of all the sets $ U \times U $ such that $ U \in \mathcal{H} $. A covering $ \mathcal{H} $ of $ X $ is said to be *even* if there is a neighbourhood $ W $ of the diagonal $ \Delta $ of $ X \times X $ such that the covering $ (W(x))_{x \in X} $ is finer than $ \mathcal{H} $. $ \mathcal{H} $ is said to be *divisible* if there is a neighbourhood $ W $ of $ \Delta $ in $ X \times X $ such that $ \dot{W} \subset V_{\mathcal{H}} $.

a) Show that every even open covering of $ X $ is divisible [cf. Exercise 19 b)].
