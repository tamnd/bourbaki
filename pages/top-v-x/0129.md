§ 1

1) Let $ f(z) = z^n + a_1 z^{n-1} + \cdots + a_{n-1} z + a_n $ be a polynomial of degree $ n $ with complex coefficients and put $ f(z) = \prod_{i=1}^n (z - z_i) $. Let $ r_0 = \sup_i |z_i| $.

a) Show that if the real number $ r > 0 $ is such that
$$
r^n \leq |a_1|r^{n-1} + |a_2|r^{n-2} + \cdots + |a_{n-1}|r + |a_n|,
$$
then $ r_0 \leq r $, and deduce that
$$
r_0 \leq \sup \left( 1, \sum_{k=1}^n |a_k| \right).
$$

b) Let $ (\lambda_i)_{1 \leq i \leq n} $ be a finite sequence of $ n $ real numbers $ > 0 $ such that $ \sum_{i=1}^n \lambda_i^{-1} = 1 $. Show that $ r_0 \leq \sup_k (\lambda_k |a_k|)^{1/k} $ [use a)].

c) Deduce from a) that, if the coefficients $ a_i $ are all non-zero, we have
$$
r_0 \leq \sup \left( 2|a_1|, 2 \left| \frac{a_2}{a_1} \right|, \ldots, 2 \left| \frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}} \right|, \left| \frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} \right| \right).
$$

d) Deduce from a) that
$$
r_0 \leq |a_1 - 1| + |a_2 - a_1| + \cdots + |a_{n-1} - a_n| + |a_n|
$$
[consider the polynomial $ (z - 1)f(z) $]. Hence show that, if the $ a_i $ are all real and $ > 0 $, we have
$$
r_0 \leq \sup \left( a_1, \frac{a_2}{a_1}, \ldots, \frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}}, \frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} \right).
$$
