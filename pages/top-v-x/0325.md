5) Let $X$ be a topological space. A set $\mathcal{S}$ of subsets of $X$ is said to be *saturated* if it satisfies conditions $(F'_I)$ and $(F'_{II})$ of no. 2 and if the closure of every set of $\mathcal{S}$ belongs to $\mathcal{S}$.

a) Show that if $X$ is normal and if $\mathcal{S}$ is saturated and covers $X$, then the set $\mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{R})$ is dense in the space $\tilde{\mathcal{C}}_{\mathcal{S}}(X; \mathbf{R})$ (no. 6, Theorem 2, Corollary 2) with respect to the topology of $\mathcal{S}$-convergence. Show that the topology of $\mathcal{S}_1$-convergence on $\mathcal{C}(X; \mathbf{R})$ is strictly coarser than the topology of $\mathcal{S}_2$-convergence.

6) a) Let $X$ be a topological space, $Y$ a uniform space, and let $\Phi$ be a filter on the set $\mathcal{C}(X; Y)$ which converges pointwise to a function $u_0$. Then $u_0$ is continuous at a point $x_0 \in X$ if and only if, for each entourage $V$ of $Y$ and each set $M \in \Phi$, there is a neighbourhood $U$ of $x_0$ and a mapping $u \in M$ such that $(u_0(x), u(x)) \in V$ for all $x \in U$.

b) Let $X$ be a quasi-compact space, $Y$ a uniform space and $\Phi$ a filter on $\mathcal{C}(X; Y)$ which converges pointwise to a function $u_0$. Then $u_0$ is continuous on $X$ if and only if, for each entourage $V$ of $Y$ and each set $M \in \Phi$, there exists a finite number of functions $u_i \in M (1 \leq i \leq n)$ such that, for each $x \in X$, we have $(u_0(x), u_i(x)) \in V$ for at least one index $i$.

$\P 7)$ Let $X, Y$ be two Hausdorff uniform spaces. For each continuous mapping $f : X \to Y$, let $G(f) \subset X \times Y$ be the graph of $f$; $G(f)$ is closed in $X \times Y$ (Chapter I, § 8, no. 1, Proposition 2, Corollary 2); hence $f \to G(f)$ is an injective mapping of $\mathcal{C}(X; Y)$ into the set $\tilde{\mathfrak{F}}(X \times Y)$ of non-empty closed subsets of $X \times Y$.

a) Show that the mapping $f \to G(f)$ of $\mathcal{C}_u(X; Y)$ into $\tilde{\mathfrak{F}}(X \times Y)$ is uniformly continuous when $\tilde{\mathfrak{F}}(X \times Y)$ carries the uniformity defined in Chapter II, § 1, Exercise 5.

b) Let $\Gamma$ be the image of $\mathcal{C}(X; Y)$ in $\tilde{\mathfrak{F}}(X \times Y)$ under the mapping $f \to G(f)$, and let $\varphi : \Gamma \to \mathcal{C}_u(X; Y)$ be the inverse mapping. Show that if $X$ is compact, then $\varphi$ is continuous on $\Gamma$ (argue by contradiction).

c) Take both $X$ and $Y$ to be the compact interval $[0, 1]$ of $\mathbf{R}$. Show that $\varphi$ is not uniformly continuous on $\Gamma$.
