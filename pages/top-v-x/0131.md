¶ 5) a) Let $ K $ be a non-commutative division subring of the division ring of quaternions $ \mathbf{H} $; show that the centre $ Z $ of $ K $ is a subfield of the centre $ \mathbf{R} $ of $ \mathbf{H} $. (Observe that every element of $ K $ commutes with every element of the field $ L $ generated by $ Z \cup \mathbf{R} $; if $ Z \not\subset \mathbf{R} $, the field $ L $ would be a maximal subfield of $ \mathbf{H} $, and $ K $ would be contained in $ L $, contrary to hypothesis.)

b) Deduce that every isomorphism $ f $ (not necessarily continuous) of $ \mathbf{H} $ onto a division subring of $ \mathbf{H} $ is an inner automorphism $ x \to axa^{-1} $ of $ \mathbf{H} $. [The restriction of $ f $ to $ \mathbf{R} $ is an isomorphism of $ \mathbf{R} $ onto a subfield of $ \mathbf{R} $, by virtue of $ a $); use Exercise 3 of Chapter IV, § 3.]

§ 2

1) Let $ a $ be a non-zero complex number, and $ n $ an integer > 0. For each real number $ r > 0 $ such that $ r^n \leq |a| $, show that there exists $ z \in \mathbf{C} $ such that $ |z| = r $ and $ |a + z^n| = |a| - r^n $. Deduce that, if $ f $ is a polynomial of degree > 0, with complex coefficients, we cannot have $ |f(z_0)| \leq |f(z)| $ at all points $ z $ of a neighbourhood of a point $ z_0 $, provided that $ f(z_0) \neq 0 $.

2) Show that, if $ f $ is any polynomial with complex coefficients, not identically zero, there exists a real number $ r > 0 $ such that
$$
|f(z)| > |f(0)|
$$
whenever $ |z| \geq r $. Hence, with the help of Exercise 1 and Weierstrass' theorem (Chapter IV, § 6, no. 1, Theorem 1), give another proof of the fact that $ \mathbf{C} $ is algebraically closed (consider the function $ f $ on the compact set of points $ z $ such that $ |z| \leq r $).

3) Show, without using Theorem 1, that the mapping $ z \to z/\overline{z} $ is a strict morphism of the topological group $ \mathbf{C}^* $ onto the topological group $ \mathbf{U} $. Deduce that the mapping $ t \to (1 + it)/(1 - it) $ [$ (1 + it)/(1 - it) = -1 $ if $ t = \infty $] is an isomorphism of the topological group $ \tilde{\mathbf{R}} $ (no. 6) onto the topological group $ \mathbf{U} $, and hence give another proof of Theorem 1.

¶ 4) Let $ K $ be the smallest Pythagorean subfield of $ \mathbf{R} $ and let $ K' = K(i) $. Let $ G $ be the multiplicative group of elements of $ K' $ of absolute value 1 (a subgroup of $ \mathbf{U} $). Show that $ G $ is not isomorphic to the additive group of numbers of $ K $ mod 1. (Observe that in the latter group there exist elements of any prime order $ p $; on the other hand, if $ p $ is a prime such that $ p - 1 $ is not a power of 2, show that $ G $ contains no $ p $th root of unity other than 1, by noting that the degree over $ \mathbf{Q} $ of every element of $ K' $ is a power of 2.)
