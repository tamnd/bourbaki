a) Let $ f $ be a finite continuous real-valued function on $ \beta X $, such that $ f(x) > 0 $ for all $ x \in X $, and such that the set $ A = \overline{f}(0) \subset \beta X - X $ is not empty. Then there is a sequence $ (a_n) $ of points of $ X $ such that the sequence of numbers $ \lambda_n = f(a_n) > 0 $ is strictly decreasing and such that $ \lim_{n \to \infty} \lambda_n = 0 $. For each integer $ n > 0 $, let $ I_n $ be an open interval with centre $ \lambda_n $ in $ \mathbf{R}^* $, such that the closed intervals $ \overline{I}_n $ are mutually disjoint, and let $ M_n = \overline{f}(I_n) \cap X $. For each subset $ H $ of $ N $, let $ M_H $ be the union of the $ M_n $ for which $ n \in H $; for each filter $ \mathfrak{F} $ on $ N $ which is finer than the Fréchet filter, let $ \mathfrak{F}' $ denote the filter on $ X $ which has the $ M_H (H \in \mathfrak{F}) $ as a base. Show that $ \mathfrak{F}' $ is a completely regular filter (Exercise 8), and that if $ \mathfrak{F}_1, \mathfrak{F}_2 $ are two distinct ultrafilters on $ N $, each of which is finer than the Fréchet filter, then there is no filter on $ X $ which is finer than each of $ \mathfrak{F}_1', \mathfrak{F}_2' $. Deduce that $ \operatorname{Card}(A) \geq 2^{2^{\operatorname{Card}(N)}} $ [use Exercise 8 d) and Chapter I, § 4, Exercise 5].

b) Suppose that $ X $ is infinite and discrete. Show that the uniformity induced on $ X $ by that of $ \beta X $ is the uniformity of finite partitions (Chapter II, § 2, no. 2 and § 4, Exercise 12) [use Exercise 11 b)]. Hence show that $ \operatorname{Card}(\beta X) = 2^{2^{\operatorname{Card}(N)}} $ (cf. Chapter I, § 4, Exercise 5). Show that there is a continuous real-valued function $ f \geq 0 $ on $ X $ such that $ \overline{f}(0) $ is not empty and is contained in $ \beta X - X $.

c) Under the same hypotheses as in b), show that if $ A $ is an infinite closed subset of $ \beta X $, then $ \operatorname{Card}(A) \geq 2^{2^{\operatorname{Card}(N)}} $. [Show first that there exists an infinite sequence $ (a_n) $ of points of $ A $, and for each $ n $ a neighbourhood $ V_n $ of $ a_n $ in $ \beta X $, the $ V_n $ being mutually disjoint; deduce that every bounded real-valued function $ f $ defined on the set $ D $ of the $ a_n $ can be extended to a continuous function on $ \beta X $; for this, consider a real-valued function $ g $ defined on $ X $ and equal to $ f(a_n) $ at each point of $ X \cap V_n $. Hence conclude that $ \overline{D} \subset A $ is homeomorphic to the Stone-Čech compactification of $ D $ (cf. § 4, Exercise 17).]

13) Let $ G $ be a locally compact, non-compact topological group. [Assuming the result false, show, by applying Exercise 7 a) to the mapping $ (x, y) \to xy^{-1} $ of $ H $ into $ G $, that $ G $ would be isomorphic to a subgroup of a compact group; now apply Chapter III, § 3, no. 3, Proposition 4, Corollary 1.]
