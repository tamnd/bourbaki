§ 3

1) A pseudometric $ f $ on a group $ G $ (written multiplicatively) is said to be *left-invariant* (resp. *right-invariant*) if $ f(xz, yz) = f(x, y) $ [resp. $ f(xz, yz) = f(x, y) $] for all $ x, y, z $ in $ G $.

2) If $ f $ is a left-invariant pseudometric, the real-valued function $ g(x) = f(e, x) $ on $ G $ ($ e $ being the identity element of $ G $) satisfies the following conditions:

(i) $ g(x) > 0 $ for all $ x \in G $, and $ g(e) = 0 $;
(ii) $ g(x^{-1}) = g(x) $;
(iii) $ g(xy) \leq g(x) + g(y) $.

Conversely, if $ g $ is any real-valued function on $ G $ satisfying these conditions, then $ f(x, y) = g(x^{-1}y) $ is a left-invariant pseudometric on $ G $.

3) The topology $ \mathcal{T} $ defined by a saturated family $ \{f_i\} $ of left-invariant pseudometrics on a group $ G $ is compatible with the group structure of $ G $ if and only if, for each $ a \in G $, each index $ i $ and each real number $ x > 0 $, there exists an index $ x $ and a real number $ \hat{p} > 0 $ such that the relation $ f_i(e, x) \leq \hat{p} $ implies $ f_i(a, axa^{-1}) \leq x $. If this condition is satisfied, the uniformity on $ G $ defined by the family $ \mathcal{F} $ is the same as the left uniformity of the topological group obtained by endowing $ G $ with the topology $ \mathcal{T} $.

4) On every topological group $ G $ there exists a family of left-invariant pseudometrics such that the uniformity defined by this family is the same as the left uniformity of $ G $.

2) Let $ G $ be a topological group whose left and right uniformities coincide. Show that this unique uniformity can be defined by a family of pseudometrics which are simultaneously left-and right-invariant (using Exercise 3 of Chapter III, § 3, show that if $ V $ is any neighbourhood of the identity element $ e $ of $ G $, then $ V_0 = \bigcap_{x \in G} xVx^{-1} $ is a neighbourhood of $ e $).

3) Let $ G $ be a topological group, let $ \{f_i\} $ be a saturated family of left-invariant pseudometrics on $ G $ which define the left uniformity of $ G $, and let $ g_i(x) = f_i(e, x) $. Let $ H $ be a normal closed subgroup of $ G $, and for each coset $ x \in G/H $ let $ h_i(x) = \inf_{x \in x} g_i(x) $; show that, if $ f_i(x, y) = h_i(x^{-1}y) $, the $ f_i $ form a family of left-invariant pseudometrics on $ G/H $ which define the left uniformity of $ G/H $ (argue as in Remark 2 of no. 1').

4) Show that the topology of a valued division ring is locally retrobounded (Chapter III, § 6, Exercise 22).
