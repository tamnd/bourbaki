N. Bourbaki, Elements of Mathematics,
General Topology, Chapters 5-10

This is the soft-cover reprint of the English translation of 1966 (available from Springer since 1989) of the later chapters of Bourbaki’s “Topologie générale”.

It completes the treatment of general topology begun in Part I (Chapters 1–4, also available in English in soft-cover).

The real numbers having been introduced in Chapter 4, the first chapters of this volume study subgroups and quotients of $\mathbf{R}$ (with applications to the “measurement of magnitudes” and to the log and exp functions), then real vector spaces and projective spaces, then the additive groups $\mathbf{R}^n$ (subgroups, quotients, homomorphisms, infinite sums and products). Analogous properties are then studied for complex numbers, in Chapter 8. Chapter 9 illustrates the use of real numbers in general topology, studying different important kinds of topological spaces: uniformizable, metric, normal Baire, Polish, Borel spaces. The final chapter deals with the various topologies of function spaces, ending with a section on approximation of functions.
