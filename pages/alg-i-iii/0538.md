$$
\psi : \bigwedge_B(B \otimes_A M) \to B \otimes_A \bigwedge_A(M)

### 6. DIRECT LIMITS OF EXTERIOR ALGEBRAS

Let $ (A_\alpha, \phi_{\beta \alpha}) $ be a directed direct system of commutative rings, $ (M_\alpha, f_{\beta \alpha}) $ a direct system of $ A_\alpha $-modules, $ A = \lim \to A_\alpha $ and $ M = \lim \to M_\alpha $. For $ \alpha \leq \beta $, we derive canonically from the $ A_\alpha $-homomorphism $ f_{\beta \alpha} : M_\alpha \to M_\beta $ an $ A_\alpha $-algebra homomorphism (no. 5, formula (12)) $ f_{\beta \alpha}' : \bigwedge_{A_\alpha}(M_\alpha) \to \bigwedge_{A_\beta}(M_\beta) $ and it follows from (13) that $ (\bigwedge_{A_\alpha}(M_\alpha), f_{\beta \alpha}') $ is a *direct system of graded* $ A_\alpha $*-modules*. On the other hand let $ f_\alpha : M_\alpha \to M $ be the canonical $ A_\alpha $-homomorphism; we derive (no. 5, formula (12)) a graded $ A_\alpha $-algebra homomorphism $ f_\alpha'' : \bigwedge_{A_\alpha}(M_\alpha) \to \bigwedge_A(M) $ and it also follows from (13) that the $ f_\alpha'' $ constitute a direct system of $ A_\alpha $-homomorphisms.
