For every ordered pair $(h, k)$ of indices such that $1 \leq h \leq p$, $1 \leq k \leq n - p$, let $\beta_{hk}$ be the component $\alpha_H$ of z corresponding to the subset H of $[1, n]$ consisting of the $p - 1$ indices in G distinct from $i_h$ and of index $j_k \in G'$; let X be the matrix $(\beta_{hk})$ with p rows and $n - p$ columns. 19. Over a finite-dimensional vector space E, let $z = x_1 \wedge x_2 \wedge \cdots \wedge x_p$ be a pure p-vector $\neq 0$; let $v^*$ be a q-form ($q \leq p$) and $u^*$ an arbitrary element of $\bigwedge(E^*)$. Show that

$$
(u^* \wedge v^*) \lrcorner z = (-1)^{q(q-1)/2} \sum_H \rho_{H, K} \langle v^*, x_H \rangle (u^* \lrcorner x_K)
$$

where H runs through the set of subsets of $[1, p]$ with q elements, K is the complement of H in $[1, p]$ and $x_H$ (resp. $x_K$) denotes the exterior product of the $x_i$ such that $i \in H$ (resp. $i \in K$).
