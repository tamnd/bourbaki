6. Let H be a subgroup of finite index in a group G. Suppose that G is the union of the conjugates of H. Show that H = G. (Reduce it to the case where G is finite by means of Exercise 5. In the latter case, note that:

$$
\operatorname{Card}\left( \bigcup_{x \in G} x H x^{-1} \right) \leq 1 + (\operatorname{Card}(H) - 1)\operatorname{Card}(G/H).
$$

7. Let p be a prime number.
(a) Show that $n^p \equiv n \pmod{p}$ for all $n \in \mathbf{Z}$. (Reduce it to the case where n is positive and argue by induction on n using the binomial formula.)
(b) Let x, y be two elements of a group G. Suppose that

$$
yxy^{-1} = x^n, \quad \text{with } n \in \mathbf{Z}, \quad \text{and that } x^p = 1.
$$

Show that $y^p xy^{-p} = x^n$; deduce that $y^{p-1}$ commutes with x.

(c) Let G be a group all of whose elements $\neq 1$ are of order p and are conjugate to one another. Show that Card(G) is equal to 1 or 2. (Use (b) to prove that G is commutative.)

8. Let A and B be subgroups of a finite group G, N_A and N_B the normalizers of A and B, $\nu_A$ and $\nu_B$ the indices of N_A and N_B in G, $r_A$ the number of conjugates of A which contain B and $r_B$ the number of conjugates of B which contain A; show that $\nu_A r_B = \nu_B r_A$.

9. Let G be a group of permutations of a finite set E; for all $s \in G$, let $\chi(s)$ denote the cardinal of the set of fixed points of s.
(a) Show that $\sum_{s \in G} \chi(s) = N t$, where N = Card(G) and t is the number of orbits of G in E (evaluate in two ways the number of ordered pairs $(s, x) \in G \times E$ such that $s(x) = x$).
(b) Suppose that, for all $a \in E$, the stabilizer $H_a$ of a is not reduced to e. Show that, if $\chi(s)$ is independent of s for $s \neq e$ and is equal to an integer k, then

$$
k \leq t \leq 2k.
$$

(Note that Card(E) $\leq Nk$.)

In the particular case where $k = 2$, find the possible orders of the groups $H_a$; show that, if $t = 3$, the order of $H_a$ cannot be $\geq 3$ for elements of two of the three orbits unless N has one of the values 12, 24 or 60.
