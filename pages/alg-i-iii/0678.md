2. Let $F$ be an alternative A-algebra, in which the relation $2x = 0$ implies $x = 0$. Show that, for all $x, y, z$ in $F$, $x(yz)x = (xy)(zx)$ (Moufang's identity). (In the identity

$$
(xy)z + y(zx) = x(yz) + (yz)x
$$

successively replace $y$ by $xy$ and $z$ by $zx$.)

$$
(N(x) + N(y))(N(z) + N(t)) = N(x\bar{z} + y\bar{t}) + N(xt - (xz)(x^{-1}y))
$$
