### 6. LINEAR EQUATIONS IN VECTOR SPACES

PROPOSITION 12. Let E, F be two vector spaces over a field K and $u : E \to F$ a linear mapping. For the linear equation

$$
u(x) = y_0
$$

to have at least one solution $x \in E$, it is necessary and sufficient that $y_0$ be orthogonal to the kernel of the transpose mapping ${}^t u$.

The orthogonal of $u(E)$ in $F^*$ is ${}^t u^{-1}(0)$ (\S 2, no. 5, Corollary to Proposition 8) and the orthogonal of ${}^t u^{-1}(0)$ in F is therefore $u(E)$ (no. 5, Theorem 7 (ii)).

We shall obtain a more convenient criterion for systems of scalar linear equations

$$
\langle x, x_i^* \rangle = \eta_i \quad (i \in I)
$$

where the unknown $x$ takes its values in a vector space E over a field K, the $x_i^*$ are linear forms on E and the right hand sides $\eta_i$ elements of K.

If we consider a basis $(a_\lambda)_{\lambda \in L}$ of E, the system (21) is equivalent to the system of equations

$$
\sum_{\lambda \in L} \xi_\lambda \langle a_\lambda, x_i^* \rangle = \eta_i \quad (i \in I)
$$

with $x = \sum_{\lambda \in L} \xi_\lambda a_\lambda$, the solutions of (22) being of necessity families $(\xi_\lambda)$ of elements of K of finite support.

DEFINITION 4. The dimension of the subspace of $E^*$ generated by the family $(x_i^*)$ is called the rank of the system (21).

PROPOSITION 13. For the system (21) to be of finite rank r, it is necessary and sufficient that the linear mapping $u : x \mapsto (\langle x, x_i^* \rangle)$ of E into $K_s^I$ be of rank r.

If F' is the subspace of $E^*$ generated by the $x_i^*$, the kernel of u is the orthogonal F of F' in E; if F' is of dimension r, F is of codimension r and conversely (no. 5, Theorem 7) and $\mathrm{rg}(u) = \mathrm{codim}_E F$ (no. 4, formula (11)).
