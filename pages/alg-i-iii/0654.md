¶ 7. Let E be a vector space of finite dimension n over a field K. For every integer r $\geqslant 1$, the symmetric group $S_r$ operates linearly on the tensor product $E^{\otimes r}$ by the action $(\sigma, z) \mapsto \sigma . z$ such that

$$
\sigma . (x_1 \otimes x_2 \otimes \cdots \otimes x_r) = x_{\sigma^{-1}(1)} \otimes x_{\sigma^{-1}(2)} \otimes \cdots \otimes x_{\sigma^{-1}(r)}
$$

for every sequence of r vectors $(x_i)_{1 \leq i \leq r}$ of E. Let $u_\sigma(z) = \sigma . z$. Show that for every sequence $(v_i)_{1 \leq i \leq r}$ of r endomorphisms of E,

(*) $$
\operatorname{Tr}(u_\sigma \circ (v_1 \otimes v_2 \otimes \cdots \otimes v_r)) = \operatorname{Tr}(w_1) \operatorname{Tr}(w_2) \ldots \operatorname{Tr}(w_k)
$$

where the endomorphisms $w_j$ of E are defined as follows: $\sigma^{-1}$ is decomposed into cycles, $\sigma^{-1} = \lambda_1 \lambda_2 \ldots \lambda_k$ and, if $\lambda_j = (i_1 \ i_2 \ldots i_n)$, we write

$$
w_j = v_{i_1} \circ v_{i_2} \circ \cdots \circ v_{i_n}.
$$

(Reduce it to calculating the left hand side of (*) when each $v_i$ is of the form $x \mapsto \langle x, a_h^* \rangle a_k$, where $(a_j)_{1 \leq j \leq n}$ is a basis of E and $(a_j^*)_{1 \leq j \leq n}$ the dual basis.)

¶ 8. Let A be an integral domain and K its field of fractions; for every A-module M, let $\tau(M)$ denote its torsion module (II, § 7, no. 10).

(a) Show that for the algebra $T(M)$ to have no divisors of zero, it is necessary and sufficient that the A-module $T(M)$ be torsion-free (observe that $T(M)_K$ is isomorphic to $T(M_K)$ and use Exercise 5).

(b) Let $u : M \to M'$ be an injective A-module homomorphism. Show that $\operatorname{Ker}(T(u)) \subset \tau(T(M))$; if $M'$ is torsion-free, then $\operatorname{Ker}(T(u)) = \tau(T(M))$ (cf. II, § 7, no. 10, Proposition 27).

(c) Give an example of a torsion-free A-module M such that $\tau(T(M))$ is non-zero (II, § 7, Exercise 31).
