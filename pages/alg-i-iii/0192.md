(a) Let $H, H' \in S$, with $H \neq H'$, and let $x \in H - \{e\}$, $x' \in H' - \{e\}$. Show that $x x' \in G - \Theta$. (Reduce it to the case where H is one of the $G_i$; write $H'$ in the form $y G_j y^{-1}$ and proceed as in the preceding exercise.)
