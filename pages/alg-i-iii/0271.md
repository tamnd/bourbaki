### 4. OPERATORS ON A TENSOR PRODUCT; TENSOR PRODUCTS AS MULTIMODULES

With the hypotheses and notation of no. 1, for every endomorphism $u$ (resp. $v$) of the $A$-module $E$ (resp. $F$), $u \otimes 1_F$ (resp. $1_E \otimes v$) is an endomorphism of the $\mathbf{Z}$-module $E \otimes_A F$; it follows immediately from (5) (no. 2) that the mapping $u \mapsto u \otimes 1_F$ (resp. $v \mapsto 1_E \otimes v$) is a *ring homomorphism*

$$
\operatorname{End}_A(E) \to \operatorname{End}_\mathbf{Z}(E \otimes_A F)
$$

(resp. $\operatorname{End}_A(F) \to \operatorname{End}_\mathbf{Z}(E \otimes_A F)$); moreover,

$$
(u \otimes 1_F) \circ (1_E \otimes v) = (1_E \otimes v) \circ (u \otimes 1_F) = u \otimes v
$$

and therefore (§ 1, no. 14) $E \otimes_A F$ has a canonical *left bimodule* structure with respect to the rings $\operatorname{End}_A(E)$ and $\operatorname{End}_A(F)$.

This being so, suppose given on $E$ a $((B'_i); A, (C'_j))$-multimodule structure and on $F$ a $(A, (B''_h); (C''_{k_c}))$-multimodule structure (§ 1, no. 14); it amounts to the same to say that ring homomorphisms $B'_i \to \operatorname{End}_A(E)$, $C'_j{}^0 \to \operatorname{End}_A(E)$ are given with pairwise permutable images, and ring homomorphisms $B''_h \to \operatorname{End}_A(F)$, $C''_{k_c}{}^0 \to \operatorname{End}_A(F)$ with pairwise permutable images. If these homomorphisms are composed respectively with the canonical homomorphisms $\operatorname{End}_A(E) \to \operatorname{End}_\mathbf{Z}(E \otimes_A F)$ and $\operatorname{End}_A(F) \to \operatorname{End}_\mathbf{Z}(E \otimes_A F)$ defined above, it is seen (taking account of (7)) that ring homomorphisms

$$
\begin{align*}
B'_i &\to \operatorname{End}_\mathbf{Z}(E \otimes_A F), & C'_j{}^0 &\to \operatorname{End}_\mathbf{Z}(E \otimes_A F) \\
B''_h &\to \operatorname{End}_\mathbf{Z}(E \otimes_A F), & C''_{k_c}{}^0 &\to \operatorname{End}_\mathbf{Z}(E \otimes_A F)
\end{align*}
$$
