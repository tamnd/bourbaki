As the $ S(M_\lambda) $ are commutative, so is their tensor product (\S 4, no. 5) and hence (no. 1, Proposition 2) there exists a unique algebra homomorphism $ h : S(M) \to \bigotimes_{\lambda \in L} S(M_\lambda) $ such that $ h \circ \phi'_M = u $; on the other hand, it is immediate that $ u(M) $ is contained in the submodule of elements of degree 1 of the graded algebra $ \bigotimes_{\lambda \in L} S(M_\lambda) $ and hence $ h $ is a graded algebra homomorphism.
