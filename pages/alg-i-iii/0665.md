$$
\begin{vmatrix}
0 & 1 & 1 & 1 & \ldots & 1 \\
1 & 0 & a_1 + a_2 & a_1 + a_3 & \ldots & a_1 + a_n \\
1 & a_2 + a_1 & 0 & a_2 + a_3 & \ldots & a_2 + a_n \\
1 & a_3 + a_1 & a_3 + a_2 & 0 & \ldots & a_3 + a_n \\
\ldots & \ldots & \ldots & \ldots & \ldots & \ldots \\
1 & a_n + a_1 & a_n + a_2 & a_n + a_3 & \ldots & 0
\end{vmatrix}
= (-1)^n 2^{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^n a_1 a_2 \ldots a_{i-1} a_{i+1} \ldots a_n
$$

15. Show that if the columns of a square matrix of order $n$ over $\mathbf{A}$ are linearly independent, the rows of the matrix are also linearly independent (cf. II, § 10, Exercise 3).

16. Let $\mathbf{E}, \mathbf{F}$ be two free $\mathbf{A}$-modules of respective dimensions $m$ and $n$. For a linear mapping $u : \mathbf{E} \to \mathbf{F}$ to be injective, it is necessary and sufficient that $m \leq n$ and that, if $X$ denotes the matrix of $u$ with respect to any two bases of $\mathbf{E}$ and $\mathbf{F}$, there exist no scalar $\mu \neq 0$ such that the products by $\mu$ of all the minors of $X$ of order $m$ are zero.

17. Let

(*) $$
\sum_{j=1}^n \alpha_{ij} \xi_j = \beta_i \quad (1 \leq i \leq m)
$$

be a system of $m$ linear equations in $n$ unknowns over a commutative ring $\mathbf{A}$. Let $x_j$ ($1 \leq j \leq n$) be the columns of the matrix $X = (\alpha_{ij})$ of this system and $y = (\beta_i)$; suppose that in $X$ all the minors of order $> p$ are zero but that $x_1 \wedge x_2 \wedge \cdots \wedge x_p \neq 0$. For the system (*) to have a solution, it is necessary that $x_1 \wedge x_2 \wedge \cdots \wedge x_p \wedge y = 0$. Conversely, if this condition holds, there exist $n + 1$ elements $\xi_j$ ($1 \leq j \leq n + 1$) of $\mathbf{A}$ such that $\xi_{n+1} \neq 0$ and

$$
\sum_{j=1}^n \alpha_{ij} \xi_j = \beta_i \xi_{n+1} \quad (1 \leq i \leq m).
$$
