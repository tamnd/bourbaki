11. Let E, F be two vector spaces over a field K and $u : E \to F$ a linear mapping. Show that for every vector subspace V of F,
$$
\dim(u^{-1}(V)) = \dim(V \cap \mathrm{Im}(u)) + \dim(\mathrm{Ker}(u)).
$$

12. Let E, F be two vector spaces and $u, v$ two linear mappings of E into F.
(a) Show that the following inequalities hold:
$$
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{rg}(v) &\leq \mathrm{rg}(u + v) + \mathrm{rg}(u) \\
\mathrm{rg}(u + v) &\leq \inf(\dim(E), \dim(F), \mathrm{rg}(u) + \mathrm{rg}(v)).
\end{align*}
$$
If E and F are finite-dimensional, show that $\mathrm{rg}(u + v)$ can take any integer value satisfying the inequalities
$$
|\mathrm{rg}(u) - \mathrm{rg}(v)| \leq \mathrm{rg}(u + v) \leq \inf(\dim(E), \dim(F), \mathrm{rg}(u) + \mathrm{rg}(v)).
$$
(b) Show that
$$
\dim(\mathrm{Ker}(u + v)) \leq \dim(\mathrm{Ker}(u) \cap \mathrm{Ker}(v)) + \dim(\mathrm{Im}(u) \cap \mathrm{Im}(v)).
$$
(c) Show that
$$
\dim(\mathrm{Coker}(u + v)) \leq \dim(\mathrm{Coker}(u)) + \mathrm{rg}(v).
$$
(If V is a supplementary subspace of $\mathrm{Ker}(v)$ in E, note that
$$
u(\mathrm{Ker}(v)) \subset \mathrm{Im}(u + v) \quad \text{and} \quad \dim(u(V)) \leq \mathrm{rg}(v).
$$)

13. Let E, F, G be three vector spaces and $u : E \to F, v : F \to G$ two linear mappings.
(a) Show that there exists a decomposition of E as a direct sum of $\mathrm{Ker}(u)$ and two subspaces M, N such that $\mathrm{Ker}(v \circ u) = M \oplus \mathrm{Ker}(u)$ and
$$
\mathrm{Im}(v \circ u) = v(u(N)).
$$
(b) $\dim(\mathrm{Ker}(u)) \leq \dim(\mathrm{Ker}(v \circ u)) \leq \dim(\mathrm{Ker}(u)) + \dim(\mathrm{Ker}(v));$ if $\mathrm{Ker}(u)$ and $\mathrm{Ker}(v)$ are finite-dimensional, show that $\dim(\mathrm{Ker}(v \circ u))$ can take every integer value satisfying the above inequalities.
(c) The following equalities hold:
$$
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{rg}(u) &= \mathrm{rg}(v \circ u) + \dim(\mathrm{Im}(u) \cap \mathrm{Ker}(v)) \\
\mathrm{rg}(v) &= \mathrm{rg}(v \circ u) + \operatorname{codim}_F(\mathrm{Im}(u) + \mathrm{Ker}(v)).
\end{align*}
$$
If F is finite-dimensional, show that $\mathrm{rg}(v \circ u)$ can take any integer value satisfying the inequality
$$
\sup(0, \mathrm{rg}(u) + \mathrm{rg}(v) - n) \leq \mathrm{rg}(v \circ u) \leq \inf(\mathrm{rg}(u), \mathrm{rg}(v)).
$$

14. Let E, F, G be three vector spaces and $u : E \to F, w : E \to G$ two linear mappings. Show that if $u^{-1}(0) \subset w^{-1}(0)$, there exists a linear mapping v of F into G such that $w = v \circ u$ (cf. § 2, Exercise 8).
