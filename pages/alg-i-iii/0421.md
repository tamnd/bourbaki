5. Let $A, B$ be two rings, $E$ a right $A$-module, $F$ a left $A$-module and $G$ a $(B, A)$-bimodule.
(a) Show that there exists one and only one $\mathbf{Z}$-linear mapping
$$
\eta : E \otimes_A F \to \mathrm{Hom}_B(\mathrm{Hom}_A(E, G), G \otimes_A F)
$$
which maps the tensor product $x \otimes y$, where $x \in E$ and $y \in F$, to the mapping $v_{x, y} : u \mapsto u(x) \otimes y$ of $\mathrm{Hom}_A(E, G)$ into $G \otimes_A F$. When $B = A$ and $G = {}_sA_d$, the homomorphism reduces to the canonical homomorphism (15) (no. 2).
(b) Show that if $F$ is a projective $A$-module and $E$ and $G$ are such that for $x \neq 0$ in $E$ there exists $u \in \mathrm{Hom}_A(E, G)$ such that $u(x) \neq 0$, then the homomorphism $\eta$ is injective.
