ALGEBRAIC STRUCTURES

6. SYLOW SUBGROUPS

**Definition 10.** — Let $G$ be a finite group. A Sylow $p$-subgroup of $G$ is any subgroup $\mathrm{P}$ of $G$ satisfying the two following conditions:

(a) $\mathrm{P}$ is a $p$-group.

(b) $(G:\mathrm{P})$ is not a multiple of $p$.

If the order of $G$ is written in the form $p^r m$, where $m$ is not a multiple of $p$, conditions (a) and (b) are equivalent to $\operatorname{Card}(\mathrm{P})=p^r$.

**Examples.** (1) In the group $\mathfrak{S}_p$ let $\zeta$ be a cycle of order $p$. The subgroup generated by $\zeta$ is a Sylow $p$-subgroup of $\mathfrak{S}_p$ for $p$ does not divide $(p-1)!$.

(2) *Let $k$ be a finite field of characteristic $p$ and let $n$ be a positive integer. The strict triangular group $\mathrm{T}_1(n,k)$ is a Sylow $p$-subgroup of the group $\mathrm{GL}(n,k)$. *

**Theorem 2.** — Every finite group contains a Sylow $p$-subgroup.

The proof depends on the following lemma.

**Lemma.** — Let $n=p^r m$, where $m$ is an integer which is not a multiple of $p$. Then

$$
\binom{n}{p^r}\ne 0\pmod p.
$$

Let $S$ be a group of order $p^r$ (for example $\mathbf{Z}/p^r\mathbf{Z}$) and $T$ a set with $m$ elements. Write $X=S\times T$ and let $E$ be the set of subsets of $X$ with $p^r$ elements. Then $\operatorname{Card}(X)=n$, whence $\operatorname{Card}(E)=\binom{n}{p^r}$ (Set Theory, III, § 5, no. 8, Corollary 1 to Proposition 11). Let $S$ operate on $X$ by $s.(x,y)=(sx,y)$ $(s,x\in S,y\in T)$ and consider the canonical extension of this operation to $E$. In the notation of no. 5, Proposition 11, the set $E^S$ is the set of orbits of $X$, that is the set of subsets $Y\subset X$ of the form $S\times\{t\}$, $t\in T$, whence $\operatorname{Card}(E^S)=m$. By no. 5, Proposition 11,

$$
\binom{n}{p^r}=\operatorname{Card}(E)=\operatorname{Card}(E^S)=m\ne0\pmod p,
$$

which proves the lemma.
