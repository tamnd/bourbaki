¶ 1) Let G be a locally compact group, $ \beta $ a left Haar measure on G, A a $ \beta $-measurable subset of G, and $ \nu $ a nonzero positive measure on G. Assume that sA is locally $ \nu $-negligible for every $ s \in G $. Show that A is locally $ \beta $-negligible. (Reduce to the case that A is relatively compact and $ \nu $ is bounded. Prove that $ \nu $ and $ \varphi_A \cdot \beta $ are convolvable and that $ \nu * \beta \varphi_A = 0 $, whence $ 0 = \| \nu * \varphi_A \cdot \beta \| = \| \nu \| \cdot \| \varphi_A \cdot \beta \| $.) Show, on admitting the continuum hypothesis, that this result may fail to hold if A is not assumed to be $ \beta $-measurable. (Take $ G = \mathbf{R}^2 $, take for $ \nu $ the Haar measure on the subgroup $ \mathbf{R} \times \{0\} $, and apply Exer. 7 c) of Ch. V, §8.)

2) Let H be the additive group $ \mathbf{R} $ equipped with the discrete topology. Let G be the locally compact group $ \mathbf{R} \times \mathbf{R} \times H $. Let $ \alpha $ be a Haar measure on G, $ \beta $ a Haar measure on $ \mathbf{R} \times H $, and $ \mu = \varepsilon_0 \otimes \beta \in \mathcal{M}(G) $. Construct a function $ f \geq 0 $ on G, locally $ \alpha $-negligible (hence such that $ \mu $ and $ f $ are convolvable) but such that no translate of $ f $ is $ \mu $-measurable. (Imitate the construction of Ch. V, §3, Exer. 4.)

¶ 3) Let G be a locally compact group.
a) Let $ \mu $ be a nonzero bounded positive measure on G such that $ \mu * \mu = \mu $. Show that the support S of $ \mu $ is compact. (Let $ f \in \mathcal{K}_+(G) $ with $ f \neq 0 $; choose a left Haar measure on G, with respect to which convolution products will be taken; we have $ \mu * f \in \mathcal{K}(G) $; let $ x \in S $ be such that $ (\mu * f)(x) = \sup_{y \in S} (\mu * f)(y) $; show that $ (\mu * f)(y) = (\mu * f)(x) $ for every $ y \in S $.)
b) Show that S is a compact subgroup of G and that $ \mu $ is the normalized Haar measure of S. (Make use of a), Exer. 21 of GT, III, §4, and Exer. 5 of §3.)

¶ 4) Let G be a locally compact group. For every $ t \in \mathbf{R}_+^* $, let $ \mu_t $ be a nonzero bounded positive measure on G. Assume that the mapping $ t \mapsto \mu_t $ is continuous for the topology $ \sigma(\mathcal{M}^1(G), \overline{\mathcal{K}(G)}) $, and that $ \mu_{s+t} = \mu_s * \mu_t $ (s, t in $ \mathbf{R}_+^* $).
a) Show that there exists a number $ c \in \mathbf{R} $ such that $ \| \mu_t \| = \exp(ct) $. (Observe that $ t \mapsto \| \mu_t \| $ is lower semi-continuous and that $ \| \mu_{s+t} \| = \| \mu_s \| \cdot \| \mu_t \| $. Make use of Prop. 18.)
b) Suppose that $ c = 0 $. Show that $ \mu_t $ converges weakly as $ t \to 0 $ to the normalized Haar measure of a compact subgroup of G. (Make use of the weak compactness of the unit ball of $ \mathcal{M}^1(G) $, and show that for every weak cluster point $ \mu $ of $ t \mapsto \mu_t $ with respect to the filter of neighborhoods of 0 in $ \mathbf{R}_+^* $, one has $ \mu_t * \mu = \mu_t $ for every $ t $, then $ \mu * \mu = \mu $; next, apply Exer. 3.)

¶ 5) Let G be a locally compact group countable at infinity, $ \beta $ a left Haar measure on G, $ a \in \mathbf{R}_+^* $, and $ (\nu_u)_{0 \leq u \leq a} $ a family of positive measures on G satisfying the following conditions:
(i) $ \nu_0 = \varepsilon_e $; $ \nu_{u+v} = \nu_u * \nu_v $ if $ u + v \leq a $; $ \nu_u = \nu_u^\vee $;
(ii) for $ 0 < u \leq a $, $ \nu_u = f_u \cdot \beta $ with $ f_u \geq 0 $ lower semi-continuous;
(iii) $ \nu_u $ is a vaguely continuous function of $ u $.
a) Let $ f \in \mathcal{K}_+(G) $. Show that, for $ 0 < u \leq a $,

$$
\int (f_{u/2} * f)(z)^2 d\beta(z) = \int f(x)(f_u * f)(x) d\beta(x).
$$
