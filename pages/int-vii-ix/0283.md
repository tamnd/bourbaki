COROLLARY. — *Let F be a barreled nuclear space; equip F' with a locally convex topology $\mathcal{T}$ intermediate to the weak topology $\sigma(F', F)$ and the topology of uniform convergence on the compact convex subsets of F. The Fourier transformation is a bijection of the set of bounded (positive) measures on F' onto the set of continuous functions of positive type on F.*

This follows immediately from Theorem 4 and the Cor. of Th. 2 of No. 10.*
