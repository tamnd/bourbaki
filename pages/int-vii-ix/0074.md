**Lemma 8. — a)** *Let $ U = (u_{ij}) \in \mathbf{SL}(n, K) $. In order that $ U \in \Omega' $, it is necessary and sufficient that $ \det(u_{ij})_{k \leq i, j \leq n} \neq 0 $ for $ k = 2, 3, \ldots, n $.*
