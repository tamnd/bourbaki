7) Let G be a locally compact group and $ \beta $ a left Haar measure on G. Show that the algebra $ L^1(G, \beta) $ has a unity element if and only if G is discrete. (Suppose G non-discrete, and let $ f_0 \in L^1(G, \beta) $. There exists a compact neighborhood V of e such that
$$
\int_V |f_0(x)|\, d\beta(x) < 1.
$$
Let U be a symmetric compact neighborhood of e such that $ U^2 \subset V $. Then, for almost every $ x \in U $,
$$
|(\varphi_U * f_0)(x)| = \int_U |f_0(y^{-1}x)|\, d\beta(y) \leq \int_V |f_0(x)|\, d\beta(x) < 1,
$$
thus $ f_0 $ is not a unity element for $ L^1(G, \beta) $.)
