3) Let T be a Polish space and $ (\mu_n) $ a sequence of bounded positive measures on T converging tightly to a measure $ \mu $. Denote by $ \mathcal{C} $ the set of subsets A of T having the following property: for every $ \varepsilon > 0 $, there exists a compact subset K of A such that $ \sup_{n} \mu_n(A - K) < \varepsilon $. Let $ \mathcal{D} $ be the set of subsets A of T that, together with their complement, belong to $ \mathcal{C} $.

a) If the sets A and $ A' $ belong to $ \mathcal{C} $, then so do $ A \cup A' $ and $ A \cap A' $; deduce from this that $ \mathcal{D} $ is a clan of subsets of T.

b) Every subset A of T whose boundary is $ \mu $-negligible belongs to $ \mathcal{D} $ (apply Prokhorov’s theorem to the interior of A, and use Exer. 2 e)).

c) Every $ A \in \mathcal{D} $ is a convergence set for the sequence $ (\mu_n) $ (cf. Exer. 2); conversely, every open or closed convergence set belongs to $ \mathcal{D} $.

d) Let $ A \in \mathcal{D} $. Show that there exist a sequence of disjoint compact sets $ K_p $ and a subset N of T having the following properties: $ \alpha) $ each $ K_p $ is a convergence set for the sequence $ (\mu_n) $; $ \beta) $ $ A \subset N \cup \bigcup_p K_p $ and $ \bigcup_p K_p \subset A \cup N $; $ \gamma) $ for every $ \varepsilon > 0 $, there exists an open neighborhood U of N such that $ \sup_{n} \mu_n(U) < \varepsilon $ (apply Exer. 2 f) and Prokhorov’s theorem to a suitable subspace of T that is the intersection of a sequence of open sets).

4) Let T be a completely regular space, t a point of T, and $ \mathcal{U} $ a set of Borel subsets of T that generates the filter of neighborhoods of t. Let I be a set equipped with a filter $ \mathfrak{F} $, and let $ (\mu_i)_{i \in I} $ be a family of bounded positive measures of total mass 1 on T. In order that $ \lim_{i, \mathfrak{F}} \mu_i = \varepsilon_t $, it is necessary and sufficient that $ \lim_{i, \mathfrak{F}} \mu_i(U) = 1 $ for every $ U \in \mathcal{U} $.

5) Let E be a real Hilbert space admitting an orthonormal basis $ (x_n)_{n \in \mathbf{N}} $. Let T be the space E equipped with the weakened topology, and let $ (a_n)_{n \in \mathbf{N}} $ be a sequence of real numbers such that $ 0 < a_n < 1 $ for all n and $ \lim_{n \to \infty} n^2 \log a_n = 0 $. For every $ n \in \mathbf{N} $, set $ \mu_n = \sum_{p \in \mathbf{N}} a_n^p (1 - a_n) \cdot \varepsilon_{n \cdot x_p} $. Show that $ \mu_n $ is a bounded positive measure on T of total mass 1 for every $ n \in \mathbf{N} $, that $ \lim_{n \to \infty} \mu_n = \varepsilon_0 $ (tight convergence), and that $ \lim_{n \to \infty} \mu_n(K) = 0 $ for every compact subset K of T (apply the criterion of the preceding exercise to the set $ \mathcal{U} $ of neighborhoods of 0 of the form $ \{ x | (a|x| \leq 1 \} $, where a runs over E). In particular, the set of elements of the sequence $ (\mu_n)_{n \in \mathbf{N}} $ is a relatively compact subset of $ \mathcal{M}_+^b(T) $ that does not satisfy Prokhorov’s condition.

6) Let T be a topological space and H a set of positive measures on T; assume that the following condition is fulfilled:
(V) Every point of T admits an open neighborhood W such that $ \sup_{\mu \in H} \mu^\bullet(W) $ is finite.

Finally, let $ \mathcal{U} $ be an ultrafilter on H.

a) Let K be a compact subset of T. Show that the induced measures $ \mu_K $ ($ \mu \in H $) converge vaguely with respect to $ \mathcal{U} $ to a measure $ \pi^K $ on K.

b) Let K and L be two compact subsets of T such that $ K \subset L $; show that $ (\pi^L)_K \geq \pi^K $.

c) For every compact subset K of T, let $ i^K $ be the canonical injection of K into T. Show that the family of measures $ i^K(\pi^K) $ admits a supremum $ \pi $ in $ \mathcal{M}_+(T) $.

d) Let $ f $ be a lower semi-continuous positive function on T, and $ g $ an upper semi-continuous positive function on T with compact support. Show that $ \pi^\bullet(f) \leq \lim_{\mu, \mathcal{U}} \mu^\bullet(f) $ and $ \pi^\bullet(g) \geq \lim_{\mu, \mathcal{U}} \mu^\bullet(g) $.
