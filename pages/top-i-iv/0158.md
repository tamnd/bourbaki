¶ 13) Let $X$ be a locally compact space and $Y$ any topological space.
a) Show that the following four statements are equivalent:
$\alpha)$ $(G, X, Y)$ is a proper correspondence between $X$ and $Y$.
$\beta)$ For each Hausdorff space $X'$ which contains $X$ as a subspace, $G$ is closed in $X' \times Y$.
$\gamma)$ $G$ is closed in $X \times Y$, and for each $y \in Y$ there is a neighbourhood $V$ of $y$ in $Y$ and a compact subset $K$ of $X$ such that $(X - K) \times V$ does not meet $G$.
$\delta)$ There is a compact space $X'$, containing $X$ as a subspace, such that $G$ is closed in $X' \times Y$.
[To prove that $\alpha) \implies \beta)$ use Exercise 12 a) and c). To show that $\delta) \implies \alpha)$, use Proposition 2 of no. 1 and Corollary 5 of Theorem 1 of no. 2.]

b) Show that if $(G, X, Y)$ is proper, then:
$\zeta)$ For every compact subset $L$ of $Y$, $\overline{G}(L)$ is a compact set.
Show also that if $Y$ is locally compact and if $G$ is closed in $X \times Y$ and satisfies $\zeta)$, then $(G, X, Y)$ is proper. [Use the equivalence of $\alpha)$ and $\gamma)$.] This last assertion is no longer valid when $Y$ is Hausdorff but not locally compact (Exercise 4).

14) Let $Y$ be a locally compact space, $X$ a topological space. Show that a mapping $f : X \to Y$ is continuous if and only if, for each compact space $Y'$ which contains $Y$ as a subspace, the graph of $f$ is closed in $X \times Y'$. [Use Exercises 13 and 10 a)].

15) Let $X$ be a Hausdorff space, $A$ a closed subset of $X$, $f$ a proper mapping of $X - A$ into a locally compact space $Y$, $Y'$ the one-point compactification of $Y$, and $\omega$ the point at infinity in $Y'$. Let $g$ be the mapping of $X$ into $Y'$ which coincides with $f$ on $X - A$ and maps each point of $A$ to $\omega$. Show that $g$ is continuous.
