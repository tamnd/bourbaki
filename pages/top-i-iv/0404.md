4) Let $ (a_n) $ be any sequence of finite real numbers $ \geq 0 $. Show that the sequence $ \left( \frac{a_n}{(1 + a_1)(1 + a_2) \ldots (1 + a_n)} \right) $ is summable in $ \mathbf{R} $ (express each term of this sequence as a difference).

5) Let $ (d_n) $ be a sequence of finite real numbers $ \geq 0 $ such that
$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} d_n = + \infty.
$$
What can be said about the convergence of the series whose general terms are
$$
\frac{d_n}{1 + d_n}, \quad \frac{d_n}{1 + nd_n}, \quad \frac{d_n}{1 + n^2 d_n}, \quad \frac{d_n}{1 + d_n}?
$$
