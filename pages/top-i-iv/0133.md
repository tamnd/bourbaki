Show that $ \varprojlim Y_\alpha $ can be canonically identified with $ X $.

For each $ \alpha $, let $ u_\alpha : X_\alpha \to X'_\alpha $ be a continuous mapping such that the $ u_\alpha $ form an inverse system of mappings.
