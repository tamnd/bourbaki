a) Show that $x \to \omega(x; f)$ is upper semi-continuous on the subspace $A$ of $X$ where this mapping is defined.

c) Let $f$ be a lower semi-continuous finite real-valued function on $X$. Show that if, at a point $a \in X$, $\omega(a; f)$ has a finite value, then
$$
\liminf_{x \to a} \omega(x; f) = 0.
$$
