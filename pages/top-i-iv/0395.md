The mapping $x \to ]\leftarrow, x]$ of $X$ into $X_1$ is strictly order-preserving, and if we identify $X$ with its image under this mapping, then $X$ is dense in $X_1$ and $\mathcal{T}_0(X)$ is induced by $\mathcal{T}_0(X_1)$. $X_1$ is connected if and only if $X$ is without gaps (§ 2, Exercise 7).
