a) Show that if $f$ is open (resp. closed, proper, injective, surjective) then so is $\bar{f}$. (To prove that if $f$ is proper then $\bar{f}$ is proper, show that the inverse image of a point of $X'/G$ under $\bar{f}$ is quasi-compact.)
