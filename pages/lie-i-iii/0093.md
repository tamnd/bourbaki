11. Let $g$ be a Lie algebra and $b$ an ideal of $g$ such that $\mathscr{D}b = b$. Show that $b$ is a characteristic ideal of $g$.

12. Let $g$ be a Lie algebra.
(a) For a derivation $D$ of $g$ to commute with all the inner derivations of $g$, it is necessary and sufficient that $D$ map $g$ into its centre.
(b) Suppose henceforth that $g$ has centre zero, so that $g$ is identified with an ideal of the Lie algebra $\mathfrak{D}$ of all derivations of $g$. Show that the centralizer of $g$ in $\mathfrak{D}$ is zero (use (a)). In particular, the centre of $\mathfrak{D}$ is zero.
(c) Show that a derivation $\Delta$ of $\mathfrak{D}$ such that $\Delta(g) = \{0\}$ is zero. ($[ \Delta(\mathfrak{D}), g ] \subset \Delta([\mathfrak{D}, g]) + [\mathfrak{D}, \Delta(g)] \subset \Delta(g) = \{0\}$ and hence $\Delta(\mathfrak{D}) = \{0\}$ by (b).)
(d) Show that, if further $g = \mathscr{D}g$, every derivation $\Delta$ of $\mathfrak{D}$ is inner (use (c) and Exercise 11).

13. Let $g$ be a Lie algebra and $a$ and $b$ two submodules of $g$. For every integer $i \geqslant 0$ we define the submodules $m_i = m_i(a, b)$ and $n_i = n_i(a, b)$ as follows: $m_1 = b, m_{i+1} = [m_i, b], n_1 = a, n_{i+1} = [n_i, b]$. Show that
$$
[a, m_i] \subset n_{i+1}
$$
for $i = 1, 2, \ldots$ (argue by induction on $i$, observing that
$$
m_i([a, b], b) = m_i(a, b)
$$
and $n_i([a, b], b) = n_{i+1}(a, b)$).

¶ 14. Let $a$ be a Lie algebra and $b$ a subalgebra of $a$. A composition series joining $a$ to $b$ is a decreasing sequence $(a_i)_{0 \leqslant i \leqslant n}$ of subalgebras of $a$ such that $a_0 = a, a_n = b, a_{i+1}$ is an ideal of $a_i$ for $0 \leqslant i < n$. $b$ is called a subinvariant subalgebra of $a$ if there exists a composition series joining $a$ to $b$.
(a) Let $b$ be a subinvariant subalgebra of $a$ and $(a_i)_{0 \leqslant i \leqslant n}$ a composition series joining $a$ to $b$. Deduce from Exercise 13 that $[\mathscr{C}^{n+p}b, a] \subset \mathscr{C}^p b$ by observing that $[a_i, b] \subset a_{i+1}$ for $1 \leqslant i < n$.
(b) Deduce from (a) that the intersection $\mathscr{C}^\infty b$ of the $\mathscr{C}^p b$ ($p = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$) is an ideal of $a$.
(c) Deduce from (b) that a subinvariant subalgebra $c$ of $a$ such that $c = \mathscr{D}c$ is an ideal of $a$.
(d) When $K$ is a field and $\dim_K a < +\infty$, show that the intersection $\mathscr{D}^\infty b$ of the $\mathscr{D}^p b$ ($p = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$) is an ideal of $a$. (Show that this intersection is a subinvariant subalgebra of $a$ and apply (c).)
