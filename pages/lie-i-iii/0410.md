4. Let $ n \in \mathbf{N}^* $, $ p $ be a prime number and $ G $ the set of matrices belonging to $ \mathbf{GL}(n, \mathbf{Z}_p) $ all of whose elements are congruent to 1 modulo $ p $ if $ p \neq 2 $ (resp. modulo 4 if $ p = 2 $). Then $ G $ is an open subgroup of $ \mathbf{GL}(n, \mathbf{Z}_p) $.

(a) Show that $ G $ has no element of finite order $ \neq 1 $.

(b) Show that every finite subgroup of $ \mathbf{GL}(n, \mathbf{Z}_p) $ is isomorphic to a subgroup of $ \mathbf{GL}(n, \mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z}) $ if $ p \neq 2 $ (resp. $ \mathbf{GL}(n, \mathbf{Z}/4\mathbf{Z}) $ if $ p = 2 $).

¶ 5. (a) Let $ \Gamma $ be a compact subgroup of $ G = \mathbf{GL}(n, \mathbf{Q}_p) $. Show that there exists a conjugate of $ \Gamma $ which is contained in $ \mathbf{GL}(n, \mathbf{Z}_p) $. (If $ T $ is a lattice of $ \mathbf{Q}_p^n $ with respect to $ \mathbf{Z}_p $ (Commutative Algebra, Chapter VII, § 4, Definition 1), show that the stabilizer of $ T $ in $ \Gamma $ is open in $ \Gamma $ and hence of finite index and $ \sum_{\gamma \in \Gamma} \gamma T $ is a lattice which is stable under $ \Gamma $.)

(b) Deduce that $ G_f $ is the union of the conjugates of $ \mathbf{GL}(n, \mathbf{Z}_p) $.

(c) Deduce that every finite subgroup $ \Phi $ of $ \mathbf{GL}(n, \mathbf{Q}_p) $ has order a divisor of $ a_n(p) $, where $ a_n(p) $ is defined by
$$
a_n(p) = (p^n - 1)(p^n - p) \ldots (p^n - p^{n-1}) \quad \text{if } p \neq 2 \\
a_n(p) = 2^{n^2}(2^n - 1)(2^n - 2) \ldots (2^n - 2^{n-1}) \quad \text{if } p = 2.
$$
(Using (a), it can be assumed that $ \Phi \subset \mathbf{GL}(n, \mathbf{Z}_p) $. Then use Exercise 4.)

(d) Show that every finite subgroup of $ \mathbf{SL}(n, \mathbf{Q}_p) $ has order a divisor of $ s_n(p) $, where $ s_n(p) = \frac{a_n(p)}{p-1} $ for $ p \neq 2 $ and $ s_n(2) = \frac{a_n(2)}{2} $.

¶ 6. In this exercise, $ l $ denotes a prime number $ \neq 2 $. If $ a $ is an integer $ \neq 0 $, let $ v_l(a) $ denote the $ l $-adic valuation of $ a $, i.e. the largest integer $ e $ such that $ a \equiv 0 \pmod{l^e} $.

(a) Let $ m $ be an integer $ \geq 1 $. We write
$$
\varepsilon(l, m) = 0 \qquad \text{if } m \neq 0 \pmod{(l-1)}
$$
$$
\varepsilon(l, m) = v_l\left(\frac{m}{l-1}\right) + 1 \qquad \text{if } m \equiv 0 \pmod{(l-1)}.
$$
Show that, if $ x $ is an integer prime to $ l $, then
$$
v_l(x^m - 1) \geq \varepsilon(l, m)
$$
and that there is equality if the image of $ x $ in the cyclic group $ (\mathbf{Z}/l^2\mathbf{Z})^* $ is a generator of this group.

(b) Let $ n $ be an integer $ \geq 1 $. We write
$$
r(l, n) = \sum_{m=1}^n \varepsilon(l, m).
$$
Show that
$$
r(l, n) = \left[ \frac{n}{l-1} \right] + \left[ \frac{n}{l(l-1)} \right] + \left[ \frac{n}{l^2(l-1)} \right] + \cdots
$$
