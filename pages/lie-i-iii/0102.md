6. Let g be a Lie p-algebra over a ring K such that pK = {0} (p prime), U its enveloping algebra and σ the canonical mapping of g into U. Let J be the two-sided ideal of U generated by the elements (σ(x))^p − σ(x^{[p]}) where x runs through g. The associative algebra Ũ = U/J is called the restricted enveloping algebra of g. The mapping σ defines when passing to the quotient a mapping ŝ (called canonical) of g into Ũ, which is a p-homomorphism (when Ũ is considered as a Lie p-algebra).

(a) The algebra Ũ and the mapping ŝ are solutions of a universal mapping problem: for every p-homomorphism f of g into an associative algebra B over K (considered as a Lie p-algebra), there exists one and only one K-homomorphism f' of Ũ into B (with their associative algebra structures) such that f = f' ∘ ŝ.
