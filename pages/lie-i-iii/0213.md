(b) Let R be a normal subgroup of F, let G = F/R and let J be the kernel of the canonical homomorphism $ \gamma : K[F] \to K[G] $. Show that J is generated as a left (resp. right) ideal by the elements $ r - 1 $, where $ r \in R $. Prove the exactness of the sequence

(*) $$
0 \longrightarrow J/(J.I) \xrightarrow{\alpha} I/(J.I) \xrightarrow{\beta} K[G] \xrightarrow{\delta} K \longrightarrow 0,
$$

where $ \alpha $ is derived by taking quotients from the inclusion of J in I and $ \beta $ is derived by taking quotients from the restriction of $ \gamma $ to I.

(c) For $ u \in I, x \in X $, let $ \overline{D}_x(u) $ denote the image of $ D_x(u) $ in $ K[G] $ under $ \gamma $. Show, using (a), that the family $ (\overline{D}_x)_{x \in X} $ defines on taking quotients an isomorphism of $ I/(J.I) $ onto $ K[G]^{\infty} $.

(d) For $ r \in R $, let $ \theta(r) $ be the image of $ r - 1 $ in $ J/(J.I) $. Show that

$$
\theta(rr') = \theta(r) + \theta(r') \quad \text{and} \quad \theta(ryr^{-1}) = y.\theta(r) \quad \text{for } r, r' \text{ in } R, y \in F.
$$

(Use the identities

$$
rr' - 1 = (r - 1)(r' - 1) + (r - 1) + (r' - 1)
$$
$$
yry^{-1} - 1 = y(r - 1)(y^{-1} - 1) + y(r - 1).
$$)

Deduce that $ \theta $ defines a homomorphism of $ R/(R, R) $ into $ J/(J.I) $ compatible with the action of G and show that the image of this homomorphism generates the K-module $ J/(J.I) $; when $ K = \mathbf{Z} $, show that an isomorphism is thus obtained of $ R/(R, R) $ onto $ J/(J.I) $ (define the inverse homomorphism directly).

(e) Let $ (r_\alpha)_{\alpha \in A} $ be a family of elements of R generating R as a normal subgroup of F. Show that the $ \theta(r_\alpha) $ generate the $ K[G] $-module $ J/(J.I) $.

(f) The matrix $ (\overline{D}_x(r_\alpha))_{x \in X, \alpha \in A} $ defines a homomorphism

$$
\rho : K[G]^{(A)} \to K[G]^{\infty}
$$

of left $ K[G] $-modules. Show that the sequence

(**) $$
K[G]^{(A)} \xrightarrow{\rho} K[G]^{\infty} \xrightarrow{\delta} K[G] \xrightarrow{\varepsilon} K \longrightarrow 0
$$

is exact, where $ \delta $ is the homomorphism $ (u_x) \mapsto \sum_{x \in X} u_x(\gamma(x) - 1) $.
