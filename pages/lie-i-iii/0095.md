19. (a) Let $L$ be an associative algebra over $K$. Given $k$ elements $x_1, \ldots, x_k$ of $L$, we write:

$$
f_k(x_1, \ldots, x_k) = \sum_{\sigma \in S_k} x_{\sigma(1)} \cdots x_{\sigma(k)}.
$$

In the algebra $L \otimes_K K[T_1, \ldots, T_k]$, where the $T_i$ are indeterminates, $f_k(x_1, \ldots, x_k)$ is the coefficient of $T_1 \ldots T_k$ in $(x_1 T_1 + \cdots + x_k T_k)^k$; it is also the coefficient of $T_1 \ldots T_k$ in:

$$
\sum_{j=0}^{k-1} (x_1 T_1 + \cdots + x_{k-1} T_{k-1})^j x_k T_k (x_1 T_1 + \cdots + x_{k-1} T_{k-1})^{k-j-1}.
$$

Given two elements $x, y$ in $L$, we define $g_{i, k-i}(x, y)$ as the coefficient of $T_1^i T_2^{k-i}$ in $(x T_1 + y T_2)^k$. Show that

$$
i! (k-i)! g_{i, k-i}(x, y) = f_k(t_1, \ldots, t_k)
$$

where $t_h = x$ for $h \leq i$ and $t_h = y$ for $h \geq i+1$.
