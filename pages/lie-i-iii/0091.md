§ 1

1. Let $ g $ be a Lie algebra and $ a $ and $ b $ ideals (resp. characteristic ideals) of $ g $. Then the set of $ x \in g $ such that $[x, b] \subset a$ is an ideal (resp. a characteristic ideal) of $ g $ called the *transporter* of $ b $ into $ a $. Show that $ \mathcal{C}_{p+1}g $ is the transporter of $ g $ into $ \mathcal{C}_p g $.

2. If $ g $ is an $ n $-dimensional Lie algebra over a field $ K $ and the centre of $ g $ is of dimension $ \geq n - 1 $, $ g $ is commutative.

3. Let $ M $ be a not necessarily associative algebra over a field $ K $, $(e_i)_{i \in I}$ a basis of the vector space $ M $ and $ c_{ijk} $ the constants of structure of $ M $ relative to the basis $(e_i)$. For $ M $ to be a Lie algebra, it is necessary and sufficient that the $ c_{ijk} $ satisfy the following conditions: (a) $ c_{ilk} = 0 $; (b) $ c_{ijl} = -c_{jik} $; (c)
$$
\sum_{r \in I} (c_{ijr}c_{rkl} + c_{jkr}c_{rlk} + c_{ktr}c_{rjl}) = 0 \text{ for all } i, j, k, l \text{ in } I.
$$

4. (a) Suppose that 2 is invertible in $ K $. Let $ a $ be a Lie algebra over $ K $. On the $ K $-module $ a' = a \times a $ we define a bracket by the formula:
$$
[(x_1, x_2), (y_1, y_2)] = ([x_1, y_1] + [x_2, y_2], [x_1, y_2] + [x_2, y_1]).
$$
Then $ a' $ is a Lie $ K $-algebra and the mappings $ x \mapsto \frac{1}{2}(x, x) $, $ x \mapsto \frac{1}{2}(x, -x) $ are isomorphisms of $ a $ onto ideals $ b, c $ of $ a' $ of which $ a' $ is the direct sum. (Consider the quadratic extension $ K' $ of $ K $ with basis 1 and $ k $ where $ k^2 = 1 $. Form $ a_{(K_1)} $ and then restrict the ring of scalars to $ K $. Then observe that $ \frac{1}{2}(1 + k) $, $ \frac{1}{2}(1 - k) $ are idempotents of $ K' $ and that $ (1 + k)(1 - k) = 0 $.)

(b) Let $ a $ be a real Lie algebra, $ g = a_{(\mathbf{C})} $ and $ b $ the real Lie algebra derived from $ g $ by restricting the field of scalars to $ \mathbf{R} $. Show that $ b_{(\mathbf{C})} $ is isomorphic to $ a'_{(\mathbf{C})} $, where $ a' $ is the algebra introduced in (a). (Consider $ V = \mathbf{C} \otimes_{\mathbf{R}} \mathbf{C} $ as a vector space over $ \mathbf{C} $ by $ (z \otimes z')z'' = z \otimes z'z'' $. Observe that $ V $ is the complexification of the real vector subspace of $ V $ generated by $ 1 \otimes 1 $ and $ i \otimes i $ and that this subspace is identified with the quadratic extension of $ \mathbf{R} $ with basis 1 and $ k $ where $ k^2 = 1 $.)
