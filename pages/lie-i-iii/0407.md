¶ 23. Let $ G $ be a real Lie group and $ c = (U, \phi, E) $ a chart on the manifold $ G $ centred on the identity element $ e $. If $ x \in U $, let $ |x|_c $ denote the norm of $ \phi(x) $ in the Banach space $ E $.

(a) Show that, if $ c' = (U', \phi', E') $ is another chart centred on $ e $, there exist constants $ \lambda > 0 $ and $ \mu > 0 $ such that
$$
\mu|x|_c \leq |x|_{c'} \leq \lambda|x|_c
$$
for all $ x \in G $ sufficiently close to $ e $.

(b) Show that, for all $ \rho > 0 $, there exists a neighbourhood $ U_\rho $ of $ e $ contained in $ U $ such that, for $ x, y $ in $ U_\rho $, $ (x, y) \in U_\rho $ and
$$
|(x, y)|_c \leq \rho \inf(|x|_c, |y|_c).
$$

(c) Suppose that $ G $ is finite-dimensional. Let $ \Gamma $ be a discrete subgroup of $ G $. Apply (b) with $ 0 < \rho < 1 $ and choose $ U_\rho $ to be relatively compact. The set $ U_\rho \cap \Gamma $ is finite. Let
$$
\{e = \gamma_0, \gamma_1, \ldots, \gamma_m\}
$$
be its elements, numbered so that
$$
|\gamma_0|_c \leq |\gamma_1|_c \leq \cdots \leq |\gamma_m|_c.
$$
Show that, if $ i \leq m $ and $ j \leq m $, the commutator $ (\gamma_i, \gamma_j) $ is equal to one of the $ \gamma_k $ with $ k < \inf(i, j) $. Deduce that the subgroup generated by $ U_\rho \cap \Gamma $ is nilpotent of class $ \leq m $.

Deduce from the above the existence of a neighbourhood $ V $ of $ e $ such that, for every discrete subgroup $ \Gamma $ of $ G $, there exists a nilpotent integral subgroup $ N $ of $ G $ containing $ V \cap \Gamma $.
