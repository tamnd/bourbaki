### 3. FILTERED BIGEBRAS

DEFINITION 2. Let $E$ be a bigebra with coproduct $c$. A filtration compatible with the bigebra structure on $E$ is an increasing sequence $(E_n)_{n \geq 0}$ of submodules of $E$ such that

$$
\begin{align*}
E_0 &= K.1, \quad E = \bigcup_{n \geq 0} E_n \\
E_m \cdot E_n &\subset E_{m+n} \quad \text{for } m \geq 0, n \geq 0 \\
c(E_n) &\subset \sum_{i+j=n} \operatorname{Im}(E_i \otimes E_j) \quad \text{for } n \geq 0.\dagger
\end{align*}
$$

A bigebra with a filtration compatible with its bigebra structure is called a filtered bigebra.

Example. Let $E$ be a graded bigebra (Algebra, Chapter III, § 11, no. 4, Definition 3) and $(E^n)_{n \geq 0}$ its graduation. We write $E_n = \sum_{i=0}^n E^i$. The sequence $(E_n)$ is a filtration compatible with the bigebra structure on $E$.
