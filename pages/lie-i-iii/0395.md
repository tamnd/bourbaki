5. Let $ (e_1, e_2, e_3) $ be the canonical basis of $ \mathbf{R}^3 $. We give $ \mathbf{R}^3 $ the nilpotent Lie algebra structure $ g $ such that $ [e_1, e_2] = e_3,\ [e_1, e_3] = [e_2, e_3] = 0 $. Let $ c_{ijk} $ denote the constants of structure of $ g $ relative to the basis $ (e_1, e_2, e_3) $.

(a) Show that on $ \mathbf{R}^3 $ the differential forms
$$
\omega_1 = dx_1,\quad \omega_2 = dx_2,\quad \omega_3 = -x_1 dx_2 + dx_3
$$
satisfy the relations
$$
d\omega_k = -\sum_{i < j} c_{ijk} \omega_i \wedge \omega_j \qquad (k = 1, 2, 3)
$$
and are linearly independent at each point of $ \mathbf{R}^3 $.

(b) Deduce that there exists, on an open neighbourhood of 0 in $ \mathbf{R}^3 $, a Lie group germ structure $ G $ such that $ L(G) = g $ and

6. Let $ G $ be a finite-dimensional Lie group, $ g $ its Lie algebra, $ g^* $ the dual vector space of $ g $, $ \sigma $ the adjoint representation of $ G $ on $ g $ and $ \rho $ the representation of $ G $ on $ g^* $ defined by $ \rho(g) = {}^t \sigma(g)^{-1} $ for all $ g \in G $.

(a) $ L(\rho)(x) = -{}^t(\mathrm{ad}\ x) $ for all $ x \in g $.

(b) Let $ f \in g^* $. Let $ G_f $ denote the stabilizer of $ f $ in $ G $; it is a Lie subgroup of $ G $ and $ g_f = L(G_f) $ is the set of $ x \in G $ such that $ {}^t(\mathrm{ad}\ x)f = 0 $.

(c) For $ x,\ y $ in $ g $, we write $ B_f(x, y) = f([x, y]) $. Let $ \beta_f $ denote the non-degenerate alternating bilinear form on $ g/g_f $ (which is therefore of even dimension) derived from $ B_f $ when passing to the quotient.
