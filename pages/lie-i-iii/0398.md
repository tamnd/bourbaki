§ 9. Let G be a compact Lie group over K of dimension n. Suppose that the Lie algebra L(G) is simple. For all g ∈ G, every integer m ≥ 1 and every neighbourhood V of e in G, let M(g, m, V) denote the set of elements of G of the form $ \prod_{i=1}^m x_i(y_i, g)x_i^{-1} $ with $ x_1, \ldots, x_m, y_1, \ldots, y_m $ in V.

(a) Show that if g is an element of G whose centralizer is not open, if m is an integer ≥ n and if V is a neighbourhood of e, then M(g, m, V) is a neighbourhood of e. (There exists $ a \in L(G) $ such that $ (\mathrm{Ad}\,g)(a) \neq a $. Let φ be an exponential mapping of G. Let y be the image of 1 under the tangent mapping at 0 to $ \lambda \mapsto (\phi(\lambda a), g) $ (where $ \lambda \in K $). Then $ b = (\mathrm{Ad}\,g - 1)(a) \neq 0 $. There exist $ x_1, \ldots, x_m $ in V such that $ \{(\mathrm{Ad}\,x_1)(b), \ldots, (\mathrm{Ad}\,x_m)(b)\} $ contains a basis of L(G). Consider the mapping

$$
(x'_1, \ldots, x'_m, y_1, \ldots, y_m) \mapsto \prod_{i=1}^n x'_i(y_i, g)x'_i^{-1}
$$

of $ G^{2m} $ into G. Show that the tangent mapping at $ (x_1, \ldots, x_m, e, \ldots, e) $ is surjective.)

(b) Let U be a neighbourhood of e in G. Let m be an integer ≥ 1. There exists an element g of G whose centralizer is not open, such that $ M(g, m, G) \subset U $. (Argue by reductio ad absurdum using the compactness of G.)

(c) Let G' be a compact Lie group over K whose Lie algebra is simple. Let $ \phi : G \to G' $ be a bijective homomorphism of abstract groups. Then $ \phi $ is continuous. (Apply (b) to G' and (a) to G.)
