**Corollary.** Let $ G $ be a finite-dimensional real Lie group, $ \mu $ (resp. $ \nu $) a left (resp. right) Haar measure on $ G $, $ k $ an integer $ \geqslant 0 $, $ u \in U_k(G) $ and $ f $ and $ g $ real-valued functions of class $ C^k $ on $ G $ with compact support. Then

$$
\int_G (R_u f) g \, d\mu = \int_G f (R_u^\nu g) \, d\mu
$$
$$
\int_G (L_u f) g \, d\nu = \int_G f (L_u^\nu g) \, d\nu.
$$

This follows from Proposition 57 and \emph{Differentiable and Analytic Manifolds}, R, 14.3.8.

### 17. LEFT DIFFERENTIAL

**Definition 9.** Let $ G $ be a Lie group, $ M $ a manifold of class $ C^r $ and $ f $ a mapping of class $ C^r $ of $ M $ into $ G $. The left (resp. right) differential of $ f $ is the differential form of degree 1 on $ M $ with values in $ L(G) $ which associates with every vector $ u \in T_m(M) $ the element $ f(m)^{-1} \cdot (T_m f)(u) $ (resp. $ (T_m f)(u) \cdot f(m)^{-1} $).
