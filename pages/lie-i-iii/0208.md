10. Let $x, y$ be in $G$ and let $r, s$ be two integers $\geq 1$. Suppose that $(x^r, y^s) = e$.
(a) Show that, for all $n \geq 1$, $(x, y)^{(rs)^n} \in C^{n+2} G$. (It can be assumed that $G$ is generated by $\{x, y\}$; then apply Exercise 9 (b), noting that $(x^r, y^s) \equiv (x, y)^{rs}$ mod. $C^3 G$.)
(b) Suppose that $x^r = y^s = e$; let $t$ denote the g.c.d. of $r$ and $s$. Show that $(x, y)^t \in C^3 G$ and deduce that $(x, y)^{tn} \in C^{n+2} G$ for all $n \geq 1$ (same method).

11. Let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$ and let $m$ be an integer $\geq 1$. Suppose that $G$ is generated by a family $(x_i)_{i \in I}$ such that $x_i^m \in H$ for all $i$.
(a) Let $H$ be given the filtration induced by the filtration $(C^n G)$ on $G$ and let $\operatorname{gr}(H)$ be identified with a graded Lie subalgebra of $\operatorname{gr}(G)$, cf. Exercise 2. Show that, for all $n \geq 0$,
$$
m^n \cdot \operatorname{gr}_n(G) \subset \operatorname{gr}_n(H).
$$
Deduce that, for all $z \in H$, $C^n G$, $z^m \in H$, $C^{n+1} G$.
(b) If $G$ is nilpotent, show that there exists an integer $N \geq 0$ (depending only on the nilpotency class of $G$) such that $z^{m^N} \in H$ for all $z \in G$.

12. Suppose that $G$ is nilpotent. Let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$ and let $m$ be an integer $\geq 1$ and let $x, y$ be elements of $G$ such that $x^m \in H$ and $y^m \in H$. Show that there exists an integer $N \geq 0$ such that $(xy)^{m^N} \in H$. (Apply Exercise 11 to the group generated by $\{x, y\}$ and its intersection with $H$.)

13. (a) Let $F$ be a free group with basic family $\{\bar{x}, \bar{y}\}$ of two elements, let $c$ be an integer $\geq 2$ and let $m$ be an integer $\geq 1$. We write $F^c = F/C^c F$ and denote by $x, y$ the images of $\bar{x}, \bar{y}$ in $F^c$. Let $F_m^c$ be the subgroup of $F^c$ generated by $\{x^m, y\}$. Show that there exists an integer $N \geq 0$ such that $z^{m^N} \in F_m^c$ for all $z \in F^c$ (use Exercise 11).
(b) Let $I^c$ (resp. $I_m^c$) be the normal subgroup of $F^c$ (resp. $F_m^c$) generated by $y$. Show that, if $N$ is chosen as above and $z \in I^c$, then $z^{m^N} \in I_m^c$ (note that $F^c/I^c$ is an infinite cyclic group with generator the image of $x$ and deduce that $F_m^c/I_m^c \to F^c/I^c$ is injective and hence that $I_m^c = F_m^c \cap I^c$). In particular $xy^{m^N} x^{-1} \in I_m^c$.
(c) Suppose that $G$ is nilpotent. Let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$ and let $L$ be a normal subgroup of $H$. Let $g \in G$ and let $m \geq 1$ be such that $g^m \in H$. Show that, if $N$ is sufficiently large, $g l^{m^N} g^{-1} \in L$ for all $l \in L$. (If $G$ is of class $< c$, choose $N$ as in (a) and use the homomorphism $f : F^c \to G$ such that $f(x) = g, f(y) = l$; note that $f(F_m^c) \subset H, f(I_m^c) \subset L$ and apply (b) above.)
