Let $ B $ denote the subring of $ K(X) $ consisting of the rational functions $ P/Q $ such that $ c(P) \geq c(Q) $ (show that this condition depends only on the element $ P/Q $, using Exercise 30 (a) and 12 (c)). (b) Show that $ B $ is a Bezoutian domain (Exercise 20) (observe that, if $ P, Q $ are two polynomials of $ A[X] $, $ P + X^n Q $ divides $ P $ and $ Q $ in $ B $ if $ m $ is sufficiently large).
