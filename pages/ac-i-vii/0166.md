24. (a) Let $A$ be a ring and $M, N$ two $A$-modules such that $M$ is finitely generated. Show that $\operatorname{Supp}(\operatorname{Hom}_A(M, N))$ is contained in the intersection $\operatorname{Supp}(M) \cap \operatorname{Supp}(N)$.

(b) Give an example where $M$ and $N$ are both finitely presented and $\operatorname{Supp}(\operatorname{Hom}_A(M, N))$ is strictly contained in $\operatorname{Supp}(M) \cap \operatorname{Supp}(N)$ (take $A = \mathbf{Z}_{(p)}$ and $N = A$).

(c) Let $M$ be the $\mathbf{Z}$-module the direct sum of the $\mathbf{Z}/p^k\mathbf{Z}$ ($p$ prime, $k \in \mathbf{N}$). Show that $\operatorname{Supp}(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Z}}(M, M))$ is not contained in $\operatorname{Supp}(M)$.

25. Let $A$ be a Noetherian ring, $X = \operatorname{Spec}(A)$ its spectrum, $M$ a finitely generated $A$-module and $Y = \operatorname{Supp}(M)$. Let $Y_k$ ($1 \leq k \leq n$) be the distinct connected components of $Y$.

(a) Show that there exists a unique decomposition of $M$ as a direct sum $M = M_1 \oplus M_2 \oplus \ldots \oplus M_n$ such that $\operatorname{Supp}(M_k) = Y_k$ for all $k$.

(b) If $a = \operatorname{Ann}(M)$ and $a_k = \operatorname{Ann}(M_k)$, show that the $a_k$ are relatively prime in pairs and that $\prod_{k=1}^n a_k = a$.

(Using Proposition 17 of no. 4, reduce it to the case where $a = 0$ and $Y = X$ and apply Proposition 15 of no. 3.)

26. Let $\phi : A \to B$ be a ring homomorphism such that the mapping

{}^a\phi : \operatorname{Spec}(B) \to \operatorname{Spec}(A)

is surjective. Let $N$ be a finitely generated $A$-module; show that, if $u : M \to N$ is an $A$-module homomorphism such that $u \otimes 1 : M_{(B)} \to N_{(B)}$ is surjective, then $u$ is surjective (apply Proposition 19 of no. 4 to $\operatorname{Coker}(u)$).

27. Give an example of a local homomorphism $\rho : A \to B$ and an $A$-module $M$ (not finitely generated) such that $\operatorname{Supp}(M_{(B)})$ is not equal to ${}^a\rho^{-1}(\operatorname{Supp}(M))$ (take $A = \mathbf{Z}_{(p)}$ and $B = \mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z}$ for some prime number $p$).

28. Give an example of a $\mathbf{Z}$-module $M$ such that, for some prime number $p$ such that $(p) \in \operatorname{Supp}(M)$, there exists no $\mathbf{Z}$-homomorphism $\neq 0$ from $M$ to $\mathbf{Z}/p\mathbf{Z}$.
