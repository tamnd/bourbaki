9. MODULES OF FRACTIONS OF GRADED MODULES

Let $ A $ be a graded ring, $ M $ a graded $ A $-module and $ A $ the degree monoid; we shall assume in this no. that $ A $ is a *group*. Recall (*Algebra*, Chapter II, § 11) that $ A $ and $ M $ are respectively direct sums of additive groups
$$
A = \bigoplus_{i \in \Delta} A_i, \qquad M = \bigoplus_{i \in \Delta} M_i
$$
with $ A_i A_j \subset A_{i+j} $ and $ A_i M_j \subset M_{i+j} $ for all $ i, j \in A $. Let $ S $ be a multiplicative subset of $ A $ *all of whose elements are homogeneous*. For all $ i \in A $, we write $ S_i = S \cap A_i $ and denote by $ (S^{-1}M)_i $ the set of elements $ m' $ of $ S^{-1}M $ for which there exist elements $ j, k $ of $ A $, an element $ m \in M_j $ and an element $ s \in S_k $ such that $ j - k = i $ and $ m' = m/s $. If $ (m'_q)_{1 \leq q \leq r} $ is a finite family of elements of $ S^{-1}M $ such that
$$
m'_q \in (S^{-1}M)_{j(q)},
$$
there exist elements $ j(q) \in A $ and $ k \in A $, elements $ m_q \in M_{j(q)} $ ($ 1 \leq q \leq r $) and $ s \in S_k $ such that $ m'_q = m_q/s $ for $ 1 \leq q \leq r $ (no. 1, *Remark 2*).

**Proposition 21.** *The ring $ S^{-1}A $ with the family $ ((S^{-1}A)_i) $ is a graded ring and $ S^{-1}M $ with the family $ ((S^{-1}M)_i) $ is a graded module over the graded ring $ S^{-1}A $. The canonical mappings $ i^S_A $ and $ i^S_M $ are homogeneous of degree 0.*

Let $ m \in M $, $ s \in S_k $, $ m' \in M_{j'} $, $ s' \in S_{k'} $ and suppose that $ j - k = j' - k' = i $; then $ (m/s) - (m'/s') = (s'm - sm')/ss' $ and $ s'm - sm' \in M_{j'+k'} = M_{j'+k'} $ and $ ss' \in S_{k+k'} $, hence $ (m/s) - (m'/s') \in (S^{-1}M)_i $ by definition; this shows that the $ (S^{-1}M)_i $ are additive subgroups of $ S^{-1}M $.
