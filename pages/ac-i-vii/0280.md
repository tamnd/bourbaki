**7.** Let $(A_\alpha, f_{\mu\lambda})$ be a directed direct system of Noetherian local rings; if $m_\lambda$ is the maximal ideal of $A_\lambda$, suppose that, for $A \leq \mu, m_ = m_\lambda A_\mu$ and that $A_$ is a flat $A_\lambda$-module. Show then that $A = \varprojlim A_\lambda$ is Noetherian and a flat $A_\lambda$-module for all $\lambda$. (If $m = m_\lambda A$ is the maximal ideal of $A$ (Chapter II, § 3, Exercise 16), show that on $A$ the $m$-adic topology is Hausdorff, observing that $A$ is a faithfully flat $A_\lambda$-module for all $A$. Then prove that, if $\hat{A}$ is the completion of $A$ with respect to the $m$-adic topology, $\hat{A}$ is Noetherian, using § 2, no. 10, Corollary 5 to Theorem 2; finally, prove that, for all $A$, $\hat{A}$ is a flat $A$-module using Theorem 1 of no. 2 and Proposition 2 of no. 3.)

**8.** Let $A$ be a Noetherian local ring, $m$ its maximal ideal and $k = A/m$ its residue field. Let $K$ be an extension of $k$; show that there exists a local homomorphism from $A$ to a Noetherian local ring $B$ such that $B/mB$ is isomorphic to $K$ and $B$ is a flat $A$-module. (Consider first the case where $K = k(t)$, distinguishing two cases according to whether $t$ is algebraic or transcendental over $k$; consider next a family $(K_\lambda)$ of subfields of $K$ containing $k$, which is well ordered by inclusion and such that, if $K_\lambda$ has a predecessor $K_\mu$, $K_\lambda = K_\mu(t_\mu)$ for some $t_\mu \in K_$. Finally, apply Exercise 7.)
