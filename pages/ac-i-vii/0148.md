The mappings $ u_{m,A_s} $ form a direct system, whose direct limit is a mapping (called canonical) from $ M = \mathrm{Hom}_A(A_s, M) $ to $ M_{(\mathcal{F})} $. Similarly, if $ u : M \to N $ is a left $ A $-module homomorphism, the $ v_m = \mathrm{Hom}_A(1, u) : \mathrm{Hom}_A(m, M) \to \mathrm{Hom}_A(m, N) $ form a direct system, whose direct limit is a commutative group homomorphism

$$

$$

$$
