¶ 6. Let A be a ring and E an A-module.

(a) Show that for a prime ideal p of A to belong to Supp(E), it is necessary and sufficient that there exist a submodule F of E such that $p \in \operatorname{Ass}(E/F)$ (to see that the condition is necessary, consider a submodule F of E of the form px, where $x \in E$; to see that the condition is sufficient, use Proposition 7 of no. 3).

(b) Suppose that A is Noetherian and E finitely generated. Show that for every prime ideal $p \in \operatorname{Supp}(E)$, there exists a composition series $(E_i)_{0 \leq i \leq n}$ of E such that, for $0 \leq i \leq n - 1$, $E_i/E_{i+1}$ is isomorphic to $A/p_i$, where $p_i$ is a prime ideal and one of the $p_i$ is equal to p.

¶ 7. Let A be a Noetherian ring, M a finitely generated A-module and $a = \operatorname{Ann}(M)$.

(a) Show that, if a is prime, a is the least element of $\operatorname{Ass}(M)$. (Note that $a = \bigcap_{p \in \operatorname{Ass}(M)} p$.)

(b) Show that every prime ideal associated with $A/a$ is associated with M. (Note that if p is a prime ideal of A the annihilator of the class in $A/a$ of an element $a \in A$, then $p = \operatorname{Ann}(aM)$ and use (a).)

(c) Let p, q be two distinct prime ideals of A such that $p \subset q$. Show that, if $M = (A/p) \oplus (A/q)$, then $\operatorname{Ass}(A/a) \neq \operatorname{Ass}(M)$.

8. Let A be a Noetherian ring, a an ideal of A, M a finitely generated A-module and P the submodule of M consisting of the $x \in M$ such that $ax = 0$. Show that $\operatorname{Ass}(M/P) \subset \operatorname{Ass}(M)$ (note that for all $x \in M$ the annihilator of $(Ax + P)/P$ is also that of $ax$ and use Exercise 7 (a)).

9. Let A be a Noetherian ring and a an ideal of A.

(a) For an ideal b of A to satisfy a: $b \neq a$, it is necessary and sufficient that b be contained in a prime ideal $p \in \operatorname{Ass}(A/a)$.

(b) Let A be the polynomial algebra $K[X, Y, Z]$ in 3 indeterminates over a field K. Let $n = AX, m = AX + AY + AZ, a = n \cap m^2$. Show that there is a prime ideal p containing a such that a: $p \neq a$ but which is not a prime ideal associated with A.

10. (a) Give an example of a $\mathbf{Z}$-module M such that $\operatorname{Ass}(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Z}}(M, M))$ is not contained in $\operatorname{Supp}(M)$ (cf. Chapter II, § 4, Exercise 24 (c)).

(b) Give an example of $\mathbf{Z}$-modules E, F such that
$$
\operatorname{Ass}(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbf{Z}}(E, F)) = 0,
$$
but $\operatorname{Ass}(F) \cap \operatorname{Supp}(E) \neq \varnothing$ (take $F = \mathbf{Z}$).
