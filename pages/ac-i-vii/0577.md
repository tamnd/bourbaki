(y) Every finitely generated ideal $ \neq 0 $ in $ \mathbf{A} $ is invertible (and therefore the finitely generated fractional ideals $ \neq 0 $ form a group).

(6) Every torsion-free finitely generated $ \mathbf{A} $-module is projective.

(E) For all $ x \in K $, the fractional ideal $ \mathbf{A} + \mathbf{A}x $ is invertible.

($ \zeta $) For all $ x \neq 0 $ in $ K $, there exists $ y \in K $ such that $ y \equiv 0 \pmod{1} $, $ y \equiv 0 \pmod{x} $ and $ y \equiv 1 \pmod{1-x} $.

($ \eta $) If $ (a_i) $ is a finite family of ideals of $ A $, for the system of congruences $ x \equiv c_i \pmod{a_i} $ to have a solution it is necessary and sufficient that $ c_i \equiv c_j \pmod{(a_i + a_j)} $ for every pair of indices $ i, j $ ("Chinese remainder theorem").

($ \theta $) If $ a, b, c $ are three ideals of $ \mathbf{A} $, then $ a \cap (b + c) = a \cap b + a \cap c $.

(t) If $ a, b, c $ are three ideals of $ \mathbf{A} $, then $ a + (b \cap c) = (a + b) \cap (a + c) $.

(x) $ \mathbf{A} $ is integrally closed and every finitely generated ideal of $ \mathbf{A} $ is divisorial.

($ \lambda $) $ \mathbf{A} $ is integrally closed and for $ z \neq 0 $ in $ K $ there exist $ x, y $ in $ \mathbf{A} $ such that $ z = x + yz^2 $.

(p) $ \mathbf{A} $ is integrally closed and, if $ a, b, c $ are three finitely generated fractional ideals $ \neq 0 $, the relation $ ab = ac $ implies $ b = c $.

(v) For every finitely generated $ \mathbf{A} $-module $ M $, the torsion submodule of $ M $ is a direct factor of $ M $.

($ \sigma $) Every ring $ B $ such that $ \mathbf{A} \subset B \subset K $ is an integrally closed domain.
