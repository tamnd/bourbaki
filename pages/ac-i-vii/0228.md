PROPOSITION 11. Let $ \mathbf{A} $ be a Noetherian local ring, $ m $ its maximal ideal, $ \hat{\mathbf{A}} $ its $ m $-adic completion and $ B $ a ring such that $ \mathbf{A} \subset B \subset \hat{\mathbf{A}} $. Suppose that $ B $ is a Noetherian local ring whose maximal ideal $ n $ satisfies the relation $ n = mB $. Then

$$
n^k = m^kB = \hat{m}^k \cap B
$$

for all $ k \geq 1 $, the $ n $-adic topology on $ B $ is induced by the $ m $-adic topology on $ \hat{\mathbf{A}} $, $ B $ is a faithfully flat $ \mathbf{A} $-module and there is an isomorphism of $ \hat{\mathbf{A}} $ onto the $ n $-adic completion $ \hat{B} $ of $ B $, which extends the canonical injection $ \mathbf{A} \to B $.

It is sufficient to verify the relation $ n^k = \hat{m}^k \cap B $, for, as $ B $ is dense in $ \hat{\mathbf{A}} $ and the $ n $-adic topology is induced by the $ m $-adic topology, the last assertion will follow from *General Topology*, Chapter II, § 3, no. 9, Corollary 1 to Proposition 18 and the last but one from Proposition 10. The injection $ j_A : \mathbf{A} \to \hat{\mathbf{A}} $ (resp. $ j : B \to \hat{\mathbf{A}} $) defines by taking quotients an injective homomorphism

$$
i_1 : \mathbf{A}/(\hat{m} \cap \mathbf{A}) \to \hat{\mathbf{A}}/\hat{m}
$$

(resp. $ i_B : B/(\hat{m} \cap B) \to \hat{\mathbf{A}}/\hat{m} $). We know that $ m \cap \mathbf{A} = m $ and that $ i_A $ is bijective, hence $ i_B $ is bijective, which shows that $ B/(\hat{m} \cap B) $ is a field, hence that $ \hat{m} \cap B $ is a maximal ideal of $ B $ and therefore $ m \cap B = n $. As $ \mathbf{A} = \mathbf{A} + m $, $ B = A + n = A + mB $; by induction on $ k $ we deduce that

$$
B = A + m^kB = A + n^k
$$

for all $ k > 1 $. As $ n^k \subset \hat{m}^k \cap B $, it is sufficient to show that $ \hat{m}^k \cap B \subset n^k $; if $ b \in \hat{m}^k \cap B $, we may write $ b = a + z $ where $ a \in \mathbf{A} $, $ z \in n^k $; whence

$$
a = b - z \in \hat{m}^k \cap \mathbf{A} = m^k \subset n^k
$$

and $ b \in n^k $.

\* An important case where this applies is the following: $ B $ is the ring of integral series in $ n $ variables over a complete valued field, which converge in the neighbourhood of 0, $ \mathbf{A} $ is the local ring

$$
K[X_1, \ldots, X_n]_{\mathfrak{p}}
$$

where $ \mathfrak{p} $ is the maximal ideal consisting of the polynomials with no constant term and $ \hat{\mathbf{A}} $ is the ring of formal power series $ K[[X_1, \ldots, X_n]] $. \*

Remark. A local ring $ B $ such that $ \mathbf{A} \subset B \subset \hat{\mathbf{A}} $, whose maximal ideal $ n $ is equal to $ mB $ and whose $ n $-adic topology is induced by the $ m $-adic topology on $ \mathbf{A} $, is not necessarily Noetherian (Exercise 14).
