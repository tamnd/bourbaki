§ 1

1. (a) Let $A$ be an absolutely flat ring (Chapter I, §2, Exercise 17)
Show that $\operatorname{Ass}(A)$ is the set of isolated points of $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ (note that $\operatorname{Ass}(A)$ is the set of prime ideals which are annihilators of an idempotent of $A$).

(b) Deduce from (a) an example of a ring $A$ such that $A \neq 0$ and $\operatorname{Ass}(A) = \varnothing$ (cf. Chapter II, §4, Exercise 17) and for which the conclusion of no. 1, Corollary 2 to Proposition 2 does not hold.

(c) Deduce from (b) an example of a ring $A$ and a multiplicative subset $S$ of $A$ such that the mapping $p \mapsto S^{-1}p$ of $\operatorname{Ass}(A) \cap \Phi$ to $\operatorname{Ass}(S^{-1}A)$ (no. 2, Proposition 5) is not surjective.

\* 2. Let $K$ be a commutative field and $A$ the valuation ring, whose order group is $\mathbf{Q}$, consisting of the "formal power series" $\sum_r c_r T^r$, where $c_r \in K$, $r \in \mathbf{Q}_+$ and the set of $r$ such that $c_r \neq 0$ is well ordered. Let $a$ be an irrational number $> 0$, $a$ the ideal of $A$ consisting of the elements with valuation $> a$ and $C$ the ring $A/a$. Then $\operatorname{Spec}(C)$ reduces to a point $p$; for all $x \neq 0$ in $C$, $px \neq 0$, but for all $\lambda \in p$, there exists $y \neq 0$ in $C$ such that $\lambda y = 0$. In particular, $\operatorname{Ass}(C) = \varnothing$, although $\operatorname{Supp}(C) = \operatorname{Spec}(C) = \{p\}$.

3. Let $M$ be an $A$-module and $N$ a submodule of $M$. Show that every prime ideal $p \in \operatorname{Ass}(M/N)$ which does not contain $\operatorname{Ann}(N)$ is associated with $M$. Give an example of an ideal $p$ belonging to $\operatorname{Ass}(M/N)$ but not to $\operatorname{Ass}(M)$ (take $A$ to be an integral domain, $M = A$).
