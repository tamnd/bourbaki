### 6. VALUATION RINGS IN AN ALGEBRAIC EXTENSION

PROPOSITION 6. Let K be a field, $v$ a valuation on K, A its ring, L an algebraic extension of K and $A'$ the integral closure of A in L. Let 23 be the set of valuation rings on L which extend $v$ and $\mathfrak{M}'$ the set of maximal ideals of $A'$. Then the mapping $V \mapsto m(V) \cap A'$ is a bijection of 23 onto $\mathfrak{M}'$ and $m' \mapsto A'_{m'}$ is the inverse bijection.
