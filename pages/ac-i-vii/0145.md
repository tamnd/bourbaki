10. (a) Let K be a commutative field, A the polynomial ring K[X, Y], $ p = (X), q = (Y) $ and S the complement of $ p \cup q $ in A. Show that the saturation of $ p + q $ with respect to S is distinct from the sum of the saturations of $ p $ and $ q $.
