THEOREM 6. *Let M be a torsion-free finitely generated A-module. There exists a free submodule L of M such that M/L is isomorphic to an ideal of A.*

We shall denote by $n$ the *rank* of $M$ (rank of $V = M \otimes_A K$ over $K$) and consider $M$ as a lattice of $V$ with respect to $A$. Then, for all $p \in P(A)$, $M_p$ is a lattice of $V$ with respect to $A_p$ (no. 1, *Example 6*) and, as $A$, is a principal ideal domain, $M$, is a *free* $A$-module of rank $n$. We shall write:

$$
M(p) = M_p/pM_p.
$$

We shall denote by $k(p)$ the field of fractions of $A/p$ (isomorphic to the residue field of $A_p$); $M(p) = M \otimes_A k(p)$ is therefore a vector space of rank $n$ over $k(p)$. For all $x \in M$, we shall denote by $x(p)$ the canonical image of $x$ in $M(p)$.

LEMMA 7. *Let $x_i$ ($1 \leq i \leq m$) be linearly independent elements of $M$ (over $A$ or $K$, which amounts to the same) and let $L$ be the sub-A-module of $M$ generated by the $x_i$. Then, for almost all $p \in P$, the $x_i(p) \in M(p)$ are linearly independent over $k(p)$; for them to be linearly independent over $k(p)$ for all $p \in P$, it is necessary and sufficient that $M/L$ be torsion-free.*

Let $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ be elements of $M$ which, with $x_1, \ldots, x_n$, form a basis of $V$ and let $N$ be the free sub-A-module of $M$ generated by the $x_i$ ($1 \leq i \leq n$). It follows from no. 3, Theorem 3 that $N = M$, for almost all $p \in P$; as $x_1(p), \ldots, x_n(p)$ form a basis of $N(p)$ over $k(p)$, this establishes the first assertion.

If $M/L$ is torsion-free, so is $(M/L)_p = M_p/L_p$ for all $p \in P$ (no. 1, *Example 6*) and, as $A_p$ is a principal ideal domain, $M_p/L_p$ is free.
