¶ 11. Let K be a field, L a finite Galois extension of K and $ \mathcal{G} $ its Galois group. Let $ v $ be a valuation on L with residue field k and order group $ \Gamma $; suppose that $ v $ is invariant under $ \mathcal{G} $ and that the restriction $ v|K $ has the same residue field $ k $ as $ v $, so that $ \mathcal{G} = \mathcal{G}^Z = \mathcal{G}^\Gamma $ (notation of Exercise 8).

(a) Let $ x \in L^*, \sigma \in \mathcal{G} $. Show that $ x^{-1} \sigma(x) $ is a unit for $ v $, that its image $ \varepsilon_\sigma(x) $ in $ k^* $ depends only on the valuation $ v(x) $ and the class of $ \sigma $ modulo the commutator group $ \mathcal{G}' $ of $ \mathcal{G} $ and that there is thus defined a Z-bilinear mapping (also denoted by $ \varepsilon $) $ (\mathcal{G}/\mathcal{G}') \times \Gamma \to k^* $, which is equal to 1 on $ (\mathcal{G}/\mathcal{G}') \times v(K^*) $.

(b) For all $ \sigma \in \mathcal{G} $, let $ \phi(\sigma) $ be the homomorphism $ \Gamma/v(K^*) \to k^* $ mapping $ \varepsilon_\sigma(x) $ to $ v(x) $ for all $ x \in L^* $; thus there is defined a homomorphism
$$
\phi : \mathcal{G} \to \operatorname{Hom}_Z(\Gamma/v(K^*), k^*).
$$
Show that, if $ p $ is the characteristic exponent of k, the kernel $ \mathcal{N} $ of $ \phi $ has order a power of $ p $. (In the contrary case, there would exist a $ \sigma \neq e $ in $ \mathcal{N} $ and a prime number $ q \neq p $ such that $ \sigma^q = e $; for some integer $ x $ in $ L - K $, write $ \sigma(x) = x + y $, where $ v(y) > v(x) $, and calculate $ \sigma^q(x) $ to obtain a contradiction.) Deduce that $ \mathcal{G} $ is a solvable group.

(c) Suppose that $ n = [L : K] $ is prime to $ p $; show that $ \phi $ is bijective, that $ e(L/K) = n $ and that, if $ v $ is the $ lcm $ of the orders of the elements of $ \mathcal{G}, k^* $ contains the $ v $-th roots of unity. (Use (b) and Lemma 2 of no. 1.)
