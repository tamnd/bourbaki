**Definition 5.** *With the hypotheses and notation of Proposition 6, the field of invariants $ K^Z(p') $ (resp. $ K^T(p') $) of the group $ G^Z(p') $ (resp. $ G^T(p') $) in the field $ K' $ is called the decomposition field* (resp. *inertia field*) *of $ p' $ with respect to $ K $*.
