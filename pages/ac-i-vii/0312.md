¶8. Let $A$ be a Noetherian ring, $E$ a finitely generated $A$-module, $F$ a submodule of $E$ and $m$ an ideal of $A$. Let $F = \bigcap_{p \in \operatorname{Ass}(E/F)} Q(p)$ be a reduced primary decomposition of $F$ in $E$. Show that the closure of $F$ in $E$ under the $m$-adic topology on $E$ is equal to $\bigcap_{p \in \Phi} Q(p)$, where $\Phi$ is the set of $p \in \operatorname{Ass}(E/F)$ such that $p + m \neq A$. (Consider first the case where $F$ is $p$-primary in $E$ and show that, if $p + m = A$, $F$ is dense in $E$; proceed to the general case using Exercise 15 of § 1 and Chapter 111, § 3, no. 4, Corollary 1 to Theorem 3).

(y) Every element $p \in \operatorname{Ass}(M)$ is a maximal ideal of $A$.

11. Let $A$ be a ring, $M$ an $A$-module and $N$ a submodule of $M$. The set of $a \in A$ such that the homothety of ratio $a$ on $M/N$ is almost nilpotent is called the *radical* of $N$ in $M$ and denoted by $r_M(N)$. Show that $r_M(N)$ is an ideal of $A$ and that, for all $p \in \operatorname{Ass}_f(M/N)$, $r_M(N) \subset p$. If $N_1, N_2$ are two submodules of $M$, then $r_M(N_1 \cap N_2) = r_M(N_1) \cap r_M(N_2)$; if $a$ is an ideal of $A$, $r_M(aN) \supset r(a) \cap r_M(N)$ and $r_A(a) = r(a)$.
