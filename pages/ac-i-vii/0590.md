4. Let $ k $ be a field and $ A $ the polynomial ring $ k[X, Y] $.
(a) Let $ (e_1, e_2) $ be the canonical basis of the $ A $-module $ A^2 $ and let $ E $ be the sub-$ A $-module of $ A^2 $ generated by $ (X - Y)e_1, e_1 + Xe_2 $ and $ e_1 + Ye_2 $; let $ F $ be the monogenous submodule of $ E $ generated by $ (X - Y)^2e_1 $. Show that the $ A $-module $ M = E/F $ is not the direct sum of its torsion submodule and a torsion-free module.
(b) Show that the torsion $ A $-module $ A/AXY $ is not a direct sum of monogenous submodules of the form $ A/\mathfrak{p}_i^n $, where the $ \mathfrak{p}_i $ are prime ideals of height 1.

5. Let $ A $ be an integrally closed Noetherian domain and $ M $ a finitely generated $ A $-module. Show that, if $ a, b $ are two ideals of $ A $, the $ A $-module
$$
((aM) \cap (bM))/(a \cap b)M
$$
is pseudo-zero; give an example where it is not zero.

6. Let $ k $ be a field, $ B $ the polynomial ring $ k[X, Y] $ and $ A $ the submodule $ k[X^2, XY, Y^2] $ of $ B $.
(a) Show that $ A $ is an integrally closed Noetherian domain and that $ B $ is a finitely generated $ A $-module.
(b) Show that the ideal $ p = AX^2 + AXY $ of $ A $ is a prime ideal of height 1 (and hence divisorial) but that the $ B $-module $ p \otimes_A B $ is a torsion module $ \neq 0 $ and hence not reflexive; the ideal $ pB $ of $ B $ is not divisorial, the canonical mapping $ p \otimes_A B \to pB $ is not injective and $ p $ is not a flat $ A $-module.

7. Let $ A $ be an integrally closed Noetherian domain, $ E $ a finitely generated torsion-free $ A $-module and $ E^* $ its dual.
(a) Show that the canonical homomorphism $ E^* \otimes_A E \to \operatorname{End}_A(E) $ is a pseudo-isomorphism.
(b) Deduce from (a) that for $ E $ to be a projective $ A $-module, it is necessary and sufficient that $ E^* \otimes_A E $ be a reflexive $ A $-module. (Note that, if $ E^* \otimes_A E $ is reflexive, the canonical homomorphism $ E^* \otimes_A E \to \operatorname{End}_A(E) $ is bijective.)

8. Let $ A $ be an integrally closed Noetherian domain and $ M_1, M_2 $ two finitely generated $ A $-modules.
(a) Show that the $ A $-modules $ \operatorname{Tor}_i^A(M_1, M_2) $, $ \operatorname{Ext}_A^i(M_1, M_2) $ are pseudo-zero for $ i > 2 $ (reduce it to the case where $ A $ is a principal ideal domain).
(b) If $ M_i $ is torsion-free, show that $ \operatorname{Tor}_1^A(M_1, M_i) $ and $ \operatorname{Ext}_A^1(M_1, M_2) $ are pseudo-zero (same method).
(c) If $ M_i $ is a torsion $ A $-module, show that (in the notation of no. 5):
$$
\chi(M_1 \otimes_A M_2) - \chi(\operatorname{Tor}_1^A(M_1, M_2)) = r(M_2)\chi(M_1)
$$
$$
\chi(\operatorname{Hom}_A(M_1, M_2)) - \chi(\operatorname{Ext}_A^1(M_1, M_2)) = -r(M_2)\chi(M_1)
$$
$$
\chi(\operatorname{Hom}_A(M_2, M_1)) - \chi(\operatorname{Ext}_A^1(M_2, M_1)) = r(M_2)\chi(M_1).
$$
