Remark (3). We see that an integral domain $ A $ can be such that its Hausdorff completion $ \hat{A} $ admits non-zero divisors of zero.

Proposition 19. Let $ A $ be a commutative ring and $ m $ a maximal ideal of $ A $. The Hausdorff completion $ \hat{A} $ of $ A $ with respect to the $ m $-adic topology is a local ring whose maximal ideal is $ m $.
