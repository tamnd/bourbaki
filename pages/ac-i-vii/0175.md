Graduations, Filtrations and Topologies

All the rings considered in this chapter are assumed to have a unit element; all the ring homomorphisms are assumed to map unit element to unit element. By a subring of a ring $ \mathbf{A} $ we mean a subring containing the unit element of $ \mathbf{A} $. Unless otherwise stated, all the modules are left modules.

1. FINITELY GENERATED GRADED ALGEBRAS

1. SYSTEMS OF GENERATORS OF A COMMUTATIVE ALGEBRA

Let $ \mathbf{A} $ be a commutative ring and $ \mathbf{B} $ a commutative $ \mathbf{A} $-algebra. Let us recall (Algebra, Chapter IV, § 2, no. 1) that if $ x = (x_i)_{i \in I} $ is a family of elements of $ \mathbf{B} $, the mapping $ f \mapsto f(x) $ from the polynomial algebra $ \mathbf{A}[X_i]_{i \in I} $ to $ \mathbf{B} $ is homomorphism of $ \mathbf{A}[X_i]_{i \in I} $ onto the subalgebra of $ \mathbf{B} $ generated by the $ x_i $, whose kernel $ a $ is the ideal of polynomials $ f $ such that $ f(x) = 0 $, called the *ideal of algebraic relations (with coefficients in $ \mathbf{A} $) between the $ x_i $*.

**Definition 1.** *In a commutative algebra $ \mathbf{B} $ over a commutative ring $ \mathbf{A} $, a family $ (x_i)_{i \in I} $ of elements of $ \mathbf{B} $ is called algebraically free over $ \mathbf{A} $ (or the $ x_i $ are called algebraically independent over $ \mathbf{A} $) if the ideal of algebraic relations between the $ x_i $, with coefficients in $ \mathbf{A} $, is reduced to 0. A family $ (x_i) $ which is not algebraically free over $ \mathbf{A} $ is also called algebraically related (or its elements are also called algebraically dependent over $ \mathbf{A} $).*

This definition generalizes that given in Algebra, Chapter V, § 5, no. 1, Definition 1 for families of elements of a commutative field.
