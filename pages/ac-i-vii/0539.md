### 3. LOCAL CONSTRUCTION OF REFLEXIVE MODULES

We keep the notation and hypotheses of no. 2. We shall say that a property holds *for almost all* $ p \in P $ if the set of $ p \in P $ for which it is not true *is finite*.

**Theorem 3.** *Let V be a vector space of finite rank over K and M a lattice of V with respect to A.*

(i) *Let N be a lattice of V with respect to A; then, for every prime ideal p of A, $ N_p $ is a lattice of V with respect to A, and, for almost all* $ p \in P, N_p = M_p $.

(ii) *Conversely, suppose given for all* $ p \in P $ *a lattice* $ N(p) $ *of V with respect to A, such that* $ N(p) = M_p $ *for almost all* $ p \in P $. *Then* $ N = \bigcap N(p) $ *is a reflexive lattice of A with respect to A and it is the only reflexive lattice* $ N' $ *of V with respect to A such that* $ N'_p = N(p) $ *for all* $ p \in P $.
