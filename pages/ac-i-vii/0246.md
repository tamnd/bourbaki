5. FLATNESS PROPERTIES OF FILTERED MODULES

1. IDEALLY HAUSDORFF MODULES

**Definition 1.** *Let A be a commutative ring and $ \mathfrak{g} $ an ideal of A. An A-module M is called ideally Hausdorff with respect to 3 (or simply ideally Hausdorff if there is no ambiguity) if, for every finitely generated ideal $ \mathfrak{a} $ of A, the A-module $ \mathfrak{a} \otimes_A M $ is Hausdorff with the 3-adic topology.*

Putting $ \mathfrak{a} = A $ in this definition, we have already seen that M is necessarily *Hausdorff* with the 3-adic topology.

*Examples*

(1) If A is Noetherian and $ \mathfrak{g} $ is contained in the Jacobson radical of A (in other words if A is a Zariski ring with the 3-adic topology), every *finitely generated* A-module is ideally Hausdorff (*§3*, no. 3, Proposition 6).
