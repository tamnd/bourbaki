§ 1

1. In the commutative diagram (10) suppose that the ordered pair $ (u', u') $ is an exact sequence and that $ v \circ u = 0 $. Show that

$$
\operatorname{Im}(b) \cap \operatorname{Im}(u') = b(\operatorname{Ker}(c \circ u)) \quad \text{and} \quad \operatorname{Ker}(b) + \operatorname{Ker}(v) = b^{-1}(\operatorname{Im}(u' \circ a)).
$$

2. Consider a commutative diagram of commutative groups

$$
\begin{array}{ccc}
A & \xrightarrow{u} & B & \xrightarrow{v} & C \\
a \downarrow & & b \downarrow & & c \downarrow \\
A' & \xrightarrow{u'} & B' & \xrightarrow{v'} & C' \\
a' \downarrow & & b' \downarrow & & \\
A'' & \xrightarrow{u''} & B''
\end{array}
$$

Suppose that: (1) $ (u, v) $ and $ (b, b') $ are exact sequences; (2) $ v' \circ u' = 0 $ and $ a' \circ a = 0 $; (3) $ c $ and $ u' $ are injective and $ a' $ is surjective. Show that under these conditions $ u'' $ is injective.

3. Consider a commutative diagram of commutative groups

$$
\begin{array}{cccccc}
B & \longrightarrow & C & \longrightarrow & D \\
\downarrow & & \downarrow & & \downarrow d \\
A' & \longrightarrow & B' & \longrightarrow & C' & \longrightarrow & D' \\
\downarrow & & \downarrow & & \downarrow \\
A'' & \xrightarrow{u''} & B'' & \longrightarrow & C'' \\
a'' \downarrow & & & & \\
A''' & \xrightarrow{u'''} & B'''
\end{array}
$$

in which the rows and columns are assumed to be exact, $ d $ and $ u'' $ injective and $ a'' $ surjective. Show that under these conditions $ u''' $ is injective. Generalize this result.

4. Consider a commutative diagram of commutative groups

$$
\begin{array}{cccccc}
A & \longrightarrow & B & \longrightarrow & C & \longrightarrow & D \\
a \downarrow & & b \downarrow & & c \downarrow & & d \downarrow \\
A' & \longrightarrow & B' & \longrightarrow & C' & \longrightarrow & D'
\end{array}
$$

where the two rows are assumed to be exact.
