### 2. A Uniqueness Theorem

In this subsection, for any strictly positive integer $m$, any integer $i$ in the interval $[1, m]$, and any sequence $(v_1, . . . , v_m)$ in $V^m$, we write $(v_1, . . . ,\widehat{v}_i, . . . , v_m)$ for the sequence $(v_1, . . . , v_{i-1}, v_{i+1}, . . . , v_m)$ in $V^{m-1}$.

**Proposition 1.** — Let W be a hyperplane in V and $e$ be a vector in V W. Let $\varphi$ be an element of Ω(W). There exists a unique element $\omega$ of Ω(V) such that we have (7) $\omega (w_1, . . . , w_{n-1}, e) =\varphi (w_1, . . . , w_{n-1})$ for every basis $(w_1, . . . , w_{n-1})$ of W.
