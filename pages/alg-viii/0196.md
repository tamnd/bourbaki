$\P 6)$ Let A be a Artinian ring and $n=$ long$_A(A_s)$. a) Prove that the ring A is $n$-regular (Exercise 5). b) Prove that the ring A is pseudoregular. (Let $r$ be an integer such that $<(A)^r= 0$, and let $m$ be the length of the ring $B = A/<(A)$. Prove, by reducing to the case when the ring B is simple, that for every $b\in B$, there exists an element $y$ of B that commutes with $b$ and satisfies $b^{m+1}y=b^m$. Deduce that for every $a\in A$, there exists an $x\in A$ such that $a^{mr}xa^{mr}=a^{mr}.)$ c) Let $\mathfrak{I}$ be the set of nonnilpotent left ideals of A. Prove that the minimal elements of $\mathfrak{I}$ are the ideals $Ae$ generated by an indecomposable idempotent (use a) and Exercise 5, c)). Prove that every left ideal of A is the direct sum of finitely many ideals of this type and a left ideal contained in the radical. d) A left ideal $\mathfrak{a}$ of A is generated by an idempotent if and only if it is the direct sum of a finite family of left ideals $(Ae_i)_{i\in I}$, where the $e_i$ are indecomposable orthogonal idempotents.

$\P 7)$ Let A be an Artinian ring, $\mathfrak{r}$ its radical, and A the ring $A/\mathfrak{r}$. Let $\mathfrak{l}$ be a nonnilpotent indecomposable left ideal; we have $\mathfrak{l}= Ae$, where $e$ is an idempotent in A. a) Prove that the ideal $\mathfrak{n}=\mathfrak{l}\cap \mathfrak{r}$ is the unique maximal submodule of $\mathfrak{l}$. The A-module $\mathfrak{l}/\mathfrak{n}$ is simple and isomorphic to $Ae$, where $e$ is the image of $e$ in A. b) Let M be an A-module. Then $(Ae)M$ is a submodule of M, and $e(Ae)M=eM$ is an $eAe$-module. If N is an A-submodule of M, then the $eAe$-modules $e(M/N)$ and $eM/eN$ are isomorphic. If the A-module M is simple, then either we have $eM = 0$, or M is isomorphic to $Ae$ and $eM$ is a simple $eAe$-module. c) Prove that the length of the $eAe$-module $eM$ is equal to number of quotients of a Jordan–Hölder series of the A-module M isomorphic to $Ae$. d) Suppose that M has finite length and admits a unique maximal submodule N and that $M/N$ is isomorphic to $\mathfrak{l}/\mathfrak{n}$. Prove that M is isomorphic to a quotient of $Ae$ (observe that an element $x$ of $eM$ that does not belong to N generates M).
