5) Let K be a commutative field and L a finite set. Denote the algebra constructed in Exercise 17 of VIII, p. 169 by B, the central element $1-\sum_{\lambda}c_{\lambda}+\sum_{\lambda\not=\mu}r_{\lambda \mu}$ of B by $\varepsilon$, and the algebra $B/B\varepsilon$ by A. The K-algebra A is finite-dimensional, its radical $\mathfrak{r}$ has square zero, and $A/\mathfrak{r}$ is isomorphic to $K^L$. Prove that A is not isomorphic to a product of two nonzero algebras.

$\P 6)$ Let K be a commutative field and E an extension of K. Prove that the following properties are equivalent:

(i) For every separable extension L of K, the ring $E_{(L)}$ is semisimple.

(ii) The field E is a purely inseparable extension of a separable extension of K of finite degree. (Assuming (i), use the corollary of V, §17, No. 2, p. 140 and Exercise 4 of V, §2, p. 146 to prove that E is an algebraic extension of K. Then, prove that the separable closure $E_s$ of K in E has finite degree. To do this, note that in the opposite case, the ring $E_{(L)}$, where L is a separable closure of K, would contain families of orthogonal idempotents $(e_1, . . . , e_n)$ with $n$ arbitrarily large. Assuming (ii), prove that if $E_{s(L)}$ is isomorphic to $\prod^r_{j=1}C_j$, then $E_{(L)}$ is isomorphic to $\prod^r_{j=1}D_j$, where $D_j$ is a purely inseparable extension of $C_j.)$

$\P 7)$ Let E be a $p$-radical extension of a commutative field K of characteristic $p >0$. Suppose that the ring $E\otimes_KE$ is Noetherian. a) Prove that there exists an integer $k$ such that $E\subset K^{p^{-k}}$ (reason by contradiction: construct a sequence $(x_n)$ in E such that $(x_n\otimes 1-1\otimes x_n)^{p^{n-1}}\not= 0$ and $(x_n\otimes 1-$ $1\otimes x_n)^{p^n}= 0)$. b) Let $F = K(E^p)$, and let $(x_i)_{i\in I}$ be a $p$-basis of E over F (V, §13, No. 1, p. 98). Set $y_i=x_i\otimes 1$ and $z_i=x_i\otimes 1-1\otimes x_i$. Let Λ be the subset of $\mathbf{N}^{(I)}$ consisting of the families $(\alpha_i)_{i\in I}$ such that $\alpha_i< p$ for every $i$. Prove that the elements $\prod_{i\in I}y^{\alpha}_{i^i}z_i^{\beta_i}$ for $(\alpha_i), (\beta_i)\in \Lambda$ form a basis of the F-vector space $E\otimes_FE$. Deduce that I is finite (observe that the ring $E\otimes_FE$ is Noetherian). c) Conclude that E has finite degree over K (use Exercise 1, b) of V, §13, p. 170).

$\P 8)$ Let E be an algebraic extension of a commutative field K. Prove that the ring $E\otimes_KE$ is Noetherian if and only if E has finite degree over K (reduce to the case of a purely inseparable extension using the method of Exercise 6, and then apply Exercise 7).

$\P 9)$ Let K be a commutative field and A a K-algebra. Suppose that for every Artinian K-algebra B, the ring $A\otimes_KB$ is Artinian. Prove that A is finite-dimensional over K (reduce to the case when A is semisimple by observing that the $A/<(A)$-modules $<(A)^k/<(A)^{k+1}$ have finite length; then use Exercises 6 and 7).
