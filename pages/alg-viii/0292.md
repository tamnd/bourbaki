Use Exercises 14, c) and 16 to prove that B is Galois and then Proposition 12 of VIII, p. 205 to prove that we have $B = A^H$ and that H is Galois.) b) Let $B_1$ and $B_2$ be simple subrings of A containing $A^G$ whose commutants in A are simple, and let $\varphi$ be an isomorphism from $B_1$ to $B_2$ fixing $A^G$.
