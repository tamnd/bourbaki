**Corollary.** — Let E be a projective A-module, $u$ be an element of End$^f_A(E)$, and F be a projective A-submodule of E containing Im $u$. Denote by $u_F$ the endomorphism of F induced by $u$. We have

(4) Tr($u$) $=$ Tr($u_F$).

Denote by $i$ the canonical injection of F into E and by $v: E\rightarrow F$ the homomorphism deduced from $u$. We have $u_F=v\circ i$ and $u=i\circ v$. The corollary follows.
