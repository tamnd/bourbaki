**Remark.** — Let $K'$ be a commutative K-algebra. Every element $t$ of $A_{(K')}$ can be written as $\sum_{i\in I}t_i\otimes e_i$, where $(t_i)\in K'^I$. It follows from the proof of Lemma 5 that the characteristic polynomial Pc$_{A_{(K')}/K'}(t; X)$ is equal to $P((t_i),X)^n$.

### 5. Transitivity of Reduced Norms and Traces

**Proposition 7.** — Let L be a maximal commutative semisimple subalgebra of A and $a$ an element of L. We have (52) Pcrd$_{A/K}(a; X) =$ Pc$_{L/K}(a; X)$, (53) Trd$_{A/K}(a) =$ Tr$_{L/K}(a)$, (54) Nrd$_{A/K}(a) = N_{L/K}(a)$.
