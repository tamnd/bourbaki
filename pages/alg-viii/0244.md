12) Let $A_1$ be the K-algebra $K(X)[U]_{1,d/dX}$ (VIII, p. 11), $A_2$ the K-algebra K[[T]], and A the K-algebra $A_1\otimes_KA_2$. Identify $A_1$ and $A_2$ with subalgebras of A.

a) Prove that $A_2$ is the center of A, that A does not contain any zero divisors, and that the invertible elements of A are those of $A_2$.

b) Prove that the two-sided ideals of A are the ideals AT$^n$ for $n\geqslant 0$.

c) Prove that the ring A is primitive even though its center has nonzero radical (observe that a maximal left ideal of A containing $1-$ TU does not contain any two-sided ideals other than 0).

13) Let D and E be fields with centers containing K, and let V (resp. W) be a vector space over D (resp. E). Prove that the $(D\otimes_K$ E)-module $V\otimes_KW$ is free.

$\P 14)$ Let V be a vector space over a field D. Denote the endomorphism ring of the abelian group V by Ω, and identify D with a subring of Ω. Set A = End$_D(V)$.

a) Prove that A and D are linearly disjoint over their common center Z (cf. Exercise 5, d)).

b) The image of $D\otimes_ZA$ in Ω is equal to End$_Z(V)$ if and only if D has finite rank over Z. (To see that the condition is necessary, first deduce the case V = D from Exercise 5, e), and then fix an element $x\not= 0$ of V and consider the subalgebra B of End$_Z(V)$ consisting of the Z-endomorphisms $u$ of V such that $u(Dx)\subset Dx$. Observe that the equality End$_Z(V) =$ DA implies $B = D(B\cap A).)$

15) a) Let K be a commutative field, A a K-algebra, and $B = K[X_{\iota}]_{\iota\in I}$ a polynomial algebra over K. Prove that if every nonzero element of A is regular, then the algebra $A\otimes_KB$ has the same property.

b) Deduce that if D is a field of finite rank over K and E is a purely transcendental extension of K, then the ring $D\otimes_KE$ is a field.

$\P 16)$ Let D be a field, V an infinite-dimensional right vector space over D, and A a dense subring of the ring End$_D(V)$ whose socle $\mathfrak{s}$ is not zero (VIII, p. 130, Exercises 8 and 9). Let K be a subfield of the center of D and B a K-algebra. Prove that if the radical of the K-algebra $D^o\otimes B$ is nonzero, then so is that of $A\otimes B$. (Let $\mathfrak{a}$ be the set of elements $\sum u_i\otimes b_i$ of $\mathfrak{s}\otimes B$ satisfying $\sum\langle u_i(x), x^*\rangle  \otimes b_i\in \mathfrak{R}(D^o\otimes B)$ for all $x\in V$ and $x^*\in V^*$. Prove that $\mathfrak{a}$ is a two-sided ideal of $A\otimes B$ and, using Exercises 10, e) of VIII, p. 167 and 9 of VIII, p. 131, that it is nonzero and is contained in the radical of $A\otimes B.)$

17) Let K be a commutative field and D and E fields whose centers contain K. Suppose that $[E : K] =m$ is finite and that the center of one of the fields D and E is equal to K. The K-algebra $D\otimes_KE$ is then isomorphic to the endomorphism algebra of a finite-dimensional vector space $h$ over a field C (VIII, p. 222, Corollary 2).

a) Prove that $h$ divides $m$ (cf. VIII, p. 205, formula (32)).
