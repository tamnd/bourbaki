14) Let D be a field, Z its center, V a left vector space over D, and Ω the endomorphism ring of the abelian group V. Endow Ω with its natural $(D$,D)-bimodule structure. Let Γ be the automorphism group of D and ∆ the subgroup of inner automorphisms (isomorphic to $D^*/Z^*)$.
