f ) Let A be a semisimple algebra with center K. Deduce from e) that the K-algebra $A\otimes A^o$ is semisimple if and only if A has finite rank over K (cf. VIII, p. 238, Theorem 2).

6) Let A be a K-algebra with nonzero nilpotent radical. Prove that the homomorphism $\varphi : A\otimes A^o\rightarrow$ End$_K(A)$ (Exercise 5) is not injective.

7) Let $A_1$ and $A_2$ be semisimple K-algebras and $Z_1$ and $Z_2$ their centers. Suppose that $A_1$ has finite degree over K and that the ring $Z_1\otimes Z_2$ is reduced. Let $f$ be a K-algebra homomorphism from $A_2$ to $A_1$. Prove that the commutant of $f(A_2)$ in $A_1$ is a semisimple K-algebra (view $A_1$ as an $(A_2\otimes A^o_1$)-module and use Proposition 7 of VIII, p. 221).

8) Let K be a commutative field and L a radical extension of K of finite degree $>1$. Let V be an infinite-dimensional vector space over L, and let A be the K-subalgebra of End$_L(V)$ generated by the endomorphisms of finite rank and the identity mapping. Prove that the L-algebra $A_{(L)}$ has nonzero radical even though its center is equal to L (use Exercise 7).

9) Let A and B be K-algebras, M a free A-module, and N a B-module. a) Prove that the image of the canonical homomorphism $\vartheta$ from End$_A(M)\otimes$End$_B(N)$ to End$_{A\otimes B}(M\otimes N)$ is a dense subring of the ring End$_{A\otimes B}(M\otimes N)$. b) If the ring of homotheties of the B-module N is dense in its bicommutant, then the same holds for the ring of homotheties of the $(A\otimes$ B)-module $M\otimes N$. c) Suppose that there exist an element $y$ of N and a sequence $(v_n)$ of endomorphisms of N such that the elements $v_n(y)$ generate an infinite-dimensional vector space over K. Prove that the mapping $\vartheta$ is not surjective.

10) Keep the assumptions of Exercise 9 and suppose, moreover, that the B-module N is simple; denote its commutant by D. a) The mapping $\vartheta$ is surjective if and only if M admits a finite basis over A or D is finite-dimensional over K (use Exercise 9, c)). b) Prove that the $(A\otimes$ B)-module $M\otimes N$ is semisimple if and only if the ring $A\otimes D^o$ is semisimple (reduce to the case $M = A_s$; observe that $M\otimes N$ is a free $A\otimes D^o$-module, and use Proposition 5 of VIII, p. 85 as well as Exercise 9, a)).

$\P 11)$ Let A and B be K-algebras; suppose that the rings A and B are primitive and that their respective socles $\mathfrak{s}$ and $\mathfrak{t}$ (VIII, p. 130, Exercise 8) are not reduced to 0. Let D (resp. E) be the commutant of a minimal left ideal $\mathfrak{a}$ of A (resp. $\mathfrak{b}$ of B); suppose that the algebra $D\otimes E$ is simple. Prove that $A\otimes B$ is a primitive ring with socle $\mathfrak{s}\otimes \mathfrak{t}$ (observe that the $(A\otimes$ B)-module $\mathfrak{a}\otimes \mathfrak{b}$ is isotypical of finite length; use Exercise 9, a) of VIII, p. 131).
