$\P 8)$ Let A be an Artinian ring. The A-module $A_s$ is the direct sum of a finite family of indecomposable left ideals $(Ae_i)_{i\in I}$, where the $e_i$ are orthogonal idempotents (Exercise 6, d)).

a) Two A-modules of finite length M and N are said to be linked if a quotient of a Jordan–Hölder series of M is isomorphic to a quotient of a Jordan–Hölder series of N. Let R be the finest equivalence relation on the set of ideals $Ae_i$ for which two linked ideals are equivalent. The equivalence classes $\mathscr{B}_l(l\in L)$ for R are called blocks; we denote the sum of the ideals in block $\mathscr{B}_l$ by $\mathfrak{b}_l$. Prove that the ideals $\mathfrak{b}_l$ are the only indecomposable two-sided ideals of A.

(To see that $\mathfrak{b}_l$ is two-sided, observe that we have $Ae_ixe_j\subset Ae_j$ and that by Exercise 7, c), the relation $e_iAe_j\not= 0$ implies $Ae_i\equiv Ae_j$ (mod R). On the other hand, let $(\mathfrak{b}'_m)_{m\in M}$ be a finite family of two-sided ideals with direct sum A. Prove that each of the $\mathfrak{b}'_m$ is a sum of ideals $Ae_i$ and then that two linked ideals $Ae_r$ and $Ae_s$ belong to the same ideal $\mathfrak{b}'_m$ by observing that by Exercise 7, there exists an index $h$ such that $e_hAe_r$ and $e_hAe_s$ are $\not= 0.)$

b) Prove that the right ideals $e_iA$ are indecomposable (consider their images in $A/\mathfrak{R}(A))$.

c) Suppose that A is an algebra of finite degree over a commutative field K. We denote by $c_{ij}$ (resp. $d_{ij})$ the number of quotients of a Jordan–Hölder series of $Ae_j$ (resp. $e_jA)$ isomorphic to $Ae_i$ (resp. $e_iA)$ and by $r_i$ the degree of the field $e_iAe_i/e_i\mathfrak{R}(A)e_i$ over K. Prove the equality $c_{ij}r_j=d_{ij}r_i$ (determine the dimension of $e_iAe_j$ over K, and use Exercise 7, c)).

9) Let A be an Artinian ring whose radical $\mathfrak{r}$ is contained in the center.

a) Prove that every central idempotent in $A/\mathfrak{r}$ lifts to a central idempotent in A (if $e$ is an idempotent in A with central class in $A/\mathfrak{r}$, express the fact that $e$ commutes with $xe-ex$ for every $x\in A)$.

b) If $\mathfrak{r}$ is nonzero, prove that $A/\mathfrak{r}$ is commutative (observe that we have the relation $(xy-yx)z= 0$ for $x\in A,y\in A$, and $z\in \mathfrak{r})$.

c) Deduce from a) and b) that A is isomorphic to the product of a semisimple ring and a finite family of local rings $A_i$ such that the field $A_i/\mathfrak{R}(A_i)$ is commutative. d) Prove that every simple A-module is isomorphic to a minimal left ideal of A.

$\P 10)$ For a ring A, denote the set of pseudosubrings of A whose elements are all nilpotent by $\mathscr{N}(A)$ and the set of maximal elements of $\mathscr{N}(A)$ by $\mathscr{N}_{max}(A)$.

a) Let D be a field and $n$ an integer. Prove that $\mathscr{N}_{max}(\mathbf{M}_n(D))$ consists of the pseudorings $gTg^{-1}$, where $g$ is an invertible element of $\mathbf{M}_n(D)$ and T is the set of matrices $(a_{ij})\in \mathbf{M}_n(D)$ such that $a_{ij}= 0$ for $i\geqslant j$ (use Exercise 2 of VIII, p. 14). b) Let A be an Artinian ring and $\pi : A\rightarrow A/\mathfrak{R}(A)$ the canonical mapping. Show that the mapping $P\mapsto \pi (P)$ defines a bijection from $\mathscr{N}_{max}(A)$ to $\mathscr{N}_{max}(A/\mathfrak{R}(A))$. Deduce from a) that the intersection of the elements of $\mathscr{N}_{max}(A)$ is equal to $\mathfrak{R}(A)$
